**For:** families planning an indoor Daejeon outing. **Information checked:** 9 November 2026.

For an indoor family outing in Daejeon, start with the National Science Museum’s hall-by-hall admission rules. Some halls are free, while others require a paid ticket and advance reservation; availability can vary. Check the museum’s visitor page before traveling.

## Choose the visit

- **General visit:** Review the museum’s free halls and check whether a particular hall requires a ticket or reservation.
- **Planetarium:** The museum lists it among paid halls requiring advance reservation and a ticket. Check session times and age or language conditions on the booking page.
- **Monday or holiday:** Review the museum’s current closure notices and holiday-related changes.
- **Short visit:** Pick one hall or session first and allow time to move between buildings.

The [museum’s visitor information](https://www.science.go.kr/eps/cntnts/671/moveCntnts.do) lists general hours as 09:30–17:30 and gives separate hall requirements. It is at **대전광역시 유성구 대덕대로 481**, 481 Daedeok-daero, Yuseong-gu, Daejeon. The museum’s [directions](https://www.science.go.kr/eps/cntnts/1172/moveCntnts.do) suggest Line 1 to Government Complex Daejeon Station, Exit 3, then a bus from Dunsan Police Station; its listed route 604 stops at the museum. Check the current service and walking route before traveling. A visit is not necessarily entirely sheltered from rain.

If traveling with an infant, the museum lists complimentary stroller rental from its information center for ages 6–36 months; check availability on arrival. Wheelchairs and umbrellas are also listed for loan. Visit the [official booking site](https://rsvn.science.go.kr/nsm/main) for session and language details.

For a different science subject, Daejeon’s [Geological Museum](https://www.kigam.re.kr/menu.es?mid=b50200000000) focuses on geology and lists hours of 10:00–17:00. Check its own [reservation information](https://www.kigam.re.kr/narrationList.es?mid=b50601000000&tnc_seq=1) and notices before choosing it; it is a separate venue, not a hall of the National Science Museum. Do not assume both will be open on the same day.
