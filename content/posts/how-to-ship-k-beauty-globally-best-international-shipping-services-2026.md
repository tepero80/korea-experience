Decide how the products will leave Korea before buying full-size bottles or duplicates. An international web shop can deliver eligible online orders to certain destinations. Ask a physical store separately whether it offers shipping for what you buy at its counter. Keep the purchase receipt either way.

## Compare the three routes

| Route | Check before committing |
| --- | --- |
| Web shop | Select the destination and exact package size. Confirm the final shipping charge, taxes and return terms at checkout. |
| Parcel | Give the carrier the exact product, container, quantity and destination before choosing a box or paying. |
| Luggage | Check the airline and security rules for carry-on and checked bags, including transfers. |

[Olive Young Global’s shipping page](https://global.oliveyoung.com/foot-info/footer-contents?foterSeq=138) is an example of a policy for online orders. Use the live destination selector and actual checkout to check whether that route is available. If carrying the items, use the [cosmetics shopping and carry-on checklist](/blog/complete-guide-to-korean-skincare-routine-10-step-method-2026).

Already have a parcel ready to send? [Prepare the recipient details and declaration for a Korea Post counter](/blog/korean-post-office-international-shipping-guide).

## Ask the postal service about the exact product

Bring the label or a photo of it. [Korea Post’s international air-mail list](https://ems.epost.go.kr/front.Introduction04New.postal?country=EN&gubun=CON) lists perfume as prohibited and flags some lotions, creams and mists for review. Pressurized containers and dangerous-goods markings can change the answer. Ask whether the office needs the manufacturer’s safety data sheet (MSDS) for your item or whether another accepted option exists. These are the named postal service’s conditions.

Check the receiving country as well. For example, [Korea Post’s Argentina EMS page](https://ems.epost.go.kr/front.EmsApplyGoCondition.postal?nation=AR) separately lists destination restrictions. For a US address, consult the [current US customs guidance](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1919?language=en_US) before relying on a price or duty estimate you found elsewhere.

At the counter ask for the total postage, what the recipient might pay and what happens if delivery fails. Fill in the declaration with the actual items, quantities and values. Save the receipt and tracking details. If the route fails the product check, leave that item out and confirm a lawful alternative before sending anything.
