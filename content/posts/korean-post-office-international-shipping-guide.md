**Prepare the destination and an honest contents list before bringing the parcel to the counter.** This walkthrough is for someone who already has goods to send through Korea Post. Availability, restrictions and charges depend on the country, contents and service.

| Bring or check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Recipient's full address, postal code and contact | The destination operator needs enough detail to deliver or contact the recipient. |
| Item list, quantity, value, origin and weight | These support the customs declaration. |
| Exact products and packaging details | The branch can check restrictions and whether inspection or repacking is needed. |
| A return/contact address and contact method | Ask the branch what it accepts for your case. |

## Check the contents before sealing

Check Korea Post's [international air-mail restrictions](https://ems.epost.go.kr/front.Introduction04New.postal?country=EN&gubun=CON) and the destination's current conditions. Ask the branch about the actual product, size, packaging and service before assuming it can travel; liquid cosmetics, batteries or pressurized containers may need special checks. If already packed, be prepared for staff to ask about the contents or packaging. Keep receipts and product labels available.

## Make the declaration useful

Prepare an English item list with quantities and truthful values. Have the destination, origin and parcel details ready for the counter. Korea Post’s [European customs notice](https://ems.epost.go.kr/front.EmsNewsDetail.postal?db=notice&seq=1184) distinguishes a generic category such as clothing from a specific description such as wool pants. Ask the clerk which fields and codes your service needs.

If you want to enter parcel details in advance, Korea Post’s [smart-acceptance page](https://ems.epost.go.kr/front.EmsApply1100c.postal) opens its pre-entry process. Ask the branch what reference to bring and which alternatives it accepts.

## Before leaving the counter

Ask which service is available to the destination, its total charge, tracking and compensation terms, and what happens if delivery fails. Customs charges can depend on the destination and item. Keep the receipt and item number until delivery. If an EMS item is delayed or lost, [Korea Post's inquiry form](https://ems.epost.go.kr/iservice/ems/EmsSendMailFormFreeEng.jsp) asks for the item number, sender, addressee and contents; it states a claim deadline for each listed service. Confirm the applicable deadline rather than waiting indefinitely.

For a bottle or skincare purchase that has not yet been made, use the separate [skincare shipping decision guide](/blog/how-to-ship-k-beauty-globally-best-international-shipping-services-2026).
