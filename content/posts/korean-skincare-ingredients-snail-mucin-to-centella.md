## Turn the box around before comparing products

The [Korean Ministry of Food and Drug Safety’s labeling page](https://mfds.go.kr/wpge/m_645/de050601l0024.do) lists ingredients, use-by or period-after-opening information and use precautions. Compare the back or side panels of the products in your hand. The ingredient dictionary uses [달팽이점액여과물 for snail secretion filtrate](https://kcia.or.kr/cid/search/ingd_view.php?no=6319) and [병풀추출물 for Centella asiatica extract](https://kcia.or.kr/cid/search/ingd_view.php?no=540). Other forms of the same source ingredient can have different names.

| Look for | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| 전성분 | Compare the full list for the exact version, including ingredients you already know you avoid. |
| 사용기한 | Check the expiry information and any separate period after opening. |
| 사용방법 | Check where and how the product is meant to be applied. |
| 주의사항 | Read the precautions and ask for clarification if a term is unclear. |

## Compare two products on the same terms

Put a cleanser beside another cleanser, or a leave-on serum beside another leave-on serum. A prominent ingredient on the front does not resolve whether the rest of the formula suits your preferences. Photograph the front and ingredient panel together if you want to compare it with a product sold at home.
