Gugak covers a range of Korean traditional music. Choose a specific programme rather than assuming every performance has the same instruments, dance or English interpretation.

## Pick the show

| Ask | Check |
| --- | --- |
| What is on? | The dated performance page and repertoire. |
| Where? | The exact hall and address. |
| Who can enter? | The selected event's age limit. |
| Can I follow it? | English notes, subtitles or interpretation for that particular performance. |

The [National Gugak Center's 2026 Saturday concert page](https://www.gugak.go.kr/site/program/performance/detail?lang=en&menuid=001001001&performance_id=37048) lists its programme period through December 26 and identifies Umyeon-dang. Check your travel date; it is not proof of a show in a future season.

The [booking guide](https://www.gugak.go.kr/site/homepage/menu/viewMenu?lang=en&menuid=001001002001001) describes online and box-office options and says some overseas-issued cards may face restrictions. Check your selected performance's availability, total, cancellation rules and access arrangements before paying. The general guide says filming and recording are prohibited and late entry can be restricted. Save the hall and confirmation, and allow time to find the entrance.

Compare other [gugak venues](/blog/korean-traditional-music-gugak-performance-venues) if the date or location does not suit you, then return to the operator for current programme details.

Ask the venue about step-free access if you need it.
