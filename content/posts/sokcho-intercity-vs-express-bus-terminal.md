**Match the Sokcho terminal on your ticket before you open directions.** The intercity terminal is **속초시외버스터미널**. The express terminal near Sokcho Beach is **속초고속버스터미널**. Both terminal types can appear on Dong Seoul routes, so the Seoul departure point by itself does not settle where you arrive in Sokcho.

## Match the label to the place

| Ticket | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **시외** · intercity | Save **속초시외버스터미널**, 장안로 16. For a market visit, compare a live local bus or taxi from this terminal. |
| **고속** · express | Save **속초고속버스터미널**. Sokcho Beach is nearby; check the walk to your exact hotel. |
| **Sokcho** only | Open the ticket details or ask the carrier to identify the exact Sokcho terminal. Check both legs if you booked separate tickets. |

The [Sokcho City transport listings](https://sokcho.go.kr/ct/tour/tour_guide/traffic) put the intercity terminal at **장안로 16** and give **동해대로 3994** for the express terminal. [Dongbu Buslines, the express terminal operator](https://www.dongbubus.co.kr/terminal), gives **동해대로 3988** and has its own location link. Use the terminal's Korean name and the operator's map pin if the two numbers show different map results. Neither address identifies a boarding bay.

## Keep the outbound and return apart

1. **Choose the service, then read the details.** [VisitKorea’s bus booking guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=140657) explains the express and intercity booking channels. Search the correct date and direction, and compare the actual Seoul terminal as well as Sokcho’s. See the [city's express and intercity directory](https://www.sokcho.go.kr/sc/fields/traffic/transport/express) for terminal contacts.
2. **Save the Korean destination names offline.** Keep your hotel and the return terminal separate. If your return service leaves from the other terminal, map the transfer from wherever you will actually be before the departure.
3. **Find the bay in the building.** Use the boarding display or ask staff. A vehicle photo or an old bay number cannot establish today's platform. At the wrong terminal, show staff the ticket and ask for directions or a realistic change option before rushing across town.

## Plan the last leg before you board

For the market or a late arrival, compare the walking distance with a current bus departure or taxi from your actual terminal. Check the stop in the [Sokcho bus information system](https://bis.sokcho.go.kr/) and verify the direction before boarding.

For Seoraksan, search for a current connection to the national park entrance for your visit. Check the [cable-car and weather guide](/blog/seoraksan-cable-car-flexible-sokcho-day) before committing to the mountain outing. For a shorter city visit, use the [market, Gaetbae and Abai Village walk](/blog/sokcho-market-gaetbae-abai-village-walk) and leave time to return to the terminal on your ticket.
