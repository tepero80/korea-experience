Save the clinic’s advertisement with the branch, device name and date. Bring that version to the consultation and ask the treating clinician to explain what they are actually proposing. A package name may describe several steps. Ask for the manufacturer and model of each device involved and which one would be used on you.

## Match the booking to the proposal

| Detail to check | Question to ask the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Branch and clinician | What is the branch’s Korean name and address? Who will assess me and who would perform each part? |
| Advertisement | Which line in the advertisement describes the device you are proposing? Can you give me its manufacturer, exact model and the patient information? |
| Proposed use | What would you use this device for in my case? What are its risks, limits and alternatives? |
| Change at the appointment | If you propose a different device or add a step, can we review the revised plan before I decide? |
| Record | Can you give me a copy of what was agreed and, if I proceed, a summary of what was used? |

The [FDA’s overview of medical lasers](https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/surgical-and-therapeutic-products/medical-lasers) explains how medical lasers are defined and describes risks such as skin color changes. A reference to the US FDA does not establish authorization for a device or use in Korea. If the advertisement cites an authorization, ask which country and authority issued it, which model it covers, and where you can read the applicable product information.

## Check the branch separately

If a booking agent sends you a quote, ask for the treating branch’s own contact. [Medical Korea describes the registration system for institutions that attract foreign patients](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem). Check the exact branch rather than relying on a chain’s name. Registration by itself cannot tell you which clinician or device would be used at your appointment.

## Take home a copy you can understand

The [NHS consultation checklist](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/advice/before-you-have-a-cosmetic-procedure/) suggests asking about the procedure, the products involved and what could go wrong. Ask who will explain those points in a language you understand and whether the branch can give you a written summary. If the advertisement and proposal differ and the explanation is unclear, you can use the visit to ask questions and take time to decide. Before paying a deposit, [confirm the clinic’s written booking and cancellation terms](/blog/how-to-verify-korean-clinic-credentials-red-flags-to-avoid).
