## Choose by session and location

| Venue | Good fit |
| --- | --- |
| KOFA | Choose a film on the [Sangam screening calendar](https://www.koreafilm.or.kr/cinematheque/calendar/screenings). The archive lists free screenings and advance or same-day box-office booking. Seats can run out. |
| Cinecube | Use the [Gwanghwamun showtimes](https://www.cinecube.co.kr/cinema/time-order-table) for a film that fits your day in central Seoul. Check the specific screening’s price and language. |

## Check the language and ticket

1. Find the title and exact date/time. **영어 자막** means English subtitles and **한글 자막** means Korean subtitles. If the listing is unclear, ask **이 상영에 영어 자막이 있나요?** (“Does this screening have English subtitles?”).
2. For Cinematheque KOFA, read the [official ticket rules](https://eng.koreafilm.or.kr/pages/PC_00000102). If online booking in Korean is impractical, check same-day box-office availability and have another indoor stop ready. Get there ahead of the listed start.
3. For Cinecube, follow your screening’s ticket instructions and keep the usable ticket ready. The [theater guide](https://www.cinecube.co.kr/about/location) identifies the audience entrance at **B2**, rather than the business-office floor shown in the site footer.

### Find the right entrance

Use the Korean Film Archive map and [official Sangam directions](https://eng.koreafilm.or.kr/pages/PC_00000114) if heading to KOFA. Digital Media City Station and Sangam are a different outing from Gwanghwamun.
