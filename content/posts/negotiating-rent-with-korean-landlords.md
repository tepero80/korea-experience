## Ask for one written offer you can compare

Show the agent the amount you can pay upfront, your monthly limit and your desired dates. Ask whether the landlord can offer a different combination and how it changes the other charges. [Seoul’s brokerage fee table](https://land.seoul.go.kr/land/broker/brokerageCommission.do) shows different schedules for different property categories. Ask the agent for the category and basis of its fee.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Rent | Confirm the proposed deposit, monthly rent and lease dates on the same document. Ask about the payment recipient and timing. |
| Fees | Ask which services the building charge covers, what is billed separately and how variable amounts are calculated. |
| Fixes | Inspect the unit and ask for dates and responsibilities for any promised repair before move-in. Record what is included. |
| Sign | Use [Seoul’s pre-contract checks](https://land.seoul.go.kr/land/broker/estateBrokerageGuide.do) to verify the owner, agent registration and defects. Ask for clarification when any information differs between documents. |

## Compare the changed terms before accepting

Add the rent and recurring fees over the proposed term and keep the upfront deposit separate. Compare the result with the old offer. Request a copy of the final agreement and make sure it records the repairs or other terms you agreed.
