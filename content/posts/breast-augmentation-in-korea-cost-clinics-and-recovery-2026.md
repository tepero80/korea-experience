## Check the proposed device and the care that comes with it

The [FDA breast implant information](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/breast-implants/things-consider-getting-breast-implants) discusses the patient decision checklist, future monitoring and possible additional operations. Ask the Korean surgeon what applies to the device they are proposing, and request the manufacturer’s information or local patient documentation.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Device | What exact manufacturer, model and type are you proposing, and why? |
| Options | What risks, limits and alternatives should we review first? |
| Costs | What does the written estimate cover, and who pays if plans change? |
| Records | Which device card, records and aftercare instructions can I give a clinician at home? |

## Ask who provides care after the trip

Use the [UK guidance on treatment abroad](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/in-hospital-abroad) to ask about ongoing care, contact options and the possibility of changing the return trip. Take written instructions from the operating surgeon and arrange a contact that you can reach after departure.
