**For a short walk at Gwanggyo Lake Park, start by the Urban Levee at Woncheon Lake and return along the same bank.** Save **광교호수공원 어반레비**, **수원시 영통구 광교호수공원로 102**, as the start point. A search for the park as a whole can send you to a different section. The attraction covers two reservoirs, so choosing a named spot saves time.

## Pick how far you want to go

| Your plan | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Short stroll | Follow the Urban Levee by Woncheon Lake. Turn back at the first place that suits your time and footing. |
| One lake | Allow time for the roughly 3 km Woncheon circuit. Stay on the short route if a section is wet, icy or closed. |
| Both lakes | Suwon adds another 3.5 km around Sindae Lake. Save this for a longer outing with plenty of daylight. |

Suwon City lists the two circuits separately. The Urban Levee is a multi-level waterfront space, so look at the access and return from the path you choose. If a curb, steps or an exposed deck would be a problem, start on an open path near the approach and turn back before a descent you would have to climb again. [Suwon City’s Gwanggyo Lake Park guide](https://www.suwon.go.kr/sw-www/sw-visitsuwon/sw-visitsuwon-01/sw-visitsuwon-01-03.jsp)

## Get to a pin on the correct bank

If you are coming by metro, use **광교중앙역** (Gwanggyo Jungang). The city art space at the Suwon Convention Center lists **Exit 4** and about ten minutes on foot to the convention center. Use **수원컨벤션센터**, **광교중앙로 140**, as the first waypoint. For the last leg, open pedestrian directions to the named Urban Levee area and use marked crossings. The ten-minute estimate is for the convention center. Check the full walk to the water along with your return.

Suwon’s facility listing identifies the space in front of the Urban Levee kiosk by the road address above. Use that as a map reference whether the kiosk itself is open or closed. If taking a taxi, show the Korean name and full road address so the driver can choose the practical drop-off. [Suwon Museum of Art directions](https://suma.suwon.go.kr/views/come.do?gubun=3) · [Suwon’s Urban Levee location](https://www.suwon.go.kr/web/reserv/faci/view.do?seqNo=610)

## Keep a clear way back

Note the approach, nearby crossings and an easy turn-back point before following bends in the shore. The KTO lists free entry to the park, with facilities and restrooms. Hours for an indoor stop, refreshment or attraction inside the park can differ. Check the specific venue if your plan depends on it. [Korea Tourism Organization visitor details](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=176170)

For a rainy afternoon, check the convention center’s Art Space Gwanggyo program before setting out and treat the lake as an optional break. If you are staying around the fortress or Suwon Station, compare the travel time before crossing the city. The [Nammun market lunch walk](/blog/suwon-nammun-market-lunch-walk) makes a useful stop on a fortress day.
