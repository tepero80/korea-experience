Start with the itinerary the operator currently sells. Paju’s DMZ Peace Tour is a separate visit from Panmunjeom and the Joint Security Area (JSA). The Panmunjeom visitor center currently displays a public-tour suspension notice. Verify the exact sites included before paying for a departure.

| If you want… | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| Paju’s DMZ Peace Tour | Use the [Paju city visitor information](https://www.paju.go.kr/m/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?cntntsSn=374&menuCode=1&q_gubunCode=1004) to confirm the specific route, availability, identity requirements and the return time. Check the [live booking calendar](https://dmz.paju.go.kr/). Paju lists a valid original passport or residence card among accepted foreign-visitor documents. Every visitor in the group must be present for ticket issue. Follow the itinerary confirmed on your ticket. |
| Panmunjeom and the JSA | The [Panmunjeom visitor center](https://www.panmuntour.go.kr/) publishes the public-tour status. The [Ministry of Unification’s visitor information](https://www.unikorea.go.kr/web/unikorea/contents/fundstep_Panmunjon) also reports that visits for foreign nationals are suspended. Check there for a separately announced change. |
| A public stop at Imjingak | Check the nearby public visitor facilities and transport separately. A historical photo there gives place context and cannot establish access beyond the public area. |

## Before leaving Seoul or Paju

Confirm who is operating the tour, the exact departure point, whether the sites you want are included, and your options if they close. Save your departure address and return separately. If the itinerary changes, decide whether the confirmed route is still worth the trip. Choose a public day around your [Seoul base](/destinations/seoul) if a time-sensitive departure is unavailable.
