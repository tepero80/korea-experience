**For:** travelers facing a missing booking, unexpected charge or last-minute accommodation change. **Checked:** 9 November 2026. The outcome depends on your contract and the providers involved.

## First verify the reservation before paying twice

Find the original confirmation, dates, guest name and payment record. Contact the property and, if you booked through a third-party platform, contact that seller through its official channel as well. Ask each party to explain the booking status in writing. Do not share full card numbers in public or through unverified messages.

The [Korea Consumer Agency’s booking guidance](https://www.kca.go.kr/webzine/board/view?div=kca_2307&linkId=571&menuId=MENU00306) advises checking with the provider when a payment error occurs or a confirmation is missing, to avoid duplicate payment. It also recommends reviewing the cancellation conditions before booking.

- **No confirmation after payment:** Ask the seller whether booking and payment succeeded before making another reservation.
- **Property cannot find your reservation:** Show the confirmation, contact the platform and check spelling, dates and branch.
- **Unexpected extra charge:** Ask for an itemized explanation and compare it with the confirmed terms.
- **Delayed arrival:** Tell the property your expected arrival and ask about reception or self-check-in.

## Make a small evidence file

Save the original room type, number of guests, nights, price and included fees; the cancellation terms as displayed at booking; confirmation messages; and proof of any actual payment. Record whom you contacted, when, and what was agreed. Redact passport, card and identity details before sharing documents with anyone who does not need them.

If the hotel name exists in more than one neighborhood, check the Korean address and phone number on your confirmation. A similar English name is not enough to establish that you reached the right property.

## If a charge or cancellation is disputed

Ask for a clear written explanation and the next step from both property and booking platform. The [Korea Consumer Agency’s 2026 accommodation guidance](https://www.kca.go.kr/webzine/board/view?div=kca_2607&linkId=1041&menuId=MENU00306) discusses disputes where accommodation providers requested additional payment after a reservation; it does not make every charge or outcome the same.

If the issue remains unresolved, the [Korea Tourism Organization’s 1330 and complaint page](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632) describes a tourism complaint channel. Seoul visitors can also consult the [Seoul Tourist Complaint Center](https://english.visitseoul.net/tourist-complaint-center). Ask which organization can handle your case and what evidence it needs. Mediation or a complaint is not a guaranteed refund.

If your immediate safety is at risk, move to a safe public location and seek appropriate emergency help. Otherwise, keep discussions factual and avoid sending identity documents to an unverified contact.

## Before the next booking

Check the cancellation deadline and time zone, payment currency, exact occupancy, arrival arrangements and any fees directly in the final terms. A canceled flight does not automatically cancel a separate accommodation booking; contact each provider. Keep a usable contact method for late arrivals and save the address offline.
