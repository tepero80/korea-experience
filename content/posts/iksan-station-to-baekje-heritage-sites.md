Check your return booking in [Korail’s train search](https://smart.letskorail.com/ebizbf/EbizBfTicketSearchM.do?hidJobDv=NRM&lang=EN) before you leave the station. Work backward from that train to pick a sensible turnaround. A bus listing is a starting point for the day's plan. Verify the departure and pickup point locally.

| If you want… | Start with… |
| --- | --- |
| An indoor museum plus an outdoor temple site | **국립익산박물관**, 익산시 금마면 미륵사지로 362. The [directions](https://search.culture.go.kr/kor/html/sub01/0104.html) list bus 41 or 41-1. |
| An excavated palace landscape | **익산 왕궁리 유적**, 익산시 왕궁면 궁성로 666. The [heritage authority](https://www.kh.or.kr/visit/en/road/3/view.do?key=2408010003) lists bus 65 followed by a short walk. |
| A tight train connection | Use one site and compare taxi pickup with the next bus. If you rely on a hired ride, agree on where and when to meet for the return. |

## Keep the rail station saved separately

**익산역** is at **익산시 익산대로 153**, as listed by [Korail Retail](https://www.korailretail.com/content.do?menuNo=1070900). Ask station staff for today's signed taxi pickup. If you want both sites, check the site-to-site journey as its own leg. Choosing [one Iksan outing](/destinations/iksan) lets you leave more room for visit time and the walk to your departure platform.
