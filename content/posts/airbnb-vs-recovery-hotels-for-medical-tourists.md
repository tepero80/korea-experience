**Choose accommodation around your discharge instructions and the support actually supplied.** “Recovery hotel” is a marketing description here, not proof of medical staffing, continuous monitoring or emergency response. A private rental or ordinary hotel may fit some travel needs; neither should be assumed to provide care.

## Compare the service, not the label

| Need               | Ask a rental or ordinary hotel                 | Ask a recovery package                                                        |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Access             | Lift, steps, entrance, bathroom and bed layout | The same details for your actual room                                         |
| Someone present    | Who can assist, and during which hours?        | Named roles, qualifications, staffing hours and what “on call” means          |
| Meals              | Delivery access, storage and reheating         | Actual meals, ingredients and whether your prescribed restrictions can be met |
| Appointment travel | Pickup entrance, stairs and transport options  | Which transfers are included and what happens if dates change                 |
| Clinical help      | How to contact your treating team              | Who provides clinical care, where, and under whose responsibility             |
| Changes            | Cancellation, extra nights and companion rules | Separate accommodation, care and transport cancellation terms                 |

Request written answers from the accommodation and separate confirmation from your care team that the proposed support fits your needs. We have not verified a hotel nursing roster, emergency-response time or complication rate, so this page does not rank specific facilities.

## Make a simple arrival-and-follow-up plan

Save the Korean address and actual entrance. Confirm check-in hours, a companion's access, delivery handoff and how to enter if you return late. Ask about laundry, waste and any equipment before assuming the host allows them. If the room layout matters, request current images or measurements for that room rather than relying on a generic listing photograph.

Arrange the first return from the clinic and any review visits. Ask the treating team what would require contact or urgent help; the hotel should not invent clinical instructions. [Medical Korea](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient) offers non-emergency service information, while [119 is ambulance/fire and 112 is police](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145).

## Compare a full stay cost

Add room nights, companion costs, meals, transfers, any separate support and the possibility of extra nights. Ask what is refundable if treatment is postponed after consultation. Avoid choosing a non-refundable bundle before the assessment.

The [trip budget planner](/tools/trip-budget) helps separate rooms per night from costs per person. It does not price nursing or medical services. Keep those quotes separate and verify who supplies them.
