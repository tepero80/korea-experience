## Start at the visitor entrance

[Andong Tourism’s village page](https://new.tourandong.com/eng/sub1/sub1.cshtml?id=196&keyword=&page=1) describes Hahoe as an inhabited historic village. Ask at the entrance for the public paths, the parts open that day and the way back to your bus or parking. Confirm the admission cutoff with village staff on your actual date.

| Stop | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Entry | Check the quickest public route back and save the return stop on your map. |
| Lanes | Pick a few clearly marked public lanes. Keep quiet near homes and follow signs at gates. |
| Event | Ask if a mask-dance performance is running, where to enter and whether you can still make your ride. |
| Return | Leave a buffer for walking to the pickup point and for any wait or change at the entrance. |

## Give yourself a turnaround point

Choose a marked public stop where you can turn back before you start the walk. Ask about surface and slope conditions if they matter for your group. If weather or access is poor, shorten the route and leave extra time for the return. If you have not chosen the destination or transport yet, start with the [Hahoe versus Dosan Seowon guide](/blog/andong-hahoe-or-dosan-seowon-without-a-car).
