## Choose the visit that you can arrange

| Plan | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Overnight | Use the [village lodging page](https://oeam.co.kr/main/index.php?m_id=28) to select the house and your date. Open **상세보기** (details) for a listing such as **신창댁**. The calendar distinguishes available dates, booked dates and dates that require a call. Get a confirmed booking before relying on it. |
| Day visit | Read the [village admission guide](https://oeam.co.kr/main/index.php?m_id=31) for seasonal hours. Some facilities close on Mondays. Use public paths and ask before entering a private house. |
| Activity | Read the separate [activity page](https://oeam.co.kr/main/index.php?m_cd=24). Individual signup and group bookings have different rules. A seasonal harvest shown for groups does not mean an individual visitor can book it on that date. |

## Confirm the exact house before paying

Check occupancy, bedding, meals, bathroom access, check-in and cancellation conditions for the room you chose. Confirm how the booking is completed and save the address provided by the host. Use the [village lodging map](https://oeam.co.kr/main/index.php?m_cd=32) to distinguish houses with similar names. The operator lists [041-541-0848](tel:+82415410848) for activities and stays, including questions about same-day bookings.

## Check the last leg from Asan

[The village directions](https://oeam.co.kr/main/index.php?m_id=15) list buses 100 and 101 from the Onyangoncheon Station area toward **송악외암저잣거리** (Songak Oeam Market Street, near Songak Transfer Center). Check the correct direction, actual departure and return before setting out. Cheonan-Asan KTX and Onyangoncheon are separate stations.

The village marker below gets you to the area. Confirm the walk from your stop to the booked house, especially for an evening arrival or if you have luggage. If you cannot confirm a room or return ride, choose a daylight visit or compare other stops in the [hanok village guide](/blog/5-hidden-hanok-villages-2026).
