**Visit Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond first if you care most about the reflections. Start at Woljeonggyo instead if crossing the covered bridge is your priority and its confirmed closing time is tight.** Put the walk between them into your map before dinner. After a long sightseeing day, one stop with a clear way back is a good evening.

## Choose the order by the time you have

| Your evening | Where to start | When to shorten the route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You can get to the pond comfortably before last admission | Enter Donggung and Wolji first. Then choose a street route toward Woljeonggyo, via the Cheomseongdae or Gyochon area if paths are open. | Skip the detour if the walk and bridge check would use up your return buffer. |
| Crossing the bridge is the main plan | Start at Woljeonggyo and check which entrance is open. Continue to Donggung if you can still reach the ticket entrance comfortably. | Make it a bridge-only evening when the pond timing is close. |
| Dinner ran long or you have a train to catch | Verify transport first. If you still have time and a safe approach, choose one open sight or a public bridge viewpoint. | Skip a closed or poorly lit route and head back by a confirmed bus or taxi. |

Gyeongju’s [official evening walking route](https://gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=4724) connects the two sites in a longer city walk. Use the [city map](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=4618) for orientation, then check an actual pedestrian route from the gate you will use. Cut the inter-site walk or take a short ride when rain, fatigue or mobility needs make it difficult. A map estimate cannot account for wet surfaces, a closed path or your walking pace.

## Donggung and Wolji: work back from the ticket gate

**Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과 월지)** · `경상북도 경주시 원화로 102 (인왕동)` · [Find the palace and pond in Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%8F%99%EA%B6%81%EA%B3%BC%20%EC%9B%94%EC%A7%80) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EB%8F%99%EA%B6%81%EA%B3%BC%20%EC%9B%94%EC%A7%80) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EB%8F%99%EA%B6%81%EA%B3%BC%20%EC%9B%94%EC%A7%80%20%EA%B2%BD%EB%B6%81%20%EA%B2%BD%EC%A3%BC%EC%8B%9C%20%EC%9B%90%ED%99%94%EB%A1%9C%20102)).

The [city’s attraction page](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?area_uid=50&cmd=2&mnu_uid=4716) and [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=94404) list **21:30** for last ticketing or admission and **22:00** for closing. Both list individual admission of **₩3,000 for adults, ₩2,000 for teenagers and ₩1,000 for children**. The city [admission guidance](https://gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=4838) warns that arriving close to the cutoff can mean missing entry. A [longer city itinerary](https://search.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=4729) gives an earlier winter closing time, so use the attraction-specific listing as a starting point and confirm at **054-750-8655** if you are planning a winter or late arrival. Information checked November 23, 2026.

The [city’s night guide](https://gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=4859) describes after-sunset lighting. Give yourself time inside the grounds rather than arriving just for the last ticket. If it is raining or crowded, choose a shorter look around the pond and keep the ride back in your plan.

## Woljeonggyo: a river view and a crossing are separate plans

**Woljeonggyo Bridge (월정교)** · `경상북도 경주시 교동 274` · [Find Woljeonggyo in Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%9B%94%EC%A0%95%EA%B5%90) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%9B%94%EC%A0%95%EA%B5%90) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%9B%94%EC%A0%95%EA%B5%90%20%EA%B2%BD%EB%B6%81%20%EA%B2%BD%EC%A3%BC%EC%8B%9C%20%EA%B5%90%EB%8F%99%20274)).

The [bridge-specific city listing](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?area_uid=49&cmd=2&code_uid=1012&mnu_uid=4716) lists free entry and bridge hours of **09:00–22:00**. The [city’s night guide](https://gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=4859) lists the bridge interior and lighting separately from the gatehouse exhibition. Confirm posted hours, seasonal notices and the entrance before counting on a late crossing. If you only want the view, look from an open public path on the riverbank. Leave the stepping stones out of the route when it is dark, wet, icy or uncomfortable for your group.

## Set a return point before you begin

Save the name and Korean address of your lodging and check the return in [Gyeongju LIVE BUS](https://livebus.gyeongju.go.kr/) or your navigation app. Keep a pickup spot on a street you can reach, even if the site gate is closed. If you need a lift-equipped route or step-free access, confirm the individual entrances and surfaces with the sites and plan the onward street route separately. For KTX, the train station requires its own trip from the historic center. Use the [Gyeongju station transfer guide](/blog/gyeongju-ktx-to-hwangnidan) and leave a margin for your reserved train.

Use the [two-day Gyeongju itinerary](/blog/gyeongju-the-museum-without-walls-2-day-historical-itinerary-2026) for ideas before dusk. Keep your chosen evening stop and last ride together in the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
