## Check the appointment before adding it to your day

[Laneige’s official Seoul reservation page](https://www.laneige.com/int/en/bespoke/cream-skin/reservation.html) provides one example in Myeongdong. It lists online-only reservations for an approximately 30-minute session including analysis and formulation. The address is **서울 중구 명동8길 8**. [Find the address on Naver Maps](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A4%91%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%AA%85%EB%8F%998%EA%B8%B8%208). Check the operator’s page for eligibility, current openings and cancellation instructions. Availability can change before you go.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Service | Ask which measurements or product recommendations are included. |
| Price | Check whether a finished product is required, included or sold separately. |
| Visit | Confirm language, address and late arrival policy. |
| Label | Ask for the finished product’s ingredient list and use directions. |

## Use the results as shopping information

Treat the reading as a snapshot from the store’s device. Take medical concerns to a qualified clinician. The [MFDS cosmetics labeling page](https://mfds.go.kr/wpge/m_645/de050601l0024.do) provides context for checking the ingredients and precautions on the finished product.
