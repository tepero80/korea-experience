**There is no responsible universal “best clinic” choice for every traveller.** Establish the provider's identity, obtain an assessment from the relevant qualified clinician and understand follow-up before paying a deposit. This page does not rank surgeons or certify a clinic's safety.

## Start with identity and responsibility

Match the Korean legal name and branch to the [official registered-provider directory](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredhospitals). Read what the [foreign-patient registration system](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem) covers. Registration alone does not establish a surgeon's suitability for your case or guarantee an outcome.

| Decision                          | Written information to request                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Who is responsible?               | Named assessing clinician and the roles of the procedure and anesthesia teams              |
| What is proposed?                 | The procedure, alternatives, individual risks and consent in a language you understand     |
| What does payment cover?          | Itemized total, tax, deposits, cancellation, extra visits and complication-related charges |
| What support is actually present? | Staffing hours, qualifications and emergency transfer arrangements                         |
| What happens after discharge?     | Review appointments, direct contact and help after you leave Korea                         |
| How does this affect travel?      | Individual advice about flying, activities and the length of your stay                     |

Ask for clarification whenever a sales coordinator's explanation and the clinician's explanation differ. Do not accept “zero accidents,” a review score or before/after photographs as a substitute for that discussion.

## Separate the medical plan from the holiday plan

Keep appointment and follow-up dates flexible until the care team confirms them. Accommodation marketed as a recovery hotel does not by itself establish nursing care. Check the [recovery-accommodation questions](/blog/airbnb-vs-recovery-hotels-for-medical-tourists) against your discharge instructions.

Carry access to your records, receipts and a contact route you can use after going home. Ask your insurer privately about coverage; a procedure package does not establish insurance cover.

## Check the full cost

Do not budget on an assumed2026 cosmetic-medical VAT refund: the [current Article 107-3 provision](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1030626475) specifies eligible services through 31 December 2025. Request the current legal basis for any different claim before including it in your budget.

Use the [travel budget planner](/tools/trip-budget) only for non-clinical travel costs. We do not estimate a personal treatment price or recovery period.

## When you need help

[Medical Korea](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient) provides non-emergency information and complaint support. For emergencies, [use 119 for ambulance/fire and 112 for police](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145).

Your next practical step is to send a [clear consultation inquiry](/blog/booking-medical-consultations-in-korea-online-vs-walk-in), then decide on treatment only after an assessment and consent process you understand.
