## Name the task before you pick the plan

[KT’s prepaid example](https://globalshop.kt.com/mobile/mobileLteWelcomePrepaid.do?currentLang=EN) offers passport sign-up and describes identity checks that affect use of the line. [KT’s Global Shop checklist](https://globalshop.kt.com/support/supportNeed.do) lists requirements for regular mobile service, including a residence document under the foreign-national category. Ask about the plan in your own situation and whether it can complete the verification required by the Korean service you need.

| Need | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Data | Can my unlocked device activate this SIM or eSIM? How long will it remain valid? |
| Calls or SMS | Do I get a local number, and can this product receive and send the calls or messages I need? |
| Identity | Can this plan verify my identity on the exact Korean service I need? What name and document must match? |
| Monthly bill | Which ID and payment method do you accept? Is there a minimum term or charge to leave early? |

## Ask how to finish the plan

Before activating, save a copy of the terms and ask how to cancel, when the last bill is due and whether you can complete those steps after leaving Korea. If a store offers a bundle, ask separately about handset installments and cancellation of the mobile line. When you only need data and messaging apps, you may be able to meet the travel need with a visitor product. Keep your home line or another way to receive essential codes until you have tested the new line.

For airport pickup, device compatibility and the first connection on arrival, see the [traveler SIM and eSIM guide](/blog/korea-phone-plans-and-sim-cards-for-foreigners-2026).
