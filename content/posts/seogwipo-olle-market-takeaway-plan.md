**Use Seogwipo Maeil Olle Market for an early takeaway dinner if you are staying in central Seogwipo.** Save **서귀포매일올레시장**, **제주특별자치도 서귀포시 중앙로62번길 18**. Walk the market once, buy packaged treats first and collect the hot dish last. Check where you will eat before committing to a meal that needs a table.

## Take what will travel well

| What you need | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Dinner for one | Ask if a vendor sells one portion and whether packaging costs extra. Choose a hot main before adding snacks. |
| A meal for two | Split one savory dish first. Ask for two sets of utensils if you will eat in your room. |
| A longer walk | Choose sturdy packaging, ask for sauces separately and buy the hot dish just before leaving. |
| Food for the next day | Look for whole fruit or sealed snacks. Check the package and storage directions at purchase. |

Visit Jeju highlights foods such as abalone rice balls, tangerines and **흑돼지강정** (black pork with a sweet or spicy coating) at Jeju's markets, including this one. Use these as things to look for rather than a promise of a specific vendor. Ask about shellfish, pork, sauces and shared equipment if they matter to you. After takeaway, [check the accommodation’s waste-sorting instructions](/blog/korea-recycling-bunrisugeo-guide-2026) before disposing of containers and scraps.

## Plan your finish before you arrive

The Korea Tourism Organization publishes general market hours of **07:00–21:00 in summer** and **07:00–20:00 in winter**. Individual food stalls can finish earlier or sell out. Visit Jeju shows more than one address for the market across its listings. The name **서귀포매일올레시장** plus **중앙로62번길 18** is the practical starting point for matching the map to your hotel. It is the central daily market, separate from **서귀포향토오일시장**, the five-day market. [Korea Tourism Organization market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=459&vcontsId=90960) · [Visit Jeju market directions](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500731&menuId=DOM_700000000010774)

Have a place to eat in mind. Ask the accommodation whether takeaway is allowed and where you can sit. If you have no table or a long ride ahead, consider a sit-down meal nearby and buy whole fruit or sealed snacks for later. Avoid planning around a vendor closing time that you have yet to confirm.

Show **포장해 주세요** to request takeaway. **소스는 따로 주세요** asks for sauce separately. Check the total at the stall, especially when a price may refer to a piece, weight or set.

## Fit it into your Jeju itinerary

Keep central Seogwipo as the focus if your hotel is nearby. [Compare routes from Jeju Airport](/blog/jeju-airport-to-jeju-city-and-seogwipo) if you will reach town around dinner. For an old Jeju City stay on the north side of the island, [use the Dongmun dinner plan](/blog/dongmun-market-dinner-without-a-car) instead of crossing the island for takeaway.
