## Ask who sends each bill

Get the landlord or building manager to identify each charge in writing. Ask for a redacted sample of the building statement if it is unclear what a maintenance fee (관리비) covers. For individually billed services, note the account holder and the supplier responsible for that address.

| Service | Before you accept the keys |
| --- | --- |
| Power | Ask whether the building or the power company bills you and when your usage starts. |
| Water | Ask how the building divides the bill and whom to contact if a charge looks wrong. |
| Gas or heat | Confirm how the home is heated, who bills it and whether the provider needs an appointment. |
| Internet | Ask what is already installed and whether it belongs to the building, landlord or previous tenant. |

[Seoul's safety and service information](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/finf/elsi/safe/contPageDetail.do?conts_no=4FDD6EBAE82444F383F0F86FFC7175BB&lang=en) lists the electricity contact and advises checking the local city gas contact. In Seoul, [Arisu](https://arisu.seoul.go.kr/home/sub?menukey=7642) provides water bill lookup and payment services. Where the building collects a charge, start with the manager who issues the statement.

## Record the readings together

Ask the manager to show you any accessible meters assigned to your unit. Record the meter label or number, reading, date and time together. A photo taken with permission can help if the first bill includes part of the previous occupant's period. If meters are shared or in a locked cabinet, ask the manager for the handover readings or the building's allocation method. When moving out, ask for the last covered date and where the final bill will be sent.

## Book specialist work and plan for delays

If an appliance or connection needs work, [Korea Gas Safety Corporation's moving notice](https://www.kgs.or.kr/kgsmain/seoul/hea/board.do?searchBoardSn=102804) directs households to arrange gas shutoff work before moving and use a qualified professional for connection. Ask your building contact which provider serves the address and book through that provider.

For broadband, [Seoul's telecommunications guide](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/finf/elsi/comm/contPageDetail.do?conts_no=D6067AA192E8432180EDF96112A7C241&lang=en) lists provider contacts. Ask the provider to check the exact unit, installation access, contract length, cancellation terms and equipment returns. If the installer cannot come on move-in day, keep a temporary mobile data option for work and essential calls.

Use the [apartment check-in guide](/blog/korean-apartment-etiquette-what-neighbors-expect-2026) to record the building contact, maintenance process and other house rules at the same handover.
