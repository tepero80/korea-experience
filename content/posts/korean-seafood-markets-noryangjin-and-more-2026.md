At **노량진수산시장**, decide whether to buy from the fish-market floor and arrange cooking separately, or order directly from a restaurant. Get the full cost first. The seafood price and restaurant preparation or table charges can be separate.

| Your plan | Ask before paying |
| --- | --- |
| Buy a specific item | Ask the unit price, how the weight is measured and the final quantity. Confirm whether cleaning is included. |
| Eat upstairs | Confirm whether the restaurant can prepare your purchase, how it charges and what the total would be for your group. |
| Order directly from a menu | Compare one all-in restaurant price if you prefer a simpler meal. Confirm opening and available dishes that day. |

## Find the market and choose how to eat

[VisitKorea’s fish-market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=91565) identifies the market at **서울특별시 동작구 노들로 674**, with fish shops on the first floor and restaurants on the second. Save the market entrance near your station approach. Confirm the building access and restaurant separately if arriving late.

Raw fish and shellfish can carry germs. For a lower-risk choice, order seafood thoroughly cooked and served hot. Ask about shared preparation if you have food allergies. The [CDC travel food guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/preparing-international-travelers/food-and-water-precautions-for-travelers.html) explains how preparation affects foodborne illness risk.
