Want more context while exploring a palace area, historic neighborhood or museum? **Odii is a free Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) audio guide with regional stories and walking-course listings.** Start with a destination you already plan to visit, choose a track in a language you understand, and test playback before you set off. A guide does not reserve entry or confirm an attraction is open.

## Choose a way to listen

| Option | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| [Odii website](https://odii.kr/smarttour_web/walking/index) | Browse walking courses and test sound on your device. |
| [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kto.smarttour) | Check the publisher, store availability and device compatibility. |
| [iPhone app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/odii/id739645554) | Check your country’s store and current device compatibility. |

KTO describes Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese service; its app-store language listings also identify Traditional Chinese. **The languages offered for a particular story or screen may differ**, so confirm your chosen audio before making it part of your route. [KTO’s app overview](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=880&vcontsId=198570) links directly to both official stores.

## From a place idea to a listening plan

1. **Pick the place.** Start with your itinerary or browse the official [walking-course list](https://odii.kr/smarttour_web/walking/index). Choose a route that matches the area you already intend to visit.
2. **Check the story and language.** Open an audio entry, compare the place name with your destination and select a language you understand if offered.
3. **Test the audio.** Try a short section while connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data. Keep headphones and a charged phone available if useful, and check your carrier’s data terms.
4. **Check the actual visit separately.** An audio story does not establish current entry hours, fees, accessibility or whether a path is open. Use the attraction’s official website and a map for today’s route.
5. **Keep a fallback.** Save the attraction name and its Korean address separately and use signs or visitor information if the track is unavailable.

## Choose a course you can actually complete

Odii’s course list includes place names, an approximate distance, the number of points and a displayed duration. Treat these as starting information, not a personal walking-time guarantee. Stop for traffic signals, weather, breaks and accessibility needs, and check whether each place is open before committing to a route. For example, its list includes Seoul Jongno courses and an Incheon Chinatown and Fairy Tale Village course; confirm your own start and endpoint before leaving.

If you are visiting a museum with children, check age guidance and any booking instructions on that museum’s page rather than assuming all audio points are suitable. If a course includes multiple attractions, each venue can have its own entry rules.

## What if I cannot find or play a story?

- Try another spelling or search the Korean place name using the official venue’s website.
- Try a different available story or language. Not every track is guaranteed in every language.
- Check phone volume, headphones and your connection; try again on Wi-Fi if appropriate.
- If the app does not work on your device, try the [Odii website](https://odii.kr/smarttour_web/walking/index) or the attraction’s own visitor information.
- Ask a local visitor-information center or the [1330 Korea Travel Helpline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632) for help finding general travel information.

Keep your attention on roads and surroundings while listening. Respect any museum or venue rules about headphones, speaking and audio playback.
