## Decide where you want coverage

Both **컨실러** (concealer) and **쿠션** (cushion) can fit in a small bag. Choose by the area where you want coverage and how you want to apply it.

| At the shelf | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Tip concealer | A product to apply to a small area. Look for the applicator and check how the formula blends at the edge. |
| Cushion compact | A compact and puff for covering a wider area. Look for the included case, puff and contents. |
| Refill | Look for **리필**. Check that it fits the exact case you own and confirm what comes in the box. |

Use the full product name and option to identify the box. [Olive Young’s ABOUT_TONE listing](https://global.oliveyoung.com/product/detail?prdtNo=GA250631869) is one example of a named cushion with shade options. Store stock and available testers can vary by branch.

## Verify shade and packaging on the product you are buying

Ask to see two close shades of the same formula when testers are available. **웜** is warm and **쿨** is cool. The ABOUT_TONE example includes warm and cool options within shade 21. Use the exact combination of shade, undertone and formula on your chosen box.

For a product you will apply near your jaw, check a small tester swatch at the edge of that area if the shop permits. Look at the transition near your neck in neutral light and give the product a moment to settle. If you can’t sample a suitable shade, keep looking for a product your skin tone matches. Ask **이 색상 있나요?** (“Do you have this shade?”) with a photo of the full product label.

Check **단품** (a single item) and **리필** (refill), and verify whether the price is for that option. See our [Olive Young shopping guide](/blog/olive-young-shopping-guide-must-buy-k-beauty-products) for choosing a branch and checking the shelf offer.
