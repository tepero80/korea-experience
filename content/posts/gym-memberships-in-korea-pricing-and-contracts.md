## Ask about a single session before traveling

[Guro Community Sports Center’s gym page](https://www.gurosisul.or.kr/mod.asp?c=2&m=business&s=b01-3&t=2) lists a ₩4,000 adult Saturday single-use ticket, a session limit and indoor exercise shoes. Search for 구로구민체육센터 to identify the branch before contacting it. Ask whether a visitor can use the session, how to get a place and whether the listed fee still applies.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Ask “헬스장 일일 이용이 가능한가요?” to check whether a single gym visit is possible. State the date and time you want. |
| Total | Confirm entry, locker and any clothing or towel rental separately. Ask which payment the venue accepts. |
| Bring | Ask about shoes, clothing, age or ID requirements and where to check in. |
| Term | For a multi-visit pass, ask for valid dates, renewal and the written cancellation calculation before paying. |

## Keep the agreement if there is a recurring charge

The [Korea Consumer Agency’s training contract case](https://www.kca.go.kr/webzine/board/view?div=kca_2606&linkId=1023&menuId=MENU00307) illustrates why the actual purchase price and refund terms matter when lessons are bundled. Keep the terms you accepted, the receipt and any dated cancellation messages. If one-time access is unavailable, check your accommodation’s facilities or ask another venue about a single session.
