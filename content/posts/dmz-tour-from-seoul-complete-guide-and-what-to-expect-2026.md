## Decide where the trip starts

| Booking type | Check before paying |
| --- | --- |
| The official Paju DMZ Peace Tour | Find availability through [Paju’s reservation portal](https://dmz.paju.go.kr/). You need to get to Imjingak for ticketing. Plan your trip back separately. |
| A commercial package from Seoul | Save the named meeting point, departure instructions and return drop-off. Ask which Paju stops the price includes and whether an English-speaking guide is included. |
| Public Panmunjeom visit | The [Panmunjeom Visit Support Center](https://www.panmuntour.go.kr/) displays a suspension notice for general public tours. Check that official notice before booking any product that names JSA. |

Use **임진각 경기도 파주시 문산읍 임진각로 177** as a map reference for the Imjingak grounds. Confirm the exact counter or meeting spot from your booking. ## Confirm documents and access

[Paju’s visitor rules](https://www.paju.go.kr/m/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?cntntsSn=374&menuCode=1&q_gubunCode=1004) require the entire group to arrive before tickets can be issued. Bring an original accepted ID. For foreign visitors, the city lists a passport or foreign resident registration card among accepted documents. A copy or a screenshot is insufficient. Ask the operator ahead of time about children’s documents and any entry conditions specific to your party.

Ask whether the tunnel is included and how to stay with your group if someone cannot manage a steep, narrow route. The city-run tour does not provide a separate tour guide. Follow current on-site restrictions, including the city’s published photography ban for the Peace Tour route.

## Build in a return buffer

Confirm where you will be dropped off and leave a buffer before any onward train or fixed booking. Look up the operator’s cancellation and refund rules before you pay. If a booked route is unavailable, use the [independent Imjingak plan](/blog/imjingak-without-a-dmz-tour) to decide whether a public-area visit still suits your day.
