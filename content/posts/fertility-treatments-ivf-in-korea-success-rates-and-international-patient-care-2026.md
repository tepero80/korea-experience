## Start with a departmental inquiry

[AIN Hospital’s fertility department](https://www.ainwh.co.kr/hospital/timeTable.do?deptNo=11) is one example of a department with published appointment information. Use the hospital contact to ask whether it accepts international consultations and how to send records privately. Ask what type of visit its reply confirms, especially if an interpreter or a specific clinician is involved.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Visit | Ask if it can review records remotely and which visits require you in Korea. |
| Records | Ask which existing reports are useful, what language is acceptable and where to send them securely. |
| Contact | Ask which clinician will give you personal medicine instructions and whom to call if the plan changes. |
| Cost | Request a written estimate for the first appointment and clarity on which later services are separate. |

## Confirm the plan before committing to dates

[KHIDI lists information foreign patients should receive](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/notice/faqView.do?faqSn=48), including the treatment plan, possible side effects and a cost estimate. Keep the response with your itinerary and ask which parts depend on the clinical examination. Discuss any changes to medicines with the treating clinicians.

See the [maternity service intake guide](/blog/pregnancy-and-childbirth-in-korea-a-guide-for-foreign-mothers-2026) for records and communication questions if your care later transfers to an obstetric team.
