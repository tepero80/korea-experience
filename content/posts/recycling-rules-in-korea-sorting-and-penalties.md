Use the signs on the actual collection point. Ask the building manager or host which bin, bag and collection day apply to your address. [Gangnam’s district portal](https://clean.gangnam.go.kr/use/guid/USEGUID01000000.do?contentId=con2411150004) is one example of how a district explains its categories.

## Match the word to the material

| Label | What it refers to |
| --- | --- |
| 일반쓰레기 | General waste. Ask which designated bag to use. |
| 음식물류폐기물 | Food waste. Ask how the building collects it. |
| 재활용품 | Recyclables. Look for the further labels on the actual bins. |
| 종이, 캔, 유리병 | Paper, cans and glass bottles. Follow the building’s instructions for emptying and separating containers. |

Show an unclear item and ask **이건 어디에 버려요?** (“Where do I put this?”). If you cannot identify the right bin, keep the item aside until you can ask. For meals away from home, read the [takeaway sorting guide](/blog/korea-recycling-bunrisugeo-guide-2026). For a desk or other large item, check [bulky-waste collection](/blog/recycling-in-korea-a-complicated-guide-for-new-expats-2026).
