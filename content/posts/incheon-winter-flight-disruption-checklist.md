**For:** travelers whose flight may be affected by snow, wind or winter weather. **Checked:** 9 November 2026. Your options depend on your itinerary, applicable passenger-protection rules, airline and booking terms.

## Start with the operating airline, not the weather icon

A forecast alone does not establish whether your flight is operating. Look up the flight in the airline’s official app or website, then compare the airport flight display. If you have a connection or checked baggage, ask the airline what the current instructions mean for you before changing your ground transport. Check the [Korea Meteorological Administration forecast](https://www.weather.go.kr/neng/index.do) and ground transport separately; a weather alert is not itself a cancellation.

| Status | Check next |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled | Confirm the airline status, transport and check-in deadline. |
| Delayed | Confirm check-in and baggage deadlines; a delay alone does not let you arrive late. |
| Cancelled | Ask the airline or seller about rebooking, refund, assistance and applicable rights. |
| Transfer | Check whether flights are on one booking and ask the airline about your case. |

## Four steps before you move

1. Find the operating airline and booking reference. If you booked through an agent, keep both the airline and seller contact details accessible.
2. Check the flight’s current status and departure terminal. Incheon’s [departure guide](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1413/subview.do) warns that actual terminals can differ because of codeshare and airline circumstances.
3. Ask whether check-in and baggage deadlines have changed. A delayed departure time does not by itself tell you when the airline will stop accepting bags.
4. Check your airport train or bus separately. If road transport is delayed, choose a safe alternative and allow for luggage and the terminal walk.

Incheon Airport’s [flight information](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1396/subview.do) provides arrival and departure search; it directs passengers to verify precise flight details with the airline. The [airport’s arrivals FAQ](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1542/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGZmFxYmJzJTJGYXBfZW4lMkZmYXFCYnNBcnRjbExpc3QuZG8lM0ZwYWdlJTNEMiUyNg%3D%3D) notes that an arrival estimate can remain unchanged until updated departure information is received. Recheck rather than treating an old estimate as a guarantee.

## If the trip changes

Ask the airline or seller for written rebooking choices, baggage instructions, applicable passenger rights and any accommodation or refund conditions. Keep boarding documents and actual receipts. For a separate hotel, train or activity, contact the provider about its own change policy; an airline’s flight change does not automatically alter unrelated bookings.

If you are transiting internationally, verify entry and transit conditions with the relevant authorities before leaving the airport or accepting a new route. For urgent safety needs follow airport staff; use local emergency services when needed.


Before leaving, confirm your [Incheon departure terminal](/blog/incheon-terminal-flight-check) for the operating flight.
