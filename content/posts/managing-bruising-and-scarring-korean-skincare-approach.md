Ask the treating team for written aftercare instructions and which contact to use when a concern arises. Bruising and scar care depend on what was done and on your case. Ask the clinician before changing medicines, adding a product or changing the schedule they supplied.

| When | First contact |
| --- | --- |
| Concern about healing | Contact the treating clinic through its documented phone or secure channel. Ask what information they need and how soon you should be seen. |
| Medical emergency in Korea | Call **119**. Give your location and request an interpreter if needed. See [VISITKOREA’s instructions](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=217&vcontsId=248145). |
| Follow-up after returning | Arrange a clinician near you and ask the treating team how to provide the procedure note, medicines and instructions securely. |

## Get a plan before you travel

Record the clinic’s name, date and exact treatment. Save the after-hours contact and when each follow-up visit is planned. Ask how a clinician can reach the treating team, whether a copy of your records is available and which visits or extra care are included. For a potential dispute, the [mediation agency’s document list](https://k-medi.or.kr/eng/lay1/S239T368C374/contents.do) identifies useful records.

If you are deciding whether to arrange care before a trip, use the [clinic estimate and follow-up checklist](/blog/korea-plastic-surgery-cost-guide-2026). For an emergency, keep the [119 call steps](/blog/medical-emergencies-1330-vs-119-2026) available without depending on your clinic’s opening hours.
