Hahoe Byeolsingut Tallori combines masked characters, music and movement in a village performance. [VISITKOREA identifies Hahoe Village Mask Dance Theater](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/kperformance/contentsDBView.do?dataSetId=140&menuSn=894&vcontsId=103803) in Andong and describes a roughly 60-minute show. Characters include a monk, butcher and old woman; social satire is part of the drama.

## Plan around the performance

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Seasonal schedules and cancellations can change. |
| Language | Ask whether interpretation or subtitles are provided for your performance. |
| Entry | Confirm village admission, show access and where to enter. |
| Return | Check your last practical bus before committing to a show. |

Official tourism pages give differing schedules, so confirm the current date and time with Hahoe Village Tourist Information at +82-54-852-3588 before travelling. The [visitor listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=319&menuSn=1054&page=1&previewYn=Y&sContsTtl=&sort=regDt&vcontsId=1594195) gives the village address as 경상북도 안동시 풍천면 전서로 186. Ask about step-free seating and the route from the entrance if needed. For transport choices, see [Hahoe or Dosan without a car](/blog/andong-hahoe-or-dosan-seowon-without-a-car).
