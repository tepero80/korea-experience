A match day begins with a fixture you can actually attend. Use the [LoL Esports schedule](https://lolesports.com/en-US/lolesports?leagues=lck) to find the stage and teams, then follow the organizer’s notice for that match. A scheduled result or stream listing alone does not establish spectator access.

## Turn a fixture into a visit

| Check | What to save |
| --- | --- |
| Fixture | The date and local start time, including the city and the venue named for the exact match. |
| Purchase | The official seller, time zone of the sale, registration steps, supported payment and cancellation terms. Ask the organizer if the purchase instructions for visitors are unclear. |
| Admission | The permitted form of ticket, purchaser or attendee ID requirements if stated, entry time and the correct gate or floor. |
| Return | A route you can still use if the event runs long. Use the venue address from the ticket. |

For an event explicitly listed at LoL Park, the [tourism authority lists **롤파크 · 서울 종로구 종로 33 그랑서울 타워 3층**](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=194959). Do not use that address for an unconfirmed match. The organizer’s [2026 LCK Cup finals notice](https://lolesports.com/ko-KR/news/2026-lck-cup-finals-guide) placed that past event at Kai Tak Arena in Hong Kong, with different ticket sellers from a normal Seoul outing.

If the match date is unclear or the tickets are unavailable, keep a flexible evening. Use the [LoL Park versus PC bang guide](/blog/gaming-culture-visiting-lol-park-and-pc-bang-pro-centers-2026) to decide whether a separate gaming café visit works for you.
