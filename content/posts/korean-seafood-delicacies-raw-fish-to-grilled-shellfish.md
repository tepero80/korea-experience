## Decide which kind of meal you want

The [official visitor guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=1593337) describes choosing seafood and asking how it can be served. If your group wants cooked food, say that before a fish or shellfish is weighed. A restaurant menu with a fixed serving may be easier if your group wants a clear total in advance.

| Check | Ask before agreeing |
| --- | --- |
| Unit | Is the amount per piece, per weight or per set? |
| Portion | How much is the seller weighing or serving for your group? |
| Prep | Can they grill it or serve it raw? Is preparation included? |
| Total | Are seats, seasonings, stew or extra dishes separate charges? |

## Order in a way your group can check

Show the item and ask for the full total in writing or on a calculator. If seafood is being prepared by a separate counter or restaurant, confirm who you pay for each part. Ask staff about any ingredient restriction before ordering.

If you cannot get a clear explanation, choose a smaller prepared dish or a menu with a clear per-person price. To fit the meal into your day around central Busan, see the [Busan travel guide](/blog/busan-travel-guide-beaches-temples-and-street-food-2026).
