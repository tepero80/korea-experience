Incheon Airport lists a paid **Easy Pickup** service for eligible travelers who want checked baggage delivered to a home or hotel in Korea. It is designed for disabled passengers and travelers with reduced mobility, and the airport’s examples include guardians travelling with children under six, pregnant travelers, people aged 65 or older, and children under 13. Confirm your eligibility and the current service conditions with the operator before booking.

This is an airport-to-accommodation baggage service. It is different from leaving a bag in a Seoul subway locker or using a staffed storage counter. For those options, see the [Seoul and Incheon luggage storage guide](/blog/luggage-storage-in-seoul-and-incheon-airport).

## Confirm the service fits your arrival

The airport’s [Easy Pickup information](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1478/subview.do) says to reserve through Goodlugg’s app or website at least **three hours before the scheduled flight departure**. The service collects baggage checked at the departure airport and delivers it to a home or hotel in Korea. The airport lists its official KakaoTalk channel, **goodlugg**, for inquiries.

Before paying, use the live booking flow to confirm:

- your eligibility and any document or companion verification;
- that your airline, flight, baggage, and destination are supported;
- the arrival time window when staff can meet you at baggage claim;
- the current fee, baggage size and weight limits, and delivery deadline;
- what happens if the flight is delayed or a bag does not arrive.

The airport’s public summary does not give a current price or every baggage condition. Goodlugg’s [current service page](https://www.goodlugg.com/bf/disabled?language=en) provides operational details, but terms can change. Confirm the final offer directly rather than relying on an old search result or booking screenshot.

## How the handoff works

The operator describes a sequence that starts before flying: reserve and pay, check the bags with your airline, then upload photos of the bags and baggage tags as the booking instructions request. At Incheon, meet the Goodlugg staff member in the designated baggage-claim area, confirm the bags, and follow the required customs process before continuing without them. The bags are then delivered to the accommodation specified in the booking.

Keep your passport, medication, valuables, mobility equipment, and anything needed before delivery with you or in your cabin baggage. Do not put an essential item into a checked suitcase on the assumption that it will reach your room at a particular hour.

The operator’s service page says arrival windows and delivery deadlines depend on flight arrival time; it also lists exclusions and conditions that can affect use. Check the live terms for your exact flight. If you need to make a customs declaration, travel with a pet, or have baggage outside the stated limits, ask Goodlugg directly whether the service can accept your booking. Do not assume the general airport description overrides the booking rules.

## If you cannot use baggage delivery

Arrange a porter or a suitable airport-to-city transfer directly with the airport, airline, or accommodation. If you plan to use luggage storage instead, choose a locker or staffed counter by its retrieval hours and your route; storage does not include delivery to your hotel. Keep a backup plan for the first leg between baggage claim and your accommodation.

## Before you confirm

- Book at least three hours before scheduled departure, as the airport currently instructs.
- Verify flight, eligibility, bag limits, arrival window, destination, delivery time, and current price with Goodlugg.
- Upload only the bag and baggage-tag information requested by the operator.
- Keep essentials and travel documents with you until delivery is complete.

**Sources checked 17 November 2026.** The airport identifies the service and eligibility examples; the operator’s booking terms determine the current practical limits and price.
