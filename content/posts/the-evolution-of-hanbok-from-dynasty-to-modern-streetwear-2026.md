To understand the range of hanbok, compare museum objects with clothes sold today. These are separate visits in different cities; neither requires renting an outfit.

## Look closely, then browse

| Stop | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Daegu National Museum | Compare jackets, skirts, ceremonial clothing, headwear and embroidery. The [Clothing Culture Hall](https://daegu.museum.go.kr/eng/sub02_01_03_01.do) also discusses Western influences in hanbok from the 1940s to 1980s. Check current exhibitions before travelling. |
| Dongdaemun Shopping Complex, Seoul | [VISITKOREA’s market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=92336) describes traditional and modernized hanbok, fabrics and accessories at 266 Jong-ro (서울특별시 종로구 종로 266). Ask individual shops about sizing, alterations, care, prices and collection time. |

A modern garment need not reproduce a historical outfit. Compare its cut, fabric and fastening with the museum displays and choose what suits your own use. For more shopping decisions, see [modern hanbok for daily wear](/blog/hanbok-fusion-fashion-where-to-buy-modern-hanbok-for-daily-wear-2026).
