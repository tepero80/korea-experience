If you are already visiting Daereungwon, choose one Gyeongju food stop nearby and leave walking time for your next visit. A full rice-and-leaf-wrap set needs more time than buying a pastry for later.

## Pick the food stop that fits your route

| What you want | Look for |
| --- | --- |
| A sit-down meal | **쌈밥** (ssambap), rice and leaf wraps with sides. Ask which main dishes come with the set and whether they offer one serving. |
| A short bakery stop | **황남빵** (Hwangnam bread), a Gyeongju pastry associated with a sweet red-bean filling. Confirm the exact product, quantity and ingredients before paying. |

The [official Ssambap Street listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=189232) places it near Daereungwon and gives **경북 경주시 계림로 9** as its map address. Save **경주 쌈밥거리** first, then check the actual restaurant entrance. It lists hours and days off as restaurant-specific.

The [Hwangnambbang profile](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=174265) connects the bread with Hwangnam-dong. Nearby signs can also name other Gyeongju breads. Ask which one you are buying and how the shop recommends storing it if you are carrying it for later.

## Leave room for your evening stop

If you have a fixed entry or return time, confirm the meal’s ordering and waiting time before sitting down. Check the walk from the branch you selected, and choose a snack or takeaway if the meal no longer fits. If continuing after dark, use the [Donggung and Wolji to Woljeonggyo guide](/blog/gyeongju-donggung-wolji-woljeonggyo-evening-walk) to plan the route and the way back.
