## Read the trend alongside the survey

Hallyu means the Korean Wave, the international reach of Korean culture. You may see newer phases described with labels such as “Hallyu 4.0.” Use the label to ask which parts of culture the writer means and what evidence they use.

The [2026 overseas Hallyu survey](https://kofice.or.kr/www/bbs/view.do?bbsSn=53378&mnucd=103&scBbsMngSn=5) questioned 27,400 people in 30 countries and regions who already had experience with Korean cultural content. The online fieldwork ran in November and December 2025. In that group, 78.0% reported experiences in the food category and 77.9% in film. Those percentages describe the survey group. They provide a reason to look beyond concerts when you plan a trip.

## Match one interest to a visit

| Start with | Check before you go |
| --- | --- |
| Film or drama | Find the episode or scene, then look up the real filming location. Ask whether visitors can enter. |
| Stage | Use the event organizer’s listing for the date, ticket channel, entry time and item rules. |
| Food | Identify the dish from the scene, then check a current menu and ask about ingredients you need to avoid. |

One documented example is Cheonghaepogu in Wando, which the [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=76&menuSn=862&vcontsId=193183) identifies as a filming set for *The Admiral: Roaring Currents* and *The Pirates*. Confirm the set’s current status and access before planning a detour. The production connection can remain true even as visitor arrangements change.

## Pick a nearby fallback

Put the confirmed address and return route in your map app. For a long detour, decide what else you want to see in the same area in case the site is closed. At a real residential location, stay on public paths and leave entrances clear. If access is uncertain and you want an option in Seoul, use the [Songpa and Anguk filming-location guide](/blog/k-drama-filming-locations-in-seoul-2026).
