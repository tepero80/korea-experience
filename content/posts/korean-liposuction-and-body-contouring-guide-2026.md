## Get the procedure name in writing

Bring the clinic’s description of the treatment you are considering. If the quote says “LAMS” or another name you do not recognize, ask the clinician to spell it out and describe what would happen, what areas are included, and what alternatives they considered. Ask to confirm who would carry out each part and where they would do it.

[Article 24-2 of Korea’s Medical Service Act](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031808871) sets out explanation and written consent requirements for covered procedures, including surgery and general anesthesia that may cause serious harm. It has an exception for specified urgent situations. If you need interpretation, ask who will be present when options and risks are explained, and how you can review the consent in a language you understand.

| Check | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Team | Who would perform the procedure and handle anesthesia, if needed? What happens if the team changes? |
| Fees | Can I see the cost of the consultation, tests, facility, anesthesia, interpreter, and planned return visits separately? |
| Dates | Which follow-up visits would need to happen in Korea? Who should review my travel plans if the timing changes? |
| Help | Who can answer a concern during the day and after the clinic closes? How can I reach that person in a language I understand? |

## Match the quote to your follow-up plan

Check the written deposit terms against your proposed dates. Ask which costs may change after the examination and what is refundable if you decide to wait or the clinician changes the plan. Before making firm travel bookings, confirm when the team will review whether your return route is appropriate and what arrangements you would need if your plans change.

## Agree on the records before leaving

[Article 21 of the Medical Service Act](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1028699889) covers requests for your own records and sets conditions for access by other people. Ask what summary and records the clinic can provide, how long they will take, and how your usual clinician can receive them securely. Use the [guide to Korean medical documents in English](/blog/korean-medical-records-getting-english-translations) to decide which copies to request during the consultation.
