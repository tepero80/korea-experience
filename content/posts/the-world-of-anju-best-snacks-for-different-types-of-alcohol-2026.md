Find the cooked-food stalls at **광장시장 · 서울 종로구 창경궁로 88**. The [tourism authority’s food guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=189701) points to Gwangjang Market for bindaetteok. Read the actual menu and use the display to confirm the piece or plate you are ordering.

## Read the name and confirm the portion

| Menu word | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| 빈대떡 | A mung bean pancake. Ask which additions the stall uses. |
| 파전 | A [scallion pancake](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=181&vcontsId=179010). Check any seafood or other fillings. |
| 모둠전 | A mixed plate. Ask which items are included and how large the order is. |

Point to one item and say **이거 하나 주세요**. Before paying, check whether you can sit, whether seating requires another order and whether the stall offers takeaway. If staff cannot confirm ingredients or shared preparation and you have a strict dietary restriction, choose another stop.

Makgeolli is an optional pairing described in the tourism guide. A pancake and water can be the whole stop. For the route through the food lanes, return to the [Gwangjang Market guide](/blog/gwangjang-market-complete-food-guide).
