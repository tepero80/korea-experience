Check the brand on the karaoke screen or remote before looking up a number. Find the song in that brand’s catalog, match the performer, and confirm the displayed title on the room unit before you queue it. This works better than going by a number from a screenshot for a different machine.

## Find the right song on TJ or KY

1. **Identify the system.** For TJ, use [TJ Media’s official track search](https://www.tjmedia.com/song/accompaniment). For KY (Kumyoung), the [company’s FAQ](https://www.kyentertainment.kr/bbs/board.php?bo_table=faq&sca=%EA%B3%B5%ED%86%B5) links to its song search. Use the catalog matching the device.
2. **Search the title and artist.** Try the original title and performer. TJ’s instructions show how an English name may also be found in Hangul, such as **IU / 아이유**. Check alternate titles and artist spellings before assuming the venue does not have the song.
3. **Match the version.** Confirm the number, artist and version on the room screen. TJ notes that some versions need certain devices, and its web search covers only selected song languages. Ask about the room’s own list if you still cannot find the recording.

## Recognize the remote keys

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **곡명 / 가수** | Title / performer. Look for these search choices, then choose the displayed result. |
| **예약** | Add the displayed song to the queue. |
| **시작** | Start playback. If others are singing, check the queue and wait for your turn. |
| **예약취소** | Cancel a queued item. Select the item you actually want to remove. |

[TJ’s B2 manual](https://www.tjmedia.com/manual/240322_B2_manual_v3.2.pdf) shows one model’s controls. Other machines can arrange their buttons or screens differently. If you need help, show the title and artist, and ask **이 노래를 찾을 수 있나요?** (“Can you find this song?”).

## Bring a backup song

Save two or three titles with their performers before you arrive. If the first choice is unavailable, try a different spelling or choose the next song rather than entering numbers for another brand.
