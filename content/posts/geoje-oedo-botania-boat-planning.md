Oedo Botania is an island garden reached by sightseeing boat, not by road. The first decision is which Geoje pier your boat leaves from; book the boat and verify the island landing time before arranging transport to the pier.

## Choose a departure and verify the landing

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| At booking | Verify the exact Geoje pier, that the route lands at Oedo and time ashore. |
| At the pier | Follow the seller’s check-in, identification and boarding instructions. |
| On Oedo | Know the reboarding point and time before walking away from the landing. |

Geoje City's [Jisepo cruise listing](https://www.geoje.go.kr/ntour/user/ship/view.geoje?basicSection=A01&basicSid=635&menuCd=DOM_000008502004004000&pageIndex=1) describes an Oedo–Haegeumgang sightseeing course from **89-19 Jisepohaean-ro, Irun-myeon** (경상남도 거제시 일운면 지세포해안로 89-19), with enquiries at **+82-55-682-5577**. For Jisepo, [search the verified street address in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EA%B2%BD%EC%83%81%EB%82%A8%EB%8F%84%20%EA%B1%B0%EC%A0%9C%EC%8B%9C%20%EC%9D%BC%EC%9A%B4%EB%A9%B4%20%EC%A7%80%EC%84%B8%ED%8F%AC%ED%95%B4%EC%95%88%EB%A1%9C%2089-19). Different piers offer different itineraries. Ask whether your exact departure lands at Oedo, how much time is allowed ashore, when and where to reboard, and what happens if weather changes.

The city's [fare page](https://tour.geoje.go.kr/index.geoje?menuCd=DOM_000008506001005001) lists the island admission separately from boat fares, but its rates were last updated in 2022. Confirm the current total and any separate admission payment with your boat seller before booking. Check identification requirements for every passenger and arrive by the boarding deadline the operator gives you; do not assume a reservation alone is a boarding pass.

## On the island

Follow the signs around the garden and stay on visitor paths. Ask the operator about slopes, stairs and accessibility if they affect your visit. Before walking away from the quay, confirm the departure time and the boat or queue to use for the return.

Garden address: **17 Oedo-gil, Irun-myeon, Geoje** (경상남도 거제시 일운면 외도길 17). This is the island destination, not the mainland boarding address. If driving, confirm parking with your chosen pier, and check weather and sailing status on the day. A mainland alternative is sensible if the boat is cancelled. Leave a generous buffer before an onward bus because the return voyage and road transfer are separate legs.
