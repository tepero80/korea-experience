Bunjae is the Korean term used for miniature cultivated trees, often searched for as bonsai. A bunjae collection and a historic palace garden offer different visits; neither needs to fit a generalized idea of a “Zen garden.”

## Pick the setting

| Place | What to plan |
| --- | --- |
| Spirited Garden, Jeju | A landscaped garden with a miniature tree collection; check the operator's current admission and transport. |
| Secret Garden, Seoul | A timed garden visit reached through palace grounds; check both tickets and tour availability. |

[VISITKOREA describes Spirited Garden](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=141007) as having bonsai, stone features, ponds and themed areas. Its address is 675 Nokchabunjae-ro, Hangyeong-myeon, Jeju-si (제주특별자치도 제주시 한경면 녹차분재로 675). Save the address and check the return route before choosing a visit.

The [palace authority FAQ](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R404000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg) distinguishes Changdeokgung's palace grounds from the restricted Secret Garden and says visitors need admission for both. Tours and routes can change. Allow time to reach the garden entrance through the palace and follow the current timed-entry instructions.

For booking details and a fallback when a tour sells out, see [Changdeokgung Secret Garden tickets](/blog/changdeokgung-secret-garden-tickets). Ask each site about accessible paths, weather closures and photography rules before going.
