Decide whether you want an off-the-rack piece or a custom fitting before choosing a shop. [VISITKOREA’s Danha listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=215664) distinguishes the brand’s online selection from made-to-order appointments at Danha Maison. ## Choose the garment

| Piece | Before purchasing |
| --- | --- |
| Jacket | Measure shoulder, sleeve and closure over the layers you plan to wear. |
| Skirt | Check waist adjustment, length and whether a lining or underskirt is needed. |
| Set | Confirm which pieces are included and whether sizes can be mixed. |
| Online | Check shipping destination, dispatch time, exchange costs and whether alterations are final sale. |

Ask for the fabric composition and care label, and compare the seller’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. If buying as a gift, agree on an exchange route before departure. A design inspired by hanbok does not automatically qualify for a palace’s admission policy; check the destination’s current rules separately.

If the purchase is for a palace visit, use the [hanbok styling guide](/blog/hanbok-modern-styling-tips-2026) to plan the outfit and check the visit requirements.
