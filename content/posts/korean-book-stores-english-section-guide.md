## Find the edition before you travel

| Visit type | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| A specific title | Search by ISBN on [Kyobo](https://www.kyobobook.co.kr/). Open the listing and verify the language, author and edition. Look up stock for **광화문점** rather than relying on the online listing alone. |
| A browsing stop | Give yourself time to browse and ask where **외국도서** are shelved. A book sold in English is a separate edition from its Korean translation. |

If the site shows ambiguous stock, ask the branch directly. You can show a member of staff **이 책 영어판이 광화문점에 있나요?** (“Is the English edition of this book in stock at Gwanghwamun?”).

## Go to the Gwanghwamun branch

[Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/PalaceArea/Kyobo-Bookstore-Gwanghwamun-Branch/ENP040172) lists Gwanghwamun Station on Line 5, Exit 4. Use the current station signs and check the [branch’s own listing](https://store.kyobobook.co.kr/store-info/001) before heading there near closing time. A shelf display in the photograph records the 2017 shop layout.

If you cannot find the edition, ask whether another branch has stock before crossing town. If you are mainly looking for somewhere dry, keep a second indoor option from our [rainy-day guide](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets).
