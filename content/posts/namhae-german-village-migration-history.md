Namhae's German Village is a residential neighbourhood associated with Koreans who returned after working in Germany. Pair the hillside streets and sea views with the German Exhibition Hall to understand the miners' and nurses' stories. [KTO: village](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=75683) · [exhibition hall](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=68074)

| Your choice | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Village streets | Walk public lanes quietly; homes and guesthouses are private. |
| Exhibition hall | Check hours and admission before adding the indoor visit. |

The [KTO hall listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=68074) gives 09:00–18:00, last admission 30 minutes before closing, Monday and specified holiday closures, and a 1,000-won fee. These can change: call **+82-55-860-3540** for the date, including holiday exceptions. Parking is listed at the hall. The village itself has slopes; ask the hall about the current accessible entrance and facilities if needed.

Search **남해 파독전시관**, 89-7 Dogil-ro, Samdong-myeon (경상남도 남해군 삼동면 독일로 89-7). If you are relying on transit, confirm the exact stop and return before you set out, or arrange a taxi pickup. Keep driveways clear and stay out of private yards. For a separate coastal visit, see the [Sangju beach guide](/blog/namhae-sangju-silver-sand-beach-walk).
