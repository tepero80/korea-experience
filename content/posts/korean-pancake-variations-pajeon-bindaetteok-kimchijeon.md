Choose **마포전골목** inside **공덕시장 · 서울 마포구 만리재로 23** for a market counter with several types of jeon. [VISITKOREA’s alley description](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=231126) lists kimchi pancakes, pan-fried meatballs and scallop jeon and says hours vary by venue. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EA%B3%B5%EB%8D%95%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EA%B3%B5%EB%8D%95%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EA%B3%B5%EB%8D%95%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EB%A7%88%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%A7%8C%EB%A6%AC%EC%9E%AC%EB%A1%9C%2023). Check the stall’s sign for current options and takeaway before queuing.

## Read the label before filling a box

| Menu word | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| 파전 or 해물파전 | A scallion pancake. 해물 signals seafood, so ask which kinds. |
| 김치전 | A kimchi pancake. Ask about the kimchi, other additions and batter. |
| 빈대떡 | A mung bean pancake. The [tourism recipe example](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178984) includes pork, so the bean base does not tell you the full ingredients. |
| 모둠전 | An assortment. Ask which pieces you can pick and how a mixed order is priced. |

Say **이거랑 이거, 하나씩 포장해 주세요** to ask for one of each item you point to, to take away. Check the total and ask whether the stall sells single pieces or has a minimum. If you need separate packing, say **따로 포장해 주세요** and confirm that this addresses packing only. For a strict dietary restriction, check ingredients and preparation separately.
