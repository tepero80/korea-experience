**Choose a park that is worth the trip even if flowering shifts.** Seoul Forest works for a local spring walk; Taean requires its own travel plan. Jungnang is the rose option. For autumn cosmos, check the flower field within Olympic Park before crossing Seoul.

| Flower interest | Example | Check before committing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spring tulips | Seoul Forest or the Taean festival | Current planting or the event’s new venue and schedule. |
| Late-spring roses | Jungnang Rose Park in Seoul | The latest park photos and any festival access notices. |
| Autumn cosmos | Deulkkotmaru at Seoul Olympic Park | Whether the field was planted with cosmos and is open for the visit. |

## Spring tulips · Seoul Forest or Taean

[VISITKOREA’s Seoul Forest guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=225816) documents spring tulips among other seasonal displays. Use the [station-to-park walk](/blog/seoul-forest-station-short-park-walk) and make the flower beds one part of your visit.

**Save:** 서울숲 · 서울 성동구 뚝섬로 273. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%88%B2) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%88%B2) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%88%B2+%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8+%EC%84%B1%EB%8F%99%EA%B5%AC+%EB%9A%9D%EC%84%AC%EB%A1%9C+273)

For Taean, the [KTO’s 2026 festival guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1580801) listed **Nature World** in Nam-myeon. That past listing is useful evidence of where the 2026 event was held. Save the physical venue only after checking the organizer’s notice for your year. The [venue’s directions](https://www.ffestival.co.kr/page/sub1_3) identify the stop in front of the main entrance as 모레논정류장. Have the stop name and your return option ready before you leave Taean Terminal. Recheck the next service and entry conditions before traveling.

**Save:** 네이처월드 · 충청남도 태안군 남면 마검포길 200. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%84%A4%EC%9D%B4%EC%B2%98%EC%9B%94%EB%93%9C) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EB%84%A4%EC%9D%B4%EC%B2%98%EC%9B%94%EB%93%9C) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EB%84%A4%EC%9D%B4%EC%B2%98%EC%9B%94%EB%93%9C+%EC%B6%A9%EC%B2%AD%EB%82%A8%EB%8F%84+%ED%83%9C%EC%95%88%EA%B5%B0+%EB%82%A8%EB%A9%B4+%EB%A7%88%EA%B2%80%ED%8F%AC%EA%B8%B8+200)

## Late spring · Jungnang Rose Park

[Visit Seoul’s 2026 event listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/events/2026SeoulRoseFestival/ENPgldnt3) shows where the last festival was held and identifies Taereung Entrance Station’s Exit 8 as an approach. It lists the 2026 event as May 15–23. Check future announcements rather than using those dates for a later trip. The riverside walk can still be a plan when the festival is outside your dates.

**Save:** 중랑장미공원 · 서울 중랑구 묵동 375. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%A4%91%EB%9E%91%EC%9E%A5%EB%AF%B8%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%A4%91%EB%9E%91%EC%9E%A5%EB%AF%B8%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%A4%91%EB%9E%91%EC%9E%A5%EB%AF%B8%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90+%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8+%EC%A4%91%EB%9E%91%EA%B5%AC+%EB%AC%B5%EB%8F%99+375)

## Autumn · Olympic Park’s flower field

[The national tourism board's guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=230041) identifies **Deulkkotmaru** for an autumn flower walk. Inside Olympic Park, the field is a distinct destination. Check the latest planting and visitor notices, particularly if you’re arriving from the opposite side of the park. Follow signs and keep to the paths.

**Save:** 올림픽공원 들꽃마루 · 서울 송파구 올림픽로 424. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%98%AC%EB%A6%BC%ED%94%BD%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%20%EB%93%A4%EA%BD%83%EB%A7%88%EB%A3%A8) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%98%AC%EB%A6%BC%ED%94%BD%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%20%EB%93%A4%EA%BD%83%EB%A7%88%EB%A3%A8) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%98%AC%EB%A6%BC%ED%94%BD%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90+%EB%93%A4%EA%BD%83%EB%A7%88%EB%A3%A8+%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8+%EC%86%A1%ED%8C%8C%EA%B5%AC+%EC%98%AC%EB%A6%BC%ED%94%BD%EB%A1%9C+424)
