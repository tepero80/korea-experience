## Ask what the service can assess

[Asan Medical Center’s overseas consultation page](https://eng.amc.seoul.kr/gb/lang.patients.contents.remote_en.sp) describes a written second-opinion route and says its team reviews reports and tests. Ask how to provide original imaging and whether the provider accepts the language and format of your files. Use its official submission route to confirm what belongs in the request.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Question | Write the specific decision you want the specialist to address. |
| Records | Check required reports, imaging and any date or translation limits. |
| Channel | Ask whether the next step is a written reply, a video visit or an appointment in person. |
| Timing | Confirm expected turnaround, fee and any review before reserving travel. |

## Keep a record that the next clinician can use

Ask how the response will be delivered and whom to contact if a report is missing. The [UK healthcare abroad guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/in-hospital-abroad) gives context for planning care after travel. Share the written reply with the clinician coordinating your treatment.
