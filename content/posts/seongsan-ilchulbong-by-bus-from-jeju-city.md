**From Jeju Bus Terminal, compare the coastal 201 with the inland 211 and 212.** All three list **Seongsan Ilchulbong Entrance (성산일출봉입구)**. Check a Jeju City-bound return from that stop before setting out. At the peak, choose between the stair climb and the lower coastal route depending on the conditions, your energy and the time you have before the bus.

**For:** a daylight trip from Jeju City to Sunrise Peak and back. **Information checked:** 23 November 2026. Check your exact departures and site access on the day.

## Choose the bus and save the right stop

Start at **Jeju Bus Terminal (제주버스터미널)**, 제주특별자치도 제주시 서광로 174. At the terminal, use the current boarding board or ask staff to identify the bay. Use the destination and the stop sequence together.

| Route | Outbound from Jeju Bus Terminal | Return from the entrance stop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **201** | Toward **Seogwipo Bus Terminal** (서귀포버스터미널) along the east coast. [Outbound stops](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2439) | Toward **Jeju Bus Terminal** (제주버스터미널). [Return stops](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2440) |
| **211 or 212** | Toward **Seongsan Port** (성산항 or 성산포항). Check both departures. Route 212 also lists Jeju City Hall. [Outbound stops](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2300) | Toward **Jeju Bus Terminal**. [Return stops](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2301) |

Save **성산일출봉입구** as the bus stop and **성산일출봉** as the attraction. For the port-bound 211 or 212, the entrance stop comes before the port. Use your map to check the walk from the stop to the entrance. The [official attraction page](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500349&menuId=DOM_000001817000000004) lists the road address as **제주특별자치도 서귀포시 성산읍 일출로 284-12**.

## Decide whether to climb when you arrive

The [Korean Visit Jeju listing](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500349&menuId=DOM_000001719002000000) describes a **paid summit route with uphill sections and stairs** and a separate **free lower coastal route**. The lower route has gentler sections. Ask staff which stretches are open and what surfaces, slopes and steps you should expect, especially if you use a mobility aid.

The [published visitor hours](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500349&menuId=DOM_000001817000000004) vary by season. Visit Jeju lists ticket sales ending one hour before closing and a regular closure on the first Monday of the month, moving to the following day if that Monday is a public holiday. Check the listing or call **064-783-0959** before leaving Jeju City if your plans depend on the summit. If the site or coast is affected by weather, follow on-site closures and keep the option of a shorter outing or a return to the city.

## Protect the ride home

1. **Choose a departure and a backup.** Compare the entrance stop in the Jeju City-bound schedules before the day begins. A time shown at the start of the line may be earlier than the time at your stop.
2. **Locate the return boarding point.** On arrival, check the pole and direction for Jeju Bus Terminal. If a crossing is needed, locate it while you still have daylight and time.
3. **Make the climb optional.** If queues, steps or wind take longer than you planned, keep the earlier bus and shorten the visit. If you miss your chosen bus, recheck the next service and the connection to your hotel. If you need a taxi, ask at an open business or at the site for help arranging one and confirm the destination and fare estimate.

If seeing sunrise is central to the trip, check the first bus, attraction access and the sunrise time for your date before booking. A night in Seongsan or a prearranged ride may suit an early start better.
