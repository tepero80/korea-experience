## Separate the sale phases

A past CLOSE YOUR EYES fan meeting notice on Weverse shows how a fan-club verification window, members’ presale and a later general sale can appear as separate steps. Read your specific organizer announcement before buying a membership or reserving travel.

| Phase | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Verify | Separate deadline, matching organizer and ticketing accounts, and the eligible membership. |
| Presale | Date and time in Korea, seller link, eligible account and hold/payment deadline. |
| General sale | Whether the seller announces remaining seats and which ticket limits apply. |
| Day of event | Ticket retrieval, matching ID, venue entrance and any separately announced activity. |

## Know what a ticket includes

Check if there are multiple sessions and whether your seat includes anything beyond entry. Use the current notice for photo, recording, gifts, or add-on rules. Save the original confirmation and seller terms with the date and session. If you cannot meet the name or ID rules, contact the seller before paying. A fan post or reseller screenshot does not settle entry.
