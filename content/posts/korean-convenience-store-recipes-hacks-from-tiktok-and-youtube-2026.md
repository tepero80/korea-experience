## Read the package first

[Visit Seoul’s convenience-store overview](https://english.visitseoul.net/partners-en/seoul-goods-story/2025-seoul-souv/ENN14y3vq) describes hot-water cup noodles and a separate riverside machine for packet noodles. Ask staff which equipment is meant for your product if the package and signs are unclear.

| Case | Choose this |
| --- | --- |
| No heat | Pick sealed, ready-to-eat food. Check that the label does not require heating. |
| Cup noodles | Choose a cup meant for hot water and follow the line and time on the package. |
| River stop | Ask whether the shop offers the machine and container used for packet noodles before paying. |
| Short break | Buy something you can eat easily at your next permitted seating stop. |

## Build a simple meal

Choose one main item and a drink first. Add a snack only if it fits the time and place you have. Check labels and ask staff about anything you avoid. Where the store has seating, follow its rules for using the table and sorting trash. If there is no place to eat, take sealed food to a permitted spot rather than carrying an open hot container on a crowded route.
