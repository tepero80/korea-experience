Choose a dish by city and by the kind of meal that fits your route. In southwest Korea, try bibimbap in Jeonju or a braised fish meal in Mokpo. In the southeast, try pork and rice soup in Busan or braised chicken in Andong. These are examples from distinct cities. A region label alone tells you little about a restaurant’s ingredients, spice level or minimum order.

| Stop | A dish to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Jeonju | **전주비빔밥**, Jeonju bibimbap. Check the toppings and seasoning before mixing. |
| Mokpo | **갈치조림**, braised cutlassfish. Ask how many people the order serves. |
| Busan | **돼지국밥**, pork and rice soup. Ask how the rice is served. |
| Andong | **안동찜닭**, braised chicken with vegetables and glass noodles. Ask about size and spice. |

## In Jeolla: compare a mixed rice bowl and a coastal meal

[VISITKOREA’s Jeonju bibimbap guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221301) describes the local mixed rice dish and its variations. Before ordering, check whether your bowl includes beef or egg and ask how the vegetables were seasoned if you avoid particular ingredients. If you want a broader stop with soup and snacks, use the [Nambu Market eating plan](/blog/jeonju-nambu-market-eating-plan).

[VISITKOREA’s Mokpo visitor guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=74267) names braised cutlassfish as one of the port city’s specialties. Look for **갈치조림** on the menu. Ask whether the price is per serving or per pot and how many portions you must order. For a route before or after the meal, see the [Mokpo harbor and museum plan](/blog/mokpo-modern-history-museum-harbor-weather-plan).

## In Gyeongsang: choose a soup bowl or a dish to share

[VISITKOREA’s Busan pork-soup guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221285) introduces **돼지국밥**, pork and rice soup. Look for it when a bowl of soup suits your day. If you avoid pork, choose a dish whose broth the kitchen can confirm. Use the [Busan trip guide](/blog/busan-travel-guide-beaches-temples-and-street-food-2026) to choose a meal near the rest of your day.

In Andong, the [tourism listing for Jjimdak Alley](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=14987) places the braised-chicken restaurants at Andong Old Market, **경상북도 안동시 번영길 30**. Save **안동시장 찜닭골목** for navigation. Check the actual shop’s portion and heat level, particularly when dining alone or ordering for a child.

## Make it fit the day

Pick a dish and a location close to something you already plan to visit. If the dish is sold by the pot and your group is small, ask **1인분 주문 가능한가요?** (“Can I order one serving?”). Give the kitchen specific information about allergies and dietary restrictions and wait for an answer before ordering. [The Korean ordering guide](/blog/how-to-order-food-in-korean-restaurants-phrases-guide-2026) has phrases for ingredients, portions and payment.
