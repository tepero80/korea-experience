**VISITKOREA currently lists Gangneung's 2027 New Year sunrise event for December 31, 2026–January 1, 2027, at Gyeongpo Beach and Jeongdongjin Sandglass Park.** Its [official event listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=89955) shows the program window as 20:00–00:30, with a January 1 morning program from 06:00–08:00, and says admission is free. It does not assign each time block to one venue, so check Gangneung City's latest event notice before choosing where to spend the night.

## Choose one event area first

The two listed venues are separate places in Gangneung. Do not assume you can attend a late-night program at one and reach the other before sunrise. Choose the site that fits your accommodation and confirmed transport, then verify its exact program and local access with the organizer.

| Before you travel | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Event date and program | The city notice for December 31 and January 1; the VISITKOREA listing supplies the published event window but may be updated. |
| Venue | Whether your chosen event segment is at Gyeongpo Beach or Jeongdongjin Sandglass Park. |
| Outbound and return journey | Current train, intercity bus and local transfer schedules for your travel date; holiday event service can differ from a normal day. |
| Overnight plan | Accommodation check-in, late arrival, warmth, and whether you can wait indoors before the morning program. |

VISITKOREA names Gangneung City as organizer and lists **+82-33-640-5135** for inquiries. The city’s [official tourism site](https://www.gn.go.kr/tour/index.do) is the organizer link shown on the event listing; check it for newer notices. Before buying nonrefundable transport or lodging, confirm the chosen event time and venue with the city. The [VISITKOREA winter-festival overview](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=1595020) also describes sunrise festivals as running from December 31 into January 1, but it is not a live operations board.

For the nearby rail landmark and general visitor context, see the [Korea photo-friendly train-station guide](/blog/korea-s-most-photogenic-train-stations). It does not confirm event access, special services or a sunrise timetable for this date.

Sources checked November 17, 2026. Event programming, transport, access and weather may change; confirm with Gangneung City and the relevant operators before departure.
