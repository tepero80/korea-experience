**After arriving at Daejeon Station (대전역) by KTX, follow the signs for the city metro, 도시철도 or 지하철. Board Line 1 toward Banseok (반석) for City Hall, Government Complex or Yuseong Spa.** The railway station and metro stop share the Korean name **대전역**. Use the transit mode and final destination to tell them apart in your route app.

## Read the signs in this order

1. **Leave the railway platform first.** Follow the station concourse signs, then look for the city metro. Ask rail staff to point you toward **대전역 도시철도 1호선** if the route to the entrance is unclear. The [metro operator's Daejeon Station preview](https://www.djtc.kr/kor/page.do?menuIdx=1017) shows the outside approaches, concourse and platform as photographed in 2025.
2. **Pay for the metro at its own gates.** Use a supported transit card or buy a single-journey ticket for the city metro. Check the [operator's fare page](https://www.djtc.kr/kor/page.do?menuIdx=307) for current prices.
3. **Find the direction name at the platform.** On the [Line 1 map](https://www.djtc.kr/kor/page.do?menuIdx=39), Jungangno (중앙로) is the next stop toward Banseok. Dae-dong (대동) is next toward Panam. Match that first stop as a second check when you have a tight connection.

## Pick the line direction or stay on foot

| Where you are going | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Jungang Market | Walk from the railway station's west plaza. Use the [market route](/blog/daejeon-station-to-jungang-market-short-visit) to find the crossing and the market lanes. |
| City Hall or Yuseong | Take the Banseok side of Line 1 for City Hall, Government Complex and Yuseong Spa. Check your destination exit before you leave the metro. |
| Dae-dong, Sinheung or Panam | Take the Panam side of Line 1. |

If you are heading to Yuseong, save **유성온천역** in your app before going underground. The [Yuseong footbath and museum guide](/blog/daejeon-yuseong-footbath-or-museum-day) can help you choose which outing fits after you get there.

## Return to the railway station with time to spare

For the return from Yuseong Spa or City Hall, ride toward **Panam** and get off at **대전역**. Follow the railway connection, then match your train number to the current departure board. Set your return time to include the metro wait, the walk between the two stations and any luggage collection. If you need a step-free route, confirm working lifts and the correct approach with the [metro station contacts](https://www.djtc.kr/kor/organization.do?menuIdx=388) or railway staff. If the metro timing does not fit, compare a taxi to **대전역 KTX, 대전광역시 동구 중앙로 215**. Check the rail departure station on your ticket before getting in.
