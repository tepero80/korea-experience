Start with the document name. The [NHIS personal-service walkthrough](https://www.nhis.or.kr/static/html/wbmh/b/wbmhb0401.html) describes eligibility history. The [collection portal](https://si4n.nhis.or.kr/jpca/JpCab00101.do) lists premiums and payment documents.

## Find the right menu

| Need | Korean label |
| --- | --- |
| Coverage history certificate | 자격득실확인서 발급 in NHIS personal services |
| See billed and paid amounts | 보험료 고지/납부현황 in the collection portal |
| View or pay a premium | 보험료 조회/납부 in the collection portal |
| Prove a premium payment | 납부확인서 under certificate issuance in the collection portal |

## Request an eligibility certificate

In the [NHIS instructions](https://www.nhis.or.kr/static/html/wbmh/b/wbmhb0401.html), sign in, open **개인민원** (personal services) and select **자격득실확인서 발급**. Review the coverage history before issuing the certificate. Ask the recipient which dates and format it requires. If the dates look wrong, use the [coverage and enrollment guide](/blog/korean-healthcare-system-using-national-health-insurance) to prepare your questions for NHIS.

## Check a bill or request proof of payment

On the [official collection service](https://si4n.nhis.or.kr/jpca/JpCab00101.do), compare the name and billing period with your bill before paying. Use the payment certificate when the recipient asks for premium payments, and check which period it covers. If you cannot sign in or your employer and account show different records, use the [NHIS foreign resident center information](https://www.nhis.or.kr/english/wbheaa02100m01.do) to find the center serving your address and ask which ID or documents it needs.
