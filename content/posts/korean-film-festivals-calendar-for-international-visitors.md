One confirmed screening with time to reach its theater is a better anchor than three films booked at different venues. Start with the current official program. Pick one screening that shows its language information, then choose another option at the same venue or leave time to cross the city.

If the event you want is a separate awards night, [check its admission route before booking travel](/blog/korean-film-awards-shows-attending-as-a-tourist).

## Compare three festival trips

| Festival | What to check before spending |
| --- | --- |
| Busan International Film Festival (BIFF) | Copy the theater on your ticket. The [2026 archive](https://www.biff.kr/eng/addon/10000001/page.asp?page_num=11079) shows how far apart different venues can be, from Centum City to Community BIFF screenings. |
| Jeonju International Film Festival (JIFF) | Wait for the audience sales notice and verify the theater on your screening page. The [2027 call for entries](https://entry.jeonjufest.kr/en/submission.asp) is for filmmakers. |
| Bucheon International Fantastic Film Festival (BIFAN) | Check login and card support for your edition. The [2026 instructions](https://www.bifan.kr/eng/ticket/ticketing.asp) listed overseas Visa, Mastercard and JCB. |

Check the language shown on the screening card and look for separate subtitle information. Keep the organizer’s confirmation and confirm the required ticket format before leaving Wi-Fi. BIFF’s [2026 screening FAQ](https://www.biff.kr/eng/addon/10000001/page.asp?page_num=11269) required an actual mobile ticket where applicable and rejected screenshots. Treat this as an example for planning, and read the current instructions for exceptions and late-entry rules.

For Busan, pick the accommodation after locating the theater rather than by a festival name printed on a shopping street. Use the [Busan neighborhood and transport guide](/blog/busan-travel-guide-beaches-temples-and-street-food-2026) to compare where to stay. Keep the flight or hotel change terms visible until you have the screening that matters to you.
