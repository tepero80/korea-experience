If your ticket says **Wonju (원주역)**, use **강원특별자치도 원주시 북원로 1860**, the address identified by [Wonju City](https://www.wonju.go.kr/www/selectBbsNttView.do?bbsNo=161&key=313&nttNo=478768&pageIndex=943&pageUnit=10&searchCnd=all). For **Manjong (만종역)** or **Seowonju (서원주역)**, look up that station by name. Recheck the label when buying your train return on [Korail](https://www.letskorail.com/).

| Where you are going | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Central Wonju | Use a live bus search or taxi to your exact address. [Gangwongamyeong](/blog/wonju-gangwongamyeong-heritage-visit) is a separate stop in the city center. |
| Wonju History Museum | Check local buses or a taxi to 봉산로 134. Leave time to get back to your station. |
| Museum SAN or Sogeumsan | Check the city-tour bus or arrange a road transfer and return. Fit the destination's admission window into your route. |

## Save the right station and destination

For Wonju Station, search the official address rather than a platform number. ## Check a limited city-tour bus before booking a train

The [city timetable](https://www.wonju.go.kr/english/contents.do?key=6510) lists separate train stops, the bus terminal and routes toward outlying attractions. Confirm the exact circuit, operating day and return with Cheonho Tourism at **033-763-1005**. On holidays, check before relying on an exception to the regular closure. If the return is tight, arrange a taxi pickup from the attraction or choose a shorter city visit.

Compare the three options in the [Wonju destination guide](/destinations/wonju).
