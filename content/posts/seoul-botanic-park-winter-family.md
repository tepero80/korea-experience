The greenhouse at Seoul Botanic Park gives families an indoor plant-focused outing on a cold day. Plan around its winter last-entry time and remember that the outdoor park and the paid greenhouse have different operating rules. A family activity is offered for a defined period, but a listing does not guarantee available places or English support.

| Topic | Official information |
| --- | --- |
| Winter hours | November–February 09:30–17:00; last entry 16:00 for the greenhouse |
| Closed | Normally Monday; public-holiday and substitute closures require a date-specific check |
| Individual admission | Adults 19–64 KRW 5,000; ages 13–18 KRW 3,000; ages 6–12 KRW 2,000; check proof and exemptions |
| Address | 161 Magokdong-ro, Gangseo-gu, Seoul / 서울특별시 강서구 마곡동로 161 |
| Transit | Magongnaru Station, line 9 or Airport Railroad, exits 3 or 4 connect to the Open Forest; continue to the greenhouse |

The operator's [winter-hours notice](https://botanicpark.seoul.go.kr/front/board/newsView.do?sn=20761) and [visitor and fee guide](https://botanicpark.seoul.go.kr/front/introduce/useInfo.do) list seasonal hours and fees. The notice says the greenhouse opens on public-holiday Mondays; the general guide specifies closure on the following day. For a particular holiday, confirm the opening and follow-up closure with the operator at 02-2104-9716. Outdoor forest, lake and wetland areas are listed as open year-round, but they are not warm indoor replacements when the greenhouse is shut.

## Choose the experience before leaving

1. Search **서울식물원** in your map app and check the walking route from Magongnaru Station. The [operator's subway directions](https://botanicpark.seoul.go.kr/front/introduce/location02.do) connect exits 3 and 4 to the Open Forest, not directly to the greenhouse. No step-free route has been verified.
2. Arrive before winter last entry, allowing time for your family to buy tickets. Different outdoor areas do not give automatic access to the paid greenhouse.
3. For families with elementary-age children, Seoul’s [official activity listing](https://culture.seoul.go.kr/culture/culture/cultureEvent/view.do?cultcode=159718&menuNo=200010) describes a self-guided exhibition workbook program from 3 September 2026 through 16 May 2027. It specifies advance online booking by the preceding day and requires an accompanying adult. Availability depends on the date and workbook supply; greenhouse entry requires a separate ticket. Check age, language and the current booking page before traveling.
4. If the session is full, visit the greenhouse independently when open rather than promising the activity. For a fully indoor alternative, compare another venue in the [Seoul rainy-day itinerary](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets).
