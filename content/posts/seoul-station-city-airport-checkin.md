**Seoul Station's City Airport Terminal is an option only for eligible flights and passengers using the designated express train.** Confirm the operating airline, actual flight, baggage and cutoff before buying transport. If you need to keep shopping available for customs inspection, settle the tax-refund sequence before handing over your bag.

This guide uses Korean Air’s published City Airport Terminal instructions as a specific example, not a claim that every airline offers the same service. Incheon Airport says participating airlines vary by city terminal. The rules below for flight operation and exclusions are Korean Air’s rules; ask your actual operating carrier about its own service.

## Decide whether this route fits

[Korean Air's city-terminal guide](https://www.koreanair.com/contents/plan-your-travel/check-in/counter-check-in/city-airport) describes Seoul Station service for eligible same-day Korean Air international departures from Incheon. It says passengers must purchase an Incheon-bound **express** train ticket, check in and drop bags, complete the listed immigration step, then take the express train. The page also lists a check-in cutoff and counter hours; verify both with the airline for your actual departure day before leaving your hotel.

- **Carrier:** A codeshare sold by one airline may be operated by another; Korean Air lists restrictions for a flight operated by another airline.
- **Timing:** A trip that reaches the city terminal after the airline's deadline may need ordinary airport check-in.
- **Train:** The Korean Air page specifies the express train for Seoul Station check-in; a different train ticket does not automatically qualify.
- **Assistance:** Korean Air lists exclusions for several assistance cases, pets and certain oversized or special items. Ask the operating carrier.
- **Shopping:** Items may need to remain accessible before export validation. Ask the refund operator and airline before bag drop.

### Korean Air example: who cannot use it?

As checked on [Korean Air’s guide](https://www.koreanair.com/contents/plan-your-travel/check-in/counter-check-in/city-airport?hl=en), flights marketed by Korean Air but operated by another airline, certain charter or non-scheduled flights, unaccompanied minors, passengers requiring special assistance such as wheelchair assistance, and passengers traveling with a pet cannot use its city check-in service. Korean Air also excludes sports equipment, musical instruments and oversized items requiring special handling, while noting a golf-club exception. Ask the carrier about your exact item; do not infer permission from that exception.

A codeshare on a Korean Air-operated aircraft with another carrier’s flight number is listed as eligible by Korean Air, but confirm the actual booking with the carrier. These exclusions are **not** a statement of another airline’s rules. If city check-in is unavailable for you, arrange airport check-in and any needed assistance directly with your airline.

## A practical sequence

1. At your hotel, verify the departure terminal, flight status, participating airline, passenger eligibility and the city-terminal counter’s current hours and cutoff. The [airport’s city-terminal page](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1420/subview.do) identifies the Seoul Station facility on Airport Railroad B2 and says participating airlines vary.
2. Keep your passport, any boarding pass already issued, permitted carry-on essentials and any documents or goods needed before airport departure with you. Read the [shopping tax-refund guide](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) before deciding what to check.
3. Buy the eligible Incheon-bound express-train ticket. At the participating airline counter, follow its instructions for check-in and bag drop; obtain and retain the baggage receipt. Korean Air’s published sequence gives the boarding pass at this step.
4. Follow the immigration office’s instructions at Seoul Station if eligible, then board your booked express train. The airport describes the train platform at B7 and a designated departure lane at Incheon for those who complete the city-terminal process. At the airport, follow staff and signs for the applicable passage, security and any remaining passport checks; check the gate on the display.
5. If check-in is unavailable, the train is disrupted or your bag is not accepted, ask the airline and rail staff about your options. Leave enough time to complete regular airport check-in by the airline's deadline.

## Things a city check-in does not settle

A city check-in does not waive aircraft security rules, destination entry requirements, passport checks or the need to reach the gate by the boarding deadline. Airport and airline procedures can change. Ask the airline which service applies to your documents and specific baggage, and keep cash, medication and other essentials out of checked luggage when appropriate.

The airport [departure guide](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1413/subview.do) warns that terminal assignments may differ due to codeshare or airline circumstances; recheck the actual terminal. Compare the entire journey and fallback in the [airport transport guide](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train), rather than treating the city-terminal service as available to every passenger.

Sources checked 2026-11-09: Korean Air’s city-airport page and Incheon Airport’s city-terminal guide. Airline participation, eligibility, hours, cutoff, train and service requirements can change and should be confirmed again before travel.
