**Do four checks, not one “can I fly with it?” check.** A purchase can pass one rule and still fail another: screening, airline baggage, export inspection and destination customs are different decisions.

## Sort the purchase before choosing the bag

| Question | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Is it a liquid, gel or paste in international carry-on? | Check container capacity and the one-litre bag rule |
| Does it need to go in checked baggage? | Check airline allowance and hazardous-goods rules; do not assume every liquid is allowed |
| Is a tourist refund involved? | Keep goods available for required export inspection before bag drop |
| Will the destination/transit accept it? | Check current food, plant, medicine and screening restrictions with the relevant authority |

## Container capacity comes first

[Incheon Airport's international carry-on guidance](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1433/subview.do) permits liquid/gel containers of **100 mL or less**, together in **one transparent resealable 1 L bag per person**. The container capacity matters: a half-empty 200 mL bottle does not become a 100 mL container.

The airport explicitly includes **kimchi and gochujang** as liquid/gel foods for screening. Do not assume that a solid-looking food package follows a different rule. Duty-free sealed-bag conditions and transfer screening also need their own checks.

## Do not bury the inspection goods

<GuidePhoto id="exp-c-tax-refund-kiosks" caption="Refund processing is separate from airline bag acceptance. Follow the operator handling your receipts and keep any inspection goods available before checking the relevant bag." />

The [KTO tax-refund guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140736) distinguishes refund routes and goods-export conditions. Put receipts/forms with the relevant purchases and follow the actual inspection sequence. A kiosk photograph is not proof of today's terminal location or operator coverage.

## Finish the packing the day before

1. List the purchases that need airline, transit or destination confirmation.
2. Sort carry-on liquids by actual container size, not remaining content.
3. Pack fragile items deliberately and check the ticket's weight/size allowance.
4. Keep inspection goods accessible, plus the documents and valuables you must retain privately.
5. Put bag drop, refund steps and the [airport journey](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) into the departure plan with a usable buffer.
