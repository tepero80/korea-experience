Daewangam Park gives Ulsan visitors a coastal walk through pines to rocky sea views. The park also has a 303-metre suspension bridge, but it is a separate attraction with its own hours and rules. The shorter bridge to Daewangam Rock is a different structure. [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=172686)

## Choose your walk

| If you want | Pick this plan |
| --- | --- |
| The headland and pine trees | Walk toward Ulgi Lighthouse and turn back if the rocky coast is too wet or windy. |
| The suspension bridge | Check closure, last entry and group restrictions before following the bridge signs. |
| A rainy-day backup | Hold a separate museum plan and confirm its hours. |

From the entrance, follow the pine-lined path toward Ulgi Lighthouse and Daewangam Rock. Ulsan tourism describes a 600-metre pine trail to the lighthouse. Take time for the headland and turn back whenever the weather or your group requires it. Coastal surfaces can be slippery in rain; sturdy shoes and an extra layer are sensible. [Ulsan tourism](https://tour.ulsan.go.kr/tour/eng/contents.ulsan?mId=001006000000000000)

If the suspension bridge is your priority, check it first. The [park operator](https://daewangam.donggu.ulsan.kr/BBS_011List.do) lists free admission, 9 a.m.–6 p.m. operation with last entry at 5:40 p.m., and closure on the second Tuesday each month. Rain, weather warnings, sea fog or maintenance can also close it. The city tourism listing additionally reports closure on Seollal and Chuseok days. Check notices on the day: +82-52-209-3738.

The operator says wheelchairs, strollers and pets cannot enter the suspension bridge. Visitors under 13, older visitors and people with mobility difficulties should be accompanied. This restriction is specific to the bridge; ask the park about conditions on the route you intend to use rather than assuming every other path is step-free.

## Getting there

Use **대왕암공원**, 95 Deungdae-ro, Dong-gu (울산광역시 동구 등대로 95). The park's [transport FAQ](https://daewangam.donggu.ulsan.kr/BBS_011List.do) lists buses 104, 124 and local bus 32 at the Daewangam Park terminus. It distinguishes that stop from Daewangam Park Entrance, which requires another 15–20 minutes on foot. The [park campsite directions](https://daewangam.donggu.ulsan.kr/camping/Pgstret.do) list bus 124 from Taehwagang Station; verify the current direction and stop before boarding.

The park is generally open at all hours, while dangerous weather can restrict access. The park is free apart from some facilities. If staying overnight, the [official Ulsan accommodation directory](https://tour.ulsan.go.kr/tour/kor/unit/acmdtn/list.ulsan?mId=001002010000000000) includes Dong-gu options; compare the location and cancellation terms with a central base before booking.

Daewangam in Ulsan is not the offshore royal tomb with the same name in Gyeongju. Confirm the city when searching for directions.
