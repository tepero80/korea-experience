Gangwongamyeong (강원감영) is the historic compound of the former Joseon provincial governor in central Wonju. Use the gate and courtyards as the main visit, then see whether the rear garden or a small exhibition is open.

| Decision | Practical detail |
| --- | --- |
| Find it | Search **강원감영**. The operator uses **원일로 77** and [VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=188458) uses **원일로 85** for the complex. Follow the signed public entrance. |
| Plan a visit | The [operator](https://whm.wonju.go.kr/whm/page/view.php/sub_07_01) lists 09:00–22:00 for the site. Check specific rooms, event arrangements and temporary closure notices. |
| Arrive from the station | Use a local bus or taxi for your exact station. If using the [city-tour bus](https://www.wonju.go.kr/english/contents.do?key=6510), match the route and return first. |

## Walk through the compound

Look for **Pojeongnu Gate (포정루)** and **Seonhwadang (선화당)**, the governor’s office. The [city’s site history](https://www.wonju.go.kr/english/contents.do?key=2924) explains the buildings’ functions. Ask the office about current interpretation, language support and step-free options rather than assuming all spaces are available. The operator lists **033-747-2416** and publishes [temporary closure notices](https://whm.wonju.go.kr/whm/board/board.php?b_id=news&cmd=view&num=1318).

If you want a longer indoor visit, compare [Wonju History Museum](/blog/wonju-history-museum-visit) and its last admission. Plan an onward bus or taxi to the museum rather than treating it as part of this compound.
