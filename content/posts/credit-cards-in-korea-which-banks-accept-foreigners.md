A Korean **check card** (체크카드) is linked to a bank account and commonly deducts regular purchases from that balance. A **credit card** (신용카드) bills eligible spending later and requires issuer review. Decide which payment you need to make before comparing products or moving your bank account.

## Compare the right feature

| Need | Ask the issuer |
| --- | --- |
| Pay from balance | Which bank account can you link and when does each purchase debit it? |
| Pay a monthly bill | How is credit assessed and when will the bill be due? |
| Tap for transit | Is postpaid transit a separate request and when do fares debit? |
| Pay online | Does this card support the payment method used by the merchant? |

[Shinhan’s SOL Global check-card terms](https://www.shinhancard.com/pconts/html/card/apply/check/1229905_2206.html) list foreign customers with a foreign registration card as eligible to apply. Check its separate transit conditions if you need to use it on buses or subways. [KB’s global customer page](https://mapps.kbcard.com/SVC/DVIEW/HSGMCXCRSCTCR0001) lists the WELCOME PLUS debit card and directs applicants to KB Kookmin Bank branches. Ask which branch and documents apply before visiting. Each example applies to that specific product.

## Decide on your next action

If you want a credit card, read [Hana Card’s issuance overview](https://www.hanacard.co.kr/jsp/cmn/cm/O/OSA05000000N.web) as an example of how one issuer checks identity, creditworthiness and capacity to pay. Check the chosen issuer’s current documents and rules. Ask for the annual fee, billing date and what to do if the required payment method cannot be activated.
