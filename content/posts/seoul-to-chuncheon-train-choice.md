**For:** travelers planning a Seoul-to-Chuncheon day trip. **Information checked:** 9 November 2026.

For a train to Chuncheon, compare **ITX-Cheongchun** and the **Gyeongchun Line** using your actual Seoul starting point. Korail lists ITX-Cheongchun on the Yongsan–Chuncheon route and metropolitan rail on the Gyeongchun Line. These services have different ticket and boarding arrangements; check the live timetable and fare for your date.

## Choose your train

- **ITX-Cheongchun:** Search **용산 → 춘천** (Yongsan to Chuncheon) in [Korail’s booking channels](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140656). Compare available departures, ticket conditions and the station you will actually board. Keep your ticket and allow time to find its platform.
- **Gyeongchun Line:** Use a route planner from your accommodation to **춘천역**. Check the transfer station, train destination and last return service. The [Korail operations page](https://info.korail.com/info/contents.do?key=863) lists the line separately from ITX; its displayed service statistics are historical, not a live schedule.
- **Nami Island instead:** The [tourism authority’s Nami guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=215120) uses **가평역** (Gapyeong Station). Plan the onward transport from Gapyeong rather than assuming Chuncheon Station is the correct stop.

## Make the day workable

1. Choose your actual destination and compare its onward route from the station. A station named Chuncheon does not mean every attraction is within walking distance.
2. Check outbound and return times for your travel date, the route from your accommodation, and whether your preferred ITX ticket is available.
3. Confirm current payment, luggage and accessibility conditions with Korail. A rail ticket and a metropolitan transit card are not automatically interchangeable.
4. Keep a later return or an indoor alternative in mind if weather or availability changes. Check the venue and transit operator directly before relying on either.

[Find 춘천역 in Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%B6%98%EC%B2%9C%EC%97%AD) and save the train and onward route in your [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary). This guide does not provide live fares, seats or departure times.
