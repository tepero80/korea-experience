Ask the clinic directly, even if someone else arranged the booking. Specify the language you understand comfortably when discussing health. For example, [Samsung Medical Center](https://www.samsunghospital.com/en/patient-guide/faq.do?searchText=&type=TRANSLATION) asks patients to arrange interpretation when booking, says it cannot guarantee a same-day request and asks patients to check the fee. This is one hospital’s policy; confirm the details at your own appointment.

## Confirm what the interpreter will cover

| Stage | Question to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Booking | “Can you arrange medical interpretation in my language for this date and department?” |
| Appointment | “Will the interpreter be present in the consultation room or available remotely? What happens if the appointment runs late?” |
| Payment | “What is included in the fee, and what are the cancellation terms?” |
| Leaving | “How can I get the clinic’s instructions and ask a follow-up question from abroad?” |

You can show **진료 중 통역을 받을 수 있나요?** to ask whether interpretation can be arranged during the appointment. Bring a short set of questions and tell the clinician if the explanation is unclear. Ask how the service handles private information and use the clinic’s designated contact channel for records.

The [1330 travel helpline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?menuSn=454&vcontsId=140632) provides tourism information and language assistance; ask the clinic separately about interpreting medical decisions. If the appointment is routine and the necessary assistance cannot be arranged, check a different time or provider. In an emergency, [call 119 and request interpretation](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145).
