## Ask the questions before checking out

[South Tees Hospitals’ discharge guidance](https://www.southtees.nhs.uk/resources/discharge-home-following-your-day-surgery-procedure/) illustrates contacting a day surgery team about concerns after discharge. Ask your own team to explain the instructions for your operation and how to request help if the situation changes. Ask for an out-of-hours option in addition to the standard clinic contact.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Expected | Ask which changes your own team expects and what would change the plan. |
| Call | Write down the direct clinic number, hours and any out-of-hours contact. |
| Visit | Save the Korean address and instructions for an assessment if your team asks you to come in. |
| Travel | Ask whether appointment, transport or departure plans need to remain flexible. |

If the clinic asks you to buy a garment separately, use the [compression garment purchasing checklist](/blog/compression-garments-where-to-buy-in-seoul) to confirm the exact specification and fitting.

For an eyelid procedure, [ask about the right contact for a change in vision and make a separate plan for eye follow-up](/blog/double-eyelid-surgery-in-korea-complete-guide).

## Carry the instructions with you

The [NHS hospital-discharge guide](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/hospitals/going-into-hospital/being-discharged-from-hospital/) describes planning help and contacts before leaving. Keep the instructions available offline. For an urgent medical concern, follow the urgent-care advice of your treating team or local emergency services.

See the [post-discharge lodging guide](/blog/korean-medical-tourism-recovery-hotels-and-aftercare-guide-2026) to coordinate the room and journey with the care instructions.
