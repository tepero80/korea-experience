Use this checklist after identifying the treating facility. If the quote came through a coordinator, confirm it with the clinic’s own booking contact. Keep the clinic’s written answer with your deposit confirmation.

## Confirm each line before paying

| Topic | Ask the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Price | What is included, in what currency, and which items could be billed separately? |
| Deposit | How much is due now, who receives it and when is the balance due? |
| Change | What happens if I cancel, reschedule, or the clinic changes the appointment? |
| Language | Who interprets consultation and consent, when are they available and is there a fee? |
| Follow-up | Who answers later questions, when can they be reached and what records can I take home? |

Ask whether a quoted estimate includes consultation, tests or follow-up if they are relevant to the visit. Ask the clinic to state any refund deadline in Korea time and show the payment recipient. If two organizations charge separate fees, request a breakdown and the terms for each. A written quote makes it easier to notice a change before paying.

When you contact the actual clinic, [confirm how to request a medical interpreter](/blog/medical-interpreter-services-in-korean-clinics-do-you-need-one) and who will be with you during the appointment.

If a brochure cites national patient figures, [check what those statistics count](/blog/medical-tourism-safety-statistics-2026) before treating them as evidence about the facility.

If the booking started with a device advertisement, use the [laser advertisement checklist](/blog/laser-treatment-in-korea-types-and-prices) to match the branch, device and written proposal.

## Confirm the appointment and travel plan separately

Ask who the appointment is with, who explains consent and what happens if your appointment changes. Bring the clinic any records it asks for using its approved channel. Ask the treating clinician whether your dates allow any required follow-up or changes to your travel plans. Discuss any assessment or image-access request with the facility directly.

Use the [Medical Korea register](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/main.do) and its [description of facility and facilitator registration](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/intro/registration.do) if you need to match a new name on the quote. The [HIRA facility search](https://www.hira.or.kr/ra/hosp/getHealthMap.do) can help identify a location using its Korean name. Use the [identity checklist](/blog/how-to-choose-a-clinic-in-korea-red-flags) to resolve a mismatch before sending money.

At the appointment, ask for a receipt showing what was paid and an accessible way to request the visit summary. Save the dated quote, terms, receipt and clinic contact in one place you can reach after departure.
