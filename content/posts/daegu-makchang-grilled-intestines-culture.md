Start with **안지랑 곱창골목 · 대구 남구 안지랑로16길 67** if you want to compare restaurants. [VISITKOREA’s listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=78212&menuSn=351) identifies the area as an alley for marinated gopchang. Its [Daegu makchang entry](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178491) describes grilled pork intestines. Check the actual menu for **막창**, and ask which meat the item uses because a translated menu word may be broad. ## Ask three questions before the grill starts

| Question | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| 돼지막창인가요? | Whether the dish is pork makchang. If you avoid offal or pork, ask if a clearly identified alternative is available. |
| 첫 주문은 몇 인분부터예요? | How many portions the first order needs. Then check weight, side dishes and the total. |
| 언제 먹으면 돼요? | When to eat. Let staff explain what has been cooked and whether you or they finish it. |

The [Gugongtan Makchang listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=183916) is an example of meat arriving pre-grilled for further cooking. Its listed main branch is **구공탄막창 본점 · 대구 서구 국채보상로 308**, a separate stop in Seo-gu. Check the exact branch and current restaurant information before heading out.
