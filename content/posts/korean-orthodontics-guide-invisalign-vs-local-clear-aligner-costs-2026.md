If you are comparing Invisalign and another clear-aligner offer in Korea, ask each clinic to explain what the quoted plan covers. Have the treating clinician explain suitability for your case and what visits the proposed treatment involves. When appropriate, check the actual branch in the [Medical Korea registry](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredhospitals).

## Compare plans before paying

| Part | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Are the exam, scans or records and planning included? What is charged if you stop after the assessment? |
| Course | Which trays and review visits are included? What happens when a visit is missed, trays are lost, or the plan needs refinements? |
| Finish | Who fits or supplies retainers? Ask which follow-up checks and replacements are billed separately. |
| Move | Ask who can receive your records, what transfer is possible and how any remaining balance or new fees are handled. |

[Medical Korea’s disclosure guidance](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/notice/faqView.do?faqSn=48) explains the treatment and expected cost information registered facilities should communicate to international patients. Compare two written offers with that scope in mind. For general payment and deposit checks, see the [written clinic quote checklist](/blog/korea-plastic-surgery-cost-guide-2026).

## Before leaving Korea

Ask your current team which appointments they expect before and after departure. Contact the proposed receiving clinic directly about acceptance and costs. Request the records each practice needs, ask how to get them securely and keep the receipts. The [medical mediation agency’s document list](https://k-medi.or.kr/eng/lay1/S239T368C374/contents.do) gives examples of records that can matter if a dispute arises.
