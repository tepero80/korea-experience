## Choose the kind of visit

| Plan | Before leaving |
| --- | --- |
| Day visit | Use [Golgulsa’s visitor listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=104360) and check the open public areas. Confirm separately if you want to observe an activity. |
| Overnight | Choose the Templestay date and programme in the [official Golgulsa profile](https://eng.templestay.com/en/MI000000000000000019/temple/introView.do?templeId=Golgulsa). Confirm language, check-in, participation, lodging, meals and cancellation with the host. |

Read the schedule before deciding how much activity you want. Ask about stairs, walking surfaces, sitting or movement, and dietary needs ahead of booking. Keep the confirmation where you can open it offline.

Before choosing a retreat, [compare temple stay formats and booking questions](/blog/korean-temple-stay-experience-complete-guide).

## Use the host’s approach

**골굴사 · 경상북도 경주시 문무대왕면 기림로 101-5**

[Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EA%B3%A8%EA%B5%B4%EC%82%AC) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EA%B3%A8%EA%B5%B4%EC%82%AC) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EA%B3%A8%EA%B5%B4%EC%82%AC)

The [current programme directions](https://eng.templestay.com/en/MI000000000000000062/reserve/view.do?bookmarkFlag=&templeFlag=&templeId=Golgulsa&templestaySeq=17163) tell drivers to search for **골굴사** by name because the street address alone can send navigation to the old road. Follow the host’s instructions rather than an address-only route. If you arrive for a reserved stay, the host directs guests to the office in the first building on the right after the main gate. Recheck the instructions in your own confirmation.

If you are using public transport, confirm your return connection or arrange the last leg before you leave central Gyeongju. For a day visit, keep a realistic amount of time for a respectful walk and stay in areas open to visitors.
