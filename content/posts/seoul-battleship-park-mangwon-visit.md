**서울함공원** is an exhibit on the Mangwon waterfront. Choose the approach first, then decide whether boarding or just seeing the ship from shore fits the day.

| Approach | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | Start at Mangwon Station (망원역), Line 6 Exit 2. [VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=229713) estimates about 20 minutes on foot. Save the ship park as the destination. |
| Bus and walk | Use Exit 1 and take Mapo 09 to **한강공원입구**. [Seoul’s Hangang office](https://hangang.seoul.go.kr/www/contents/803.do?mid=527) lists around seven minutes of walking from there. |
| Shore or exhibition | Look at the ship from the public waterfront if the grounds are open and you do not want the interior visit. Check steps and narrow spaces with staff before buying a ticket. |

## Decide how long to stay

The [city visitor guide](https://hangang.seoul.go.kr/www/contents/803.do?mid=527) lists winter hours of **10:00–18:00 from November through February**, and **₩3,000 per adult** for individual admission. Mondays and listed holidays are closures. Check last entry before leaving, especially late in the day. Indoor areas are only part of this outing. Shorten the exposed river walk in wind or rain and follow any waterfront restrictions. Save Mangwon Station separately for the return.
