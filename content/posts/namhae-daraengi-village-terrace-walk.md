Visit Gacheon Daraengi Village (가천다랭이마을) for the view from public hillside paths. The terraces are working fields and the village is home to residents. Keep paths and driveways clear.

| If you have time for… | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A short look at the terraces | Start on the signed village paths and choose an out-and-back walk. Allow more effort for the climb to your starting point. |
| A full trail section | Check [Barae-gil section 11](https://www.namhaetour.org/01250/01251.web) and arrange your road transfer from the end. The trail does not loop back to the village. |

## Save the right address

[VisitKorea’s village page](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=90688) shows **남면로 702** in Korean and a different road address in English. The [official trail listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=68098) uses **남면로679번길 21** for the starting point. Search **가천다랭이마을** first, then match the parking lot or drop-off you plan to use. The marker here is for the village area.

## Choose your turn-around point

Wear shoes with grip and give the climb back time if you walk downslope. Stay on the public paths and keep off planted fields. The [trail foundation](https://www.namhaetour.org/01250/01251.web) currently lists the full section at 13.5 km and about five and a half hours including breaks. Check current detours and confirm the ride back from the small art museum area first. If you want a village with an indoor stop instead, check the [German Exhibition Hall](/blog/namhae-german-village-migration-history) as a separate outing.
