**From Soraepogu Station, use pedestrian directions to Sorae Wetland Ecology Park at 77 Sorae-ro 154beon-gil.** Choose a daylight walk near the salt-field area and turn back the way you came if the wind or footing worsens. Treat the ecology exhibition as an optional indoor stop. The park’s seawater footbath closes for winter.

## Choose outdoor and indoor stops separately

| Goal | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Short outdoor walk | Follow an open marked path near the salt-field area. Return from a point that fits your time and the weather. |
| Ecology exhibition | Go early in the afternoon and call first around holidays. The city lists a 17:00 entry cutoff. |
| Warm footbath | The city lists March to mid-November, with a December–February shutdown. Build a winter trip around the walk instead. |
| Poor conditions | Check posted closures. If paths are icy or wind is strong, choose an open indoor stop elsewhere. |

KTO lists trail hours of 09:30–17:30 separately from broader park access. The Incheon park overview publishes a winter exhibition closing of 17:30, while the exhibition page shows 18:00. Both list 17:00 as the winter entry cutoff. Confirm opening and the schedule with the desk at **+82 32 435 7076**, especially around Mondays and public holidays. [KTO’s park and trail information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=80678) · [Incheon’s park overview](https://www.incheon.go.kr/park/park010301) · [Incheon’s exhibition guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/park/park010302)

## Navigate from the rail stop you actually need

Search for **소래포구역** (Soraepogu Station), on the Suin–Bundang rail corridor. After leaving the station, open your walking route to **소래습지생태공원**, **인천광역시 남동구 소래로154번길 77**. Sorae Port and its market are different map results. Give a taxi driver the full park name and street address if you want to shorten the approach. Incheon Tour describes a ramp at the main visitor entrance and facilities inside the exhibition hall. Check the path surface and return crossings if your group needs step-free access. [Incheon Tour directions and access notes](https://itour.incheon.go.kr/ssst/ssst/detail.do?cotId=ITD21121615132555085)

## Look for the habitat from the marked path

The site combines salt fields, tidal channels and reed beds. Follow the main visitor paths and use an open viewpoint to look across the landscape. Keep off the mud and salt beds unless you are taking part in an authorized activity. Wildlife depends on tide and season. A photograph from a summer walk shows what the habitat can support under different conditions.

For winter planning, stop for warmth before heading out on the longer paths. The [city’s footbath operating information](https://www.incheon.go.kr/park/park010303) also allows closures in rain or strong wind during its normal season. Save the route back to Soraepogu before starting, and use the same approach if a detour is unfamiliar.
