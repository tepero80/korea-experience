For skiing, pick a resort and check your actual slope, equipment and ride back. For an ice event, take the trip only when the organizer confirms that your specific session is running. Indoor plans are useful if weather disrupts either outing.

| What you want | Confirm first |
| --- | --- |
| A beginner ski or snowboard day | Look for an open beginner slope and a lesson or equipment booking that fits the same hours. Confirm separate costs for transport, lift access and rentals. |
| A snow outing with no skiing | Check the operator’s sledding or visitor facilities, and whether a family needs its own reservation or tickets. |
| An ice-fishing session | Check the current festival edition, then the status and reservation rules for the exact activity. Follow only the staffed visitor route. |

## Check the resort and the ride back separately

The [Yongpyong operator’s open-slope page](https://www.yongpyong.co.kr/eng/skiNboard/openStatusBoard.do) lists which slopes are running. Use it as a final check rather than judging conditions from a winter photo. Lift hours, rental counters and last return transport are separate. In the [Korea ski-resort comparison](/blog/winter-ski-resorts-comparison-vivaldi-park-vs-yongpyong-vs-alpensia-2026), start with your travel time and check what you would do if your intended slope closes.

## Enter only an open, staffed ice activity

The [national tourism calendar’s Hwacheon listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=96732) lists January 9–31, 2027 for Hwacheon, while its detailed program description still refers to 2026. Treat the dates as a planning lead. Confirm current details through the organizer linked there, including arrival time, reservation, return and status of the specific activity. Use the designated access point and go onto ice only once the event is open and on-site staff direct you. Keep off unmarked or closed ice, even if people are nearby or the surface appears solid.

Use the [ice festival opening checklist](/blog/korea-ice-festival-opening-check) before leaving. If the activity is suspended, choose an open managed attraction that fits the ride back. A public winter forecast is no substitute for the organizer’s on-site assessment.
