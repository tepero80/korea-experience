Choose the shop around the meal you can actually prepare and carry. A supermarket may be convenient for a mixed shopping list; a local market can work for a smaller purchase from individual sellers. Check the actual location before heading out.

| If you need | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| Several packaged groceries | The particular supermarket's opening hours and closure notice |
| A few fresh items | The market's opening and the individual seller's quantity and payment options |
| Online delivery | Service area, account and payment requirements, delivery slot and accommodation rules |
| Food for a short stay | Fridge, cooking equipment and how much you can finish |

## Confirm the specific store

[E-Mart's FAQ](https://store.emart.com/faq/faq.do?tab=goTabP) directs shoppers to branch-specific opening and closure information and notes that some branches close twice a month. This does not establish which day your branch closes. Check its current listing before traveling, especially on a holiday.

The Korea Tourism Organization describes a [service program at named traditional markets](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=1590057), including efforts around price display and card acceptance. That program does not prove that every stall takes every card. Ask before choosing items and keep another payment option available.

If your recipe calls for a fermented paste or soup soy sauce, use the [Korean pantry label guide](/blog/learning-to-cook-korean-food-at-home-essential-pantry-items-2026) to choose the right product and size.

## Buy for your actual kitchen

Check whether your accommodation permits cooking and provides a refrigerator, utensils and a way to dispose of packaging. Read the label and ask staff about ingredients or allergens where needed. A translation app can help identify a product, but it cannot guarantee a safe answer for a serious allergy.

For online grocery orders, verify the delivery zone, the checkout requirements and whether the hotel accepts deliveries before paying. Do not assume a foreign card, local phone number or same-day slot will work. If the order is unsuccessful, check its status before placing another.

If the market itself is part of your day, choose a location using the [regional market guide](/blog/traditional-markets-outside-seoul-worth-the-trip).
