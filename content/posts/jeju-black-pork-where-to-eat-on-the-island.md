## Pick the stop by where you end the day

| Area | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Old Jeju City | [Dombe Don](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CNTS_200000000012749) sits on Black Pork Street near Dongmun Market. Ask if the menu offers both grilled pork and **돔베고기**, Jeju-style sliced boiled pork. |
| Nohyeong | Go to the [Donsadon main branch](https://m.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CNTS_000000000020104&menuId=DOM_000001719003000000) if this is on your route. Visit Jeju lists Tuesday as its weekly closure; confirm directly and use the U-pyeong-ro address to distinguish it from other branches. |
| Jungmun | [Donirang’s main branch](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CNTS_000000000020670&menuId=DOM_000001719003000000) is a south-side option. If your hotel is in central Seogwipo, check the actual evening return route before making the detour. |

## Read the menu before ordering

Ask for **흑돼지 (heuk-dwaeji)** for black pork. **오겹살 (ogyeopsal)** is a belly cut and **목살 (moksal)** is a neck or shoulder cut. Before agreeing to a set, check its weight, minimum order and which parts of the meal come with it. If you need a solo portion, **혼자 먹을 건데, 1인 주문이 가능한가요?** means “I’m eating alone. Can I order for one?”

If a dipping sauce is labelled **멜젓 (meljeot)**, ask about fish ingredients. If an allergy matters, identify the specific ingredient and ask about shared utensils and cooking surfaces.

## Keep the return easy

Pin the restaurant and your lodging before you go. Check last order, the wait and your route back before committing.
