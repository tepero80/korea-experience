## Choose a restaurant booking

The Templestay Information Center lists Balwoo Gongyang as a temple-food restaurant on its fifth floor. Ask about the current service, booking options and the specific meal being served. Save your booking confirmation, street address and floor together.

| Booking | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Course | Total per diner, start time, set menu and length of the meal. |
| Menu | Stocks, fermented seasonings, toppings and any substitutions the kitchen can confirm. |
| Kitchen | Whether a requested restriction can be accommodated with the equipment and kitchen process in use. |
| Arrival | Date, party size, late arrival policy and access needs. |

## Ask about the complete meal

Send the names of the ingredients you avoid and ask for a reply about the exact menu. Tell the restaurant if cross-contact matters to you and check with staff before eating. Choose another meal if the kitchen cannot confirm what you need. The name of the cuisine alone does not give that confirmation.

For a group with different needs, decide on one acceptable menu first and keep the written reply handy when you arrive. To cook or learn with a host rather than reserve a dining table, follow the [temple food day-program guide](/blog/korean-temple-food-dining-experience-guide-2026).
