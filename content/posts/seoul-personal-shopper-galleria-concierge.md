**A currently listed personal-shopping option for visitors in Seoul is the concierge at Galleria LUXURY HALL.** Galleria describes it as a personal shopping service for foreign customers. Its English page lists email contact for English, phone hours of 11:00–18:00, and the meeting point as the **WEST 5F Global VIP Lounge**. It does not publish a price, service duration, product scope, booking lead time or eligibility rules, so ask before planning around an appointment.

This is a service at one department store in Apgujeong. It is not evidence of a citywide personal-shopping tour or a guarantee that a particular brand, product, language or time slot will be available.

## Contact the service in your language

| Contact route | Galleria’s published details | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | Email `globalvip@hanwha.com` | Send your preferred date and time window, shopping interests and budget range. Ask whether the service is available and whether any fee, membership or advance notice applies. |
| Chinese | WeChat IDs listed: `yu01041984484`, `Galleria_LuxuryHall`, `y01047534484` | Confirm which account is current before sharing trip details. |
| Japanese | LINE ID listed: `galleriajp` | Ask for appointment availability and the service’s current scope. |
| Phone | `+82-2-3449-4484`, 11:00–18:00 | The published phone hours are service contact hours, not the department store’s opening hours. |

These channels and the location come from [Galleria’s official concierge page](https://dept.galleria.co.kr/en/global-service/concierge-service.html), checked **November 17, 2026**. Contact information can change; check the page again on your travel date.

## Ask these questions before you go

Galleria’s page does not answer these planning details. Ask the concierge directly and wait for confirmation:

- Is a personal-shopping appointment available on my date and time?
- Which departments, brands or product types can the appointment cover?
- Is there a fee, purchase minimum, membership requirement or deposit?
- How long is the appointment, and which languages can the shopper use?
- Should I bring identification, a reference photo, size information or a product list?
- Can the appointment accommodate my mobility or communication needs?

Do not assume the service is free, that a same-day visit is possible, or that every luxury brand participates. If you need a product from one specific brand, ask whether the concierge can confirm its stock; otherwise contact that brand’s counter separately.

The store’s general shopping hours are separate from the concierge contact window and may vary by day or holiday. Check Galleria’s [current store information](https://dept.galleria.co.kr/en) before setting your arrival time.

## Make the appointment useful

Before writing, narrow your request to a few priorities. For example, list the product category, preferred brands, approximate budget, size or shade information, and any must-have language support. Those details help the concierge tell you whether the service fits your plan; they do not guarantee stock or a recommendation.

You can use this short English email draft:

> Hello, I will be visiting Galleria LUXURY HALL WEST on [date]. Is a personal-shopping appointment available between [time window]? I am interested in [categories/brands] and have a budget of approximately [range]. Please let me know the service duration, any fee or eligibility requirements, and what I should bring. Thank you.

Send only the information needed to ask for an appointment. Wait for an official reply before sharing any sensitive personal or payment details.
