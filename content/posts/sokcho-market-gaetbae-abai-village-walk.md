**Start at Sokcho Tourist & Fishery Market, then walk to the downtown Gaetbae landing.** Cross the harbor channel to the Sinpo side if the ferry is running. Keep the village lanes optional, and choose your return before you board. If the ferry is closed, use the signed bridge route only if there is enough time and the walking conditions suit you.

## Choose the version that fits your day

| Time | Choose this route |
| --- | --- |
| Short visit | Market → downtown landing → out-and-back ferry if both directions are operating. Turn back at the landing if the queue eats into your margin. |
| Longer walk | If the conditions suit you, add the lanes around Abai Village or follow the [city's longer 3 km course](https://www.sokcho.go.kr/ct/tour/recommend/sokchoroad/road04). The 3 km figure is for the city's full course. |
| Ferry closed | Check the signed pedestrian bridge approach if the ferry is closed or boarding is difficult. If the detour, stairs or elevator uncertainty will be a problem, stay on the market side or arrange a taxi. |

## Follow the route from the market

1. **Market · 속초관광수산시장.** Start at **중앙로147번길 12**, the [Korea Tourism Organization’s market address](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=75204). Shops keep their own hours; leave room for the market to be your whole outing if the weather changes.
2. **Downtown Gaetbae landing · 아바이마을 갯배.** Use **중앙부두길 51**, the address on [Sokcho City's ferry listing](https://www.sokcho.go.kr/ct/tour/attraction/experience?contentSeq=118). Ask for the downtown landing so a directions app does not send you to the opposite bank. At the landing, confirm both directions, the last return and the accepted payment. The [city's English walking map](https://sokcho.go.kr/ct/page/sokchotour/download/Walking_Tour_Map_English.pdf) and an [older VisitKorea listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=234&menuSn=977&vcontsId=174444) differ on payment.
3. **Sinpo side and Abai Village · 아바이마을.** Follow the city walking route from the landing toward the village lanes and Seorak Bridge. Families who fled the north during the Korean War settled in this area. The [city route map](https://www.sokcho.go.kr/ct/tour/recommend/sokchoroad/road04) gives the historical context and marks public restrooms near the market and under Seorak Bridge at the village entrance.

## Decide how you will get back

The [city’s bridge guide](https://sokcho.go.kr/ct/tour/attraction/experience?contentSeq=98) describes the pedestrian route and elevator and stairs between the bridge and the village. Confirm the elevator and your ground-level approach before relying on them. Ask ferry staff about boarding if you use a wheelchair, have a stroller or have trouble with changes in level; the city labels ferry access as requiring assistance. Do not assume either route is step-free throughout.

If you are leaving town the same day, check the [correct Sokcho bus terminal](/blog/sokcho-intercity-vs-express-bus-terminal) before you leave the market. For a separate mountain day, start with the [flexible Seoraksan cable-car plan](/blog/seoraksan-cable-car-flexible-sokcho-day).
