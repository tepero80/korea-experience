For a compact introduction to central Tongyeong, combine Gangguan harbour, Jungang Market and the hillside lanes of Dongpirang. [KTO](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=77738) places the market beside Dongpirang and Gangguan in front of it. Start at the water if you want a level waterfront stop, then decide whether the climb into the village suits you.

## Start by the water

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A short walk | Stay by Gangguan’s public waterfront and stop at the market. |
| A hillside walk | Add Dongpirang from the market side and turn back if the steps or slope do not suit you. |
| A boat later | Leave time for the separate trip to the passenger terminal. |

Search **강구안** (Gangguan), 경상남도 통영시 통영해안로 328. Walk around the public harbour edge and look toward the moored fishing boats. Across the road, **통영 중앙시장** offers seafood and other goods; individual vendors keep their own hours. Market address: 경상남도 통영시 중앙시장1길 14-16. [KTO: Gangguan](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=77863) · [KTO: market](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=77738)

## Climb to Dongpirang

From the market area, look for **동피랑마을** and take a lane uphill. The village address is 경상남도 통영시 동피랑1길 6-18. [KTO](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=113285) describes the hillside village, its 2007 mural project and views toward Gangguan. Paintings change: enjoy what is present instead of expecting a particular image from an older photograph.

The hill involves slopes and steps. If a climb is uncomfortable, stay by the harbour and market or check a live map for a suitable approach; do not assume the village is step-free. Return downhill when ready and continue along the waterfront. Allow a flexible amount of time rather than treating this as a timed circuit.

This is a residential neighbourhood. Keep voices low, avoid blocking doorways and do not photograph people or peer into homes without permission. Take rubbish with you and be mindful of traffic in narrow lanes.

## Fit it into a longer trip

The harbour walk is separate from the lower cable-car station and the passenger ferry terminal. Check each destination on a map before ordering a taxi or planning a connection. Consider staying near the harbour if you want to return to this area in the evening, and confirm your property's exact location and cancellation terms directly.
