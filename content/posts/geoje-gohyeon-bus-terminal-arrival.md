Gohyeon Bus Terminal is a practical arrival point for central Geoje, but it is not the same place as the island's ferry and sightseeing-boat piers. Before buying a ticket, match your arrival terminal to your hotel or next stop, then plan a separate local transfer.

## At the terminal

Search **고현버스터미널**, 10 Gohyeoncheon-ro, Geoje (경상남도 거제시 고현천로 10). Geoje City identifies its tourist information desk inside the terminal; its location is useful for finding the building, not a particular departure platform. [Public Data Portal](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15013112/standard.do)

The city's [intercity bus information](https://bis.geoje.go.kr/main/busServiceInfo.do?action=intercityBus) lists Gohyeon terminal enquiries at **1688-5003** and identifies Busan Seobu/Sasang as a connecting terminal. Routes and departure times can change; check the ticket seller for your date and distinguish intercity services from local bus 2000. The city's [live bus information system](https://bis.geoje.go.kr/map/realTimeBusInfo.do?action=searchLineList) lists route 2000 between Geoje and Hadan. Confirm its current stops and schedule before travelling.

## Choose your onward leg

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Historic park | Use 거제도포로수용소유적공원 and confirm a city bus direction or taxi. |
| Island garden | Use the exact pier in your Oedo booking and leave time from the terminal. |
| Overnight first | Keep baggage and late check-in in mind when choosing the hotel side. |

For the [POW Camp Historic Park](/blog/geoje-pow-camp-historic-park), search **거제도포로수용소유적공원**, 61 Gyeryong-ro. The city lists a local route through the terminal and POW camp, but verify the exact direction and stop in its [route information](https://www.geoje.go.kr/index.geoje?menuCd=DOM_000000106003001004) before boarding. A taxi is an alternative if luggage or the schedule makes the bus inconvenient.

For [Oedo Botania](/blog/geoje-oedo-botania-boat-planning), choose and book a boat departure port first. Jisepo, Jangseungpo and other piers are distinct; do not ask for “the ferry terminal” without its name. Geoje City's [Jisepo cruise listing](https://www.geoje.go.kr/ntour/user/ship/view.geoje?basicSection=A01&basicSid=635&menuCd=DOM_000008502004004000&pageIndex=1) gives the address 경상남도 거제시 일운면 지세포해안로 89-19 and contact +82-55-682-5577. Confirm the current sailing and local route before departure.

If you stay overnight, compare a Gohyeon base for terminal access with lodging near the pier or attraction you will use. Confirm late check-in and the actual return transfer with your accommodation. Allow extra time for weather-sensitive boats and avoid promising a tight intercity connection.
