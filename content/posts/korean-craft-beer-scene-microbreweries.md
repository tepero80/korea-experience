## Pick a place for the experience you want

[Visit Seoul](https://korean.visitseoul.net/editorspicks/%EC%88%98%EC%A0%9C%EB%A7%A5%EC%A3%BC-%EC%88%98%EC%A0%9C%EB%A7%A5%EC%A3%BC_/17262) lists a Magpie craft beer stop on Noksapyeong-daero. [Tap Public’s own site](https://www.tappublic.com/) describes a self-pour format. Use that example to decide whether you want to pay by the pour, then confirm the current branch, address and serving terms before setting out.

| Visit | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| One drink | Ask which beer is available and what serving sizes you can order at the bar. |
| Self-pour | Ask how you start a pour, how it is measured and how you settle the bill. |
| Takeout | Ask which cans are available and whether you can carry them to where you are going. |
| Brewery | Check the actual production site, visitor access, advance booking and return transport. |

## Keep the day flexible

Match the venue to the area where you will spend the rest of the evening. Check whether food or nonalcoholic choices are available if someone in the group wants them. Agree on a point to leave, then look up your ride using the actual address, not just the company name.

If you want to see how traditional rice wine is made rather than stop at a beer venue, compare your options in the [makgeolli brewery visit guide](/blog/makgeolli-brewery-tastings-2026).
