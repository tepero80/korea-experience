## Identify the F-5 route before counting years

The Permanent Resident chapter of the [Korea Immigration Service Visa Navigator](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/immigration_eng/230/570965/artclView.do) lists different routes. General permanent residence, permanent residence after points-based F-2, and family- or qualification-based routes can ask for different evidence. Start by requesting the current name and code for the route you are considering. A period under one status may be treated differently from the same period under another.

| Record to prepare | What you need the immigration office to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Each previous status and its start and end dates | Which statuses count for the proposed route, and which official records show those dates? |
| Departures, returns and gaps between statuses | How does the route count time outside Korea or consecutive stays under different statuses? |
| Employment, income or supporting records that could apply | Whose income or other proof is accepted, what period should it cover and which documents are current? |
| Integration or other evidence specified by the checklist | Which program, assessment or alternative evidence is accepted, and which conduct documents apply? |

## Reconcile the evidence before filing

List your status periods and travel dates in order. For example, if you changed from E-7 to F-2, put the change date on the timeline and record trips taken during each period. Ask whether your office needs an official history with past status changes as well as a record of entry and departure. [Government24's entry and departure certificate service](https://m.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12700000024&HighCtgCD=A10001&Mcode=10020&tp_seq=01) explains the request channels. If a date in your documents differs from the date you remember, bring the discrepancy to the office before treating the period as eligible.

Save the version or date of the route checklist you were given. Ask what originals and copies must be shown and in what format. If you need a foreign police certificate or another document issued abroad, confirm who must issue it, what translation or authentication is accepted and how recent it must be before ordering.

If your long-term question is citizenship, [prepare a naturalization consultation](/blog/korean-citizenship-naturalization-process) with your stay and address timeline.

## Keep current permission and approval separate

Confirm the filing office and what to do if your current authorization expires, you plan to travel or your circumstances change while your case is pending. Keep the submission receipt and use the office's instructions for any extension or follow-up. If the application is approved, ask about both your new status and your card. [Korea Immigration's stay administration guidance](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration/1515/subview.do) describes the separate permanent-resident card renewal system.

If you are currently deciding whether to apply for F-2 residence first, use the [F-2 route comparison](/blog/f-2-resident-visa-korea-requirements-and-process-2026) to confirm the exact route and its conditions before making that decision.
