Pick one side of Suncheon Bay National Garden (순천만국가정원) around the gate that suits the group. Start on the east if you are coming from Suncheon Station and want the lake or themed gardens. Start west for the west-side family attractions, subject to their separate notices. In a short visit, build in time to return to your gate.

## Choose an entrance around the first stop

| Priority | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Lake and gardens | East Gate (동문). Use the [station-to-East-Gate route](/blog/suncheon-station-to-national-garden-east-gate) and pick a loop on the garden map. |
| Family facilities | West Gate (서문), 국가정원1호길 47. Check which stops are open. |
| Tidal flats and reeds | Suncheon Bay Wetland entrance. Check travel time and last admission for this separate site. |

The [garden operator](https://scbay.suncheon.go.kr/garden/) lists a standard daytime adult ticket of ₩10,000 at this check. Special facilities, night sessions and transport have separate conditions. A pass to enter the garden does not tell you whether a pavilion, program or animal area is open. The operator’s map and notices should decide what you put on the route. Take a photo of the route board on arrival if you need to navigate offline.

## Make a garden loop before adding the bay

Choose a first landmark on the map, one or two gardens nearby, and a place to rest. Commit to a cross-garden leg only after checking how far your group has walked and where you need to leave. If you have a tight train, return to the same gate and keep the travel back to the station outside your sightseeing time budget.

For a longer day, the [SkyCube timetable and ticket site](https://www.skycube.co.kr/) lists services between Garden Station (정원역) and Suncheon Bay Station (순천만역). The wetland-side station is separate from the reed-bed trails. The operator lists a connecting reed train to Mujingyo (무진교) for SkyCube passengers, with its own timetable. Check both legs and the last return; when operations or your train window are uncertain, keep the [wetland trail](/blog/suncheon-bay-wetland-reed-fields) for another visit.

## Use the right Korean map name

Search **순천만국가정원 동문** or **순천만국가정원 서문** for the gate you actually want. Use **순천만습지** when heading to the coastal reserve. Add the gate and the train collection time to the [trip board](/tools/itinerary), then save optional stops separately.
