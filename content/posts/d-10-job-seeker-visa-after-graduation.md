## Put the school and immigration dates on one timeline

Check the expiry on your residence record, then ask the school for the date it can issue the required graduation or expected-graduation document. The [Ministry of Justice’s December 2025 update](https://www.moj.go.kr/bbs/moj/189/601114/artclView.do) announces changed stay and internship conditions for foreign graduates of Korean universities. The [Study in Korea residence overview](https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/ko/life/residenceAndStayInfo.do?tab=job-seeker-visa) still lists earlier limits in its D-10 explanation. Ask [1345](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/1862/subview.do) which limits apply and which proof the office will accept at the time of filing.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Dates | Save the current expiry, the date you expect to finish and the school’s document issuance date. |
| Proof | Ask whether expected-graduation evidence is acceptable if the final certificate will be issued later. |
| Route | Confirm whether the first D-2 to D-10 application has any document exceptions in your case. Ask about eligibility before relying on an exemption. |
| After | Confirm the rules that apply to any internship, first job, extension or overseas trip after filing. |

## Ask your school for the document you were told to provide

Contact the international office with the type of proof and the filing date confirmed by immigration. If the school cannot issue it before your permitted stay ends, ask immigration what lawful route is available. Keep the answer and the submission record where you can access them.

See the [general D-10-1 checklist](/blog/d-10-job-seeker-visa-korea-complete-guide-2026) for documenting the professional field and the activities you plan to pursue.
