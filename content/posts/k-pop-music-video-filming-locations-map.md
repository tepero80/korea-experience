## Choose the stop you can actually visit

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s filming-location guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=217571) connects Yongma Land with TWICE’s Like OOH-AHH and Everland Rocksville with a filmed BTS performance of Dynamite. Check that you have the exact venue and program you mean before setting out.

| Stop | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Yongma Land | The Seoul outdoor studio may be hired privately. Confirm visitor access, fees and photography rules for your day. |
| Everland | The filmed performance took place in Yongin. Match the park opening, ticket, zone access and ride operation to your own itinerary. |

If you found the location through a clip, use the [Korean video planning checklist](/blog/korean-youtube-culture-popular-creators-and-trends) to check the scene’s date and the operator’s current access rules.

## Save the correct Korean address

- Yongma Land · 서울특별시 중랑구 망우로70길 118 · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yongma+Land+Jungnang+Seoul)
- Everland · 경기도 용인시 처인구 포곡읍 에버랜드로 199 · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Everland+Yongin)

Before taking a dedicated trip, check the return route and save a fallback in the part of the city where you started. Ask before using tripods, costumes or larger equipment.
