If you want to try local wine, first decide whether you would enjoy a separate regional visit. A city wine list may include imported bottles. Ask the seller which wines are made in Korea and where the producer is based, then ask which styles are served by the glass.

## Choose the kind of stop

| Plan | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Yeongdong | Visit the wine tunnel to compare a local exhibition and shop. Confirm whether tasting is offered that day. |
| Muju | Look for meoru wild grape wine at the cave and ask which programs require a separate reservation. |
| City bar | Ask whether there is any Korean-made wine by the glass and what food is required with an order. |

[VISITKOREA’s Yeongdong Wine Tunnel listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192451) describes its wine culture and sales halls. For a separate regional visit, the [Muju Meoru Wine Cave guide](https://tour.muju.go.kr/cave/contents.do?key=367) describes a wild grape wine attraction in a former construction tunnel. Match the route to your existing base and check your return before the visit.

For a purchase, ask to see the producer and origin on the label, taste only when offered and check your own airline and arrival-country rules before buying a bottle to carry home. For a simpler evening meal, see the [Korean anju menu guide](/blog/korean-drinking-snacks-anju-pairing-guide).
