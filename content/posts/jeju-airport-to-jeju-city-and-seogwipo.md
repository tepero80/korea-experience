**Check your hotel’s Korean address before choosing a bus.** For **Jeju City**, match a local bus stop to the hotel. For the **Jungmun resort area**, check airport limousine **600 or 601**. For **Seogwipo**, the **600** continues from Jungmun, while **800 and 801** serve different stops farther south. If your flight arrives late or the walk with luggage looks difficult, compare the full trip with a taxi from the airport.

**For:** travelers arriving at Jeju International Airport (CJU) without a rental car. **Information checked:** 23 November 2026. Check your boarding point, departure and stop on the day.

## Match the bus to your hotel

| Your hotel is near… | What to look up before boarding |
| --- | --- |
| **Dongmun Market or the old town in Jeju City** | Check **316 toward Samyang** (삼양). Look for **Dongmun Rotary** (동문로터리) or **Gwandeokjeong** (관덕정) in the stop list, then compare the walk to your hotel. |
| **Jeju City Hall or Nohyeong** | Check **365 toward Jeju National University** for City Hall (제주시청). For **Nohyeong Post Office** (노형우체국), check **316 in the opposite direction, toward Hagwi** (하귀). |
| **A Jungmun resort or ICC Jeju** | Start with **600 or 601** and match the named stop to your hotel entrance. **601 terminates at the convention center** (제주국제컨벤션센터). Check whether your departure stops at the property or at a roadside entrance. |
| **Seogwipo harbor, Seobok Exhibition Hall or the KAL Hotel area** | Look at airport limousine **600** via Jungmun. Confirm the actual stop and the walk to your address. |
| **Seogwipo Bus Terminal or Seogwipo Registry Office** | Check **800 toward Seogwipo Bus Terminal** (서귀포버스터미널), or **801 toward the Registry Office transfer stop** (서귀포환승정류장·서귀포등기소). These are different destinations. |

The [Jeju bus network guide](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule) gives the route patterns. Use its directional timetables for [316 toward Samyang](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2229), [316 toward Hagwi](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2228), and the [airport-to-Seogwipo 800 and 801](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2352) to check the stop sequence. Ask your hotel for the Korean stop name if the booking address gives only a broad area.

## Find your boarding point after baggage claim

The [airport arrival guide](https://www.airport.co.kr/jejueng/cms/frCon/index.do?MENU_ID=210) directs passengers to the local bus area by **Gate 2**, the limousine stand to the **right of Gate 5**, and the taxi stand **across the crossing from Gate 3**. Follow the current signs and confirm the route number on the stop board. The airport’s [English bus guide](https://www.airport.co.kr/jejueng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=1&MENU_ID=90) identifies the **600** at Gate 5. Use the directions on the current board for other services.

1. **Find the arrival stop first.** Search your hotel’s Korean address in a map app. Check which side of the road you need and whether you can manage the walk with your bags.
2. **Check direction as well as the route number.** For an 800 or 801 to Seogwipo, look for Seogwipo Bus Terminal or the Registry Office stop as the destination. Jeju Bus Terminal is in the other direction. In the [official mobile bus timetable](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/listSchedule), look for your boarding stop followed by your hotel stop in the list.
3. **Check payment and luggage before lining up.** Use the [official fare and transfer guide](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/busfare) to check the current fare for your service. Ask staff about oversized bags, a stroller or mobility equipment if you need extra space. For a step-free trip, verify boarding access, curb cuts and the hotel’s accessible entrance.

## If your flight lands late

Compare the last suitable departure with **when you expect to reach the curb**, allowing time to collect your bags. The province lists a separate [late-night 800-1 trip to the Seogwipo Registry Office](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2357). Confirm it runs on your date and check whether that stop works for your hotel. If you need a taxi, use the airport stand and show your full Korean address. For a longer trip to the south coast, confirm the payment method and ask the driver for an estimate before setting off. If you need a specific vehicle, ask airport staff or your hotel to help arrange one.

If the last bus has left, the [official taxi page](https://www.airport.co.kr/jejueng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=2&MENU_ID=90) gives examples by destination and vehicle class, but the amount depends on your actual trip. If a delay means you may miss your hotel’s check-in window, contact the hotel from the terminal and confirm how you will get in. For the return, check the airport-bound stop separately and plan to arrive with time for airline check-in.
