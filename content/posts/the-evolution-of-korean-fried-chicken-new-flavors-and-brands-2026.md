## Choose the coating first

The [BBQ menu](https://mt.bbq.co.kr/menu/menuList.asp) groups its chicken by preparation and seasoning. These names can help you navigate a menu. Ask the actual branch to confirm the flavor and ingredients because the label alone does not tell you the heat level or sweetness.

| Label | What you are choosing |
| --- | --- |
| 후라이드 | Fried chicken in the plain category. Ask what the coating contains and whether a sauce comes with it. |
| 시즈닝 | A seasoning category. Ask how the flavor is applied and whether it is spicy. |
| 양념 | A sauced category. Ask about the sauce and whether it can be served separately. |
| 반반 | A half-and-half order. Verify the two flavors and whether you can mix the styles you want. |

## Make a mixed order easier to share

Start with one flavor the whole group can eat and a second choice for people who want a different coating. Ask for the order to be split or labelled if the branch offers that. Keep a receipt or screenshot with the selected options. When the menu only offers one sauce with the combination you want, a smaller separate order may be simpler.

For the size, cut and pickup or delivery details, use the [fried chicken ordering guide](/blog/how-to-order-korean-fried-chicken-portions-sauces-sides-and-delivery-terms).
