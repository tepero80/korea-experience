If your money is in a Korean bank account, start with two routes you can actually use. Ask your bank about overseas remittance, or 해외송금, and check whether a transfer provider supports your identity document, funding account and recipient route. Once both routes are available, compare how much the recipient would receive for the same total debit in won.

## Check who can move money from your account

The [Seoul government banking guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/living/convenience/1-banking/) lists examples of documents to have ready, including a passport, residence card, and records such as an employment contract or receipts. Ask your bank what it needs for the funds and purpose you describe. Get the recipient’s bank instructions directly, including the account holder’s name as recorded, the account number and any bank codes or address the route requires.

Your mobile banking access may need separate setup. [Hana Bank’s FAQ for foreign customers](https://kebhana.com/cont/customer/customer01/customer0102/customer010204/index%2C1%2Clist%2C2.jsp) describes advance branch registration for its online remittance service. If you are comparing an app, check the sender requirements first. [SentBe’s sign-up guide](https://help.sentbe.com/en/support/solutions/articles/67000748335--individual-customers-how-do-i-sign-up-) lists a valid Korean account in your own name and valid ID among its checks. Look up your exact destination and receiving method before spending time on verification.

## Put the two quotes side by side

| Quote field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Starting point | Same total won debit, with the sending fee included. |
| Payout | Same country, currency and payout method, such as deposit into the recipient’s bank account. |
| Recipient receives | Amount in the receiving currency and whether the provider describes it as fixed or estimated. |
| Other charges | Intermediary or recipient-bank deductions, and whether the provider can quote or cover them. |
| Timing | Quoted delivery window and when the rate expires. |

If you need the recipient to receive a specific amount, reverse the comparison. Ask both providers how much each will debit in won for that same payout. If a provider cannot tell you about downstream deductions, leave that cost open in your comparison. Ask its support team whether the amount shown is what will arrive.

If one of the quotes is from Wise, [check whether your account can fund it](/blog/sending-money-abroad-bank-transfer-vs-wise) before using it as a comparison.

## Review the details before you submit

1. Confirm the full account details and spelling directly with the recipient. Verify any last-minute change through a contact method you already trust.
2. Read the final screen or branch quotation. Note the debit, expected payout, and which charges may remain. Check what support can do if you entered a detail incorrectly.
3. Keep the receipt and transaction reference. If the transfer is delayed, check its status with the provider before submitting another.

If your account is new, use the [Korean bank branch checklist](/blog/opening-a-bank-account-in-korea-step-by-step-guide) to ask about transfer channels and restrictions before leaving the branch.
