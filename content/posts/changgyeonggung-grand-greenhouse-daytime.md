Kick off at Changgyeonggung’s main gate, Honghwamun (홍화문), and allow time to reach the Grand Greenhouse (대온실) within the palace grounds. Come in daylight if the greenhouse interior is your priority: the palace and greenhouse do not always share the same evening access.

## Plan the greenhouse first

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Greenhouse interior | Start by checking the palace schedule and walk directly to 대온실 in daylight. |
| A short visit | Pair the greenhouse with Chundangji and leave enough time to walk back to Honghwamun. |
| Palace grounds after dark | See which areas remain open and check the separate greenhouse winter rule. |

The [palace authority’s current schedule](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do) lists ordinary palace opening from 09:00 to 21:00 with last entry at 20:00, normally closed Mondays, subject to its holiday rule and notices. Its table separately restricts the greenhouse interior after 18:00 in December, January and February; do not assume a late palace ticket guarantees indoor access. Check the authority immediately before visiting.

## A simple daytime route

From Honghwamun, use the palace map to orient yourself around the main hall, then continue towards Chundangji (춘당지) and the greenhouse. Allow for the return walk; the greenhouse is a destination within the palace, not the entrance itself. [KTO](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=226136) identifies it as a historic greenhouse and explains that palace entry is required.

Check [official fees](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R703000000.do?schGroupCode=cgg) and current notices rather than assuming a free admission category applies. Search **창경궁 홍화문** for the main gate or **창경궁 대온실** for the building; the main palace address is **서울특별시 종로구 창경궁로 185**. If you plan to continue to Changdeokgung, check its separate admission and closing time before using the connecting gate. Our [ordinary evening guide](/blog/changgyeonggung-ordinary-evening-visit) covers the later visit separately. Add the entrance and your return time to the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
