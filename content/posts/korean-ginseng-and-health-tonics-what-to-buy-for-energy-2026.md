## Identify the category on the package

The [Korean Ministry of Food and Drug Safety’s labeling guide](https://www.mfds.go.kr/mfds/eng/html/sub01/MFDS-DE01-10-05-L0001.jsp) describes the health functional food mark and the information on that category’s label. Look for **건강기능식품** on the package. Check a drink or food label on its own terms if it uses a ginseng illustration.

| Label detail | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| 원재료명 | The exact ingredient list. Compare the pack you are buying even when the box designs look similar. |
| 주의사항 | Cautions for this product. Ask what the warning says before buying. |
| 보관방법 | Storage instructions, including anything that would matter during travel or after opening. |
| Date | Check the date printed on your specific pack and follow the storage instructions. |

## Compare claims with the information on the back

Read the product’s stated function in context. A translated product name or an image on the front gives you little detail for a purchase decision. If you take medicine, the [NIH guidance on Asian ginseng](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/asian-ginseng) recommends asking a health professional about possible interactions. Keep the packaging or a picture of the full label if you want to ask later. Use the [Korean packaged food label guide](/blog/korean-packaged-food-allergy-labels) to check additional ingredient and allergy terms.
