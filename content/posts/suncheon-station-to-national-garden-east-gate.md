**From Suncheon Station, cross to the stop opposite the railway station and take city bus 66 to 국가정원(동문), the National Garden East Gate stop.** The [garden operator’s directions](https://scbay.suncheon.go.kr/garden/0016/0007/) estimate about 21 minutes. Check the next departure before crossing, and leave extra time for the wait, the walk to the gate and your return train.

## Save the right stop and gate

| Save this | Look for |
| --- | --- |
| Suncheon Station | **순천역**, 순천시 팔마로 135. The garden-bound stop is across from the railway station. |
| Bus stop | **국가정원(동문)**. Confirm that the stop list for your 66 includes this stop after **순천역**. |
| Garden entrance | **순천만국가정원 동문**. For a taxi, use the East Gate parking address **순천시 국가정원1호길 162-11**. |

There is a separate [East Gate map link in the garden’s directions](https://scbay.suncheon.go.kr/garden/0016/0007/). A search for only **순천만국가정원** can lead to the garden’s general address at the West Gate. For the [Suncheon Bay Wetland visitor area](https://www.suncheon.go.kr/tour/tourist/0002/), use **순천만습지**. If you want both places, look up that separate leg before setting out.

## Board with the stop sequence in view

1. **After leaving the train**, open route 66 in the [city bus information system](https://its.sc.go.kr/) or in your usual map app. Pick the direction that puts **국가정원(동문)** after your departure stop. Start with the stop opposite the railway station and match the stop name on the pole to your app.
2. **If you are unsure**, ask the driver **국가정원 동문에 가나요?** (“Does this go to the National Garden East Gate?”). Keep the destination visible on your phone and follow the displays or announcements.
3. **Get off at 국가정원(동문)**, then follow the East Gate signs and use marked crossings. Save that arrival stop in your map before you go inside. Look up the station-bound boarding stop separately for the way back.

## Set your turnaround time before you enter

The [garden’s visitor page](https://scbay.suncheon.go.kr/garden/0016/0001/0001/) lists a **19:00 last entry** and a regular closure on the **last Monday of each month**. Check [visitor notices](https://scbay.suncheon.go.kr/garden/0019/0001/) for date-specific changes. If you have a train to catch, decide when to exit the garden using the actual station-bound trip, then reserve time to walk through the station and find your platform. Use one garden area near the East Gate when you have a short visit.

If you want to visit the Wetland on the same day, compare the Wetland’s separate opening times with your return. [SkyCube](https://www.skycube.co.kr/) also has its own schedule. Check its last useful departure in each direction before adding it to an afternoon visit.

## Keep a plan for a late bus or a weather change

If the next bus leaves too little garden time, compare a taxi to **순천만국가정원 동문**. For the return, ask the East Gate information desk **순천역 가는 버스는 어디서 타요?** (“Where do I catch the bus to Suncheon Station?”). If you need a low-floor bus or a step-free approach, ask the station desk and the garden about each part of the journey. The [Korea Tourism Organization’s accessibility notes](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/ms/detail.do?cotId=61fbd20f-7bb2-4b94-8483-db1e51b88e9c) cover the site itself.

If the garden no longer fits before your train, stay near the railway station. The [local Yeokjeon Market](https://korean.visitkorea.or.kr/detail/ms_detail.do?cotid=ae1edfad-79ba-4577-aa95-f3afcfe99ee6) is a possible short visit when stalls are open. For a rail day with several stops, save the gate, ride back and departure buffer in your [itinerary](/tools/itinerary).
