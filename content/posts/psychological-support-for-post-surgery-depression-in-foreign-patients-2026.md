## Choose who to call

If you need immediate medical help in Korea, call **119**. [Visit Seoul’s emergency guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/medical-emergencies) says you can ask for an ambulance in English. Give your current address or a nearby landmark. For more on making the call, see the [emergency numbers and interpretation guide](/blog/medical-emergencies-1330-vs-119-2026).

If you are distressed after a procedure and want support, tell the treating clinic what you have noticed and when it started. Ask to speak with a clinician who can review your concerns and tell you how to arrange further help. The [Medical Korea Information Center](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient) can help you find relevant institutions or ask about booking. For another nonemergency starting point, see [how to ask for mental health support in Seoul](/blog/dealing-with-loneliness-mental-health-resources).

| When | What to ask or save |
| --- | --- |
| At clinic | Ask who will answer a concern about how you are feeling and whether staff can help arrange a mental health appointment. |
| At night | Save the after-hours number or written instructions for where to seek help if the clinic is closed. |
| At home | Agree on a contact for follow-up questions and check which records your usual clinician will need. |

## Ask about language and availability

You can say, “I had a procedure on [date] and I’d like to speak to a clinician about how I’m feeling. Is there an appointment in [language], or can you arrange an interpreter?” Ask who would interpret, whether they would join the whole consultation, and what the appointment and interpretation would cost. If the clinic cannot see you before you leave, ask which service you can contact next and whether your usual provider can take over. You can ask a person you trust to help with a call or accompany you if you want.

## Set up the handoff before traveling

Ask your team what written summary, medication list and follow-up instructions it can provide. [Seoul National University Hospital’s records page](https://www.snuh.org/global/en/patients/EN02005.do), for example, lists discharge summaries and a separate clinician request step for its mental health records. Ask your own provider about timing, language and how to collect the documents. Confirm what your next clinician needs and the secure way to send it before sharing health details.
