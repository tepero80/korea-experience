## Identify who is offering each service

Check Medical Korea’s [registered hospital directory](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredhospitals) and [registered facilitator directory](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredfacilitators) independently. Match the legal name and contact details. Ask the hospital’s international desk what it includes before paying someone else for the same work.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Get a written list of bookings, interpretation, transport and follow-up tasks. Ask which hours and visits are excluded. |
| Fees | Ask who bills you, whether the coordinator is also paid by the clinic and what it costs to reschedule or add a visit. |
| Care | Address clinical questions and consent directly to the treating team, using interpretation if needed. Ask who handles questions after the coordinator leaves. |
| Docs | Keep the clinic’s address and direct number, the coordinator’s agreement and a copy of each provider’s estimate. |

## Compare the two offers using the same needs

Ask both parties who can help during an evening call, a changed appointment or a request for records. Medical Korea’s [written information guidance](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/notice/faqView.do?faqSn=48) lists expected costs and important treatment information to discuss with the institution. If you can get the booking and communication help you need directly, use that offer as the comparison.

See the [Medical Korea registration guide](/blog/choosing-a-registered-medical-institution-in-korea-official-verification-steps-f) for matching the exact legal clinic name to the right government register.
