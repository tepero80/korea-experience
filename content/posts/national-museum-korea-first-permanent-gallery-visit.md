The National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) rewards a focused visit. If you have limited time, choose a few permanent galleries and leave special exhibitions or the Children’s Museum for a separate plan. The museum is reached from Ichon Station (이촌역), not Seoul Station.

## Plan around your time

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| First visit | Go to the Path to History and pick one gallery before adding more. |
| Family booking | Check the separate Children’s Museum session first. |
| Rainy day | Keep the permanent galleries as the main stop and leave garden time flexible. |

The [museum’s visitor information](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0101000000.do) lists the permanent galleries as free and gives separate arrangements for special exhibitions and the Children’s Museum. Check the actual day’s hours and closures: the museum lists later hours on Wednesdays and Saturdays, and selected permanent-gallery closure days. The [children’s reservation guide](/blog/national-museum-korea-childrens-museum-reservation) covers the family session separately.

## From Ichon to the galleries

The [museum’s subway directions](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0105010000.do) identify Ichon Station on Line 4 and the Gyeongui–Jungang Line. Follow the museum signs from the station and leave time for the walk into the building. Ask at the information desk for the current floor map, facilities and any accessibility support.

For a first-floor route, find the ten-story Gyeongcheonsa pagoda on the [Path to History](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0201100000.do?showHallId=406012&showroomCode=DM0065), then choose the Prehistory and Ancient History galleries if you want one clear theme. Take a break before deciding whether to go upstairs. The [KTO listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=83891) notes the outdoor stone artworks as a different part of the visit. Search **국립중앙박물관**, **서울특별시 용산구 서빙고로 137**, and save the visit and return station on the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
