**Choose Hahoe Village for lanes and traditional homes in a lived-in village, or Dosan Seowon for the buildings and grounds of a Confucian academy.** They are in different parts of Andong. If traveling by bus, pick one and find a confirmed return before leaving town.

## Choose one main destination

| Place | What makes it a better fit |
| --- | --- |
| **Hahoe** | Choose the [traditional village](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=111087) if you want time to walk the lanes and see the village landscape. Check changes in entry and the transfer from the area where your bus drops you. |
| **Dosan** | Choose the [Confucian academy](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=111174) if you want to focus on its courtyards and the story of scholar Yi Hwang. Confirm the walk between the bus stop and the academy. |

For navigation, save **안동 하회마을, 안동시 풍천면 전서로 186-8** or **도산서원, 안동시 도산면 도산서원길 154**. Check the entrance and ticket point separately from the attraction pin. Opening hours change seasonally, and the return connection may require leaving before closing.

## Find the bus by direction and destination

The [Andong city tourism guide](https://new.tourandong.com/public/sub5/sub1_2.cshtml) lists **210 or 급행2** for Hahoe and **512 or 급행3** for Dosan. From the current rail and coach hub it lists Hahoe options on 210 and 급행2, and **급행3** for Dosan. If you board downtown near **교보생명** (Kyobo Life), the published conventional services use different sides. The city directs Hahoe-bound 210 passengers to the stop across from Kyobo Life and Dosan-bound 512 passengers to the stop in front.

Open the [city route system](https://bus.andong.go.kr/route) and choose the actual departure that lists **하회마을** or **도산서원**. Variants can serve different endpoints. Use the [academy’s directions](https://www.andong.go.kr/dosanseowon/contents.do?mId=0504000000) and [city timetable notices](https://www.andong.go.kr/portal/bbs/list.do?mId=0606010100&ptIdx=66) as cross-checks. Save the Korean destination and the next few stops before boarding.

## Work backward from the trip back

1. Find the stop and direction for the return to **your actual next destination**. Downtown and the rail–coach hub are different drop-offs.
2. Allow time to walk to the bus and leave before the last usable departure. If a particular exhibition or performance matters, verify its own date and time separately.
3. If there is no workable return, arrange a taxi in advance or give the day to the Old Market and the [Wolyeonggyo riverside walk](/blog/andong-wolyeonggyo-evening-walk). See the [Andong arrival guide](/blog/andong-station-and-bus-terminal-to-downtown) before planning a train or coach connection.
