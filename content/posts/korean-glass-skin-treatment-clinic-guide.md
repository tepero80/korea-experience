## Check the branch before paying

Ask for the clinic’s registered name, branch address and registration number. Match them against [Medical Korea’s directory of registered hospitals and clinics](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredhospitals). The directory lets you narrow by region and facility type. [Medical Korea explains](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/reliable) that foreign-patient registration and accreditation are separate. Confirm which services the branch offers and who will see you directly with the clinic. If you cannot match the listing to the branch, ask the [Medical Korea Information Center](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient) for help before sending money or records.

## Turn the package name into a list

“Glass skin” can be used to advertise different services. Ask whether each line on your quote is a product applied to the skin, a procedure performed with a device, or an injection. Get the names in writing so the clinician can explain what is being proposed and why. Use these questions to compare offers.

| Ask | What to get in writing |
| --- | --- |
| Items | The name of each service, any product or device involved, and which services you can leave out. |
| Staff | The names and roles of the people who will consult with you and carry out each procedure. |
| Price | The price for each item, tax, any separate fee and the rules for deposits or changes. |
| Care | Who can explain the consent form, which follow-up visits are needed and how to contact the clinic after the visit. |

## Decide after speaking to the clinician

Ask the clinician how the proposed service fits your skin concerns, history, medications and travel dates. Request an explanation of risks, alternatives and the option to leave the appointment after the consultation. Ask who can interpret during both the discussion and consent. If the clinic changes the proposed items on the day, ask for an updated quote and time to consider it. Use the [guide to confirming a clinic appointment](/blog/booking-medical-consultations-in-korea-online-vs-walk-in) to check whether your booking is for a consultation, a procedure or both.
