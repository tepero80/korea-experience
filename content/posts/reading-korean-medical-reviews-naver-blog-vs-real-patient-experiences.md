## Keep the source and branch attached to the story

Look for words such as **광고** for advertising and **협찬** for sponsorship. [A Korean Fair Trade Commission case](https://case.ftc.go.kr/com/dscv/dscvHtmlView.do?docCnvrMnNo=14914&docId=20251203165009524429) provides a general example of why disclosures matter with paid reviews. Check the publication date, exact branch and author’s stated relationship before using the post to form questions.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Does the author describe how they heard about the provider and who paid for the visit? |
| Branch | Match the Korean name, street address and department with the clinic’s own information. |
| Claim | Ask what part of the story the clinic can confirm and whether it applies to the proposed visit. |
| Care | Request the name of the clinician and details on how you will reach follow-up support. |

## Verify the booking independently

Use [Medical Korea’s foreign-patient registration information](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem) to understand the provider registration system. Confirm the clinic name and contact directly. Use the dates and arrangements the provider confirms for your trip.
