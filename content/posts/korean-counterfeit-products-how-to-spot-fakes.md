If a branded item matters to you, check **who is selling it and whether the brand identifies that seller as an official or authorized outlet** before paying. A label, sticker, barcode or neat stitching alone cannot establish authenticity. Korea Customs notes that some counterfeits closely resemble genuine products and can be difficult to distinguish by appearance. [Korea Customs Service](https://www.customs.go.kr/kcs/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?nttSn=10147925&nttSnUrl=f5ed25a22b2d1a48a99964d40a9a1a4f)

## At the shop, before paying

| Check | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Seller and exact outlet | The brand may identify authorized sales channels. |
| Price and product variant | An unusually low offer merits further questions. |
| Receipt and conditions | These help document a later question or dispute. |

1. **Identify the seller.** Note the shop name and location. Check the brand's own website for a store locator or authorized-seller list, or contact the brand using the contact information on its official site. For example, [COSRX lists its official and authorized sales channels](https://www.cosrx.com/pages/stores); this applies to COSRX products, not every brand.
2. **Compare the offer.** Check the same product and variant on the brand's site. An unusually low price is a reason to pause and ask questions, not proof that an item is fake. Customs recommends checking the seller and price. [Korea Customs Service](https://www.customs.go.kr/kcs/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?nttSn=10147925&nttSnUrl=f5ed25a22b2d1a48a99964d40a9a1a4f)
3. **Ask about the transaction.** Confirm the total price, any return conditions and what receipt will identify the purchase. If the seller cannot answer or you are uncomfortable, take time to check elsewhere.
4. **Keep evidence.** Save the receipt and note the shop, date and product. If a concern arises, keep the packaging and any product or transaction details that the brand or consumer adviser asks for.

A seller absent from a brand's list is not automatically selling counterfeits. Authorization and product authenticity are different questions; ask the brand if you need a determination.

## If you are concerned after purchase

Contact the seller and the brand with your receipt, item details and your concern. Ask the brand what evidence it needs and whether it can assess the item. Do not present your suspicion as a confirmed finding without an assessment.

For a consumer dispute, the [Korea Consumer Agency](https://www.kca.go.kr/odr/pg/ma/cnsutInfo.do) describes consultation through **1372**; it may lead to a separate remedy process if the issue remains unresolved. Language availability and the outcome are not guaranteed. Korea Customs lists **125** for suspected counterfeit sales or distribution. Reporting is separate from requesting a refund. [Korea Customs Service](https://customs.go.kr/kcs/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?bbsId=1362&mi=2891&nttSn=10179605&nttSnUrl=0f8a9958a2a2a2905f59d9887bb2906f)
