## Choose your first session

| What you want | What to confirm before paying |
| --- | --- |
| Ski lesson | A first-timer lesson, available beginner terrain, and whether ski boots, skis and lift access are part of the booking. |
| Board lesson | A snowboard lesson in a language you understand, a suitable board and boots, and any age or size rules. |
| Stay off the slopes | A separate indoor or sightseeing plan and a meeting point. Check lift and cable car tickets independently. |

Start with the [operator’s ski school](https://www.yongpyong.co.kr/eng/skiNboard/ypSkiSchool.do) and the [live slope and lift status](https://www.yongpyong.co.kr/eng/skiNboard/openStatusBoard.do). If the lesson or suitable terrain is unavailable, change the date or compare the other resorts before committing to nonrefundable transport.

## Buy the parts that your session needs

1. **Lesson.** Get the meeting time, location and language confirmed. Ask what happens if the resort closes the terrain used by your class.
2. **Rental.** Check the [operator’s equipment list](https://www.yongpyong.co.kr/eng/skiNboard/utilizationFee/rental.do). Ask separately about helmets and clothing, and leave time for someone to fit the boots and equipment before your lesson.
3. **Lift access.** Ask the school whether your session needs a lift ticket, where it is accepted and what period it covers. Compare this with any package you are considering.
4. **Return trip.** Check the exact pickup, final departure and alternative before leaving your hotel. The [train and shuttle guide](/blog/ski-resort-train-shuttle) explains how to plan the last leg.

## Find the right building at YongPyong

Pin **모나용평 스키장, 강원특별자치도 평창군 대관령면 올림픽로 715** before leaving. The resort’s [ski house directory](https://www.yongpyong.co.kr/eng/skiNboard/house.do) lists ticketing and rental locations at Dragon Plaza. Confirm the school’s meeting point in your booking message, because a resort map pin only identifies the general arrival area. If your group will split, agree on a named indoor spot and time to meet again.

## If the resort day falls through

Check the operator’s closure information and refund or rescheduling terms first. A weather disruption can also affect departure roads or a sightseeing lift. Pick an indoor plan closer to where you are staying or reschedule the lesson for a day with reliable transport.

If you came here for **ice climbing**, the [UIAA currently lists the Cheongsong World Cup for January 15–17, 2027](https://www.theuiaa.org/event/2027-uiaa-ice-climbing-world-cup-cheongsong-south-korea/). This is a separate spectator trip in Cheongsong. Check the event organizer for public access and the final venue before making travel plans.
