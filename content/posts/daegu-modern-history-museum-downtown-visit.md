**Quick answer:** Daegu Modern History Museum is a free indoor stop for travelers interested in the city’s modern history. The operator lists 09:00–18:00 with entry completed by 17:30. Save **대구광역시 중구 경상감영길 67** and check the current closure notices before visiting.

## Pick a day and get there

The [Daegu Foundation for Culture and Arts visitor page](https://www.dgfca.or.kr/page/126) says the museum closes on Mondays, January 1, and the days of Seollal and Chuseok; when Monday is a public holiday, the following day is closed. Safety work and other special closures can also affect a visit. General admission is free. A separate national museum-directory page lists an older seasonal closing time, so use the operator's current year-round hours and check before any time-sensitive trip.

The foundation’s [directions](https://dgfca.or.kr/map_list?html_cd=11) identify **Jungangno Station, Line 1, Exit 4**, as a public transport starting point. They say the museum has no parking lot. From your accommodation, compare a current map route with a taxi and plan the return before going. The foundation lists **053-430-7910** for the museum; ask about accessibility and language assistance if those determine your visit.

## What to see

The operator says the building was constructed in 1932 for a bank and reopened as a city museum in 2011. Its first-floor guide lists a history timeline, exhibitions about modern culture and education, and a reconstructed bus experience; a special-exhibition space is on the second floor. Start at the information desk and ask which rooms are open today. Exhibits and any hands-on activity can change; do not assume a specific display or translation is available on your date.

## Build the next step into your day

Use the museum as an indoor first stop, then check a current walking route to your next central Daegu destination or return to Jungangno Station. If you want to combine it with a market meal, first check the individual market’s opening hours and confirm the journey in your map app; do not assume a market stall is open just because the museum is. If weather or mobility needs make the onward walk difficult, compare a short transit or taxi connection instead.
