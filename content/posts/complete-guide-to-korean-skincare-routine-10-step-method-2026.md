The ten-step list groups many products you may see on store shelves. Start with the categories you actually use and the number of containers you want to carry. A set can include two products you would use for the same goal or sizes you cannot comfortably take home.

## Read the product categories at the shelf

| Group | What you might find |
| --- | --- |
| Cleanse | Oil or balm cleansers, water-based cleansers and optional exfoliating products. |
| Apply | Toner, essence, serum, ampoule or sheet mask. Product directions tell you how it is meant to be used. |
| Finish | Eye cream, moisturizer and sun protection. Pick a format you recognize and check its label. |

Check **전성분** for ingredients, **사용기한** for the use-by label and **사용상의 주의사항** for precautions. If you avoid an ingredient, check the actual box or manufacturer’s listing for that product version. Ask for an unopened box when the displayed bottle is a tester. The [FDA’s general cosmetics guidance](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/resources-consumers-cosmetics/using-cosmetics-safely) also advises reading the warnings and following product directions.

## Do the bag check before paying

For an international departure from Incheon, the [airport’s published liquids rules](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1432/subview.do) require containers of up to 100 mL inside a single clear resealable bag of up to 1 L per traveler. The [airport’s examples](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1434/subview.do) include toner, lotion and sunscreen. The size printed on the container matters even when it is partly empty. A 150 mL bottle with 30 mL left still has a 150 mL capacity.

If the full-size set will not fit the liquids bag, choose smaller packages or verify the product and packing rules for your checked bag. Aerosols can have separate conditions. Incheon’s page describes a sealed duty-free liquid bag exception. Check handling at any transfer airport before purchasing. When shopping, compare exact products and travel sizes with the [Olive Young shopping guide](/blog/olive-young-shopping-guide-must-buy-k-beauty-products).
