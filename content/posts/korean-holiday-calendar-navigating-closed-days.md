Start with the national calendar, then check each place you need that day. A palace can open on its usual day off because it is a holiday, while a museum can close on the holiday itself. Also check the day after the holiday before rebuilding the itinerary.

## Mark the dates that affect your trip

The [Korea AeroSpace Administration’s published 2027 calendar](https://www.kasa.go.kr/prog/plcyBrf/brief/kor/sub01_01_04/view.do?plcyBrfNo=431) lists these periods. It uses a five-day workweek for its long-weekend examples. The dates are for **2027**, and operators publish their own hours and booking notices.

| Period | Dates and what to check |
| --- | --- |
| **New Year** | January 1 is the holiday. January 2 and 3 are the weekend. Check January 1 attraction closures and restaurant hours separately. |
| **Seollal** | February 6 to 8, with the substitute holiday on Tuesday, February 9. Lunar New Year’s Day is February 7. Check both the outbound and return train dates. |
| **Chuseok** | September 14 to 16. The main day is September 15. Check individual facilities for the whole period and the first day after it. |

## Check each attraction’s holiday rule

- **Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung.** The [Royal Palaces and Tombs Center timetable](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do) lists Tuesday as the regular closing day at Gyeongbokgung and Monday at Changdeokgung. When that day falls on a public or substitute holiday, the operator lists an opening on the holiday and a closure on the first non-holiday afterward. Check dated notices for your visit and any separate garden or event ticket.
- **National Museum of Korea.** Its [visitor information](https://mvcm.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0101010000.do) lists closures on January 1, Lunar New Year’s Day and Chuseok. Check the current year’s notice and any separate schedule for children’s or special exhibitions.

## Make one backup for transport, meals and sightseeing

1. **Train.** Look for the [Korail holiday notices](https://m.letskorail.com/mbt/m_notice.html) for your particular travel dates. Check both directions and any booking window or ticket release instructions before committing to a connection.
2. **Meals.** Confirm the exact branch you plan to visit. Save a nearby alternative and check when it serves food. A chain’s general hours may differ from the branch’s holiday hours.
3. **Activities.** Keep one nearby option with verified hours. Save addresses and reservation details offline. If a palace closes, move it to a confirmed day rather than assuming the next day will work.
