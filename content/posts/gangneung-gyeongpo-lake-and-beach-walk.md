**Start at Gyeongpo Beach if you want a short seaside walk, or start at Gyeongpodae Pavilion if the lake is the main reason for your trip.** For a first visit without a car, choose one end as your starting point and save your ride back before you walk away from it.

| What you have time for | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| About an hour at Gyeongpo | Start at **경포해수욕장**. Walk one stretch of the shore and turn around at a point you can easily retrace. |
| A longer walk and a break | Start at **경포대**, look over the lake, then follow your map along a lakeside section. Add the beach only if you have enough time to get back. |
| Strong wind, warnings or poor footing | Skip exposed paths and keep to open, signed areas. If conditions worsen, switch to a confirmed indoor stop in town. |

These are planning allowances for time at Gyeongpo. Add the ride from Gangneung, waiting at bus stops and the return trip separately.

## Save the right drop-off

- **Gyeongpo Beach 경포해수욕장** is at **강원특별자치도 강릉시 창해로 514**. KTO lists a 1.8 km sandy shore between the lake and sea. You can choose a small section without walking its full length. Check current swimming rules and lifeguard coverage independently of shoreline access. [KTO beach information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=111055)
- **Gyeongpodae Pavilion 강릉 경포대** is at **강원특별자치도 강릉시 경포로 365**. KTO places it on a small hill overlooking the lake. Save the pavilion separately from **경포호**, the lake itself. If slopes or surfaces matter to you, ask the tourism desk about the approach from your drop-off. [KTO pavilion information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=110841)

If the lake is your priority, plan a lakeside out-and-back from a checked access point near your drop-off. Leave enough time to return the same way if a crossing, slope or surface makes the longer route impractical. KTO lists the lake as a protected area where fishing is prohibited. The [Gangneung Baugil association’s 16 km Section 5](https://www.baugil.org/html/course/5course.html) covers a much longer coastal route. [KTO lake information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=111060)

## Check the way back to Gangneung Station

The [city bus timetable](https://bis.gn.go.kr/schedule) lists **934-1** from **KTX공영주차장환승장** to the beach terminus, and **934** in the other direction. Use these numbers as options to look up for your travel day. Confirm the actual boarding point and next departure in the [live bus search](https://bis.gn.go.kr/search). If the timing does not fit your train, arrange a taxi or shorten the walk.

Look for local notices before heading out. [KTO’s park listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=91009) warns that access may change at short notice in bad weather. For a change of plan, use the [Ojukheon and Jungang Market half-day](/blog/gangneung-ojukheon-and-jungang-market) and check indoor exhibition hours first. If the priority is coffee by the water, compare [the station-to-Anmok plan](/blog/gangneung-station-anmok) before trying to fit both beaches around one train.
