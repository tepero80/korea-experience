## Choose one outing from your base

| Start from | What to see and check |
| --- | --- |
| **Sokcho** | Walk the Seoraksan valley for rock walls and the Biseondae bridge. Check the trail and your last ride back. |
| **Jeongeup** | Look for maples and pond reflections near Uhwajeong. Check traffic and any seasonal shuttle. |
| **Seoul** | Choose a short Seoul Forest walk. Check which trees are showing color and whether the path is open. |

## From Sokcho, walk the Seoraksan valley

**설악산소공원 · 강원특별자치도 속초시 설악산로 1055**

[Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%84%A4%EC%95%85%EC%82%B0%EC%86%8C%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%20%EC%86%8D%EC%B4%88%EC%8B%9C%20%EC%84%A4%EC%95%85%EC%82%B0%EB%A1%9C%201055) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%84%A4%EC%95%85%EC%82%B0%EC%86%8C%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%20%EC%86%8D%EC%B4%88%EC%8B%9C%20%EC%84%A4%EC%95%85%EC%82%B0%EB%A1%9C%201055) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%84%A4%EC%95%85%EC%82%B0%EC%86%8C%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%20%EC%86%8D%EC%B4%88%EC%8B%9C%20%EC%84%A4%EC%95%85%EC%82%B0%EB%A1%9C%201055)

Use [Sogongwon Park](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=204732) as the starting landmark. Follow the signs through Sinheungsa toward Biseondae. The [park service's route notes](https://www.knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/visitCourseGuide.do?parkId=120400&vcursId=120400V006) give about **3 km and 1 hour 20 minutes one way** for the valley section. Allow additional time for both directions, photos and rests. You may meet sloping stone steps and uneven ground, even on this lower route. Make Biseondae your turn-back point. For a shorter day, turn around near Sinheungsa instead of taking the stair climb to Geumganggul.

Check the [park map](https://www.knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/parkMap.do?parkId=120400) and [current access notices](https://seorak.knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/visitCourseMain.do?menuNo=7020002&parkId=120400) before you leave Sokcho. If you prefer the cable car, the [operator](https://www.sorakcablecar.co.kr/guide) describes same-day tickets at the site and weather-dependent operation. Check the status before making it the only reason for your trip. A [market and waterfront walk in Sokcho](/blog/sokcho-market-gaetbae-abai-village-walk) is a separate backup if the mountain day falls through.

## From Jeongeup, walk toward Uhwajeong

**내장탐방지원센터 · 전북특별자치도 정읍시 내장산로 938**

[Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%82%B4%EC%9E%A5%ED%83%90%EB%B0%A9%EC%A7%80%EC%9B%90%EC%84%BC%ED%84%B0%20%EC%A0%95%EC%9D%8D%EC%8B%9C%20%EB%82%B4%EC%9E%A5%EC%82%B0%EB%A1%9C%20938) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EB%82%B4%EC%9E%A5%ED%83%90%EB%B0%A9%EC%A7%80%EC%9B%90%EC%84%BC%ED%84%B0%20%EC%A0%95%EC%9D%8D%EC%8B%9C%20%EB%82%B4%EC%9E%A5%EC%82%B0%EB%A1%9C%20938) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EB%82%B4%EC%9E%A5%ED%83%90%EB%B0%A9%EC%A7%80%EC%9B%90%EC%84%BC%ED%84%B0%20%EC%A0%95%EC%9D%8D%EC%8B%9C%20%EB%82%B4%EC%9E%A5%EC%82%B0%EB%A1%9C%20938)

Start on the **Jeongeup side** for Uhwajeong. The [park's autumn route notes](https://www.knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/visitVnewsDtl.do?vnewsId=VNEWSM001176) place the pavilion on the lower approach between the entrance and visitor information center. For a pond reflection, pause at the public approach path and keep space for people passing. Check [current park notices](https://knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/visitCourseMain.do?menuNo=7020101&parkId=120600) before relying on a shuttle or committing to a longer trail. Confirm your return bus or taxi pickup before going farther in. The [Baekyangsa and Naejangsan comparison](/blog/korea-s-best-fall-foliage-spots-without-the-crowds) shows where the other entrance fits.

## Stay in Seoul for a shorter walk

**서울숲 · 서울 성동구 뚝섬로 273**

[Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%88%B2%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%84%B1%EB%8F%99%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%9A%9D%EC%84%AC%EB%A1%9C%20273) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%88%B2%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%84%B1%EB%8F%99%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%9A%9D%EC%84%AC%EB%A1%9C%20273) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%88%B2%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%84%B1%EB%8F%99%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%9A%9D%EC%84%AC%EB%A1%9C%20273)

Pick one part of the park on the [city's English map](https://parks.seoul.go.kr/maps/dreamforest/dreamforest_map_ENG_20230525.pdf) and give yourself time to slow down if you find color. Check the [park's latest visitor notices](https://parks.seoul.go.kr/park/detailView.do?pIdx=5) and use the [short Seoul Forest station walk](/blog/seoul-forest-station-short-park-walk) to choose an approach. If you prefer Changdeokgung's Secret Garden, check the [garden ticket guide](/blog/changdeokgung-secret-garden-tickets) for the timed entry and palace ticket before you build your day around it.

## Check the color and the return before you leave

1. Look for a dated observation for the mountain at the [weather agency's foliage page](https://www.weather.go.kr/w/forecast/life/seasonal-observation/autumn-leaves.do). Check the actual site and date shown before extrapolating to another valley or city.
2. Treat older forecasts as records of earlier seasons. [VisitKorea's 2026 forecast](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=1593230) was for that season, and future dates can shift.
3. On the morning of a mountain visit, confirm the lower route is open and pin the place where you will catch your return ride. If rain, wind or ice makes the walk unsuitable, switch to the shorter plan.
