Choose by the first thing you need your line to do. Loading Naver Map is a data task. Calling a guesthouse is a voice task. Receiving a one-time text and verifying your legal identity with a Korean carrier are separate requirements.

## Match the service to the task

| Your job in Korea | Ask the seller this before you pay |
| --- | --- |
| **Data** | Can my exact unlocked phone use this plan for maps, translation and messaging throughout my stay? |
| **SMS code** | Is there a Korean number? When do incoming texts start working, and will the reservation app accept this number? |
| **Calls and replies** | Are outgoing local calls and texts enabled? Does the package include credit, or must I top up? |
| **Identity check** | Does this resident plan support the service’s carrier identity check? Which documents and name spelling does it require? |

[LG U+’s visitor product page](https://www.lguplus.com/korea-sim/eng/pc/product/esim?tab=data) distinguishes ordinary app verification texts on its voice and SMS option from carrier identity checks for services such as banking or government. It says its visitor product does not provide the latter. [KT’s visitor eSIM terms](https://m.roaming.kt.com/eng/esim) describe incoming calls and SMS after entry verification on its data product, with outgoing service on a separate product. Check the precise option and the app you need. “Data only” does not tell you whether incoming texts will work.

## Set up a visitor line without losing your fallback

1. **Check the phone.** Ask your home carrier whether it is unlocked. For eSIM, check your model and its country of sale against the product restrictions. An EID indicates eSIM support and the carrier still needs to accept the specific device.
2. **Check installation and when usage starts.** Save the carrier’s directions offline and use a stable connection for setup. Confirm any passport check or requirement to visit a counter. The [LG U+ installation guide](https://www.lguplus.com/korea-sim/eng/pc/support/blog/korea_esim_sim_installation_guide) gives different steps for its data-only and voice options.
3. **Test the service you paid for.** Load a map with Wi-Fi off. If calls or texts matter, test them before leaving the seller or airport counter. Keep your home line or another option available until the new one works, and check possible roaming charges.

## Staying longer? Confirm the documents and number

Ask whether the plan can cover your full stay and whether you can keep your number if you switch. For example, [KT’s visitor prepaid SIM extension page](https://m.roaming.kt.com/eng/sim/cf/ext) caps that specific product at 90 days including extensions. If a resident service asks for mobile identity verification, show the actual prompt to the carrier. Ask which plans and documents it accepts and whether the registration must match the name that service already has.
