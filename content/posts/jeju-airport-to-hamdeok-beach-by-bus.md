A direct bus can work well after an early arrival in Jeju if you can take your luggage to your accommodation near Hamdeok. Search for **함덕해수욕장** as the destination, then confirm the actual bus stop before boarding. The line number alone is insufficient because both routes serve the airport in opposite directions.

## Choose the direction

| At Jeju Airport | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| [Eastbound express 101](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=1950) | The sequence should show the airport followed by stops towards Jocheon, Hamdeok and the east coast. Look for the beach stop on your run. |
| [Eastbound local 325](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2511) | The airport should be followed by the old-city area and stops towards Hamdeok. The operator labels the outbound beach stop **함덕서우봉해변**. |
| Taxi | Use the exact hotel name if you have bags. For the beach itself show **함덕해수욕장** and the destination address. |

At the airport, follow the current ground-transport signs after collecting your bags. Ask **함덕해수욕장 가나요?** (“Does this go to Hamdeok Beach?”) if you are unsure at the bay. Let your flight, baggage and check-in determine which departure you can realistically make. For a late arrival, check the [province's service notices](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/) as well as the timetable for your date. A late journey listed on a notice may serve different stops from a regular daytime bus.

## Set up the return before you walk

Save the [airport-bound 101](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=1951) and [airport-bound 325](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2512) separately and confirm which side of the road you need. The land marker in our map is the beach. Use [Jeju Bus Information](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/) for the exact stop position and scheduled or real-time departure. For a flight, use a departure with a comfortable backup or make another transport plan.

The beach address is **제주특별자치도 제주시 조천읍 조함해안로 519-10**. If you want the coast but are staying in another part of the island, compare [car-free Jeju bases](/blog/where-to-stay-in-jeju-without-a-car) and the route from that base before making the airport your transfer point.
