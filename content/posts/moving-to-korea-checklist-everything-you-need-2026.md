## Give every early task one owner and one deadline

Put your first-night booking, Korean address, check-in instructions, local contact and transport fallback on one offline note. Confirm which airport and terminal you arrive at before choosing a pickup point. For Incheon, start with the [airport’s transport information](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1542/subview.do) and use the [airport-to-hotel comparison](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) to account for bags, transfers and the walk at the other end. If your scheduled arrival is late, check the operator’s last usable departure after allowing for immigration and baggage.

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Before departure | Save the first-night address, arrival terminal and a check-in contact offline. Agree on what to do if you arrive late. |
| Before packing medication | Check the active ingredient, quantity and carry-in conditions. Ask Customs or the competent authority about uncertain items. |
| First business day | Confirm the registration step for your status, deadline and which address evidence the office accepts. |
| Before a long lease | Get deposit, rent, charges, move-in date and payee in writing. Extend the temporary stay if you need time to compare. |

## Take the first-night kit separately

Keep your passport, arrival paperwork, an offline booking, a charger, an independent payment backup and items you need straight away accessible. Make a separate list of things you can buy after looking at the room. Ask the host what bedding and utilities are already in place before paying for extra baggage or shipping goods to an address you may leave soon.

For personal medication, the [Incheon Airport Customs notice](https://www.customs.go.kr/incheon_airport/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?bbsId=2961&mi=12612&nttSn=10183523&nttSnUrl=ab4440206c518a9b2724795ab74c8880) describes conditional allowances and exceptions. Use the [personal medication entry checklist](/blog/bringing-personal-medication-into-korea-traveler-guide) to verify the active ingredient, permitted amount and whether separate authorization is needed. Ask the accommodation provider which payments are due before check-in and confirm how a second card or cash backup would work for those expenses.

Once you have a delivery address, [compare written mover quotes and shipping handoffs](/blog/moving-companies-in-korea-international-vs-local) before committing goods to transit.

## Confirm administration before depending on it

Keep the exact status or code on your approval and your expected dates handy. Ask the [Korea Immigration Service contact center](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/1862/subview.do) whether a registration step applies, the deadline, how to find the responsible office and what address evidence it will accept if you start in temporary accommodation. Ask your school, employer or sponsor to identify documents they will provide and when. Record the official answer and the date you checked it.

When you plan around a phone plan or local bank account, check the provider directly for the service you need and the documents it accepts. Keep a way to pay for lodging and transportation while your plans are still in progress.

After arrival, [confirm your NHIS category and its effective date](/blog/korean-health-insurance-nhis-guide-for-foreigners-2026) if a new job or address changes how coverage may be handled.

## Move from a short stay to a lease when the terms make sense

Separate money due on arrival, recurring costs and any amount tied up in a deposit. Use the [jeonse and wolse worksheet](/blog/jeonse-vs-wolse-korean-rental-system-explained) with written offers. Check the location in person when practical, including the route home with bags and what it is like at the hour you will usually return. Before handing over money, confirm the payee and get the amount, timing and terms in writing.

Once a lease and handover date are confirmed, go to the [first-week apartment guide](/blog/korean-apartment-etiquette-what-neighbors-expect-2026) for waste collection, quiet hours and the contact for building issues.
