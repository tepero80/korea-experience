Go to the **Uiamho lower station (의암호 정차장)** for the cable car across the lake. Check the operator's current service and last boarding before you travel. You can ride for the lake view and return from the upper station, or add the separate upper walkway if it is open and suits your group.

## Get to the lower station

Save **춘천 삼악산 호수케이블카**, at **강원특별자치도 춘천시 스포츠타운길 245**. The [operator's directions](https://www.samaksancablecar.com/home/produce/map.php) list city bus **16** from both rail stations to the cable car stop. Confirm the current boarding stop and direction with the [Chuncheon traffic portal](https://www.chuncheon.go.kr/traffic/) or your live map.

| Start | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **남춘천역** | At Namchuncheon Station, use Exit 2. The operator estimates around **30 minutes** on bus 16, plus your wait. |
| **춘천역** | At Chuncheon Station, use Exit 1. The operator estimates around **50 minutes** on bus 16, plus your wait. |
| Taxi | Check a taxi to the lower station if you have heavy bags or a short connection. For the way back, arrange where to meet your ride before heading up. |

Bus estimates are for planning, and traffic can change them. Save the station that appears on your return rail ticket. The [Seoul-to-Chuncheon train guide](/blog/seoul-to-chuncheon-train-choice) explains the rail options.

## Check service, tickets and the return

The [operator's status page](https://www.samaksancablecar.com/home/index.php) shows separate conditions for the cable car and the upper walking trail. Look for maintenance notices as well as weather interruptions. The [fare and boarding guide](https://www.samaksancablecar.com/home/guide/charge.php) says same-day boarding closes one hour before the end of service, and tickets can sell out earlier. Check the exact cutoff and fares for your date. Allow time to reach the lower station, buy or collect a ticket and queue before that cutoff.

The cabins can be shared with other groups and have no heating or cooling. Dress for the day's conditions. Set a latest time to board back down based on the onward bus or train, with room for a queue and the trip across town.

## Decide whether to add the upper walk

The [operator's walkway guide](https://www.samaksancablecar.com/home/guide/walk.php) lists an **822 m round-trip** deck walk, with its own opening hours and weather restrictions. It has no connection to the hiking trails. Wheelchairs, strollers and dogs are excluded from the deck. For the ride itself, the [Korea Tourism Organization's accessibility listing](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/opentour/detail.do?cotId=3674fe6a-275b-4167-bae6-afe1e133334e) describes wheelchair boarding support and access to parts of the upper station. Call **1588-4888** to confirm what suits your chair or mobility needs and which areas you can reach. The second-floor lounge is stairs-only in that listing.

If cable car service is suspended, switch to [Soyanggang Skywalk and the Maiden Statue](/blog/chuncheon-station-to-soyanggang-skywalk) only after checking conditions there. If the weather rules out both, check [Chuncheon National Museum's opening days](https://chuncheon.museum.go.kr/eng/sub01_01.do) for an indoor option. Keep both your onward connection and one fallback in the [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary).
