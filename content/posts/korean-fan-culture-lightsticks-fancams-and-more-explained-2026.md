A light stick can be part of a concert's coordinated lighting, while a fancam is a video focused on a performer. Neither term tells you what your ticket permits. Read the notices for the specific artist, venue and event before buying equipment or filming.

| Your question | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| Will my light stick synchronize? | The approved version, battery type, app and seat-pairing instructions in your event notice. |
| Can I film or take photos? | The event's recording and device rules, including any permitted moments. |
| Can I bring a banner? | The permitted dimensions and how high it may be held. |
| Is a membership needed? | The exact ticket or event application requirements; membership and admission are different. |

## A real example: versions matter

For SEVENTEEN's 2024 Incheon concerts, the [organizer's notice](https://weverse.io/seventeen/notice/18405) stated that version 3 could pair for wireless effects, while versions 1 and 2 could not. It also described on-site pairing booths and batteries for that event. This historical example does not establish compatibility for a later concert: check your own show's notice and any updated app instructions before spending money.

If your event supports seat pairing, confirm your ticket's seat information and the official app before attempting it. Booths, opening hours and supplies are event-specific; do not assume an on-site pairing service or replacement batteries will be available.

## Fancams do not imply permission to film

An organizer may publish official performance clips, while a fan-created video is subject to the actual event's rules. For example, [BTS's Busan sound-check notice](https://shop.weverse.io/en/shop/USD/artists/2/notices/13187) prohibited photos, video and audio recording at that event. Other events can have different rules or explicitly permitted periods. A phone being allowed inside does not by itself mean recording is allowed.

Read the notice before entering and follow staff directions. Avoid blocking another person's view with a sign or raised device. Do not film in private or restricted areas, and do not assume an artist's itinerary or personal details shared by another fan are appropriate to use.

## Before concert day

Save the official ticket and event notice, check the required identification and any name-matching conditions, and review approved bags and devices. If no notice explains whether an item is allowed, contact the organizer or leave it behind. For a separate decision about public music-show admission, see [the music-show recording guide](/blog/how-to-attend-k-pop-music-show-recordings-in-seoul-2026); its eligibility must also be checked for the specific broadcast.
