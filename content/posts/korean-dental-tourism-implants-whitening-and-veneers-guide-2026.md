## Use the correct booking desk

[Seoul National University Hospital’s FAQ](https://snuh.org/global/en/about/faqList.do) explains that its dental hospital uses a separate route through the International Dental Service Center. The [city’s dental hospital listing](https://medical.visitseoul.net/organization/hospital/hospitalDetail/120) identifies that service. Ask whichever provider you choose to confirm the dental department, location and scope of the booking.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Exam | Ask whether the slot is for assessment, imaging or a particular treatment. Find out what previous records or images the dentist can review. |
| Visits | Ask whether any stage depends on the exam, a laboratory or a later check. Get written dates or a clear indication of which dates remain provisional. |
| Costs | Ask for a written estimate that separates the exam, any necessary work and follow-up. Ask about deposits and changed appointments before paying. |
| Records | Plan how to obtain clinical notes or images in a usable format and whom to contact if you need follow-up after traveling. |

## Match the clinic plan to your travel dates

The [NHS treatment abroad checklist](https://www.nhs.uk/using-the-nhs/healthcare-abroad/going-abroad-for-treatment/treatment-abroad-checklist/) recommends discussing record transfer and aftercare in advance. Ask your dentist what they would do if the plan takes longer and what can be arranged once you are home. Get the agreed scope before committing to additional travel or bookings.

See the [appointment confirmation guide](/blog/booking-medical-consultations-in-korea-online-vs-walk-in) to distinguish a discussion with the clinic from a confirmed appointment.
