Use Seongsu Station (성수역) on Line 2. Begin with one shopping stop, then head to **Ttukdo Market (뚝도시장)** for a meal. If lunch is your priority, the [district’s market page](https://www.sd.go.kr/main/contents.do?key=1696) directs visitors from Exit 3. For workshops first, use the [tourism office’s shoe-street approach](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Seongsu-Handmade-Shoes-Street/ENP027649) from Exit 1 and choose a shop that is open.

## Choose your stops

| Stop | Check before going |
| --- | --- |
| Shoe workshops | Ask a specific shop about ready-made sizing, custom-order lead time, fitting and collection if you leave Korea soon. |
| Dior Seongsu | The [boutique locator](https://www.dior.com/fashion/stores/en_us/south-korea/seoul/7-yeonmujang-5-gil) lists **7 Yeonmujang 5-gil** and 11:00–20:00 daily at the time of review. Ask the branch about entry for your date. Use [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/maps?cid=13887469895188303092) or [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%94%94%EC%98%AC%20%EC%84%B1%EC%88%98%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%84%B1%EB%8F%99%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%97%B0%EB%AC%B4%EC%9E%A55%EA%B8%B8%207). |
| Ttukdo Market | Save **서울 성동구 성수이로 32-15 일대**. Check the vendor’s hours, portion and payment before ordering. |

## Allow yourself to skip a queue

Check the actual walking segments from your station exit or shop to the market. The market marker shows the area around the address. Choose the entrance that works for the vendor you want. If a shop has a long wait or would take the rest of your afternoon, go straight to your next stop. If you prefer the park, follow the [Seoul Forest and café route](/blog/seongsu-dong-dior-luxury-vs-market-eats-in-24-hours) on a separate visit.

Ttukdo is also a useful meal stop when a boutique visit is brief. The district lists food vendors there, with individual hours varying. Check the price and total at the counter using the [traditional market ordering guide](/blog/jeong-culture-in-markets-2026).
