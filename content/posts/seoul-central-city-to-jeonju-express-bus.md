If trains to Jeonju are full or you are already near southern Seoul, search for a direct express bus from **Central City Terminal (센트럴시티터미널)** to **Jeonju Express Bus Terminal (전주고속버스터미널)**. Start with a real departure in the ticket search. The two large Seoul bus halls share a subway station, and Jeonju has two bus terminals close together, so keep both names on your phone.

## Match the ticket terminals before you pay

The Korea Tourism Organization lists Jeonju among the routes from the [Central City express bus hall](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=471&vcontsId=140657). The [Jeonju city transport guide](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000106012001000) lists the exact departure and arrival selections and warns that the intercity terminal has a different address.

| Need | Read this on the ticket or map |
| --- | --- |
| Depart from southern Seoul | **센트럴시티(서울)** or Central City on the Seoul/Honam side. |
| Arrive by express bus | **전주고속터미널** or **전주고속버스터미널**, 가리내로 70. |
| Hold a different intercity ticket | Check the terminal named on it before setting out. The Jeonju intercity terminal is at 가리내로 30. |
| Compare rail first | Search **용산역 → 전주역** as well as Seoul Station. Jeonju City notes that Yongsan has more departures. |

Use [KOBUS express bus booking](https://www.kobus.co.kr/main.do) or the official links in Jeonju’s transport guide. Check the exact date, departure time, fare and seat type in the listing. If a payment or local verification step blocks you, use the terminal’s staffed counter when open and have a backup departure in mind. A search result is only an option until you have a usable ticket. On a busy weekend, check your return at the same time instead of relying on availability after dinner.

## Enter the Honam side and find your bay

Save **센트럴시티터미널, 서울특별시 서초구 신반포로 176**. For the subway, use **고속터미널역** on Line 3, 7 or 9. Inside the connected complex, follow signs for **센트럴시티** or **호남선** (Honam lines). The adjacent **경부선** (Gyeongbu) hall also has express buses, so use the hall on your ticket. The [Seocho district terminal map](https://www.seocho.go.kr/upload/common/96caba7a-850d-4f6f-913a-063c881ff248.pdf) shows the two halls and links from the subway.

Check the bay number on the day. Compare **전주**, your departure time and the bay with the terminal display. Keep the ticket format that your booking provider issued. If your bus stops at another point in Jeonju before the main terminal, stay on until your booked arrival unless you have confirmed a better stop for your hotel.

## Continue from the Jeonju express terminal

Save **전주고속버스터미널, 전북특별자치도 전주시 덕진구 가리내로 70**. Use that terminal as the start point when asking a route app or taxi to take you to **전주한옥마을** (Hanok Village). If someone is meeting you, send the full name and address. Reopen your return ticket before you set out for the terminal, since a walk between the two nearby buildings can cost time.

Traffic affects the arrival and your time in the village. If you are making a day trip, save the return bay and keep time to get back from your last stop. For a compact first outing, use the [Gyeonggijeon and Omokdae walk](/blog/jeonju-gyeonggijeon-omokdae-walking-route) to choose the order of your sights and the [intercity bus guide](/blog/intercity-bus-travel-booking-apps-and-routes) if you need a different route.
