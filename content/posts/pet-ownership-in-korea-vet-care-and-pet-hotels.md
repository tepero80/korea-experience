## Decide who will care for your pet each day

If you want your dog or cat with you overnight, ask the hotel to confirm the **exact room category**. Separate boarding may be useful during a day trip if you can reach the provider within its pickup hours. Find a veterinary backup near your hotel or boarding location before you need it.

| Booking | Ask the operator to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Pet-friendly guest room | Species, weight, number of pets, documents, extra fee, deposit and any restrictions on leaving your pet alone. |
| Separate boarding | Acceptance for your dates and pet, feeding routine, drop-off and pickup windows, staffing overnight and who can authorize care. |
| Veterinary backup | Which branch to call, after-hours intake and its location relative to your itinerary. |

## Read a property's policy before paying

For example, [Jeju Shinhwa World's official FAQ](https://www.shinhwaworld.com/content-problemen.htm?lang=en) says dogs under 10 kg are eligible for designated Somerset Pet-Friendly Rooms, with vaccination documentation including a rabies certificate. The policy is specific to those rooms. Ask the operator to confirm the age or breed conditions, any charges and what happens if your pet cannot check in. If you are close to the weight cutoff, ask for written confirmation before you book.

For a boarding example, [Myomyo Banjeom](https://www.myomyo.co.kr/) lists cat rooms with CCTV and a reservation contact. It lists **서울 용산구 원효로 51, 3층 303호** and **02-707-2235**. Ask when staff are on site, how updates are sent and how feeding or existing prescriptions are handled. Confirm both available dates and the total fee with the boarding provider.

## Save an after-hours contact close to your last stop

[Helix Animal Medical Center's Seocho branch](https://helix-amc.com/seocho) lists **02-2135-9119** and **서울 서초구 신반포로 162 르본시티 2층**. It publishes overnight emergency hours from 21:00 to 09:00 and an extra nighttime charge. Call the branch before traveling to confirm current intake. Save the local contact and address chosen for your own route, especially when you will be outside Seoul.

Ask your accommodation or boarding provider how to contact you and which clinic they would call. Keep a copy of vaccination records, any existing vet instructions and a local phone number the provider can reach.
