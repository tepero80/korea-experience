## Match the legal operator before providing ID

Open [KoFIU’s notices](https://www.kofiu.go.kr/kor/notification/notice.do) yourself and find the most recent **가상자산사업자 신고 현황** entry. Open the dated list and match the legal operator and service to the official website or app terms. If someone sent you an app or referral link, check the operator through a channel you found independently. An accepted report confirms the operator’s reporting status. Check the service and whether it can accept your account separately.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Match the legal entity to the official list. Ask whether the service you want is covered. |
| ID | Ask which passport or residence documents the provider accepts for your situation. Use only its verified submission channel. |
| Bank | For KRW, ask the provider which bank handles real-name deposits and withdrawals. Confirm directly with that bank that your own account qualifies. |
| Send | Before using a transfer feature, check the network, recipient details, verification and both ends’ withdrawal or deposit rules. |

[Article 7 of the financial transaction reporting law](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1023714483) defines the real-name account arrangement and allows exceptions for specified operators. Ask the exchange and partner bank to confirm that the specific account you hold works with the KRW service. If you need an account for daily expenses, follow the [Korean bank account checklist](/blog/opening-a-bank-account-in-korea-step-by-step-guide) for that separate task.

## Pause if you cannot verify a payment request

The [FSC’s fraud warning](https://www.fsc.go.kr/po010106/88039?curPage=&srchBeginDt=&srchCtgry=6&srchEndDt=&srchKey=&srchText=) flags promises of guaranteed returns, claims of inside information and pressure to sign up through referral links. Pause if someone asks you to send money to a private account, share a password or private key, or make another payment to release funds. If you already paid, keep the messages and transaction records, contact your bank through a number you found independently, and use the official reporting channels in the notice.

The [FSC’s user-protection summary](https://www.fsc.go.kr/eng/pr010101/82683) explains bank custody of users’ cash deposits and separation of customers’ virtual assets. These safeguards address custody and conduct. You can still lose money when prices fall or when a transfer goes wrong.

## Keep records and check the rule for your transaction

Save dated account statements, transaction descriptions, purchase and sale amounts, and fees. The [National Tax Service’s resident guide](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=238935&mi=40370) lists January 1, 2027 as the start date for covered transfers and lending under the amendment it discusses. The [nonresident guide](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=238936&mi=40371) describes a separate withholding framework and treaty procedure. Ask NTS or a qualified cross-border tax adviser to confirm your tax residence and the rules for your transaction date and type.
