## Can you visit before a flight?

The **National Aviation Museum of Korea (국립항공박물관)** is a separate building outside Gimpo Airport's Domestic Terminal. It works as a standalone indoor stop or before a flight once you have allowed for the walk back, bag drop, security and your airline's boarding deadline. From the domestic terminal exit, the museum operator describes a walk of **about 400 metres**. If you would need to leave a secure connection or your remaining time is tight, stay at the airport.

| Plan | Route and checks |
| --- | --- |
| Arriving by subway | Follow Domestic Terminal to the first floor, then the museum signs. Count the station walk separately from the outdoor 400 metres. |
| At Domestic Terminal in dry weather | Walk toward Domestic Parking Lot 2 and follow the museum signs. Use the marked pedestrian route. |
| Rain or bulky bags | Check the free airport shuttle at first-floor Gate 4. Confirm the bus label and where to catch the return. |

## Walk or take the shuttle

At **Gimpo Airport Station (김포공항역)**, follow **국내선** for Domestic Terminal and go to the first floor. The [museum's official directions](https://www.aviation.or.kr/contents.do?menuno=65) send you toward **국내선 제2주차장** (Domestic Parking Lot 2) and mark the outdoor walk from the terminal exit. Copy **서울특별시 강서구 하늘길 177** into your map, then check the posted pedestrian signs. The map below marks the museum building.

The operator lists a free airport shuttle from **Gate 4 on the first floor**. The passenger-and-cargo service **여객+화물청사** lists a museum stop. The passenger-terminal-only service **여객청사전용** lists Domestic Parking Lot 2, with a walk after you get off. The museum lists service about every 15 to 20 minutes. Ask for the direction and return stop before boarding, especially if you have a flight. Allow time for picking up bags and walking between the gate and your airline counter.

## Choose an exhibition or book an activity

The [operator lists general exhibition admission as free](https://www.aviation.or.kr/contents.do?menuno=65) and publishes **10:00 to 18:00**, with **last entry at 17:30**. Monday, New Year's Day, Seollal and Chuseok are listed closures. When Monday is a public holiday, the museum says it opens that day and closes the next weekday. [Check current notices](https://www.aviation.or.kr/board.do?boardno=24&menuno=68) for individual gallery or programme changes.

For a short visit, pick one open history, industry or future-of-aviation section from the [city's exhibition overview](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/National-Aviation-Museum-of-Korea/ENP038021). Set an alarm for your return before you start looking around. For a simulator or other activity, use the [official booking list](https://www.aviation.or.kr/reservation.do?gcode=gcode1&menuno=189) to check the individual time, price, age or height rules.

If you are visiting without a flight, the [Seoul Botanic Park winter guide](/blog/seoul-botanic-park-winter-family) gives you another indoor outing in western Seoul. Budget a separate subway leg. The Aviation Museum is at **Gimpo (GMP)**, so use the [Incheon airport transfer guide](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) for an **ICN** arrival.
