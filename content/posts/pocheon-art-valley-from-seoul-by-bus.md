Pocheon Art Valley (포천아트밸리) is a former granite quarry with a lake, sculpture areas and a monorail. From Seoul, plan on a train-to-bus transfer and a final local connection; the stop named for Art Valley on the main road is not the entrance itself.

## Build the journey around the last connection

| Your choice | How to plan it |
| --- | --- |
| Metro and buses | Take Line 1 to Uijeongbu Station (의정부역), take 138 or 138-5 toward Pocheon to **신북면행정복지센터**, then 73 toward 포천아트밸리. |
| Less waiting at the transfer | Compare a taxi for the final leg from the main-road area; check the fare and return pickup. |
| A fixed evening commitment | Check the return before leaving Seoul, and consider a closer outing if local connections do not fit. |

The [Korea Heritage Agency route](https://www.kh.or.kr/visit/kor/road/6/view.do?key=2408220006) describes bus 138 from the Uijeongbu area and a transfer to 73. An [Uijeongbu city guide](https://www.ui4u.go.kr/depart/Downfiles/0902020900/DreamStart.pdf) also describes a transfer near Sinbuk-myeon. The [venue transport FAQ](https://artvalley.pcfac.or.kr/sub06/sub02_view.html?wr_id=23) and the [Gyeonggi route listing](https://www.gbis.go.kr/service/busInfo/metroBus.action?centerId=4165000000&cmd=searchMetroCenter) shows 73 as a separate, infrequent service, with intervals of roughly 130–150 minutes when checked. Confirm a practical return before leaving; a taxi is a useful backup. Stops and times can change: use [Gyeonggi bus information](https://www.gbis.go.kr/) or the operator before setting out, confirm the exact boarding side and return, and do not mistake **신북면행정복지센터·포천아트밸리** on the main road for the site entrance.

## At the quarry

Search **포천아트밸리**, **경기도 포천시 신북면 아트밸리로 234**. At the entrance decide between the uphill walk and the monorail. The [official venue](https://artvalley.pcfac.or.kr/) and [KTO listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=66111) distinguish entrance admission from monorail charges. The [venue FAQ](https://artvalley.pcfac.or.kr/sub06/sub02_view.html?page=view&wr_id=18) lists 09:00–18:00 with last entry at 17:00 and a regular closure on the first Tuesday of each month; holiday and special notices can change that. Check the monorail timetable, maintenance and weather; its ticket is separate from admission.

Cheonjuho (천주호) is the lake within the former quarry. Stay on open signed paths and allow time to return downhill. After snow or rain, ask staff about ice and route access rather than relying on a summer path description. Save the entrance and confirmed return on the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).

If the monorail is essential, use the [venue directions](https://artvalley.pcfac.or.kr/sub02/sub03.html) for its direct phone number, **031-531-2622**. At the site, follow signs for the approach that is open and manageable for your group. Another Pocheon day could focus on [the national arboretum](/blog/korea-national-arboretum-gwangneung-forest-visit) instead.
