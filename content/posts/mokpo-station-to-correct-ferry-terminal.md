If you are arriving in Mokpo by train and continuing by ferry, check the **terminal named on your specific booking** before choosing a bus or taxi. Mokpo has more than one passenger terminal, and departure locations can depend on the vessel or change with operations.

## Before leaving the station

- **Confirm your ferry:** Check the operator’s current notice and booking for the vessel, terminal and boarding deadline.
- **Confirm identification:** Ask which documents are accepted for every passenger, including children.
- **Choose a connection:** Search the exact terminal from Mokpo Station; compare the correct bus stop and taxi.
- **Protect the trip:** Allow time for luggage and boarding, and check weather and cancellation notices.

The [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=483&vcontsId=140669) describes ferry connections from Mokpo. The right stop, route and travel time depend on your actual terminal. Ask your ferry operator for current contact details, identification requirements and boarding instructions.

For Jeju-bound sailings, [Seaworld Express Ferry's notice](https://seaferry.co.kr/noticepage.php?noticegubn=1) distinguishes Mokpo Port International Passenger Terminal and Samhak Pier Passenger Terminal for different services. Vessel and pier assignments can change. Do not enter a generic “Mokpo ferry terminal” destination and assume it matches your ticket: verify the precise address and check-in deadline with the operator.

## If your train is late or the weather changes

Contact the ferry operator before choosing another sailing or transport. Ask whether the reservation can be changed, what fees apply, and where to board. No replacement boat, refund or guaranteed connection is implied. If official weather or evacuation warnings make travel unsafe, follow the authorities' guidance.
