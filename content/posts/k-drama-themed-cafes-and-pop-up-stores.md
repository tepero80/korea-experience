A café used in a drama, a café with themed décor and a limited promotional pop-up offer different experiences. A filming connection does not guarantee that a particular seat or prop remains.

## Pick your stop

| Type | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Filming café | Current business hours, seating and whether the setting still looks familiar. |
| Temporary pop-up | Organiser, exact dates, location, admission and reservation rules. |
| Drama set | Whether it is open to independent visitors and what remains. |

[VISITKOREA identifies Hakrim](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=47863) as a filming café for *My Love from the Star* and *Reply 1988*. Copy its listed address: 서울특별시 종로구 대학로 119 2층. The listing advises calling +82-2-742-2877 to check hours. Its second-floor location makes it sensible to ask about access if stairs are a concern.

A [2022 tourism article](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=224799) identifies Grandpa Factory in Seongsu as a *Vincenzo* café scene; confirm its current operation before making a special trip. For pop-ups, find the production or venue's current announcement rather than relying on an old social post. Ask before photographing other customers and allow for a purchase at a working café.
