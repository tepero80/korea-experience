## Start with the current expiry date

[Article 25 of Korea’s Immigration Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1028070139) sets the rule for obtaining permission to stay beyond the current period. Use [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/Main.pt) to check the appropriate service. An office reservation schedules a visit. Verify how and when the extension application itself must be filed.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Expiry | Use the end date on your immigration record and check the applicable filing cutoff. |
| Online | Confirm that the service accepts your status and that you can sign in and upload everything it asks for. |
| Office | Verify the branch, reservation and the documents you must show in person. |
| Receipt | Keep the submitted application number, payment record if applicable and any request for more documents. |

## Get help before the deadline if filing fails

If the service is unavailable or there are no office slots, contact the [Immigration Contact Center](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/1862/subview.do) before expiry for the route and timing. Check what the submitted application permits while it is being reviewed and keep the response.
