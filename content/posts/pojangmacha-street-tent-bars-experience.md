## Start near Jongno 3-ga

Korea Tourism Organization describes temporary tents near Jongno 3-ga Station Exit 6 after sundown. Use that exit as a starting point and do one pass to see which tents are actually serving. Look for 포장마차 on signage. Seoul Tourism describes dishes such as rolled omelet, udon and seafood pancake, which gives you an idea of what to ask for.

| Before you take a seat | Ask or check |
| --- | --- |
| How much | Show the dish on the menu and ask for its price and portion. Ask if there is a cover or minimum order for your group. |
| Drinks | If you want food only, ask whether a nonalcoholic drink or a food-only order is possible. |
| Food | Point to the item and ask about broth, seafood or other ingredients that matter to you. |
| Second stop | Save a sit-down kitchen nearby and its closing time before you go. |

If you need a bookable seat indoors, [check the Seoul cocktail lounge and rooftop options](/blog/cocktail-bars-speakeasies-and-rooftop-lounges) before choosing between an indoor and an open-air evening.

## Plan around exposure and the ride home

Treat the outside seating as a flexible choice in rain, wind or cold. Shorten the visit or go straight to an indoor place if your group needs a predictable seat, accessible entry or a restroom. Keep a separate dinner reservation if arriving on a tight schedule.

Check the last workable ride before ordering a second dish. If you want an indoor tasting instead of another tent, the [makgeolli bar guide](/blog/makgeolli-bars-traditional-rice-wine-culture) explains what to order and how to check the seating arrangement.
