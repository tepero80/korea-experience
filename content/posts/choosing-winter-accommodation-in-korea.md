**A good winter base makes the daily routine manageable.** Compare the actual room, arrival and return journey, rather than choosing only by the lowest outdoor temperature or a cozy photograph.

## Send these questions before paying

| Part of the stay | Confirm with the property |
| --- | --- |
| Sleeping room | Heating method/control, suitable bedding and who can help at night |
| Bathroom | Whether it is private, entirely indoors and usable for your needs |
| Wet items | Where shoes and clothing may safely dry; permitted practices |
| Arrival | Exact entrance, steps/slopes, luggage route and delayed check-in contact |
| Daily travel | Useful station/bus stop and actual outdoor exposure after returning |

[KTO's climate guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140636) describes cold, dry winter conditions. That supports preparation, but does not prove that an individual room is warm or independently adjustable. Keep missing property answers visible until resolved.

## Compare the last walk, not the map radius

Open the exact entrance in a local map and trace the route from the transport you will use. A short straight-line distance can hide a hill, steps, a large interchange or the wrong side of a building. Check lifts or assistance directly when required; an overview marker is not a verified accessible entrance.

If you expect a late arrival, combine the [airport fallback](/blog/incheon-airport-late-night-arrival-guide) with the property's late check-in instructions. Saving one transfer can be worthwhile, but only if the vehicle, stop and final entrance actually work for your party and luggage.

## Keep heating and drying advice property-specific

Ask staff how to operate the supplied controls and where to dry wet items. Do not cover heaters, adjust boiler valves or bring unapproved heating equipment. At a [hanok stay](/blog/hanok-stay-booking-checklist), separately confirm the sleeping surface, bathroom route and fire rules; a general ondol description does not settle them.

## Put recovery into the day

Pack for your actual outdoor blocks with the [winter packing guide](/blog/korea-winter-packing-checklist), then choose a nearby meal or indoor stop before the group gets exhausted. A [rain/cold alternative](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets) should reduce exposure and travel, not add several cross-city transfers.
