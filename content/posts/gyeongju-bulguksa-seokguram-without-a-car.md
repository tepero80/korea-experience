**Take a bus to Bulguksa, then ride bus 12 to Seokguram. Before leaving Gyeongju, pick a return bus from Seokguram and confirm the onward ride from Bulguksa.** The temple and grotto share a [UNESCO World Heritage listing](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/736/). They have separate entrances, and the road between them climbs about 9 km according to [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=94436).

This is a plan for travelers starting in Gyeongju without a rental car. Operator information checked on November 23, 2026. Check both departure directions again on your travel date.

## Choose the bus sequence before you leave town

| Leg | Where to look | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central Gyeongju → Bulguksa | From terminal stop **고속버스·시외버스터미널 01045**, compare **10** via the Bomun area and **11** via Tongiljeon | The regular loops also start from **황리단길공영주차장 01522**. Compare travel from your actual stay. |
| KTX Gyeongju Station → Bulguksa | Look for **700** at **경주역(KTX) 03142** and check your departure | If the wait does not work for your visit, use a taxi or make the temple visit from town after dropping your bags. |
| Bulguksa → Seokguram | Take **12** from **불국사 01504** or the uphill ticket-office stop **불국사매표소 01495** | On the city route list, the next mountain stop should be **석굴암주차장**. |
| Seokguram → Bulguksa → town | Catch **12** at **석굴암주차장 01199**. Then take a town-bound service from the stop shown in your planner. | The downhill ticket-office stop is **01496**. The loop ends at **불국사 01161**, a different stop from **01504**. |

The [grotto operator’s access page](https://www.seokguram.org/bbs/content.php?co_id=map) names buses 10, 11 and 700 for reaching Bulguksa and route 12 for the grotto leg. It still uses the former KTX station name and describes a simple hourly return. For current information, search **12** in the [city route finder](https://its.gyeongju.go.kr/busLineInfo.do) and choose the **불국사 → 불국사** loop. Check arrivals by stop number with [Gyeongju LIVE BUS](https://livebus.gyeongju.go.kr/). The city route record showed the stop order but no day-specific time rows when checked. Confirm your departures on the stop display or with the operator before relying on a particular time. Check the official [city bus fare page](https://its.gyeongju.go.kr/busCharge.do) for current fares and transfer rules.

## Start with the return time, then choose your visit order

1. **Pick a downhill bus you can actually catch.** Start with the Seokguram parking-area departure and work backward. Leave time for the signed walk, any admission wait and the walk back to the bus stop. Where the timetable allows, choose a departure that leaves another usable return as a backup.
2. **Use your time at Bulguksa before or after the grotto.** If the uphill bus is due soon, go up first and see the temple on the way back. If you have a comfortable wait and a later workable downhill bus, see Bulguksa first. Check the temple’s last admission again if you plan to return there after the grotto.
3. **Check the town connection at Bulguksa separately.** Keep the stop name and direction for your hotel or station. If you have a timed train, build in another margin for the ride to KTX Gyeongju Station. The central bus terminal and railway station are separate starting points.

**At Seokguram:** get off at **석굴암주차장**, the parking-area stop. Follow signs to the grotto. The [site directions](https://www.seokguram.org/bbs/content.php?co_id=map) describe approximately ten minutes on foot from parking to the precinct. That is one way and does not include your visit or a return buffer. Use the addresses below to open the correct destination. In your transport app, search **석굴암주차장** separately to find the bus stop.

## If the bus, trail or weather changes your plan

- **Still at Bulguksa and the uphill bus leaves no practical return:** stay at the temple and return to town. If the grotto is your priority, ask your lodging or an available taxi operator to confirm the uphill trip and the downhill pickup before you go. Provide **석굴암주차장** for pickup and check the fare. A ride up does not arrange the return.
- **Already at Seokguram:** confirm the next downhill service at the parking-area stop. If the last bus has gone, use the saved pickup name and ask staff to help arrange a taxi if available. Allow time before any booked rail departure.
- **Considering walking between sites:** the [Korea National Park Service’s trail guide](https://www.knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/visitCourseGuide.do?parkId=121900&vcursId=121900V001) identifies a marked trail between Bulguksa and the grotto parking area. Check the [park’s current access notices](https://public.knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/visitCourseMain.do?menuNo=7020002&parkId=121900), exact section, daylight and footing. The summit trail is a different section. Treat the walk as a separately planned outing and keep a bus or taxi option for the return.

If the mountain leg falls through, use the central alternatives in the [two-day Gyeongju itinerary](/blog/gyeongju-the-museum-without-walls-2-day-historical-itinerary-2026). For a train departure on the same day, start with the [KTX station transfer and return plan](/blog/gyeongju-ktx-to-hwangnidan). Save the departure, the downhill bus and the backup in your [trip board](/tools/itinerary) before leaving town.
