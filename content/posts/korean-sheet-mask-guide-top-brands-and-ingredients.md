## Compare one packet with a box

Use the **sheet count** to compare prices and the milliliter label to check the pouch size. Buy a single or small set if you are trying a variety for the first time. When the contents match, divide each shelf price by the number of usable sheets.

| On the shelf | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **1매** or **1장** | One sheet. Useful when you want to try one variant. |
| **10매입** | Ten sheets. Check whether the box contains one variety or a mix. |
| **27 mL × 10** | Ten units with 27 mL each. Confirm whether the pouch size will work for your luggage. |
| **전성분** and **사용방법** | Full ingredient list and directions. Look for them on the box or individual wrapper. |

For example, **₩15,000 for 10 sheets comes to ₩1,500 each**. At ₩2,000 per individual packet, it takes eight packets for the singles to cost more than the box. Those figures show the math. Read the shelf label for the actual prices, and check whether you need all ten.

## Match the name to the ingredients and directions

[Abib's Heartleaf label](https://en.abib.com/products/heartleaf-sticker) is an example of a named variety sold in a 27 mL × 10 format. Use the full product name and the ingredient panel to compare two boxes with similar colors. **어성초** means heartleaf. A front-label ingredient does not tell you the rest of the formula, so check the full list if you already avoid something.

Read the use directions on the packet you have, including how long to leave it on. Keep the box or a photo of its instructions if you separate the pouches for gifts. Online offers and packaging can differ from the version on the shelf.

## Pack the wet pouches before the airport

The [Incheon Airport liquids FAQ](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1434/subview.do) explains the limit for international cabin liquids, sprays and gels. Its examples do not specify wet sheet masks. If you need to carry wet pouches in the cabin, confirm how the airport will screen them and leave room in your liquids bag. Check the rules again if you go through security during a connection.
