**For a planned consultation, contact the clinic before travelling there. For an emergency, use emergency services.** An online request does not confirm a booking until the provider replies, and walk-in acceptance, waiting time and language support vary by branch and day.

## Choose the right first action

| Your situation                              | First action                                                                                                                      | Do not assume                                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Planned specialist or elective consultation | Send an inquiry through the provider's own channel                                                                                | An advertised language is available at every appointment |
| You need a routine appointment soon         | Call the branch and describe the request privately                                                                                | Walking in will be quicker or cheaper                    |
| You cannot identify the right service       | Ask the provider or [Medical Korea](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient) for non-emergency information                   | A booking platform can assess symptoms                   |
| Emergency                                   | [119 for ambulance/fire;112 for police](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145) | An email or social-media reply is an emergency response  |

[1339 is KDCA's infectious-disease information service](https://www.kdca.go.kr/eng/4276/subview.do), not a general English clinic-booking desk. Keep that distinction in your saved contact list.

## Send one clear inquiry

Copy and adapt this message in the provider's official booking form. Send medical details only through an appropriate private channel.

> I would like a consultation on [date range]. Please confirm the branch address, clinician or department, appointment time, available consultation language, consultation fee and whether an interpreter has an additional charge. What documents should I bring? Is a deposit required, and what are the cancellation terms?

If you are arranging an elective procedure, add: “I want a consultation before deciding on treatment. Is the consultation separate from a procedure booking?” Do not send passport images through an unsolicited chat account.

## Confirm before leaving your accommodation

1. Match the provider's Korean name and branch address. Use [official registration information](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem) as a starting identity check for foreign-patient services.
2. Save the provider's actual confirmation, not just your sent message.
3. Check local date/time, arrival instructions, entrance and any accessibility needs.
4. Ask about documents, payment methods, insurance paperwork and the full fee.
5. Save a cancellation/contact route and arrange interpretation before consent.

If no reply arrives, do not treat the appointment as confirmed. Try the official telephone or choose a different confirmed service. No reliable universal wait-time or booking-percentage figure is available for this guide.

## Make the return trip simple

Put the Korean address and appointment into your [private trip board](/tools/itinerary). Do not put medical records in a shared itinerary. Leave a travel buffer and ask the provider how any follow-up affects your plans.
