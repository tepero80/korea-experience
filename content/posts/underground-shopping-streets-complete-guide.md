Gangnam Station's underground shops and Goto Mall are separate destinations. Pick the one that fits your route before setting your map or boarding the train.

## Pick the station

| Mall | Station |
| --- | --- |
| Gangnam | Gangnam, Lines 2 and Shinbundang. |
| Goto Mall | Express Bus Terminal, Lines 3, 7 and 9. |

[Visit Seoul’s Goto Mall guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Goto-Mall/ENP029546) identifies the entrance near Exit 8-1 of Express Bus Terminal Station. Search 강남역 지하도상가 for Gangnam Station or 고속터미널역 for Express Bus Terminal, then check the mall signs at the station. The [Gangnam listing](https://sisul.or.kr/gha/store/info.do?key=2309210001&sc_mallSn=19) gives its address as underground at 396 Gangnam-daero (서울특별시 강남구 강남대로 지하 396).

Individual store hours differ; check your specific shop before a special journey. Compare fit, price and condition before paying, and ask the seller about exchange or return terms and payment methods. Note your entrance or station exit so you can retrace your route. For a broader shopping decision, use the [Seoul shopping district guide](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam).
