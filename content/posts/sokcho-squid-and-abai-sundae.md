## Order the dish you mean

| Korean menu | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| 오징어순대 | Ojingeo sundae is stuffed squid. Ask the shop what is in its filling and how it is cooked. |
| 아바이순대 | Abai sundae uses pork casing. [KTO’s Dancheon listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=188441) describes a traditional filling with pork blood and sweet rice. |

If you need to avoid an ingredient, check the actual recipe and shared cooking surfaces. For example, **오징어 또는 돼지고기가 들어가나요?** means “Does it contain squid or pork?” For an allergy, identify the specific ingredient and ask about cross-contact before buying.

## Choose a food stop that fits your route

### Daepo Port for a stall stop

Use the map point for **Daepo Port Original Fried Food Alley**. [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=1564703) lists squid sundae among the foods sold in the alley and gives different hours by shop. Walk through first and ask the stall whether it has a portion available. Allow a separate journey if you are starting at Sokcho Jungang Market.

### Abai Village for a sit-down stop

[VisitKorea lists Dancheon Restaurant](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=188441) with both kinds of sundae. Confirm availability at the restaurant before ordering. If you were already heading to the market, use the [Jungang Market and Abai Village walk](/blog/sokcho-market-gaetbae-abai-village-walk) to choose your approach. If the ferry is unavailable, use a land route and check the current map directions from your side of the harbor.
