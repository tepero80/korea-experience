## Choose a place to ask about an appointment

For a local contact, the [Ministry of Health and Welfare directory](https://www.mohw.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a10706040300) lists Seoul district mental health centers. Call the center to ask what it provides and whether it can help in English. For a routine private counseling appointment, [With Me Counseling Center](https://withmecounseling.co.kr/en) publishes an English inquiry route. For a medical appointment, [JEJ Clinic](https://jejclinic.com/english/) publishes a contact for English psychiatric consultations. Confirm what each provider can offer you on your dates.

| Ask about | Details to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Service | The type of visit and whether an English-speaking clinician is available. |
| Timing | The earliest appointment date and where you need to go. |
| Payment | The fee, cancellation terms and documents available for an insurance claim. |
| Continuity | If you are leaving soon, ask what follow-up is possible and what records can be provided. |

## Write a short first message

Try: “I’d like to ask about an appointment in English. Could you tell me what kind of visits you offer, the next available date and the full fee?” Ask where to send any records securely once an appointment has been discussed. For other scheduled medical care, see the [Seoul English-speaking clinic guide](/blog/english-speaking-clinics-in-seoul-complete-list). If someone is in immediate danger, use emergency services instead of an appointment form.
