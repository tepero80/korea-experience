## Start with the branch receiving you

[Medical Korea’s registration guide](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem) explains the foreign-patient provider and facilitator system. Ask for the registered Korean facility name, exact branch address and current registration certificate when a booking is marketed to overseas patients. Keep the clinic and any separate agency contacts distinct.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Branch | Does the estimate name the room and branch where you will meet the clinician? |
| Clinician | Who will assess you, explain options and perform the procedure? |
| Product | What exact injection is being proposed? Can you see the patient information? |
| Aftercare | Who handles an urgent question during the stay or after your departure? |

## Compare what the appointment includes

[KHIDI guidance for foreign patients](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/notice/faqView.do?faqSn=48) covers treatment, potential side effects and estimated charges. Ask for the same details from each provider and compare what follow-up and language support are actually available on your dates.
