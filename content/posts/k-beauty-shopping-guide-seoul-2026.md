Decide whether you want to refill a list in one store or set aside time to compare shades and textures. Myeongdong and Seongsu are separate trips. Choose one as the first stop and add the other only if it serves a different need.

## Choose by what you want to buy

| Goal | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Replace products you already use | Check the exact formulation, size and name against your list. Compare the total after offers. |
| Compare shades or textures | Choose a showroom and ask what testers or booked sessions are available that day. |
| Buy gifts for several people | Compare sealed package dimensions, exchange conditions and luggage space. |

**Myeongdong.** [Olive Young’s Myeongdong Town listing](https://myeongdongtown.oliveyoung.com/en/) gives **서울특별시 중구 명동길 53**. The site describes the services available on each floor. Save the specific branch and confirm stock for any reason you are making a detour.

**Seongsu.** [Amorepacific lists AMORE Seongsu](https://design.amorepacific.com/puzzlewood/) at **서울 성동구 아차산로11길 7**. [Visit Seoul’s neighborhood guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/editorspicks/2026-Seongsu/ENNky3epf) describes a showroom where visitors can compare and test products. Ask whether the service you want is running and requires a reservation. The setting is also useful if you want to see products from several brands within Amorepacific’s range.

Before your return, check the airline’s rules for liquids and the branch’s refund and tax-refund conditions. Save anything you could not buy locally for another trip instead of spending the rest of the day crossing town. The [Seoul shopping districts guide](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam) helps you combine shopping with your existing route.
