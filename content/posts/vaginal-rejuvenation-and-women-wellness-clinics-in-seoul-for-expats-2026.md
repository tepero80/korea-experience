## Ask the clinician to name the medical concern and options

The [American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists’ patient information](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/vaginal-rejuvenation-labiaplasty-and-other-female-genital-cosmetic-surgery) explains how a marketing phrase can cover different procedures. Ask a clinician to assess the concern and explain each option. If a device is proposed, get its exact name and proposed use. The [FDA device overview](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/products-and-medical-procedures/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices) illustrates why intended use matters in US authorizations. Ask for relevant Korean product information for care in Korea.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Concern | Which symptom or concern is the clinician assessing? |
| Options | What options are appropriate to discuss, including leaving the decision until later? |
| Evidence | What benefits and limits are supported for that exact use? |
| Care | Who can follow up privately and in a language I can use after departure? |

## Give sensitive decisions time

Ask about confidential interpretation and secure records transfer. If you receive different recommendations, seek an independent opinion before changing your itinerary. The [NHS surgery-abroad guide](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/advice/cosmetic-surgery-abroad/) gives questions about responsibility for checkups and complications.
