Scalp micropigmentation uses pigment to change the appearance of the scalp. Ask who performs it and exactly what service the quote covers. If the offer includes a medical diagnosis, medicine or another medical procedure, ask who is responsible for each component and verify the institution separately.

## Verify the booking details

| Check | Ask before paying |
| --- | --- |
| Provider | Who performs the service? What is the exact address and who handles the consultation? |
| Total | What area and appointments are included? Ask about deposits, cancellation, touch-ups and extra visits. |
| Hygiene | Ask the provider to explain its hygiene procedures and to give you written instructions and a contact for concerns. |
| Follow-up | Who can respond after you leave Seoul or Korea? Ask what records you can take with you. |

## Check the rules for your booking date

The [Supreme Court’s May 21, 2026 judgment](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/precInfoP.do?precSeq=622115) concluded that the ordinary cosmetic scalp tattooing described in that case fell outside unlicensed medical practice under the former Medical Service Act. The [Tattooist Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=279291) gives a scheduled start of October 29, 2027. Ask the provider to identify which requirements apply to its actual service on your date.

If a medical facility is involved, check its information through the [Medical Korea registration guidance](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem). Ask a clinician if you have an individual health concern. Use the [Korea emergency call guide](/blog/medical-emergencies-1330-vs-119-2026) if you need urgent help during your visit.
