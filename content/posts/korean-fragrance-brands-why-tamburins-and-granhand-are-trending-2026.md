## Use the right branch names

[Tamburins lists its Samcheong branch](https://www.tamburins.com/kr/store/korea/) at 서울 종로구 율곡로3길 84. The [tourism listing for GRANHAND Gahoe](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=175458) gives 서울특별시 종로구 북촌로11길 61. Check both stores’ current terms. GRANHAND’s Gahoe branch is distinct from the shop it calls Bukchon.

| Stop | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| First shop | At Tamburins Samcheong, ask which formats you can sample. Label the strip with the name and type. |
| Next shop | At GRANHAND Gahoe, ask for one or two scents in a similar format. Use a fresh strip for each. |
| Before paying | Compare size and price for the same format. Ask about return policy and stock of the exact product. |
| Before flying | Confirm the container and packing rules for your airport and airline. |

## Give the comparison time

Save the Korean branch names on your map and check the walking route, hills and opening hours before trying both in one outing. If the first store meets your brief, keep the second optional. For testing method and a list you can carry into any branch, use the [fragrance sampling guide](/blog/korean-fragrance-boutiques-how-to-sample-scents-and-find-official-brand-stores).
