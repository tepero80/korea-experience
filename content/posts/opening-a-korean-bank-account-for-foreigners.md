List the next payment you need to make or receive. A short-stay visitor paying for meals and transport has different requirements from a resident receiving wages or paying a landlord by bank transfer. Confirm whether the merchant, employer or landlord actually requires a Korean account.

## Match the payment task to a backup

| Task | What to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Daily spending | Which cards your likely merchants take and how you will obtain won when needed |
| Pay or rent | Accepted receiving or payment rail and the date you must use it |
| Tapping or online | Which specific card or payment service the merchant accepts |
| App transfers | Accepted device, phone number, identity proof and any extra setup |

If an overseas card and access to won cover a short stay, start with the [foreign-card ATM guide](/blog/foreign-card-cash-withdrawals-in-korea-atm-labels-fees-and-fallback-options) before rearranging your trip around account opening. If you need a Korean account for a specific payment, confirm that the account can handle the amount and timing before giving out the details or committing to that payment method.

## Check each service before leaving the bank

The [Seoul financial guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/FINANCIAL_GUIDE_BOOK_FOR_FOREIG_ERS_IN_KOREA.pdf) treats ATM, internet and mobile banking as distinct services. Ask the bank whether it can issue the card you expect and how you will activate it. Ask which transfers you can perform in the app, what authentication or phone details it requires, and what to do after changing your phone number. [Seoul’s branch guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/living/convenience/1-banking/) describes a cash-card example. Ask what your selected card can actually do, including any separate online or overseas use you need.

A remote identity flow can apply different rules from a branch appointment. [Hana’s dated notice](https://www.kebhana.com/cont/news/news01/news0101/1501326_118324.jsp) is one example. If the features meet your needs, use the [branch visit checklist](/blog/opening-a-bank-account-in-korea-step-by-step-guide) to confirm the documents. If activation takes longer than expected, agree on an alternative with the payer or payee before the due date.
