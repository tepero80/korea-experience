## Match the shelf label to the dish

Three similarly sized containers may hold very different seasonings. Write down the ingredient from your recipe or save its Korean spelling so you can compare products at the shelf.

| Look for | What to use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **고추장** · gochujang | Chili paste for a dish such as bibimbap or tteokbokki. Read any extra words that describe how the paste is seasoned. |
| **된장** · doenjang | Fermented soybean paste for a dish such as doenjang jjigae. Check that the jar matches the paste in your recipe. |
| **국간장** · gukganjang | Soup soy sauce. Look for this wording when the recipe specifies a soy sauce used to season soup. |
| **쌈장** · ssamjang | A seasoned sauce for wraps. If your recipe asks for one of the other pastes, compare the label before using this in its place. |

## Pick a size you will finish

[CJ's official store](https://m.cjthemarket.com/the/product/category-product-list?ctgrId=004402) shows separate 500 g Haechandle gochujang and doenjang examples. [Sempio's soup soy sauce listing](https://www.sempio.com/market/ganjang/476) shows 500 mL and 860 mL sizes for its **한식국간장**. Compare the actual package if you want a smaller amount. For a few meals, think about how much you will use and whether the kitchen can store an opened pack as directed.

Read the complete ingredient and allergen information if you avoid certain ingredients. A heading such as **고추장** identifies the product type. Additional words and the ingredient list can identify a seasoned version that tastes different or contains ingredients you are trying to avoid.

## Shop for one meal or one souvenir

- **For the rental kitchen**, check its utensils and storage first. Use one paste and follow a recipe that calls for it before adding several staples to your basket.
- **For a food market**, [VisitKorea's shopping guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/web_book/enu/travel_guide/contents/shopping_s11.html) describes Gwangjang as an ingredient stop and shows how market specialties vary by region. Exact products depend on the seller. Buy a labeled and sealed pack if you need the ingredient information later.
- **For a flight**, check your baggage allowance and the destination's import rules before you pay. Our [guide to packing Korean purchases](/blog/packing-korean-shopping-liquids-and-souvenirs-for-your-flight) explains why pack format and destination rules both matter.
