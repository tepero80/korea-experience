## Choose by what is in the bowl

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s haejangguk guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/thingsToDo/foodTrip/special_view.do?vcontsId=178218) describes versions with beef, bean sprouts and other ingredients. Its [bean sprout and rice soup guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/thingsToDo/foodTrip/special_view.do?vcontsId=178212) describes **콩나물국밥**, a common breakfast option. Ask about stock, toppings and seasonings at the place where you order.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 해장국 | Check which version the restaurant names and ask about the base broth. |
| 콩나물국밥 | Bean sprout and rice soup. Ask about broth, egg and seafood seasonings if relevant. |
| 선지 | A term for coagulated blood. Ask staff how it is used if you want to avoid it. |
| 매운맛 | Spicy flavor. Ask if there is a milder option and whether seasoning can be served separately. |

## Show a clear question at the counter

Ask **육수에 뭐가 들어가나요?**, meaning “What goes into the stock?” Confirm ingredients with staff for an allergy or dietary restriction. Check the shop’s current hours if you are planning breakfast before a train.
