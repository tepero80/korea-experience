**Check the operating flight number and terminal on your e-ticket, then verify it again with your airline and the airport before traveling.** A familiar airline name is not enough: codeshare and operational changes can mean the terminal differs from the one you expected.

This checklist is for passengers arriving at or leaving Incheon International Airport. It is a planning aid rather than a live flight-status feed. For a codeshare, use the operating carrier and flight details, not just the seller's name.

## Before booking the transfer

1. Read the e-ticket for the **airport, operating carrier, flight number, terminal and local date**. If the booking omits any of them, ask the airline.
2. Search your flight using the airport's [flight search](https://www.airport.kr/sites/ap_en/index.do), which offers arrival and departure searches. The airport specifically warns that codeshares and airline circumstances can change the actual arrival or departure terminal.
3. Use that terminal when selecting your rail station, bus stop, taxi destination or pickup. Keep the reservation details available offline and check again nearer departure.
4. If separate flights connect at Incheon, ask both carriers about the boarding pass, bags and the route between terminals. Terminal information alone does not confirm that your connection is protected.

| What you know | What to do next |
| --- | --- |
| E-ticket shows a terminal | Check the actual flight with the airline and airport; then book transport for that terminal. |
| Airlines list different terminals | Ask the operating airline which terminal applies to your flight and check again on travel day. |
| Two separately booked flights | Confirm baggage recheck, onward boarding pass, entry eligibility and deadlines with both carriers. |
| Terminal changes after you booked transport | Contact the transport operator and airline; calculate the full new route and check-in cutoff. |

## If you have already arrived at the wrong terminal

Contact your airline immediately. Explain your flight number, where you are, and whether you still need to check baggage. Ask for the latest check-in cutoff and directions. Airport information staff can help you find your route, but they cannot promise that the airline will reopen check-in or hold the aircraft. Avoid treating an estimated transfer time as a guarantee: traffic, the next service and walking distance vary.

Before purchasing a replacement ticket, ask the airline or seller about changes, fees and your original ticket's terms. Keep confirmation of any change; a missed flight may have implications for later flights on the booking.

## On arrival: terminal versus concourse

Incheon's [arrival instructions](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1439/subview.do) distinguish passengers arriving at a passenger terminal from those arriving at the concourse, where the airport describes a shuttle train to the terminal. Follow the current signs and instructions from airport staff; the correct public meeting place depends on the actual arrival terminal.

If someone is meeting you, send the terminal and arrival flight information after you confirm them. Agree on an identifiable public meeting point and a fallback if the flight or immigration takes longer than expected. Do not send a photograph of your passport or reservation details in a public post.
