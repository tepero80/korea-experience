## Find the UV filters on the actual package

Korean shops may display **무기자차** (mineral), **유기자차** (organic, often called chemical) or **혼합자차** (a combination). Use those words to start comparing bottles, then check **전성분**, the full ingredient list. Zinc oxide is a mineral UV filter. Organic filters include ethylhexyl triazone. One ingredient name by itself does not identify every filter in a mixed product.

| What you need | What to check in the shop |
| --- | --- |
| Daily walking | The stated protection and a finish you are comfortable wearing. |
| Swimming or a sweaty hike | An explicit water-resistance statement and the use directions. |
| Layering with makeup | A shop tester, if available, to compare visible cast, shine and how it layers. |
| A cabin-only trip | The capacity printed on the tube, plus space in the airport liquids bag. |

Compare the manufacturer's list to the box. [Dr.G Green Mild Up Sun+](https://www.dr-g.com/our-products/green-mild-up-sun) lists zinc oxide. [Beauty of Joseon's Relief Sun label for Australia](https://beautyofjoseon.com/products/relief-sun-rice-probiotics?country=AU) lists several organic UV filters, including ethylhexyl triazone. These illustrate how to read label types. Check the variant on the Korean shelf before assuming the same formula.

## Match the label to the day outside

Check the protection markings and follow the application directions on the package. For water or heavy sweating, verify that the label explicitly describes water resistance. The [FDA's sunscreen guidance](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/understanding-over-counter-medicines/sunscreen-how-help-protect-your-skin-sun) explains why products can wash off and need reapplication. Add shade, a hat or other clothing when useful.

If a tester is available, compare texture and visible residue after the sunscreen settles. Apply the amount directed on the package when you go outdoors.

## Check the tube size before you leave Korea

The [Incheon Airport liquids FAQ](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1434/subview.do) lists sunscreen with gels. For an international departure, it gives a **100 mL maximum per container** and one transparent ziplock bag per person for qualifying items. The size of the container matters, including when the tube is only partly full. Check security rules at connections as well.
