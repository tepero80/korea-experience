## Read the release and the store listing separately

[Visit Seoul’s WITHMUU listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/MapoArea/WITHMUU-HONGDAE/ENPkbyxzt) identifies its official-merchandise shop on the second floor of AK PLAZA Hongdae. Its [Ktown4u COEX listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/Ktown4u-COEX/ENP0rc3u4) locates a merchandise floor at 513 Yeongdong-daero. Pick a shop near your other plans, then check whether the exact product is sold there.

| Sale type | Confirm before leaving |
| --- | --- |
| Shelf stock | Exact version, selling branch and whether the store can confirm current stock. |
| Pop-up | Visit dates, booking, QR and ID requirements, plus whether walk-ins are offered. |
| Member sale | What eligibility is required, when it is checked and whether a public sale is announced. |
| Bonus gift | Whether it comes with this version and whether any stock is left. |

HYBE’s [past V8 pop-up notice](https://weverse.io/seventeen/notice/36935) illustrates how a temporary sale can require a Weverse login, a reservation and a live entry QR. Use the current listing for your event and verify any membership requirement by name.

## Save the address and keep a nearby fallback

- WITHMUU Hongdae: **서울 마포구 양화로 188, AK플라자 2층**. [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=WITHMUU%20AK%20PLAZA%20Hongdae).
- Ktown4u COEX: **서울 강남구 영동대로 513, 코엑스아티움**. [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ktown4u%20COEX%20Artium%20Seoul).

Save the full product name or a screenshot of the listing so staff can identify the version. Confirm the price and what is included before paying. If the branch cannot confirm stock or the pop-up is full, browse ordinary stock along your existing route or use the [Myeongdong versus online comparison](/blog/k-pop-merchandise-shopping-myeongdong-vs-online) to decide whether to order later.
