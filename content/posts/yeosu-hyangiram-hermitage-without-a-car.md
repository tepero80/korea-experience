Hyangiram (향일암) is a coastal Buddhist hermitage on Dolsan, a longer outing than the Expo waterfront. A taxi can simplify the road journey, but it does not remove the uphill approach on foot. If steep steps or narrow passages are a concern, ask the site about the current route before committing to the trip.

## Plan the return before the outbound trip

| Option | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Local bus | The transfer, outbound direction and last practical return from 임포(향일암). |
| Taxi | Fare estimate and where to meet your return ride. |
| Shorter outing | Choose a nearer waterfront stop if the weather or return connection is uncertain. |

[Yeosu City’s Hyangiram page](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/hyangilam) describes an option from Yeosu Expo Station using bus 2 and a transfer to 111 at Seogyodong Catholic Church, continuing to Impo (향일암). Treat this as a route to check, not a promise of a direct bus or a current timetable: confirm the exact stop, direction, departure and return with local transit information. For a taxi, show the driver **향일암** and agree where to meet for the return.

## The final approach is part of the visit

A [Ministry of Justice travel feature](https://www.immigration.go.kr/sites/immigration/file/ebook/gongzone/2025/2025_gongzone_vol72.pdf) describes steep steps and a narrow passage between rocks. Wear shoes suited to uneven surfaces and allow time to climb and descend. There is no basis here to promise a step-free route. Keep voices low, observe photography signs and give worshippers space.

Check the [KTO listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=104697) and contact the site at **061-644-4742** for current access and opening details. Published sources differ on addresses and older admission details; verify your intended entrance before navigating. Sunrise is weather-dependent and may require a taxi before a useful bus departure. If the climb or return does not fit, choose the nearer [Odongdo outing](/blog/yeosu-expo-station-to-odongdo) instead.
