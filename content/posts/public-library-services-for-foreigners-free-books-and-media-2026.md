You can read without assuming you qualify for a lending card. On-site reading, taking books away and using licensed digital media are separate services.

## Choose a reading option

| Need | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Free indoor reading | Starfield Library at COEX; check current opening hours. |
| Outdoor reading | Seoul Outdoor Library when the programme is operating; follow the on-site instructions for returning books. |
| Take-home or digital loan | Ask the particular library about visitor eligibility and identification before relying on it. |

[VISITKOREA lists Starfield Library](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=60729) as a free reading space. Copy 서울특별시 강남구 영동대로 513 스타필드 코엑스몰 B1. It is an indoor public reading space in a mall, not a municipal lending-card guarantee.

The [city's outdoor library guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-outdoor-library/) says anyone visiting Seoul Plaza can read the curated books there; check current dates and weather arrangements. The city's [2025 lending announcement](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-metropolitan-library-expands-from-citywide-to-nationwide-loans/) discusses citizens and foreign residents, and does not establish that a short-term tourist can borrow. Ask staff about eligibility, available languages and media access before registering.

For a central indoor stop and directions, see [Seoul City Hall Library](/blog/seoul-city-hall-library-indoor-visit).
