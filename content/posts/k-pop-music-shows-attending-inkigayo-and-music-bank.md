## Identify the program and recording

KBS publishes mobile audience ticket guidance and lists Music Bank. SBS maintains a separate Inkigayo audience information page. For performer-specific prerecordings, check that performer’s current entry notice as well. An application for one audience does not establish entry to the other.

| What you found | Next check |
| --- | --- |
| TV show notice | Confirm the episode date, account rules, selection announcement and accepted proof of identity. |
| Artist notice | Read the fan-club or purchase conditions, check-in place and the required arrival window. |
| Fan repost | Open the broadcaster or artist original and check for amendments. |
| No ticket yet | Plan a different activity. An application or screenshot of a fan post is not access. |

## Save the confirmation in one place

Record the venue, check-in location, arrival time and name used to apply. Look up the venue with its Korean name and check your journey home. If you change your phone or account, verify how you will display the ticket before the day.
