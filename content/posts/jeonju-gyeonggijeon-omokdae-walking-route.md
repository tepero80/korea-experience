**Visit Gyeonggijeon first if going inside matters.** Then walk east along Taejo-ro and add Omokdae only if the hill and conditions suit you. For a shorter outing, stay around the shrine, Jeondong Cathedral and Pungnammun and finish with a meal near Nambu Market.

Set aside **90 minutes for a short shrine-and-streets visit**, or **about two to three hours with the hill and a longer break** as planning allowances. Add travel from Jeonju Station and any queue separately. The [city’s walking map](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/images/file/map/05_mapWalking.pdf) shows the shrine, Taejo-ro, the crafts exhibition hall and Omokdae in sequence.

## Check last admission before choosing the hill

The [municipal Gyeonggijeon listing](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000003&dataSid=10115&menuCd=DOM_000000101006001000) gives the following routine hours. Check the listing and temporary notices for your date. Its standard non-resident adult individual admission is **₩3,000**.

| Months | Posted hours and last admission |
| --- | --- |
| March–May, September–October | 09:00–19:00 · **Last entry 18:00** |
| June–August | 09:00–20:00 · **Last entry 19:00** |
| November–February | 09:00–18:00 · **Last entry 17:00** |

## Walk in this order

1. **Gyeonggijeon 경기전.** Save **전북특별자치도 전주시 완산구 태조로 44**. Start with the shrine inside the compound. If seeing a portrait or exhibition is the main point for you, check what is on view before you assume it is included on your date. [Official shrine information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=108728)
2. **Jeondong Cathedral 전주전동성당.** Decide whether to stay outside or enter only when visitor access is permitted. The [KTO visitor listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=108717) is separate from Gyeonggijeon’s admission schedule. Respect worship and any limits on photography.
3. **Taejo-ro 태조로 to Omokdae 오목대.** Follow the city map along Taejo-ro toward the crafts area, then check the signed climb. The [city’s Omokdae page](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000003&dataSid=9742&menuCd=DOM_000000101006001000) places it on a hill southeast of the shrine. Decide whether to continue before the ascent, especially in rain or near dusk.

## Late, wet or avoiding the climb?

- **Past last admission:** use the exterior heritage streets and save the ticketed shrine for another day. A nighttime event has its own schedule and ticket.
- **Wet or icy approach:** skip the hill if footing is poor. Stay on the main village streets or choose a confirmed indoor stop. Ask the tourism desk about the exact approach if slopes, curbs or surfaces matter.
- **Short on time:** finish near Pungnammun and choose one stop from the [Nambu Market eating plan](/blog/jeonju-nambu-market-eating-plan). Allow for the walk and time before a booked train.

For the start and return legs, see [which bus or taxi to take from Jeonju Station](/blog/jeonju-station-to-hanok-village). Keep your chosen village endpoint and ticket cutoff in the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
