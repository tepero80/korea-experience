If your first flight lands at **Incheon (ICN)** and your next flight leaves from **Gimpo (GMP)**, you need to leave Incheon and travel to a different airport. For a domestic departure, your destination is **Gimpo Domestic Terminal (김포공항 국내선청사)**. Take the **AREX All Stop train (일반열차)** when its timetable fits your connection. The AREX Express to Seoul Station skips Gimpo.

## Check the airport codes before buying tickets

| What your booking shows | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Arrival at **ICN**, next departure from **GMP** | Plan an airport change. Confirm Korea entry requirements for your trip, who handles checked bags, and what happens if the first flight arrives late. |
| Both flights use **ICN** | Follow your airline’s Incheon connection instructions. Some [transit-only domestic flights](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1467/subview.do) use special procedures within Incheon. |
| Separate bookings and a tight window | Ask the onward carrier about change options before buying or before leaving Incheon. A train departure can fit your schedule while the airline’s bag-drop deadline does not. |

Check the actual terminal and operating carrier on each ticket, including codeshares. For a self-transfer with checked baggage, plan to **collect and recheck your bags** unless your airline expressly confirms another arrangement. [Incheon’s arrival guide](https://iasc.airport.kr/ap_en/1439/subview.do) shows immigration, the first-floor baggage claim and the route into the arrivals hall. Confirm the entry requirements that apply to your passport before booking an airport change.

## Take AREX All Stop from the terminal where you land

1. Once you reach public arrivals, follow **Airport Railroad (공항철도)** to the **B1 Transportation Center** at your own terminal. [Incheon’s rail directions](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1511/subview.do) cover both terminals. You can start from T1 or T2 without transferring terminals first.
2. Look for **All Stop (일반열차)** on the Seoul-bound side and confirm **Gimpo Airport (김포공항)** on the stopping pattern. Seoul’s [official airport rail overview](https://english.visitseoul.net/airport-to-seoul) shows Gimpo on the All Stop route. Keep your fare card or ticket to exit the station.
3. At **Gimpo Airport Station (김포공항역)**, follow **Domestic Terminal (국내선)** signs. You do not need to transfer to subway Line 5 or 9. The [Gimpo airport operator](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimpoeng/cms/frCon/index.do?MENU_ID=410) describes the rail access on the west side of the domestic terminal’s first floor. Allow time to reach your airline’s counter after leaving the train.

Open the AREX operator’s timetable for [Terminal 1](https://www.airportrailroad.com/train/normal/info/100/0) or [Terminal 2](https://www.airportrailroad.com/train/normal/info/110/0). Pick the weekday (**평일**) or holiday (**휴일**) tab and look under **서울역 방면** for trains heading toward Seoul Station. The timetable also displays Express trains, so identify an **All Stop** departure and confirm **김포공항** on its stopping pattern. Base your choice on when you reach the station after arrivals.

## Set a taxi cutoff before you land

Work backward from the operating airline’s published deadline. Leave time for **the domestic terminal walk, check-in or bag drop, security, and reaching your gate**. The [Gimpo domestic boarding guide](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimpoeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=1&MENU_ID=220) shows the sequence and identification checks. Check each deadline with your own airline and keep your passport accessible. There is no universal connection window for this airport change.

| Situation after you collect your bags | Next move |
| --- | --- |
| All Stop service leaves time for the walk and airline deadline | Take the train and check the terminal signs at Gimpo. |
| You have bulky luggage or the next train is too late | Check a taxi estimate and traffic from your actual Incheon terminal. Compare that arrival time with your airline deadline before you commit. |
| Neither option leaves a workable window | Contact the onward airline about a later flight or change options before buying a new ticket or paying for a ride you may not be able to use. |

Use an [official taxi stand at Incheon](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1515/subview.do). Show the driver **김포공항 국내선청사로 가 주세요** (“Please take me to Gimpo Airport Domestic Terminal”). Save the terminal name and your airline’s contact details offline. The map marks the domestic terminal building and the two origin stations. Follow current airport signs for gates and pickup points.

If all your connections actually stay at Incheon, use the [Incheon terminal and flight check](/blog/incheon-terminal-flight-check). If you are spending the night in Seoul after a delayed arrival, switch to the [late-night Incheon arrival guide](/blog/incheon-airport-late-night-arrival-guide) before confirming a new flight or hotel.
