## Choose the station before you travel

| Place | Station and approach |
| --- | --- |
| Goto Mall | **Express Bus Terminal Station** (고속터미널역), Lines 3, 7 and 9. [Visit Seoul lists Exit 8-1](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Goto-Mall/ENP029546). Save 고투몰 or 강남터미널 지하도상가. |
| Gangnam Station arcade | **Gangnam Station** (강남역), Line 2 and the Shinbundang Line. Follow the [listed Exit 8 approach](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/Gangnam-Station-Underground-Shopping-Center/ENP017740) for the arcade under Gangnam-daero. |

## Make the shopping stop fit your trip

- Visit Seoul lists Goto Mall’s regular hours as 10:00–22:00 and reports Lunar New Year and Chuseok closures. Check the day and shop separately. The photo shows a closed corridor.
- If you have an intercity bus, first find the **terminal and boarding area printed on your ticket**. The [Central City information](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/Central-City-Terminal/ENP029548) is a separate point of reference. Keep enough time to walk from your chosen mall exit back to boarding.
- If you need a particular size or return policy, ask before paying. Leave a market or department-store fallback in your route if the trip is mainly for one item.
