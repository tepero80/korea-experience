**서울역 means Seoul Station. 용산역 means Yongsan Station.** Check the departure station and train number before deciding where to go. [Korail’s official search](https://smart.letskorail.com/ebizbf/EbizBfTicketSearchM.do?hidJobDv=NRM&lang=EN) can show the actual service for your route and date.

| Your ticket or next leg | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Seoul Station | Use **서울특별시 용산구 한강대로 405**. If arriving by AREX, follow signs to the intercity rail concourse and allow time for the indoor transfer. |
| Yongsan Station | Use **서울특별시 용산구 한강대로23길 55**. Plan a separate connection if you are at Seoul Station. |
| Booking now | Compare the exact services first and include the hotel or airport-to-station trip in the decision. |

## Compare a separate transfer when the names differ

From Seoul Station, check a southbound **Line 1** service that stops at **Yongsan**. The route can suit travelers with compact luggage. Compare a taxi if station access, bags or time require it. [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/other/I-Park-Mall/ENP001702) confirms that Yongsan has Line 1 and Gyeongui–Jungang access. The city’s [station addresses](https://english.visitseoul.net/essential-Info-article/Tourist-Information-Centers_/396) identify which complex to use. If changing from AREX to a train at the other station, allow time to exit, make the city connection and reach the intercity departure board.

The [Jeonju tourism guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=229053) uses a Yongsan departure as one example. Check Korail for the station on your exact service before buying. If you are already at the wrong station and the train is close, ask staff to compare your options promptly.
