## Pick the entrance for the scene you want

| What you want | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Pavilion and pond | **Baekyangsa, Jangseong.** The [VisitKorea temple listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=104966) describes the approach to Ssanggyeru Pavilion. A short out-and-back gives you time by the pond. |
| The main Naejangsan approach near Jeongeup | Start with the **Jeongeup side** of the park on the [National Park Service map](https://www.knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/visitCourseMain.do?menuNo=7020087&parkId=120600). Pin the exact entrance and check the transfer and return from there. |

## Check two updates before booking

1. Compare the latest dated foliage photos from the [park](https://www.knps.or.kr/front/portal/visit/visitCourseMain.do?menuNo=7020087&parkId=120600) and the [weather agency’s seasonal observations](https://www.weather.go.kr/w/weather/seasonal.do). Find the place and date actually shown.
2. Read the park’s current notices. The [November 23 update](https://www.knps.or.kr/front/portal/open/pnewsDtl.do?menuNo=8000517&pnewsGrpCd=PNE01&pnewsId=PNEWSM035524&searchParkId=120600) illustrates how restrictions for specific paths can change. Confirm the public approach and any trail extension separately.
3. Check your trip back from the exact side of the park. Save the transport information before heading into an area with weak reception.

An ordinary weekday outside a holiday is a planning preference. Expect queues if color or weather draws visitors, and leave room to turn back. If your base is Gwangju and the outdoor plan no longer works, choose a separate [Gwangju National Museum visit](/blog/gwangju-national-museum-children-visit).
