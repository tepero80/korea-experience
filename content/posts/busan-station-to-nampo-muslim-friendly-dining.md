**For a Muslim-friendly meal between Busan Station and Nampo, choose the stop that fits your bags and schedule, then call that specific restaurant before going.** The Korea Tourism Organization lists two New Little India branches on this route. They are starting points for the search. Confirm current operation, the dishes you want and any other requirements directly before making plans. [Korea Tourism Organization restaurant guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/asis/upload/Muslim-friendly_restaurants.pdf)

## Choose the stop that fits your day

| If you are | What to save |
| --- | --- |
| Arriving by train | **뉴리틀인디아 부산역점**, 부산 동구 중앙대로 205. Call [+82 51 467 4127](tel:+82514674127). Ask when they can serve you before a departure. |
| Spending the evening near BIFF Square | **뉴리틀인디아 남포점**, 부산 중구 비프광장로 33-1. Call [+82 51 245 4127](tel:+82512454127). Ask for last order, the floor and the building entrance. |
| Working around prayer time | Ask the individual branch whether you can use a prayer space during your visit. Keep a separate plan if the branch cannot confirm access. |

Share the full branch name and address with your group. If you use a lift or have luggage, ask which entrance to use and whether you can reach your table with your bags.

## Take Line 1 toward the old port

From the KTX terminal, follow signs for **도시철도 1호선** to the separate Busan Station metro entrance. Take Line 1 toward **다대포해수욕장** (Dadaepo Beach). The train stops at **중앙** (Jungang), **남포** (Nampo) and **자갈치** (Jagalchi) in that order. Jagalchi is useful for the BIFF Square side of the neighborhood. Check the walking route from your chosen station and save the return toward Busan Station using the official timetable and your local map. [Busan Transportation Corporation timetable](https://www2.humetro.busan.kr/homepage/english/stationtime/page/view.do?menu_no=10060102) · [Visit Busan’s downtown route](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000202012001000&uc_seq=2809)

## Check the dish and keep a fallback

In the Korea Tourism Organization’s system, **Muslim Friendly** means some halal dishes are available and alcohol may be sold. The directory has a separate certified category. Ask staff whether the specific dish suits your requirements, including the meat source, sauces and shared preparation. If you require a certificate, ask to see the current certificate. A busy market is a good place to explore if you already have dinner arranged. Choose a packaged snack you can check or a meal booked elsewhere when neither branch can confirm what you need. [Korea Tourism Organization category definitions](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/thingsToDo/subMuslimFriendly.do)

If you are still deciding where to sleep, [compare Nampo with Seomyeon and Haeundae](/blog/where-to-stay-in-busan-neighborhoods). This meal plan is most convenient when the old port is already part of your route.
