**Start with the reason for the appointment and the language you need.** A cosmetic menu and an English booking page do not tell you whether a branch provides the assessment you want. Ask who will see you and how you will understand the consultation, consent and follow-up.

## Send the right inquiry

| You need to establish | Ask the branch                                                             |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Service               | Does this department assess the concern I am contacting you about?         |
| Clinician             | Who is the assessing clinician, and what are their qualifications?         |
| Language              | Is the clinician fluent in my language, or is an interpreter arranged?     |
| Scope                 | Is interpretation available for consultation, consent and follow-up?       |
| Price                 | What is the consultation fee, and are interpreter or review fees separate? |
| Timing                | Is the appointment confirmed, and is any referral or preparation needed?   |

Share symptoms and records privately with the provider. This guide cannot decide which specialty you personally need. If the provider cannot assess your concern, ask about the appropriate service rather than buying a cosmetic package instead.

## Check the provider's identity

Search the Korean name and branch through [Medical Korea's registered-provider directory](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredhospitals) and read the [registration system's scope](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem). Use the operator's own address and booking channel. A registration listing is not a personal clinical recommendation, and we do not turn unverified online ratings into a ranking.

Save the written confirmation and Korean address before travelling. Keep a direct return route and some schedule flexibility until any follow-up is clear. The [consultation booking guide](/blog/booking-medical-consultations-in-korea-online-vs-walk-in) includes a message you can adapt.

## Keep medical and retail costs separate

Obtain a full written quote rather than comparing promotional starting prices. The [current cosmetic-medical VAT provision](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1030626475) specifies eligible services through 31 December 2025; do not assume a 2026 refund. Buying retail skincare products is a different tax-refund question.

For non-emergency service inquiries, [Medical Korea](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient) is an official information route. For emergencies use [119 for ambulance/fire or 112 for police](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145).
