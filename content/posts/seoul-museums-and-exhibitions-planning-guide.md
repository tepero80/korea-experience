**Choose what you want to understand, not the largest number of museums.** One suitable collection with enough time to look, rest and have a meal usually makes a more usable day than several rushed admissions.

## Decide the type of visit

| You want… | Confirm |
| --- | --- |
| A general collection visit | Permanent-gallery access, closures and the areas you care about |
| A particular exhibition | Current run, ticket price, timed entry and cancellation |
| A children's activity | Exact session, age conditions and booking confirmation |
| A weather backup | Actual opening plus the exposed entrance/transport legs |

Free general admission does not automatically include a special exhibition or reserve a children's session.

## Worked example: National Museum of Korea

The [museum's visitor information](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0101010000.do) currently lists:

| Visit detail | Listed condition |
| --- | --- |
| Regular hours | 09:30–17:30 Monday/Tuesday/Thursday/Friday/Sunday |
| Extended hours | 09:30–21:00 Wednesday/Saturday |
| Last entry | 30 minutes before closing |
| Admission | Permanent galleries free; charged special exhibitions and reserved Children's Museum separate |
| Closures | Check January 1, Seollal, Chuseok and quarterly first-Monday gallery maintenance notices |

The first-Monday gallery dates normally concern March, June, September and December; the operator lists a March 2026 exception. Read the actual notice for your date rather than applying a pattern blindly.

## Choose the correct building and session

For a craft-focused visit near Anguk, use the [Seoul Museum of Craft Art visitor guide](https://craftmuseum.seoul.go.kr/preview/visit). It lists ordinary galleries **10:00–18:00**, with last entry 30 minutes before closing. Its Children's Museum requires online advance booking and closes **every Monday**, including Mondays when the general museum's public-holiday exception applies. General viewing is free; a separately hosted exhibition may charge.

The checked expanded evening opening ends **30 November 2026** and varies by building and weekday. Read the actual timetable rather than carrying an evening assumption into a December visit. Decide which collection or activity you want, then confirm that exact building and session.

## Make the visit comfortable

Allow a few hours as an editorial starting block, choose priorities and add a rest. Check photography/publication rules before taking or sharing images; NMK's visitor information explicitly restricts commercial photography and some equipment.

### Access and language help at the National Museum

| What the operator confirms | What still needs checking for your visit |
| --- | --- |
| Its [subway directions](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0105010000.do) identify Ichon Station, Exit 2 and an elevator toward Exits 1/2 | The lift's current operation, your exact route and any assistance requirements |
| The visitor page lists free wheelchairs/strollers in the Great Hall; strollers are for children up to 15 kg | Equipment availability and suitability; the listing is not a reservation |
| The directions page lists Korean/English visitor information at +82-2-2077-9046 | Current support and arrangements; contact before leaving if assistance is essential |

Use the [museum contact page](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0609000000.do) if your question needs the operator. This confirms specific published services, not an independently audited continuous step-free journey from every hotel or station exit.
