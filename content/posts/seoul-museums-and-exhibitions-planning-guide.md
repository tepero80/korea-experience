Use the National Museum for a broad history block or the Craft Museum for a focused craft visit around Jongno. Give one museum a proper block and add a nearby meal; special exhibitions may have different tickets from the permanent collection.

## Choose a collection and neighborhood

Pick one substantial collection or exhibition rather than several distant museums. Start with art, history or crafts and check travel from your actual accommodation. Museums differ in rooms, seating and reservation needs.

## Separate permanent and special admission

General collection access can differ from paid exhibitions and children's sessions. Check the actual exhibition page for ticket, date, language and age conditions. Free entry does not mean every event is unreserved.

**Check the operator:** [National Museum of Korea visitor information](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0101010000.do) · [Buk-Seoul Museum of Art official visitor guide](https://sema.seoul.go.kr/en/visit/bukseoul).

## Read last admission and closures

Use the official venue's schedule, allowing for last entry, holidays and installation closures. NMK and SeMA branches publish different patterns. Never apply one branch's timetable to the whole institution.

## Plan a comfortable visit

Check lockers, coat storage, seating, lifts and stroller or wheelchair services. Verify bag and photography rules. Facilities listed for another museum do not establish what is available at your destination.

## Add one nearby rest stop

Combine the visit with a verified food, tea or craft stop in the same area. Allow time for travel and last orders. Skip the optional stop if the exhibition fills the afternoon.

## Check rights before publishing pictures

Visitor photography permission is not automatically commercial publication permission. Use individually licensed images or written authorization. Do not download museum promotional photographs without checking their terms.

## Before you go

- Confirm the exact branch
- Check exhibition tickets
- Check closures and last entry
- Review facilities
- Read photography permissions
- Choose a nearby rest stop

Save the current operator notice and your own booking conditions offline. Recheck any dated information before paying or traveling.
