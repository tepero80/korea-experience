Bring a shortlist and compare the **exact size, number of items and total price** on the shelf. Before paying, ask the branch whether it offers an immediate tourist refund or issues paperwork for a later claim. Keep your passport and receipts available.

## Choose a branch that fits your day

If you are already in central Seoul, start with [KTO's Olive Young Myeongdong Town listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=263&menuSn=929&vcontsId=248785). The shop is on floors 1–2 at **53 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul**. Save **서울 중구 명동길 53** for your map app and check current hours before going. For a quick restock, try a branch near a stop you already plan to visit. Confirm stock with the branch if a specific product is your only reason for going.

A product listing or coupon in the global shop may describe a different sale. The [Olive Young Global membership page](https://global.oliveyoung.com/display/page/membership) describes benefits for its global shop. Check the shelf label, current store promotion and final total at the Korean store rather than assuming an online discount applies.

## Make a small list before entering

| If you want | Check in store |
| --- | --- |
| A product you already use | Exact name, formula or shade, and size. Packaging can look similar across versions. |
| A promotion set | Which products and sizes it contains, whether the extra item is one you need, and the price per usable item. |
| A gift | Whether the packaging travels well. Leave room for airport baggage and liquid rules. |
| A new product | Read the label and choose one item you can compare with what you already use. |

## At checkout, identify the refund route

Ask before paying whether the store participates and which route it offers. The [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=140736) lists **₩15,000 or more per qualifying receipt**. Its immediate refund rules set a payment limit **below ₩1,000,000** and a qualifying trip total of **₩5,000,000 or less**. Visitor eligibility and seller participation also matter.

- **Refund deducted at checkout.** Present your passport when asked and keep the receipt. The same claim is not paid a second time at the airport.
- **Refund paperwork issued.** Keep the documents and the unused goods. Check the [Incheon Airport tax refund steps](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) before packing, especially for goods going in a checked bag.
- **Unsure what you were given.** Ask the cashier to show whether a refund already changed the total or whether you need a later claim. Confirm the branch's exchange or return conditions before using the product.
