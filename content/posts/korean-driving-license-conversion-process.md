## Check the original licence and issuing authority

The [Korean KoROAD exchange page](https://www.safedriving.or.kr/diGuide/selectDiGuide05.do) describes exchange for a valid full licence issued by a competent foreign authority. It lists embassy confirmation or an apostille as forms of authentication, with conditions. Contact the exchange office about the specific document you plan to obtain before paying.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Licence | Ask whether your original licence and its class qualify for exchange and what class would be issued. |
| Proof | Confirm the accepted issuer, document format, date and whether a translation is required. |
| Bring | Ask about your passport, residence card, travel records and photos, and what the office will keep. |
| Tests | Confirm which medical checks or written tests apply to the issuing jurisdiction. |

If you have moved since your residence card was issued, use the [foreign resident address report guide](/blog/how-to-register-your-foreign-address-change-online-and-offline-2026) to check which documents the reporting service accepts.

## Call the office before the visit

The [English KoROAD overview](https://www.safedriving.or.kr/enGuide/selectEnGuide22.do) explains the general review and examination sequence. Confirm details with the selected office using the current Korean page if wording differs. Ask whether you must apply in person and when or how the original licence can be returned if it is retained.

See the [Korean licence options guide](/blog/getting-a-korean-driver-license-as-a-foreigner-2026) to find the new-licence process if exchange is unavailable or check the separate visitor rental route.
