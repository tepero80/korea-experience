**If you want a short lakeside walk in Gyeongju, start at Bomun Lakeside Plaza and turn back along the shore.** Save **보문호반광장**, **경주시 보문로 424-33**. Choose the full walk if you have time for about 8 km, plus breaks and the ride back. Gyeongju City suggests starting the full circuit near Donggungwon.

## Choose one route before going

| Time | Best fit |
| --- | --- |
| Short outing | Start at Bomun Lakeside Plaza and walk toward the water performance area. Return along the same stretch. |
| Longer walk | Use the city’s full circuit from Donggungwon. Allow about two hours of walking plus time for stops. |
| Wind, rain or ice | Make the plaza your turnaround point. Stay on open paths and leave longer bridge and deck sections for a different day. |

The city’s itinerary describes the shore section between the water performance area and the plaza as roughly twenty minutes one way. Going from the plaza, you can walk part of that stretch in reverse and turn at a place that suits your group. Allow time for the same ground on the return. The published walking time excludes stops. [Gyeongju City’s lake walking route](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=4740)

## Use the plaza to find the short route

Open pedestrian directions to **보문호반광장**. Check for the plaza’s street address when the map offers results for the whole lake or a hotel. On arrival, find the public path by the lake and use the plaza as your reference point. If you are coming from downtown, compare bus and taxi routes to the plaza before leaving. Check the road stop and direction for the journey back. ## Give the full loop its own time

For the loop, save **경주동궁원**, **경주시 보문로 74-14**. This is the garden by Bomun Lake. The downtown palace and Wolji Pond use a separate address. The city route describes the nearby **물너울교** bridge and follows the shore past several named landmarks, including Bomun Lakeside Plaza and **호반1교**. Read the route in advance and follow posted detours. A bridge, raised deck or path by the water may require a change of plan. If an uninterrupted step-free route matters, check the section you intend to use and plan an easy return.

The lake lies in the Bomun resort area. In a single afternoon, a short walk leaves room for a ride back and a downtown stop. Make the longer loop the main outing, especially if you intend to stop for food or visit an attraction along the way. Check the route from the correct station if you are coming by train.
