Start with an indoor gallery at **국립익산박물관**. The finds give you some context before you walk out to the large, open temple grounds. The photograph shows the restored pagoda at Mireuksa, beside the museum.

| What to check | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| General museum admission | The [museum operator](https://iksan.museum.go.kr/eng/html/sub01/0101.html) lists free admission, **09:00–18:00**, with final entry at **17:30** on open days. Check Monday and holiday closures before you travel. |
| Children’s museum | Check the separate reservation link on the operator’s site if this is part of your plan. |
| From Iksan Station | The [official directions](https://search.culture.go.kr/kor/html/sub01/0104.html) list buses **41 and 41-1**. Check today’s stop, direction and return, or compare a taxi. |

## Walk out to the pagoda

Reserve daylight to see the pagoda and the scale of the former compound. Ask locally about uneven or closed sections if your walk depends on step-free surfaces. If rain is heavy, leave more time for the museum and only walk outside when conditions suit. Museum opening and outdoor access have separate checks.

See the [Iksan hub](/destinations/iksan) before pairing this with Wanggung-ri. Use a point-to-point route that you can confirm during your visit.
