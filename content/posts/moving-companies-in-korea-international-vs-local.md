## Make every quote use the same information

List the large and fragile items, estimated number of boxes, stairs, elevator access and where a vehicle can stop at both addresses. Ask how the mover verifies the inventory and access assumptions. For a move within Korea, [Korea Consumer Agency guidance](https://www.kca.go.kr/home/sub.do?menukey=4005&mode=view&no=1004458186) recommends checking whether the company has the appropriate permit and verifying vehicle and staffing details in the quote.

| Quote detail | What to ask for in writing |
| --- | --- |
| Packing and labor | Who supplies boxes, packs, carries, and reassembles furniture? |
| Building access | Do stairs, a lift reservation or a longer carry change the quote? |
| Delivery | Where does the mover hand over the goods? Who covers storage if the address or date changes? |
| Damage or loss | Who handles claims? What is covered, excluded or subject to a deadline? |

## For an overseas move, name each handoff

[Korea Customs Service's shipping guide](https://customs.go.kr/kcs/ad/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=8454&mi=8454) distinguishes port delivery from delivery to the residence and work inside the home. Ask what the quoted term means on your contract, including unloading, unpacking and any destination charges. Write down who receives your shipment in Korea and who contacts you if customs or delivery is delayed.

[The English customs guide for household effects](https://www.customs.go.kr/english/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=5505&mi=10804) lists documents such as the packing list and bill of lading and explains that some goods are dutiable. Confirm with Customs and the shipment handler what applies to your items and timing before packing. Ask who sends each document and how to correct the inventory before it is submitted.

## Check delivery against your inventory

Save the written quote and inventory, and photograph the condition of fragile or valuable items. At delivery, check the count and visible condition with the crew. Record any missing item or damage immediately, keep the paperwork and use the claim contact and deadline on your agreement.

If the address or access date is still uncertain, ask what holding the shipment would cost and who would be responsible for arranging it. Keep the items you need on arrival with you and use the [first-week arrival checklist](/blog/moving-to-korea-checklist-everything-you-need-2026) to plan the gap.
