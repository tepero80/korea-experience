**On an ordinary opening day, use a regular palace ticket at Changgyeonggung's Honghwamun gate. Last entry is 20:00 and closing is 21:00.** The night route covers selected grounds. If you want to go inside the greenhouse from December through February, plan to arrive in time to reach it before its 18:00 evening closure.

## Check that it is a regular evening

The [official English admission page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E702000000.do?schGroupCode=cgg&schGroupCodeNm=Changgyeonggung%3Cbr%3EPalace) lists a regular opening day from 09:00 to 21:00 with final entry at 20:00, normal Monday closure, and a **₩1,000 individual fee for foreign adults ages 19–64**. Use the operator's current fee table for other ages and exemptions. A special program can use a different ticket and suspend ordinary evening visits, as in the [operator's dated November 2026 example](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R403000000.do?id=20260902131858531105&schBcid=notice01&schM=view). The [current route notice, updated November 20, 2026](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R403000000.do?id=20260227165959365512&schM=view), lists restricted restoration areas and the daytime cutoffs for palace connections.

| What you want to see | How to time your visit |
| --- | --- |
| **Evening grounds only** | Aim to arrive before 19:30 for a buffer before last entry. Follow the open route signed from the main gate. |
| **Daeonsil in winter** | From December through February, reach the greenhouse before 18:00. Confirm any temporary closure before departure. |
| **Day sections and the night route** | Enter during daylight. The [operator's seasonal table](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do) gives the time when visitors move into the evening area. |
| **Secret Garden** | Visit Changdeokgung by day under its separate last-entry and ticket rules. The connecting gate has its own cutoff. |

## Navigate from Honghwamun

Save **Honghwamun (홍화문)** or **Changgyeonggung (창경궁)** at **서울 종로구 창경궁로 185**. Search for the [named gate in Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%B0%BD%EA%B2%BD%EA%B6%81%20%ED%99%8D%ED%99%94%EB%AC%B8%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%B0%BD%EA%B2%BD%EA%B6%81%EB%A1%9C%20185) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%B0%BD%EA%B2%BD%EA%B6%81%20%ED%99%8D%ED%99%94%EB%AC%B8%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%B0%BD%EA%B2%BD%EA%B6%81%EB%A1%9C%20185) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%B0%BD%EA%B2%BD%EA%B6%81%20%ED%99%8D%ED%99%94%EB%AC%B8%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%B0%BD%EA%B2%BD%EA%B6%81%EB%A1%9C%20185)). The [palace directions](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R707000000.do?schGroupCode=cgg) name Hyehwa Station, Line 4, Exit 4. If the map offers several palace gates, use Honghwamun for this plan. The connection to Changdeokgung is a daytime option with a seasonal cutoff explained in the [palace FAQ](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R404000000.do?schGroupCode=cgg).

Inside, follow signs toward **Myeongjeongjeon** and **Chundangji** before deciding whether to continue to **Daeonsil**. The [published night-area table](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do) also includes **Tongmyeongjeon** and the greenhouse precinct. Posted directions take priority where a path is blocked. If you are mainly going for the greenhouse, work backward from that interior cutoff instead of the palace closing time.

If a notice suspends regular admission, check [Deoksugung and SeMA](/blog/deoksugung-to-seoul-museum-of-art) for a central Seoul alternative. If you want a specifically ticketed night event, use the [palace night program comparison](/blog/palace-starlight-night-tours-2026) and match the palace name and event date before making a booking.
