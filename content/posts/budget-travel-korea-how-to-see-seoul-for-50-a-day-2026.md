Start by pricing one realistic day around where you sleep. Leave your total in won until you check the exchange rate for your own payment method. A low activity subtotal still needs food, a return trip and a room if those belong in your budget.

## Compare two outings

| One adult | Cost example |
| --- | --- |
| Palace and a walk | **₩6,100** for two subway base fares and Gyeongbokgung admission. |
| National Museum of Korea | **₩3,100** for two subway base fares. The permanent galleries are free. |
| Four separate subway rides | **₩6,200** for four base fares on a regular card you already own. |

Seoul City lists [₩1,550 as the adult subway card base fare](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/subway/). These examples assume independent base-distance trips and exclude any card purchase, distance surcharges and transfer discounts. A transfer can be part of the same priced journey. Count the actual trips and check both directions.

At the time of review the [Climate Card one-day pass](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/climate-card/) is ₩5,000. Add the listed ₩3,000 purchase cost if you need a new physical card. Four covered base-fare trips would cost ₩6,200 at regular adult card prices, compared with ₩5,000 with an existing Climate Card or ₩8,000 with a newly purchased physical one. Check the route, start date and exclusions first.

## Add the costs you control

- **Admission.** The [palace fare table](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R703000000.do) lists ₩3,000 for a foreign adult age 19–64. Check the [palace calendar](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E702000000.do) before leaving. The [museum’s visitor page](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0101010000.do) separates free permanent galleries from charged special exhibitions and lists closure information.
- **Meals.** Pick a breakfast, lunch and dinner option near your route. Use posted menu prices and check ordering minimums or add-ons before setting your allowance.
- **Room and arrival.** Add your confirmed room total per night and split it by the number sharing only if that matches your booking. Price the airport ride, luggage storage and any late-night fallback separately.
