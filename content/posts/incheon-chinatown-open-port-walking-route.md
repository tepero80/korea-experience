**Start at Incheon Station Exit 1 and walk through Chinatown to the Open Port streets and Incheon Art Platform.** Pick the Jajangmyeon Museum for food history or the Incheon Open Port Museum for the port and railway story. Finish at Sinpo International Market if you want a second food stop, or turn back to the station. Save Jayu Park for a dry day when everyone is comfortable with an uphill walk.

This route is for a first visit to Incheon's mainland old town. Allow about **2–3 hours on site** for a museum, street stops and a food break. That is a planning estimate. Add your own train rides, meal wait and time for the hill or market extension. The museum hours and local route references were checked on **23 November 2026**.

## Choose your route before leaving the station

| Your plan | Walk in this order | Where to stop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Have 60–90 minutes | Incheon Station → Chinatown gate → one nearby stop → Incheon Station | Choose street views or one museum. Save the market and hill for later. |
| Have a half-day | Incheon Station → Chinatown → one museum → Art Platform → optional Sinpo Market | Return from Incheon Station for Line 1, or check whether the Suin–Bundang service from Sinpo fits your journey. |
| Rain, ice or limited walking | Incheon Station or taxi drop-off → one confirmed indoor venue → meal nearby | Keep Jayu Park and the historic boundary stairs outside this plan. Confirm the entrance and return pickup in advance. |

The [city's Open Port walking guide](https://eng-itour.incheon.go.kr/cmn/board/BBSMSTR_000000000080/2467bbsDetail.do) places Chinatown, the museums and the Art Platform on one route. The time allowances above give you room to stop and adapt.

### Start at the Chinatown gate

Use [Incheon Station Exit 1](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%EC%97%AD%201%EB%B2%88%20%EC%B6%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%EC%97%AD%201%EB%B2%88%20%EC%B6%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%EC%97%AD%201%EB%B2%88%20%EC%B6%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC)), **인천역 1번 출구**. Both metropolitan subway Line 1 and the Suin–Bundang Line serve Incheon Station. From Exit 1, use the pedestrian crossing toward the Chinatown gateway. If coming from Seoul on Line 1, check that the train is going to **인천** before boarding. [Incheon Art Platform directions](https://inartplatform.kr/intro/location), [VISITKOREA Chinatown and museum approach](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221247)

Use **제물포구** when copying a current address. Older brochures may still say **중구** for the same mainland street. The city's July 2026 district changes moved the inland part of the former Jung-gu into Jemulpo-gu. [Incheon administrative district changes](https://www.incheon.go.kr/IC01070101)

### Pick one museum and protect the admission time

| Museum | What you will see | Korean name and address to save |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jajangmyeon Museum | Food history and recreated interiors in the former Gonghwachun restaurant building. Plan your meal separately. | **짜장면박물관** · 인천광역시 제물포구 차이나타운로 56-14 |
| Incheon Open Port Museum | Port, railway and street history inside a former bank building. Continue to the Art Platform afterwards. | **인천개항박물관** · 인천광역시 제물포구 신포로23번길 89 |

The foundation lists both venues **Tuesday–Sunday, 09:00–18:00**, with **last admission at 17:30**. A public holiday on Monday moves the closure to the following day. The foundation also lists closures on January 1 and the days of Seollal and Chuseok. Check the current notices before going. If you plan to visit several foundation venues, compare the individual rates with the five-venue combined ticket, currently listed at **₩3,400 per adult**. The Art Platform has its own programme. [Jajangmyeon Museum](https://www.jcf.or.kr/main/space/museum4.jsp), [Incheon Open Port Museum](https://www.jcf.or.kr/main/space/museum2.jsp), [foundation hours, prices and notices](https://www.jcf.or.kr/main/space/leaflet.jsp)

### Continue to Incheon Art Platform

From Chinatown, follow the Open Port streets toward the museum block and **인천아트플랫폼**. Search [Incheon Art Platform](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%EC%95%84%ED%8A%B8%ED%94%8C%EB%9E%AB%ED%8F%BC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%EB%9F%89%EB%A1%9C218%EB%B2%88%EA%B8%B8%203) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%EC%95%84%ED%8A%B8%ED%94%8C%EB%9E%AB%ED%8F%BC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%EB%9F%89%EB%A1%9C218%EB%B2%88%EA%B8%B8%203) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%EC%95%84%ED%8A%B8%ED%94%8C%EB%9E%AB%ED%8F%BC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%EB%9F%89%EB%A1%9C218%EB%B2%88%EA%B8%B8%203)) using **인천광역시 제물포구 제물량로218번길 3**. Use the brick warehouse buildings as a landmark. Open the [operator's current exhibitions](https://inartplatform.kr/home/home) before building an indoor visit around the complex. Programmes can have their own dates and admission conditions.

### Finish with food or head for the train

Have jajangmyeon in Chinatown if you want a seated noodle meal. For a street-food stop at the other end of the walk, follow your map to [Sinpo International Market](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%8B%A0%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AD%EC%A0%9C%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%8B%A0%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AD%EC%A0%9C%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%8B%A0%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AD%EC%A0%9C%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC)), **신포국제시장**. Look for **닭강정** for sweet and spicy fried chicken, and ask for the portion size before ordering. The tourism board lists shop-by-shop hours and closures, so confirm any stall that is important to your visit. [Sinpo Market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=88101), [market food and rail access](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=977&vcontsId=174438)

If you finish at Sinpo, [Sinpo Station Exit 4](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%8B%A0%ED%8F%AC%EC%97%AD%204%EB%B2%88%20%EC%B6%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%8B%A0%ED%8F%AC%EC%97%AD%204%EB%B2%88%20%EC%B6%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%8B%A0%ED%8F%AC%EC%97%AD%204%EB%B2%88%20%EC%B6%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC)) is the market approach listed by VISITKOREA. Compare the return route with walking back to Incheon Station, especially if you want a Line 1 train. Check the actual entrance if you need a lift.

## Adapt to Monday, rain or the climb

- **Monday or after last admission:** Keep the Chinatown street walk, an open food stop and the Open Port building exteriors. Check the Art Platform's own listing if you want an exhibition. Save the foundation museums for a confirmed open day.
- **Clear weather and good footing:** Add [Jayu Park](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%9E%90%EC%9C%A0%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%9E%90%EC%9C%A0%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%9E%90%EC%9C%A0%EA%B3%B5%EC%9B%90%20%EC%9D%B8%EC%B2%9C%20%EC%A0%9C%EB%AC%BC%ED%8F%AC%EA%B5%AC)) between Chinatown and the Open Port streets. Incheon's [tourism guide](https://eng-itour.incheon.go.kr/cmn/board/BBSMSTR_000000000080/5811bbsDetail.do) describes the approach from Chinatown as uphill. Use that as a condition for the extra stop and keep the park optional.
- **Wheelchair, stroller or trouble with stairs:** Confirm the station exit, road crossing and venue entrance separately. The [national accessible-tourism listing](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/ms/detail.do?cotId=7cd5a47f-e86f-40dd-9e1b-b513470b8797) describes a ramp via the Jajangmyeon Museum rear entrance. Call the museum at **032-773-9812** to confirm how to reach and use it on your date. A venue ramp does not verify the full route from the train.

If your first stop in Incheon is the airport, use the [Incheon Airport arrival guide](/blog/incheon-airport-survival-guide-2026) to plan immigration, bags and the ground transfer first. For this walk, add your chosen museum, food stop and return station to the [day-by-day trip board](/tools/itinerary).
