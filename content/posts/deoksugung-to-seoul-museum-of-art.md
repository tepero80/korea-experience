**Visit SeMA first if you are starting late, especially on a winter weekend.** Seoul Museum of Art's Seosomun Main Branch stops admitting visitors an hour before closing. Deoksugung normally admits visitors until 20:00 and closes at 21:00, which leaves more flexibility for the palace. Check the two venues separately on Mondays and holidays.

## Choose the visit order before you leave

These cutoffs are based on the museum's [Seosomun visitor information](https://sema.seoul.go.kr/en/visit/seosomun) and the Royal Palaces and Tombs Center's [general opening hours](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do). The museum may have separate booking or admission terms for your exhibition. Plan to reach the entrance before its cutoff.

| When you arrive | Which stop to put first |
| --- | --- |
| **Early afternoon** | Choose either order after checking your exhibition. Give the outdoor palace the brighter or drier part of the day. |
| **Winter Saturday, Sunday or public holiday** | Put the museum first if the visit could run late. Its posted winter weekend and holiday closing is 18:00, with last entry at 17:00. |
| **Summer weekend or public holiday** | The published museum cutoff is 18:00 for a 19:00 close. Verify which schedule applies on your date. |
| **Tuesday–Thursday or Friday** | SeMA's usual last entry is 19:00 Tuesday–Thursday and 20:00 Friday. Start with the venue or exhibition that is more important to you. |
| **Monday or 1 January** | The museum lists these as closure days. Deoksugung normally closes Mondays and has separate holiday rules. Check both calendars before setting out. |

## Walk from the palace gate to SeMA Seosomun

Begin or finish at **Daehanmun (대한문)**, the Deoksugung gate at **서울 중구 세종대로 99**. The [palace directions](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R707000000.do?schGroupCode=dsg) point visitors to City Hall Station, Exit 2, on Lines 1 and 2. From the forecourt, follow **Deoksugung-gil (덕수궁길)** beside the wall toward **서울시립미술관 서소문본관**, **서울 중구 덕수궁길 61**. Use [this museum search in Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%8B%9C%EB%A6%BD%EB%AF%B8%EC%88%A0%EA%B4%80%20%EC%84%9C%EC%86%8C%EB%AC%B8%EB%B3%B8%EA%B4%80%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A4%91%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%8D%95%EC%88%98%EA%B6%81%EA%B8%B8%2061) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%8B%9C%EB%A6%BD%EB%AF%B8%EC%88%A0%EA%B4%80%20%EC%84%9C%EC%86%8C%EB%AC%B8%EB%B3%B8%EA%B4%80%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A4%91%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%8D%95%EC%88%98%EA%B6%81%EA%B8%B8%2061) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%8B%9C%EB%A6%BD%EB%AF%B8%EC%88%A0%EA%B4%80%20%EC%84%9C%EC%86%8C%EB%AC%B8%EB%B3%B8%EA%B4%80%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A4%91%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%8D%95%EC%88%98%EA%B6%81%EA%B8%B8%2061)) and check that the selected result says **서소문본관**. SeMA also publishes [direct subway exits on its visitor page](https://sema.seoul.go.kr/en/visit/seosomun) if you are starting at the museum.

**SeMA Seosomun is a separate museum from MMCA Deoksugung**, which is inside the palace grounds. Confirm the venue named on your exhibition listing before following the route. SeMA's visitor page lists an elevator plus wheelchair and stroller loans. If you need a step-free approach, call SeMA at **+82 2-2124-8800** for the entrance it recommends and check the station lift route separately.

## Adjust for rain, late arrival or a closed gallery

Check the SeMA exhibition listing before travel. General admission is free except for special exhibitions, which can have separate terms. If rain continues, make the gallery the main stop. If the last entry has passed and the palace is still admitting, choose the palace or keep the stonewall walk as an outdoor stop in suitable weather. For a different evening with a confirmed open gate, check [ordinary evening admission at Changgyeonggung](/blog/changgyeonggung-ordinary-evening-visit) before crossing town.
