## Ask about the refund before accepting the discount

The [Korea Consumer Agency’s procedure-package example](https://www.kca.go.kr/home/sub.do?menukey=4005&mode=view&no=1003881199&page=1) illustrates why unused sessions and deductions can matter when a package ends early. Ask for the calculation in a language you can understand. Separate the clinic’s deposit from payments to an interpreter, coordinator, hotel or transport company.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Payee | Name the business receiving each payment. Request the contract and receipt showing the amount, currency and what the deposit secures. |
| Change | Ask how a changed appointment or procedure affects the charge. Confirm when the deadline begins and which contact can accept a change. |
| Cancel | Ask how they calculate the balance if you cancel before the first visit or stop after using one part of the package. |
| Record | Keep messages with dates, the payment record, the terms you accepted and any written refund decision. |

## Send a clear request if plans change

Use a contact channel stated in the agreement and keep proof of when you wrote. Explain the service, payment and outcome you are requesting. For a dispute about medical care, [K-MEDI’s guidance for foreign patients](https://k-medi.or.kr/eng/lay1/S239T385C453/contents.do) explains its mediation route. A refund question about a separate booking may need its own contract or consumer contact.
