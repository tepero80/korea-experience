Start with a product list and compare the final payable price, including shipping or the cost of a store trip. A displayed discount is only useful if the exact size, offer channel and eligibility match your purchase.

## Read the offer first

| Offer | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Today’s Special | [Olive Young describes it](https://corp.oliveyoung.com/en/newsroom/editorial/205) as a one-day online-only Korean app and website offer; do not assume the shelf price matches. |
| Member coupon | Check your membership tier, expiry, minimum spend, exclusions and whether it applies online or in store. |
| Sale event | Compare the exact size and final price after applicable coupons. Limited or timed offers may run out. |
| Global order | Check destination, shipping and coupon terms on the Global platform; do not assume Korean app benefits transfer. |

The company says OLIVE DAY offers tier-dependent member benefits on the 25th–27th, while O.Y SALE is seasonal. Dates and terms can change. Check whether you can register and use a benefit before making a special trip; a visitor without an eligible account can still compare the ordinary in-store price.

Keep receipts and confirm return and tax-refund conditions at checkout. For choosing a branch and building a short list, use the [Olive Young shopping guide](/blog/olive-young-shopping-guide-must-buy-k-beauty-products).
