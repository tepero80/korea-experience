Before selecting a restaurant, ask your hotel whether deliveries are accepted and exactly where to meet the courier. The address and contact method matter as much as the menu.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Address and handoff | Ask for the Korean street address and the permitted lobby or entrance pickup point. |
| Account and payment | Check the actual app's signup and checkout before expecting a particular card or pay-on-delivery option. |
| Order total | Review food, delivery fees and minimum order before confirming. |
| Alternatives | Ask the hotel about nearby takeaway or an ordering service that covers your address. |

Before you place a chicken order, [check the bone, sauce and portion words in the actual menu](/blog/how-to-order-korean-fried-chicken-portions-sauces-sides-and-delivery-terms).

## Check before committing

Check the current delivery app’s account rules and payment screen for your own phone and card. If the app offers payment on delivery, confirm that option before submitting the order. If account verification stops you, ask the hotel about permitted ordering or takeaway options.

[Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=194469) describes hotel-lobby delivery and notes that some hotels allow room delivery. Ask your own property first; do not put a room number or private phone number into a public message. Tell the courier the authorized meeting point and follow the hotel's instructions.

## If checkout will not work

Do not submit the order repeatedly without checking whether one was accepted. Ask the hotel to help identify a current service, or order takeaway directly from a nearby restaurant. Availability, coverage, payment and fees vary by merchant and time. Keep the order confirmation privately until the food arrives.
