## Read the table before you move a bowl

[Korea.net’s table manners guide](https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Society/view?articleId=175173&pageIndex=1) describes keeping rice and soup bowls on the table and using a spoon. You can take a small spoonful from your bowl and set the spoon down between bites. At a family or formal meal, watch how the host starts and follow the setup they give you.

| What arrives | A simple next step |
| --- | --- |
| Your own rice bowl | Leave it in front of you and use the spoon. |
| Small plates between diners | Check if they are for the whole table. Take a little to your side plate if provided. |
| A hot pot with a ladle | Use the ladle and ask for a separate bowl if you need one. |

## Ask for the piece of tableware you need

Show **앞접시 하나 더 주세요.** (One more small plate, please.) You can use it to separate the food you have taken from dishes everyone is sharing. Ask **숟가락 하나 더 주세요.** (One more spoon, please.) if you need another spoon.

At a restaurant, observe where the staff set each dish down. A separate bowl in front of one seat is likely for that diner. If everyone has a bowl and there is a single dish at the center, ask if it is shared before rearranging the table.
