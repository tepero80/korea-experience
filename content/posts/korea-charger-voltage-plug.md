**For:** travelers bringing phones, laptops or personal appliances to Korea. **Checked:** 9 November 2026. Always check the label and manufacturer instructions for your exact device.

## Check voltage before buying an adapter

Korea’s standard household supply is **220 volts at 60 Hz**, with outlets commonly having two round holes, according to [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?menuSn=375&vcontsId=140041). A travel plug adapter changes the plug shape; it does not necessarily convert voltage. A device whose input rating does not cover 220 V needs a compatible solution recommended by its manufacturer, or should be left unplugged.

| Label | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Input 100–240 V, 50/60 Hz | The marked range covers Korea’s standard supply. Check plug fit, grounding and the manufacturer’s instructions. |
| Input 110–120 V only | Do not connect directly to a 220 V outlet through a simple plug adapter. Ask the manufacturer for a suitable solution. |
| No label | Do not guess. Check the manual or official device support before use. |
| Loose plug | Use an appropriate correctly rated adapter; never force a plug. |

## Read the right part of the charger

Look for **INPUT** on the power brick, not only the USB output. Input describes the supply the charger accepts. The voltage range and frequency must cover the local supply; confirm any additional requirements with the manufacturer. For a product-specific example, see [Philips’ travel guidance](https://www.usa.philips.com/c-f/XC000020630/can-i-travel-with-my-philips-grooming-or-beauty-product).

For example, a charger marked “Input 100–240 V ~ 50/60 Hz” has a voltage and frequency range that includes Korea’s 220 V and 60 Hz. That example does not certify a particular adapter, cable or appliance.

## High-power devices need extra care

Hair dryers, curling irons and travel kettles can draw much more power than a phone charger. Check the appliance’s actual input rating and manufacturer instructions. If a compatible converter is needed, its output, wattage and other specifications must suit the device; an ordinary plug adapter alone is not a voltage converter. If you cannot establish compatibility, ask the accommodation about an available appliance or leave yours unused.

A socket’s shape does not itself guarantee grounding. If a device requires a protective earth connection, use a compatible grounded setup consistent with its instructions. Stop using any adapter that is damaged, loose, overheating or visibly unsafe.

## At your accommodation

Ask **어댑터를 빌릴 수 있나요?** — Can I borrow an adapter? KTO notes that some hotel front desks may lend or sell adapters; availability and fit vary. For overnight charging, follow the device and accommodation instructions rather than covering chargers or placing them on soft bedding.

Keep a separate plan for power banks on your flight: airline cabin and battery rules are a different issue from using a wall outlet. Read our [Incheon power-bank guide](/blog/incheon-power-bank-carry-on-rules) before departure.
