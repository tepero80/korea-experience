## Price the same trip twice

Put the same dates, room type, party size and cancellation terms in both searches. Check each hotel’s all-in total, including taxes and any extra-person fee, and note where it is relative to the places you want to visit. A room far from those stops can add both travel time and fare.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Room | Compare the full stay in won. Divide by nights only to compare like-for-like quotes. |
| Arrive | Add the airport or station transfer and any intercity fare separately. A flight or train arrives at a real place, so check the leg to your hotel. |
| Rides | Use the official fare for each adult ride you plan. [Seoul’s table](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/subway/) shows ₩1,550 for a standard base transit-card ride. [Busan’s urban rail table](https://www2.humetro.busan.kr/homepage/english/page/subLocation.do?menu_no=100601040101) lists ₩1,600 for section 1 and ₩1,800 for section 2 by adult transit card. |
| Extras | Add meals and the specific activities on your itinerary. Group free stops and ticketed ones separately. |

## Use the fare difference in context

For eight rides that all qualify for the quoted base or section 1 adult card fare, the examples total ₩12,400 in Seoul and ₩12,800 in Busan. This example excludes discounts, higher fare bands, airport and intercity transfers. Replace those eight rides with your routes, then add the room and the remaining items. For a longer stay, check a written housing offer and the deposit separately before comparing cost of living.
