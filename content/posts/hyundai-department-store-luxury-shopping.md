For a planned purchase, identify the **branch and individual counter** first. The Hyundai Seoul in Yeouido and Hyundai Department Store Trade Center in Gangnam are different destinations. Check the shop, item and opening arrangements before crossing the city.

## Choose a branch for your route

| Your area | Branch and checks |
| --- | --- |
| Yeouido | **더현대 서울 · 서울 영등포구 여의대로 108**. Check the counter floor, exact item and route back to Yeouido Station. |
| COEX | **현대백화점 무역센터점 · 서울 강남구 테헤란로 517**. Confirm the counter hours and allow time to navigate the complex. |

The [Hyundai Seoul branch page](https://m.ehyundai.com/mobile/DP/en/branch.do?branchCd=B00140000&branchType=10&lanType=EN) describes its access from Yeouido Station. As a concrete example of why branch names matter, [Tamburins lists separate counters](https://www.tamburins.com/en/store/korea/) at both locations. This does not establish that any other brand or desired item is available there.

## Before paying

Ask the counter whether your size or model is in stock, whether an appointment or queue applies, and the total price. Ask the checkout about payment, return conditions and whether your particular purchase qualifies for a tax refund; retain your receipt and check the process before leaving. For the Yeouido visit itself, use [The Hyundai Seoul planning guide](/blog/shopping-at-the-hyundai-seoul-a-guide-to-korea-coolest-mall-2026).
