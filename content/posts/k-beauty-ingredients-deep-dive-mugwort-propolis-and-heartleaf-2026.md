Turn the box around before comparing front-label claims. Look for **전성분** (full ingredients), **사용기한** (use-by date), **개봉 후 사용기간** (period after opening) and **사용상의 주의사항** (precautions). Korea’s [MFDS labeling guide](https://mfds.go.kr/wpge/m_645/de050601l0024.do) describes the packaging information that must be provided and rules for different types of packaging. If only the tester is on display, ask to see the sealed box for the size you will buy.

## Match the shelf term to the full list

| Shelf term | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Mugwort · 쑥 | Check the botanical name and form on the list. [This mugwort essence](https://www.klairs.com/products/mugwort-essence) names *Artemisia princeps* extract. |
| Propolis · 프로폴리스 | Read the rest of the formula. [This COSRX ampoule](https://b2c.cosrx.com/products/full-fit-propolis-light-ampoule) lists propolis extract together with several other ingredients. |
| Heartleaf · 어성초 | Check whether the list says extract or plant water. [Anua’s toner](https://pages.anua.com/products/heartleaf-77-soothing-toner) lists flower, leaf and stem water. [Its pads](https://anua.com/products/heartleaf-77-toner-pad-160ml) list extract and also gluconolactone. |

## Do a last check at the counter

Match the product name, size and version to the ingredient photo you saved. If you need to avoid a specific substance, show its written name and ask to see the complete list. If you cannot verify it or cannot read the precautions, leave that product on the shelf. Check the seal and the use-by date before buying multiples.
