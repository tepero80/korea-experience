F1963 is a former wire factory in Busan that now houses several cultural and commercial spaces. Pick one book, food or exhibition stop as the reason for the visit. The [complex operator](https://f1963.org/) publishes programs; opening hours or admission for one tenant do not establish access to the others.

| Starting point | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Mangmi Station | [Visit Busan](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=372) gives Line 3 Exit 4, local bus Suyeong-gu 2 and **산정아파트** as the alighting stop. Verify the current stop, direction and service. |
| Taxi or another bus | Use **부산광역시 수영구 구락로123번길 20**. Check the road approach and the return separately. |
| Rainy or cold day | Choose a confirmed indoor tenant first and leave time for outdoor parts of the approach. |

## Pick your first door

Search **F1963** for the site and check the tenant’s own information for its space. Seats, tickets, workshops and event access can work differently. If your first option is closed, check another listed venue before starting the route, or choose a different indoor stop from the [Busan destination guide](/destinations/busan).

Do not infer accessible access from the exterior photograph. Check the approach and any thresholds with the venue if that matters to your party. Before leaving, confirm the ride to your hotel or the line for your next neighborhood.
