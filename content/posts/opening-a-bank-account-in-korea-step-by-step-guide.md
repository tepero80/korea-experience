Call the branch where you plan to open the account. Say why you need it, such as receiving payroll or paying household bills. Give your situation without sending photos of your identity documents in a public message. Ask which originals to take and whether the branch handles applications in a language you can use.

## Build your branch folder

| Purpose | Ask whether to bring |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Passport, residence card and any alternate proof the branch accepts |
| Payroll | The specific employment or payroll document it wants |
| Housing | An accepted bill or other purpose evidence, if requested |
| Contact | The address and phone information it needs for the services you want |

[Seoul’s banking overview](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/living/convenience/1-banking/) lists a passport and foreign registration documentation for its branch example. The [city financial guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/FINANCIAL_GUIDE_BOOK_FOR_FOREIG_ERS_IN_KOREA.pdf) gives examples of evidence for the account’s purpose. Ask your own branch to confirm the list, account type and accepted identity documents for your status. If you are waiting for your residence card, ask what options exist before visiting.

If you need to send money home, [confirm which providers accept your account and compare two usable quotes](/blog/sending-money-abroad-from-korea-best-methods-2026).

If you need an account for a virtual-asset service, [verify the operator and its bank-account rules separately](/blog/cryptocurrency-in-korea-exchanges-and-regulations).

## Ask what works the day the account opens

Ask the teller to show the applicable withdrawal and transfer limits for each channel you expect to use. Record which limits apply at the counter, at an ATM and through the app, along with any separate activation or later review. Ask what can change the restrictions and what evidence the branch would require. Then use the [card and mobile features checklist](/blog/opening-a-korean-bank-account-for-foreigners) to confirm the account will support the transactions you need.

An app’s identity check can have its own rules. [Hana’s dated notice](https://www.kebhana.com/cont/news/news01/news0101/1501326_118324.jsp) removed passports from certain remote identity checks. Ask about the current channel you will actually use. If the branch cannot proceed, request the exact missing document and ask whether you can return once it is available.
