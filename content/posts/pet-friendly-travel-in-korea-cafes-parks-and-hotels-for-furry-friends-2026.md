A pet-friendly listing is a starting point, not a promise that every room or area accepts your animal. Start with the animal's species, size and travel dates, and get each operator's answer before paying.

If you need separate care for part of the trip, check the [short-stay pet room and boarding guide](/blog/pet-ownership-in-korea-vet-care-and-pet-hotels) before booking.

## Plan each step

| Step | Confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Entry | Ask the quarantine authority about your origin, animal and documents. |
| Flight | Obtain airline approval and check the carrier and connection rules. |
| Room | Confirm the animal, room type, fee and whether it may be left alone. |
| Day out | Ask about indoor areas, leads, carriers and restricted areas. |

[Incheon Airport’s arrival quarantine page](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1442/subview.do) directs travelers arriving with animals to the Animal and Plant Quarantine Agency. Ask the agency about the documentation and inspection required for your particular animal. Requirements depend on the animal and journey, including your return or onward destination; check both directions with the relevant authorities and carriers.

In Busan, [The Petel listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=199801) describes a Busan pet hotel with a café. Confirm current admission, which areas you can use, and the policy for your animal directly; a listing is not a room reservation. Bring cleanup supplies and plan water and rest stops. For the rest of the trip sequence, use the [first-trip planning guide](/blog/first-trip-to-korea-planning-guide).
