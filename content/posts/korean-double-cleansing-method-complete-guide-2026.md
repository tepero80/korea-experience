A double cleanse adds a removal step before your regular face wash. [Cleveland Clinic](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/double-cleansing-explained) explains that most people can wash once with a gentle cleanser and that a separate step can help remove harder-to-remove products.

## Decide what needs removing

| Today | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| No hard-to-remove product | Use your usual gentle face wash as directed. |
| Makeup or sunscreen left behind | Check for a remover intended for the product you wore. Follow its label, then decide whether a gentle face wash is still useful. |
| Burning or tightness after washing | Simplify your routine and stop the product that seems to irritate. Ask a clinician if it continues. |

The [American Academy of Dermatology face-washing guide](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/face-washing-101) recommends a gentle, nonabrasive cleanser, fingertips and lukewarm water. Its [makeup guidance for acne-prone skin](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/acne/causes/makeup) includes using an oil-free makeup remover when appropriate. You can keep a routine that already removes what you wear comfortably.
