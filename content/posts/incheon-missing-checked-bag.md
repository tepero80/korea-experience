**Before leaving the baggage-claim area, tell the airline's baggage representative that your checked bag is missing or damaged and ask how to obtain a written report.** Keep your baggage receipt, boarding pass and a description of the case. The airline manages checked-baggage cases; outcomes and compensation are not automatic.

This guide concerns a checked bag arriving on a flight. An item left in the terminal, on a bus or on a train follows a different lost-property route.

## Make the report at the airport

Incheon's [lost or damaged baggage guidance](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1451/subview.do) says to contact your airline directly about a missing checked bag. For damaged bags it identifies a counter behind the baggage carousel. Ask airport staff where your carrier's representative is and how to report your case.

| Issue | First contact and details |
| --- | --- |
| Checked bag missing | Contact the operating airline; have your baggage receipt, flight details and delivery contact ready. |
| Case or contents damaged | Contact the airline promptly; keep your baggage receipt and photographs of the damage. |
| Item left on the aircraft | Contact the operating airline with the flight number, seat if known and item description. |
| Item left in the terminal or on airport transport | Contact the responsible [airport lost-property office or transport operator](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1549/subview.do); note where and when you last saw it. |

Ask the airline for the report reference, a copy or confirmation, the channel for tracking the case, and what to do if your accommodation changes. Confirm whether it requires a separate written claim, what documents are needed, and the applicable deadline. Do not put a baggage tag number, passport image or phone number in a public message.

## If you have left the airport

The airport says you can still contact the airline directly about damaged baggage after leaving. Do so as soon as possible, explain where you are staying, and ask what documentation and deadlines apply. Photograph damage and keep relevant receipts. A reference number helps track a report; it does not itself establish compensation.

If the bag was sent to a different airport or involved multiple airlines, ask the operating carrier and the carrier handling baggage at your arrival airport which team owns the case. Preserve your itinerary and any written instructions. Do not assume every connecting airline or insurance policy treats the incident identically.

## Replacing essentials and changing hotels

Ask the airline **before buying** what reasonable essential purchases it will consider, how to submit receipts, and any limits or exclusions. Check travel insurance terms separately. Neither an airport information page nor this guide promises reimbursement.

If you move to another city while the bag is being traced, update the airline using its official support channel. Ask whether it can deliver there, who pays any delivery charge, and how you will be contacted. Keep medication and other time-sensitive essentials in your carry-on when allowed; speak to your doctor and airline about special storage or documents before departure.

## If your problem is an item, not a checked bag

Incheon's [lost-and-found page](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1549/subview.do) separates terminal property, items on aircraft, bonded-area property, and airport bus or railway items by responsible organization. A duty-free-area item may take extra time to reach the public-area lost-and-found office through customs. If uncertain, ask the airport information desk rather than submitting sensitive personal details to an unofficial site.
