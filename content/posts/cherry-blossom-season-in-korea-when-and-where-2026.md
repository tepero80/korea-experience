**Leave your blossom day flexible, then choose one Jinhae site as your first stop.** Yeojwacheon is the stream walk. Gyeonghwa is the former railway station. Save each Korean name separately and check your actual ride back before visiting both.

## Choose a day by bloom, access and your return ride

Use spring as your planning season and local reports as your final decision. The [Korea Tourism Organization's spring guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1580801) gives sites that routinely attract blossom visitors. Its 2026 listings are historical. The [2026 Gunhangje program](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=124524) ran March 27–April 5. As of November 24, 2026, this guide has no verified 2027 schedule or bloom prediction. Confirm the next official announcement before booking around the festival.

| When you decide | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Booking a spring trip | Keep one day flexible. Choose a base that also works if the flowers are early or late. |
| Several days ahead | Look for recent photos or official blossom updates from the specific site. Check the current festival and street notices separately. |
| Day of travel | Save your local stop, return service and alternate pickup location. Start with one site if access or crowds slow the walk. |

## Yeojwacheon · Follow the stream

[VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=93625) locates the blossom stream in Yeojwa-dong. Start near Romance Bridge if you want an easy-to-name meeting point. Keep any bridge crossing or lower path optional if crowd controls or mobility needs affect your route.

## Gyeonghwa · Visit the former railway

[VISITKOREA's station listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=81715) identifies a former station where trains no longer stop. Save the park name and plan road transport. Passenger rail journey planners can help with the longer intercity leg; the photo stop itself needs a separate last mile. Use temporary event signs if they change pedestrian access.
