## Check the hours with the library

| Source | Listed hours |
| --- | --- |
| [English desktop guide](https://hanok.seoul.go.kr/front/eng/exp/expLibrary.do?tab=1) | Daily 09:00–18:00 |
| [Korean mobile guide](https://hanok.seoul.go.kr/m/kor/exp/expLibrary.do?tab=1) | Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–17:00. Monday closed. |

Call **02-765-1373** in Korea or **+82-2-765-1373** from abroad to confirm the day and time. Ask whether visitors can sit and read during your planned visit. If you need step-free access, a particular book, or a class, mention it when you call. The city describes a collection about Bukchon, hanok and Seoul, as well as children's books. The listings give limited information about borrowing and languages.

If you cannot confirm access, make the library an optional stop. Check the [Seoul Museum of Craft Art](/blog/seoul-museum-of-craft-art-visitor-guide) as a nearby indoor alternative, or choose [Unhyeongung](/blog/unhyeongung-anguk-traditional-house-visit) if you want to visit a public hanok.

## Find the turn from Gyedong-gil

**북촌마을서재 · 서울특별시 종로구 계동2길 11-9**

From Anguk, use Gyedong-gil as your approach and turn toward the **Gyedong 2-gil** address in the [city walking map](https://english.seoul.go.kr/wp-content/themes/seoul-e/assets/images/hanok/img/hanok-map-2.pdf). Confirm your individual route on the day. [Bukchon Culture Center](https://hanok.seoul.go.kr/front/util/bcgTour03.do?lang=KOR) is a separate stop at **37 Gyedong-gil**, so check the address before following a similar map result. Give yourself extra time if slopes or uneven lanes are difficult for your group.

## Follow the rules for the street you use

The city’s [Red Zone notice](https://english.seoul.go.kr/notice-on-restrictions-for-tourist-visits-to-bukchon-special-management-area-effective-from-november-2024/) restricts tourist visits on a designated part of **Bukchon-ro 11-gil** from **17:00 to 10:00 the following morning**. Review the map and signs before you add that street to your route. Keep residential gates clear and leave photography of private spaces to the people who live there.
