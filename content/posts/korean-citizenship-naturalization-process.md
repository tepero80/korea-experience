## Ask which naturalization route applies

[Articles 5–7 of the Korean Nationality Act](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1016665855) set out different routes. Use the table to prepare a question for the nationality office. Bring any family connection that could be relevant, even if you are unsure whether it changes your route.

| Route | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| General | Which periods count toward five continuous years of domicile, and which record shows I hold a status allowing permanent residence? Korea Immigration Service identifies [F-5 as permanent-residence status](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration/1515/subview.do). |
| Family | For a simplified route, does a specific family connection apply, and what residence or marriage dates need proof? Ask which general requirements still apply. |
| Special | Which condition in Article 7 could apply, and what evidence would support it? |

General naturalization also has rules on adulthood, conduct, livelihood, basic knowledge, and public interest. Ask how the office checks those requirements for the route it identifies.

## Bring a timeline before ordering certificates

Write down your Korean addresses and stay-status changes with dates. Add departures, returns, and gaps so the office can tell you how to count your domicile. Note relevant family events and which country would issue each supporting record. Keep identification numbers and original documents private until you know which secure submission channel the office accepts.

Ask [Korea Immigration Service at 1345](https://www.immigration.go.kr/moj/196/subview.do) which office handles your case. Confirm the current checklist, which documents need translation or authentication, how recently they must have been issued, and which assessment or exemption applies. If you are still working out permanent residence, start with the [F-5 route and stay-record guide](/blog/permanent-residency-requirements-f-5-visa).

## Check the steps after a decision

[Article 4 of the Nationality Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1030981759) describes acquisition upon the required oath and delivery of the naturalization certificate, with an oath exemption in specified circumstances. Ask how you will receive each notice and what to do if your stay permission needs attention or you plan to travel while the case is pending. Confirm any obligations involving your existing nationality before acting. If your current authorization may expire, consult the office about [the applicable stay procedure](/blog/changing-visa-status-while-in-korea) independently of the nationality application.
