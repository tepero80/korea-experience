Ask the facility for a written consultation and price estimate before paying a deposit. Match the full clinic name and branch to the [Medical Korea registry](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredhospitals) when it recruits international patients. Government registration and the clinic’s description give you information to check during a consultation.

## Compare the same items

| Ask about | Request in writing |
| --- | --- |
| Visit | Assessment, tests, clinician and the proposed scope. |
| Procedure | Separate charges for the procedure, anesthesia, medicines, materials and facility use. |
| Payment | Currency, taxes, deposit, how it is credited, and the change or cancellation terms. |
| Follow-up | Included visits, possible extra costs, the after-hours contact and care after you leave Korea. |

[Medical Korea’s explanation](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/notice/faqView.do?faqSn=48) describes treatment, possible side effects, contract terms, expected cost and dispute information that registered facilities must communicate. If comparing a [HIRA-listed non-covered charge](https://www.hira.or.kr/npay/index.do), check the clinic, item and date, then get the total for your case.

## Confirm what happens if plans change

Ask how a changed treatment plan or a postponed visit affects the price. Ask the clinician how follow-up and any travel restrictions apply to you before locking in a flight. Keep the estimate, agreement, receipt and names of the treating team. If a dispute arises, the [medical mediation agency](https://k-medi.or.kr/eng/lay1/S239T368C374/contents.do) lists the documentation it may need. See the [aftercare contact checklist](/blog/managing-bruising-and-scarring-korean-skincare-approach) for planning a handover.
