Wonju History Museum (원주역사박물관) introduces the city’s history and traditions. Search **강원특별자치도 원주시 봉산로 134**. If you meant Museum SAN, use its own address outside central Wonju.

| Decision | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| When to arrive | Published hours are 09:00–18:00. The [visitor guide](https://whm.wonju.go.kr/whm/page/view.php/sub_01_01) and [homepage](https://whm.wonju.go.kr/whm/main.php) give different last-entry times. Aim for **before 17:00** and confirm. |
| Ticket and closures | The visitor guide lists free admission and Monday and specified holiday closures. Check the day after a public holiday too. |
| From your station | Review a live bus or taxi route to Bongsan-ro 134. The [municipal dataset](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15017323/standard.do) lists local buses serving the museum, without a specific station boarding point. |

## Plan the indoor visit

Start with the local-history and folk-life galleries, then check the current exhibition. Ask about language support, any accessible entrance you need, and which areas are open. Contact the museum at **033-737-4371**. Check the museum’s photography rules before taking photographs inside, especially for commercial use.

For a separate outdoor stop, visit [Gangwongamyeong](/blog/wonju-gangwongamyeong-heritage-visit) if the weather and operating hours suit. If catching a train, [use the station printed on your ticket](/blog/wonju-station-onward-transport) as your final destination in a route planner.
