## Pick a route that fits your base

| Where you are staying | Best use of your time |
| --- | --- |
| Seoul | Use Seokchon Lake as a flexible neighborhood walk. Check [the park listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/jamsilarea/Seokchon-Lake-Park/ENP001136) before you choose which day to go. |
| Busan or Changwon | Consider a Jinhae day after checking the departure and return. Start with [Yeojwacheon Stream](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=93625) or the former [Gyeonghwa Station](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=&vcontsId=81715); add the second if transit and crowds allow. |

Leave a second flexible day in your itinerary if blossoms are a priority. Use dated bloom photos or current local updates to decide whether a detour is worth the journey. Independently check the [current Jinhae Gunhangje event listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=124524) for festival dates and access updates. An announced festival can take place before or after the best viewing.

## Start with one Jinhae stop and plan the way back

- **진해 여좌천 로망스다리** is for an unhurried stream-side walk. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%A7%84%ED%95%B4%20%EC%97%AC%EC%A2%8C%EC%B2%9C%20%EB%A1%9C%EB%A7%9D%EC%8A%A4%EB%8B%A4%EB%A6%AC) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%A7%84%ED%95%B4%20%EC%97%AC%EC%A2%8C%EC%B2%9C%20%EB%A1%9C%EB%A7%9D%EC%8A%A4%EB%8B%A4%EB%A6%AC) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%A7%84%ED%95%B4%20%EC%97%AC%EC%A2%8C%EC%B2%9C%20%EB%A1%9C%EB%A7%9D%EC%8A%A4%EB%8B%A4%EB%A6%AC)
- **창원 진해 경화역 벚꽃길** is a separate outdoor attraction. **Passenger trains do not stop here.** Plan the last leg by road or on foot from a verified service, and check festival-day pickup points separately. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%B0%BD%EC%9B%90%20%EC%A7%84%ED%95%B4%20%EA%B2%BD%ED%99%94%EC%97%AD%20%EB%B2%9A%EA%BD%83%EA%B8%B8) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%B0%BD%EC%9B%90%20%EC%A7%84%ED%95%B4%20%EA%B2%BD%ED%99%94%EC%97%AD%20%EB%B2%9A%EA%BD%83%EA%B8%B8) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%B0%BD%EC%9B%90%20%EC%A7%84%ED%95%B4%20%EA%B2%BD%ED%99%94%EC%97%AD%20%EB%B2%9A%EA%BD%83%EA%B8%B8)

Set a return time before you add your second stop. Save the Korean name of the stop or station you actually need for the way home. Build a buffer for crowds, rain or an unexpected wait and check temporary street and shuttle changes with the organizer.

## Keep a Seoul walk in reserve

Search for **서울 석촌호수** and use one section of the lake if you have only a short break in your day.
