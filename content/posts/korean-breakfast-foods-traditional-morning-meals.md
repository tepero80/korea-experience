If you have time to sit down, decide whether you want a spoonable porridge or soup with a serving of rice. The Korean name on the sign can narrow the choice. Check the branch’s current opening time before planning a meal around a train reservation.

## Find your bowl on the menu

| Name | How to choose |
| --- | --- |
| 죽 · juk | Rice porridge. The word before 죽 gives the flavor. For example, 전복죽 has abalone. |
| 국밥 · gukbap | Soup and rice. 콩나물국밥 names the bean sprouts. Ask if the rice comes mixed in. |
| 설렁탕 · seolleongtang | Ox-bone soup. Find out whether rice comes in the bowl or separately and taste before seasoning. |

The [KTO description of kongnamulgukbap](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178212) identifies it as a breakfast dish associated with Jeonju. The [Wando abalone food guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/food/ext/special_view.do?menuSn=603&menuSn=913&page=1&sort=regDt&vcontsId=178109) describes jeonbokjuk. The [seolleongtang entry](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178349) explains how the broth is made and how it can be seasoned at the table. For a soft bowl, use **죽** plus the neighborhood where you are staying. Confirm the ingredients in your actual order.

## A Jeonju example near Dongmun Art Street

The [official listing for Waengi](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=228776) describes a shop specializing in bean sprout and rice soup and identifies anchovy and seafood in its broth. Save **전주 왱이콩나물국밥전문점 · 전북특별자치도 전주시 완산구 동문길 88**. The vegetables in the dish name alone do not tell you how the soup was made.

If you need to be at a station soon, ask whether the place will seat you right away and have a second option saved.
