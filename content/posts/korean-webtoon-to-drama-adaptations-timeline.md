## Match the versions before you begin

[WEBTOON publishes the English comic True Beauty](https://www.webtoons.com/en/romance/truebeauty/list?title_no=1436), and [Netflix’s series article](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/true-beauty-release-date-news) identifies its live-action adaptation. Use the publisher, cast and format to distinguish the versions before looking up episode details.

| Goal | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Read the original | Open the publisher’s English edition and verify which chapters are available to you. |
| Watch the series | Check the live-action title in your region and whether you can select the subtitles you need. |
| Compare without spoilers | Read a short starting section and watch the opening episode before opening fan summaries. |
| Look for a visitor event | Check an official publisher or venue announcement for date, reservation and availability. |

## Build the plan around access

Check your account before travel if you plan to read or watch during a train ride. Download through the provider only where that option is offered. For a trip, choose a public event, bookstore or exhibition only after checking its current listing. The [visitor guide to webtoon platforms and pop-ups](/blog/how-korean-webtoons-are-read-platforms-translations-and-visiting-official-pop-up) helps you match those options to your day.
