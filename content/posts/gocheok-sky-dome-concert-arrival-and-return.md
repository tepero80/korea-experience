**For a concert at Gocheok Sky Dome, take Seoul Subway Line 1 to Guil (구일역) and start with Exit 2.** Save your gate from the promoter's notice separately from the venue pin. Before the show, check the last departure that reaches your hotel stop and the last train for any line you need to change to. A train that leaves Guil can still end short of your destination.

## Arrive at the right gate

1. **Check the station stops before getting on.** Line 1 branches. In your route app, open the stops for the specific train and confirm that it calls at **구일**.
2. **Follow the operator's exit guidance.** [Seoul Facilities Corporation](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/skydome/introduce/location_transportation.jsp) lists **Guil Exit 2**, roughly five minutes on foot. [Seoul Metropolitan Government](https://english.seoul.go.kr/gocheok-sky-dome/) also names Exit 2. [Guro-gu's English page](https://www.guro.go.kr/eng/contents.do?key=3272) gives Exit 1. Use the venue operator's directions for ordinary arrival, and follow staff or temporary signs if the event changes pedestrian access.
3. **Match the gate in your ticket notice.** Enter **고척스카이돔, 서울특별시 구로구 경인로 430** for the general venue, then use the organizer's named gate, pickup desk or accessible entrance. The walk to the building and the time to reach your gate are separate. If a step-free route matters, ask venue or event staff to confirm your entrance and current lift access before traveling.

## Choose a return before doors open

| Your ride | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Line 1 and another subway line | Set the correct date and departure direction in your route app. Check which station the last service reaches and whether you can still make the transfer. |
| Night bus | The venue and Seoul's [night bus directory](https://news.seoul.go.kr/traffic/archives/27974) list N16. Verify the actual stop, direction, live departure and the final leg to your lodging. |
| Taxi | Agree on a legal pickup point with your driver or follow the event's pickup instructions. Show the hotel name and Korean address, and allow for a queue. |

Set your own stay-or-leave time by working backward from your last useful connection. Allow time to get out of your section, walk to the stop and get through the crowd. If your return is close, decide with your group before the encore whether to leave early or use the backup. For N16, read the time at your boarding stop. A timetable for departure from the route terminus cannot tell you when the bus reaches the dome.

If staff divert you away from Exit 2 after the show, follow the route they announce and check departures from the station you can actually reach. Save a meeting point outside the main pedestrian flow in case your group gets separated.

## Save the details your group needs

Share the date, gate, return stop and backup pickup point before leaving your hotel. Use the [concert ticket guide](/blog/how-to-get-k-pop-concert-tickets-ticketing-guide) if someone still needs to confirm ticket access, and put the return cutoff and hotel address on the [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary).
