## Check which refund rule applies to your insured months

Give the National Pension Service (NPS) your nationality, the visa status you held during each period of work and your planned departure date. If dates or employers are missing from your record, [check your pension deductions and enrollment history](/blog/korean-pension-system-understanding-deductions) before filing.

The [English NPS refund guide](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/frgnrlpsmrfnd/getOHAI0015M0.do) lists three ways an insured foreign worker can qualify. Check the current country table and ask whether any minimum contribution period applies to you.

| Possible basis | Question to ask NPS |
| --- | --- |
| Country rule | Does my country offer Korean nationals an equivalent benefit? Does the listed minimum insured period apply? |
| Social security agreement | Does the agreement with my country cover a lump-sum refund in my circumstances? Ask how claiming affects any later use of those periods. |
| Visa rule | Which months were covered under E-8 training employment, E-9 non-professional employment or H-2 visiting employment? |

The [Korean NPS eligibility guide](https://www.nps.or.kr/pnsinfo/ntpsklg/getOHAF0084M0.do) distinguishes **E-8 seasonal work** from the older **E-8 training employment** category. Seasonal work does not qualify under that visa provision. Ask NPS whether a separate basis applies to you. Request the calculation from NPS if eligible. It uses the insured period and applicable interest rules.

## Apply at a local NPS office before departure

1. **Start by checking your documents.** The English NPS list for applying in Korea includes the claim form, passport, Residence Card (called the Alien Registration Card in the guide), proof of the account in your name and evidence that you will depart within one month of applying.
2. **Ask your employer when it will report your loss of coverage.** Tell the NPS office your last workday and flight date. Confirm the application method and keep your acceptance paperwork.
3. **Choose a payment method with the branch.** Ask which bank transfer or overseas remittance options you can use. Confirm the account details while you still have access to the account and supporting documents.

## Use airport pickup only after NPS confirms the timing

The [NPS airport instructions](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/frgnrlpsmrfnd/getOHAI0015M0.do) describe an advance claim at a local NPS office that includes choosing airport payment and supplying a backup bank account. Ask your former employer to confirm it reports the loss of coverage by the day before departure. NPS publishes separate flight windows by Incheon terminal and excludes certain days, including weekends and public holidays.

On the day, the published process starts at the **Terminal 1 NPS counter**, including for Terminal 2 departures. It then involves bank processing before immigration and foreign-currency pickup after immigration. Confirm the hours for all three stops and allow time to reach your gate. If your schedule does not fit, ask NPS about transfer or remittance before you fly.

## If you have already left Korea

NPS lists overseas post and an authorized representative as options. People returning to some countries can apply through their local social insurance institution. Confirm the local NPS office, form versions, ID copies, account proof, authentication and translation requirements before sending documents. Ask which filing deadline applies to your claim and how to follow its status.
