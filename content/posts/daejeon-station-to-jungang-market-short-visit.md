**For Jungang Market, leave Daejeon railway station toward the west plaza, 서광장.** Save **대전 중앙시장, 동구 대전로 783** and use a marked crossing. The market is opposite the station, but its lanes cover more ground than a single market hall. Pick one food or browsing stop and keep enough time to return to your platform.

## Decide before you leave the concourse

| Your window | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Tight | Stay in the station if your train is soon, you still need your ticket, or you cannot confirm your luggage arrangements. |
| One stop | Choose a snack, a shop or one lane near the side you enter. Use the same crossing on the way back. |
| More time | Follow signs for one themed lane. Allow for queues, traffic at the crossing and any luggage pickup on return. |

Before leaving, set a turn-back time based on your next departure. Work backward from when you want to be on the platform and include the return walk, lifts or stairs, and any bag collection. Recheck the platform on the railway screens when you return.

## Find the west plaza and pick a lane

1. **Start at 대전역.** The [national railway station record](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15067652/fileData.do) gives **대전광역시 동구 중앙로 215**. Follow the rail station's **서광장** signs. The [Korea National Railway station notice](https://www.kr.or.kr/boardCnts/view.do?boardID=52&boardSeq=1109173&page=176) identifies the west plaza as the market side. For a step-free approach, ask the station staff which lift serves that side today.
2. **Cross to 중앙시장.** The [Dong-gu market guide](https://www.donggu.go.kr/dg/tour/84/7/sights?tourAttracSeq=7) places the market across from the railway plaza. If your app shows several names, enter the market name and **대전로 783** together. The [district's market directory](https://www.donggu.go.kr/dg/kor/contents/135) lists individual markets in the complex at different addresses.
3. **Look for 먹자골목 for food or 헌책방 for used books.** Dong-gu also lists a fish lane and shops selling dishes and accessories. [VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=82407) says opening days and hours vary by shop. Leave time for a meal only if a stall is open and the queue fits your return time.

## If the short stop does not fit

Stay near the station if a crossing, luggage or the weather makes the return uncertain. If you have most of the day, compare the [Yuseong footbaths and Currency Museum](/blog/daejeon-yuseong-footbath-or-museum-day) or plan the [National Science Museum with children](/blog/daejeon-national-science-museum-family-visit). Both are separate outings. Add your return train to the [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary) before adding another district.
