Call the card issuer with the exact product name. Ask what it accepts for your residence status, employment or other circumstances, and where it takes the application. A branch account and the issuer’s remote identity check can have different requirements. [Hana Bank’s dated identity notice](https://www.kebhana.com/cont/news/news01/news0101/1501326_118324.jsp) is one example for specific remote services. Confirm the current process for the card you want.

## Before you order documents

| Check | Question for the issuer |
| --- | --- |
| ID | Which originals are accepted for my current residence status? |
| Income | Which records are accepted, for what period, and how recent must they be? |
| Channel | Can the application and identity check be completed in a branch or remotely? |
| Billing | Which account is used, what is the due date and how do I confirm activation? |

[Hana Card’s assessment page](https://www.hanacard.co.kr/jsp/cmn/cm/O/OSA05000000N.web) lists examples of work and income evidence by situation and outlines identity, credit and payment-capacity review. These are examples from one issuer. Ask whether it wants a particular certificate or a substitute. For KB specifically, its [global customer center](https://mapps.kbcard.com/SVC/DVIEW/HSGMCXCRSCTCR0001) provides an English and Chinese inquiry route.

## Submit only after you know the process

1. Use an official channel and confirm how the issuer contacts you for verification. Keep the application reference and use the official contact to check any unexpected message.
2. If the issuer asks for additional proof, confirm the name, validity period and accepted submission method. If your application cannot be reviewed yet, ask whether a missing document or application channel is the reason.
3. If approved, confirm the first billing date, fees, payment account and any separate transit or online settings before relying on it.

If you are still deciding whether you need credit, compare [resident check cards and credit cards](/blog/credit-cards-in-korea-which-banks-accept-foreigners). If the issuer asks for a separate payment account, confirm that with the [bank branch checklist](/blog/opening-a-bank-account-in-korea-step-by-step-guide) before visiting.
