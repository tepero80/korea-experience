The practical question is what you can visit today. Start from the public route or venue address, then work backward from the return you want. A building seen on screen may be a home or a different business now.

If your priority is a music video or a filmed performance, [compare Yongma Land and Everland](/blog/k-pop-music-video-filming-locations-map) before you plan a trip outward from Seoul.

## Choose the outing by access

| Outing | What to save before you leave |
| --- | --- |
| Suwon, *Lovely Runner* | The [official Haenggung-dong route](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/hdrdslt/hdrdsltView.do?crsSn=564819) links Haengnidan Street, the mural village, Hwahongmun and Banghwasuryujeong in about 790 metres. Leave the café portion optional. |
| Gangneung, *Guardian* | Save **주문진 방파제 · 강원특별자치도 강릉시 주문진읍 해안로 1609** from the [tourism listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=174269). Allow a separate transfer from the station or bus terminal and check your return before leaving the city centre. |
| Yongin, Korean Folk Village | Choose a [ticketed folk-village visit](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=111995) to see historical settings. Save **한국민속촌 · 경기도 용인시 기흥구 민속촌로 90**. The [venue’s filming information](https://koreanfolk.co.kr/operation/filming) is for productions. Use the visitor listing to plan access and check before looking for a particular scene. |

At residential lanes, stop on public pavement and avoid blocking doors. At the breakwater, stay off any closed or wave-washed section. If the coast is a poor fit for the day's conditions, spend it in central Gangneung and keep the onward train or bus. Check the [Gangneung rail and bus comparison](/blog/seoul-to-gangneung-ktx-vs-express-bus) before buying a ticket.

For a route led by one show, use the [Lovely Runner walk through Suwon](/blog/lovely-runner-filming-locations-2026). If you only have a few hours in the capital, use the [Seoul filming-location guide](/blog/k-drama-filming-locations-in-seoul-2026).
