## Start with the park

The [official tourism route](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/hdrdslt/hdrdsltView.do?crsSn=346602) identifies Seoul Namsan Park as a stop where Jisoo and Lisa saw cherry blossoms on BLACKPINK House. The public park is the destination here. Save 남산공원 on your map and check which approach gives you a manageable walk.

| Check | Plan the stop |
| --- | --- |
| Approach | Look for the route from your current neighborhood and check slope, stairs and distance. |
| Light | Choose daylight for a first route or dusk for the view, allowing time to come back down. |
| Weather | Hold a flexible indoor stop for rain or poor visibility. Cherry blossom timing depends on the season. |
| Access | Check the terms of any transport or attraction you intend to use inside the area. |
