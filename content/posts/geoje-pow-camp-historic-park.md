Geoje POW Camp Historic Park combines surviving traces of a Korean War prisoner-of-war camp with exhibitions and reconstructions. Approach the site as a place to understand captivity and conflict, and allow time to read the displays rather than treating the setting as an amusement ride. [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=105588)

## What to expect

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| History is the priority | Start with the signed exhibits and read which spaces contain original materials. |
| Mobility or wet weather | Ask which paths and indoor rooms are currently available. |
| Monorail is a priority | Check it separately before purchasing an admission product. |

The historic park stands at **61 Gyeryong-ro, Geoje** (경상남도 거제시 계룡로 61). KTO describes exhibitions about prisoners' lives and the Korean War as well as artifacts. Some outdoor areas and exhibits are reconstructions or later interpretation; look for the site's labels before assuming a structure is an original camp building. Content about war and imprisonment may be distressing for younger children.

Check the [Geoje Marine Tourism Development Corporation](https://www.gmdc.co.kr/) or call **+82-55-639-0625** for the current opening, entry price and any renovation restrictions. The [city’s current page](https://www.geoje.go.kr/index.geoje?menuCd=DOM_000008506001006000) and the current KTO listing both give Tuesday as the usual closure. Check holiday exceptions against today’s operator information. Do not assume an older schedule applies. Monorail operation and its ticket are separate planning decisions: confirm the present package and weather status before purchasing.

## Getting there and accessibility

Search **거제도포로수용소유적공원**. From Gohyeon, check the city [bus route information](https://www.geoje.go.kr/index.geoje?menuCd=DOM_000000106003001004) for a terminal–POW camp stop or take a taxi; verify the current stop direction and return schedule. The park is on a hillside. A [KTO accessibility guide](https://korean.visitkorea.or.kr/detail/rem_detail.do?cotid=07a5675b-8bd2-4fe8-bd45-c199b298de4d) describes parts of the site as difficult for wheelchair users; contact the park for the current route and help available before visiting.

For a quieter or weather-affected visit, ask which exhibition spaces are open before departure. Carry water, wear appropriate walking shoes and leave room for the return journey. The park and the Gohyeon bus terminal are different places, so check your last bus rather than relying on a short connection.
