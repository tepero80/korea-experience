**Andong Station and Andong Bus Terminal stand next to each other on Gyeongdong-ro, away from the old downtown station.** For a first stop at Andong Old Market or a central hotel, check a city bus from **안동역(안동터미널)** to the actual market entrance or hotel. A taxi is simpler with luggage, a late arrival, or a short connection.

## Save the right building and destination

| Start or stop | What to save |
| --- | --- |
| **Rail** | **안동역**, 경상북도 안동시 경동로 122-16. Use the current rail station for your train back. |
| **Coach** | **안동터미널**, 경상북도 안동시 경동로 130. Return to this building for your booked intercity or express coach. |
| **City center** | Use **안동구시장** for the Old Market area, **안동찜닭골목, 번영길 30** for the jjimdak alley, or the Korean street address for your hotel. |

Andong City's [station listing](https://www.tourandong.com/map/resource.cshtml?seq=1149) records the 2020 move. The [terminal operator](https://www.buspia.co.kr/terminal/andong/main.php) gives the coach terminal address and booking links. Some street directions still mention **구 안동역**, the former railway station in the center. Use it as a local landmark when relevant. Your train uses the current station next to the coach terminal.

## Take the city bus that fits your stop

1. Search the exact downtown destination from your arrival building. For the Old Market, the [Korea Tourism Organization’s market guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=977&vcontsId=174447) estimates about 20 minutes by bus from either building. Add time to reach the city bus stop, wait, and walk from your downtown stop.
2. Check both the bus number and the next stops in the [city route system](https://bus.andong.go.kr/route). The station and terminal are adjoining buildings, but the side of the road and direction still matter. Use current stop signs and [city timetable notices](https://www.andong.go.kr/portal/bbs/list.do?mId=0606010100&ptIdx=66) if the route varies.
3. Follow the walking route from your chosen stop. The national tourism listing gives [the address for Jjimdak Alley](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=14987). Save your hotel address separately if you intend to leave bags before eating.

## Plan the last leg back before exploring

Look up your return from the actual hotel, market entrance or riverbank where you will finish. Add time for walking to your stop, waiting, and reaching the right boarding area. If the local bus leaves little margin, arrange a taxi and show the driver the address of the rail or coach building. For an onward coach, verify the departure bay and booking through the terminal operator.

For a short outdoor first evening, see the [Wolyeonggyo bridge walk](/blog/andong-wolyeonggyo-evening-walk). If you are arriving in time for a rural trip, [compare Hahoe Village and Dosan Seowon](/blog/andong-hahoe-or-dosan-seowon-without-a-car) before traveling into town. A suitable service can make it easier to start from the rail and coach hub.
