## Compare the whole trip before buying

1. Search every departure in [Korail’s official booking site](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/main) and write down the standard-class fare per traveler. Mark which trips are operated by Korail.
2. Group the covered trips by **travel day**. The [operator’s pass guide](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/passengerGuide/ticketTypes/korailpass) describes selecting the travel dates within ten days from the pass start. Check that each date you need can be selected.
3. Compare the sum of only those covered fares with the pass. Add SRT, local transit and other separately paid tickets to both budgets where applicable. Keep seat availability and any extra charges in the comparison.

| Selected days | Published adult pass price |
| --- | --- |
| Two | ₩131,000 |
| Three | ₩186,000 |
| Four | ₩234,000 |
| Five | ₩275,000 |

Prices above are shown in [Korail’s pass guide](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/passengerGuide/ticketTypes/korailpass) and [its published price notice](https://info.korail.com/info/selectBbsNttView.do?bbsNo=199&key=911&nttNo=25965). Check the total shown for your group in the current portal. Avoid using an older group discount or an old consecutive-pass price as the baseline.

**Worked example:** Suppose two departures you can actually buy cost ₩59,800 each. Individual tickets total **₩119,600**, or **₩11,400 less** than the adult two-day pass. If you add a hypothetical eligible third ride costing ₩20,000 on the same two travel days, the total becomes **₩139,600**. The pass then costs **₩8,600 less** before considering seats, upgrades or extras. Use the prices for your own departures in place of these numbers.

## Check coverage and seats separately

The [Korail pass guide](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/passengerGuide/ticketTypes/korailpass) excludes **SRT, metropolitan rail and temporary tourist trains**. If the train you need is SRT, price it separately and check the [KTX and SRT comparison](/blog/ktx-vs-srt-high-speed-train-comparison-and-booking) before you travel to the station. Korail describes [Pass+ local transport](https://info.korail.com/info/selectBbsNttView.do?bbsNo=199&key=911&nttNo=25965) as a separately paid, prepaid card feature. Budget local fares separately.

Korail specifies **up to two seat reservations per person, per day**. When planning several legs in a day, decide which ones need seats. Check individual departures first if you need to sit together, use specific seating or take a holiday train. Decide before buying whether you can accept the conditions if the preferred seats are unavailable. A pass purchase alone does not secure a seat.

## Reserve and confirm each departure

After buying, use **My Ticket → Seat Reservation** in [Korail’s pass flow](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/passengerGuide/ticketTypes/korailpass) to reserve each needed departure. Save the pass and seat reservation and carry the passport used for the booking. Read the [pass FAQ](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/korailpass/faq) before deciding to change dates or cancel. Use the station on the final ticket, and save the carriage and seat details separately for each leg.
