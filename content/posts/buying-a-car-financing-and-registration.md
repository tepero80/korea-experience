## Agree who will file before you pay

Ask for the vehicle identification number (VIN), plate number and seller’s name as shown on the registration record. Check that the car and sale documents match. Ask the registration office what identity and address papers it accepts for you and whether it can process your particular sale online. [Car365’s transfer guide](https://www.car365.go.kr/ccpt/cvlcpt/guid/detailGuidance.do?cvlcptCd=4) lists different forms by sale type and lists the Residence Card for foreign applicants visiting the office.

| Sale type | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Dealer | Ask whether the dealer files the transfer for you. Get the dealer-form transfer certificate and the filing date in writing. Confirm how you will receive the completed registration. |
| Private | Confirm which direct-sale transfer form and seller proof to bring. Ask if the seller can confirm the sale in person, or whether the registry needs a sale-specific seal or signature certificate. |
| Proxy | Ask the office which authorization and identity copies it accepts from the person filing on your behalf. |

[Seoul’s vehicle registration guide](https://world.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/vehicle-purchase-insurance/) explains when the seller’s seal certificate may be omitted, including a direct sale the seller confirms in person at the district office. [Article 26 of the Vehicle Registration Decree](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=118237) sets the deadline for a purchase at **15 days from the date of purchase**. Ask what counts as that date in your paperwork, and record the deadline even if the dealer handles the filing.

## Put insurance and payment on the same timeline

1. Ask the insurer to confirm the vehicle, who may drive it, and the date and time coverage begins. Car365 checks compulsory insurance before letting you file online. Confirm what proof the office needs if you file in person.
2. Ask for an itemized total covering the car price, any dealer charge, tax, registration fees and any bond cost that applies. If you need financing, get the lender’s actual written terms before agreeing to buy. Compare the down payment, total amount repaid, fees and early repayment terms.
3. Agree when you will receive the keys and when you will get proof the transfer is complete. Keep the agreement, proof of payment and your completed registration. Arrange a different way to collect the car if you cannot legally drive it yet.

## Check the inspection date and your licence separately

Ask for the next statutory inspection date for that exact car. The [Korea Transportation Safety Authority’s inspection table](https://main.kotsa.or.kr/portal/contents.do?menuCode=01010200) varies the interval by vehicle and use. A pre-purchase mechanical inspection helps you assess the car’s condition before paying, even when its statutory inspection is current.

If you will drive the car, confirm which [Korean licence, exchange or visitor permit rules](/blog/getting-a-korean-driver-license-as-a-foreigner-2026) apply to you before arranging pickup.
