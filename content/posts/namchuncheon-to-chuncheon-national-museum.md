If you are coming by train for an indoor visit to **Chuncheon National Museum**, use **Namchuncheon Station (남춘천역)** when your service stops there. Save the museum’s Korean name and address before leaving the station. The museum lists bus **100** from Namchuncheon Station to its rear-stop area. Check the live departure and use a taxi if the wait, walking approach or weather would take too much of your visit.

## Find the museum from your actual station

Save **국립춘천박물관, 강원특별자치도 춘천시 우석로 70**. The [museum’s directions](https://chuncheon.museum.go.kr/kor/sub01_0109.do) show the bus approach from Namchuncheon and the stop **박물관후문**. Check which side of the road to board on in your map app. Match the stop name and the bus’s direction, especially if the route planner shows more than one service number. Use [Chuncheon’s traffic portal](https://www.chuncheon.go.kr/traffic/) to check route notices and the link to live bus information.

Chuncheon Station (춘천역) is a different stop from Namchuncheon. If your ticket brings you to Chuncheon Station, compare the route from there rather than following the Namchuncheon instructions. If you change plans in town when the weather turns, set your real starting point in the map and compare a bus with a taxi to the main building. Plan the trip back separately if you have a reserved train.

## Use the main building as your indoor plan

The [museum’s visitor information](https://chuncheon.museum.go.kr/kor/sub01_0101.do) lists the main building as **free, 09:00–18:00**, with last admission at **17:30**. It usually closes Mondays, January 1 and the days of Seollal and Chuseok. When a Monday is an eligible public holiday, the museum opens that day and normally closes the next weekday. Confirm holiday notices before changing a train booking or counting on a Monday visit.

| Time | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Under an hour | Pick one gallery in the main building. Leave time to get to your next stop. |
| A longer visit | Ask what is on the media wall, then choose a gallery before or after a screening. |
| With children | Check the children’s building hours and the rules for the activity you want. |
| Returning by train | Set your leave time before you enter. Check the return from your actual exit. |

Choose a gallery about Gangwon history before trying to cover the entire collection. The [English museum site](https://chuncheon.museum.go.kr/eng/index.do) lists the exhibition areas. The [museum home page](https://chuncheon.museum.go.kr/kor/index.do) lists hourly screenings from **09:00 to 17:00**. Its [brochure](https://chuncheon.museum.go.kr/thumbnail/fileInfo/FI_202412160124109831.pdf) locates the media wall in the main hall. Ask the desk which screenings are running and fit the rest of your outing around the next one if it suits you.

The [children’s museum page](https://chuncheon.museum.go.kr/prog/yeyakInfo/CHILDMUSEUM/kor/sub01_0204/list.do) posts different hours and a **17:00** entry cutoff. Check specific activities as well as building entry. The visitor page lists elevators from the children’s museum area toward the main entrance. Call the main desk at [+82 33 260 1500](tel:+82332601500) to confirm the route from the stop or drop-off you will use.
