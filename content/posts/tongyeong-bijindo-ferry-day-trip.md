Bijindo is an island trip, so choose the return sailing before planning the beach. The boat leaves **Tongyeong Port Passenger Terminal**, not the cable-car station or every harbour in the city. The booking site's [directions](https://www.maemuldoferry.com/useinfo/location/) give **234 Tongyeonghaean-ro** (경남 통영시 통영해안로 234). The Korea Tourism Organization likewise identifies the passenger terminal as the departure point for Bijindo. [KTO](https://korean.visitkorea.or.kr/detail/rem_detail.do?cotid=b8c9634a-26d3-4a7a-acd8-2735fc5e071b)

## Choose both directions

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Outbound | Use Tongyeong Port Passenger Terminal and check the destination pier. |
| Return | Book or confirm the return from the pier you intend to use. |
| Weather delay | Check updates and use a mainland backup rather than rushing another sailing. |

The [booking site’s directions](https://www.maemuldoferry.com/useinfo/location/) list Hansol Shipping (한솔해운) at Tongyeong Port Passenger Terminal. The [date-based booking page](https://www.maemuldoferry.com/) shows Bijindo Oehang departures and returns. Naehang and Oehang are different pier names. Verify both directions and the exact pier on your ticket before paying. KTO’s route feature gives an estimated sailing time of about 40 minutes. Pick the return first because it determines how long you can stay.

Take identification acceptable to the operator for every passenger. Its [boarding FAQ](https://www.maemuldoferry.com/) requires identification and describes documentation for children; international visitors should check the accepted documents and name details before booking. Arrive with time for ticketing and boarding checks, and ask the seller about check-in deadlines, return-ticket procedure and cancellation rules.

## Weather and the beach

Rain does not necessarily cancel a boat, but weather can disrupt sailings. Check the operator on the day and have a mainland alternative. The [Korea Maritime Transportation Safety Authority](https://www.komsa.or.kr/prog/pssgCurState/kor/sub03_0202/list.do?jbcd=L&pageIndex=1&pageUnit=100) also publishes passenger-vessel operating information; use the operator for final confirmation.

[KTO describes Bijindo Beach](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=188864) as having sand on one side and pebbles on the other. That is a place to enjoy the scenery, not a guarantee that swimming is supervised or conditions are safe. Carry water and sun protection, keep the last return sailing in mind, and check local park notices before taking a longer trail.

## Do not mix up ports

Tongyeong has several departure points. The terminal above serves the Bijindo route described here; a ticket from another port needs its own directions. For an overnight stay near the harbour, check how you will reach the terminal at your actual check-in time. If the sea forecast is uncertain, leave flexibility in your accommodation and onward transport plans.
