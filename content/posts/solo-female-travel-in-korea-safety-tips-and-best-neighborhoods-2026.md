A convenient neighborhood can make a solo trip easier, but its name cannot guarantee a safe stay. Compare the exact accommodation entrance, your likely arrival time and the route you will actually use.

## Choose by your day

| Base | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| Myeongdong | Your specific station exit and walk to the property. |
| Hongdae | Evening noise and the final walk to your room. |
| Jongno | Your route to the places you plan to visit most. |

[VISITKOREA identifies Myeong-dong](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=88011) as a shopping district with a street beginning at Myeong-dong Station on Line 4. The choices above are route-planning prompts, not safety rankings. Before booking, ask the property about late check-in and how to reach the entrance; save its address and contact details offline.

Before an evening out, check the last useful connection and a backup route. Share your plan with someone you trust if useful, keep your phone charged, and move toward staffed public places if you feel uncomfortable. For immediate police help call **112**; Seoul lists **119** for emergencies and **1330** for tourism and interpretation in its [emergency guidance](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/living/disaster-evacuation-tips-citizens/emergency-ready-app/). If calling is difficult, ask someone nearby to help and give your location.
