Start on **Line 4 at Myeongdong Station**. Use **Exit 6 for Migliore and the shopping streets**. If Daiso is your only stop, the station-side branch is on the other side of the main road, between Exits 1 and 4. Pick two stops for a short visit and leave some time for checkout.

## A route you can follow with a short list

| Stop | Map search | Why go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Migliore** | **밀리오레 명동점**, 서울 중구 퇴계로 115 | Start near Exit 6 for clothes and accessories. Browse floors 1–2. Individual shop hours can differ. |
| **Daiso Myeongdong Main** | **다이소 명동본점**, 서울 중구 명동길 43 | Find this branch on Myeongdong-gil if you are continuing toward Olive Young. |
| **Olive Young Myeongdong Town** | **올리브영 명동 타운**, 서울 중구 명동길 53 | Check brands and package sizes on floors 1–2. Use the branch page for current hours. |
| **Daiso Myeongdong Station** | **다이소 명동역점**, 서울 중구 퇴계로 134-1 | Choose this separate branch between Exits 1 and 4 if Daiso is your priority. KTO describes it as a 12-floor shop. |

Sources for the route: [KTO's Migliore listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=220958), [Daiso Myeongdong Main](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=146952), [Daiso Myeongdong Station](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=194390) and [KTO’s Olive Young Myeongdong Town listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=263&menuSn=929&vcontsId=248785).

## Use the time you actually have

- **For clothes or accessories:** start near Exit 6. Take measurements in centimeters and check the exchange policy before buying.
- **For beauty:** go straight to your target branch, then look at one nearby store if you still need a comparison. Use the [Olive Young in-store guide](/blog/olive-young-shopping-guide-must-buy-k-beauty-products) for packs, checkout and receipts.
- **For small gifts:** choose the Daiso branch that fits your approach. Leave heavy or fragile purchases until last. If bad weather or limited mobility changes the route, pick one branch and check its entrance and floor access before heading out.

## Keep receipts separate from the goods you pack

Ask participating sellers about immediate refunds or refund paperwork before paying. The [Korea Tourism Organization's guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=140736) starts at **₩15,000 per qualifying receipt**. Seller participation and traveler eligibility matter, and the actual refund can vary.

If the shop issues paperwork, follow the [airport tax refund guide](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) before checking luggage. If you want boutiques or an independent design district instead, compare [Seoul's shopping neighborhoods](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam).
