**If you are sleeping in old Jeju City, start dinner at Dongmun Traditional Market and keep a nearby sit-down option saved.** For the market, search **동문재래시장**, 제주시 관덕로14길 20. Save the route back to your accommodation before ordering, especially if you are relying on a bus or carrying takeaway.

## Pick your dinner format

| If you want | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A few local dishes | Browse the evening stalls and ask which items are being cooked to order. Try a savory item and one fruit or snack before buying more. |
| Seats and a meal | Save a restaurant near the market before leaving your hotel. Confirm the last order and whether a table fits your group. |
| Takeaway | Ask for **포장** and choose something easy to carry. Ask for sauce separately if you have a long walk back. |
| A late arrival | Check your actual arrival and dinner times together. Carry a backup meal if the market stalls or restaurant kitchen may close before you reach them. |

For ideas, Jeju's tourism board describes abalone gimbap, skewers and Hallabong drinks around the market. Individual vendors and menus change. Treat the market as a place to choose from what's open that evening. [Visit Jeju’s Dongmun night-market food guide](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/themtour/view?contentsid=CNTS_000000000022892)

## Check the stall and pick a return route

The Korea Tourism Organization lists the traditional market and the night market separately. Check the evening information for your travel date, and confirm that the stall you want is open before treating it as dinner. The map pin locates the market area. A specific stall or gate may need a second search. [Traditional-market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=459&vcontsId=91650) · [Night-market visitor information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=491&vcontsId=140737)

If walking from your hotel, save your return directions before entering a busy aisle. For a bus, open both directions in the [Jeju bus information system](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/?lang=en) and check the evening departure from the actual boarding stop. Add a taxi fallback if you cannot confirm a usable return. Having the Korean hotel name and road address ready makes it easier to explain the destination.

## Save one sit-down fallback

For a pork meal, the Korea Tourism Organization lists **Dombedon (돔베돈)** at **제주시 관덕로15길 25**, near the market. It lists a Jeju black-pork set and several cuts. Call [+82 64 753 0008](tel:+82647530008) to check seats, minimum portions and last order before you walk over. If you need a different diet, save a second option that can explain its ingredients and preparation. [Dombedon visitor listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=86091)

Useful questions at a stall are **포장해 주실 수 있나요?** for takeaway and **이거 하나에 얼마예요?** for the price of one. If ingredients or shared cooking surfaces are a concern, ask before paying. Keep a meal you can verify as a fallback when a vendor cannot answer clearly.
