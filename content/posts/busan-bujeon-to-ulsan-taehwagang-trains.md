For a day trip from Busan to Ulsan, start by comparing the Donghae Line metropolitan train and the KTX-Eum from **Bujeon Station (부전역)** to **Taehwagang Station (태화강역)**. They serve the same broad city-to-city journey, but have different ticketing arrangements and may leave at very different times. Check the service for your travel date before choosing.

## Which train fits your plan?

- **KTX-Eum:** Search Bujeon → Taehwagang in [Korail](https://www.korail.com/) for your date. Check the fare and conditions and buy the selected train ticket.
- **Metropolitan train:** Check current Donghae Line departures and confirm accepted payment or single-journey tickets.
- **A tight return:** Compare actual return departures and allow time from your Ulsan destination back to Taehwagang.

Korail's [metropolitan rail operations page](https://info.korail.com/info/contents.do?key=863) identifies the Donghae Line from Bujeon to Taehwagang. The Korea Tourism Organization also describes a KTX-Eum option on this route. Times, prices and departure frequencies can change; neither option should be assumed to be available at a particular time without a current search.

## Find the right Bujeon station

Busan Metro Line 1's Bujeon station and Korail's Bujeon railway station are distinct locations. If arriving on Line 1, follow the signs for the Donghae Line or Korail Bujeon and allow time for the walk. Check the current signs and step-free route for your date.

At the railway station, check the train name, destination and boarding instructions. A metropolitan train and a KTX-Eum do not use the same booking process; do not assume a transit-card tap is a KTX ticket. Ask station staff if a platform or gate is unclear.

## On arrival in Ulsan

**Taehwagang Station** and **Ulsan Station** are different stations. Confirm which one is printed on your booking or route. Taehwagang National Garden is a separate destination from Taehwagang Station: search your actual onward bus or taxi route and choose the garden entrance that fits your plan. Do not assume the station exit is the garden entrance.

For the return, search from the same correct station name. If you miss a reserved train, ask Korail about the ticket's change or refund conditions rather than assuming it can be used on another service.

[Find 태화강역 in Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%ED%83%9C%ED%99%94%EA%B0%95%EC%97%AD) and save the onward journey in your [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary).

## Verify the journey

The [Korea Tourism Organization's Busan and Ulsan guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=1593337) describes both train choices. Korail's [2025 service announcement](https://info.korail.com/info/selectBbsNttView.do?bbsNo=199&key=911&nttNo=25904) describes the Donghae KTX-Eum stopping at Taehwagang. Information checked 9 November 2026. Check live schedules, fares and route conditions before travel.
