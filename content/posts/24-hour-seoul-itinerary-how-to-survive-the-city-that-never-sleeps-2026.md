Make Gyeongbokgung, Gwangjang Market and Dongdaemun your anchors. Save the Korean names and your lodging address before leaving. The blocks give you a starting point for a full day. Keep one optional stop so you can shorten the route in rain, cold or a long queue.

## Set the day around three stops

| Block | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 09:00–11:30 | Start at **Gyeongbokgung (경복궁)**. Allow time for the gate, ticket and the grounds. Add a short optional stop nearby only if the morning runs on time. |
| 12:00–14:00 | Eat at **Gwangjang Market (광장시장)**. Use Line 1 Jongno 5-ga Exit 8 as a market approach listed by Visit Seoul. |
| 15:30–18:00 | Choose a section of **Cheonggyecheon** or a confirmed exhibit at **Dongdaemun Design Plaza (동대문디자인플라자)**. End near a station or pickup point for your lodging. |

The [palace operator](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E702000000.do) lists 09:00 opening on regular days, seasonal last admission and a Tuesday closure. If your only full day is Tuesday, check the [operator’s schedule](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do) for **Deoksugung (덕수궁)** or move the palace visit and begin with a confirmed indoor stop. Check holiday exceptions and temporary notices for the exact date.

If you add Bukchon, check the [city’s red-zone map and restrictions](https://english.seoul.go.kr/notice-on-restrictions-for-tourist-visits-to-bukchon-special-management-area-effective-from-november-2024/). The restriction applies to the designated area between 17:00 and 10:00 the next day. Start after 10:00 and keep paths and doorways clear. Skip the detour if the palace visit took longer than planned.

## Check the food alley, then plan the way back

The [official market listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/Tourist-Attractions-list1/Gwangjang-Market/ENP000286) gives separate hours for the food alley and other sections. Confirm your actual stall and ask about portion size and payment before ordering. For a longer meal stop, use the [Gwangjang food guide](/blog/gwangjang-market-complete-food-guide).

[Visit Seoul’s evening route](https://english.visitseoul.net/editorspicks/2026-seoulnight/ENN3bjkpz) describes a walk along Cheonggyecheon toward DDP. Check a suitable way down to the stream and current path conditions. In heavy rain, choose street level or use the subway to Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station. Check the [city’s night bus information](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-expands-citywide-night-bus-service/) and the last connection to your hotel, including any transfer. If you still want dinner, verify the kitchen’s last order using the [late-night Seoul guide](/blog/late-night-dining-24-hour-restaurants-in-seoul).
