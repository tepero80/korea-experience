## Write down what would make the decision clearer

The [NHS decision guide](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/advice/cosmetic-procedure-right-for-me/) advises considering your reasons and expectations and allowing time to decide. Ask what the consultation can realistically clarify. If you receive support for a mental-health concern, ask that clinician to discuss how the decision fits your situation.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | Which specific concern would I like to discuss, and what changes are realistic? |
| Pressure | What decisions can wait? Ask about a cancellation period or how to postpone. |
| Support | Who could help me think it through without relying on the clinic’s sales process? |
| Aftercare | Who would provide physical and practical support before travel home? |

## Keep an itinerary that works either way

Check the [NHS advice on surgery abroad](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/advice/cosmetic-surgery-abroad/) about arranging care before returning. Keep accommodation and transport flexible where possible, and ask for dated terms in writing. A consultation may be worthwhile even if you decide to wait.
