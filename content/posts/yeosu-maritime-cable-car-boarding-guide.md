Yeosu Maritime Cable Car (여수 해상케이블카) crosses the water between Jasan Park on the mainland and Dolsan Park. Choose your boarding side and whether you need a return before buying a ticket: a one-way ride leaves you on the opposite side of the water.

## Choose the station and the ticket

| Your priority | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Expo Station or Odongdo | Start by checking Jasan Park’s Haeya Station (자산 해야정류장). Check how to reach the boarding entrance. |
| Staying on Dolsan | Check Nora Station (돌산 놀아정류장) and current parking arrangements. |
| Return to your start | Choose the round trip and leave time for the last return. |
| Continue on the other shore | Check the onward bus or taxi before taking a one-way ride. |

[Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=220123) identifies Haeya at Jasan and Nora at Dolsan. Search for the station name rather than only the park: a parking area or park entrance is not necessarily the boarding door. Compare the route from your own accommodation before deciding which side is more convenient.

## Standard or crystal?

The [KTO attraction listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=73202) describes standard and glass-floor crystal cabins. Choose the glass floor if that view suits your group, or the standard cabin if you prefer an ordinary floor. Check today's prices, ticketing cut-off, cabin availability and operating notices with the [cable-car operator](https://www.yeosucablecar.com/) or by phone at **061-664-7301**. Published official sources give different hours, so the operator’s current notice matters more than an old timetable.

Allow extra time at busy periods. If wind or maintenance interrupts service, a coastal walk may be an alternative, subject to its own weather conditions; [Odongdo](/blog/yeosu-expo-station-to-odongdo) is one option. Add your chosen station and return plan to the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
