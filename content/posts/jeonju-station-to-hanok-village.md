**Start with the last walk to your destination.** For the main heritage sights, look up a city bus from Jeonju Station to **전동성당·한옥마을**. If you have heavy bags, an inconvenient bus departure or a hotel deep in the lanes, ask for a taxi to a specific drop-off that your host can confirm.

This guide starts at the railway station **전주역**. The [city arrival guide](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000106012001000) distinguishes it from the express, intercity and airport limousine terminals. Leave those names out of your map search unless that is where your own ticket arrives.

## Choose a useful drop-off

| Your first stop | Where to aim | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gyeonggijeon or Jeondong Cathedral | **전동성당·한옥마을** on the city bus, or **경기전** as a taxi destination | Check the bus direction and the time you will actually reach the ticket gate. |
| A hanok stay | The property address plus the nearest vehicle-accessible drop-off | Send your host the Korean booking address and ask which corner to use. |
| Nambu Market | **남부시장** or an entrance confirmed by your host or map | Keep the luggage drop separate if the lane is busy and the first stop is lunch. |

## Take the bus from the railway station

Look up **전주역첫마중길** and **전동성당·한옥마을** in the [Jeonju Traffic Information Center](https://its.jeonju.go.kr/its/bus2.view). Compare a current departure on **119** and its stop sequence. The [city’s printed transport map](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/images/file/map/01_mapTour_kor.pdf) also lists **79** between the railway station and the cathedral area. Check the current stop board or live departure list before relying on a route from the printed map.

1. Exit toward the city side for the stop shown in your planner. The city and KORAIL announced a **partial opening of station facilities in July 2026** during improvement work. Follow current signs instead of using an old station photograph as a diagram. [City station notice](https://www.jeonju.go.kr/planweb/board/view.9is?boardUid=ff8080818b5bc5cf018ba8ca7216641f&contentUid=ff8080818990c349018b041a87fe3960&dataUid=9be517a89eb4c412019f69c010bf1b7b)
2. Confirm the next stops in order before you board. A route number alone does not establish that the bus reaches your side of the village.
3. Get off for the landmark you chose. Save the stop and your accommodation address offline in case you lose mobile data.

## When a taxi is the easier choice

Use a taxi when its drop-off saves a difficult walk or the bus wait compresses your visit. Show the driver **전주한옥마을** plus your exact Korean address or a landmark the host has confirmed. Check where you can meet and where the final walk starts. For a wheelchair, a mobility aid or several large bags, ask station staff or your host about a suitable vehicle before relying on a standard taxi.

## Protect the trip back

Start from your actual last stop and look up the **station-bound** side again. If the displayed bus will not leave enough time for traffic, the station approach and the platform, use the taxi option before your margin is gone. Confirm the station name and departure in your rail booking. Save both the village stop and **전주역** in your [trip board](/tools/itinerary).

Once you know where you will get off, use the [Gyeonggijeon and Omokdae walk](/blog/jeonju-gyeonggijeon-omokdae-walking-route) to plan the ticketed visit, or choose a meal with the [Nambu Market guide](/blog/jeonju-nambu-market-eating-plan).
