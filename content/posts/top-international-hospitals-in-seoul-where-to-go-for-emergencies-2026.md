## Call for help in an emergency

| Need | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Medical or fire | [Call 119](tel:119) from Korea. If you cannot make a call, ask someone nearby to call. |
| Police | [Call 112](tel:112) if you need immediate police help. |
| Travel language help | [1330](tel:1330) handles tourism information and interpretation. Call 119 directly for a medical emergency. |

1. Give your location first. Use a Korean street address if you know it. Name the hotel, station exit, entrance or nearby landmark and the floor if useful.
2. Say what happened and how many people need help. For English, ask for English and follow the dispatcher’s instructions.
3. If staff or another person can assist, ask them to meet responders at the correct entrance. Keep your phone available.

- **구급차를 불러 주세요.** Please call an ambulance.
- **여기는 …입니다.** This is … .

## If you are arranging a hospital visit

For a visit you can plan, check the [official E-Gen emergency department finder](https://www.e-gen.or.kr/egen/search_emergency_room.do) if you need to locate an **응급실**. Listings and service availability can change. Check the department and exact entrance with the hospital. For routine care, confirm clinic hours and whether in-person or telephone language assistance is available.

Keep your accommodation’s Korean address and your insurer’s assistance number available offline. If you have time before a planned visit, bring your ID, relevant medicine details and insurance information. For urgent help, make the 119 call first.
