Korea’s long-distance buses are useful when a train does not stop near your destination or you want a direct connection between regional cities. Start by searching the exact trip date, then choose the route and departure terminal shown by the booking result. The words **express bus** and **intercity bus** describe different route patterns, but the terminal and departure shown on your ticket are what you need to follow.

For visitors, the Korea Tourism Organization currently lists **Klook and Hanpass** for express-bus booking and **Hanpass** for intercity-bus booking, with English and other language support. It also lists foreign-issued cards as accepted for bus tickets at machines and counters. Route availability and ticket issue rules still depend on the specific departure, so confirm the booking and whether you need a printed ticket before you leave for the terminal. ([KTO foreign-visitor transportation guide, PDF](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/contents/travel/KoreaTransportationGuide_enu.pdf))

## Choose the route type by the trip you need

**Express buses** mainly use expressways and usually travel between the departure terminal and their destination, with rest-area stops possible. **Intercity buses** may use expressways and national roads and can stop at towns along the way. Intercity services cover many smaller destinations and can connect regional cities directly. Some terminals serve both types; others are separate. ([Korea Tourism Organization: Express Bus & Intercity Bus](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140657))

Use this quick decision:

- If you already know the destination terminal, search it on the booking service and follow the departure point shown.
- If one service does not show the route, check the other bus type’s official reservation system or the other intercity reservation site. Route coverage differs by operator and terminal; a missing result does not prove there is no bus.
- If you are choosing between a bus and a train, compare the actual departure and arrival locations and times for your date. This guide does not promise that buses are always cheaper or faster.

The Korean Tourism Organization links express schedules and reservations to [KOBUS](https://www.kobus.co.kr/main.do). For intercity services, it lists [TxBus](https://intercitybuse.tmoney.co.kr/main.do) and [BusTago](https://www.bustago.or.kr/newweb/en/index.do). The KTO’s visitor-facing guide also lists [Klook](https://www.klook.com/korea-bus/) and [Hanpass](https://go.hanpass.com/) for travelers who need a multilingual booking flow. Check the current route, language, payment and ticket-issue options on the service itself before paying.

## Book the bus for your date

1. **Search the exact departure and destination names.** City names can have more than one terminal. Check the full terminal name in the result, not just the city.
2. **Select your travel date and departure.** Schedules, available seats, fares and booking windows vary by route. Use the live operator or booking page instead of relying on old route tables.
3. **Check how you will receive the ticket.** Depending on the route and booking channel, you may board with the mobile ticket or need to collect a printed ticket at the departure terminal. Confirm this in the booking confirmation and the service’s instructions. ([KTO bus-use guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140657))
4. **Confirm the reservation is complete.** The BusTago reservation guide specifically tells customers to check reservation status after payment if a connection or browser error interrupts the confirmation. ([BusTago English service](https://www.bustago.or.kr/newweb/en/index.do))
5. **Save the confirmation and terminal details.** Keep the departure terminal’s Korean name/address and your ticket accessible offline. Treat a payment screen alone as insufficient proof of a confirmed seat; check the booking lookup or confirmation.

If a route is not offered online, the KTO lists ticket offices and terminal kiosks as other purchase channels. Its current foreign-visitor transportation guide marks foreign-issued cards as accepted at bus ticket machines and counters. Availability may differ by terminal or route, so keep another payment option available where possible and ask the terminal staff if the ticketing method is unclear. ([KTO foreign-visitor transportation guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/contents/travel/KoreaTransportationGuide_enu.pdf))

## Check your Seoul departure terminal twice

Seoul has several long-distance terminals, and express and intercity buses to a similar destination can leave from different places. The Korea Tourism Organization lists these main terminals and nearby subway stations:

- **Seoul Express Bus Terminal (서울고속버스터미널):** Express Bus Terminal Station, Lines 3, 7 and 9.
- **Central City (센트럴시티터미널):** Express Bus Terminal Station, Lines 3, 7 and 9.
- **Seoul Nambu Terminal (서울남부터미널):** Nambu Terminal Station, Line 3.
- **Dong Seoul Terminal (동서울종합터미널):** Gangbyeon Station, Line 2.

These are orientation aids, not a substitute for the address on your ticket. Central City and Seoul Express Bus Terminal share a subway station but have distinct departure areas. Paste the Korean name into [NAVER Map](https://map.naver.com/) and compare the result with your booking. Check the exact terminal and platform for your date in the operator or booking service, then verify the departure board on arrival. ([KTO terminal list](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140657))

**Dong Seoul Terminal update:** Seoul has a modernization project for the terminal, and the city has discussed temporary terminal arrangements as part of its planning. This guide does not give a relocation date or assume a change has taken effect. Before traveling, check the [terminal’s current notices](https://www.ti21.co.kr/) and use the exact departure address on your ticket; see the [Seoul project status page](https://news.seoul.go.kr/citybuild/archives/537004) and [city announcement on the modernization plan](https://www.seoul.go.kr/news/news_report.do?nttNo=448644).

## At the terminal and on the bus

- Follow the booking service’s ticket-collection instructions. If it requires a printed ticket, allow time to collect it at the departure terminal.
- Check the departure board for your platform. The KTO advises passengers to board at the displayed platform, typically five to ten minutes before departure. ([KTO bus-use guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140657))
- Read the ticket for seat, departure time and any boarding instructions. Keep the ticket until you reach your destination.
- Do not assume your bus has a restroom or that a rest stop is guaranteed. Use the terminal facilities before departure and check with the operator if you need a stop.
- Bus classes and fare categories vary by route. Compare the options shown for your trip rather than relying on a fixed price or a promised seat layout.

## If something goes wrong

**Your route does not appear:** Check the other bus reservation system (express versus intercity), then check the other listed intercity site. If it still does not appear, ask the departure terminal whether that route is sold there or runs on your date. The KTO notes that routes change and recommends checking in advance.

**You paid but have no ticket confirmation:** Use the platform’s reservation lookup before paying again. BusTago’s official service guide cautions that a network interruption can obscure whether a reservation succeeded. If the booking is still unclear, contact the platform or terminal using the contact information on its official page.

**The ticket says to print:** Go to the departure terminal’s ticket office or kiosk with the booking details and follow the booking service’s collection instructions. Do not assume that a reservation number or payment receipt is itself a boarding ticket.

**You are unsure which terminal to use:** Compare the complete terminal names and addresses on the ticket and map. In Seoul, the major terminals are not all at the same location. The KTO warns travelers to double-check their boarding point.
