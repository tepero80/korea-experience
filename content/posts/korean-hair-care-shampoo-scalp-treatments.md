Decide whether you want shampoo, a conditioning product or a separate scalp product. The [Dr.FORHAIR product catalog](https://www.drforhair.com/collections/all) shows examples including shampoo, exfoliator, tonic and a conditioning mask. Use it to recognize the formats, then check the label, size and local price on the item you would actually buy.

## Find the format on the shelf

| Korean term | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| 샴푸 | Shampoo. Check size, fragrance and rinse directions. |
| 트리트먼트 | Treatment or conditioning product. Check where to apply it and whether it is rinsed out. |
| 두피 스케일러 | Scalp exfoliating product. Check the exact use directions and precautions. |
| 두피 토닉 | Scalp tonic. Check whether the label says to leave it on or rinse it out. |

Look at **전성분** for the full ingredients and **사용상의 주의사항** for precautions. If you avoid a fragrance or ingredient, check the actual sealed bottle or box. Use the [Korean cosmetics label guide](/blog/korean-skincare-ingredient-labels-for-visitors-translation-claims-and-patch-test) if you need help reading it.

## Read the hair-loss wording carefully

The [MFDS category list](https://www.mfds.go.kr/wpge/m_639/de050601I001.do) includes functional cosmetics that may help alleviate hair-loss symptoms. If the box says **탈모 증상 완화**, read the full wording. The [agency’s consumer notice](https://www.mfds.go.kr/brd/m_61/view.do?seq=54352) flags drug-like promises such as preventing hair loss or growing hair. Ask a clinician about hair loss or persistent scalp concerns.

If you are buying a large bottle, check the packed size and your baggage rules before paying. If deciding between branches, use the [Olive Young shopping guide](/blog/olive-young-shopping-guide-must-buy-k-beauty-products) and verify the specific size locally.
