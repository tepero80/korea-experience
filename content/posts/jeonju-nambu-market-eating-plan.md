Nambu Market can be a meal stop on a Jeonju day, but a daytime shop, the Friday–Saturday night market and the early-morning riverside market have different schedules. Pick the experience first and check it before making a special trip; individual sellers can close earlier or operate on different days.

| Topic | Planning information |
| --- | --- |
| Market address | 19-3 Pungnammun 1-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju / 전북특별자치도 전주시 완산구 풍남문1길 19-3 |
| Night market | Korea Tourism Organization’s recent listing says Friday and Saturday 17:00–24:00 |
| Early-morning riverside market | Recent tourism listing says daily 04:00–09:00 between Ssajeon Bridge and Maegokgyo Bridge |
| Contact | Merchants’ association: 063-284-1344 |

The [recent official tourism report](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1593611) and the [market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=75565) describe the area. An older official tourism itinerary lists different night-market hours; the newer report supports the times above, but call the association or check current announcements for your date. Special performances mentioned in reports are not necessarily repeated.

## A low-stress food stop

1. Save **전주 남부시장** and the address in a local map app. Choose a defined entrance and check your actual route, rather than assuming every stall is beside one landmark.
2. Walk the stalls once before ordering. Ask the price, portion and whether you can pay by card at the individual stall; a general listing that says cards are accepted is not proof that every vendor accepts your foreign card.
3. If you want a warm meal, compare a soup restaurant and its current menu with whatever stalls are operating. Ask about stock, sauces, allergens and preparation rather than assuming a familiar dish is vegetarian or safe for a food allergy.
4. For a night market visit, arrive with a backup meal in mind; an official report notes some vendors may sell out before the listed closing. For an early visit, make sure your accommodation and return transport fit the hour.
