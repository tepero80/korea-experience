## Start with the schedule, then choose the exit

Open the [Mapo district outdoor performance calendar](https://www.mapo.go.kr/site/culture/stage_schedule/app_schedule) and look for **홍대, 야외공연 일정표**. Select the date you will actually be there. Match the zone in the listing to signs at street level. A venue can be available for booking even when nobody is scheduled to perform.

| Area | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| R2, Street You Want to Walk | Start at **Hongik University Station Exit 8**. [Visit Seoul’s R2 directions](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Hongdae%20R2%20Busking%20Street_/48933) list about 170 metres to the area. |
| R1, Red Culture Market | Start at **Hongik University Station Exit 9**. [Visit Seoul’s R1 directions](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/HONGDAE-R1/ENPidz0r2) identify a separate event area. |

Look up **홍대 걷고싶은거리**. Use the calendar’s venue details for the final walk. ## Plan a second reason to be there

If you want to see a particular act, use the performer’s own current announcement as an additional check. If the district calendar is empty, plan a meal or shop you would enjoy on its own. Make the street performance a flexible part of the evening.

Where a crowd forms, keep the walking route clear and follow venue instructions. Check your return service before going further from the station. If it rains or the crowd is uncomfortable, save another [Seoul outing](/destinations/seoul) and try Hongdae on a different date.
