**For a history-focused half-day in Daeyeon, pair the Busan Museum with the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea.** The museum offers indoor galleries about the region; the cemetery is an active place of remembrance and requires quiet, respectful behavior. Both have free admission, but their opening schedules differ—especially in winter—so check both before setting out.

## Check the two schedules before you go

| Stop | Hours and closure |
| --- | --- |
| Busan Museum | Tue–Sun, 09:00–18:00; closed Mondays and Jan 1. If Monday is a holiday, the following day is the closure. |
| UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea (UNMCK) | Open 365 days; 09:00–17:00 October–April, 09:00–18:00 May–September. |

Checked on **November 15, 2026** against the official museum and cemetery sites. The cemetery closes earlier in the current October–April season. Check for notices or ceremonies before visiting.

## Plan the visit around the cemetery's winter closing

1. **Check both official pages on the day before.** The cemetery is open daily, while Busan Museum closes Mondays and January 1. Confirm any temporary notices.
2. **Start early enough for the outdoor memorial grounds.** In October through April the cemetery closes at 17:00. Do not leave it for the end of an afternoon itinerary.
3. **Visit Busan Museum for the regional context.** Its official facility page lists galleries covering prehistoric and ancient history as well as later eras and art. Choose the exhibits that interest you rather than trying to cover every room.
4. **Use a current map for the transfer.** Both addresses are on UN Pyeonghwa-ro, but check the actual walking or bus route, weather and your mobility needs. Do not assume the route is sheltered or step-free.
5. **Enter the cemetery as a place of remembrance, not a park.** Keep quiet, stay off the grass, do not eat, smoke, exercise, ride, litter, pick flowers or bring pets. Follow the posted dress code and all staff directions.

The sequence above is a planning suggestion, not an official tour route. In winter, leave a buffer before the cemetery's 17:00 closing; daylight and walking comfort can change with weather.

## Getting to each site

### Busan Museum

The museum lists **Daeyeon Station, Line 2, Exit 3**, followed by about a 5-minute walk toward UN Rotary. Bus 134 serves the museum stop from Busan Station/Nampo-dong; buses 68 or 138 serve the stop from Seomyeon/Gaya. Confirm the live route and stop in your transit app.

### UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea

The cemetery lists **Daeyeon Station, Line 2, Exit 3**, then about a 15-minute walk. It also lists Bus 68 from Seomyeon and Bus 134 from Busan Station to the UNMCK stop. The city tourism page lists additional buses; check the cemetery's own directions for the latest route.

## What to expect at the museum

Busan Museum is an indoor cultural-history stop. Its official facility information describes the Dongnae Exhibition Hall's prehistoric, ancient and medieval collections and the Busan Exhibition Hall's Joseon, modern, contemporary and art galleries. The museum also lists wheelchairs and strollers for free use at its information desk; availability can vary, so contact the desk if you need to plan around one.

Hours are 09:00–18:00 Tuesday through Sunday. The linked visitor page does not show a separate last-admission time; if arriving near closing, confirm with the museum rather than assuming entry until 18:00.

## How to visit the cemetery respectfully

UNMCK is a cemetery for fallen UN forces from the Korean War. The official grounds guidance asks visitors to keep quiet, avoid smoking, drinking, food, littering, pets, riding and exercise, stay off the grass, and not pick flowers or plants. Its dress code excludes sweatsuits, overly revealing clothing, string slippers and clothing items designed for exercise. These rules are specific to the cemetery; follow all signs and instructions from staff.

The cemetery's official site includes a map and a virtual tour, which can help you understand the grounds before visiting. Do not photograph or record other visitors in a way that intrudes on their privacy; the site's user guidance prohibits unauthorized capture or screen recording of its live camera feeds.
