Pair two separate places in Daeyeon if you have time for both. **Busan Museum** has indoor regional-history galleries. The **UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea** is a place of remembrance with its own visit rules. Check which venue you want to prioritize and save both addresses.

## Check the two schedules before you go

| Stop | Admission and timing |
| --- | --- |
| Busan Museum | Free. The [museum operator](https://museum.busan.go.kr/busan/bsview) lists 09:00–18:00 and **last admission at 17:00**. Usually closed Mondays and January 1. When Monday is a holiday, the published rule moves the closure to the next working weekday. |
| UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea | Free. The [cemetery operator](https://unmck.or.kr/eng/main/) lists 09:00–17:00 in October–April and 09:00–18:00 in May–September. Check for ceremony or closure notices. |

## Plan the visit around the cemetery's winter closing

If you arrive late in October–April, choose your priority early. The museum’s last admission and the cemetery’s closing both fall at 17:00. Leave room for the walk and for each visit. Use a shorter plan if rain or mobility needs make the link between venues impractical.

## Getting to each site

### Busan Museum

[The city's directions](https://museum.busan.go.kr/busan/bsmap) give **Daeyeon Station, Line 2, Exit 3**, followed by about **10 minutes** toward UN Rotary. Use **부산광역시 남구 유엔평화로 63** for the museum. Ask the information desk ahead of time if you are relying on a loan wheelchair or stroller.

### UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea

The [cemetery’s route](https://unmck.or.kr/eng/02_unmck/?mcode=0502050000) gives the same subway exit and about **15 minutes** to the cemetery. The address is **부산광역시 남구 유엔평화로 93**. ## What to expect at the museum

Pick an exhibition in the Dongnae or Busan hall from the [museum map](https://museum.busan.go.kr/busan/bhfacillity00). Check the current entrance and access conditions separately from the historic courtyard in the photo.

## How to visit the cemetery respectfully

Follow the [operator’s conduct and dress guidance](https://www.unmck.or.kr/eng/02_unmck/?mcode=0502040000). Walk quietly, stay on marked paths and make room for remembrance visits and ceremonies. Check the official notice and staff directions when you arrive.
