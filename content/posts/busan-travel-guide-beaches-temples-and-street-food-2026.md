Start with a single outing, especially when you arrive with bags or late in the day. The [official Busan map](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/contents/region/ENG/Busan_TouristMap_ENG.pdf) shows why the old port, the beaches and Osiria work as different clusters.

## Choose the area that fits your arrival

| First stop | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Busan Station or Nampo | Check the hotel’s bag policy and the Line 1 route. Try Jagalchi and nearby Nampo once your bags are settled. |
| Gimhae Airport | Go to your base first. The [airport’s rail guide](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=3&MENU_ID=110) shows the light rail and Metro Line 2 transfer at Sasang. [Compare the connection](/blog/gimhae-airport-to-busan) and final walk. |
| Ferry terminal | Match the terminal name and address on the booking with your carrier, then choose a cluster. |
| A coastal base | Choose Haeundae for the beach or Gwangalli for the bridge view. Leave time for the return ride. |

For a trip built around screenings, [check Busan’s film-festival ticket and venue plan](/blog/korean-film-festivals-calendar-for-international-visitors) before choosing where to stay.

If your market stop is a full meal, [agree on portions and preparation at Jagalchi](/blog/korean-seafood-delicacies-raw-fish-to-grilled-shellfish) before committing to an order.

## Use one anchor and one optional stop

For the old port, the [official Jagalchi listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=69318) gives **52 Jagalchihaean-ro**. At a seafood counter, ask for the total and whether preparation, seating or sides cost extra before agreeing. Walk into Nampo only if you have time and the weather works.

If the temple is your priority, the [tourism authority](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=108618) lists **86 Yonggung-gil** in Gijang. Check the [Osiria-to-Yonggungsa route](/blog/osiria-to-haedong-yonggungsa-temple) for the last leg and the stairs. Check the actual transfer and return service before fitting it alongside a beach. Keep the beach as a simpler substitute if the temple route will cut too close to a reservation or your way back.

Use the map below to compare the clusters. For a longer visit, the [two-day rail weekend](/blog/seoul-to-busan-weekend-planner-train-neighborhood-base-and-route-choices) turns the central port and coast into separate days.
