**Choose one neighborhood first.** Mullae fits a walk past workshops and art spaces. Ihwa is a hillside residential walk with stairs. Treat the street as the visit: individual works, studio hours and painted shutters can change.

| If you want… | Start here | Practical check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workshops and contemporary art spaces | Mullae, Line 2 Exit 1 | Confirm a specific gallery's hours separately. Leave working entrances clear. |
| Older lanes and hillside views | Ihwa, Line 4 Hyehwa Exit 2 | Expect stairs and narrow residential lanes. Preview the route if slope or access matters. |
| An indoor art option | A scheduled public museum or gallery | Check the current hours and exhibition before changing routes. |

## Mullae · Walk a working neighborhood

[Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Mullae-Art-Village/ENP002789) describes metalworking businesses alongside art studios, with different street scenes when businesses close. Start on public streets around the listed address and let open studios or announced exhibitions guide your stops. Stay out of work areas, and move aside if someone needs to use a loading entrance. Ask before photographing individuals or interiors.

**Save:** 문래창작촌 · 서울 영등포구 도림로128가길 13-8. ## Ihwa · Choose the climb and respect the homes

[The city’s listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/nature/IhwaMuralVillage/ENPz90c1l) gives Hyehwa Exit 2 and notes stairs. [Korea’s tourism organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=138020) describes the residential setting. Use quiet voices, keep doors clear and keep the camera pointed away from inside homes. Plan for the walk and views even if older murals shown in photographs have changed.

**Save:** 이화벽화마을 · 서울 종로구 이화장길 70-11. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%9D%B4%ED%99%94%EB%B2%BD%ED%99%94%EB%A7%88%EC%9D%84) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%9D%B4%ED%99%94%EB%B2%BD%ED%99%94%EB%A7%88%EC%9D%84) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%9D%B4%ED%99%94%EB%B2%BD%ED%99%94%EB%A7%88%EC%9D%84+%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8+%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC+%EC%9D%B4%ED%99%94%EC%9E%A5%EA%B8%B8+70-11)

For a skyline and city-wall walk, check [Naksan Park's route information](https://english.visitseoul.net/nature/naksan-park/ENP003700); its paths do not establish a step-free route all the way from the station. If your approach is difficult or it rains, switch to the [Deoksugung and Seoul Museum of Art route](/blog/deoksugung-to-seoul-museum-of-art) and check which stops are open.
