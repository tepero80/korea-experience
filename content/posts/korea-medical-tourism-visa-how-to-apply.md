## Describe your purpose before choosing a category

[Medical Korea’s information for overseas patients](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/usefulinfo) lists C-3-3 and G-1-10 in its visa overview. Ask the consular office which rules apply to your passport, place of application and actual visit. Use the [Korea Visa Portal](https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?MENU_ID=10102&LANG_TYPE=EN) to locate visa services and cross-check the current application route.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Say whether you intend to attend a consultation, receive treatment, or stay to recuperate. |
| Proof | Ask if the hospital should issue an appointment or treatment confirmation in a specific form. |
| With you | Check a companion’s application and evidence separately. |
| Timing | Ask about processing and rules if your clinical plans change before the trip. |

If you are already in Korea and your purpose changes, use the [Korea status-change checklist](/blog/changing-visa-status-while-in-korea) to check the appropriate process with immigration.

## Match travel dates to confirmed steps

Ask the clinic which dates are booked, which depend on examination and how it can provide a confirmation for the consulate. Confirm any booking or application terms before making payments. Keep copies of submissions and the written answers from the relevant offices.

See the [Korean clinic booking guide](/blog/booking-medical-consultations-in-korea-online-vs-walk-in) to distinguish a discussion from a confirmed appointment.
