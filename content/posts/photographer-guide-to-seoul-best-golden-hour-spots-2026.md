Choose a viewpoint based on the direction you want to photograph and how you will get down after dark. A clear day and an open path matter more than a published rule about the best exposure time.

| The photograph you want | Plan for |
| --- | --- |
| City from above | Check the approach to Namsan, keep the final downhill or transit leg in your plan and choose a view you can reach before sunset. |
| Skyline from the river | Pick one signed public Hangang access point and a return station. The lead photo shows the skyline from the Han River toward Jamwon in December 2023. |
| Reflections or lit streets | Bring a short backup route close to public transit. Check weather and current walkway access first. |

## Find the Jamwon approach

[Seoul’s park office](https://hangang.seoul.go.kr/www/contents/659.do?mid=455) lists **Line 3, Jamwon Station Exit 4**, then about 15 minutes on foot toward Jamwon Hanshin Apartments and **잠원나들목**. ## Leave time to find the composition

Check local sunset for your date and get to the viewpoint with time to walk the approach. Check wind and rain before taking a tripod to the river. Follow signs and leave shared paths clear. Keep your return point saved separately from the photo spot. Use the [Seoul destination guide](/destinations/seoul) to choose another outing if the weather changes.

Check current restrictions and facility rules before bringing a drone. The [Korean official drone portal](https://drone.onestop.go.kr/) gives location and permission guidance. A public viewpoint does not establish flight or filming permission. Plan this outing from public ground if you have not verified the requirements.
