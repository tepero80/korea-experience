## Ask how the two options differ in your case

The [American Dental Association’s patient guide](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/all-topics-a-z/veneers) describes porcelain and composite veneers and advises involving a licensed dentist. Ask what the examination found, the preparation each option would need and how the plan would handle any existing issues.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Exam | Which alternatives should I consider and why is this option being proposed? |
| Visits | Which appointments are for decisions, preparation, fitting or review? |
| Quote | Is the estimate per tooth? Ask what it includes, and what could change. |
| Records | Which records and contact details could a dentist at home use? |

## Reserve time for the plan to change

Use the [UK guidance on healthcare abroad](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/in-hospital-abroad) to prepare questions about ongoing care. Ask the dentist what happens if a fitting or review needs to move and how the team can advise someone who is already back home.
