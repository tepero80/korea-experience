Start with the product type. Oil and balm are useful shelf words, but the directions on the particular package tell you how to use it.

## Match the package label

| Label | What you are holding |
| --- | --- |
| 클렌징 오일 | Cleansing oil |
| 클렌징 밤 | Cleansing balm |
| 클렌징 폼 | Foaming cleanser |
| 젤 클렌저 | Gel cleanser |

## Read how it goes on and comes off

Look for **사용방법** (directions), **전성분** (full ingredients) and **사용상의 주의사항** (precautions). **마른 얼굴** means a dry face, **젖은 얼굴** means a wet face, and **미온수** means lukewarm water. Ask the shop to show the instructions for the exact product if a shelf sign only gives the benefits.

[Sulwhasoo’s Korean oil directions](https://membership.sulwhasoo.com/kr/ko/products/gentle-cleansing-oil.html) show an example that starts on dry skin and then adds water. Innisfree sells a [cleansing balm in a tube](https://www.innisfree.com/kr/ko/dp/product/105478). [COSRX’s gel cleanser](https://www.cosrx.com/products/low-ph-good-morning-gel-cleanser) gives instructions for wet hands and face. Compare the actual labels before assuming all products in a category work the same way.
