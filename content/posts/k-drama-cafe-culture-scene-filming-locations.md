Start with a location that actually appears in the drama. [Incheon Tourism’s location guide](https://eng-itour.incheon.go.kr/cmn/board/BBSMSTR_000000000080/2513bbsDetail.do) identifies the Hanmi Bookstore area and the street by Jayu Park. Its [K-drama feature](https://eng-itour.incheon.go.kr/cmn/board/BBSMSTR_000000000080/6422bbsDetail.do) also lists Jemulpo Club. Allow time for the move between Baedari and the Open Port rather than treating every stop as a single café visit.

## Choose one stop in each area

| Area | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Baedari | Save **한미서점 · 인천 제물포구 금곡로 9** from the city guide. View the bookstore from the street if it is closed or busy. |
| Jayu Park | Search **자유공원**. Incheon identifies the street by the former scene outside G-Mart. Storefronts can change, so use the park as your landmark. |
| Jemulpo Club | Search **제물포구락부** and check the current exhibition and opening conditions before committing to an indoor stop. |

In wet weather or if hills are difficult, keep a short bookstore stop or choose one Open Port venue and a nearby café. Cafés are useful rest stops without a filming claim. Use the [Open Port and Chinatown walking route](/blog/incheon-chinatown-open-port-walking-route) for the hill and rainy-day alternatives.
