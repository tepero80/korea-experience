Follow the Taehwa River into a broad city garden, then choose how much of the Simni Bamboo Grove to explore. The grove stretches for about four kilometres, but you can turn back whenever you wish. Come by daylight for the garden and river; return after sunset for the illuminated Milky Way Trail. [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=14885)

## Plan your visit

| If you want | Pick this plan |
| --- | --- |
| A short daytime walk | Start near the information centre, sample the bamboo grove and return along the river. |
| An evening visit | Check the Milky Way Trail lighting that day and confirm your ride home. |
| A long garden visit | Give the grove more time without committing to the full route. |


The garden is free and open around the clock, according to [Ulsan's garden FAQ](https://www.ulsan.go.kr/s/garden/contents.ulsan?mId=001004004000000000). The information centre and lighting have separate schedules. The city lists evening lighting for the Milky Way Trail, but published closing times differ. Check the current lighting time with the centre before making a special evening trip: +82-52-229-3147. Flower displays change with the season, so treat them as a bonus rather than a promise.

Start at the information centre, take a short section of bamboo path, then return to the riverbank for an open view. A longer visit can follow more of the grove; the stated four-kilometre extent is not an instruction to complete a fixed loop. Bring comfortable shoes and check conditions after heavy rain. The garden spans a large area, so agree on a meeting point before separating.

## Getting there

Search for **태화강 국가정원 안내센터**, 5 Saetgangnam-gil, Jung-gu (울산광역시 중구 샛강남길 5). The [city's directions](https://ulsan.go.kr/s/garden/contents.ulsan?mId=001001004000000000) list buses 523, 713, 763, 1115, 1733 and 9001 from Taehwagang Station to the Taehwagang National Garden / Donggang Hospital stop. Routes change: confirm direction, stop and live service before boarding. Taehwagang Station and KTX Ulsan Station are different stations; the same city page lists separate KTX-station services.

The city describes the bamboo grove as an accessible tourism site, but conditions vary along this large garden. Ask the centre about your chosen entrance, toilets and current route before travelling with a wheelchair or stroller. [Ulsan tourism](https://www.ulsan.go.kr/tour/eng/contents.ulsan?mId=001002002000000000)

## Where to stay and what next

For an overnight trip, compare a central Nam-gu base with accommodation near the coast according to your next day's transport. Ulsan's [official accommodation directory](https://tour.ulsan.go.kr/tour/kor/unit/acmdtn/list.ulsan?mId=001002010000000000) is a starting point; check the property's location, accessibility and current terms directly. For a different landscape, plan a separate trip to Daewangam Park; for an indoor history visit, choose Jangsaengpo Whale Museum. Allow for travel between them rather than assuming they are walkable from the garden.
