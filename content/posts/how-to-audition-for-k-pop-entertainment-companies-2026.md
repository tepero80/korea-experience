## Check the agency page before filming

Start at the agency’s own audition site and open the specific call. As one example, [JYP’s ongoing online audition](https://audition.jype.com/audition/auditions/1) lists vocals, dance, rap, acting and modeling. Its current notice says applicants can come from any nationality, that a submission cannot be edited, and that successful applicants are contacted individually. Keep those details tied to this notice.

| Step | Confirm it on the official notice |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Does it accept an online submission, or does it give a venue and time for in-person entry? |
| Fit | Check its category, eligibility and whether a parent needs to be involved. |
| File | Review the clip, contact information and upload rules before pressing submit. |
| Reply | Save the application confirmation and verify any follow-up using the agency’s published contact. |

## Keep the Korea trip useful while waiting

If you receive a message asking for a payment or sending you to an unfamiliar address, check the sender through the agency’s official site first. If you are under 18, have a parent or guardian review contact, forms and any proposed meeting. Build a separate plan for the trip if you are already going. A [dance class in Seoul](/blog/k-pop-dance-classes-trainee-experience-2026) can be something to book for its own merits when you have no confirmed agency appointment.
