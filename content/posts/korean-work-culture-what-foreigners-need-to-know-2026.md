## Read the offer as a set of practical commitments

[KOTRA’s labor guide](https://www.investkorea.org/ik-en/cntnts/i-5049/web.do) highlights wages, work hours and holidays or leave as contract terms to check. Compare the written offer with your conversation notes. Ask for clarification or a revised copy if the pay basis, deductions or hours you discussed differ.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Pay | Ask which amounts are fixed or conditional, how wages are calculated and which deductions apply. Confirm what a quoted monthly figure includes. |
| Hours | Check working days, shift times, breaks, overtime arrangements and the holidays or leave stated for this role. |
| Work | Confirm the employing entity, regular duties, work location and proposed start date. Ask for agreed changes in writing. |
| Help | Use [the ministry’s 1350 consultation guidance](https://1350.moel.go.kr/home/hp/ch/callchat.do) or [Seoul’s labor information for foreign residents](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/finf/empl/lbrp/contPageDetail.do?conts_no=3FE93823837543D6B3AE6DF096C4EF19&lang=en) to check the language and channel appropriate for your question. |

If you are still deciding where to teach, [compare EPIK and private academy offers](/blog/teaching-english-in-korea-epik-vs-hagwon-complete-guide-2026) before committing to an intake or start date.

If you already have a teaching offer, use the [E-2 application preparation guide](/blog/teaching-english-in-korea-e-2-visa-process) to check which items you, your sponsor and the deciding office each handle.

If the employer proposes E-7 sponsorship, [confirm the occupation and who supplies each piece of evidence](/blog/employer-sponsored-visa-e-7-professional).

If a listing asks for TOPIK or Korean fluency, [confirm the test and the language used on the job](/blog/korean-language-requirements-for-jobs-topik-guide-2026).

If the work has started and the pay or hours differ from the agreement, [collect the records and find the correct labor-help channel](/blog/korean-labor-laws-your-rights-as-a-foreign-employee-2026).

Before a busy first week, ask how the team [handles competing deadlines and urgent messages](/blog/understanding-pali-pali-culture-in-the-korean-workplace-2026).

## Confirm which conditions apply before starting

Keep the version you sign. Check any immigration permission with the competent office before starting. If you disagree about a term after reading it, take the full contract and the relevant messages to an appropriate employment adviser.

See the [D-10 job-seeker status guide](/blog/d-10-job-seeker-visa-korea-complete-guide-2026) if your next step is asking immigration which status or permission applies to the role.
