**Start with the exact site category.** At Seoul’s Nanji Campground, a standard pitch is for your own gear. Glamping supplies items listed in its booking. Compare the two for your date before making any transport or equipment plans.

| Booked option | What the published example tells you | What to verify for your date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General D pitch | Seoul’s December 2026 listing says to bring personal camping equipment such as a tent and sleeping bag. It reports no equipment rental at the shop. | Site size, power, group capacity, winter services and whether you can carry your gear to it. |
| Glamping | The August 2026 listing included beds, bedding, refrigeration, climate control and some cooking equipment. | Current inclusions and what personal items you need, plus bathroom arrangements and rules for cooking. |

## Find the current booking before buying gear

1. Open [Seoul Public Service Reservation](https://yeyak.seoul.go.kr/) and search for **난지캠핑장**. Match the month, site category and overnight dates.
2. Read the actual listing before paying. Confirm the reservation account, payment, cancellation and check-in requirements. If you cannot complete a requirement, contact the facility or choose another date or accommodation.
3. Check seasonal notices. In the [December General D example](https://yeyak.seoul.go.kr/web/reservation/selectReservView.do?rsv_svc_id=S261031154046761002), the listing says showers are suspended from December 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027. Apply each notice to the facility and period stated. The [August glamping page](https://yeyak.seoul.go.kr/web/reservation/selectReservView.do?reSvc=Y&rsv_svc_id=S260702132310472718) shows a past inventory and does not establish later availability.
4. Save the campground entrance and confirm how you'll get back with your gear. Separate the time at the riverside park from the transport to the gate. If the booking or weather is unsuitable, plan a shorter daytime outing instead of bringing a tent unannounced.

## Get to Nanji Campground

[Seoul City’s overview](https://english.seoul.go.kr/life-platform-hangang/fun-times-at-the-hangang-river/) distinguishes campsite, barbecue and campfire areas. Match the reservation to the actual entrance. The [2021 reopening notice](https://english.seoul.go.kr/hangang-parks-nanji-camp-to-reopen-in-april-remodeled-in-12-years/) documents the renovation. Use the current site plan and signs for the pitch layout, facilities and allowed equipment.
