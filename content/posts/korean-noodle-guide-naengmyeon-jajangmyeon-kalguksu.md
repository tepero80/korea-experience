Look at the menu format first. A cold noodle bowl, a sauced noodle plate and a hot noodle soup can all be a whole meal. The name on the sign usually tells you what the shop specializes in. It does not tell you every ingredient in the kitchen.

## Choose what you want to eat

| Menu label | What to expect and ask |
| --- | --- |
| 물냉면 | Cold noodles in broth. Ask what the broth is made from. [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178819) gives beef as one example. |
| 비빔냉면 | Cold noodles with a spicy sauce. If you want less heat, ask whether the sauce can come separately. |
| 짜장면 | Noodles with a dark bean sauce. Ask whether it contains pork and what comes with a set. [Incheon Tourism](https://eng-itour.incheon.go.kr/cmn/board/BBSMSTR_000000000080/3725bbsDetail.do) explains the Chinatown connection. |
| 칼국수 | Knife-cut noodles in hot soup. Ask about the stock and whether rice or extra dishes come with a set. |

At Seoul’s [Namdaemun Kalguksu Alley](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=183511), the tourism listing describes noodle soup and barley rice at counter stalls. Search **남대문시장 칼국수골목**. [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%82%A8%EB%8C%80%EB%AC%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%B9%BC%EA%B5%AD%EC%88%98%EA%B3%A8%EB%AA%A9) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EB%82%A8%EB%8C%80%EB%AC%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%B9%BC%EA%B5%AD%EC%88%98%EA%B3%A8%EB%AA%A9) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EB%82%A8%EB%8C%80%EB%AC%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%B9%BC%EA%B5%AD%EC%88%98%EA%B3%A8%EB%AA%A9%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A4%91%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%82%A8%EB%8C%80%EB%AC%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5). The map search is for the alley, not an individual stall. Check the posted hours and set before taking a seat.

Show **이거 한 그릇 주세요** to ask for one bowl. If you need to confirm ingredients, ask **육수는 뭘로 만드나요?** (What is the broth made from?). Check that the kitchen can answer about the specific ingredient or shared preparation if that matters. Use the [restaurant ordering guide](/blog/how-to-order-food-in-korean-restaurants-phrases-guide-2026) for staff, kiosks and the bill.
