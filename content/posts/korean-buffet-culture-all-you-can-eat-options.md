The headline price tells you little if you cannot make the full seating window or if the item you want is an extra. Look for both the session and what is included before reserving.

## Read the conditions that change the decision

| Check | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| **뷔페** (buffet) | Where to help yourself, when the counters close and whether the session ends at a fixed time. |
| **무한리필** (unlimited refill) | Which items are refillable, whether you order from staff and whether there is a last order. |
| Group total | What each adult and child pays for your session, whether everyone must choose a plan, and any options or excluded drinks. |
| Changes | How to cancel, arrive late or reduce the party size. Read any published leftover policy for the specific offer. |

For example, [La Seine at Lotte Hotel World](https://www.lottehotel.com/world-hotel/en/dining/restaurant-la-seine) lists separate lunch and dinner sessions on weekends and holidays and publishes adult and child rates. Use the venue’s terms for the date and party you actually have. Do not apply one venue’s policy to another.

## Before you book

Allow time to reach the branch, check in and eat. Ask how long the seating lasts and what the total includes. If you are keeping a train or a timed ticket afterward, an ordinary à-la-carte meal can offer more flexibility.
