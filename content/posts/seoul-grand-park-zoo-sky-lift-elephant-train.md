Take Line 4 to **대공원역**, then head from **Exit 2** toward the park information center. Plan both the ride or walk to the zoo entrance and the walking you want to do inside the zoo.

| Method | Decide before going |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | Follow signs from the information center to the zoo. Check walking conditions and save energy for the exhibits you want. |
| Elephant Train | The [park operator](https://grandpark.seoul.go.kr/conts/contsView/ko/S001001001004001.do) lists an information center stop, a zoo stop and a Seoul Land stop on the loop. An adult ride is **₩2,000 one way**. Check current boarding and accessibility for large strollers or mobility equipment. |
| Sky Lift | The [operator notice](https://grandpark.seoul.go.kr/board/view/ch/S001001003007/B030.do?bbs_no=107230&pageIndex=1) schedules the old lift to end operations on **December 27, 2026**. Check the operating notice for your day and have an independent walk or train plan. |

<GuidePhoto id="gwacheon-grand-park-historic-sky-lift-2008" caption="The old open chairlift over the park lake in 2008. Check the operator’s current notice before planning any lift ride." />

## Keep the return and admission separate

Ride fares and zoo admission are separate. Seoul Land has its own stop and admission. If using the Elephant Train back, check the service and ticketing at the zoo stop before you go in. Ask the [park operator](https://grandpark.seoul.go.kr/conts/contsView/ko/S001001001004001.do) about mobility equipment access if the itinerary depends on the train. For other weather-sensitive day trips, start from the [Seoul hub](/destinations/seoul).
