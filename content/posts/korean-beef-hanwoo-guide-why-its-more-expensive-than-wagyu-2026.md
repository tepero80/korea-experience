Hanwoo or **한우** names Korean native cattle. Check the menu or counter tag for the cut, origin and weight. The [Korea Institute for Animal Products Quality Evaluation](https://www.ekape.or.kr/contents/list.do?menuId=menu193590) lists quality grades such as 1++, 1+ and 1 and separate yield grades A, B and C. Its quality assessment includes marbling and other traits. The yield letter describes meat yield and serves a different purpose.

## Read one label before comparing another

| Look for | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| 등심 or 갈비 | The cut. Compare the same cut across two offers. |
| 100g or 1인분 | The price unit. If it says one serving, ask how many grams it includes. |
| 첫 주문 | The first order. Ask about the minimum and whether you can add another cut later. |
| 상차림비 | A possible table charge. Ask what is included and whether it is charged per guest. |

For example, at a quoted **₩12,000 per 100 g**, a minimum of 300 g calculates to **₩36,000** before any table fee. Those numbers illustrate the calculation and do not quote a shop. Point to the number and ask **100그램 가격인가요?** to confirm the price is per 100 grams.

The [tourism guide to hanu](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221268) describes butcher-restaurants where diners choose a cut and pay separately for table service.
