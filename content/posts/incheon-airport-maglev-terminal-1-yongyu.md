The free Incheon Airport maglev is a short landside ride from Terminal 1 toward Yongyu. It is not the Airport Railroad to Seoul and it does not start at Terminal 2. For an onward flight, use it only when your available time and entry arrangements make an outside trip practical.

## Find the right train

At **Terminal 1**, follow signs to the Transportation Center (교통센터) and the maglev station on its second floor. The [airport operator](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1513/subview.do) lists Terminal 1, Long-term Parking, Joint Government Office, Paradise Hotel & Resort, Water Park and Yongyu. From an AREX arrival at Terminal 1, move to the maglev station in the Transportation Center.

| Your situation | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Time for a short landside ride | Check the operator's current timetable and both directions before boarding. |
| Starting at Terminal 2 | Allow for transfer to Terminal 1 first; check current airport transfer options and time. |
| Tight connection or uncertain entry | Remain in the terminal and use an airport facility instead. |

The airport page lists service Tuesday–Sunday, 10:00–17:00, with Monday and certain holiday closures. Incheon city reports the line resumed after a long suspension in 2025. Services can change, so confirm live status and the last return with airport information **1577-2600**. [Airport guide](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1513/subview.do), [Incheon city announcement](https://www.incheon.go.kr/mayor_9/MA05010000/view?beginDt=&curPage=212&endDt=&repSeq=DOM_0000000013303245&srchMainManagerDeptNm=&srchRepContents=&srchRepTitle=).

The station is landside: international transit passengers need to be eligible to enter Korea and must allow time for immigration, return security and their airline's boarding deadline. Check your individual requirements with official authorities and your airline. If the schedule or buffer does not work, skip the ride.

See the [Incheon destination guide](/destinations/incheon) for a longer city outing. Check the inter-terminal transfer separately if your ongoing flight leaves from Terminal 2.
