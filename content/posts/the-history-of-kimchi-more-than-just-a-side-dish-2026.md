At Museum Kimchikan, start with an independent exhibition visit, then decide if making kimchi fits your schedule. The [museum’s visitor information](https://kimchikan.com/en/visit/info) says individuals can view without an advance reservation during opening hours; check the current admission fee and holiday closures before you go.

## Choose a visit or a class

| Option | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Self-guided exhibition | Use the free QR audio guide, available in English and other languages; bring your own earphones. |
| Kimchi making | Open the [individual program page](https://www.kimchikan.com/rsv/detail/PR0004), open its calendar and check available dates, age eligibility, price and cancellation terms. |
| Group docent | The museum lists Korean-language docent tours for groups of 10 or more by advance inquiry; interpretation must be arranged separately. |

The listed 2026 Kimchi! Basic individual program is in English and lasts 60 minutes. The museum says it may cancel if fewer than four people register, and admission is charged separately. Check current availability and ask about ingredients, allergies and whether you can take your finished kimchi away before booking.
