**For:** families planning an indoor cultural visit in Gwangju. **Information checked:** 9 November 2026.

Gwangju National Museum has main galleries and a children’s museum with timed sessions. Check the [museum’s visitor information](https://gwangju.museum.go.kr/kor/sub01_01.do) and [children’s museum session page](https://gwangju.museum.go.kr/prog/experience/child/sub01_03/list.do) separately before going. The children’s booking page is in Korean; confirm language support and current availability with the museum if needed.

## Choose your visit

- **Main galleries:** The museum lists 10:00–18:00, with entry ending 30 minutes before closing. Admission is free except for paid special exhibitions.
- **Children’s museum:** The operator lists five timed sessions between 10:00 and 17:00. For individuals, online advance booking is recommended; same-day on-site places depend on availability. Include accompanying guardians in the reservation.
- **With an infant:** A separate playground is listed for children aged 36 months or under, with no reservation; check its break time and visitor registration on the session page.
- **On a holiday or Monday:** Check current closure notices for each space before traveling. The Korean visitor page lists a temporary closure on 14 December 2026; details can change.

The museum lists free stroller and wheelchair loans, subject to availability. For the children’s museum, the operator normally asks visitors to leave strollers in storage; limited exceptions may apply. Children aged five or under should be accompanied and supervised by a guardian. Call **062-570-7048** for children’s museum questions or **062-570-7050** for general visitor assistance, and verify current contacts on the official pages.

## Get there and plan a fallback

The address is **광주광역시 북구 하서로 110** (110 Haseo-ro, Buk-gu, Gwangju). Use the museum’s [directions](https://gwangju.museum.go.kr/kor/sub01_04.do) and check current local transport for your starting point. [Find 국립광주박물관 in Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EA%B5%AD%EB%A6%BD%EA%B4%91%EC%A3%BC%EB%B0%95%EB%AC%BC%EA%B4%80). The grounds and movement between areas may involve time outdoors, so a rainy-day visit is not necessarily entirely sheltered.

If your preferred children’s session is full, ask about the current on-site process or visit the main galleries instead. Do not assume a standby place or a particular activity will be available. Save the time, booking and route in your [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary).
