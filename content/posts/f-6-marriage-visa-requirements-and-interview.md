If you are applying abroad as the spouse of a Korean citizen, first confirm which embassy or consulate can accept your application. The [Los Angeles consulate’s long-term visa overview](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/us-losangeles-en/wpge/m_20421/contents.do) separately mentions a child-raising route. Ask the relevant authority for separate instructions if you are applying on that basis or already hold status in Korea.

## Check the mission before ordering certificates

The [Seattle consulate](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/us-seattle-en/brd/m_23042/view.do?itm_seq_1=0&itm_seq_2=0&multi_itm_seq=0&page=1&seq=2) requires an appointment and in-person submission. The [Houston consulate’s Korean instructions](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/us-houston-ko/brd/m_5634/view.do?seq=1202684) also allow mail and list the area it serves, with a separate office for Dallas–Fort Worth. These are local examples. Use the mission covering your address and check how it accepts your route, which documents must be originals and which fees apply to your nationality.

| To prepare | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Form | Which [application form](https://www.visa.go.kr/downfile/VisaapplicationForm_EN.pdf) or online form to use, photo rules, passport validity and accepted payment. |
| Marriage | Which Korean register extracts and foreign records are needed, issue-date limits, and translation or authentication instructions. |
| Sponsor | Invitation and guarantee, evidence of income or housing, and which exceptions need supporting documents. |
| Language | Which shared-language evidence the mission accepts and whether it requests supporting material. |
| Other checks | Whether health or criminal-record documents apply to your case. Houston’s instructions describe exceptions and the evidence requested for them. |

When you are joining family in Korea, use the [family visa route checklist](/blog/f-visa-family-reunion-requirements) to identify which relationship and sponsor documents to confirm before ordering certificates.

## If the consulate asks for an interview

Use the appointment or follow-up message for the location, instructions and deadline. Review the dates and addresses you actually submitted. Bring the requested originals and explain any difference between your current circumstances and an earlier form. Ask the mission about an interpreter or another accommodation if you need one.

Keep a copy of what you sent and save the official receipt. Share passports, private photos and messages only through the submission method the mission specifies. Leave travel dates flexible until you know the decision and the visa conditions.
