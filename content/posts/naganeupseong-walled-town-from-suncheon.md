Plan Naganeupseong as a separate outing from central Suncheon. Start with the lived-in lanes and thatched houses, then add an open section of wall if the stairs, surface and weather suit your group. Arrange the return before leaving town.

## From Suncheon Station or the bus terminal

Search **낙안읍성**, address **낙안면 충민길 30**, in your map. The [city’s visitor bus routes](https://www.sc.go.kr/traver/guide/0002/0001/) list 61, 63 and 68 via the station and terminal. Trips can be widely spaced. Compare times in both directions and check that the outbound vehicle actually stops at the village, especially if the sign names a farther terminus. Ask for **낙안읍성 가는 버스** when checking the outbound ride. A taxi for one or both legs can be easier if the bus return conflicts with your booked train.

## Walk the village first

| Your plan | How to walk it |
| --- | --- |
| Village lanes | Follow a signed lane, stop at a public exhibit and circle back to the gate. Ask which buildings you may enter. |
| A view from the wall | Walk the village first. Ask which stairs are open and where to come down. |
| Rain or limited mobility | Check paths with staff and choose a ground-level loop that suits your group. |

The [city’s heritage profile](https://sc.go.kr/kr/info/0013/0001/0003/) describes thatched houses, the former guesthouse and protected old trees within the walls. Follow signs into public exhibits and leave private houses, gardens and work areas alone. The walls provide a different perspective, but the visit works without a full circuit. Check the [official ticket desk schedule](https://www.sc.go.kr/nagan/page03/0001/) for your month. The operator’s individual standard adult price is ₩4,000 at the time of this check. Outdoor paths can be slippery in rain, and performances or workshops may have separate schedules.

## Plan the return before adding another stop

Pin the boarding stop on the correct side for central Suncheon and keep a margin to collect luggage and catch your booked train. If the road connection allows only a short visit, choose the village or [a National Garden outing from Suncheon Station](/blog/suncheon-station-to-national-garden-east-gate) for the day. Do not try to fit both around a narrow train window. Add the return bus or rail time to the [trip board](/tools/itinerary) first, then fit the village into the time left.
