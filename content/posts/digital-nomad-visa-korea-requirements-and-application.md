Korea Immigration Service says the F-1-D Workation Visa became a permanent program on June 30, 2026. The [autumn 2026 immigration update](https://www.immigration.go.kr/sites/immigration/file/ebook/gongzone/2026/2026_gongzone_vol77.pdf) describes a route for adults aged 18 or older who own an overseas business or work for an overseas company, can work remotely and have at least one year of experience in the same industry. The visa is for remote work tied to an overseas business. Ask immigration which status would cover any work you expect to do for a Korean employer or client.

## Find the income band for your proposed address

GNI below means the Korean per-person gross national income benchmark. Ask the receiving office for the figure and evidence it uses at the date you apply.

| Age | Income by region |
| --- | --- |
| 18–34 | Metro 1.5 × GNI · Other areas 1 × GNI |
| 35+ | Metro 2 × GNI · Other areas 1.5 × GNI |
| With family | Metro 2 × GNI · Other areas 1.5 × GNI |

**Metro:** Seoul, Incheon and Gyeonggi, excluding areas that qualify under the designated population-decline rule.

**Other areas:** Outside the metro area or in eligible designated population-decline or at-risk areas. Confirm the exact address. The family row gives the separate threshold for a qualifying accompanying family.

For example, a solo applicant aged 32 would compare the 1.5× and 1× bands before committing to their address. An eligible accompanying spouse or minor child changes that comparison. The [Singapore mission](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/sg-en/brd/m_2444/view.do?page=1&seq=761493) and [Philippines mission](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/ph-en/brd/m_3277/view.do?seq=738705) show why you should read the requirements for the office handling your case. Their checklists ask for different evidence.

## Confirm your timescale before you book

The immigration update describes a maximum of three years with extensions. The [Singapore embassy instructions](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/sg-en/brd/m_2444/view.do?page=1&seq=761493) describe an initial year and year-by-year extensions handled in Korea. Check which period applies to your own issued permission and how to request any extension.

When you know the address and eligibility band to confirm, use the [F-1-D filing checklist](/blog/korea-digital-nomad-visa-f-1-d-complete-guide-2026) to identify the right office and collect the documents it asks for.
