Book a personal color or styling session if you want a palette, fit advice or a shopping list. If you want someone to **come into shops with you**, ask for that service in writing before paying. A studio consultation generally takes place at one address, and department-store personal shopping may have eligibility restrictions.

## Choose the help that matches your trip

| What you need | Start here | Confirm before paying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colors and makeup guidance | A studio with a clearly stated English or interpreted service | Who interprets, whether makeup or a written palette is included, and whether your group is assessed individually |
| Help assembling outfits | Look for a styling or wardrobe program that covers fit and your budget | Whether you need a core session first and whether any store visits are included |
| Help on the shop floor | Ask about a department-store program or get a written quote from a provider for the exact stops you want | Eligibility, branch, stores and hours covered, total fee, and what happens if an item is out of stock |
| A short visit with a fixed shopping list | Shop independently in one district using the store or brand's official directory | Your measurements, return policy and tax refund paperwork |

## Examples you can verify with the operator

- **Hybrid Rainbow Studio, Jamsil.** The studio offers in-person personal color and image consultations conducted directly in English. Check its [program comparison and booking calendar](https://www.hybridrainbowstudio.com/) for what your session includes, the deposit and available times. Its published consultation takes place at the studio.
- **ColorLover, Seongsu.** The operator's [advanced personal color program](https://www.colorloverlab.com/consultations/advanced-personal-color/) uses an English interpreter. It lists the wardrobe or makeup review as an add-on to the main consultation. Confirm a session time with the studio before paying, and show them reference photos if you want to discuss a specific wardrobe.
- **Hyundai Department Store, Apgujeong or Trade Center.** Hyundai's [guide for international shoppers](https://ehyundai.com/NS/lang/en/NS000004.do) describes arranged shopping guides for **global VIP customers**. It asks for your visit time, brands, meeting place and contact details and lists English and Chinese interpretation by reservation. Ask the branch whether you qualify before building your itinerary around the service.

The [Shinsegae Main Store](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=106990) is a separate landmark in central Seoul, shown in the historical cover photo. Check the current branch directory for the brands you need. The photo does not show a consultation or confirm a concierge service.

## Ask these questions before you book

1. **What exactly does the price cover?** Request the total for your group in won, the session length, interpretation costs, any deposit and how overtime is charged.
2. **Who will be there?** Confirm the English-speaking staff member or interpreter, the meeting point and whether they will go to shops with you.
3. **Can I change my booking?** Ask for the cancellation and late-arrival rules, including when the deposit becomes nonrefundable.
4. **Can we check sizes first?** Send the brands, clothing measurements in centimeters and a spending limit. Ask the provider to confirm any stock checks they can perform before you travel.

If all you need is a lipstick shortlist or two clothing stores, choose a single area in the [Seoul shopping district guide](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam) and compare prices yourself. Ask each seller about [tourist tax refunds](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) before paying, then keep the receipts you need for your flight.
