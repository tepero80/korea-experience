**A change of status depends on your current status, proposed activity and the rules in force for your case.** Do not assume that being in Korea, making a reservation or receiving a job offer authorizes a new activity. Confirm the application route and what you may do while a decision is pending with Immigration.

## Gather the facts before asking

| Information to prepare privately              | Why it matters                                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Current status and expiry date                | Identifies your starting position and deadline               |
| Nationality and current travel document       | Rules and required evidence can depend on your circumstances |
| Proposed activity and target status           | Studying, job seeking and employment are different requests  |
| Graduation, employment or sponsorship details | Identifies the applicable category and documents             |
| Proposed start date and travel dates          | Helps clarify timing, departure and re-entry questions       |

Use [Hi Korea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/Main.pt) for official notices, forms and appointment information. Its Immigration Contact Center is **1345**, listed Monday–Friday 09:00–22:00 excluding public holidays; available languages vary by time. Confirm the route for your case before relying on a blog checklist.

## Two old rules this page corrects

### D-10: do not apply an old six-month rule to everyone

The [Ministry of Justice's December 2025 notice](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/moj/189/601114/artclView.do) describes longer job-search periods for **graduates from domestic universities**: up to three years with one-year extensions. That stated group matters. It does not mean every D-10 applicant automatically receives three years or that approval is automatic.

### E7: look up your subcategory and occupation

[Notice 2025-406](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/immigration/211/488810/download.do) sets 2026 wage criteria for 1 February–31 December 2026: E-7-1 KRW 31.12 million, E-7-2/E-7-3 KRW 25.89 million and E-7-4 KRW 26 million annually, with occupation-specific exceptions. These figures alone do not establish eligibility. Ask Immigration which criteria and exceptions apply to the actual occupation and application date; do not reuse an old one-size GNI formula.

## Questions to take to Immigration

1. Can I apply for this change from my current status while in Korea, or is another route required?
2. Which exact forms and supporting documents apply to my circumstances?
3. When must I submit, and does a visit reservation itself protect my status? Do not assume it does.
4. What activities are permitted before approval, and what proof of the decision will I receive?
5. How would departure or re-entry affect the application?

Save the official answer, date and reference privately. Do not post passport numbers, registration cards or case documents in the community. This page is an application-preparation aid, not a decision on an individual's legal status.
