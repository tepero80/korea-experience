## Identify both categories before booking

[Article 24 of Korea’s Immigration Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1028070139) sets the rule for obtaining permission before starting activities under a different status. Write down your current status, expiry date, proposed category and first date of the new activity. Ask the [Immigration Contact Center](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/1862/subview.do) whether you can apply inside Korea and which forms and supporting documents you need.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Route | Confirm whether your exact status change can be filed in Korea. |
| Proof | Ask which documents the employer, school, family member or other sponsor must provide. |
| Visit | Check the responsible branch and whether an office reservation is required. |
| Timing | Ask what you can do while waiting and how travel outside Korea would affect the application. |

## Check the actual submission and outcome

Use [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/Main.pt) to check any electronic service, appointment or application status that applies. Keep the submission record and ask about what to do before your current authorization expires. Wait for required permission before starting the new activity.
