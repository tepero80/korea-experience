## Give each stop a different job

Korea Tourism Organization identifies Myeongdong as a shopping area and places Garosu-gil near Sinsa Station. This suggested itinerary pairs them for different purchases. Search the actual store name and address, not just “Gangnam,” to make sure your second stop is in the right area.

| Stop | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Stop 1 | In Myeongdong, compare the exact shade, size or edition at two places on your shortlist. Carry a saved label or screenshot. |
| Stop 2 | Near Sinsa and Garosu-gil, visit a specific boutique, design shop or fitting you cannot finish at the first stop. |
| Flexible fallback | If the boutique is closed or you are short on time, finish in Myeongdong and keep Sinsa for another day near the area. |

## Check the return before you cross the river

<GuidePhoto id="archive-gangnam-sinsa-garosugil-crosswalk-2017" caption="Garosu-gil near Sinsa in November 2017. Use a current shop address to find the second stop in this route." />

Look at the live journey from the shop you are leaving to the exact Sinsa address. Add time for street navigation and fitting or checkout. If your next booking is near the accommodation, check the return journey as well. Leave an earlier checkout buffer instead of treating a store’s listed closing time as its last customer entry.

Keep receipts with the matching items and ask each shop about exchange and refund conditions before paying. For more options by neighborhood, see the [Seoul shopping district guide](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam).
