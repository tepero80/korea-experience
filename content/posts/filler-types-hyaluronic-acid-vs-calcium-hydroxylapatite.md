## Read the quote as a starting point

The [FDA guide to filler materials](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/fda-approved-dermal-fillers) distinguishes hyaluronic acid gels from calcium hydroxylapatite particles carried in a gel. That description concerns examples regulated in the United States. For a product proposed in Korea, ask the clinic for its name, patient labeling and the clinician’s explanation of the proposed use.

| Quote detail | Ask the clinician |
| --- | --- |
| Material | What exact product and formulation do you intend to use? |
| Use | What does the local labeling say, and why are you proposing it for this visit? |
| Risks | Which risks and alternatives should I understand before giving consent? |
| Care | Who handles follow-up and how would I get help after traveling home? |

## Ask for the information in writing

The [FDA consumer guide](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/dermal-filler-dos-and-donts-wrinkles-lips-and-more) advises reviewing patient labeling. Ask for a document you can read and a written estimate tied to the same product. If the clinic changes its plan, ask for the updated labeling and costs before proceeding.
