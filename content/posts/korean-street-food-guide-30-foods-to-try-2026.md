## Walk in with a three-item plan

Visit Seoul identifies Jongno 5-ga Station Exit 8 for Gwangjang Market. Approach the food aisle and choose a counter that can answer a quick question about portions. Order one serving first so you can see how much arrives. Recheck what is open if you are there outside the main meal period.

| Taste | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| First bite | Look for gimbap (김밥), small rice rolls wrapped in seaweed. Ask how many pieces are in the order. |
| Shared hot dish | Look for bindaetteok (빈대떡), a mung bean pancake. Ask about the filling and whether one serving is enough for your group. |
| Last snack | Pick either tteokbokki (떡볶이, rice cakes in sauce) or eomuk (어묵, fish cake). Ask about sauce or broth if ingredients matter. |

## Keep the order flexible

Station exits and the food aisle get busy. Stop out of the walking line before opening your map, and keep one hand free if carrying food. Decide whether you want to eat at a stall or take the order with you, as the counter may have a different setup for seated orders. Check the total before adding sides.

For guests who want a seat or need to ask more about ingredients, give that counter extra time or have a sit-down meal on the same trip. The [Gwangjang Market guide](/blog/gwangjang-market-complete-food-guide) covers arrival, seating and how to keep this stop connected to the rest of Seoul.
