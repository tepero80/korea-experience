Choose the Sky Capsule for a small, elevated coastal ride between Mipo and Cheongsapo; choose the Beach Train when you want the longer Mipo–Cheongsapo–Songjeong route. They are separate services with different boarding and ticket conditions, so check your direction before paying.

## Pick the route before the ticket

| Trip you want | Boarding and return |
| --- | --- |
| Elevated ride | Sky Capsule between Mipo and Cheongsapo. Plan the next leg from the opposite end. |
| Continue to Songjeong | Beach Train from the station on your ticket. Choose ride count and return separately. |
| Flexibility | Walk an open part of the Green Railway. Check conditions and keep another ride back available. |

The [Korea Tourism Organization listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=34413) identifies the Sky Capsule's shorter route and the Beach Train's longer route. Do not assume a capsule ticket includes the Beach Train, or that all ticket types allow unlimited reboarding. Check the [operator](https://www.bluelinepark.com/) for available dates, station direction, fare, ticket rules and operating notices before traveling.

### Book by departure station

Use the [English booking page](https://www.bluelinepark.com/eng/booking.do) to choose the exact departure station and ride. The [passenger rules](https://www.bluelinepark.com/eng/information.do) distinguish the Beach Train’s first boarding from later rides. Intermediate boarding may involve a queue and seats are unassigned. Leave margin before the last onward train rather than relying on a close connection after the capsule.

## Build a coast day around Cheongsapo

Start at the station written on your reservation, with enough time to find the correct platform. Cheongsapo is a natural place to pause before the next leg; check the [Green Railway guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=172690) for the coastal walking corridor and nearby Daritdol Observatory. A walk is a separate decision from a ride: watch the time, gradient and weather and use an open route only.

Search **해운대 블루라인파크 미포정거장**, **청사포정거장** or **송정정거장**, matching your ticket. If a desired slot is unavailable, compare the other service or walk a short permitted section rather than assuming space will be available on arrival. Save the correct boarding side and return leg on the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
