**For:** travelers planning a daytime food stop in Daegu. **Information checked:** 9 November 2026.

**Visit Seomun Market during the day if you want to choose between a sit-down meal and a smaller snack while exploring Daegu.** Start with the regular market rather than assuming the night market is open. Check the individual food stall’s menu, payment method and seating before ordering: these can vary even when the wider market is open.

## Before you leave

- **Address:** 45 Keunjang-ro 26-gil, Jung-gu, Daegu / 대구광역시 중구 큰장로26길 45.
- **General hours:** 09:00–18:00 according to Daegu Tourism and KTO; individual shops can differ.
- **Regular closure:** First and third Sundays, according to the city; check individual shops and holiday changes.
- **Contact:** Daegu Tourism lists 053-256-6341.
- **Transport:** KTO identifies Seomun Market Station on Daegu Metro line 3; check your entrance and accessible route.

The [Daegu city listing](https://tour.daegu.go.kr/index.do?menu_id=00002945&menu_link=%2Ffront%2Ftour%2FtourMapsView.do%3FtourId%3DKOATTR_229) and [KTO venue information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=87243) agree on these general daytime hours. An older KTO food article gives different hours, so call or check the seller’s current information when a particular meal is important to your plans.

## Pick food that fits your visit

[KTO’s Seomun guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=174449) mentions kalguksu, sujebi, flat dumplings and hotteok among the market’s foods. Treat these as ideas, not a live list of dishes available at every stall.

- **A warm meal:** Look for kalguksu or sujebi; ask about broth, portion and seating.
- **Something to share:** Ask about flat dumplings or pancakes, their toppings, portion and total price.
- **A snack while exploring:** Consider hotteok or another small item; ask about takeaway and where you can eat.

If you have an allergy or dietary restriction, ask about the **actual ingredients and cross-contact at that stall**. A dish name or photograph cannot establish whether it is suitable. If you cannot get a clear answer, choose another option or ask someone to help translate.

## Order and pay

Point to the item, confirm its quantity and displayed price, then ask **카드 돼요?** (“Can I pay by card?”). KTO lists card acceptance for the market, but that does not guarantee that every food seller accepts every international card or phone wallet. Carry a second way to pay, such as another card or some Korean won. If a payment fails, ask whether the first transaction completed before trying again; keep your PIN and banking codes private.

Ask whether you should eat at the stall or take the food away. If your first choice is closed, choose a different type of meal rather than relying on an unverified shop. Check your return route before leaving the market, especially if you are connecting to another city.

## Want to stay for the night market?

The regular daytime market and the separate night market can follow different schedules. Check the [KTO venue page](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=87243) and a current operator notice for your date. A daytime stall’s closing time does not establish whether an evening food stall will open.
