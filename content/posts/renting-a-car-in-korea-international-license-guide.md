## Check three things before paying

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Permit | [Korea’s Road Traffic Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1030974359) recognizes qualifying permits under both the 1949 Geneva and 1968 Vienna conventions, as well as other specified arrangements. Confirm that your issuing authority and vehicle class qualify. |
| What the desk accepts | Ask the exact rental branch which originals you must show. Bring the valid original permit, passport and home driving licence for an international permit booking. Check that the names align. |
| Contract and payment | Confirm driver eligibility, accepted card and cardholder, extra-driver registration, damage cover and deposit. Use the operator’s own terms, such as [SK](https://homepage.skdirect.co.kr/en/useGuide/shortRental/rentalQualification) or [LOTTE](https://lotterentacar.net/hp/eng/usertip/renting-a-car-in-korea.do). |

Check both the validity of the documents and the time since entry. Article 96 describes up to one year from entry for qualifying authorizations, subject to the covered class. Do not rely on a commercial card sold online as an “international driving licence” without checking the issuer and the branch’s rules.

If you are planning a longer stay, the [KoROAD licence guide](/blog/getting-a-korean-driver-license-as-a-foreigner-2026) explains the separate routes for a new Korean licence and licence exchange.

## For a pickup at Jeju Airport

Open the [airport rental information](https://www.airport.co.kr/jejueng/cms/frCon/index.do?MENU_ID=100) and your booking together. A brand can send you to a shuttle bay for an off-airport desk. Save the supplier’s exact bay and return address, such as the one provided by [LOTTE Jeju Auto House](https://lotterentacar.net/hp/eng/carLife/jejuAuto.do). The airport photo does not show today’s rental shuttle boarding point.

Before leaving the desk, save the return pin, after-hours procedure, roadside contact and fuel or charging requirement. Return the vehicle early enough to take the shuttle back for your flight.

If the desk cannot accept your documents or the hours do not fit, keep the [Jeju Airport bus options](/blog/jeju-airport-to-jeju-city-and-seogwipo) ready for your actual destination.
