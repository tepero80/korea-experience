Ask the seller for the total for the quantity you want before you pay. If the price is fixed, decide whether to buy or compare another stall. At shops displaying multipacks or wholesale prices, check whether you can buy an individual item before comparing prices.

## Start at Hoehyeon

For the market, save **남대문시장** and **서울 중구 남대문시장4길 21** in your map. [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Namdaemun-Market/ENP000085) identifies Hoehyeon Station on Line 4 as the nearby subway stop. The [market association's access information](https://www.namdaemunmarket.co.kr/01_int/01.php) also lists nearby exits. Pick the exit for your first shop or check an accessible route if stairs matter.

The address locates the market area. Shop hours depend on the seller, according to [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=106358). Save the building or shop name and confirm its hours separately if that purchase is your reason for visiting.

## Compare the same quantity

| What you see | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Per-piece price next to a bundle | Ask for the total for the exact number you want. |
| Several similar items | Compare size, material and condition before comparing price. |
| Display or sample item | Confirm the item you will take home and check it before paying. |
| Gift or item too large for your bag | Check packing and any separate delivery charge before buying. |

If you want to ask about two items, say **두 개면 얼마예요?** (How much for two?). To check whether you can buy one, ask **한 개도 살 수 있어요?** (Can I buy just one?). You can ask once, accept the answer and walk away politely if the price or quantity does not work for you.

If you want an overnight base nearby, [check the hotel entrance, room location and luggage options](/blog/accommodation-near-traditional-markets) before you book.

## Confirm payment and what happens after buying

Ask **카드 돼요?** (Can I pay by card?) before the seller starts packing. If the price or payment instructions differ from what you understood, ask for the complete total again before handing over cash or tapping a card. Check your change or card amount and ask for a receipt if you need one. Ask about an exchange before buying whenever fit, size or condition is uncertain.

If you did not find what you came for, use the [Seoul shopping district guide](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam) to choose another area based on the item. Check an individual shop's hours before making the extra trip.
