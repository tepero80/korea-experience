Sanjeong Lake (산정호수) offers a lakeside walk in Pocheon, but it is a long day from Seoul. Bus 1386 is an option from Dobongsan Station; check the current outbound and return departures before deciding whether the trip fits your day.

## Decide the transport first

| Your plan | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Bus from Seoul | Reach **도봉산역광역환승센터**, board 1386 signed **산정호수.상동주차장** and stay on to the upper parking terminus. |
| Return on the same day | From the upper parking terminus check a departure signed **도봉산역광역환승센터** before you leave Dobongsan. |
| Easier schedule | Compare a taxi for the last leg or stay closer to the lake; allow for traffic. |

[Gyeonggi’s official bus listing](https://www.gbis.go.kr/service/busInfo/metroBus.action?centerId=4165000000&cmd=searchMetroCenter) lists 1386 between the upper parking terminus and Dobongsan transfer center. [Hanwha Resort](https://www.hanwharesort.co.kr/irsweb/resort3/resort/rs_contact.do?bp_cd=0701) identifies the older 138-6 designation; its printed departure table differs from current Gyeonggi information, so do not rely on those times. Confirm the exact stop, direction, departure and return with [Gyeonggi bus information](https://www.gbis.go.kr/) or Pocheon Transport at **031-531-8813**.

For the upper terminal, search **산정호수 상동주차장**. ## Choose a shore walk

The [KTO lake listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=90691) identifies a lakeside walking destination and gives **포천시 영북면 산정호수로 402**. The [Pocheon city listing](https://www.pocheon.go.kr/ktour/viewTursmResrceWeb.do?key=9674&resrceNo=7&sa2=CT011001) marks the wider lake area; its map point is not the upper parking bus stop. Check the local map and open path conditions, choose a turnaround and leave time for the bus. In winter, snow, ice and daylight can change the route; wear suitable footwear and follow local closures.

A winter festival described in an [older KTO article](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/HallyuNew/contentsView.do?dataSetId=76&menuSn=862&vcontsId=217882) is not proof that ice activities are open this season. Do not step onto the lake unless an authorized operator has opened a designated activity and directs visitors there. If paths or activities are closed, return to your warm indoor base and check the next bus. Record the return on the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).

For a different outing from Seoul, compare [Art Valley’s quarry and monorail](/blog/pocheon-art-valley-from-seoul-by-bus). Leave the two trips separate unless you have confirmed each onward leg.
