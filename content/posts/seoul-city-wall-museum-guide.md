If you want context for Seoul’s old city wall without committing to a long outdoor walk, start at the free Seoul City Wall Museum near Dongdaemun Station. Its exhibitions explain the wall from the Joseon era to the present; you can then decide whether the weather and your energy suit an outdoor stop. The museum is generally closed on Mondays and January 1.

| Before you go | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Hours | 09:00–18:00; last entry 17:30 |
| Admission | Free |
| Location | Floors 1–3, Seoul Design Support Center, 283 Yulgok-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul |
| Korean address | 서울특별시 종로구 율곡로 283 서울디자인지원센터 |
| Subway | Dongdaemun Station, lines 1 or 4, exit 1 or 10; the museum estimates a five-minute walk |

Check the [museum’s current visitor information](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/hanyangMuseum/watchGuide.jsp) and [directions](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/hanyangMuseum/comingAway.jsp) before leaving; opening arrangements can change. For questions, the listed number is 02-724-0243.

## Make the visit useful

1. Save the Korean address in your map app and navigate to **한양도성박물관**. The museum is in Heunginjimun Park, rather than inside Dongdaemun Station.
2. Begin with the permanent exhibition for the wall’s historical context. The museum estimates approximately 20 minutes for a quick look or about 40 minutes with explanations. Allow additional time if you read slowly or are visiting with children.
3. Decide on your next stop after you see the weather. The park outside can be a rest stop in suitable conditions; it is not an indoor rain shelter. Do not assume a particular wall section is accessible: check the route and any closures on your map before setting out.

## If plans change

On a Monday, choose another activity after checking its own opening calendar. For a wet day, keep your route flexible and use our [rainy-day itinerary](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets) to compare indoor alternatives. If the short walk or steps on your chosen outdoor route matter to your group, check accessibility with the museum rather than assuming an entirely step-free journey.

The museum asks visitors not to bring food inside. Its [visitor FAQ](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/board/NR_boardList.do?bbsCd=1184) suggests the outdoor park for a rest or packed meal when conditions permit. Photography rules, including restrictions on flash and tripods, are on the visitor information page.
