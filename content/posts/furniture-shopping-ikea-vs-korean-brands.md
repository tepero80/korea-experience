A short stay may not justify buying furniture. If you are furnishing a longer stay, compare the exact item and the cost of getting it inside and assembled. A Korean seller may quote delivery and installation differently from IKEA, so request the same information from each rather than assuming one is cheaper.

## Compare the whole order

| Check | Ask before paying |
| --- | --- |
| Fit | Measure the room, doorway, lift and stair turns; check both assembled and packaged dimensions. |
| Delivery | Give the full address and floor. Ask about access, delivery date, indoor placement and extra charges. |
| Assembly | Ask if assembly and wall anchoring are included, and whether the landlord permits fixing furniture to walls. |
| Returns | Request the return window, collection fee and treatment of opened or assembled goods in writing. |

[IKEA Korea’s assembly page](https://www.ikea.com/kr/ko/customer-service/services/assembly/) says assembly is available with its regular or customized furniture delivery, and the price depends on the item. The page is in Korean; confirm eligibility and the total for your address during checkout. Ask a Korean brand or retailer for an equivalent itemized quote, including disposal of packaging and any old furniture.

Agree on a delivery slot when someone can receive the order. For smaller pieces you can carry yourself, plan a home-goods stop using the [Seoul shopping districts guide](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam).
