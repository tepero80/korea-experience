Start by deciding what you are comfortable ordering. **낙지전골** is octopus hot pot. **홍어삼합** usually combines fermented skate, pork and kimchi. Ask what the kitchen serves that day, how it is prepared and how many people the portion serves.

| Your preference | Ask for |
| --- | --- |
| Cooked octopus | **낙지전골**. Confirm the minimum order and how much it serves. |
| A fermented skate platter | **홍어삼합**. Confirm whether pork is included and what portion sizes are available. |
| Cooked skate | Ask about **홍어전** (pan-fried skate) or another cooked skate dish. Check whether the kitchen uses a shared fryer or surfaces. |

## Check preparation before ordering

[VisitKorea’s Mokpo restaurant listings](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=57607) describe both octopus hot pot and skate platters. Its [guide to fermented skate](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221391) explains the regional preparation and serving styles. Use these to identify menu terms, then confirm opening, availability, price and ingredients directly with the restaurant.

Choose a cooked alternative if unsure about raw preparations. Avoid attempting to swallow whole octopus. If someone cannot breathe or speak because they are choking, promptly ask someone to call **119** and follow emergency instructions. The [Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency guide](https://health.kdca.go.kr/healthinfo/biz/health/gnrlzHealthInfo/gnrlzHealthInfo/gnrlzHealthInfoView.do?cntnts_sn=6227) explains what severe choking looks like.

Pair the meal with a nearby part of the [Mokpo destination guide](/destinations/mokpo). If you have a ferry or train, keep the restaurant-to-terminal leg separate and choose a meal you can finish comfortably before departure.
