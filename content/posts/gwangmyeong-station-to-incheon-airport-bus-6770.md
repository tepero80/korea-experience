Transfer to **KORAIL airport bus 6770 at Gwangmyeong Station’s west Exit 4 on the first floor** if your train stops there and you can book a useful onward departure. The bus serves Incheon Airport Terminal 1 and then Terminal 2 from Gwangmyeong. Station airline check-in is a separate decision. You can take the bus and check in at the airport.

## Book the airport leg before choosing the train

Check the terminal on your airline booking. If the terminal is unclear, use the [Incheon terminal guide](/blog/incheon-terminal-flight-check) before choosing your bus destination. Then search for the service in KORAIL Talk or ask a KORAIL ticket counter for the 6770 departure from **광명역** on your date. KORAIL confirms that the app supports reserving a bus seat. The fare and departure on the ticket screen are the ones to use.

The [KORAIL reopening notice](https://info.korail.com/info/selectBbsNttView.do?bbsNo=199&key=911&nttNo=23870) published an adult fare of ₩16,000 and estimates of 50 minutes to T1 and 65 minutes to T2. Use these as planning references and allow for traffic. The airport’s general operating window and [KORAIL’s printed timetable](https://www.korail.com/docs/foreign/city_airport_terminal_guidebook_en.pdf) differ. Confirm the departure against a date-specific search instead of treating either page as a promise of a first or last bus.

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| KTX stops here | Choose a bus with time to leave the train, reach Exit 4 and board. |
| You have an eligible flight and time for city check-in | Allow for the B1 counter and the walk back to the bus upstairs. |
| Your flight is too early for the counter or the airline is ineligible | Take the bus if the airport arrival works and check in at your terminal. |
| Your train does not stop here or the bus timing is poor | Compare a rail connection through Seoul Station or another verified airport transfer. |

## Walk to the correct side of the station

Save **광명역, 경기도 광명시 광명역로 21**. On arrival, read the concourse signs for **서편** (west) and **4번 출구** (Exit 4). If you already have your bus ticket and will check in at the airport, stay with the first-floor exit signs. Outside, match **6770** and **인천공항** to the bus and departure on your ticket. Follow the [airport’s station boarding directions](https://airinfo.airport.kr/ap_en/1420/subview.do) if the station layout is unfamiliar.

## Decide if checking in downstairs will help

For a participating airline on a same-day international departure, follow signs to **도심공항터미널** on **B1 on the west side**. Incheon Airport lists counter hours of **06:30–17:00**, with check-in closing three hours before a T1 departure and three hours twenty minutes before a T2 departure. Plan around the earlier constraint and leave time for a queue. Check with the operating airline about codeshares, baggage restrictions and participation on your date. At Gwangmyeong, departure immigration is handled at Incheon Airport. Budget time for the airport process even if you have already dropped a bag.

If the bus is sold out, delayed or too close to flight check-in, compare a taxi from the station with a rail itinerary that actually reaches Seoul Station and the airport. Recheck the complete door-to-terminal time, including the next available departure. The [airport transfer guide](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) explains the rail and road choices from Seoul. Save the terminal, booked bus and airport arrival buffer together in your [itinerary](/tools/itinerary).
