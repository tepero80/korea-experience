Choose the place by what you want to see. A broadcasting exhibition in Yeouido can fit into a city day. A large historic outdoor set in Yongin needs a separate return plan. Watching live production requires specific permission from the producer.

## Compare the visit you can book

| Visit | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| KBS Visitors Hall | Exhibition reservation, language assistance and which floors you can access. |
| A historical open set | Admission, walking surfaces, weather and the return route. |
| A paid guided package | Exact places included, transport, language and cancellation terms. |
| A live recording | A dated audience invitation and the producer’s confirmed permission. |

## KBS Visitors Hall in Yeouido

The [KBS visitor guide](https://audiencecenter.kbs.co.kr/kbstourhall/tourGuide) says the exhibition is free with a reservation and asks visitors to book at least five days in advance. Check the current rules for your date, especially the visitor group, Korean-only commentary and stairs to the exhibition floors. Ask about interpretation and accessible routing before counting on a tour. [KBS directions](https://about.kbs.co.kr/eng/index.html?sname=kbs&stype=location) show the main building near National Assembly Station Exit 4. Save **KBS 본관 · 서울 영등포구 여의공원로 13** and confirm which entrance your reservation uses.

## Dae Jang Geum Park in Yongin

[VISITKOREA identifies Dae Jang Geum Park](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=192854) as an MBC historical set with past production credits including *Jumong*. Save **용인 대장금파크 · 경기 용인시 처인구 백암면 용천드라마길 25**. Ask which visitor areas are open and give your group time for the route back. For a package, confirm how much time is actually spent inside the set, what admission covers and whether any advertised activity runs on your date.
