Plan a family outing from door to door. A venue may have a stroller facility while the route from the station or parking area still has steps, slopes or an elevator change.

| Part of the trip | Confirm before leaving |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel to transport | Entrance, lift and space for your stroller and bags |
| Station to venue | Correct exit, working elevators and the final approach |
| Inside the venue | Stroller rules, changing or feeding facilities and rest areas |
| Return | Pickup point, transit connection and a shorter fallback |

## Check the facility, not just a map symbol

The Korea Tourism Organization's [accessible-tourism guide](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/main/intro01.do) describes information fields for stroller rental and nursing rooms. Use those fields to identify questions, then contact the venue about current availability, reservation and age or size conditions. A listing is a starting point, not proof that every path is accessible on the day.

For example, [Everland's amenities page](https://wwwrod.everland.com/web/multi/english/everland/everland_guide/amenities/Amenities.html) lists baby services and a stroller rental office. Confirm current location, availability, costs and attraction restrictions with the park before relying on a rental. Bring the equipment your family needs for the trip to the venue; a rental inside the park does not solve the station-to-gate leg.

## Leave time to stop

Choose one main activity and a nearby place to eat or rest. Record the exit, return pickup point and accommodation address offline. If a lift is unavailable or a child needs a break, shorten the route instead of squeezing in another distant attraction. Ask staff for the current accessible route and a suitable restroom.

For the overnight part of the trip, confirm room capacity, bedding and bathroom arrangements in the [family room booking guide](/blog/booking-family-hotel-rooms-in-korea).
