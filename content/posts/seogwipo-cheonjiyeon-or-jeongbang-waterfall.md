Start with how you want to walk, then check today's entry window. The two falls have separate entrances. Use their Korean names when asking for directions in Seogwipo.

| If your priority is… | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Wooded walk and an inland pool | **Cheonjiyeon (천지연폭포)**. Budget time for the path out and back. |
| A waterfall next to the sea | **Jeongbang (정방폭포)**. Start earlier and expect stairs both ways. |
| A stroller or mobility aid | Start by checking Cheonjiyeon with the venue. Visit Jeju documents uneven sections and a threshold at the ticket machine. |
| A late arrival | Check Cheonjiyeon's current notices and last admission, then check your way back to town. |

## Cheonjiyeon: Plan the walk from the ticket area

Use **남성중로 2-15** to reach the visitor entrance. The [city ticket office](https://eticket.seogwipo.go.kr/contents?bmcode=cheonjiyeon) currently lists 09:00–22:00 and last admission at 21:20. Temporary notices can change the schedule. [Visit Jeju's accessibility notes](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500618) place accessible toilets near the ticket area and identify a threshold at the self-service ticket machine. Ask the venue which route and ticket option suit you if you use a mobility aid. Cheonjiyeon in central Seogwipo is a different destination from **Cheonjeyeon (천제연폭포)** in Jungmun.

## Jeongbang: Allow time to climb back

Use **칠십리로214번길 37**. [Visit Jeju](https://m.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500531) describes stairs from the ticket area and currently lists 17:30 as the last admission, with hours subject to sunset and weather. Worn or wet steps can affect your footing. Stay on the opened route and give yourself time to climb back. If access closes, check the other waterfall independently or make a [market stop](/blog/seogwipo-olle-market-takeaway-plan) in town if its opening fits your plans.
