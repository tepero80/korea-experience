**For a first trip, spend three days in one base and explore one part of Jeju each day.** Stay in **Jeju City** if the airport and bus day trips shape your plans, or choose **central Seogwipo** if you mainly want the south coast. Spring and autumn are good starting points for outdoor walks. For swimming, a ferry trip or a summit hike, choose your dates around the conditions and access that activity needs.

## Choose when to visit Jeju

| Season | How to plan the day |
| --- | --- |
| **Spring** · flowers and coastal walks | Pick a walk you would enjoy outside peak bloom. Check the actual flowering site before booking around it. |
| **Summer** · beach time | Choose a beach near your base and check its swimming season. Keep a city plan for rain, heat or rough water. |
| **Autumn** · coast and volcanic scenery | Give the coast and mountain separate days. Check wind, rain and trail access, then adjust the order if needed. |
| **Winter** · coast or snowy scenery | Plan an earlier return and an indoor option. Check ice and wind, and stay on the main island if the ferry or trail closes. |

## Get from the airport to the right base

If flying, look for **Jeju International Airport (CJU, 제주국제공항)**. Jeju City and Seogwipo are different areas. Match your hotel’s exact Korean address to the route. The [airport’s transport guide](https://www.airport.co.kr/jejueng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=1&MENU_ID=90) lists the **600** limousine via Jungmun toward Seogwipo. For other services use the [Jeju bus system](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/), and open the [stop-by-stop arrival guide](/blog/jeju-airport-to-jeju-city-and-seogwipo) for city, Jungmun or south-coast options.

Check the last **usable** departure against the time you expect to reach the curb after baggage claim. Look up both directions for every day trip. If the last leg is impractical with luggage, compare a taxi to the full Korean address. For a mainland ferry, check your departure port, operator and terminal on your ticket and protect the return against a cancellation. The [Korea Shipping Association’s booking site](https://island.haewoon.co.kr/) is a starting point for passenger route searches.

<span id="rental-car-highly-recommended"></span>

**Bus or rental car?** Buses suit trips arranged around a few stops. Compare a car for scattered sites or tight connections. Before paying, [check driving documents and rental requirements](/blog/jeju-car-rental-no-arc-2026), identify the rental shuttle and verify the insurance, pickup and return terms for your booking.

## Choose where to stay

| Base | What to check before booking |
| --- | --- |
| **Jeju City** · early flights and bus day trips | Compare the hotel with Dongmun Market and Jeju Bus Terminal. They are separate stops. |
| **Central Seogwipo** · south-coast outings | Look near Maeil Olle Market for dinner and check your walk from the bus stop with luggage. Jungmun is a different base. |
| **Seongsan** · an early start in the east | Check your first and last transport and how you would return if wind changes the plan. |
| **Aewol or Hallim** · west-coast outings | Check the actual village, your evening transport and the walk between the stop and lodging. |

Start with the two places you most want to visit. Compare the full round trip from each hotel, including road crossings or stairs. The [car-free base comparison](/blog/where-to-stay-in-jeju-without-a-car) shows how to test the stop and the evening return before booking.

## A realistic three-day itinerary

1. **Arrival and one neighborhood.** In Jeju City, pair [Samseonghyeol with Dongmun Market](/blog/jeju-city-samseonghyeol-dongmun-heritage-walk). In Seogwipo, browse [Maeil Olle Market](/blog/seogwipo-olle-market-takeaway-plan) and use the time left for the nearby streets. Keep the arrival day adjustable if your transport is delayed.
2. **East coast.** Make [Seongsan Ilchulbong](/blog/seongsan-ilchulbong-by-bus-from-jeju-city) the main stop. The [official site listing](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500349&menuId=DOM_000001817000000004) describes the tuff cone and visitor areas. Check current access before planning a climb. If Udo is your priority, give the ferry its own day and verify both sailings with the operator.
3. **South coast or a shorter last day.** For a full day in Seogwipo, [choose Cheonjiyeon or Jeongbang Waterfall](/blog/seogwipo-cheonjiyeon-or-jeongbang-waterfall) and check the return first if you are coming from Jeju City. For a shorter outing from Jeju City, [visit Hamdeok Beach by bus](/blog/jeju-airport-to-hamdeok-beach-by-bus) if conditions and your airport transfer fit. From a west-coast base, choose one stretch near Aewol or Hallim and check the bus back.

**Rain plan:** In Jeju City, the [Folklore and Natural History Museum](https://www.jeju.go.kr/museum/guidance/view.htm) is an indoor option. Check its closing day before going. When wind makes an east-coast or ferry day uncertain, swap it with your neighborhood day. If the sea is rough, choose an inland activity.

## Check access for the outing you care about

- **Hallasan:** Treat a summit hike as its own day. On Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa, check the [summit reservation rules](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/board/boardView.do?bbsId=notice_cn&seq=1286) and [trail status on the park’s booking site](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/main/main.do?language=enUS) before leaving. The [park describes Seongpanak](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/contents/contents.do?id=61&language=en_US) as 9.6 km one way. Allow for the return, confirm cutoff points and choose a lower route only when the park reports that route open.
- **Udo:** [Visit Jeju’s Udo guide](https://m.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500477&menuId=DOM_000001817000000007) advises checking operations for the day because conditions can disrupt sailings. Check the actual terminal, last return and vehicle restrictions before setting off. Keep a [main-island alternative](/blog/jeju-winter-ferry-checklist-udo-gapa-marado).
- **Manjanggul:** [VISITKOREA lists about 1 km of the lava tube as open to visitors](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=111165). Check that day’s opening, last entry and cave conditions before planning your route. Bring a layer and shoes with grip.
- **West-side tea country:** The [Osulloc site map and directions](https://www.osulloc.com/kr/ko/store-introduction/jeju-map) show the Tea Museum and Innisfree Jeju House in one complex. Check the current venue hours and bus back before combining it with a coastal stop.

## Choose a meal close to the day's route

The [official Maeil Olle Market listing](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500731&menuId=DOM_700000000010774) describes stalls with produce, seafood and prepared food, and notes that hours vary by shop. In Jeju City, Dongmun is another option after a nearby outing. For a seated meal, match a [Jeju black-pork restaurant](/blog/jeju-black-pork-where-to-eat-on-the-island) to your location and confirm its opening. If you have dietary requirements, [check with the restaurant before traveling](/blog/jeju-muslim-friendly-dining-in-jeju-city).

## Before you book

- **Save in Korean:** hotel address, airport transfer stop, your main stop each day and your fallback.
- **Compare actual costs:** one room per night, each traveler’s fare or the shared cost of a rental, and ticketed extras. Check whether baggage, insurance and parking are included before comparing totals.
- **Leave one flexible block:** confirm timetables, conditions and venue openings close to the visit. Keep return options where you can reach them if your data connection fails.
