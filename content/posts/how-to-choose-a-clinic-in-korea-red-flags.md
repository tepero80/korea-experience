An English marketing name may cover several branches or be used by an intermediary. Get the exact Korean clinic name, address and business registration number in a written message before sending a deposit. Ask who will receive each payment.

## Match the clinic, branch and intermediary

| Check | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Clinic | Legal name and exact address on the appointment confirmation |
| Agent | Separate legal name, business number and role if a third party is involved |
| Payee | Name on the payment request and who will issue the receipt |
| Visit | Exact location, department and contact at the treating facility |

1. Open the [Medical Korea foreign-patient register](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/main.do). Use **등록기관 조회** if you have the business registration number, or **유치기관 현황조회** to search by organization name. In the latter search, **의료기관** means medical facility, **유치사업자** means facilitator, and **유지** filters for active registration.
2. Check the two entities separately. Match the Korean name and address. Use the [HIRA hospital search](https://www.hira.or.kr/ra/hosp/getHealthMap.do) as a second facility lookup. A result for another branch leaves the quoted site unverified.
3. If a name or listing does not match, ask the clinic to clarify through its own published contact. If necessary, ask the registration office listed on Medical Korea. Pause payment while a mismatch remains unexplained.

## Read the result for what it establishes

[Registration](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/intro/registration.do) concerns recruitment of foreign patients. [Accreditation](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/korp/cert/intro.do) is a separate assessment for facilities that apply. Use the registry to match the organization, then confirm the individual appointment directly. Ask the facility who is expected to treat you and how it handles changes. A receipt or advertised tax benefit cannot establish a listing.

Message you can send: “Please provide the Korean legal name and road address of the branch, its business registration number, the name of any booking agent, and the legal name that will appear on my receipt.”

After the identities match, use the [appointment and deposit checklist](/blog/how-to-verify-korean-clinic-credentials-red-flags-to-avoid) to request written terms. For immediate help, use the [guide to emergency and assistance numbers](/blog/medical-emergencies-1330-vs-119-2026).
