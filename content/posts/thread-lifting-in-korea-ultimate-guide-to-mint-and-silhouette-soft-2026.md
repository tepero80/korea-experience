## Check what the quote refers to

Ask the treating clinician to name the exact thread product and explain why it is proposed. The [NHS guide to choosing a cosmetic practitioner](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/advice/choosing-who-will-do-your-procedure/) lists questions about the practitioner, procedure and aftercare. Use them to prepare for the consultation in Korea.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Which exact product would you use, and what does the local product information say? |
| Options | What are the alternatives, limits and risks in my case? |
| Timing | Can I discuss it today and decide later? Which charges apply if I wait? |
| Contact | Who can assess a problem and how can I contact the team once I leave? |

## Keep follow-up within reach

The [NHS guide to cosmetic procedures abroad](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/advice/cosmetic-surgery-abroad/) recommends establishing how complications and aftercare will be handled. Ask who will check you and which appointments depend on in-person assessment. If you receive preparation instructions, reconcile them with your clinician and the prescriber responsible for your medicines.

See the [same-day consultation guide](/blog/same-day-consultations-and-procedures-pros-and-cons) if the clinic asks you to settle the decision within one visit.
