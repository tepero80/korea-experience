## Choose the district that fits your trip

| Where you are | A practical plan |
| --- | --- |
| Busan | Pick Hurshimchung in Dongnae. [Visit Busan](https://www.visitbusan.net/en/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000302008001000&uc_seq=1754) lists the bathhouse on floors 4 and 5 and gives an approach from Oncheonjang Station, Line 1, Exit 1. |
| Central Korea | [VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1591589) identifies a cluster of baths and hotels around Onyangoncheon Station in Asan. Call the exact bath before setting off and check your returning train. |

### Hurshimchung in Dongnae

Look for **허심청 부산 동래구 온천장로107번길 32**. Use **Oncheonjang** as the Busan Metro station name, then check the onward walk. For current day-entry prices, last admission and what the ticket includes, call [051-550-2200](tel:+82515502200). ### Onyang in Asan

Use **온양온천역 충남 아산시** as the rail reference. Check that the train you want stops there and save a separate address for the bath you confirmed. A hotel with a hot-spring facility may have different terms for overnight guests and people visiting only to bathe. Before selecting a facility, [check public bath etiquette and accessibility questions](/blog/korean-public-bath-visit-guide-etiquette-facilities-and-accessibility).

## Ask about the part of the visit that matters to you

Check whether a bath ticket includes any common rest area, how changing rooms are organized, whether towels or clothes are supplied, and whether there are rules for children, tattoos or mobility assistance. If privacy or a private room matters, ask explicitly before traveling. Follow the venue’s posted washing, changing and phone instructions.

Leave an alternative near your base. In Busan you can [visit a museum instead](/blog/busan-museum-un-memorial-cemetery-respectful-half-day) if a wet-weather plan or your group’s needs change.
