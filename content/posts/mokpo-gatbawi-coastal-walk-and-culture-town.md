**For the close-up view of Mokpo’s Gatbawi rocks, go to the coastal footbridge at 166-1 Namnong-ro.** Use the free walkway when the approach is open. Keep Mokpo Natural History Museum at 135 Namnong-ro as a separate indoor stop, and check its last ticket time before committing to the outdoor visit.

## Choose the stop that suits your day

| When | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Clear daylight | Take the Gatbawi footbridge first. Add the museum if you still have time before its ticket cutoff. |
| Rain, snow or fog | Check walkway access and museum hours before leaving. Go straight to the museum if open. |
| Tight train or ferry | Pick one stop. Save the ride back to your departure terminal before beginning the walk. |
| Mobility constraints | Ask whether the approach and floating section suit your needs. Verify the museum entrance separately. |

## Go to the waterside approach

Use **갓바위 해상보행교**, **목포시 남농로 166-1**, in your map app or show it to a driver. The [city visitor guide](https://www.mokpo.go.kr/tour/attraction/cultural_assets/list?idx=7444&mode=view) gives the bridge’s street address. A map result for the natural monument can land on the rocks by the water, so use the street address for the approach. From the signed walkway, you can see the two weathered formations side by side. The rounded tops give the rocks their name. A *gat* is a traditional Korean hat.

The [Korea Tourism Organization listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=81622) lists free access, from 05:00 to midnight in summer and from 05:00 to 23:00 in winter. Heavy rain, snow or fog can close the bridge. If access matters to your plan, call the [Gatbawi tourist information center](https://health.mokpo.go.kr/eng/tourism_shopping/guide) at **+82 61-273-0536** during its published daytime hours. Follow closures and barriers at the site.

The city describes parts of the bridge as fixed and floating. If a wheelchair, stroller or walking aid is essential, ask which connection is currently open and what the ramps are like. Start in daylight for an easier look at the surface and the crossings. If the conditions feel unsuitable, keep the visit on land and use the museum if it is open.

## Switch to the museum before last tickets

Save **목포자연사박물관**, **목포시 남농로 135**, as a second pin. The [museum visitor listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=197916) gives typical hours of 09:00–18:00, with tickets stopping an hour before closing. Its posted adult admission is ₩3,000. It normally closes on Mondays and New Year’s Day, with adjusted hours on some holidays and summer dates. Check the date on the listing or call **+82 61-274-3655**. Dinosaur fossils and geological exhibits make this a substantial visit by itself.

Gatbawi Culture Town lies away from the modern-history halls around the old harbor. On a short trip, plan one area at a time. If you are staying by Mokpo Station and want to remain near the old port, use the [two modern-history halls guide](/blog/mokpo-modern-history-museum-harbor-weather-plan) to choose the closer visit. If you need to catch a boat, save the [correct Mokpo passenger terminal](/blog/mokpo-station-to-correct-ferry-terminal) before you leave Namnong-ro.
