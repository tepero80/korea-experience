## Choose the stop you mean

| Stop | What to find |
| --- | --- |
| DDP | The [Seoul Goods listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/partners-en/seoul-goods) puts the outlet inside the **DDP Design Store, B2**, near Eoullim Plaza. Start here for a short visit focused on design gifts. |
| Doota | The [Doota listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=106371) places the fashion mall at **275 Jangchungdan-ro**. Check the floor and current branch for a specific shop. |

## Follow the campus signs

At Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station, use the approach for DDP. At the plaza, look for **어울림광장** and **디자인스토어**. The [official tourism map](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=67162) gives the campus location. Use the on-site signs to go down towards B2 rather than guessing the floor from a generic campus pin.

If stairs are difficult, check the station and complex for an elevator route before choosing an exit. If you came for a particular exhibition or pop-up, confirm its hall and date separately.

## Make a short gift stop work

Pick one main purpose before moving between plazas and the mall. At the Design Store ask about the maker, packaging and stock if you want a specific item. If the store is closed or you have extra time, use the separate Doota map and check the shops you want. For traditional crafts, plan a different outing with the [Insadong souvenirs guide](/blog/insadong-traditional-crafts-and-souvenirs).

If you have time for one outdoor stop after Doota, the [Seoul Tourism walking route](https://english.visitseoul.net/walking-tour/K-POP-CHEONGGYE/ENN5by7ci) links DDP with Heunginjimun Gate. Look for **흥인지문**, at **서울특별시 종로구 종로 288**, and view it from the public sidewalk. The gate is separate from the DDP campus.
