## Read cover by expense, one line at a time

The [UK FCDO’s treatment-abroad guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/in-hospital-abroad) says standard travel insurance does not normally cover trips for elective surgery and that some related costs can be excluded. Use that guidance to frame questions. Your own policy and written answers from its insurer determine what you can rely on.

| Possible expense | Ask your insurer about |
| --- | --- |
| Planned appointment or procedure | Whether it is covered or excluded, which provider is accepted and whether prior authorization is required. |
| Later care for a complication | Whether complications are excluded, which dates or events matter and what approval is needed. |
| Additional night or new ticket | Whether these costs have separate limits or triggers, and whether a companion’s costs are included. |
| Emergency medical transport home | When the insurer arranges it, who decides and which approval or emergency number applies. |

If the provider is still undecided, ask both facilities the same [questions about the visit and follow-up](/blog/choosing-between-large-hospitals-vs-boutique-clinics) before checking cover.

## Give the insurer your real itinerary

Say that you plan to receive treatment in Korea. Give the relevant dates, procedure and any other details the insurer asks for through its official channel. [FCDO travel insurance guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/foreign-travel-insurance) explains why disclosures and cover for getting home matter. Ask the insurer to confirm its answer in writing and point to the relevant clauses, limits, exclusions and excess. Keep clinic estimates separate from what your insurance confirms.

Before you pay, check how a change or cancellation would work with the clinic, hotel and airline. Make a realistic budget for anything the insurer excludes or has not confirmed. Keep the policy number and the insurer's assistance contact available during travel.

If you already have a bill, the [travel insurance claims guide](/blog/insurance-claims-for-medical-tourists-can-you-get-reimbursed-2026) explains what to collect and what to ask the claims team.

**Editorial check.** The sources provide examples of insurance questions. Ask your own insurer or a licensed adviser to interpret a specific policy.
