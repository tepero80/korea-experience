Start with the game, not a famous stadium name. Open the [KBO schedule](https://www.koreabaseball.com/Schedule/Schedule.aspx) for your travel month and confirm the date, teams and venue. If no fixture is listed, leave the time flexible until the schedule is published.

## Buy for the specific home fixture

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Fixture | In the KBO schedule, look for **경기** (match) and **구장** (stadium). Save the venue on the selected fixture. |
| Seller | Use the [KBO list of club ticket channels](https://koreabaseball.com/Kbo/League/Map.aspx). Different clubs use different sellers even when they share a stadium. |
| Ticket access | Confirm the actual provider’s account, verification, payment and pickup rules. Before paying, ask how you will show the ticket at the gate. |
| Weather or change | Check the home team’s announcement on game day. Read the seller’s postponement, cancellation and refund rules before you travel. |

Do not assume a foreign card, overseas phone or local box office will work for every event. If the official sales channel cannot confirm a route for your circumstances, keep an alternative date or activity.

## Before you travel to the stadium

Keep the venue and ticket details available offline. For example, [VISITKOREA identifies Jamsil Baseball Stadium](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=1566942) as **잠실야구장**, on Olympic-ro in Songpa. Use the venue on your own fixture rather than an old map or a previous season’s ticket.

Decide whether to eat before or at the game. Jamsil’s KTO listing includes food facilities, but it does not settle what a particular gate will allow in your bag. Check the club’s event notice before packing drinks, containers or outside food. Leave time to reach the right gate.

For an outdoor game in Seoul, keep a [rainy-day route](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets) nearby as a backup.
