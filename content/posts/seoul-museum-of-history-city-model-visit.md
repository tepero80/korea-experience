**Go to Seoul Museum of History near Gwanghwamun for a free visit about the city itself.** With a short window, start with the city model on the third floor. Allow a longer visit if you want to follow how the city changed across eras. The [museum’s visitor page](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/eng/visit/visInfo.jsp) lists ordinary hours of 09:00–18:00, with last admission at 17:30, and closes the galleries on Mondays and January 1.

## Pick a first room on the third floor

Use the [museum’s floor map](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/eng/visit/floor/floor3rd.jsp) before heading upstairs. The model in the Seoul Panoramic Theater can give you a sense of the scale of the city and the relationship between areas you plan to visit. Ask whether that room is open when you arrive. For a route through old Seoul, start instead in the Joseon capital gallery and look for the parts on life within the city wall and beyond it.

| Goal | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| A short visit | Go to the model first and pick one nearby section if time allows. |
| Old Seoul | Start with the Joseon capital gallery and look for recognizable districts from your walk. |
| The changing city | Choose the modern gallery and follow the city’s growth after liberation. |
| More context in English | Ask for an audio player at the first-floor information desk before entering the galleries. |

Use the model to orient yourself around the river, and to see how the neighborhoods on your itinerary sit in relation to one another. A museum model shows the city at the point represented in the display. Use an up-to-date map when you are choosing the exact street, station exit or crossing for your next stop.

The operator’s [audio guide instructions](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/eng/visit/audioGuide.jsp) say the loan is free and requires an ID card, which you collect when returning the player. Confirm accepted ID or player availability at the desk if this matters for your visit. The same page describes exhibit QR videos in Korean, so arrange the English loan before going upstairs.

## Walk from the right station

Save **서울역사박물관, 서울특별시 종로구 새문안로 55**. The [museum’s subway guide](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/eng/visit/location/locSubway.jsp) lists Gwanghwamun Station on Line 5, exits 6–8, at about 470 meters. Seodaemun Station on the same line, Exit 4, is about 600 meters. Pick the station and crossing that suit your starting point. In heavy rain or with mobility requirements, ask the information desk at [+82 2 724 0274](tel:+8227240274) about the approach, elevators and the entrance to use.

The route from the station includes outdoor pavement. If the weather is worsening, set the museum as the main indoor activity and leave a longer neighborhood walk for a better day. If you intend to visit after 18:00, check that date’s museum notice or call before traveling. This plan uses the ordinary daytime window.

## Choose what comes next

If you are visiting for a specific temporary exhibition, check its dates and conditions on the [museum’s special exhibition calendar](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/eng/board/NR_boardList.do?bbsCd=1042&q_exhCd=all). Keep a rest break before adding a second venue. For art around City Hall, use the [Deoksugung and Seoul Museum of Art route](/blog/deoksugung-to-seoul-museum-of-art). For a visit built around objects from across Korea, compare the [Seoul museum planning guide](/blog/seoul-museums-and-exhibitions-planning-guide) before choosing your collection.
