A marketplace listing can be posted by a seller other than the platform. Before buying, identify that seller and check the brand’s own website for its official sales channels or ask the brand directly. Matching photos or packaging does not prove the item is genuine.

## Check the purchase, not just the package

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Seller | Compare the seller’s identity with the brand’s official information. Ask the brand if its authorization is unclear. |
| Listing | Compare the exact product name, size and ingredients with the brand’s information; ask about unexplained differences. |
| Payment and delivery | Read return terms and delivery timing. Pay through a method with a documented dispute process and keep the order confirmation. |
| Health claims | Be wary of claims that a cosmetic treats or prevents disease. The [MFDS reporting page](https://www.mfds.go.kr/wpge/m_660/de010410l001.do) identifies such claims as a reporting category. |

## If an order raises concerns

Do not rely on a barcode scan or a batch code as a conclusive test. Keep the listing URL, seller details, receipt and photos; contact the seller or platform and ask the brand how to verify the product. MFDS provides a Korean-language reporting channel for suspected illegal online distribution and misleading advertising; its page explains the information and identification required. Stop using a product that concerns you and seek appropriate medical advice if you have a reaction.

For purchases in person, use the [general seller checklist](/blog/korean-counterfeit-products-how-to-spot-fakes).
