**Choose Yuseong's herbal footbath for an outdoor stop if it is open, or the Currency Museum for an indoor visit on a museum opening day.** Yuseong District lists the main outdoor footbath **closed November 2 through December 18, 2026** for remodeling and directs visitors to **한방족욕장** during the works. Check the district's latest notice before counting on either pool.

## Choose one stop first

| First stop | Check before leaving |
| --- | --- |
| Footbath | Check the [works notice](https://www.yuseong.go.kr/bbs/BBSMSTR_000000000099/view.do?nttId=B000000144769Md0fR1) and outdoor conditions. Look for **한방족욕장** during the closure. |
| Museum | Check the [operator's visitor page](https://museum.komsco.com/museum/contents/3) and current notices. The Currency Museum usually opens **10:00–17:00** and closes Mondays and listed holidays. |
| Short rail gap | Stay near Daejeon Station and choose a [Jungang Market stop](/blog/daejeon-station-to-jungang-market-short-visit) if the return margin allows it. |

## Get to the footbath area by metro

From Daejeon Station, take Line 1 toward **반석 (Banseok)** and get off at **유성온천 (Yuseong Spa)**. Check your departure and exit on the [Daejeon Transportation Corporation route planner](https://www.djtc.kr/kor/cyberStation.do?menuIdx=28). The park address used by [Yuseong District's herbal footbath guide](https://www.yuseong.go.kr/tour/sub01_03.do) is **대전 유성구 봉명동 574**. Save **유성온천공원 한방족욕장** as the destination because both facilities share the park address. Follow the posted pedestrian route around the works.

<GuidePhoto id="daejeon-yuseong-spa-metro-exit" caption="A Yuseong Spa Station entrance in June 2019. Check which exit and lift serve your current walking route to the park." />

The [district's outdoor footbath page](https://www.yuseong.go.kr/tour/sub01_02.do) describes the main pool and normal operating hours. The dated closure notice takes precedence during the works. Confirm the current pool and hours with the district at **042-611-2830**, especially near the planned reopening. The district lists the herbal footbath as free. Take a small towel and follow the on-site washing signs and pool rules.

## Plan the Currency Museum as a separate trip

Enter **화폐박물관, 대전광역시 유성구 과학로 80-67** in your map. Check door-to-door public transport for your starting point or compare a taxi, especially in poor weather. The museum and footbaths are in different parts of Yuseong. Check the transfer and return before putting both on one itinerary.

The Currency Museum displays Korean and overseas money, including coins and banknotes. Admission is free. Individuals and families can visit without booking under the [operator's rules](https://museum.komsco.com/museum/contents/3), while groups of 10 or more need a reservation. The [museum notice board](https://museum.komsco.com/museum/article/notice/?pageIndex=1) lists an elevator repair notice. If you need a step-free route, call **042-870-1200** to confirm the elevator and the exhibits you can reach. Check special closures before setting out.

For science exhibits and a different family outing, see the [National Science Museum guide](/blog/daejeon-national-science-museum-family-visit). Check its opening day and room access separately before switching plans.
