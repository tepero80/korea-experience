Save **익산 왕궁리 유적**. At Wanggung-ri, you see archaeological remains across an open compound, including building sites and the standing stone pagoda. Start with the Baekje Royal Palace Museum if it is open and you want help reading the landscape.

| Your priority | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| By bus | The [heritage route guide](https://www.kh.or.kr/visit/en/road/3/view.do?key=2408010003) lists **65** from Iksan Station followed by a short walk from the exhibition hall stop. Confirm the current stop and return before you board. |
| Indoor interpretation | Check the museum’s access hours separately through the [heritage visitor listing](https://www.kh.or.kr/visit/en/road/3/view.do?key=2408010003) or **063-859-4631**. |
| Walk the palace grounds | Leave daylight and check weather and surfaces. The landscape is open, so a short circuit can be easier in cold wind or rain. |

## Decide whether to add a second site

The pagoda, traces of buildings and open spaces make this visit different from [Mireuksa and the National Museum](/blog/iksan-national-museum-mireuksa-visit). If the next transfer is unclear or the return train is close, use the on-site museum and grounds as the outing. For a two-site day, check the transfer and get your station return settled early.
