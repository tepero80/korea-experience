A duty-free label tells you how the purchase is sold. To decide which offer is cheaper, match the exact product and size, use the amount you will actually pay, and check whether you can collect and carry the order on your trip.

## Put three offers on the same basis

| Offer | Number to compare |
| --- | --- |
| **Pre-order** | Downtown or online duty-free checkout total after a discount you qualify for, converted to the currency your card charges. Check the order cutoff and departure pickup. |
| **Airport shop** | Current price and pack size at a shop in your terminal. Use the [Incheon Airport store directory](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1531/subview.do) to find the store, then confirm price and stock with that retailer. |
| **Regular city shop** | Price after a refund confirmed by the seller, when eligible. Compare the full price if the seller cannot confirm eligibility or the refund amount. |

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s shopping guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=489&vcontsId=140736) separates duty-free purchases from retail tax refunds. Do not subtract a second refund from a duty-free price. Use our [tourist tax refund guide](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) for regular shops.

## Compare the actual package, not the headline discount

Suppose a 50 mL product costs **₩34,000** at a city shop and an airport two-pack of the same 50 mL product costs **₩62,000**. In this example, the two-pack costs **₩31,000 per bottle**, but you spend ₩28,000 more upfront than buying one. A smaller bottle, extra samples or a different formulation changes the comparison. These are example numbers, not prices at a named store.

Before paying, enter the final price in one currency and add any card or conversion fee you know you will pay. Verify the allowance and declaration rules with the customs authority where you will enter. If you are returning to Korea, use [Korea Customs’ traveler allowance guide](https://customs.go.kr/english/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=2743&mi=8069). If you cannot estimate a cost or confirm a discount, compare the undiscounted total first.

## When an order is a poor fit

- **You need to use it in Korea.** A pre-order for departure pickup does not solve that need. Check collection terms before buying. [Shilla’s pickup instructions](https://www.shilladfs.com/comm/kr/en/shoppingGuideStep3) describe collection in the bonded departure area for the purchases covered by its guide.
- **Your gate is far from the counter or you may change flights.** Verify your exact voucher location and retailer terms. If the price difference is small, buying from a shop you can reach can be the better choice.
- **You have a transfer with another security check.** Keep any required sealed duty-free bag and receipt. Check the airline and transfer airport rules before buying liquids. [Incheon Airport’s carry-on guidance](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1432/subview.do) explains its sealed-bag requirements.
