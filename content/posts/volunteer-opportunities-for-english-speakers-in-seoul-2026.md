## Read the application dates and conditions first

Seoul’s [2026 Global BARO announcement](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-global-baro-volunteer-corps-opens-recruitment-for-200-new-members/) is an example of a public program for foreign residents. Its linked recruitment notice closed on March 31, 2026 and listed residence and language requirements. Look for the organizer’s current notice and explicit terms for visitors.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Ask whether the program accepts your exact type of stay and what documentation the organizer requires. |
| Tasks | Ask what you do on site, whether training and repeat shifts are required and what language the team uses. |
| Terms | Verify money, meals, accommodation or reimbursement in writing. Provide a clear explanation of the duties and benefits when checking with [immigration at 1345](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/1862/subview.do). |
| Visit | Ask for the actual venue and shift only after the organizer confirms you can take part. Save the public contact and a cancellation route. |

## Choose an activity you can confirm

If the host cannot confirm eligibility or the visa question is unresolved, keep the day flexible. Ask whether a public event at the same venue accepts ordinary attendees, or choose a short conversation exchange with explicit visitor eligibility.

See the [visitor language-exchange guide](/blog/language-exchange-meetups-and-apps) if your goal is to practice Korean and meet people at a public session.
