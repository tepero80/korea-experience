## Identify what you will do

The tourism directory for Hadong Tea Cultural Center lists a tea etiquette experience. Hadong is a separate regional visit, so build it around your route and the venue’s confirmed session. Ask whether the experience is a demonstration, a guided tasting or a hands-on lesson with the equipment.

| Need | Best fit |
| --- | --- |
| Learn how to serve tea | Ask for a darye (다례) or tea etiquette session and confirm whether guests practise pouring. |
| See how leaves are made or picked | Ask about the specific processing or harvest activity, dates and location. |
| A quiet break between museums | Choose a tea house with the opening hours and seating you need. |

## Make the booking usable

Send the date, group size, preferred language and any seating requirement. Keep the name in Korean and the exact entrance on your map. Ask what is included before paying, including the tea, snacks, equipment and anything you are expected to wear or bring.

If you want the flavor rather than the formal session, the [Insadong tea-house guide](/blog/tea-houses-in-insadong-2026) helps you compare a quick drink with a slower courtyard or indoor stop in central Seoul.
