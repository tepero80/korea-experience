## Discuss both the crease and how your eyes feel

Tell the clinician what you want to discuss about the crease. Separately mention what you notice about dryness, whether the lids close easily, drooping or a change in what you can see. Ask whether brow position or eyelid function needs to be assessed before deciding on a crease change. [Moorfields Eye Hospital’s guide to eyelid droop](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/ptosis-droopy-eyelid) explains that an upper lid sitting low can affect the upper field of vision. Mention any previous eye operations and ask if the team needs records from your eye clinician.

| Topic | Ask at the consult |
| --- | --- |
| Crease | What exact change would this procedure aim to make, and what would stay the same? |
| Sight | Should you assess how the lids sit over my eye or arrange a separate eye visit? |
| Dry eye | Could the plan affect dryness or how completely my lids close? |
| Review | Who would assess my eyes before travel, and how would I reach urgent care after hours? |

## Know where to get an urgent eye assessment

[Guy’s and St Thomas’ patient guidance on blepharoplasty](https://www.guysandstthomas.nhs.uk/health-information/blepharoplasty) discusses dryness and poor eyelid closure as possible issues and calls for immediate reporting of any loss of vision. Ask the clinician which risks apply to the procedure you are considering and request written instructions that distinguish your next scheduled review from symptoms needing immediate care.

If you lose vision after a procedure, seek urgent medical care immediately. [Visit Seoul’s official emergency guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/medical-emergencies) lists **119** for immediate medical help in Korea. Use the [emergency contacts and language help guide](/blog/medical-emergencies-1330-vs-119-2026) to prepare your location and callback details. Before leaving the clinic, save where it wants you to go after hours. Ask which examinations or records your eye clinician at home would need and how to arrange that review before you travel.
