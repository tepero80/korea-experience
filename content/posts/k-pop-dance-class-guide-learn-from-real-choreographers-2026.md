A K-pop cover class, an instructor's original choreography class and a class taught by a song's credited choreographer are different offers. Check the specific teacher and session; a famous studio name alone does not establish who will teach or which song will be covered.

## Choose your session

| Your priority | Check |
| --- | --- |
| First dance class | The level, pace and any age restrictions. |
| A particular song | Whether that song is actually listed for your date. |
| A specific instructor | The current schedule and any announced substitutions. |
| Filming or access | Whether the class is filmed and what alternatives or adjustments are possible. |

The [1MILLION FAQ](https://www.1milliondance.com/faq) describes Step By 1M for people with no prior dance experience, Learner for beginner and intermediate students, and Master or Popup for intermediate and advanced students. Check its [schedule](https://www.1milliondance.com/schedule/week?fs=4) for the instructor and class actually offered on your date.

The studio describes account creation, ticket purchase and a personal QR code for entry. Its FAQ says Step By 1M classes are not filmed and suggests choosing an unfilmed class or telling staff before a filmed class if you do not wish to participate in filming. Check current terms, cancellation deadlines, price, language and location before paying. Bring water and comfortable clothing and shoes; ask about any style-specific equipment.

Allow time to reach the correct studio and save the booking confirmation. For another K-pop themed stop, see [entertainment company visitor alternatives](/blog/k-pop-entertainment-company-tours-sm-jyp-hybe).
