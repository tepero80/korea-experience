A pre-loved bag has two separate questions: **what condition is acceptable to you, and what evidence and remedy does this seller offer?** Ask both before paying; a certificate or an inspection label alone does not establish certainty.

## Read the actual inspection terms

Ask the seller to show the inspection terms for the category and listing. Read what the condition report covers and who is responsible if it is wrong. A policy page does not establish that an individual bag passed inspection. Ask for a specific written response before paying a deposit.

| Check | Ask or inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | Who inspected this item, when, and what written record is provided? |
| Condition | Examine corners, lining, odor, straps, seams and closures with permission; record disclosed wear. |
| Contents | Which dust bag, strap, receipt or other parts are included? |
| Remedy | What is the deadline and procedure for a dispute, what proof is required, and who pays return shipping? |
| Travel logistics | Can a visitor pay, receive the item and resolve a problem after departure? Check delivery time and total fees. |

Keep the receipt, listing and agreed terms. If you need help as a tourist, the [1330 Tourism Complaint Center](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?menuSn=454&vcontsId=140632) lists its contact options. A seller’s return policy does not settle every legal right.
