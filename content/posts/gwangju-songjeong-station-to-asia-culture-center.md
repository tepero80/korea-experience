**From Gwangju-Songjeong railway station, take Gwangju Metro Line 1 toward Sotae or Nokdong and get off at Culture Complex, 문화전당역.** You stay on the same train. The Asia Culture Center is a complex with separate halls and levels, so save the building named on your booking before you leave the station.

## Choose the correct railway station and platform

Your rail ticket should say **광주송정**. **광주역 (Gwangju Station)** is a different railway station. At Gwangju-Songjeong, follow signs for **도시철도 / 지하철**, then board toward **소태 (Sotae)** or **녹동 (Nokdong)**. The operator lists Culture Complex before either terminus. Check the published [Sotae timetable](https://www.grtc.co.kr/subway/contents/directionOfSotae) or [Nokdong timetable](https://www.grtc.co.kr/subway/contents/directionOfNokdong) for the day and direction. **평동 (Pyeongdong)** is the return direction toward Gwangju-Songjeong.

| Your plan | What to save |
| --- | --- |
| **Train arrival** | Save **광주송정역**, 광산구 상무대로 201, as your railway starting point and return station. |
| **ACC visit** | Save **국립아시아문화전당**, 동구 문화전당로 38, and the hall name from your program details. |
| **Step-free route** | Check the [operator’s Culture Complex station map](https://www.grtc.co.kr/cyber?timetable=16) for lifts. Ask the ACC which approach reaches your hall with the fewest changes in level. |

## Find the right hall at Culture Complex

1. **Confirm the exit before going up.** The [ACC’s directions](https://www.acc.go.kr/en/contents.do?PID=090203) list Culture Complex Station as the stop. The [venue foundation’s visitor guide](https://www.accf.or.kr/cms/files/ACC%EC%9E%AC%EB%8B%A8%ED%99%8D%EB%B3%B4%EB%AC%BC2023%EC%98%81%EB%AC%B8_202312270923.pdf) points to Exits 5 and 6. Use a lift or a different exit if the station map and the hall directions show a better route for your needs.
2. **Check the hall and ticket together.** The [current ACC visitor page](https://www.acc.go.kr/en/contents.do?PID=090202) lists separate indoor and outdoor hours, Monday and January 1 closures, and different terms for the museum, performances and paid exhibitions. Use the program listing for last entry and reservation details. Opening the plaza does not tell you whether an indoor hall is open.
3. **Plan the return before choosing another stop.** Save your rail ticket and look up the westbound metro from Culture Complex. Allow time for the lift or street-level exit, the transfer to the railway building, and reaching the right rail platform. If there is an interruption or your last connection is tight, compare a direct taxi against the time shown on your ticket.

## Choose a second stop only if the timing fits

For a meal, Yangdong Market is a different Line 1 stop on the way back toward Gwangju-Songjeong. The [Korea Tourism Organization’s market guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?menuSn=977&vcontsId=174454) describes the market and its location. Check individual shop hours and keep time to reenter the metro. A visit to the [Gwangju National Museum with children](/blog/gwangju-national-museum-children-visit) works better as a separate outing if you need time for the exhibits.
