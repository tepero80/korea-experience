## Choose the correct service for the new address

[Seoul Metropolitan Government’s guide](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/finf/immi/addr/contPageDetail.do?conts_no=679AD313DF2349B8819FFA654A11AEAA&lang=en) gives the general deadline of 15 days from moving in and lists immigration, community service centers and eligible online filing. Ask [the Immigration Contact Center](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/1862/subview.do) what deadline and service apply to your status, especially if you hold F-4 status or your household circumstances are unusual.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Write down the actual move-in date and the cutoff confirmed for your status. |
| Proof | Check what the office accepts for a lease, dormitory or accommodation provided by someone else. |
| People | Confirm which household members must file their own change or appear on the report. |
| Receipt | Keep the acceptance record and confirm that the new address appears in the system. |

When several records change at once, use the [immigration, employer and provider checklist](/blog/dealing-with-korean-bureaucracy-arc-to-taxes) to verify each update.

## Use an in-person fallback if needed

Review [HiKorea’s official services](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/Main.pt) and the office listed for the new address. If the online service will not accept your documents, contact the relevant office before the deadline for the correct path.
