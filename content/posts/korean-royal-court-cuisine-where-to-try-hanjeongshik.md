Hanjeongsik can describe a Korean set meal with several dishes. A restaurant may serve a hanjeongsik course based partly on royal cooking. Read how the restaurant describes its menu, and choose based on the dishes and dining format you want.

## Compare the menu before choosing a restaurant

| Check | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Tradition** | Does the operator explain the dishes it draws from palace cooking, or describe a broader regional or seasonal menu? |
| **Format** | Ask whether courses come one at a time, which plates are shared, and when the meal starts and ends. |
| **Price** | Confirm the current price for one guest, whether everyone must order a course, and whether drinks or a performance cost extra. |
| **Food needs** | Send the restrictions before booking. Ask if stocks, sauces or shared preparation can be changed. Choose a different meal if the restaurant cannot confirm what you need. |

A dish name can help you understand part of the meal. It is more useful to check the full menu and what the provider says about it than to judge the entire meal by one dish or a higher price.

## Two operator examples to read before making a booking

**Korea House (한국의집).** [Its operator’s menu](https://kh.or.kr/combine/content/list/913) describes a course based on court cooking alongside elements of other Korean food traditions. Check the [published seasonal notice](https://www.kh.or.kr/brd/board/644/L/SITES/100/menu/371?bbIdx=119978&brdType=R) and the booking page for the date. Search specifically for the meal you want, and ask whether a performance requires a separate reservation.

**Jihwaja (지화자).** [Its reservation page](https://en.jihwajafood.co.kr/Reservation) lists its location on Jahamun-ro, describes its order requirement per guest and asks guests to share allergy information. Check the [operator’s menu notes](https://en.jihwajafood.co.kr/a-la-carte) for seasonal changes and items that need advance notice. Confirm the course, date and any changes by contacting the restaurant.

## If you want a shorter or more flexible meal

Compare a lunch set or a nearby market lunch if you cannot allow time for a course. Your travel day can work better with a meal close to the place you are already visiting.
