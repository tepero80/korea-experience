## Read the label before you test

ABOUT_TONE’s Water Layer Fit Cushion selector gives 21.5 Warm Beige (21.5 웜 베이지) and 21.5 Cool Beige (21.5 쿨 베이지) the same shade number. Use the full shade name for that product, and read the label again when you switch to another brand or range.

| Check | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Current shade | Bring the brand, product line, shade name and number of a product you already know. |
| Undertone | Read the exact full shade name on the tester and on the box you plan to buy. |
| Finish | Ask for the specific finish you prefer, such as a more matte or luminous look. |
| Refill | Check that the refill names your exact compact and line. |

## Confirm the shade you can actually buy

Ask a staff member how they handle testers and see the shade in even lighting if possible. If the branch has a tester but no boxed stock, ask for the nearby branch or exact product name rather than choosing by number alone. Confirm whether purchases can be exchanged and how the shop handles an opened product.

If you cannot match the shade on this visit, take a photo of the display label if the store permits it and leave the purchase for later. For branch choice and checkout, see the [Olive Young shopping guide](/blog/olive-young-shopping-guide-must-buy-k-beauty-products).
