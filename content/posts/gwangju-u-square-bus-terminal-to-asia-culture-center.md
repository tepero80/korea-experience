**From Gwangju U-Square, start with city bus 09 toward Mudeungsan National Park (Jeungsimsa).** Board at 광주종합버스터미널 on the 현대자동차 side, then get off at 국립아시아문화전당(구.도청) or 문화전당역. Choose the stop that fits the hall on your ticket. If your bus arrives late or you cannot confirm the crossing with luggage, compare a taxi to your named ACC building.

## Confirm where your coach arrives

Check that your ticket says **광주종합버스터미널** or **유·스퀘어**, at **광주 서구 무진대로 904**. [Gwangju Tourism lists the terminal and address](https://tour.gwangju.go.kr/eng/sub.cs?m=47). If you are arriving by KTX or SRT at Gwangju-Songjeong, take the [rail station route to the ACC](/blog/gwangju-songjeong-station-to-asia-culture-center). These arrival points are in different parts of the city.

Save your exact venue before leaving the terminal. A ticket may say **예술극장** (ACC Theater), **어린이문화원** (ACC Children), or **문화창조원** (ACC Creation). Save the hall, level and any ticket collection information so you have them available on the street.

## Find the city bus in the right direction

Open the [official Gwangju bus route search](https://bus.jeonnam-gwangju.go.kr/busmap/lineSearch), search for **09**, and open the stop list. Find **광주종합버스터미널 (현대자동차방향)**. In the checked route order, the two ACC stops come after this terminal stop on the way toward **무등산국립공원(증심사)**. The terminal stop in the other direction is labeled **기아차중문방향**. Match the stop and direction on the sign as well as the bus number. Check current service for your actual date.

The [ACC’s current Korean directions](https://www.acc.go.kr/main/contents.do?PID=060102) also list **34** and **1003** from the express terminal. Use another bus only after its current stop list shows one of the two ACC stops in the direction you need.

## Budget for the last walk inside the complex

The center is at **광주 동구 문화전당로 38**. [ACC’s building and gate map](https://www.acc.go.kr/main/contents.do?PID=060201) shows numbered site gates, elevators and separate floor plans. The bus stop **문화전당역** uses the station name. It does not tell you which ACC entrance to use. If you need a lift, check the route from your actual drop-off to the hall before crossing.

If your phone is offline after the coach ride, keep a screenshot of the stop list and the hall name. Show both to terminal staff and ask where to cross. Before heading over for an unticketed visit, check the [ACC’s hours and admission page](https://www.acc.go.kr/en/contents.do?PID=090202). If you must catch another coach, start the return search from the hall or exit you will actually use. For the reverse 09, look toward **첨단공영차고지**. The stop named **문화전당역** in that direction is marked **국립아시아문화전당방향** in the city list. Confirm the boarding point again, since the outbound and return stops with the same name are in different places. Save your boarding deadline and the two addresses in the [itinerary planner](/tools/itinerary).
