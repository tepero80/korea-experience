## Choose a format that fits your time

Seoul’s [Gangnam Global Village Center](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/ainf/ctrs/gvil/gang/intr/contPageDetail.do?conts_no=87822BFD3E7F45708837952DF19B646E&lang=en) lists conversation groups for Koreans and foreign residents. Ask about eligibility and the next session if you are only visiting. For an online exchange, review the [HelloTalk safety guide](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/safety) and its [search and unknown-chat settings](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/faq/chats-learning-features/1510) before you contact anyone.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Group | Check that the listing is current and ask who can join, whether registration is needed and if there is a fee. |
| App | Use privacy controls to choose who can find you and message you. Keep your accommodation, travel documents and financial details private. |
| Meet | Suggest a public place and agree on a short session. Keep your own transport plan and feel free to end the meeting. |
| Talk | Offer one small topic, such as ordering tea or introducing yourself. Ask whether they want a correction and split the time between both languages. |

## Send a clear invitation

Try “안녕하세요. 한국어와 영어로 20분 정도 이야기해 보고 싶어요.” It opens a short Korean and English conversation. State your visit dates only if useful. If someone pushes for money or redirects the conversation to an unexpected link, pause and use the platform’s block or report controls.

See the [beginner Korean guide](/blog/korean-language-learning-guide-for-beginners) to choose a few phrases you can practice in the first conversation.
