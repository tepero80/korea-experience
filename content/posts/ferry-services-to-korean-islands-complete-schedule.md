Start with your **date, departure terminal, destination and direction**. Change the search to check the return sailing. Use the booking link supplied by your operator or look up the [Korea Shipping Association’s official ferry booking app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=ko&id=ksa.ticket.sale), listed in Korean as **KSA 여객선예매**. Confirm the details with the operator. Use the downloadable or printed terminal name from your booking rather than relying on a generic map search.

## Worked example: Incheon to Deokjeokdo

Deokjeokdo's [official recreation-forest directions](https://www.foresttrip.go.kr/pot/fi/dr/selectDrctnsDtlView.do?hmpgId=ID02030127&menuId=002007) identify the route from Incheon Coastal Passenger Terminal (인천항 연안여객터미널) to Jin-ri pier (진리 선착장). For this route, [Korea Express Ferry](https://www.kefship.com/m/) provides a booking link and current sailing notices. Search the exact date and both directions and check the available ticket and boarding instructions before buying. If the booking page or payment does not work for you, ask the carrier or terminal about current alternatives; do not assume that a seat will be available at the counter.

Taking a vehicle is a separate decision. The recreation-forest page says to contact the shipping company directly about car-ferry reservations. **Do not assume every passenger vessel carries vehicles or that a listed vessel is still operating.** The [carrier's notices](https://www.kefship.com/m/) are where to check whether a particular sailing carries vehicles. Ask the operator about current vehicle service before arranging the rest of your trip.

## Prepare for boarding

The [Incheon Port Authority boarding guide](https://www.icpa.or.kr/icferry/content/view.do?contentKey=215&menuKey=1186) says each passenger needs a ticket and photo identification; it lists a passport among accepted IDs. It advises advance reservation holders to collect tickets at least 30 minutes before departure and pass the gate by 10 minutes before departure. Check your own carrier's current instructions, including whether your booking uses a mobile ticket, and leave enough time to find the correct terminal and desk. Ask the carrier which ID and documents your party needs.

Save the Korean destination and the operator's contact details with your booking. The [port's operator notice](https://www.icpa.or.kr/icferry/article/view.do?articleKey=2557&boardKey=8&currentPageNo=1&menuKey=965) lists Korea Express Ferry at **1577-2891** and the coastal terminal's sailing-information line at **1599-5985**. Check the current language and service hours before relying on a call.

## Recheck both sailings

The port authority says wind, fog, tides and vessel circumstances can change operations or departure times. Its notice says that the day's operating decision can be checked from 06:30 through the terminal's website or information line, or with the carrier. Check again before leaving for the terminal and ask about the return while on the island. A favourable weather forecast or a purchased ticket is not a sailing confirmation.

If the return is uncertain, contact the operator and your accommodation early. Ask about the ticket's refund or change conditions and the next available sailing; a separate hotel, flight or train has its own terms. Keep a mainland alternative and enough flexibility for a disruption rather than promising yourself a same-day connection.
