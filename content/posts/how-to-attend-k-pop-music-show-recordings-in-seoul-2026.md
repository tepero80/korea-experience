## Start with an official episode listing

1. Find your date on the [KBS Music Bank audience page](https://kbsticket.kbs.co.kr/requestnew/list.do?m_seq=9). Open that episode and check its status, age limit, application window, confirmation date and venue.
2. Follow the [KBS audience application guide](https://kbsticket.kbs.co.kr/mobile/popupnew/guide.do) and apply only if you meet the rules. Check which identity document the notice accepts. An application is a request, and your confirmation gives you the details needed for entry.
3. Save the official confirmation and allow time for the stated check-in. KBS’s November 20 and 27, 2026 listings used different buildings and both showed applications closed when checked.

Check your confirmation for the public entrance, queue instructions and ID. A map pin identifies a building. It does not give access to the studio or staff areas.

For a different show, start at the broadcaster's own program page, such as [MBC Show! Music Core](https://m.imbc.com/program/1000788100000100000). Check the exact audience process rather than using one broadcaster’s ticket for another. If there is no public opening for your dates, a separately ticketed [Hongdae live show](/blog/seouls-indie-music-hubs-2026) gives you a practical alternative.
