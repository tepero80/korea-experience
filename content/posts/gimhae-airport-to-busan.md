**For:** arrivals at Gimhae International Airport heading to Busan. **Information checked:** 9 November 2026. Check the airport and operator on your travel date: routes and departure times change.

## The quick choice

For a predictable rail-based journey, take the Busan–Gimhae light rail from Airport Station and change to the metro at **Sasang for Line 2** or **Daejeo for Line 3**. For a direct stop nearer your hotel, compare the airport limousine bus; the next departure may require a wait and the stop may still require a walk or taxi. A taxi reduces transfers but costs depend on distance and traffic.

The airport’s [official public transport guide](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=3&MENU_ID=110) identifies both rail transfer points. Its [bus page](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=1&MENU_ID=110) lists the airport limousine routes and warns that schedules and fares can change without notice.

| Plan | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Seomyeon | Light rail via Sasang and Line 2, or the Seomyeon/Bujeon airport bus; check your hotel stop. |
| Haeundae | Light rail via Sasang and Line 2, or the Haeundae/Gijang bus; some departures do not go to Gijang. |
| Busan Station or Nampo | Rail with a further metro transfer, or taxi; check the last leg. |
| Late or carrying large bags | Compare taxi with the next bus or train and check actual service hours. |

## 1. Identify your arrival terminal and hotel

Save your hotel’s Korean address and phone number offline, and note whether you arrive at the domestic or international terminal. The airport serves separate terminals; terminal signs and boarding positions matter. Its [passenger assistance guide](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=2&MENU_ID=220) describes public transport from the international terminal, including the Airport light rail station across the road from Gate 3. Follow current airport signs in case arrangements change.

## 2. Take the train if transfers suit you

1. Follow signs for the Busan–Gimhae light rail at Airport Station. Board toward **Sasang** for Line 2 or toward **Daejeo** for Line 3; these are different directions.
2. At your chosen interchange, follow signs for the appropriate Busan Metro line and check the final station and platform direction.
3. Confirm the correct exit for your hotel. Account for the station-to-hotel walk, luggage, steps and lifts; if step-free access is essential, verify lift operation with the operator.
4. Pay using a suitable accepted transport card or ticket as instructed at the station. Do not assume a foreign credit card will operate as a local transit card.

Seomyeon is on Lines 1 and 2, while Busan Station and Nampo are on Line 1. For Busan Station, for example, a transfer from Line 2 to Line 1 at Seomyeon is one route to check. Verify the route on a current transit map before traveling.

## 3. Compare the limousine bus

The airport lists routes toward **Seomyeon/Bujeon** and **Haeundae/Gijang**. Search the stop list for your actual hotel address, check which departures run to your stop, and confirm the next departure and payment conditions with the operator. Road congestion and a long wait can change the comparison with rail. Keep the last portion of the trip in mind: a named district is not necessarily your hotel door.

If the bus you intended to catch has left, check the next departure and your rail or taxi alternative. Do not assume a reservation is necessary or accepted unless the operator says so for that service.

## 4. If choosing a taxi

Use the designated airport taxi stand and show the Korean address, not just the English hotel name. Ask the driver to confirm the destination and payment method before setting off. The airport’s arrival guide places the international-terminal taxi stand across the road from Gate 3; follow current signs.
