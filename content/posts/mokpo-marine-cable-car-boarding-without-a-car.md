**From Mokpo Station, start at the cable car’s Bukhang (North Port) terminal and choose a round trip if you want to finish on the same side of the harbor.** The route crosses to Gohado Island via Yudalsan. If you start or finish on the island, confirm the road ride and the last service in the direction you need before you buy a ticket.

## Choose where to board

| Your plan | Best starting point |
| --- | --- |
| From the train | **Bukhang**. Search **목포해상케이블카 북항승강장**, at **목포시 해양대학로 240**. |
| Already on Gohado | **Gohado**. Search **목포해상케이블카 고하도승강장**, at **목포시 고하도안길 186**. Check your return to town. |
| Visit Yudalsan | Ask which direction allows you to get off and reboard. Set aside time for that stop and for any walk you add. |

Use the Mokpo city route description to match the cable car station to your ticket. If your directions app offers more than one North Port result, check that the pin is the **cable car terminal**. The passenger pier is a separate destination. [Mokpo city route description](https://biz.mokpo.go.kr/tour/attraction/area?idx=83802&mode=view), [VisitKorea Gohado address](https://korean.visitkorea.or.kr/detail/rem_detail.do?cotid=20815e9c-cc02-4e70-b00a-7c505b7a8f52)

## Get to Bukhang from Mokpo Station

The [city’s transport directions](https://youth.mokpo.go.kr/www/life_welfare/sewolferry/traffic_guidance) show bus **1** from **목포역**, stop **14022** toward **원광한의원**, to **목포해상케이블카**, stop **32012** toward **어민동산입구**. These directions are a starting point from a dated city guide. Check the bus number, direction, and walk to the cable car building in a live transit app before boarding. For the way back, search from the terminal again. The nearest city-bound stop can differ from the one where you got off.

If buses are infrequent or the return is tight, compare a taxi using the Korean terminal name and street address. If you need a step-free approach, check the stop-to-entrance walk and cabin rules separately. The [Korea Tourism Organization’s accessibility guide](https://korean.visitkorea.or.kr/detail/rem_detail.do?cotid=9bfa12e3-c60a-409c-b050-9ed489fcf09d) describes the approach from Bukhang parking, which is different from the approach on foot from a bus stop.

## Check the return before committing

- **Allow time beyond the ride.** [VisitKorea’s cable car listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=60936) estimates about 40 minutes for the round-trip ride. Add the wait, station access, time off the cabin, and the trip back to Mokpo Station.
- **Confirm the direction and stop rules.** The city describes directional one-way tickets. Ask which Yudalsan stops are allowed with your ticket and whether you need another ticket if you change direction.
- **Check weather and the last return.** Tourism listings differ on hours and fares. Call the cable car on **061-244-2600** to confirm today’s service, ticket cutoff, and final return from the station you plan to use. Ask for the last cabin back when you check the closing time. [VisitKorea’s weather and contact information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=60936)

If the cable car is suspended or the wait uses up your train buffer, switch to the [two modern history museum halls](/blog/mokpo-modern-history-museum-harbor-weather-plan) if they are open. For an onward boat, use the [Mokpo ferry terminal guide](/blog/mokpo-station-to-correct-ferry-terminal) and follow the terminal and check-in deadline on your ferry booking.
