For Haedong Yonggungsa (해동 용궁사), plan the last road leg and the walk down to the coastal temple, not just the train to Osiria. Bus can keep costs down; a taxi is useful if the timetable or your group’s energy makes the connection awkward.

## From Osiria to the temple

| Option | What to look up |
| --- | --- |
| Bus | Osiria Exit 1 and the correct bus direction to 용궁사·국립수산과학원. |
| Taxi | Ask for the temple approach and choose a return meeting point. |
| Steps are a concern | Contact the temple about the actual walk from the drop-off. |

[Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=195451) describes taking bus 139 or 1001 from Osiria Station (오시리아역) Exit 1 to Yonggungsa Temple–National Institute of Fisheries Science stop, then walking to the temple. That 2024 route description is a starting point: confirm today’s boarding stop, bus direction and return using current local transport information. Search **용궁사·국립수산과학원** for the stop and **해동 용궁사** for the destination. A taxi to the temple approach can simplify the transfer, but check the fare and arrange the return.

## The approach matters

The [official temple listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=108618) gives **부산광역시 기장군 기장읍 용궁길 86**. The KTO feature identifies 108 stairs connecting the entrance with the interior. A road drop-off does not make the full temple visit step-free. Ask the temple about current access and path conditions if stairs are difficult, and allow time to climb back.

At the coast, keep to signed paths and leave room for worshippers. In rain or rough weather, check the site's current notices and take extra care on steps. Call **051-722-7744** for current hours and access; published descriptions differ on the closing time. If you are also considering the [Haeundae coast ride](/blog/haeundae-sky-capsule-vs-beach-train), leave a travel margin between separate attractions and your return. Add the confirmed stop and return to the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
