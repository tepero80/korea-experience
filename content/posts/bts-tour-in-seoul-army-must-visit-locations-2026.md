A BTS connection can mean a past performance, a music video or a member's photograph. These are public places to enjoy in their own right; none is a promise of seeing an artist.

## Choose your connection

| Stop | BTS connection |
| --- | --- |
| Palace (경복궁) | The group performed “Idol” at Geunjeongjeon and “Mikrokosmos” at Gyeonghoeru for The Tonight Show in 2020. |
| Nodeul Island (노들섬) | VISITKOREA connects the Hangangdaegyo Bridge area with the “Run” music video and a later Season’s Greetings shoot. |
| Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) | RM shared a photo by the east lake, according to the official itinerary. |

The [Korea Tourism Organization's BTS course](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/hdrdslt/hdrdsltView.do?crsSn=399187) covers roughly 29.6 km: treat it as a menu, not a short walk. Copy the Korean names into your map and check the exact entrance, travel time and current access. The palace is a ticketed heritage site with its own hours and closures; check its official visitor information before setting out. Stay on public paths and respect other visitors and any barriers.
