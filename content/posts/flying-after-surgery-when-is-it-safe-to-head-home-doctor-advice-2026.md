## Separate the clinical decision from the airline paperwork

Tell your treating team the dates, flight lengths, connections and any help you may need on the journey. Ask what follow-up they expect before departure and how to reach them if your condition changes. The [UK travel guidance on treatment abroad](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/in-hospital-abroad) recommends checking plans with a clinician and says an airline may ask for advance confirmation.

| Who to contact | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Treating clinician | Whether your proposed journey is appropriate, the follow-up required and what documents the team can complete. |
| Operating airline for each leg | Whether a medical certificate or its Medical Information Form (MEDIF) is required, how to submit it and when to expect an answer. |
| Ticket issuer or travel agent | Change options, the cost of moving multiple segments and how to retain onward connections. |
| Insurer, where applicable | Whether a change or extra stay is covered and whether approval is needed before spending. |

If you are considering rhinoplasty, [ask the surgeon which checks must happen before you leave Korea](/blog/rhinoplasty-in-korea-complete-guide-cost-and-best-clinics-2026). Bring the answer to your airline conversation.

## Ask the airline for its current instructions

Use the **operated by** line on your booking, especially for a codeshare. [Korean Air](https://www.koreanair.com/contents/plan-your-travel/special-assistance/disability-assistance/medical-services?hl=en) lists recent surgery among cases needing a medical certificate and provides a MEDIF process. [Emirates](https://www.emirates.com/us/english/before-you-fly/health/medical-information-forms/) describes separate passenger and treating-clinician sections and may ask for further information. Ask about the deadline and the latest form for your flights, then wait for an answer before relying on the booking.

If a flight, operator or medical situation changes, ask who must be notified and whether your approval or assistance request needs to be updated. Keep the airline’s response and the clinic’s contact somewhere you can find offline.

## Keep a practical fallback for a changed departure

Check a room extension, the cost and timing of a new flight, and who will handle a missed connection. If symptoms change while you are deciding, contact your treating team or seek urgent care as appropriate. Ask about costs in writing before changing plans where possible. Our [insurance checklist for planned care](/blog/medical-tourism-insurance-for-korea) lists the expenses to ask an insurer about.

Once your itinerary is confirmed, use the [Incheon terminal check](/blog/incheon-terminal-flight-check) to plan the journey from your accommodation.

**Editorial check.** These are airline and travel processes. Ask your treating clinician about the timing that applies to you.
