**Treat the palace buildings and the Secret Garden as two admission decisions.** Choose a garden entry time first, then check whether your party also needs palace-building admission. The Royal Palaces and Tombs Center publishes separate reservation guidance for the two areas. Rules and the operating schedule below were checked 9 November 2026 and can change.

## Quick decision table

| Plan | Check before you pay |
| --- | --- |
| Palace buildings only | Use the [official Changdeokgung visitor page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E702000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg&schGroupCodeNm=Changdeokgung) for opening and last admission; you do not need to buy a garden slot to visit only the buildings. |
| Secret Garden too | Check a timed garden place and the separate palace admission requirement on the [official reservation page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R601000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg). |
| A palace pass or free admission | Check what it covers. The official [fee table](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R703000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg) says the palace pass excludes the Secret Garden; eligibility and evidence differ by person. |
| Monday or a public holiday | Changdeokgung ordinarily closes Monday, but the official page says a public or substitute holiday on the regular closing day may move the closure to the following day. Check notices for your exact date. |

## Choose your garden date, then check the ticket conditions

The official [booking page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R601000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg) states that advance online garden booking opens at 10:00, six days before the visit date, and closes the day before. The [official visitor FAQ](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R404000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg) describes a quota with online and same-day ticket-office places. A quota is not availability: a busy date can sell out, and festival arrangements can override the usual procedure.

1. Check the dated notice and opening day before arranging transport or hanbok rental.
2. Choose a timed garden entry and confirm the language and admission conditions shown in the live reservation flow. The official visitor FAQ says to enter at the reserved time; a guided explanation or independent exploration of open areas may be available. Follow the current session instructions.
3. Check whether each person also needs a palace-building ticket. Some visitors qualify for free or discounted building admission; the rules depend on age and eligibility. Take the required evidence and verify it on the official page.
4. Save the confirmation and allow time for the palace entrance and the walk to the garden entrance. The operator’s [visitor FAQ](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R404000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg) says the walk takes at least 15 minutes and recommends entering the palace at least 20 minutes before garden entry. At busy times allow more; the FAQ suggests up to an hour. Missed garden entry cannot be refunded according to that FAQ.

**Temporary exception:** the operator's page lists a Korea Grand Festival free-opening period from 29 October to 15 November 2026, excluding ordinary Monday closure, with online booking unavailable for the listed palace-building admission. This notice appears under palace-building admission; the page separately lists garden reservations. Confirm live garden availability for your date and do not assume that garden capacity is unlimited or free.

## What to budget for

The official [fee table](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R703000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg) lists ordinary adult palace-building admission at ₩3,000 and ordinary adult garden admission at ₩5,000. For an adult who must pay both, that is **₩8,000 per person**, before transport or any rental. Age, exemption, special-event and pass rules may change what an individual pays; confirm on the official pages rather than multiplying the example for every traveler.

## Arrival and a flexible alternative

The palace's official English page gives **99 Yulgok-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul**. Korean address: **서울특별시 종로구 율곡로 99**. Search that address in a local map app and confirm your actual route. The site identifies Anguk Station as a subway option; verify the exit, walking route and accessibility for your party before travelling. If rain, mobility concerns or a late arrival affect your plans, contact the palace using its official visitor page instead of assuming that the garden route will suit everyone.

If the garden session is unavailable, you can decide whether the palace buildings alone still suit your day. Check each alternative's own closure and booking conditions.
