## Get a name you can check

If a clinic advertises fat freezing and a fat-dissolving injection, ask who would assess you and what problem each proposal is meant to address. A package name can leave out the actual product. Ask the clinic to write down the details you would need to verify the offer.

| Offer | What to write down |
| --- | --- |
| Cool | Device maker, model, Korean authorization number, and the area the clinic proposes to treat. |
| Inject | Product name, maker, and active ingredients. If an offer includes more than one product, ask staff to list each one and how the clinician plans to use it. |
| Both | The clinician’s name, what benefit and risks they see for you, and how to contact the team if a concern comes up after the visit. |

## Compare the proposal with the Korean product record

Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety explains how to look up device information using the name and [authorization number](https://mfds.go.kr/wpge/m_529/de050901l001.do). The ministry’s [medicine database](https://nedrug.mfds.go.kr/searchDrug) lets you search by product or ingredient and check the listed record. Ask the clinic to show the record for the proposed device or product. Confirm what areas or uses the record covers and ask the clinician to explain any difference between that record and your proposal.

The [U.S. FDA warning on unapproved fat-dissolving injections](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/buying-using-medicine-safely/using-fat-dissolving-injections-are-not-fda-approved-can-be-harmful) describes adverse reactions after products the FDA had not approved. Korean authorization depends on the specific local product and use. Ask about the risks and alternatives for the exact product and ask who can explain a record you cannot match. Wait to decide until you can identify what each offer includes.

## Save the contact and the treatment details

If you go ahead, ask what written details the clinic can give you and which concerns it wants you to report. Save its daytime and after-hours contacts. If you are already arranging your trip home, use the [follow-up contact and records guide](/blog/dealing-with-complications-who-to-contact-in-emergencies) to decide what to keep and share securely with your next clinician.
