## Follow a clip back to an operator

[Visit Seoul’s One More Trip](https://english.visitseoul.net/onemoretrip) is a starting point for local experiences. The [Korea Tourism Organization’s coverage of the Korea Food Grand Master Center](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=600&page=1&sContsTtl=&sort=regDt&vcontsId=189628) points to the center’s cooking videos. Use these sources to identify a provider, then check its current booking or opening information separately.

| Clip | What to search and verify |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | Search 산책 and the neighborhood name. Check the station exit or entrance shown. |
| Class | Search 체험 or 예약 and the provider name. Check dates, language and booking contact. |
| Food | Search 시장 or 메뉴 and the stall name. Check the branch, hours and whether the dish is seasonal. |
| Filming | Search 촬영지 and the program name. Check access, photography rules and private property boundaries. |

## Turn one clip into one usable stop

Write down the Korean place name, upload date, the detail you want to see and an alternate nearby stop. Use captions for clues about signs or directions. Confirm dates, costs and addresses with the operator before relying on them. For a music video location, start with the [filming-location access checklist](/blog/k-pop-music-video-filming-locations-map) before adding a detour.
