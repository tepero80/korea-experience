Start by finding out what has already been settled for the year. Ask each employer for your wage-income withholding receipt, called 근로소득 원천징수영수증. Check whether the final settlement included income from each employer and whether the evidence for the claim you want to check was submitted.

## Start with receipts from every employer

| Case | First thing to check |
| --- | --- |
| Left a job | Ask the former employer for the final-pay settlement and its withholding receipt. |
| Changed jobs | Ask the new payroll team whether it received the former employer’s receipt and included those wages. |
| Two employers | Ask the principal payroll team which employers and amounts it included. |
| Missed a deduction | Find the evidence, mark which return or settlement omitted it, and confirm the filing route for that year. |

The [NTS final-pay FAQ](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?mi=&nttSn=1347723) explains employer settlement when a worker leaves during the year. The Income Tax Act describes how employers combine wages for [workers who changed jobs](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1032883555) and [workers with multiple employers](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1032885199) when the relevant records are submitted. Compare both the income year and the employer on the receipt before concluding a wage was missed.

## Take the right evidence to NTS

Create a short summary with the income year, employer names, wages and withholding per receipt, the pay or settlement date, and the specific amount or document that appears missing. Keep a copy of any return you already filed. Bring this summary and the evidence to NTS or send it through a channel it asks you to use.

If wages were left out, ask whether a regular global income return is required. If all income was reported and you only found a missing eligible deduction, ask whether a regular return, a [correction claim called 경정청구](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1030472339), or another procedure fits the year and filings you already made. If you already filed and found omitted income, explain that difference when requesting help.

## Verify the correct filing year and deadline

The [Income Tax Act’s regular filing provision](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1032881431) sets the ordinary global income return period in May of the year after the income year for residents who are required to file under that provision. Check the deadline for your own situation, especially if you are moving overseas or dealing with a past filing. The [NTS foreign taxpayer filing guide](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?bbsId=30698&mi=10788&nttSn=1350825) links to English instructions for 2025 income. Use the instructions for the year you need.

If your employer is still collecting documents for the current settlement, follow the [year-end settlement checklist for foreign employees](/blog/tax-returns-for-foreign-workers-in-korea) first.
