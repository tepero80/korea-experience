If you already have a package, take it or a clear photo of the front and ingredient panel. If you do not have a specific product in mind, prepare a short description of what you want help with and let the pharmacist ask questions. The [Korea Tourism Organization pharmacy guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=110&menuSn=923&vcontsId=217224) suggests using a translation app and a photo to explain your request.

## Show this at the counter

| Korean phrase | When to show it |
| --- | --- |
| 약사님과 상담할 수 있나요? | “Can I speak with the pharmacist?” |
| 제가 복용 중인 약은 이것입니다. | “This is what I currently take.” Show the actual list or packaging privately. |
| 저는 이 성분에 알레르기가 있어요. | “I am allergic to this ingredient.” Point to the specific name. |
| 사용 방법을 적어 주실 수 있나요? | “Could you write down how to use it?” |

## Read the instructions with the pharmacist

If a different product is offered, ask the pharmacist to explain the active ingredient, its strength and how it differs from what you showed. Ask which information applies to that exact package, including how to use it and whether you need to speak with a clinician. If you cannot confirm the instructions, ask for written clarification before deciding. Do not infer a dose or combine treatments from translated packaging alone.

Use the [Seoul pharmacy finder guide](/blog/24-hour-english-pharmacies-in-seoul-2026) to locate an open pharmacy and confirm its hours. In an emergency in Korea, call **119** and give your location.
