Jeju Stone Park (제주돌문화공원) combines a museum with open paths and outdoor displays. Choose how far to walk after you see what is open and when you need to leave. Take the [231 toward the park](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2203) only if the current stop list matches your journey, then save the [return timetable](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/detailSchedule?scheduleId=2206) separately.

| At the park | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| On arrival | Get the route map and ask which sections are open. [VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=76736) lists **09:00–18:00** and sales until **17:00** on opening days. Check Monday and holiday changes. |
| A first walk | Use [VisitJeju’s Course 1](https://visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500547) as orientation for the wooded approach, Stone Museum and outdoor displays. Pick a turnaround based on daylight and bus time. |
| Rain, wind or a late start | Ask which indoor areas you can reach and return from. Keep a shorter stop if the outer route makes the ride back tight. |

## Keep the bus stop in your return plan

Save **제주돌문화공원** and the stop for your direction independently. Bus numbers with similar destinations can use different roads. Give yourself time from the displays to the exit and from the exit to the stop. See the [Jeju hub](/destinations/jeju) for a separate trip with easier return options from your base.
