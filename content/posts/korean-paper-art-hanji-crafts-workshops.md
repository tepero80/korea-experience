## Choose an exhibition, demo or class

| Your plan | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| See the exhibits | The [museum](https://www.hanjimuseum.co.kr/main/idx) lists Tuesday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 with last entry 30 minutes before closing, and free exhibition entry. Check any holiday or special closure before leaving. Look for the history and living halls. |
| Watch or try a short activity | Check the [museum’s experience guidance](https://www.hanjimuseum.co.kr/main/menu?gc=908UZDG) and current notices. Ask what is running on your date, whether a place is available and whether there is a reservation requirement. |
| Make a sheet in a structured session | Use the [Millennium Hanji Center booking guide](/blog/traditional-paper-hanji-making-workshops-in-jeonju-2026). Save the booking venue’s address separately from the museum. |

For the quickest visit, choose the exhibitions first and keep the experience optional. At either venue, ask about the language of instruction, step-free entry and whether a finished piece can be taken away on the same day. If the museum is closed, check the other venue’s timetable before making the trip across town.
