**Go to Ojukheon first if you want to see the house and museum, then take a bus or taxi to Jungang Market for a meal.** For a late arrival, start downtown and save the museum for another day. This is a separate outing from the east-coast beaches.

Allow about **three hours for both stops** as a planning estimate. Give Ojukheon roughly an hour, then leave enough time for the transfer, a meal and your next departure. Add extra time for a long lunch queue or if you want to see more of the exhibits.

| Timing | Best order |
| --- | --- |
| Early visit | Ojukheon and the museum, then Jungang Market and nearby Wolhwa Street. |
| Near 17:00 | Go straight to the market instead of rushing from the station. |
| Holiday | Indoor exhibits close on January 1, Seollal and Chuseok day. Visit the outdoor grounds if conditions suit you. |

## Start at Ojukheon and the Municipal Museum

Save **강릉시 오죽헌·시립박물관**, at **강원특별자치도 강릉시 율곡로3139번길 24**. The complex includes the historic house, indoor exhibitions and outdoor grounds. Its regular hours are **09:00–18:00**, with ticket sales ending at **17:00**. Standard individual adult admission is **₩3,000**. Check for exceptions before traveling. [KTO visitor information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=110674) and [Gangneung’s operating ordinance](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/ordinInfoP.do?ordinSeq=2146155)

For light rain, put the indoor exhibitions first and shorten the time in the grounds. Expect outdoor walking between parts of the complex. For step-free access or wheelchair or stroller loans, check the entrance approach and current availability with the museum. KTO lists ramps and loan equipment, along with the on-site contact details. [KTO accessibility and holiday information](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/ms/detail.do?cotId=b4e76408-f34d-4778-8641-dbcceb340ece)

## Finish downtown at Jungang Market

Save **강릉 중앙시장**, at **강원특별자치도 강릉시 금성로 21**. Check the next suitable bus in the [Gangneung bus information system](https://bis.gn.go.kr/search). If the wait and walk would use up your museum or train buffer, use a taxi and show the Korean destination. Check both stops before committing to a bus journey.

At the market, choose your meal first and browse afterward. KTO lists **08:30–21:00** as a broad operating window and says hours vary by shop. If you have time after eating, make a short stop on [Wolhwa Street](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=249838), beside the market. [KTO market information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=66096)
