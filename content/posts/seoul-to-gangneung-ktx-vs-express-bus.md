**Start with KTX if Seoul Station or Cheongnyangni is convenient to your hotel. Check the express bus from Seoul Gyeongbu if Gangnam is easier or the train you want is full.** In Gangneung, the railway station and the express bus terminal are separate arrivals. Compare each with your hotel or first stop before buying.

## Choose by where you start and finish

| Choice | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| **KTX** | For Gangneung Station, search **서울 (Seoul)** and **청량리 (Cheongnyangni)** separately. Use the origin on the actual ticket and the time you can reach that platform from your hotel. |
| **Express bus** | Use **서울경부 (Seoul Gyeongbu)** as the confirmed origin for this route. If you are nearer Gangbyeon Station, check Dong Seoul separately and use whichever terminal the selected ticket shows. |
| **First stop** | Compare the last leg from **강릉역** and **강릉고속버스터미널** to that exact address. An arrival labeled Gangneung does not tell you which building or onward stop to use. |

The [Korea Tourism Organization train guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?menuSn=470&vcontsId=140656) explains the KTX network and the available ticket channels. The [bus operator’s route list](https://en.dongbubus.co.kr/?redirect=no) names Seoul Gyeongbu and Gangneung and shows a separate listing for Gyeongpo Beach. Search your date to see what is bookable before using any of these as a boarding plan. For other origins and bus ticket systems, compare the [Korea Tourism Organization long-distance bus guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=471&vcontsId=140657).

## Book the trip in this order

1. **Save the first address.** If the first stop is a beach, pin that beach by name. For a hotel, use the Korean address from the reservation rather than a neighborhood name.
2. **Check both rail origins at [Korail](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/main).** Choose **Gangneung** as the destination. Compare the same travel date, similar departure times and travel to the actual station. If the booking page fails to load, the tourism organization lists the station ticket office and Korail’s English booking assistance.
3. **Check the bus through the operator’s Gangneung route list.** Open the booking link for that route and confirm origin, destination and passenger class. Read the exact boarding location, including the building, when you receive your ticket. A bus departure from Gangbyeon and one from Gangnam are separate trips to the terminal.
4. **Keep the return visible before you commit.** For a day trip, note the return station or terminal next to the booking confirmation and confirm the local leg after your last sight. A slower service that leaves from the right area can make the day easier than rushing to a distant platform.

## Save the two Gangneung arrivals

- **Gangneung Station (강릉역)** · `강원특별자치도 강릉시 용지로 176`. Korea's [national visitor information dataset](https://www.data.go.kr/en/data/15013112/standard.do) puts the rail station’s tourism desk at this address.
- **Gangneung Express Bus Terminal (강릉고속버스터미널)** · `강원특별자치도 강릉시 하슬라로 15`. Use the [bus operator’s terminal directory](https://www.dongbubus.co.kr/terminal) for the express terminal address. Follow the booking confirmation and the terminal’s posted signs for boarding.

Check the [city bus information system](https://bis.gn.go.kr/search) or a map for the onward route and its direction. Leave a margin if you must switch modes between the two buildings. Fares, running times and seat availability depend on the departure you select. Compare the door-to-door trip and use the displayed booking amount instead of estimating from an older fare table.

## Pick the first outing

From the rail station, use the [Gangneung Station to Anmok guide](/blog/gangneung-station-anmok) to plan a separate waterfront stop. For a different stretch of coast, choose the [Gyeongpo Lake and beach outing](/blog/gangneung-gyeongpo-lake-and-beach-walk). If the wind or rain makes a long walk unattractive, plan [Ojukheon and Jungang Market](/blog/gangneung-ojukheon-and-jungang-market) around the heritage site’s last entry and the market stalls you want to visit.
