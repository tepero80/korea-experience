**Choose Vivaldi Park for a day from Seoul if you can confirm the return coach. Try MONA YongPyong if you want more terrain choices, or Alpensia if you want to stay in the resort village and can confirm a suitable run and lesson.** Compare what’s open for your dates and skill level before paying for transport or a room.

## Pick the resort that fits your trip

| Resort | Who should start here |
| --- | --- |
| <strong style={{ overflowWrap: 'normal' }}>Vivaldi Park</strong> | A Seoul-based day trip. Confirm a visitor coach in both directions and allow time for rentals, a lesson and the walk back to pickup. |
| <strong style={{ overflowWrap: 'normal' }}>MONA YongPyong</strong> | A Pyeongchang stay where you want a choice of ski runs. Check the open-slope board for your level and book the ground transfer if you arrive by rail. |
| <strong style={{ overflowWrap: 'normal' }}>Alpensia</strong> | An overnight stay for a mixed group. Check the beginner slope, lesson slots and walking route from the exact hotel you’re booking. |

## Vivaldi Park: day trip from Seoul

The [Vivaldi Park winter notice](https://www.sonohotelsresorts.com/skiboard/info) still listed the **opening date as undecided** when checked on November 23, 2026. The operator says weather can change slope availability and operating hours. Helmets are required for skiing, and the minimum age listed for ski lifts and moving walkways is four.

- **Find the right shuttle.** The [resort transport page](https://www.sonohotelsresorts.com/skiboard/location?isFooterFrom=Y) separates local reservations and visitor shuttle bookings. The [English visitor coach site](https://vivabus.rideus.net/en/vivabus/) lists routes from areas including Hongik University and Sports Complex stations. Search your exact dates, check the boarding point and confirm the return before paying for a lesson.
- **Check the cancellation clock.** The visitor coach operator lists a full refund for cancellation **before 16:00 Korea time the day before boarding**. Check the most recent terms before checkout. The operator says an unbooked departure may not run, so reserve ahead.
- **Decide what everyone will do.** If some people want snow play without skiing, check Snowy Land’s own schedule and tickets through the resort before promising it as a backup. Allow enough time to return rentals before the last coach.

## MONA YongPyong: check the runs you came for

MONA YongPyong published a [2026–27 opening notice](https://www.yongpyong.co.kr/eng/about/newsRead.do?noticeId=1704) on November 18. Use the [open-slope board](https://www.yongpyong.co.kr/eng/skiNboard/openStatusBoard.do) to check the runs and sessions available when you visit. A season opening notice can precede the opening of your preferred terrain.

- **Check the active run and the lift that reaches it.** Look at the operator’s run labels and open sessions, especially if your group has mixed ability or wants a particular area.
- **Plan the trip from Jinbu Station.** Read the [resort’s transport instructions](https://www.yongpyong.co.kr/eng/about/car.do). The Jinbu shuttle is listed for eligible guests with reservation or membership proof, and seats are limited. Confirm that your booking qualifies and keep a road-transfer fallback.
- **Compare the actual lodging location.** Check where you’ll collect rental equipment and start a lesson. A resort address can cover multiple buildings. Allow time for the walk or any on-site connection in ski boots.

## Alpensia: book the room and lesson around the same slope

Alpensia lists **Alpha** as its beginner run and **Bravo** as intermediate on the [Ski 700 trail guide](https://www.alpensia.com/en/ski/introduction-slope.do). The [lift-fee page](https://m.alpensia.com/ski/use-cost-lift.do?lang=en) still marked the 2026–27 season as being prepared at review. Check the [current slope and lift board](https://www.alpensia.com/ski/slope-now.do) before treating either run as open.

- **Choose the exact building.** InterContinental, Holiday Inn Resort Hotel and Holiday Inn & Suites are separate properties in the resort village. Check the walk from your booking to the rental counter and your lesson meeting point.
- **Check transport eligibility.** The [Alpensia transport page](https://www.alpensia.com/en/guide/directions-shuttle.do) lists a Jinbu shuttle for resort guests who can show qualifying proof. Confirm the current timetable and seats with the operator. Nearby MONA YongPyong is a separate resort, so verify transfer, ticket and meeting details for the one you booked.

## Price the whole trip before you confirm

1. **Check the lesson first.** Ask about language, age, group size, meeting point and whether a lift ticket or rentals are required. Check the route from the counter to the lesson.
2. **Count the separate charges.** Compare transport both ways, lift access, equipment, clothing or helmet hire, lessons and any lodging. A package price is comparable only after you know what it includes.
3. **Coordinate cancellation windows.** Save the operator’s cutoff in Korea time, whether weather-related changes are refundable, and what you can change if the suitable slope closes.
4. **Plan the return.** Save the stop name in Korean and leave time to return gear. If the opening, transfer or lesson is uncertain, favor flexible bookings or choose a day with confirmed service.
