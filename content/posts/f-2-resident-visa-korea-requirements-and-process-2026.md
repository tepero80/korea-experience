## Compare the routes using your actual status

Open the English attachment on the [Korea Immigration Service Visa Navigator page](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/immigration_eng/230/570965/artclView.do) and start with the Resident chapter. The handbook is a 2023 overview. Use it to identify a route to ask about, then check the latest requirements for that code with immigration.

| If this is your situation | What to ask immigration |
| --- | --- |
| You are considering F-2-7 because of your job or qualifications | Which applicant group covers you? Which version of the points rules applies, and what evidence can count for each claim? |
| You are considering F-2-99 because you have lived in Korea for several years | Which prior status periods count toward continuous residence? Ask about time abroad, changes of status and any dates that are unclear in your record. |
| Your reason involves family, investment or another basis | What is the exact residence code for that basis? Ask which route instructions and documents match it. |

## Prepare questions before paying for documents

Make a one-page record with your current status and expiry date, past status changes and the start date of your current job or studies. Note any trips abroad that may matter to your residence history. If you are considering F-2-7, mark which points would depend on proof from an employer or school. If you are checking F-2-99, identify gaps in your timeline before requesting older records. Keep this working list private and share documents only through the filing channel the authority gives you.

Ask [Korea Immigration on 1345](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/1862/subview.do) which tax year, document dates and evidence formats it accepts for your subcategory. Get the applicable checklist before paying for translations, certificates or language assessments. If you plan to change employers, take side work or move, describe the planned change and ask how it would affect your pending application and any permission or reporting requirements.

## Make a plan for the current expiry date

[Korea Immigration's stay administration page](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration/1515/subview.do) explains booking an immigration office visit through HiKorea. Ask which office handles your address, whether your route can be filed in Korea and when it must be received. If you cannot gather the documents or get an appointment before your current authorization expires, contact immigration promptly about the options for your present status. Ask separately what to do if you need to travel while an application is pending.
