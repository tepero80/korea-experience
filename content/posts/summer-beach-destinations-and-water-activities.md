Choose a beach by the day you want. Gwangalli works for a city waterfront walk and bridge views. Hamdeok works for a shore outing from Jeju City. Daecheon makes sense if the official festival schedule matches your dates. For swimming, first check the specific beach’s current operating dates, staffed hours and designated zones. Plan a shore visit if those conditions do not fit.

| Your plan | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| City shore and bridge views | Gwangalli (광안리해수욕장), with a separate decision about any evening show. |
| Jeju shore and a bus return | Hamdeok (함덕해수욕장). Choose a return bus before walking farther along the shore. |
| Lake or open coast when swimming is closed | Plan a land-based or indoor alternative. Check any seasonal restrictions for the place you choose. |
| A named event or booked lesson | Check the organizer’s current date, exact venue, cancellation terms and designated activity zone. |

## Check the water rules at your exact beach

The [Korea Coast Guard](https://www.kcg.go.kr/donghaecgh/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?nttSn=70831) directs visitors to designated open beaches with safety staff. Check conditions and the swimming area on site and follow lifeguards’ instructions. The [Jeju Tourism Organization’s guidance](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CNTS_300000000014629) also advises visitors to stay out of ports and to reassess swimming plans in strong winds, swells or rain. A calm-looking port or a shallow stretch in a photo is not evidence of safe swimming. If you cannot confirm the conditions, stay on land.

## Busan beach walks and events

Use [Busan’s official beach directory](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/beaches) to compare Gwangalli, Haeundae, Songjeong and other options. A beach can be open for walking while sea-bathing hours and staffed services are seasonal. The city’s [dated 2026 season notice](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/1732740) illustrates that different beaches open on different dates. Check a notice for your travel year. At Gwangalli, check the [official drone show notice](https://www.visitbusan.net/schedule/view.do?boardId=BBS_0000010&dataSid=6343&menuCd=DOM_000000304003000000) before timing dinner or the ride home. Wind and rain can change the program.

## Jeju shore visits and the return bus

Start with a [bus plan from Jeju Airport to Hamdeok](/blog/jeju-airport-to-hamdeok-beach-by-bus). Check the bus direction and return stop. For Hyeopjae, use the [official visitor listing](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500697&menuId=DOM_700000000010776) to verify the current season and services. Choose each beach separately. If wind or rain changes the plan, save a shorter shore walk or return to your base before the bus becomes inconvenient.

## Boryeong and Daecheon events

The [Boryeong Mud Festival organizer](https://mudfestival.or.kr/en/festival/main.html) lists Daecheon Beach as the venue. Check the edition and the event zone on the organizer’s page before booking transport or buying a ticket. Festival entry and sea bathing are separate decisions. For a walk, choose a public shore route and give the evening transport its own time.

For a separate metro-based walk, plan [Dadaepo Beach from Line 1](/blog/dadaepo-beach-from-busan-metro).
