Yutnori (윷놀이, also written yunnori) is a board game; jegichagi (제기차기) is a shuttlecock-kicking game. Both can be part of holiday activities, but ask a venue whether equipment is available on your date.

## Try the basics

| Game | How to start |
| --- | --- |
| Yutnori | Divide into two teams, toss four sticks and move markers according to the result. The aim is to get all four markers around the board first. Ask your host to explain local rules and shortcuts. |
| Jegichagi | Kick a jegi repeatedly, trying to keep it from touching the ground. Give players clear space and adapt the challenge to their ability. |
