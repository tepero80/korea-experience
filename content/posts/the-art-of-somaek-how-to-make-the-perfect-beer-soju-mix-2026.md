## Check what has been poured

[Korea Tourism Organization’s description](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=189721) explains that somaek combines beer and soju and that proportions vary. If a table orders bottles together, ask who is mixing the drinks and whether you can keep your choice separate. You can ask about a glass without accepting it.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Mix | “맥주랑 소주를 섞은 건가요?” asks whether beer and soju have been mixed. |
| Size | Ask how a particular glass is prepared. The size of the glass and the proportion of each drink can change, so use the details of your serving. |
| Choice | “저는 물이나 탄산음료로 할게요” lets the server know you would like water or a soft drink. |
| Label | If you want a beer-style product, look at the container. The [food regulator’s 2024 notice](https://www.mfds.go.kr/brd/m_1105/view.do?seq=33714) distinguishes 무알코올 and 비알코올 in its inspection guidance. Ask to see the exact product when alcohol content matters. |

## Decide before someone tops up the glass

Say which drink you want when ordering, rather than asking someone to guess from the appearance of your glass. If you need to avoid alcohol and the ingredients are uncertain, choose a separate soft drink or water. Ask about bottle sizes and prices before adding another order.
