**For:** travelers visiting the Anguk area. **Information checked:** 9 November 2026.

For travelers near Anguk who want a short look at historic Korean architecture, Unhyeongung is a free cultural site close to the station. It is useful between other plans, but opening hours and access to individual spaces can change for events. Check the venue before traveling.

| Info | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Admission | Free |
| Location | 464 Samil-daero, Jongno-gu, Seoul / 서울특별시 종로구 삼일대로 464 |
| Subway | Anguk Station, line 3, exit 4; the city tourism listing gives approximately 103 m |
| Closed | Mondays, except public-holiday Mondays according to the city tourism listing |
| Contact | 02-766-9090 |

The [official Seoul tourism listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/walking-tour/UnhyeongungPalace/ENP000471) describes a special evening schedule from September 1 through November 29, 2026: Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday until 21:00, with last admission at 20:30. Its general hours are not fully consistent with other seasonal information, so **confirm the relevant date and last entry directly with the venue** rather than assuming that every day closes at 19:00 or 21:00.

## An easy way to include it

1. Search **운현궁** or copy the Korean address into your map. From Anguk Station exit 4, follow the current walking route.
2. Before entering, check signs for event closures and photography restrictions. Treat the buildings and grounds as a heritage site, not a photo set available for unrestricted use.
3. Look at the courtyards and the arrangement of the buildings. If a child wants an activity, check the venue’s current calendar for age, language, price and booking conditions; cultural programs are not necessarily available every day.
4. Continue to another Anguk-area activity at your own pace. Confirm its opening hours separately rather than assuming that sites share the same closure day.

The tourism listing notes accessible restrooms, but we have not verified the whole route or all interior thresholds. If stairs or uneven surfaces would affect your trip, contact the venue first and ask about the current accessible route.

## If you arrive after entry closes

Do not assume evening hours apply on Thursday or on dates after November 29, 2026. Choose another activity after checking its current opening information, or reschedule this visit. Save the official listing and phone number before leaving your accommodation.
