## Ask for the name and route

The [Korean MFDS safety notice](https://impfood.mfds.go.kr/CFBBB02F02/getCntntsDetail?cntntsSn=282845) warned about cosmetic uses outside authorized medicine labeling. An [FDA safety reminder](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/fda-reminds-compounders-not-use-dietary-supplement-grade-glutathione-injectables) describes adverse events in the United States after certain compounded glutathione injections. Check the exact product and proposed use with the clinician instead of applying these findings to a different product or country.

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Ask for the exact name and the local patient or packaging information. |
| Use | Ask which authorized use in Korea applies to the advertised injection. |
| Details | Ask who will assess your suitability and which risks and options apply. |
| Label | For a topical cosmetic, compare ingredients, use instructions and precautions on the box. |

## Compare only the information you can verify

Use the [MFDS cosmetics labeling guide](https://mfds.go.kr/wpge/m_645/de050601l0024.do) to identify the ingredients and precautions on a topical product. If you are being offered an injection, ask for the regulator’s product entry or patient leaflet and time to ask your clinician about the proposed use.

See the [Korean skincare-label guide](/blog/korean-skincare-ingredients-snail-mucin-to-centella) to understand the Korean terms on a packaged cosmetic.
