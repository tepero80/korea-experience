## Pick breakfast around the first booking

| Need | Breakfast plan |
| --- | --- |
| Hot bowl and a seat | Confirm a porridge shop near the hotel. The official visitor directory lists Seowon on Myeongdong Street as a porridge specialist that opens early. |
| Browse and share | Use Gwangjang Market later in the morning if the counters you want are open. Look for bindaetteok (빈대떡, mung bean pancake) or gimbap (김밥, seaweed rice rolls). |
| Fixed departure | Buy a simple takeaway near the hotel or station. Keep enough time to find your platform or meeting point. |

Seowon is one named place to look up. Check the latest hours on your travel date and ask whether the menu you want is available. If you want a plain bowl, show 죽 (juk, porridge) and point to a specific menu item. Ask about stock, toppings and seafood before ordering when ingredients matter.

## Make a short first-morning plan

1. Save two addresses the evening before, one near your bed and one near your departure point.
2. Check the walking time for the actual exit and put the train or tour time first.
3. On a flexible morning, head to the Gwangjang food aisle to pick a counter you can see operating. Agree on an order and portion before taking a seat.
4. If the market is quiet and you have a timetable, use your backup and keep the market for lunch.

If you are looking for gukbap, a soup-and-rice breakfast rather than porridge, use the [Korean breakfast food guide](/blog/korean-breakfast-foods-traditional-morning-meals) to identify the right menu, including a named Jeonju stop.
