From Yeosu Expo Station (여수엑스포역), head for Odongdo Entrance (오동도 입구), then cross the causeway to the island. Walk if your group has time and energy for both the approach and the island paths. A taxi to the entrance saves that energy, especially with luggage or in poor weather; check the fare and return arrangements before setting out.

## Choose where to spend your walking time

| From Expo Station | Allow for |
| --- | --- |
| Walk to 오동도 입구 | The route to the mainland entrance, the causeway and your time on the island. |
| Taxi to 오동도 입구 | Confirm the drop-off. The island approach is still part of the visit. |
| Train after the walk | Set a turnaround time and plan the return to 여수엑스포역. |

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s Yeosu overview](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1593187) describes the Expo Station and Odongdo area as suitable for walking or public transport. A station-to-island walk is not a single flat loop: the entrance, causeway and island paths are separate legs. Check the crossing and current route in a navigation app rather than counting on a fixed walking time.

## On Odongdo

The [official Odongdo listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=93901) identifies the island as part of Hallyeohaesang National Park. At the entrance check the site map and notices, then choose a signed route towards the wooded paths or lighthouse. Conditions and gradients vary; ask on site about accessibility instead of assuming every trail is level. Turn back if coastal weather or footing deteriorates.

Search **오동도 입구** for the mainland entrance and **오동도** for the island. If you want another view of the coast, compare boarding on the Jasan side in our [cable-car guide](/blog/yeosu-maritime-cable-car-boarding-guide). Keep the return to your hotel or train on the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
