## Which day should you visit?

Pick **Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday** if you want to walk around Jongmyo at your own pace. On Monday, Thursday and Friday, enter at a scheduled time. If you want the English tour, the published departures on those regular timed days are **10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00**. Tuesday is the regular closing day. Check the operator's calendar and notices for your date before arranging another booking around it.

| When | Route and checks |
| --- | --- |
| Wed, Sat, Sun and public holidays | Visit independently. Arrive before last admission. Ask staff about the passage to Changgyeonggung if you want to visit both. |
| Mon, Thu and Fri | Enter at a set time. Look for the English option when reserving. Allow about 50 minutes for the visit. |
| Tue | Normally closed. Use the date-specific notice for holiday exceptions. |

The [heritage operator's Korean visiting calendar](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do) lists independent entry every Wednesday. The [English Visit Seoul listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/PalaceArea/Jongmyo/ENP000507) still describes the last Wednesday of the month. Use the operator's calendar for your visit. From November to January it lists 09:00 to 17:30, with last admission at 16:30. A timed day still requires a scheduled entry within that window.

## Book or start at the main entrance

On the [official booking page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R601000000.do?schGroupCode=jms), look for **[영어] 시간제관람예약** for English timed admission. The operator lists 100 online and 100 on-site places per timed session. Use the online allocation for a fixed itinerary and have a second time in mind if your first is full. Check the [admission table](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R703000000.do?schGroupCode=jms) for age and fee rules. The standard fee for foreign visitors aged 19 to 64 is ₩1,000. The operator lists free admission on Wednesdays and age-based exemptions on other days. Bring proof if claiming an age exemption.

Use **종묘**, address **서울 종로구 종로 157**, as the starting point. Take Line 1, 3 or 5 to **Jongno 3-ga** and follow the operator's directions from exit 11 or 8. This is a visit to a [royal ancestral shrine](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E105010000.do). Plan time for the courtyards and the ceremonial buildings, including Yeongnyeongjeon when your visit route includes it.

## Pair Jongmyo with Changgyeonggung

On an independent-entry day, ask at Jongmyo about the **North Gate (북신문)** and Changgyeonggung's **Yulgok-ro entrance (율곡로 출입문)** before you head up the grounds. The visiting calendar lists both gates for these days. The [palace operator's FAQ](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R404000000.do?schGroupCode=cgg) estimates 10 to 20 minutes from Honghwamun to the North Gate through palace grounds. The sites have separate admission. Confirm both gates and their cutoffs with staff. Evening hours at Changgyeonggung do not extend Jongmyo admission.

If the connecting gates are shut, go back to the Jongmyo main entrance. Route on the street to **Honghwamun (홍화문)** at **서울 종로구 창경궁로 185**. The Changgyeonggung map pin here marks that street entrance. If you want the palace after dark, use the [ordinary Changgyeonggung evening guide](/blog/changgyeonggung-ordinary-evening-visit) and plan your return from Honghwamun.
