**Use Central Park Station Exit 3 for the Tri-Bowl, the Urban History Museum and an easy start by the water. Use Exit 2 if you are meeting someone by the West Boat House.** Pick one shore and turn back at a bridge or landmark that suits your group. For an optional boat ride, confirm the operating boat house and last boarding before walking along the canal.

This guide is for a short visit in Songdo or a break between commitments near the convention center. Allow **45–75 minutes for one waterfront section** or **about two hours if you add the museum**. Those are planning allowances. Boat waiting, hotel walks and transit are separate. Information checked **23 November 2026**.

## Choose your exit and turnaround point

| What matters today | Start here | Where to turn back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First visit or a short gap | **Exit 3 → Tri-Bowl exterior → canal-side path.** Take the branch toward the water and choose an out-and-back route. | Return the way you came before the next commitment. |
| Meet near the western waterfront | **Exit 2 → West Boat House → Sunset Garden.** Use the boat house as a meeting landmark. | Turn back after the garden if you have a train or event to catch. |
| Museum or changeable weather | **Exit 3 → Incheon Urban History Museum.** Walk a short waterfront section before or after only if conditions suit. | Keep Exit 3 as your return point and check the museum's last admission. |
| A boat ride matters most | Look up the **East Boat House** and confirm the meeting point on your booking. | Return to the agreed boarding area and allow time for the walk to your next stop. |

The [subway operator's exit guide](https://www.ictr.or.kr/main/subway/subwayStation.do?line_no=1&station_no=137) lists the West Boat House near Exit 2 and the park, Tri-Bowl and museum near Exit 3. Search [센트럴파크역 on Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%84%BC%ED%8A%B8%EB%9F%B4%ED%8C%8C%ED%81%AC%EC%97%AD) and select **Incheon Subway Line 1, station I137**. The station address is **인천광역시 연수구 인천타워대로 지하 240**. For the waterside park itself, save [송도 센트럴파크](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%86%A1%EB%8F%84%20%EC%84%BC%ED%8A%B8%EB%9F%B4%ED%8C%8C%ED%81%AC), **인천광역시 연수구 컨벤시아대로 160 일원**. [Park facilities and location](https://www.insiseol.or.kr/park/songdo/facility/central.jsp)

### Follow the water for as long as you have time

At the Tri-Bowl, get your bearings at the water. On a short outing, stay on one bank and turn back once you have used about half of your walking time. If you head west, the park operator places the **West Boat House and Sunset Garden** at that end. If you go east to meet a boat, enter the East Boat House's full Korean name, **이스트보트하우스**, in your map app and compare it with the pickup point you were given. A park-wide pin can leave you with an extra walk. The [Incheon Free Economic Zone Authority](https://www.ifez.go.kr/main/tour/view.do?tour_fclt_sn=4) gives the waterway length as **1.8 km**. Distance varies with the stretch of path and crossings you choose.

### Confirm the boat service separately

The [Korea Tourism Organization's park listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=61406) lists free park admission and warns that East Boat House hours can change with season and weather. For the boat you actually want, confirm the **boarding deck, last boarding, current price, group capacity and weather policy** before paying. The [IFEZ East Boat House listing](https://www.ifez.go.kr/journaleng/life/view.do?jrnl_pst_sn=641) provides **032-831-4609** for enquiries. If the boat is unavailable, keep the waterfront walk and choose a turnaround point near the station.

## Plan for weather and mobility

- **Need a lift?** The subway operator maps a lift route near **Exit 3**. Check its status and the surface route to your destination. The [park operator's garden descriptions](https://www.insiseol.or.kr/park/songdo/facility/central.jsp) include areas with changing elevation. Choose a short section you can comfortably return along, and verify the route before attempting any bridge or alternative bank.
- **Need an indoor stop?** Save **인천도시역사관**, **인천광역시 연수구 인천타워대로 238**. The [museum's English visitor page](https://www.incheon.go.kr/museum/MU050601) lists free entry, **09:00–18:00** with **17:30 last admission** and Monday closures except holidays, plus New Year's Day closure. Check current notices for your visit.
- **Just enough time after an event?** Choose a single bank, tell your group which exit you will return to and check the [park operator's notices](https://www.insiseol.or.kr/park/songdo/notice/notice.jsp) if using a seasonal facility. For an evening walk, check your return train and weather before going farther along the water.

Coming straight from a flight? Compare the [airport coach and taxi options for Songdo hotels](/blog/incheon-airport-to-songdo-hotels). For a separate day around port-era streets and markets, start with the [Incheon Chinatown and Open Port walk](/blog/incheon-chinatown-open-port-walking-route).
