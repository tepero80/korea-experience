Start with **where and when you can return the bike**. A Seoul Bike trip, a staffed Busan rental and a Jeju rental follow different rules.

## Pick the simplest rental for your route

| Where | Start here and check before you ride |
| --- | --- |
| Seoul | **[Seoul Bike / 따릉이](https://www.bikeseoul.com/).** Check the visitor ticket, ride limit and your return dock. |
| Busan | **[City rental counter directory](https://www.busan.go.kr/depart/ahbicycle04).** Call about hours, accepted ID, available bikes and the last return. |
| Jeju | **[Fantasy Bicycle Path](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/themtour/view?contentsid=CNTS_300000000013850&menuId=DOM_200000000011166).** Choose a segment and contact a nearby rental shop about bike fit, helmet, deposit and pickup and return. |

## Seoul: buy the pass and return at a marked station

1. Open the English instructions from the [Seoul Metropolitan Government](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-bike-instructions-and-chatbot-service-for-international-tourists/). Select the visitor or non-member ticket offered for your ride and check the duration before paying.
2. Pick a departure station and at least one return station before unlocking. Plan buffer time for crossings and finding the return dock. A day ticket can have a shorter limit on a continuous checkout. See the [city’s return and extra-time rules](https://topis.seoul.go.kr/openEngBicycle.do).
3. At the end, follow the app’s locking instructions and check for a confirmed return. Keep the bike number and station name if you need to contact support. If you cannot buy a ticket or suitable bikes are unavailable, walk your chosen park segment or use a staffed rental whose conditions you can confirm.

## Busan: confirm the counter before traveling

The city lists the **Geumjeong** counter in the public parking area north of Oncheonjang Station (온천장역 북측 공영주차장), phone **051-519-4554**. It also lists the **Suyeong** counter on Gwangannamhaebyeon-ro (부산 수영구 광안해변로 100), phone **051-610-4555**. Call about the weekday/weekend schedule, last return, ID for overseas visitors and availability. A free-rental listing does not say whether a visitor can borrow a bike without the required documentation.

## Jeju: book near a short out-and-back route

Use the official route overview to choose a short coastal segment, then contact an operator serving that area. Ask where to ride, how to return safely, and whether a one-way drop-off is offered for your booking. Have a short turnaround point and enough time to ride back to the agreed return location. If the wind, visibility or conditions make the ride unsuitable, switch to a non-cycling stop from the [Jeju three-day guide](/blog/jeju-island-complete-travel-guide-best-time-to-visit-2026).
