If your Korean employer withholds income tax from your monthly salary, ask its payroll team how it handles year-end settlement, called 연말정산. Your employer compares the final tax calculated for the year with the tax it already withheld. The difference can result in a refund through payroll or an additional deduction from pay. The [National Tax Service’s year-end settlement FAQ](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?mi=&nttSn=1347723) explains this reconciliation.

## Ask payroll for the year and submission deadline

Name the year in which you earned the wages. Payroll may be collecting information in the following calendar year. The [NTS English manual](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?mi=10790&nttSn=1347688) linked here covers 2025 income. Use the edition for your own income year when checking any eligibility or document rule.

| What to ask for | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Steps | Confirm the income year, cutoff and secure way to send employer forms and evidence. |
| Proof | Ask what comes from Hometax and what would need separate proof for that year. |
| Tax receipt | Ask for the 근로소득 원천징수영수증 and check wage income, tax withheld and the settlement amount. |
| Next payslip | Ask where the adjustment will appear and which amounts are national or local income tax. |

## Compare methods only if payroll confirms eligibility

The [Tax Incentive Limitation Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1033251637) describes an optional 19% national flat rate for qualifying foreign employment income. Eligibility depends on the start of your work in Korea, the tax period and employer conditions. The general rule uses a December 31, 2026 cutoff for starting work in Korea and a period of up to 20 years from that first work date. The article of law also sets out exceptions, including for qualifying regional headquarters. Confirm your own dates and employer with payroll or NTS before electing a method.

The [NTS comparison of the two methods](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/ad/help/2020yearEndTaxCalclPage.do?mi=135022) explains that the optional rate generally gives up deductions and credits used in ordinary settlement and compares different taxable pay bases. Ask payroll to show both calculations for the same tax year if the option applies, with local income tax shown separately. There is no single salary at which the optional method is best for everyone.

## Keep a copy of what payroll used

Save the receipt and payroll’s submission confirmation in a place you can still access after you leave your job.
