## Start with the event and the record

Write down what changed, the effective date, and any deadline you have been given. Then identify who controls the record. The [immigration application form guide](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/immigration/209/561864/artclView.do) lists an address report, a change to registered details, and a workplace application or report as separate services.

| Event | First check |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Record the move-in date. Identify which address report applies and which proof the receiving office accepts. |
| New passport | Keep both passports or a record of the old details. Ask immigration whether a registered-details report is due and what date starts the deadline. |
| Job change | Ask immigration if any permission or reporting applies. Ask each employer which payroll records or tax receipts it can provide and when. |
| Login error | Record the service, date, and error text without exposing IDs or codes. Ask that provider which name, phone, or identity record it uses. |

## Confirm the correct reporting deadline

The [Immigration Act’s address rule](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1028069497) gives foreign residents registered under Article 31 **15 days from moving in**. The [Overseas Koreans Act’s domestic residence rule](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1021410415) gives **14 days** for a move from a domestic residence reported under its Article 6. Call [the 1345 Immigration Contact Center](https://www.immigration.go.kr/moj/196/subview.do) promptly if you are unsure which record applies. For channels, proof and a fallback if online filing fails, use the [foreign-resident address reporting guide](/blog/how-to-register-your-foreign-address-change-online-and-offline-2026).

## Follow up with each organization separately

Once a report is accepted, save the receipt and note when you verified the displayed record. Ask your bank, mobile provider, and employer which details they still hold and whether they need proof of the change. If the new name or address does not appear, show the relevant receipt through the organization’s secure support channel.

For a payroll or income-tax issue, keep a record of the tax year, employer, and receipt number. Ask payroll what it submitted, then contact the [National Tax Service's foreign-employee help service](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/na/ntt/selectNttInfo.do?mi=&nttSn=1347723) if you need to confirm which office or process handles the remaining issue. The linked example covers 2025 income. Ask for the instructions for your own tax year.
