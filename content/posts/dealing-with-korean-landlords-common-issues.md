## Report what you see and what you need

Write down the affected room or appliance, when the problem began and any impact on using the home. If it is safe, keep dated photos and share them through the contact in your agreement. Ask who will arrange a visit, when it can happen and what access the worker needs.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Report | Use a short message with the issue, address or unit details for the responsible contact, and two times the worker can visit. |
| Agree | Confirm the scope, who will authorize the work and whether there will be any cost before agreeing to pay for it. |
| Save | Keep photos, replies, dates, agreed access and any invoice. Follow up in writing if the visit or repair changes. |
| Help | Ask about the eligibility and appointment for [Seoul’s foreign resident consultation](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/ainf/ctrs/frct/srch/dopr/doprListPage.do?cntr_no=04E34E2046394333BCC451C20A4850D9) or use the [city housing counseling list](https://housing.seoul.go.kr/site/main/content/sh04_020100) to check the appropriate route. |

## Ask for the next step if a repair stalls

Bring the lease, the messages and any proposed charges to a counselor and explain the result you need. For disagreement about changes to payments or the lease, get case-specific advice before taking action.

See the [deposit protection checklist](/blog/korean-jeonse-housing-system-explained-for-foreigners-2026) if your separate concern is the return of a large deposit at the end of the lease.
