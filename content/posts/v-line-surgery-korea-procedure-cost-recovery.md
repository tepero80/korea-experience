## Ask which problem the clinic proposes to address

Start with two descriptions. Say what you want to ask about the shape of your lower face. Then describe how your jaw works now, including any problems with chewing, opening your mouth or the way your teeth meet. Tell the team if you wear braces or a retainer. Ask the surgeon to name the procedure and describe its aim in plain language.

[Cambridge University Hospitals’ guide to corrective jaw and chin surgery](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/patient-information-and-consent-to-jaw-or-chin-corrective-surgery-orthognathic-surgery/) describes bite correction that orthodontics alone cannot achieve as one reason for corrective jaw surgery. It describes a change in chin position as a separate aim. Ask the clinic whether it is proposing an appearance change, a bite correction or both, and who would assess your bite before you decide.

| Check | Ask the treating team |
| --- | --- |
| Aim | What exact procedure are you proposing and what issue is it intended to address? |
| Bite | Would a dentist, orthodontist or oral and maxillofacial clinician need to see me first? |
| Nerves | If the plan could affect feeling in my lip or chin, what would you assess before treatment and whom would I contact if feeling changes? |
| Review | Who would check jaw movement and my bite after the procedure, and when would they need to see me in person? |

## Arrange a handoff for jaw and dental care

Ask your usual clinician which records they would need if you have treatment elsewhere. The [NHS treatment abroad checklist](https://www.nhs.uk/using-the-nhs/healthcare-abroad/going-abroad-for-treatment/treatment-abroad-checklist/) recommends discussing how to transfer medical notes and how the teams will coordinate aftercare. Ask the Korean clinic what findings, imaging reports and treatment notes it can share, and which provider would be responsible for each follow-up visit.

Tell the treating team your planned departure date without assuming it will still work. Ask what review they would require before you leave and what could change the plan. Use the [checklist for medical records in English](/blog/korean-medical-records-getting-english-translations) to match each document to the clinician who will receive it.
