If you pre-order duty-free goods for an Incheon departure, confirm the **retailer's pickup counter and your departure terminal** before paying. Enter the passenger's details as shown on the passport, then keep the physical passport, boarding pass and order voucher ready for pickup.

## Choose the right shopping route

| Your purchase | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| **Buying at the airport** | Check [Incheon Airport's duty-free directory](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1531/subview.do) for stores in your terminal. Confirm stock and opening hours before relying on a particular shop. |
| **Pre-ordering from a duty-free retailer** | Read the retailer's deadline and pickup instructions for your flight. Confirm the pickup location before paying. |
| **Shopping at a participating city retailer and receiving tax refund paperwork** | Follow the [tourist tax refund guide](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim). That process uses the shop's receipt and export verification rather than a duty-free pre-order voucher. |

The [Korea Tourism Organization's guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=140736) explains the two systems. If you are comparing prices, use the actual sizes, currency, taxes or fees at your destination, and the total at checkout.

## Before you order

1. **Confirm your flight.** Check the terminal with your airline and verify that the retailer serves that departure. Look again if the airline, date, flight number or terminal changes.
2. **Check the last order time.** Use the retailer's current deadline for that item and flight, then read the cancellation and missed-pickup terms. Avoid an order that leaves little time for pickup.
3. **Check the destination and connections.** Duty-free purchase in Korea does not determine entry allowances abroad. Check the customs authority where you enter and your airline's instructions, particularly if you will pass through transfer security with liquids. If you are entering Korea again, consult [Korea Customs' traveler allowances](https://customs.go.kr/english/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=2743&mi=8069) for that arrival.

## Collect the order before boarding

- **Save the pickup instructions offline.** Save the voucher and exact retailer, terminal, floor and counter. Leave time after departure formalities to get from there to your gate.
- **Bring the original documents.** In its [official product and pickup instructions](https://eng.lottedfs.com/kr/product/productDetail?prdNo=20000863052), Lotte asks for the physical passport, exchange voucher and boarding pass. Passenger and departure details must match the order. Follow the instructions for your own retailer.
- **Check the package at handover.** Check the item, quantity and name before leaving the counter. For liquids, keep the receipt and any required sealed bag and verify transfer security rules before you open it.
- **If the order is missing or your flight changed, contact the retailer.** Ask whether the order can be moved, cancelled or refunded under your terms. Confirm before heading toward a counter in another terminal.

A rushed departure is a reason to skip a pre-order. Finish the [packing and souvenir checks](/blog/packing-korean-shopping-liquids-and-souvenirs-for-your-flight) and leave time for your [airport transfer](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train).
