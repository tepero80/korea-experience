**Do not travel to an ice festival based only on a general event listing. Check the organizer's own notice for the exact date, activity and transport before leaving.** The Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) notes that when ice is too thin to meet safety standards, an ice festival may delay opening or offer fewer activities. A festival's advertised season does not guarantee that ice fishing, skating or another ice activity is operating today.

[VISITKOREA lists January 9–31, 2027](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=96732) for Hwacheon. Its activity details still refer to 2026. Check the current organizer’s notice for 2027 hours, tickets and any changes before booking your travel.

## Check four things on the day

1. **Find the exact date and year.** Open the festival's official website and check its latest notice or status post. Some pages continue to show last year's details while the next season is being updated.
2. **Confirm the specific activity.** Check whether the event site is open today and whether the activity you want is operating, delayed, restricted or unavailable. Do not walk onto ice or enter a closed area unless the organizer has opened that activity and staff permit it.
3. **Check ticket and reservation conditions.** Confirm whether tickets are sold online or on site for your chosen activity, whether a reservation is still available, and what the organizer says about cancellations or refunds if conditions change.
4. **Recheck the journey.** Confirm the day's shuttle or bus information and the return connection. If opening is delayed or canceled, know whether you can switch to a nearby indoor stop or return earlier.

| What the listing says | What to verify with the organizer |
| --- | --- |
| “Winter festival” or a month-long season | Whether the festival grounds open on your exact visit date and hours |
| Ice fishing, sledding or skating listed | Whether that individual activity is open under today's ice and weather conditions |
| Online tickets or tours appear in search | Whether the official booking page accepts your date and what the ticket covers |
| A transport route is shown | Today's shuttle departure, pickup point, return schedule and disruption notice |

KTO's [2026–2027 winter festival overview](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=1595020) explains that ice thickness can affect opening and activities and says to check the festival's official website or social channel for updates. The [Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival organizer](https://www.narafestival.com/01_icenara/) publishes its own schedules, activity pages and visitor-safety information. The [Pyeongchang Trout Festival organizer](https://festival700.or.kr/) has separate program, booking and notice sections; its currently visible season may be an earlier edition, so confirm the year and current announcement instead of copying a date from a search result.

## If the status is unclear

Contact the organizer using the number or inquiry route on its official page. If you cannot confirm that the activity is open, do not make a long journey on the assumption it will operate. For general visitor-information or interpretation help, the [1330 Korea Travel Hotline](/blog/1330-travel-helpline-8-languages-2026) may help you find information; it does not replace the organizer's live operating decision.

Pack waterproof shoes with traction and warm layers for a long outdoor visit, but gear does not make closed ice safe. The [winter festival guide](/blog/winter-activities-ski-resorts-and-ice-festivals) can help compare event types; this checklist is for the final go/no-go check.

Hwacheon’s published date range was checked November 24, 2026. Event schedules, reservations and ice conditions can change. Check the organizer’s notices for current access and operation.
