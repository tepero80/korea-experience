## Book a visit with the person who would operate

Ask the clinic for the surgeon’s name, where the procedure would take place and whether this booking includes a consultation with that surgeon. Korea describes its separate systems for registration and accreditation on the [Medical Korea registration page](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem). Match the clinic’s name and address to the listing, then ask the person who would operate about their qualifications and experience with cases like yours. The [NHS guidance on choosing a cosmetic practitioner](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/advice/choosing-who-will-do-your-procedure/) recommends meeting the person who will do the procedure before making a decision.

Bring a short history of nasal injuries, previous procedures or symptoms such as difficulty breathing. Ask what the clinician would assess before discussing a plan. The [NHS rhinoplasty guide](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/cosmetic-procedures/cosmetic-surgery/nose-reshaping-rhinoplasty/) describes breathing changes, changes in smell and bleeding among the possible complications. Ask the surgeon which risks apply to the plan they are proposing and how the team would handle them.

| Topic | Ask before paying |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | What would you aim to change? Would you assess my breathing before making a recommendation? |
| Choice | What are the alternatives, including waiting? What can this approach reasonably achieve? |
| Quote | Can I see separate charges for the surgeon, facility, anaesthesia, tests, interpretation and follow-up? |
| Care | What would I need to pay or arrange if the plan changes or I need an extra visit? Who would see me? |

## Compare the offers on the same terms

Get the provider’s name, the proposed procedure, an itemized estimate and the deposit and cancellation terms in writing. Ask which charges could change after an examination. If you are offered a different procedure on the day, ask for a fresh explanation and estimate before making a decision.

Tell the clinician when and how you plan to return home. Ask which appointments need to happen in Korea, what would change your departure plan and who would handle a review once you are home. Use the [return travel and airline paperwork guide](/blog/flying-after-surgery-when-is-it-safe-to-head-home-doctor-advice-2026) to check the travel details separately. If you are unsure how to explain a specific feature you want to retain or change, use the [guide to describing your preferences](/blog/ethnic-rhinoplasty-preserving-your-features-in-korea) before the consultation.
