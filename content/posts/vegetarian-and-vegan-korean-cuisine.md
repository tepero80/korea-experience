## Ask about ingredients you cannot see

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s vegetarian ordering guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=476&vcontsId=140732) points out that broth, gimbap fillings and kimchi seasoning can contain ingredients a visitor might miss. Start by explaining which ingredients you avoid. Ask whether a relevant component is prepared in advance and which part, if any, the kitchen can change.

| Menu type | Ask about |
| --- | --- |
| Soup or noodles | Meat or anchovy broth and seasonings already mixed in. |
| Gimbap | Ham, imitation crab and egg. Ask whether the fillings are prepared separately. |
| Bibimbap | Meat, egg and what is mixed into the sauce. |
| Side dishes | Fish sauce and salted seafood in kimchi. Check individual side dishes separately. |

## Show the server your version of the request

Start with **고기나 생선이 들어가나요? 육수에도 들어가나요?** (“Does it contain meat or fish? Is either used in the broth?”). If you are vegan, add **계란, 우유, 버터도 먹지 않아요.** (“I also do not eat eggs, milk or butter.”). Mention seafood and any other ingredients you avoid. Ask which dish the kitchen can serve with those requirements.

Save a clearly described fallback menu near where you plan to eat. If you want temple cuisine as a separate outing, follow the [temple food experience guide](/blog/korean-temple-food-dining-experience-guide-2026) to check what is offered and how to visit.
