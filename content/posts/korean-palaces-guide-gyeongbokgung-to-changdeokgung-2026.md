**Choose Gyeongbokgung for the main court and the guard ceremony. Choose Changdeokgung for grounds shaped around the hills, and book a timed Secret Garden ticket if the garden is the reason for your visit.** On a regular Monday, start with Gyeongbokgung. On a regular Tuesday, start with Changdeokgung. Check holiday exceptions for your exact date.

## Choose one palace or plan both

Standard fees below are per adult aged 19–64. They cover daytime entry and were checked on November 23, 2026. Read the palace notices before setting out.

| Choose for | Palace and tickets |
| --- | --- |
| **Main court** | **Gyeongbokgung** usually closes Tuesday. ₩3,000 for the palace. Check the guard ceremony at Gwanghwamun. |
| **Garden** | **Changdeokgung** usually closes Monday. ₩3,000 for the palace buildings. The Secret Garden adds ₩5,000 and has a separate timed entry. |

Both palaces can change their closing day around a public holiday. On a day when both are open, choose the garden time first. It is easier to move a daytime Gyeongbokgung visit around a fixed booking than to rush through Changdeokgung to make the garden gate on time.

## Gyeongbokgung: visit the palace and see the guard

Start at **Gwanghwamun (광화문)**, the main gate at the south end. For a short visit, walk from the gate through the main court toward Geunjeongjeon. Add the view of Gyeonghoeru beside the pond if you have time and that area is open. Keep a rest stop in reserve if the courtyards are cold or windy.

- **Palace hours.** The [official Gyeongbokgung admission page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E702000000.do) lists 09:00–17:00 in November–February, with last admission at 16:00. Check the seasonal table for other months.
- **Guard change.** The [Korea Heritage Agency ceremony schedule](https://www.kh.or.kr/cms/content/view/824) lists the main ceremony at 10:00 and 14:00. It lists shorter guard-duty performances at separate times. Treat a performance as conditional and check current notices, especially in bad weather.
- **Hanbok.** Eligible clothing can waive ordinary palace admission under the [official hanbok guidelines](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E701000000.do). Confirm how the rule applies to your visit and leave time to return rented clothing. Admission to a timed or special program can have separate rules.

## Changdeokgung: book the Secret Garden separately

Start at **Geumhomun (금호문)** on the west side while Donhwamun is being repaired. The [official restoration notice](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R402000000.do?id=20241125093054576568&schM=view) and the [current Secret Garden instructions](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R601000000.do?pageType=more&resIdx=202401090246&schGroupCode=cdg&schM=view) identify that temporary entrance. Check the gate instructions again before you go. The palace address and the garden checkpoint are different navigation targets.

<GuidePhoto id="changdeok-garden-christian-bolz-2017" caption="A pond and pavilions in Changdeokgung’s Secret Garden (Huwon), photographed in 2017. The garden is a separate timed visit inside the palace." />

1. **Check the date and session.** Open the [Royal Palaces reservation listing](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R601000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg), choose **후원관람예약** (Secret Garden reservation) and follow its booking link. Advance sales open at **10:00 Korea time six days before the visit** and close the day before. Put the booking time in your calendar if the garden is central to your plan.
2. **Allow for two admissions.** The normal total for an adult aged 19–64 is **₩8,000**. The palace building area is ₩3,000 and the garden is ₩5,000. Check the [official Changdeokgung admission page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E702000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg&schGroupCodeNm=Changdeokgung) for exemptions, discounts and seasonal hours. A palace admission exemption does not automatically cover the garden.
3. **Get to the inner gate on time.** The [palace FAQ](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R404000000.do?schGroupCode=cdg) recommends entering the palace at least 20 minutes before your garden time. Allow more for the ticket queue and the walk inside. The published instructions say that a late arrival is ineligible for admission and a refund. Guided tours are optional under the current admission instructions. Confirm the language and entry time offered for your session if you want a guide.
4. **Have a same-day fallback.** The palace lists separate walk-up garden tickets, sold from 09:00 while places last. If the garden is sold out, visit the palace buildings on their own or use the time for another stop. Avoid counting on a later garden entry unless you obtain that ticket.

The [Royal Palaces heritage guide](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E102030000.do) explains why the buildings and garden follow the terrain. If steep paths or steps are a concern, ask the palace about the current route before buying the garden ticket and choose the palace buildings or a shorter visit if needed.

## Fit the visit into a day in Jongno

- **One free morning.** Pick one palace, one feature and time for the walk back to transit. Anguk Station on Line 3 is a practical starting point for Changdeokgung. Use the temporary gate instructions instead of assuming the shortest map route ends at the visitor entrance.
- **A full day with both.** Put the garden booking and its arrival buffer in your plan first. On the walk between palaces, allow time for lunch. Bukchon includes a residential [restricted visiting zone](https://world.seoul.go.kr/notice-on-restrictions-for-tourist-visits-to-bukchon-special-management-area-effective-from-november-2024/) that visitors can enter only from 10:00 to 17:00. Stay on a main route if you are early, late or short on time.
- **An evening or indoor alternative.** Use the [palace night guide](/blog/palace-starlight-night-tours-2026) to check whether a separate evening program has confirmed dates. If the weather makes a long outdoor visit difficult, choose an [indoor Seoul route](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets). To put one palace into a longer trip, start with the [three-day Seoul itinerary](/blog/seoul-3-day-itinerary-perfect-first-time-visitor-guide-2026).
