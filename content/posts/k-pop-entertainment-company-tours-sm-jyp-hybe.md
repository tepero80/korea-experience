**Pick the public experience you want.** HiKR Ground is a visitor center with K-pop sets. K-POP SQUARE in Hongdae is a retail stop with merchandise and temporary events. Treat agency offices as workplaces and visit only facilities with announced public admission.

| What you want | Check this first |
| --- | --- |
| Film a short clip on a themed set | HiKR Ground. Check the experience floor’s opening and any temporary program rules. |
| Buy music merchandise or attend a pop-up | K-POP SQUARE HONGDAE. Check the exact event, branch, dates and entry method. |
| Watch a live performance | Choose a public lineup or a ticketed event. Match the venue and entry instructions on the official listing. |

## Central Seoul · HiKR Ground

The [Korea Tourism Organization's official page](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/hikr) introduces its K-pop music-video sets and lounge. Check the floor you plan to visit: the experience floors and the lounge follow different access rules. The [city’s stream-walk guide](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2019588) pairs the area around Gwangtonggyo with HiKR Ground. Use the street-level address to find the door and follow each room's current filming instructions.

## Hongdae · K-POP SQUARE

[The operator's store directory](https://linefriendssquare.com/pages/about-us) and [VISITKOREA's listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=199935) identify the Hongdae shop. Look for a dated announcement for any specific artist, merchandise drop or event. A store's posted hours do not establish pop-up access, admission by reservation or stock. If your intended event has ended, use the [Hongdae neighborhood guide](/blog/hongdae-shopping-street-indie-brands-and-vintage) for a flexible alternative.

Check a public activity before committing a transfer, especially when you have a concert later. Keep sidewalks and working entrances clear. An agency address does not provide a public tour, and a fan event does not guarantee an artist appearance.
