Jangsaengpo Whale Museum offers an indoor introduction to whales and the history of a former whaling port. Its displays include a whale skeleton, whaling tools and other artifacts. This is a history-and-ecology visit: the museum is a distinct attraction from the nearby Whale Life Experience Center and recreated village. [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=85947)

## What to see

| Venue | What to plan for |
| --- | --- |
| Whale Museum | Indoor exhibits on whales and the former whaling port. Confirm the museum ticket. |
| Whale Culture Village | A reconstructed settlement with its own entry and schedule. |
| Whale Life Experience Center | A separate venue with different exhibits. Check its details only if you plan to go. |


Start with the museum exhibits to understand the port's past and the animals associated with it. The [Nam-gu district government](https://www.ulsannamgu.go.kr/eng/contents/visiting/whale_tourism.do) says the museum collects and preserves whaling artifacts following the 1986 prohibition of whaling. The nearby reconstructed village offers a separate way to explore local history; check its own ticket and schedule if you want to add it. A visit to the museum does not require a visit to an animal exhibit or a boat trip.

## Hours and tickets: check before leaving

The official listings do not fully agree on opening time or holiday exceptions. They describe Monday and major-holiday closures, but exceptions vary. Confirm the actual date, current fee and any age or access rules with the [operator](https://www.whalecity.kr) or call +82-52-256-6301 before travelling, particularly after a public holiday.

## Getting there and planning your stay

Search **장생포 고래박물관**, 244 Jangsaengpogorae-ro, Nam-gu (울산광역시 남구 장생포고래로 244). Check a current Korean map for bus routes from your actual arrival station; Ulsan Station and Taehwagang Station are different places. Allow a transfer rather than assuming the museum is beside either station. For an overnight trip, use the [city accommodation directory](https://tour.ulsan.go.kr/tour/kor/unit/acmdtn/list.ulsan?mId=001002010000000000) to compare Nam-gu properties and confirm the location and booking terms directly.

For a day with outdoor time, pair the museum with Taehwagang National Garden, leaving room for the transfer. If weather makes a coastal walk difficult, the museum is an indoor option, subject to its own opening schedule. Contact the museum for current step-free access, lifts and exhibit information before relying on a particular route.
