**Start with Jeju City for an airport-first trip or several bus day trips.** Choose old downtown near Dongmun if you want meals and evening walks close to your hotel, or look near Jeju Bus Terminal if several day trips start there. Choose **central Seogwipo** for a south-coast stay and **Jungmun** if your plans focus on a particular resort or ICC Jeju. Compare the hotel’s real walk and evening return before booking.

**For:** travelers using buses, with a taxi as a fallback. **Information checked:** 23 November 2026. The best base depends on your exact hotel, destinations and dates.

## Pick a base for the journeys you will repeat

| Base | A good fit when… | What to check before booking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Old downtown, Jeju City** | You want dinner near Dongmun Market (동문재래시장) and a walk around Sanjicheon or Tapdong. | Map the hotel to its closest usable stop and to **Jeju Bus Terminal**. Dongmun Market, the terminal and the airport are separate stops. |
| **Jeju Bus Terminal area** | Your plans use several departures from the terminal, such as the eastbound services to Seongsan. | Compare staying near the departure bay with a hotel near your preferred dinner area. Check both walks after dark and the route back from your last planned stop. |
| **Central Seogwipo** | You want the Maeil Olle Market area and several south-side outings. | Compare the hotel with the central market, the Registry Office transfer stop and **Seogwipo Bus Terminal** on a map. Use the stop your actual route lists. |
| **Jungmun resort area** | You are attending an event at **ICC Jeju** or want most activities within one resort area. | Check the walk between the bus stop, the hotel lobby and dinner. For a day out, confirm a usable evening service back to the same part of Jungmun. |

[Visit Jeju’s Dongmun listing](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500745&menuId=DOM_000001820000000002) describes its connection to Sanjicheon and Tapdong. The [official Maeil Olle Market listing](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500731&menuId=DOM_700000000010774) places it in central Seogwipo. The [ICC Jeju listing](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500539) locates the venue in Jungmun. The [provincial bus network guide](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/publicTrafficInformation/generalBusSchedule) identifies the terminals and route patterns.

## Test the hotel on a map before you pay

1. **Pin the lobby.** Copy the hotel’s Korean road address from your reservation. Inspect the walk from the stop on the side where you would arrive, including road crossings and any hill or stairs.
2. **Test a real sightseeing day.** Choose the outing you most want to do. In the [official Jeju bus timetables](https://bus.jeju.go.kr/mobile/schedule/listSchedule), check your boarding stop and direction on the way out and the return stop at the time you expect to leave. Save a backup departure and the last leg to the hotel.
3. **Check the evening.** Pick a dinner option near the hotel or a verified route home. If your recurring route needs a taxi at both ends, compare the total cost and effort with a hotel nearer the right stop.
4. **Verify your access needs.** If you need a step-free room or vehicle, ask the hotel about the actual lobby and room route and ask the operator about access on your planned bus. Include the way back in that check.

## Decide whether to move hotels

- **A short trip:** Keep one base if it gives you the routes and evenings you want. Build each day around one part of the island, then return to the same hotel.
- **Several days split between north and south:** Consider two bases if you would otherwise make the same cross-island journey on consecutive days. Check bag drop, check-in and the one-way move before committing.
- **Sunrise or repeated plans in the east:** Compare a night near your first stop in Seongsan with leaving Jeju City very early. Check the actual early service or arrange a taxi with your lodging if the timing matters. Use the [Seongsan bus day plan](/blog/seongsan-ilchulbong-by-bus-from-jeju-city) to test the day and the return.

Once you have an address, use the [Jeju Airport transfer guide](/blog/jeju-airport-to-jeju-city-and-seogwipo) to match the arrival bus to your hotel. Keep the [Jeju destination collection](/destinations/jeju) handy for dining, ferry and day-trip planning.
