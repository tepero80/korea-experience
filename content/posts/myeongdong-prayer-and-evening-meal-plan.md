**Call Saffron before you build a Myeongdong evening around dinner and prayer.** The restaurant is on floor 2 of **LOTTE CITY HOTEL MYEONGDONG**, **서울특별시 중구 삼일대로 362**. The hotel lists it as a Middle Eastern halal restaurant. A Korea Tourism Organization feature reports a prayer room. Ask the restaurant about access and the meal details you need on your date. [Hotel’s Saffron listing](https://app.lottehotel.com/myeongdong-city/en/dining/restaurant-saffron) · [Korea Tourism Organization feature](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/MuslimFriendly/alamArabiKoreaView.do?dataSetId=243&menuSn=983&vcontsId=228708)

## One call before you go

| If this matters | Ask the restaurant |
| --- | --- |
| Dinner at a set time | Is the kitchen open on my date? Can I book a table for this time? |
| Prayer | Is there a prayer space available to diners at my visit time? Do I need to ask staff for access? |
| Iftar | Can I be seated and served at the time I give you? Do I need to pre-order? |
| Dietary standard | Can you confirm the ingredients and preparation for the dishes I plan to order? Can I see the current certificate if I require one? |
| Access | Which hotel entrance and lift should I use, and what is the route to the prayer space? |

The hotel posts **11:30–22:30**, with **last orders at 21:30** and a closure every other Sunday. Call the number on the hotel’s current dining page, [+82 2 6112 1054](tel:+82261121054), to confirm which dates are affected. Set prayer time separately using a source you follow for Seoul and your specific date. Agree on an arrival time with your group before you keep shopping. [Hotel’s dining hours and reservation contact](https://app.lottehotel.com/myeongdong-city/en/dining/restaurant-saffron)

## Save the right hotel and a backup

Search **롯데시티호텔 명동** or paste **중구 삼일대로 362**. The hotel’s directions list Euljiro 3-ga and Myeongdong stations. Check the route from where you actually are. In a group, send the full hotel name, street address and **2층** (second floor) before people split up. [Hotel directions and Korean road address](https://app.lottehotel.com/myeongdong-city/ko/about/location)

If plans change, decide whether your meal and prayer arrangements can be separate. Ask your accommodation about a space you can use, and use the [Korea Tourism Organization’s Muslim-friendly travel directory](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/thingsToDo/subMuslimFriendly.do) to research a backup meal. The directory uses different labels. “Muslim Friendly” can mean some halal dishes are available and alcohol may be sold. Check the individual menu and preparation against your requirements. [Korea Tourism Organization’s category definitions](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/thingsToDo/subMuslimFriendly.do)

Keep water and a snack that suits your requirements for an evening with uncertain timing. For another day or a separate dinner district, [see Seoul dining and grocery options for Muslim travelers](/blog/finding-halal-food-in-seoul-top-restaurants-and-supermarkets-2026). Confirm the individual venue before setting out.
