**Pick the scene you care about before crossing Seoul.** Seoul Book Bogo in Songpa is an indoor *Hotel del Luna* stop. The *Business Proposal* umbrella scene is on the street by the Seoul Museum of Craft Art in Anguk. Each works as a short visit in its own neighborhood.

| Scene | Practical starting point | What could change your plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Hotel del Luna*, episode 2 | Line 2 Jamsillaru Exit 1 → Seoul Book Bogo | Opening hours, exhibition layout and the current photography policy. |
| *Business Proposal*, yellow umbrella scene | Line 3 Anguk Exit 1 → Seoul Museum of Craft Art frontage | Foot traffic, weather and museum hours if you want to go inside. |

If you have a separate day, [compare a Suwon filming walk, a Gangneung coast stop and a ticketed Yongin visit](/blog/k-drama-filming-locations-you-can-actually-visit).

If you have space for more than filming sites, [choose a Korean Wave stop around film, a performance or food](/blog/hallyu-4-0-what-next-for-the-global-korean-wave-2026).

## Songpa · Seoul Book Bogo for Hotel del Luna

[Visit Seoul's scene guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/hallyu/Retro-Seoul-in-Hotel-del-Luna/31701) identifies the book venue used in episode 2. [Seoul City](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2019405) gives the address and Line 2 Jamsillaru approach. [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/HallyuNew/contentsView.do?dataSetId=76&vcontsId=230934) records its 2025 reopening after renovations. Treat the historic reference as a way to identify the location. Check today's program and hours before leaving, and follow signs about photos inside.

**Save:** 서울책보고 · 서울 송파구 오금로 1. ## Anguk · The Business Proposal umbrella street

[Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/hallyu/the-drama-that-stimulates-your-love-cells/ENN041167) identifies the street at the Seoul Museum of Craft Art as the scene location. Use [the museum listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/PalaceArea/Seoul-Museum-of-Craft_/38147) for Anguk Exit 1 and the pinned frontage. Keep moving if pedestrians need the sidewalk, or continue to [Unhyeongung](/blog/unhyeongung-anguk-traditional-house-visit) if the frontage is crowded.

**Save:** 서울공예박물관 · 서울 종로구 율곡로3길 4. If you have only a few hours, add one nearby stop to your chosen neighborhood. In the rain, check museum or indoor venue hours before relying on it. A different exhibition or street appearance is normal when years have passed since filming.
