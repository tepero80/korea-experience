Namsangol Hanok Village offers a practical introduction to traditional Korean houses without leaving central Seoul. Admission is free, while individual activities may have their own charges and booking conditions. This is largely an outdoor visit: keep an indoor alternative for rain or very cold weather.

| Topic | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Traditional houses | April–October 09:00–21:00; November–March 09:00–20:00, Tuesday–Sunday |
| Mondays | Normally closed; Seoul’s recent city guidance says the houses open when Monday is a public holiday, including substitute holidays |
| Traditional garden | City information lists it as open 24 hours; this is separate from house opening |
| Admission | Free; activity charges may apply |
| Address | 28 Toegye-ro 34-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul / 서울특별시 중구 퇴계로34길 28 |
| Subway | Chungmuro Station, lines 3 and 4, exits 3 or 4 |

See [Seoul City’s village introduction](https://news.seoul.go.kr/culture/archives/66) and [recent city visitor information](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2019415). The houses on the site were moved and restored from locations elsewhere in Seoul; they are not presented here as houses continuously lived in at this location.

## A simple family plan

1. Save **남산골한옥마을** and its Korean address in your map app. Check the route from the station for your group’s needs.
2. Enter during the traditional houses’ opening hours and let children look for differences between the buildings and courtyard spaces. Respect barriers and notices; do not assume every room is open to enter.
3. Check the village’s current activity calendar for a craft or performance. Confirm the date, minimum age, language, cost and reservation method for that particular session; past festival activities are not a promise that an activity will run on your date.
4. If weather deteriorates, switch to an indoor choice using the [Seoul rainy-day itinerary](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets).

## What to verify with a stroller

The grounds include outdoor paths. We have not independently verified a fully step-free route, accessible toilets, or stroller access inside individual houses. Contact the venue or check its latest accessibility information before making this the centerpiece of a family day.


Check the [village website](https://www.hanokmaeul.co.kr/) for notices before your visit. Opening, activity availability and holiday exceptions may change.
