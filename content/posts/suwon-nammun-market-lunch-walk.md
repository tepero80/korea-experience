**The Nammun market area is several markets around Paldalmun Gate, so pick one lunch stop before adding a second.** Start at Jidong for a market meal if its food suits you. Motgol is a better choice for browsing prepared foods and choosing a smaller bite. If visiting Hwaseong Haenggung is your priority, check its last admission before spending time at the markets.

## Which market should you put in your map?

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s Nammun listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=977&vcontsId=1590054) groups nine markets under Nammun. The city’s [market register](https://www.suwon.go.kr/sw-www/deptHome/dep_eco/eco02/eco02_01/eco02-01_08.jsp) lists individual names and addresses. Search for the specific stop before leaving the gate or palace.

| What you want | Map name | What to check when you arrive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orient yourself by the fortress’s south gate | **팔달문** · Paldalmun | Use it as a landmark. Follow marked pedestrian crossings to the side of the road you need. |
| A sit-down market meal | **지동시장** · Jidong Market, 수원시 팔달구 팔달문로 19 | Jidong is known for sundae and other pork dishes. Check the menu, serving size and price before sitting down. |
| Browse prepared food, rice cakes and snacks | **못골종합시장** · Motgol Market, 수원시 팔달구 수원천로258번길 10-12 | Some stalls primarily sell take-home food. Confirm what you can buy in a small portion and where you can eat it. |
| Browse hanbok and textiles instead of adding another meal | **영동시장** · Yeongdong Market | Treat the textile aisles as a separate stop. The [KTO market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=70886) describes the market’s shops and location near the gate. |

## A lunch walk you can shorten

1. **Choose your first stop at Paldalmun.** Use the street-level pavements and crossings around the gate. If coming from the palace, navigate to the market you actually want rather than entering the generic Nammun name again.
2. **Eat or browse at Jidong.** If you want a sit-down meal, ask what the quoted price covers. For dietary restrictions, show a written Korean description and ask about ingredients and shared preparation. Move on when the seller cannot confirm what you need.
3. **Continue to Motgol only if you have time.** Start with one aisle or buy a snack to carry with you. The [city’s English market map](https://www.suwon.go.kr/webcontent/ckeditor/2019/1/28/22c61a9a-2220-4abc-a683-ea4bd025f901.pdf) shows the markets around Suwoncheon. Use your map’s current pedestrian route rather than assuming a riverside ramp, underpass or crossing is usable.
4. **Finish with your next booked or time-sensitive stop.** Leave daylight and walk time for the [palace and fortress route](/blog/suwon-hwaseong-fortress-a-deep-dive-into-king-jeongjo-legacy-2026). If conditions make the walls impractical, check whether [Hwaseong Museum](/blog/suwon-hwaseong-museum-before-fortress) is open before going there.

Allow about **an hour for one market meal**, or **up to 90 minutes for lunch and a short Motgol browse**, as editorial planning allowances. Add the walk from the palace, any queue and the return leg separately.

## If your first choice doesn’t work

- **The stalls are quiet or closing:** use the city or tourism listing for the market contact and a current map listing for your chosen shop. Check business hours and closures by stall, especially on holidays. Keep an open sit-down alternative on the way back.
- **A narrow or wet lane is difficult:** stay with one confirmed entrance and the street-level approach. Ask the venue about threshold height, seating and toilets before relying on them. The market map locates the area but does not establish step-free access.
- **You have little time after the palace:** pick either Jidong or Motgol and save the other market for a later visit. Put the exact stop and return station on your [day-by-day trip board](/tools/itinerary) before leaving the palace area.
