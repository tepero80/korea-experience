Start with the actor's agency or official account and follow its link to the named ticket seller. A fan meeting announcement should identify the event, venue, sale dates and ticket terms. Fan club membership is not a ticket and does not guarantee a seat or a personal interaction.

## Before the sale

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Event and seller | Match the venue and date to the official announcement. |
| Presale eligibility | Confirm whether membership, registration and a matching account are required. |
| Identity and payment | Check the accepted documents, name rules and supported payment methods. |
| Collection and entry | Read the event-specific ID, ticket pickup and accessibility instructions. |

The [official March 2026 Xiumin fan meeting notice](https://weverse.io/xiumin/notice/34012) names its ticket seller, requires a valid ID and specifies mobile tickets only. This past event illustrates why you should inspect the organizer’s actual instructions before paying. Your own event may instead use on-site pickup. Check which ID is accepted for a visitor, whether the attendee’s name needs to match, and whether any verification must be completed before purchase. Keep the confirmation available and read the event’s age, cancellation and photography terms.

Only book nonrefundable travel after deciding what happens if you cannot obtain a ticket. For more on ticket accounts and sale preparation, see [K-pop concert ticketing](/blog/how-to-get-k-pop-concert-tickets-ticketing-guide).
