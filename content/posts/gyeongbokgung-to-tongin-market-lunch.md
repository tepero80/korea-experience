**Plan Gyeongbokgung in the morning, then walk west to Tongin Market for an early lunch.** Wednesday through Saturday is the simplest window to plan around. If the coin lunchbox matters, verify the café's hours on the day. If it is shut, buy lunch directly from an open stall or stop at a Seochon restaurant instead.

## Start with the palace, then head west

Use **Gyeongbokgung (경복궁)**, 서울 종로구 사직로 161, for the palace. The Royal Palaces and Tombs Center lists Tuesday as its normal closure and posts holiday exceptions on the [palace hours page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R702000000.do). Decide which courtyards you most want to see before you enter so an extended palace visit leaves room for lunch.

If the palace's west gate, **Yeongchumun (영추문)**, is open for exit, use it to reach Seochon. The [Korea Tourism Organization's Seochon guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=194514) identifies this as the western gate. If staff direct you out another way, leave by Gwanghwamun and use Gyeongbokgung Station Exit 2 as a street-level waypoint. From Exit 2, head north along Jahamun-ro toward the market. Save **Tongin Market (통인시장)**, 서울 종로구 자하문로15길 18, in your [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%ED%86%B5%EC%9D%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9E%90%ED%95%98%EB%AC%B8%EB%A1%9C15%EA%B8%B8%2018) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%ED%86%B5%EC%9D%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9E%90%ED%95%98%EB%AC%B8%EB%A1%9C15%EA%B8%B8%2018) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%ED%86%B5%EC%9D%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9E%90%ED%95%98%EB%AC%B8%EB%A1%9C15%EA%B8%B8%2018)). The [KTO walking guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/contents/columns/Explore%20the%20Cheong%20Wa%20Dae%20Area.pdf) places both the market and Sejong Village Food Street on the Exit 2 side.

| If your priority is | Use this plan |
| --- | --- |
| **Both the palace and coin lunch** | Aim for Wednesday through Saturday and set a lunch alarm. Check the coin counter's last sale before leaving the palace. |
| **Food whenever you finish the palace** | Use open stalls or a restaurant near Exit 2. Ask each vendor about service and payment. |
| **Tuesday, a holiday, or the third Sunday** | Check both venues separately. If the palace is closed, save the paired route for another day. |

## Use the lunchbox system

Find **도시락카페 통** (Lunchbox Café Tong) in the market's customer center. Ask for the current coin price, final exchange time and where to sit. Take the container to stalls marked as participating, then return to the café for seating if available. Decide how much food you want before exchanging coins. Seoul's [visitor listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/Tongin-Market/ENP000281) and [city feature on the café](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2014553) give conflicting weekday schedules, including which day the café closes. The city feature lists a separate coin-sale cutoff. If the café is the reason for your trip, ask the market at **+82 50-71378-0940**, the contact listed by Visit Seoul.

If you want to check whether coins are still available, show **지금 엽전 살 수 있어요?** (“Can I buy coins now?”). If you are paying at a stall instead, show **카드 돼요?** (“Can I pay by card?”). Plan a sit-down meal on Sejong Village Food Street if café seating is unavailable or your group needs more time. Once lunch is settled, use the [Seoul three-day plan](/blog/seoul-3-day-itinerary-perfect-first-time-visitor-guide-2026) to decide whether to continue across Jongno or keep the afternoon on the west side.
