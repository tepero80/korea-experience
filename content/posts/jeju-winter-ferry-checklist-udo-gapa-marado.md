**For:** visitors planning a ferry from Jeju to Udo, Gapado or Marado, or a longer sailing between Jeju and the mainland. **Checked:** 15 November 2026. This is a check-before-you-go guide, not a live sailing notice or timetable.

## Check the exact route, not only the island’s weather

Jeju can be clear while a particular sailing is delayed, under review or canceled. Check the port, route, direction and date shown on your own ticket. The Korea Maritime Transportation Safety Authority (KOMSA) provides a [current passenger-vessel operation lookup](https://www.komsa.or.kr/prog/pssgCurState/kor/sub03_0202/list.do?jbcd=G) and a [next-day sailing forecast](https://www.komsa.or.kr/kor/sub03_0207.do). The forecast covers only some routes and is an estimate: fog, sudden weather changes or vessel conditions may change the plan. KOMSA says to reconfirm the actual sailing on the day.

For a local Jeju trip to Udo, the official tourism page for [Seongsan Port Passenger Terminal](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CNTS_300000000014703) describes the terminal and says visitors buy a round-trip ticket, complete a boarding form and present ID. It also says the ferry can arrive at Cheonjin Port or Haumokdong Port, and advises checking the sailing on the day because times and arrival ports can vary with weather and vessel operations. Confirm current boarding requirements and the correct terminal with the operator before setting out. For Gapado, Marado or a Jeju–mainland trip, use KOMSA’s current-operation lookup to find the relevant route and status; do not use one route’s notice as confirmation for another.

| What the status means | What to do next |
| --- | --- |
| A next-day forecast says **operate** | Treat it as a forecast. Recheck the operator or current operation lookup before leaving. |
| It says **under review** or gives mixed services | Do not treat the trip as confirmed. Ask the operator when the decision will be made. |
| The sailing is canceled or suspended | Ask the operator about available rebooking, refund terms and the next operating option. Terms depend on the ticket and carrier. |
| The return sailing is uncertain | Contact your accommodation and onward carrier early; do not assume a same-day return or an extra room is available. |

## Before you leave your hotel

1. Open the official status page and choose the route and direction printed on your booking. Check the departure port as well as the island name.
2. Confirm the operator’s latest message for that sailing. If a forecast and the operator’s notice differ, ask the operator; do not infer that a ferry is operating from a general weather forecast.
3. Check how to reach the port and whether that ground route is running. Leave time for the correct terminal and any walk between a bus stop, ticket desk and boarding point.
4. Save the ticket, operator contact and accommodation contact offline. If your travel party needs language help, ask your accommodation or an official visitor information desk to help contact the carrier.
5. Recheck the return sailing before starting an island activity. Keep a mainland or Jeju-based alternative that does not depend on making one unconfirmed departure.

For Udo specifically, VISITJEJU says travelers buy a round-trip ticket, complete a boarding form and present ID at Seongsan Port. Its page says ferries may arrive at Cheonjin or Haumokdong Port; confirm the current arrival port because it can vary with weather and vessel operations.

For longer Jeju–mainland routes, a missed ferry may affect a separate train, flight or hotel booking. Those bookings have their own change terms. Contact each provider before buying a replacement; a ferry cancellation does not automatically change an unrelated reservation or guarantee reimbursement. Keep the carrier’s written notice and your actual receipts if you later need to ask a provider or insurer about a claim.

## If the sailing changes

Use the ferry operator’s instructions first. Do not travel to another port or buy a second ticket until you confirm that the replacement route is operating, accepts your booking and leaves enough time to reach it. Ask what happens to the original ticket and whether the operator can document the cancellation. KOMSA also provides a [sailing or cancellation certificate search](https://www.komsa.or.kr/prog/crtfctSailing/kor/sub03_0206/list.do?searchJbcd=G); the available certificate depends on the recorded operation history.

If a ferry is confirmed canceled, re-plan the island day around what you can still reach safely from your current base. For a Jeju stay, see our [Jeju island planning guide](/blog/jeju-island-complete-travel-guide-best-time-to-visit-2026). If your itinerary also includes a flight, check the separate [Incheon winter flight disruption checklist](/blog/incheon-winter-flight-disruption-checklist); ferry and airline decisions come from different operators.

### Quick checklist

- Exact route, port, direction and travel date match the booking.
- The operator’s current notice has been checked, not only tomorrow’s forecast.
- Both outbound and return plans have a confirmed status or a realistic alternative.
- Ground transport to the port and separate onward bookings have their own checks.
