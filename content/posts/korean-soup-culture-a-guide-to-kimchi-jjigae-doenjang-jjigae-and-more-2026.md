## Identify the base and the extra ingredients

| Menu name | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| 김치찌개 · kimchi jjigae | [KTO’s examples](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140728) include pork, tuna and ham. Look for an ingredient in front of the name, such as 참치 (tuna). |
| 된장찌개 · doenjang jjigae | The [soybean paste stew](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178309) may include tofu, vegetables or clams. Ask about both stock and toppings. |

The bowls can look quite different across restaurants. If heat matters, ask **이거 매워요?** (Is this spicy?). A soybean paste base does not tell you how the restaurant seasons the finished broth.

## Confirm what comes to the table

**밥도 나오나요?** means “Does this come with rice?” If you are eating alone and see a single price on the board, show **이거 1인분인가요?** (Is this one portion?). Ask the price of a larger order before committing to a pot for the table.

If you need to avoid meat or seafood, tell the staff what you cannot eat and ask what is in the broth before ordering. If they cannot verify it, choose another dish.
