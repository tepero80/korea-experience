For an easy Seoul Forest (서울숲) outing, start at Seoul Forest Station and choose a short park route before adding Seongsu cafés or the river. The park is large; deciding where to turn around matters more than trying to see every section.

## Choose your starting point

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Park first | Use Seoul Forest Station and check the approach from Exit 4. |
| Cafés first | Start from Ttukseom if it matches your Seongsu plan, then walk into the park. |
| Brief visit | Choose the Family Lawn loop and a return point before adding other park sections. |

[Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=225816) describes Seoul Forest Station Exit 3 and Ttukseom Station Exit 8 as approaches. Check the current station and walking map before travel; these are different stations. Once inside, use the visitor map to choose a loop through a landscaped area, a rest stop and a return to your chosen exit.

## Keep the walk flexible

The [park listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=95429) distinguishes the open park from facilities that may have their own hours and closures. If a particular garden, wildlife area or program matters, check the [Seoul parks site](https://parks.seoul.go.kr/) before setting out; do not count on an animal encounter or seasonal flowers. In rain or extreme heat, shorten the loop and choose an indoor stop instead.

Search **서울숲** or use **서울특별시 성동구 뚝섬로 273** for the park. If continuing to Seongsu, check your actual café or station destination rather than treating the whole neighborhood as one exit. Save your chosen entrance and return station on the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
