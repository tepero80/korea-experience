For a contemporary home-goods stop in Bukchon, [Visit Seoul lists Parsnips](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/2024-parsnips/ENPwlwiuo) at **84-3 Gyedong-gil, Jongno-gu**, near Anguk Station Exit 3. Its listing describes decor and accessories on the first floor and furniture and lighting on the second, with hanok views. It lists 11:00–20:00 and closure on national holidays; confirm current hours before visiting.

## Choose by what you can take home

| Item | Ask before paying |
| --- | --- |
| Small ceramics or tableware | Ask for dimensions, material and protective packing; check whether it fits your luggage. |
| Paper or textile accessory | Ask about care, moisture and how it should be folded or packed. |
| Lighting or furniture | Measure the packed item, request delivery terms in writing and check voltage and plug compatibility where relevant. |

## Ask about the object itself

A hanok setting does not establish that every object is handmade, Korean-made or traditionally produced. If that matters to you, ask the seller about its maker, material and origin, and have the description included on your receipt. Compare the full cost of the object, packing and shipping before committing.
