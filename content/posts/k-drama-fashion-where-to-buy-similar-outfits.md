## Build the search around one item

Use a reference image to describe the type of jacket, knit or bag you want. Look for the shape and fabric you can actually wear. [KTO’s Myeongdong information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=263&menuSn=929&vcontsId=248785) describes a general shopping area. [Its Seongsu information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=249795) highlights designer stops and pop-ups you can check before going.

| Need | How to search |
| --- | --- |
| One basic item | Start where you already plan to shop and compare fabric, fit and total price. |
| Local design | Save a specific public showroom address and confirm its date or hours. |
| A reference | Ask for the silhouette, not an exact costume claim. Check the label if a shop claims a collaboration. |
| A fallback | Try a comparable piece in a second shop. Keep one easy alternative if fitting or stock is limited. |

## Check the purchase you will travel with

Check your actual measurements rather than guessing from a letter size. Ask whether the store allows trying on the item, what its exchange conditions are and how it will fit in your bag.
