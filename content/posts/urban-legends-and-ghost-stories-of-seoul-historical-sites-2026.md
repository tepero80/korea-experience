## Use official history to orient yourself

The [Seoul Metropolitan Government history page](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seodaemun-prison/) describes the site and the history hall that opened in 1998. Search **서대문형무소역사관** for the present visitor entrance. The [official tourism listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=111259) gives the address **서울특별시 서대문구 통일로 251**. Use the map below to plan your route.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Story | Save the exact place name from the story. Check which parts appear in official historical material. |
| Visit | Confirm opening hours and last entry for the exact date. |
| Conduct | Follow visitor rules and give memorial exhibits the same care as other historical displays. |
| Backup | Choose another daytime stop if the hall is closed or access to part of the site is restricted. |

## Keep folklore and museum information separate

When sharing the visit, label what you heard as a story and link the institution’s history for facts about the site. Use only public entrances and paths, and ask staff before photographing an exhibit or other visitors.

See the [Seodaemun Prison History Hall route](/blog/seodaemun-prison-history-hall-dongnimmun) for the approach from Dongnimmun and a nearby fallback.
