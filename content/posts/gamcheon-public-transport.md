**For:** Busan visitors who want to reach Gamcheon without a car. **Information checked:** 9 November 2026. Bus routes and local operating arrangements can change.

## Take the metro, then a village bus uphill

The [official Visit Busan guide](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=365) recommends **Busan Metro Line 1 to Toseong Station, Exit 6**, then a village bus to **Gamjeong Elementary School** and a short walk. It lists Saha 1-1, Seo-gu 2 and Seo-gu 2-2 on that approach. Check the direction, actual stop and service before boarding; route numbers alone are not a guarantee that every trip follows the same path.

**Destination:** Gamcheon Culture Village, 203 Gamnae 2-ro, Saha-gu, Busan  
**Korean address:** 부산광역시 사하구 감내2로 203

| Starting point | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Toseong | Take Line 1 to Exit 6; check the village bus direction and stop. |
| Goejeong | Visit Busan also lists Exit 6 and buses Saha 1 or 1-1; confirm the trip reaches the school stop. |
| Mobility or weather concerns | Consider a suitable taxi or another visit; slopes and steps make accessibility variable. |

## Step-by-step from Toseong

1. Take Line 1 to Toseong. Follow signs for Exit 6; check a current station plan for an accessible exit if stairs are a problem.
2. Find the village bus stop serving Gamcheon. Show the destination name **감천문화마을** to the driver or staff and confirm the direction before boarding.
3. According to Visit Busan, leave the bus at **Gamjeong Elementary School** (감정초등학교), then walk to the village entrance. The tourism guide describes this as around five minutes; your pace and route can differ.
4. Save your return route before moving into the lanes. Ask locally where to catch a bus downhill, because the inbound and outbound stops need not be identical.

If the bus is crowded, a taxi from your current position may be easier for some groups. Confirm the destination and how you will pay before leaving; costs and travel times vary.

## Choose an itinerary that matches your energy

Gamcheon is a working residential neighborhood as well as a visitor destination. Visit Busan asks visitors to keep noise down, keep the area clean, and not use drones. Stay out of private entrances and be considerate when taking photographs. Shops and individual activities may operate on different schedules; the official listing gives seasonal visitor hours, so check before making a late visit.

For a relaxed half-day, visit the village first, then return toward Toseong or Nampo for a meal. Allow for waiting and hills rather than promising a fixed door-to-door duration. If heavy rain, extreme heat, mobility needs or a tired child make the village unsuitable, substitute an indoor attraction and confirm its opening hours before traveling.

## What to ask

- 감천문화마을 가는 버스 맞나요? — Does this bus go to Gamcheon Culture Village?
- 감정초등학교에서 내려 주세요. — Please let me off at Gamjeong Elementary School.
- 지하철역으로 가는 버스는 어디서 타나요? — Where can I catch the bus back to the metro station?
