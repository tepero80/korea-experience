## Read the invitation or message first

Look for **장례식장**, the funeral hall, and **빈소**, the room where condolences are received. A [Korea.net account of funeral customs](https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Society/view?articleId=173942) discusses customary visits and **부의**, a term associated with condolence offerings. Ask your contact about the right time, the address and whether the family welcomes a visit.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Hall | Verify the address and entrance. A hospital and its funeral hall may have separate entrances. |
| Room | Save the room number and the name shown on the notice. |
| Visit | Ask what the family prefers. Keep your phone silent and follow the host’s cues. |
| Gift | Ask a trusted contact whether the family accepts offerings before bringing one. |

## Keep the condolence brief and considerate

A short, sincere greeting is appropriate when you are unsure what to say. Let the host indicate what to do at the room. Ask before taking any photograph or sharing a message about the funeral.
