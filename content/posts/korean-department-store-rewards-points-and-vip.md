Before signing up for a department-store rewards program, ask whether your **specific account can earn and use points on this purchase**. A shopping discount, a rewards balance and a tourist tax refund are separate things. Their eligibility and conditions may differ.

## Four questions at the counter

| Check | Ask before signing up |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Can a short-stay visitor register, and which documents or identity check are required? |
| Earn | Does this shop and product qualify, and must the account be presented before payment? |
| Use | Can this account redeem points here, and are there minimums or expiry dates? |
| Alternatives | Is there a current visitor offer without membership? Does a tax-refund condition apply separately? |

Get the offer's current terms from the operator or the store. Do not assume a purchase counts toward a VIP tier, that every concession participates or that a discount is available without a qualifying account.

## Example: L.POINT account levels

[L.POINT's privacy notice](https://m.lpoint.com/app/center/LWDD100300.do?prvGrpId=A07) describes information collected for foreign-member registration and says some L.POINT and L.PAY services are restricted until identity verification. It also says email verification creates a simplified membership with restrictions on some services, including point earning. The [program terms](https://m.lpoint.com/app/center/LWDD100101.do?prvGrpId=LP01) distinguish membership categories.

These notices do not establish that a particular traveler can register, earn or redeem at a particular counter. Ask the program or store to confirm the exact route, accepted identification and eligible purchase before sharing information. Read the data notice and consent screen yourself; there is no need to create an account if the benefit does not suit you.

## Make the decision at checkout

Ask **이 구매에 포인트 적립이 가능한가요?** (“Can I earn points on this purchase?”) and **외국인도 가입할 수 있나요?** (“Can a foreign visitor join?”). If you intend to leave Korea soon, also ask how and when points can be used and whether they expire. Keep the receipt and any promotion terms.

If registration is unavailable, ask for any non-member visitor offer and compare the final price.
