## Choose the meal around the branch you have

[Korea.net’s food guide](https://www.korea.net/K-InfoHub/SubMainDetail/view?articleId=4100&headwordCd=75&headwordGroupCd=28&pageIndex=1) shows how people pair triangle gimbap and cup noodles. Its [feature on ramyeon](https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Culture/view?articleId=138467) describes hot water as one way to make noodles at a shop. Check today’s shelf and ask about the equipment at the branch you are in.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Pick | Choose a main item and check the filling, storage label and any heating directions on the packet. |
| Pay | Take the items to the checkout before using shared equipment for a meal. |
| Heat | Ask about the microwave, 전자레인지, and follow the package instructions for the container and time. |
| Go | If seating or equipment is unavailable, choose a ready-to-eat item or take it somewhere you can heat it. |

## Ask one short question when the packet is unclear

Show the package and ask **이거 데워 먹나요?**, meaning “Do I heat this before eating?” For food restrictions, get help reading the actual ingredients on the item. Keep drinks, toppings or sides separate if you cannot verify them.
