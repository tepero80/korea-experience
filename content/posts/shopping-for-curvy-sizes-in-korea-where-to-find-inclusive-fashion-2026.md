Retail numbers can be an initial filter. Fit depends on the individual garment, its cut, fabric and room for movement. Use an item you already own as a reference instead of relying on an international size conversion chart.

## Compare like with like

| Check | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Top | Bust width across a similar garment laid flat, plus length and shoulder width. |
| Trousers | Flat waist, hip and rise on a pair you can sit in comfortably. |
| Chart | Whether the seller shows a flat garment width, full garment circumference or body size. |
| Fit | Stretch, layering room, fitted or relaxed cut, and details for the size you want. |

For example, a garment that measures 55 cm across the chest when laid flat is about 110 cm around that part of the garment. That figure is not a recommended body measurement. It shows why checking the chart’s measurement method matters. The [Romistory cardigan listing](https://romistory.com/product/detail.html?cate_no=259&display_group=1&product_no=61985) illustrates item-specific fitting notes. Read the notes attached to the garment you actually want.

## Check the stock before choosing a branch

[09WOMEN’s office and offline-center listing](https://www.09women.com/shop/todaygoods.html) identifies shops at **64 Yanghwa-ro, Mapo-gu** (Hapjeong) and **127 Dongnam-ro, Songpa-gu** (Munjeong). Use its branch contact to check today’s hours, availability and fitting. Bring the product page or take a screenshot with the code and color. Ask the shop whether the exact size is available there and whether you may try it on.

You can show staff **이 상품 이 사이즈 입어 볼 수 있나요?** (Can I try this item in this size?). If the size is only available online, ask about delivery timing and returns before ordering to your travel address. At checkout, check which return or exchange rules apply and whether your departure leaves enough time.
