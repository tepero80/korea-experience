**For a short seaside outing by train, book a service that stops at Jeongdongjin and walk from the station to the nearby beach.** The Korea Tourism Organization estimates about three minutes on foot. Leave the station by the public exit and follow the signed access on the day. Save the train back before you decide to add Sandglass Park.

## Choose the station-side beach or a longer outing

| Time | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Short stop | Use the beach by Jeongdongjin Station and stay near your return route. |
| Longer stop | Add Sandglass Park if your group has time to walk there and back. |
| Rough seas | Watch from an open public promenade or choose an inland stop. Follow barriers and announcements. |
| New Year | Check the separate festival plan and event-day transport. |

KTO describes two useful public beach areas, one in front of the station and one by Sandglass Park. Treat the park as an extension. Its address is **강릉시 강동면 헌화로 990**. The station-side access uses **강릉시 강동면 정동역길 17**. Use your actual pin if you need a pickup because the addresses take you to different places. [KTO’s beach-by-train guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=1591437) · [Sandglass Park directions](https://gtdc.or.kr/opentour/dzdocument.php?xch=intro&xid=opentour_location)

## Get off at the intended station

On the rail booking screen, check **Jeongdongjin (정동진)** as a stop on that exact departure. **Gangneung (강릉)** is a different station. Prefer a train plan you can manage in both directions and leave time to get back through the station before boarding. Work backward from your train to pick a turnaround time at the beach. [Korail’s English booking portal](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/main)

Keep to marked pedestrian routes and use assigned crossings. Some views from the train or station overlook the shore. The quick visit starts outside the station. If using a wheelchair or traveling with a stroller, check the route from your platform to the exit and the surface at the beach access separately. The KTO accessibility information is useful when deciding which facilities suit your group. [KTO accessibility listing](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/ms/detail.do?cotId=60b3ac94-f4ac-44ca-a6f5-213db72833cb)

## Decide whether the coast fits the day

A shoreline walk and a swim are different plans. The designated swimming season is published separately. Follow signs, lifeguard instructions and current closures. In strong wind or high waves, use the [winter coast safety guide](/blog/korea-coastal-winter-wave-safety) and choose a dry public viewing area away from the water’s edge.

The plan above is for a regular outing. Use the [Jeongdongjin New Year festival guide](/blog/jeongdongjin-2027-new-year-sunrise-festival-plan) for the holiday program and altered access. If you will instead arrive at Gangneung Station and want a simpler first stop, use the [Gangneung Station to Anmok guide](/blog/gangneung-station-anmok).

If the railway setting is the reason for your trip, [compare public station photo stops](/blog/korea-s-most-photogenic-train-stations) to choose between a coast, a historic building and a spring rail-heritage site.
