**Choose by what you want to do.** For a visit about making hanji from pulp, contact Jeonju Hanji Museum in Palbok first. For a short craft using prepared paper, check the Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall in the Hanok Village.

| Goal | Where to check |
| --- | --- |
| Make a sheet | The [Jeonju Hanji Museum](https://www.hanjimuseum.co.kr/main/) publishes opening hours. Call first to ask whether the exhibition and the hands-on paper session can be visited on your date. |
| Try a craft | The [Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall program](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/index.jeonju?facSid=RE_0001005&menuCd=DOM_000000201010001000) lists a hanji lamp craft. It describes on-site registration and limited materials. Check the session and current price before going. |

## Jeonju Hanji Museum in Palbok

The operator lists **Tuesday to Saturday, 09:00–17:00**, with last admission at **16:30**, and free entry. Regular closures include **Sunday, Monday, January 1 and the Seollal and Chuseok holiday periods**. The [city tourism listing](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000003&dataSid=9780&menuCd=DOM_000000101010000000) also carries an undated notice about temporary suspension of museum visits. Call **[063-210-8103](tel:+82632108103)** before making the detour. Confirm gallery access, whether individual visitors can join a session, and the time of the last session. The [museum FAQ](https://www.hanjimuseum.co.kr/main/menu?gc=908UZDG) and current notices are useful checks for group bookings.

The museum is on **전주시 덕진구 팔복로 59**. Use the Palbok address rather than searching for a generic hanji center. ## A short hanji craft in the Hanok Village

The city’s visitor listing gives regular craft hall hours of Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–18:00. Ask at the hall about today’s materials, language support, the length of the activity and any age restrictions. Allow extra time if you need instructions in English or are visiting with children.

Find the craft hall at **전주시 완산구 태조로 15 전주공예품전시관**.
