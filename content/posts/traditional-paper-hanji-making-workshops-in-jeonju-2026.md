For a paper-making experience in Jeonju, start with the [Jeonju Millennium Hanji Center’s operator page](https://www.jmhm.kr/main/?menu=86). Its program describes preparing mulberry bark, sheet forming, drying and finishing. It is different from simply decorating an existing paper object.

## Reserve the experience

| Your goal | Where to begin |
| --- | --- |
| Make paper | Start at the Millennium Hanji Center’s production experience booking. |
| Visit another venue | Check Jeonju Hanji Museum’s current schedule and workshop arrangements separately. |

The Korean-language page says online reservation is required: follow 체험예약바로가기 to the booking page and select an available session. The listed experience is about 50 minutes for adults and elementary-school-age children or older, with up to 20 participants per session. Groups of seven or more must also inquire by phone. The operator lists Thursday to Saturday sessions but excludes public holidays and event days; confirm the actual calendar before booking.

The center is at 전북특별자치도 전주시 완산구 흑석로 85, Jeonju, and lists +82-63-281-1630 for inquiries. Ask about current fees, English support, accessibility, what participants actually make and whether the paper can be collected that day. A submission or open calendar is not a confirmed booking until the operator confirms it.

For another stop, [Jeonju Hanji Museum’s FAQ](https://www.hanjimuseum.co.kr/main/menu?gc=908UZDG) discusses paper making and requires advance online reservations for groups of 20 or more. It also notes that experiences can pause during the artisan’s vacation, so call first. If you mainly want the exhibition, [plan a separate visit to the Hanji Museum on Palbok-ro](/blog/korean-paper-art-hanji-crafts-workshops).
