At the counter, a familiar word can have more than one meaning. Read the word in Korean, use the setting, and ask one specific question before accepting an extra or paying. You can show the Korean text on your phone if the pronunciation is unfamiliar.

## Four words to recognize

| Word | What to do when you see or hear it |
| --- | --- |
| **서비스** | If staff offer an item and call it 서비스, it may be complimentary. Ask **이거 무료인가요?** (Is this free?) if the price is unclear. In a review, 서비스 can simply mean customer service. |
| **칠성사이다** | The [original Chilsung Cider bottle label](https://company.lottechilsung.co.kr/kor/qrProduct/spec/list.do?prdt=ChilsungCider_Pet) describes a lemon-lime soft drink. Check the specific label if you care what a different drink contains. |
| **콘센트** | This means an electrical outlet. Ask **콘센트가 어디에 있나요?** (Where is the outlet?) or **이 콘센트를 써도 될까요?** (May I use this outlet?). |
| **핸드폰 번호** | A mobile number. On a form, check which country code or number format it accepts before entering your own. |

The Korean Basic Dictionary lists [several meanings of 서비스](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=71116). A complimentary item is one possibility, so check when the spoken offer and the total are unclear. The same dictionary has entries for [콘센트](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicMarinerSearch/search?mainSearchWord=%EC%BD%98%EC%84%BC%ED%8A%B8&nation=eng) and [핸드폰](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/m/eng/searchResultView?ParaSenseSeq=&ParaWordNo=58170&fileNo=&imgCount=&multiMediaSeq=&nation=eng&searchKind=&searchKindValue=&shortenUrl=&studySeq=).
