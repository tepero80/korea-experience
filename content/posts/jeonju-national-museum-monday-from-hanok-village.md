**The main Jeonju National Museum usually opens on Mondays, with free permanent galleries from 10:00 to 18:00 and last admission at 17:30.** Pick the main galleries if you are looking for a museum day that fits around the Hanok Village. Check the date first: on **Monday, December 14, 2026**, the museum has announced that both indoor buildings will be closed.

## Check which building will be open

The [main museum’s visitor guide](https://jeonju.museum.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a10101000000) lists January 1, Seollal day and Chuseok day as regular closures. Special shows can charge. Read the [temporary closure notice](https://jeonju.museum.go.kr/board.es?act=view&bid=0001&list_no=8981&mid=a10105010000&nPage=5) for the December 14 exception. It says the outdoor garden and outdoor parking will stay open. Save a separate indoor plan if that date is your only day and the weather rules out a garden visit.

The [children’s museum’s own guide](https://jeonju.museum.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a50201000000) lists 10:00–17:30, with breaks from 12:00 to 13:00 and 15:00 to 15:30. It closes on the third Monday each month and has its own holiday and temporary closures. Individuals can normally walk in, subject to room capacity. Groups have a weekday advance-reservation process. If your group arrives during an inspection break, visit the main galleries first or use the time for your cross-town transfer.

## Get there from Hanok Village

Save **국립전주박물관, 전북특별자치도 전주시 완산구 쑥고개로 249**. In your route app, use the name of the national museum as the destination. The [operator’s directions](https://jeonju.museum.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a10106000000) list the **전주박물관** and **전주역사박물관** stops. Compare the route from where you actually are and confirm the destination stop before boarding. The same page estimates about 20 minutes by taxi from Hanok Village. Allow extra time for traffic, waiting or a group pickup.

Allow for an exposed walk into the grounds on wet or very cold days. If you need a step-free approach from a taxi or the bus stop, ask the museum at [+82 63 223 5651](tel:+82632235651) about the drop-off and entrance you should use.

## Pick one collection and set a return time

Start with the Jeonju and Joseon Royal Court gallery if you just visited Gyeonggijeon. The museum’s [Jeonju and Joseon Royal Court gallery description](https://jeonju.museum.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a10202050000) explains the city’s connections to the royal court. The main collection also includes calligraphy, history, and arts and crafts. Choose one as the main stop and use the rest as options. Ask at the entrance about any gallery closure that affects your plan.

For a Monday visit with younger children, keep the children’s building optional until you have checked the date. If its schedule does not fit, let each person choose one thing to look for in the main museum, such as a calligraphy piece or an object linked to the royal court. That gives a shorter indoor visit a clear shape.

If your onward train is fixed, save the return toward Jeonju Station before committing to a second building. Our [Jeonju station to Hanok Village guide](/blog/jeonju-station-to-hanok-village) helps when you are also planning the arrival leg.
