**Book an assessment, not a promised result.** If an aesthetic procedure is part of your Korea trip, establish who will assess you, what exact product is proposed and how you will get help after leaving the clinic. This page does not select a treatment, dose or recovery schedule for you.

## Compare the consultation, not the promotion

A menu headed “Botox,” “filler,” “lifting” or “skin booster” is not enough information to compare two offers. Ask for the exact product/device and proposed use in writing. Do not assume different brands have interchangeable approvals, doses or uses, or that a claim about one country's regulator settles authorization elsewhere.

| Question to take to the clinician                                   | What you need back                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What is the problem you are treating, and what are my alternatives? | An explanation that includes postponing or declining treatment |
| Who performs each part of the procedure?                            | Named roles and qualifications                                 |
| What exact product, manufacturer and route are proposed?            | Details you can independently verify                           |
| What are the material risks for my history and current medicines?   | An individualized discussion, not a general success percentage |
| What should I do if symptoms change after the visit?                | Written instructions and an escalation contact                 |
| How does my flight and remaining itinerary affect the plan?         | A clinician's advice for your dates                            |

Check the provider through [Medical Korea's registration information](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem). Registration is not an endorsement of a particular procedure. Bring your medicine list and relevant treatment records to the consultation; disclose them privately to the care team, not in a public comment.

## Get a quote you can compare

Request a KRW total that separates consultation, product/procedure, tax, review visits and any interpreter charges. Ask who pays for an extra visit or treatment change and what happens to a deposit if you decline after assessment. A lower headline price can describe a different product or scope.

Use the [trip budget planner](/tools/trip-budget) for accommodation and transport. Keep the clinical quotation separate; that tool does not estimate medical prices or determine whether a procedure is appropriate.

## Leave room for the decision to change

Save the clinic's Korean address and a direct route home. Ask about combining proposed procedures, flying and your plans for exercise, heat or sun. Do not substitute a blog's fixed aftercare timetable for the treating team's instructions. If a proposal is rushed or unclear, you can keep the consultation and postpone treatment.

For non-emergency service information use [Medical Korea](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient). For emergencies, [VISITKOREA identifies119 for ambulance/fire and 112 for police](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145). Do not post medical records or identifying photographs in the community.
