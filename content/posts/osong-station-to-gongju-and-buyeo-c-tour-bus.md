At **Osong KTX Station**, the regional **C-Tour Bus (C-투어버스)** connects to **Gongju Bus Terminal** and **Buyeo Intercity Bus Terminal**. Start by choosing one town and checking both directions for your date. The [Korea Tourism Organization guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=481&vcontsId=140665) lists a ticket kiosk by **Exit 7** and boarding at **Platform 8**. Match the destination and departure on the ticket to the signs at the station.

## Choose your stop and return

| Choose | What the ticket should say |
| --- | --- |
| Gongju for the fortress or museum | **공주종합버스터미널** (Gongju Bus Terminal). |
| Buyeo for the museum or Gungnamji Pond | **부여시외버스터미널** (Buyeo Intercity Bus Terminal). |
| Returning to a reserved KTX | **오송역** (Osong Station) as your destination. |

Published examples show roughly **40 minutes** from Osong to Gongju and **90 minutes** to Buyeo. These are scheduled times. The November [national tourism timetable](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=481&vcontsId=140665) shows Osong departures toward Buyeo at **08:40, 10:30, 14:40 and 16:30**. It shows the **19:00** departure from Buyeo stopping in Gongju at **19:50** and reaching Osong at **20:30**. These last listed services leave little room for error if you have a train booked. Confirm the trip for your date, reserve early enough to allow for delays, and save the next available rail or bus alternative.

## Board the regional coach at Osong

Save **오송역, 충청북도 청주시 흥덕구 오송읍 오송가락로 123**. When leaving the train, look for the bus transfer center sign **버스환승센터**. If you need to buy a ticket there, the Exit 7 kiosk is the location to look for. Use the numbered **8번 승강장** sign and confirm the bus says **공주** or **부여**. The same route also runs the other way toward **청주공항** (Cheongju Airport), so check the direction before boarding.

The [C-Tour operator](https://www.c-tourbus.com/) and the tourism guide describe two services. Buy a ticket for the **regional circular coach** for Osong–Gongju–Buyeo. The sightseeing DRT service is a separately requested local ride. Its pass cannot be used for the regional coach. The tourism guide lists the C-Tour Bus, Tmoney GO and Bustago apps for regional tickets, as well as terminal kiosks. If an app will not complete payment, try the kiosk with time to spare and check the amount displayed before buying. Keep a usable ticket and a copy of your destination in Korean.

## Continue from the terminal and protect the way back

In Gongju, get off at the bus terminal in Singwan-dong and plan a separate local connection to Gongsanseong Fortress or the museum. The terminal and Gongju KTX Station are separate locations. In Buyeo, use the intercity terminal as your reference point for a local bus, DRT request or taxi. Check any local ride’s pickup point separately from the regional coach.

For the return, the KTO guide lists **Platform 1** at Gongju and the **Incheon Airport bay** at Buyeo. At Buyeo, verify your C-Tour ticket’s actual destination because the bay label points to a different airport. If no C-Tour return works, ask the ticket counter about regular coaches. [Buyeo County’s terminal timetable](https://www.buyeo.go.kr/html/kr/refo/refo_060501.html) is a starting point. Protect your [onward rail connection](/blog/ktx-vs-srt-high-speed-train-comparison-and-booking) before choosing the last sightseeing stop.
