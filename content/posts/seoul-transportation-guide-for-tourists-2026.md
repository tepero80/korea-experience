## Compare the route from door to door

[Visit Seoul’s transport guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/subway) explains the subway, buses and taxis. Start with the two exact addresses. Compare nearby entrances, transfers and how long it will take to get out at the end of the trip.

| Route | Choose it when |
| --- | --- |
| Subway | The stations are convenient, the transfers make sense and the correct exit serves your destination. |
| Bus | There is a direct route with a better final stop. Check the direction on the street and tag off as instructed. |
| Taxi | The workable transit option has ended, or your group and bags are hard to manage at transfers. Check vehicle size first. |
| On foot | The connection is short and the path fits your bags, mobility and weather. Check the actual street route. |

## Save a last-service fallback

For a late trip, look up the station, direction and your actual stopping station. Check whether a train or bus ends its service before you reach your stop. Save the Korean destination address and a backup way to move your group. Check elevators along the route if you need them, including the correct street exit.

This guide starts once you are in Seoul. If your first stop is the airport, see the [Incheon transfer comparison](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) for the airport part of the journey.
