## Write down the concerns separately

If you are considering care for your jawline and chest, describe each concern in your own words when booking. State what brought you in and when you noticed it. Ask who should evaluate each concern and whether the first visit is for assessment only.

| Topic | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Jaw | Which clinician should evaluate the jawline concern? If I also have a bite, chewing, or dental concern, who should assess that before agreeing to any plan? |
| Chest | Who can assess changes in breast tissue and recommend any tests or referral they consider appropriate? |
| Costs | Can you quote each consultation, test, interpretation service, and planned visit separately? |
| Dates | If both teams want follow-up, could the appointments conflict? Who would review my travel dates and coordinate if the plan changes? |

## Get each assessment before discussing a package

The [NHS overview of gynaecomastia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/gynaecomastia/) describes several possible causes of breast enlargement in men. Ask a clinician for assessment, especially if you have a persistent change, pain or a lump. Describe what you noticed and bring the medicines and supplements you take to the clinical discussion.

If either team proposes surgery, ask it to explain its own plan, risks, alternatives and next steps. [Article 24-2 of Korea’s Medical Service Act](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031808871) describes explanation and written consent requirements for covered surgery and general anesthesia. Ask for separate explanations and only agree to the services you intend to receive.

For a lower-face consultation involving an existing bite or chewing concern, [ask which provider would assess jaw function and what records they need](/blog/v-line-surgery-korea-procedure-cost-recovery).

## Decide who handles questions after the visits

Confirm who can answer a question about each concern after hours and whether one person can help coordinate both plans. Ask which documents each team can provide and how your usual clinician can receive them with your permission. For help confirming who can speak with you or arrange an interpreter, use the [guide to finding English-speaking medical care in Seoul](/blog/finding-english-speaking-doctors-in-seoul) before paying for the appointments.
