**Start at Dongmun Traditional Market and walk to Samseonghyeol before its ticket window shuts.** Stop by Jeigak along the old-town route if the approach is open. On a late start, reverse the order and visit the sacred grove before getting food. The folklore museum on the same street is a useful extension when its separate calendar fits.

## Set the order before you go

| When | Pick this order |
| --- | --- |
| Morning | Start at Dongmun, make the old-town walk, then visit Samseonghyeol. |
| Late afternoon | Go to Samseonghyeol first. Leave the market or dinner until afterward. |
| Rain | Put the folklore museum first if it is open. Keep the outdoor portion short. |
| Museum closed | Plan around the market and Samseonghyeol. Check each venue’s hours separately. |

This is a self-guided walk using part of the [Jeju Tourism Organization’s Seongan Olle Route 3](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CNTS_300000000014329). Its published order puts the market before Jeigak and Samseonghyeol, then continues to the natural history museum area. Use it for the stop order. A guided departure has its own schedule and booking conditions.

## Walk from the market to the grove

Begin at **동문재래시장**, **제주시 관덕로14길 20**. Save the full Korean name and use the gate nearest your starting point. There are multiple market entrances, and vendor hours vary. Take a food break before walking on if the grove’s entry window leaves you enough time. Read the [market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=459&vcontsId=91650) before you leave.

Enter **제이각 쉼터** as an optional waypoint, then **삼성혈**, **제주시 삼성로 22**, as your destination. Use pedestrian navigation through the old town. Follow crossings along the signed streets and skip the waypoint if an approach is closed or your group needs a simpler walk. Put both pins into your phone before leaving the market, so you have a direct route even if the trail signs are hard to spot.

## Leave time before the ticket cutoff

Visit Jeju publishes 09:00–18:00 for Samseonghyeol, with last ticket sales at **17:30** and a 10:00 opening on January 1, Seollal and Chuseok. The Korea Tourism Organization lists **₩4,000 per adult**. Check the [regional visitor details](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500300) and [admission prices](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=91127) before going.

Once inside, use the site’s displays to learn the tradition before visiting the viewing area. According to Jeju’s founding legend, three ancestors emerged from the holes at this site. View the ground from the permitted path and follow any signs around the grove. Leave time to explore the outdoor paths as well as the exhibition.

## Add the museum or finish back at Dongmun

For a separate indoor stop, use **제주특별자치도 민속자연사박물관**, **제주시 삼성로 40**. The [museum’s visitor page](https://www.jeju.go.kr/museum/guidance/view.htm) lists 09:00–18:00, ticket sales ending at 17:30, and a regular Monday closure with holiday exceptions. Choose the folk exhibits for a closer look at how people lived on the island. Save the walk back or your ride before entering.

If you are staying downtown, extend the visit with [a car-free Dongmun dinner](/blog/dongmun-market-dinner-without-a-car). If your hotel is in Seogwipo or you are leaving from the airport, plan your [Jeju transfer](/blog/jeju-airport-to-jeju-city-and-seogwipo) separately and protect your departure time.
