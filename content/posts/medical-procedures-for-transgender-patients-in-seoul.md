## Confirm the service and who will see you

Contact the clinic through a channel it lists. Describe the appointment you want in your own words, whether it is a first consultation or help continuing care you already receive. Before sending medical history, ask which department and clinician can assess your request, which documents they need, and who will have access to them. Check the provider’s registration through [Medical Korea’s registration guidance](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem) and ask the clinic directly whether the particular service is offered.

| Topic | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Which name does your ID, billing or certificate process require? What name can staff use when speaking with me? |
| Access | Can I speak privately? Can a support person attend if I want one? Who would interpret, and what would that cost? |
| Price | What would I pay for the consult and any tests? What would happen to my deposit if the plan changes? |
| Care | What follow-up would you need me to attend, and how could my usual clinician get the details with my permission? |

## Decide what to share and with whom

You can ask how your name will appear in waiting-room calls, appointment messages and receipts. Ask the clinic to explain its rules for an interpreter or another person to receive sensitive information. [Article 21 of the Medical Service Act](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1028699889) covers requests for your own records and conditions for access by other people. Confirm which ID or permission forms the clinic requires and how it will send the records. The [English medical documents guide](/blog/korean-medical-records-getting-english-translations) helps you ask for the copy your next clinician needs.

## Plan continuity before you travel

If you take medication, ask your current clinician and the Seoul provider how to avoid gaps during the visit. Check any entry permit with [the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety’s controlled medicine guidance](https://www.mfds.go.kr/brd/m_74/view.do?Data_stts_gubun=C9999&company_cd=&company_nm=&itm_seq_1=0&itm_seq_2=0&multi_itm_seq=0&page=118&seq=43906&srchFr=&srchTo=&srchTp=0&srchWord=) using the specific active ingredient. If you are considering a procedure, ask the treating clinician to put the options, next visit and contact for questions in writing before you make travel commitments.
