**Pack for how long you will be outside, not only the month on the ticket.** A museum-and-café day and an exposed fortress walk need different clothing, shoes and recovery breaks.

## Match the bag to the day

| Type of day | Prepare first | Change the plan when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City sightseeing with indoor stops | Layers you can adjust, warm accessories and a small day bag | Wind, rain, snow or fatigue makes the outdoor block uncomfortable |
| A long outdoor walk | More protection for exposed time, suitable footwear and a warm-up stop | Surface conditions or official warnings make the route unsuitable |
| Skiing or winter mountain activity | Operator-specific clothing, equipment and transport instructions | The operator changes conditions or you lack suitable equipment |

[KTO's seasonal guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140636) describes winter as cold and dry and recommends winter clothing and footwear that helps with snowy conditions. That broad advice is not a technical equipment rating or a guarantee against slipping.

## Pack the small items you will use repeatedly

- Adjustable base/mid/outer layers for indoor–outdoor changes.
- Gloves, scarf and head/ear protection appropriate to your planned exposure.
- Comfortable footwear for the expected surfaces and enough socks for wet days.
- A way to protect essentials from precipitation and keep hands free when carrying bags.
- Your own medication information, chargers and the hotel's Korean address offline.

## Check the room and the last outdoor approach

Ask about the actual station exit, steps, luggage route and late reception. A short straight-line distance is not the whole winter walk. For a hanok or unfamiliar property, confirm heating controls, bedding and whether the bathroom route stays indoors; use the [winter-accommodation questions](/blog/choosing-winter-accommodation-in-korea).

Leave a warm-up or meal break inside a long outdoor day. Keep a [nearby indoor alternative](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets) if weather changes, instead of trying to finish every outdoor stop.
