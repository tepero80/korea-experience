Pick a shop you can reach comfortably from the palace you actually intend to visit. For a Gyeongbokgung outing, save **경복궁 · 서울 종로구 사직로 161** and mark the shop as a separate stop. A photo at the entrance and a short circuit inside can be the whole plan.

## Make the itinerary fit the outfit

| Stop | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| At the fitting | Walk, sit and try a few steps in the complete outfit. Ask the fitter to adjust anything that catches underfoot. Carry your phone and the shop address in a secure pocket or bag. |
| At the palace | Look at the [official hanbok rules](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E701000000.do) for the upper and lower garments. Leave the fitting only after the shop explains what it has supplied. The palace makes the admission decision. |
| Before adding a stop | Set a leave-by reminder that accounts for walking back and changing. Skip an optional courtyard if your group is moving slowly. |
| After returning the clothes | Add an Insadong walk or tea stop here instead of keeping it inside the rental window. |

The [Gyeongbokgung listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=87740) gives seasonal last entry and a normal Tuesday closure. Check holiday exceptions before paying for a rental. For rain or heat, confirm that the rental still suits your plans and ask about the shop's change policy. On residential streets, leave entrances clear and take photos from public space.
