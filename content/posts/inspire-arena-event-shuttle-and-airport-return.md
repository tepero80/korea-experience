**Read your event's return instructions before choosing your outbound train.** An [INSPIRE Arena event transport notice](https://www.inspireresorts.com/pdfs/Arena_Shuttle_Info_E.pdf) shows a free inbound shuttle from Airport Cargo Terminal Station **Exit 2** and an after-show shuttle to **Incheon Airport Terminal 2**. Check whether your show posts those same stops, how late it runs, and where you can go from its drop-off point.

## Get the right shuttle for your ticket

| What your notice says | Your next step |
| --- | --- |
| Airport Cargo Terminal Station Exit 2 | Use the AREX all-stop train to **공항화물청사역**. Go to Exit 2 and match the queue to your show name. |
| Resort shuttle, Terminal 1, Terminal 2 or a city pickup | Check the exact floor, bay and departures in the [regular resort shuttle listing](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/gmpikhi-inspire-entertainment-resort/location/). It operates separately and seats are first come, first served. |
| An organizer's coach or a separate booking | Verify pickup, return drop-off, check-in deadline and ticket or booking terms with the promoter before leaving. |

[INSPIRE’s first-party transport overview](https://blog.inspireresorts.com/news/inspire-arena) also shows how the regular airport shuttle reaches the resort. Use the current timetable for the exact boarding bay and your event notice for the concert bus.

[AREX's route guide](https://www.airportrailroad.com/train/express/introduce) shows Airport Cargo Terminal on the all-stop line. The airport Express serves the passenger terminals and Seoul Station. For the arena itself, save **인스파이어 아레나, 인천광역시 영종구 공항문화로 127**. Older listings can still show Jung-gu. Use the arena name and your event's instructions to find the gate on the resort site.

## Plan from the actual drop-off

In the [arena's example notice](https://www.inspireresorts.com/pdfs/Arena_Shuttle_Info_E.pdf), the after-show queue forms in a designated area outside the arena. The bus goes to **Terminal 2**, and the operator warns that boarding can take more than 30 minutes. A different setup can include other stops: for example, the [2026 EDC Korea organizer](https://korea.edc.com/en/travel/shuttles/) listed separate routes at different points of its event. Use the notice for your own show to check both the final shuttle and the final connection.

- **Returning to central Seoul?** Open the [Terminal 2 AREX timetable](https://www.airportrailroad.com/train/normal/info/110/0), select your date and direction, and verify where your last feasible train terminates. Include the queue, road trip, walk to the station and any subway transfer. If that margin is tight, arrange a confirmed ride, taxi or local overnight plan.
- **Going to an airport hotel or flying?** Check the terminal on your booking. If the bus drops you at T2 and you need T1, allow time for [the airport's inter-terminal connection](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/6636/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGYmJzJTJGYXBfZW4lMkY5MzclMkYxMzgyMTAlMkZhcnRjbFZpZXcuZG8lM0Y%3D). Check your airline's check-in cutoff separately.
- **Need a particular boarding arrangement?** Ask the organizer about your gate, vehicle and boarding location before the event. If you arrange a ride, confirm where the driver can legally pick you up and share the address of your final destination.

Keep the instructions offline, including the show name, shuttle cutoff and hotel address. Use the [airport transfer guide](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) to compare the trip from the actual passenger terminal once you know your return route.
