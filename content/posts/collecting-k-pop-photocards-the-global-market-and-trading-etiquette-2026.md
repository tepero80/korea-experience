## Identify the right version

The official listing for an album may name which cards were inside its version. A separate seller notice can define an entirely different random bonus. Save the correct source for the card you are looking for before comparing listings.

| Ask about | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Group, member, release, edition and whether it was an album inclusion or a shop benefit. |
| Condition | Clear photos of the front and back, edges and any marks you care about. |
| Terms | Total price or exact trade, meeting or shipping arrangement, and what happens if the item differs. |
| Container | Sleeve, backing and rigid protection for carrying it home. |

## Keep the transaction simple

Use the shop or platform’s process when you need proof of the exchange. If meeting at an event, check whether the venue allows trades, choose an appropriate public location, and avoid blocking entrances. Ask permission before photographing a person or another seller’s display. Leave the deal if you cannot establish the item and terms.

If you would rather buy a new album, read the [album inclusions checklist](/blog/k-pop-album-unboxing-culture-photocards-and-inclusions) to see which format and accessories the edition actually includes.
