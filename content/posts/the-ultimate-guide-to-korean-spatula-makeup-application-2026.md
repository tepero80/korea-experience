Ask for **메이크업 스패츌라** (makeup spatula) or **파운데이션 스패츌라** (foundation spatula). A makeup spatula can help you spread a little liquid base onto chosen areas before blending. If you mostly apply cushion foundation or prefer the finish of your current brush or sponge, save the space in your bag.

## Compare the tool with the job

| Option | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Broad, flat tip | You want to spread a small amount over a wider area. |
| Narrow tip | You want to place product on a smaller area, then blend it. |
| Brush or sponge | You want to apply and blend with a tool you already know how to clean. |

The [Fillimilli example at Olive Young Global](https://global.oliveyoung.com/product/detail?prdtNo=GA250430170) shows separate broad and narrow tips, stainless material and a pouch. Use it as a picture and specification reference. Check the exact local tool for a smooth edge, whether the cover comes with it, and the instructions for cleaning and drying. Ask the branch about its price and stock before making a special trip.

## Try a small amount and clean the tool

If the product instructions allow it, place a small amount of your base on a clean palette, spread it lightly, then blend with your own clean applicator. Follow the maker’s cleanup instructions and let the tool dry before putting it back in the pouch. Avoid sharing cosmetics and keep their containers clean, as described by the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/potential-contaminants-cosmetics/microbiological-safety-and-cosmetics).
