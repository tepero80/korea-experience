**The 2026 horse exhibition at Seoul’s National Folk Museum has ended.** For a visit on Jeju, choose the protected pasture for an outdoor view or the Gasi-ri horse museum for the island’s pastoral history. A museum ticket and a riding session may have different conditions.

## What ended in Seoul

[Korea.net’s exhibition notice](https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Culture/view?articleId=284757) identifies the National Folk Museum of Korea in Jongno as the host and March 2, 2026 as the final date. The museum is a separate attraction with its own current program. Check for a current exhibition before going there to see horse-related objects.

## Choose a practical Jeju stop

| If you want… | Choose | Check before setting out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A seasonal outdoor view | Jeju Horse Pastureland, on 516-ro | Whether horses are out and where visitors may stand. |
| A museum about Jeju’s pastoral culture | Jeju Horse Park in Gasi-ri | Museum hours, the correct entrance and your transport back. |
| A riding activity | Contact the Gasi-ri site before travel | Weather, age or group requirements, appointment, and whether service is running. |

## Jeju Horse Pastureland · View from the visitor side

[Visit Jeju identifies the protected herd](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500177) and describes its pasture as a seasonal view. [Its horse travel guide](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/themtour/view?contentsid=CNTS_300000000014078) says the interior is restricted. Use signed public viewing areas. A pasture visit offers no guarantee of seeing horses near the road. Keep children with you and follow signs about where to stop and stand.

## Gasi-ri · Museum and activities at Jeju Horse Park

The [local listing](https://www.visitjeju.net/kr/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500588) describes a pony museum and riding facilities. Check [current English visitor information](https://www.visitjeju.net/en/detail/view?contentsid=CONT_000000000500588&menuId=DOM_000001817000000001) and contact the site before counting on an activity. Decide how you can return before leaving for this inland stop, especially with children or without a car.
