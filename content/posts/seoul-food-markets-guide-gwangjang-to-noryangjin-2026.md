Give yourself one market stop based on what you want to eat. At Gwangjang you can choose a prepared dish from a stall. At Noryangjin the seafood sales area and restaurant areas have different roles. A useful plan leaves time to ask about cost and preparation at the right counter.

## Pick the market by the meal

| If you want | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| A ready-to-eat dish or a few small bites | **광장시장** (Gwangjang), **서울 종로구 창경궁로 88**. Check the food aisle and decide whether to sit at the stall or take a portable order. |
| To choose seafood and confirm how it is served | **노량진수산물도매시장** (Noryangjin), **서울 동작구 노들로 674**. Decide whether you want a takeaway product or a restaurant to prepare it. |

Use the [Gwangjang listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=106345) or the [Noryangjin listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=91565) to verify the address. Search by the full Korean name in your map and choose the entrance for the stall or floor you need.

## Ask about the part of the bill you cannot see

At Gwangjang, start with one portion and ask how it will be served if you want to move on quickly. The [KTO food overview](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1595642) lists mung bean pancakes and mini gimbap as examples. The [Gwangjang food guide](/blog/gwangjang-market-complete-food-guide) helps locate the right part of the market.

At Noryangjin, the [KTO building listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=91565) separates the product area from the restaurants. Ask what the product total covers. If another counter or restaurant will cook, prepare or seat you, ask for that price before agreeing. Check whether each amount is for the whole order or per person, and note who charges it.

If seafood is the reason for this trip, see our [Noryangjin seafood guide](/blog/korean-seafood-markets-noryangjin-and-more-2026) and save a backup meal before you leave.
