## Choose a public stop

| Show | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Itaewon Class | The Noksapyeong pedestrian overpass near the Itaewon Elementary School Education and Culture Center. Start at the subway exit and use the public approach. |
| Squid Game | The Baegun Market area in Ssangmun-dong. Go for a neighborhood walk, then confirm which shops are open when you arrive. |

### Itaewon Class: start at Noksapyeong Station

Use [Visit Seoul’s route](https://english.visitseoul.net/hallyu/Diving-into-Itaewon-Class/ENN034824) from **Line 6, Exit 3**. It puts the overpass across the road from the exit. Use the marked crossing and pedestrian path. [VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/HallyuNew/contentsView.do?dataSetId=70&menuNm=tour&page=8&vcontsId=200508) identifies the nearby school center as a landmark. Check for stairs and walking detours at the actual approach.

### Squid Game: visit the Baegun Market area

[VisitKorea’s route](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=72&menuSn=862&vcontsId=200823) uses the Baegun Market area and a filmed storefront as landmarks. Treat that address as orientation for the public street. Shop names and opening times can change. Review the return route and plan the rest of the day in northern Seoul if distance makes a second stop impractical.
