Before replacing or discarding a bed, desk or appliance in a rental, confirm with the landlord whether it belongs to the property and who is responsible for removal. Ask the building manager where approved bulky items should be placed and whether the building has its own arrangements. For everyday waste, see [sorting at your accommodation](/blog/recycling-rules-in-korea-sorting-and-penalties).

## Plan removal before moving day

1. List each item, its dimensions and condition. Confirm ownership and agree who will pay and book the pickup.
2. Find the bulky-waste page for the district where the item is located. Check whether the item is accepted, the fee, reporting method, collection location and lead time. Appliances may have separate options.
3. Make the required report and keep the confirmation. Follow the district’s instructions for marking the item and setting it out; coordinate access with the building manager.
4. Check the actual collection date and allow time before handing back the keys. If in doubt, contact the district or its designated collector.

## Gangnam example

| Item | First check |
| --- | --- |
| The item belongs to the furnished rental | Ask the owner before moving or discarding it. |
| The item is yours | Confirm the district accepts it and that the pickup date fits your move. |

The [Gangnam resource portal](https://clean.gangnam.go.kr/use/guid/USEGUID01000000.do?contentId=con2411150004) describes advance reporting for bulky household waste and asks households to attach the receipt or display the registration number. Confirm the fee, collection point and date for the specific item when you apply. Another district can use a different process, schedule or price.
