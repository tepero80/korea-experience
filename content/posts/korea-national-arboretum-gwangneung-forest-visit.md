Plan an outdoor visit to Korea National Arboretum (국립수목원) in Gwangneung Forest. Choose the day and arrival method first, then leave enough time to walk back to the gate and catch the return.

| Arrival | What to arrange |
| --- | --- |
| Bus or on foot | On-site admission while capacity remains. Check the date and visitor cap before traveling. |
| Car | Reserve parking on the [official booking page](https://reservenew.kna.go.kr/?selectReservationType=RESERVATION_NOMAL). Same-day reservations depend on spaces and the booking cutoff. |

## Pick a day and confirm access

The [operator lists](https://reservenew.kna.go.kr/intro) Tuesday through Sunday from March to November and Tuesday through Saturday from December to February. Check holiday exceptions before going. From November through March, the listed last entry is **16:00** and the grounds close at **17:00**. If driving, search for visitor parking at **광릉수목원로 415**. Call **031-540-2000** about capacity, eligibility for free admission or special entry arrangements. The operator lists a [Forest Museum closure for remodeling](https://kna.forest.go.kr/kfsweb/cop/bbs/selectBoardArticle.do?bbsId=BBSMSTR_1031&ctgryLrcls=CTGRY106&mn=UKNA_05_01_01&nttId=3219933) through its announced December 2026 construction period. Confirm reopening before counting on it as an indoor stop.

## Plan the return from Gwangneung Forest

For public transport from Seoul, start with Line 1 to **Uijeongbu Station (의정부역)**. A [municipal directions document](https://www.suwon.go.kr/common-upload/upload/board/2025/3/13/ec313b51-d712-4a37-ad31-fc305f1a5fee.pdf) describes bus 21 from the area near Exit 5. Use [Gyeonggi Bus Information](https://www.gbis.go.kr/) to verify the active boarding stop, the direction to **국립수목원** and the exact return stop before relying on it. The [route listing](https://www.gbis.go.kr/service/busInfo/metroBus.action?centerId=4136000000&cmd=searchMetroCenter) gives route endpoints, so check the departures at the arboretum rather than using its terminal times. Consider a taxi if your return plan is tight.

Look for the signed visitor entrance and choose a route around your time and walking comfort. Ask staff about any slopes, conditions or weather closures that matter to your group. If winter access or transport does not fit, compare [Pocheon Art Valley](/blog/pocheon-art-valley-from-seoul-by-bus) on a separate itinerary.
