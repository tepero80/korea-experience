**At Dongdaegu, leave the national rail station and follow signs for Daegu Metro Line 1. Choose Jungangno for Dongseongno or the modern history museum, Banwoldang for a Line 2 connection, and Myeongdeok to transfer to Line 3 for Seomun Market.** Save the return journey before leaving the station if you have another booked train.

## Start at the right Dongdaegu entrance

Your rail ticket should say **동대구** if this is where you arrive. **대구역** is a different station. At the mainline station, look for **도시철도 1호선 / 지하철** and the **동대구복합환승센터** signs. The [national rail station listing](https://www.kric.go.kr/jsp/board/portal/sub05/est/estation_BasicDetail.jsp?q_station=A030280164&q_stationNm=%EB%8F%99%EB%8C%80%EA%B5%AC%EC%97%AD) places the metro entrance by the transit center southwest of the rail station and points rail arrivals toward Gate 3. Follow the posted route and check the lift route before taking stairs with luggage. The city’s [official transit portal](https://car.daegu.go.kr/metro/subway) links to station exits, facility maps and train schedules.

**Rail station (동대구역)** · `대구광역시 동구 동대구로 550` · [Find the Dongdaegu mainline station in Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%8F%99%EB%8C%80%EA%B5%AC%EC%97%AD).

At Line 1, use the platform toward **설화명곡 (Seolhwa-Myeonggok)** for the downtown stations below. Check the board if a train shows a shorter terminal. The first stop in that direction is **신천 (Sincheon)**. A rail ticket gets you to Dongdaegu. Use a local fare card accepted by the metro or buy a metro ticket at the station. For current fare rules and facility details, use the [Daegu Transportation Corporation information linked by the city](https://car.daegu.go.kr/metro/subway). Information checked November 23, 2026.

## Pick the exit by what you want to do first

| First stop | From Dongdaegu | Exit or next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dongseongno or the Daegu Modern History Museum | Line 1 directly to **중앙로 (Jungangno)** | The museum operator directs visitors to **Exit 4**. Confirm the best exit in your map for shops or a different address along Dongseongno. |
| Banwoldang, or a destination whose station is on Line 2 | Line 1 directly to **반월당 (Banwoldang)** | Use the exit for your saved street address, or stay in the station and follow **2호선** signs for the correct direction. |
| Seomun Market | Line 1 to **명덕 (Myeongdeok)**, then Line 3 toward **칠곡경대병원** to **서문시장 (Seomun Market)** | The city’s metro guide uses **Exit 3** for the market approach. For the elevator route, check the operator’s station map before choosing an exit. |

The [city transit guide](https://car.daegu.go.kr/metro/subway) gives access to live operator routes, maps and exit information. The [museum operator’s directions](https://dgfca.or.kr/map_list?html_cd=11) and the [city’s metro sightseeing guide](https://tour.daegu.go.kr/file/7999575debef43ea8705141c1a7f7154.pdf) provide the named exits to the museum and market. The PDF is for orientation. Confirm current facility and exit conditions from a station map.

- **Daegu Modern History Museum (대구근대역사관):** `대구광역시 중구 경상감영길 67`. [Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%8C%80%EA%B5%AC%EA%B7%BC%EB%8C%80%EC%97%AD%EC%82%AC%EA%B4%80)
- **Seomun Market (서문시장):** `대구광역시 중구 큰장로26길 45`. [Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%84%9C%EB%AC%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5)

## Protect your return when time or luggage is tight

- **You want one central stop before another train.** Choose a direct Line 1 ride to Jungangno or Banwoldang. Allow time for the street exit, return metro, separate rail entrance and finding the platform. Confirm your mainline reservation after reversing directions.
- **You need a step-free route or have heavy luggage.** Check both station exits plus the line change at Myeongdeok. If an elevator is out of service or the connection does not suit your group, use the signed taxi area and show the driver the actual address. Confirm whether the vehicle can take your luggage or mobility equipment.
- **You will return late.** Use the [metro operator’s timetable](https://www.dtro.or.kr/index.do?menu_id=00000270) for your date, station and direction, and check how far the last train runs. If you will finish at Seomun, check the Line 3 leg and the Line 1 connection separately. For the rail journey out, confirm the train number, time and **동대구** rather than **대구** on your ticket.

For a museum-first visit, check closing and last admission in the [Daegu Modern History Museum guide](/blog/daegu-modern-history-museum-downtown-visit). For a market-first visit, use the [Seomun Market daytime food guide](/blog/seomun-market-daytime-food-visit) for the hours and ordering choices. Put the chosen stop and return train in your [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
