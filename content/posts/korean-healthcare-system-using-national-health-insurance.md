First check whether Korean National Health Insurance is active for you today. The [NHIS guide for foreigners](https://www.nhis.or.kr/english/wbheaa02900m01.do) describes three different routes. Ask what start date is recorded and keep the reply or certificate for reference.

| Route | Where to check |
| --- | --- |
| Workplace | Ask payroll when the company reported your enrollment and which pay period it covers. Recheck after starting or leaving a job. |
| Dependent | Ask NHIS what it needs to review eligibility and family documents, and whether the application is active. |
| Local | Ask NHIS how your residence status and time in Korea affect the start date, and check the notice or premium in your name. |

The six-month reference applies to the general rule for eligible local subscribers. Some residence statuses have different starting conditions. A short-stay visitor should check their travel insurer’s coverage, local contact and claim documents before relying on reimbursement.

## Check how the bill was calculated

[NHIS publishes a 2026 workplace health contribution rate](https://www.nhis.or.kr/english/wbheaa02500m01.do) of 7.19% of monthly average wage, generally split equally between employer and employee. Check long-term care and adjustments separately. The [2026 local-subscriber notice](https://edi.nhis.or.kr/portal/images/popup/20251204_pop01longdesc.html) uses a different calculation and may apply differently depending on your case. Ask NHIS to explain the category, dates and items on your own bill.

## Before you check in at a clinic

Ask whether the visit and expected items can be billed under your confirmed qualification, and what you would pay yourself. Bring your ID and any referral or documentation the intended clinic requests. If a notice is missing or your employer and NHIS records differ, use the [NHIS foreign resident center and English consultant details](https://www.nhis.or.kr/english/wbheaa02100m01.do) to confirm the next step. Use the [NHIS online document guide](/blog/navigating-the-national-health-insurance-nhis-website-2026) to look up your coverage history or premium payment record.
