Karrot (당근) is useful when you know the item you want and can arrange a handover. Start by checking whether you can register, complete the app’s current neighborhood verification and communicate with the seller; do not assume a short-term visitor can use every payment feature.

## Choose a handover

| Route | Before agreeing |
| --- | --- |
| Local | Ask for recent photos, dimensions, faults and a public meeting place. Confirm the price and time in the app. |
| Delivery | Check the shipping fee, recipient address, arrival window and payment protection in the actual listing. |
| Either | Avoid requests to leave the platform or transfer a deposit to an unfamiliar account. Inspect before paying where possible. |

[Karrot’s announcement](https://about.daangn.com/company/pr/archive/%EB%8B%B9%EA%B7%BC-%EB%B0%94%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC%EB%A7%A4-%EA%B1%B0%EB%9E%98-%EB%B2%94%EC%9C%84-%EC%A0%84%EA%B5%AD-%ED%99%95%EB%8C%80/) says eligible 바로구매 (Buy Now) shipped listings can appear in nationwide search, with a phased rollout; the home feed remains neighborhood-focused. Check what your account actually offers instead of assuming every listing ships nationwide.

For a face-to-face pickup, test the item if practical, carry only what you can transport and leave if the situation feels wrong. If browsing without arranging a meeting suits your trip better, try [Dongmyo’s vintage market](/blog/vintage-shopping-in-dongmyo-a-treasure-hunter-guide-2026).
