**Plan around the traveler's actual needs, not an age label.** Choose one main stop, a comfortable way there and a reliable place to rest. A short itinerary can still involve a steep street, long interchange or inaccessible restroom, so check each leg separately.

| Part of the day | Ask before booking |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel room | Is the route from entrance to room step-free, and does the bathroom fit the traveler? |
| Station or stop | Which exit and elevator connect the street to the correct platform? |
| Attraction | What is the route from entrance to the area you actually want to see? |
| Meal and rest | Are seats, a suitable restroom and a return ride available when needed? |

## Build one manageable outing

Choose a main venue and a nearby meal or rest stop. Put the hotel entrance, station exit, attraction entrance and return point on a map. Ask the operator about the walking surface, slopes, steps, elevator outages and the distance between those points. Travel time depends on the traveler and conditions; use your own pace and leave room to shorten the outing.

The Korea Tourism Organization's [accessible-tourism information guide](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/main/intro01.do) describes searchable features including accessible entrances, elevators, restrooms and wheelchair rental. Use a listing to prepare specific questions, then confirm current access and equipment with the venue. A wheelchair symbol does not prove the complete route suits every traveler.

## Check the details that matter

For example, the tourism organization's [Wando Tower listing](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/ms/detail.do?cotId=121334f8-5822-41e4-9231-ad4800ead5d0) lists an elevator and accessible restroom but also describes the approach ramp as steep. That is a reason to ask about assistance and the actual entrance, not a promise of an easy route. Confirm opening hours and the day’s conditions before making a special trip.

Ask whether a wheelchair or other mobility aid can be reserved, whether a companion is needed and where the aid is collected. At a hotel, request the actual doorway, bed and bathroom details that matter to your group, preferably in writing before paying. Discounts and free admission can depend on nationality, documentation and venue; check the named operator's rules rather than assuming every visitor over a particular age qualifies.

## Keep a return option

Save the hotel address and a contact offline. Arrange a return pickup point that the traveler can reach, and keep the option to skip the next stop if energy or weather changes. For a specific facility en route, use the [restroom planning guide](/blog/finding-public-restrooms-in-korea-station-mall-and-accessible-facilities) and confirm the current access with staff.
