Eunpyeong Hanok Village (은평한옥마을) offers mountain views and residential lanes; the nearby history museum adds an indoor stop. Choose a museum opening day if the weather is cold, and allow time for a bus after the subway.

## Choose your approach

| Subway | Continue by bus |
| --- | --- |
| Yeonsinnae (연신내역), lines 3 and 6 | Exit 3; [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Eunpyeong-History-Hanok-Museum/ENP44b0tv) describes 701 or 7211 toward 하나고·진관사·삼천사입구. |
| Gupabal (구파발역), line 3 | Exit 4; Visit Seoul describes 7723 to the same stop. |

Check a current local map and bus arrival information before leaving; routes can change. For the return, verify the correct side and bus destination at the stop.

## See the neighborhood respectfully

Walk the public lanes without entering private homes or peering over walls. The mountain backdrop and tiled roofs are the draw; photographs are best taken without disturbing residents. If conditions are icy, shorten the outdoor portion rather than attempting steep or slick lanes.

The **Eunpyeong History Hanok Museum (은평역사한옥박물관)** at **서울특별시 은평구 연서로50길 8** explains local history and hanok construction. The [museum operator](https://museum.ep.go.kr/) lists 09:00–18:00 and last admission at 17:00. Check Monday, public-holiday and substitute-day closures. Verify [museum notices](https://museum.ep.go.kr/) before setting off, including any exhibit closures and fees. On a closed day, make it a short outdoor visit or choose another confirmed indoor stop.

Save **은평한옥마을** and the museum, then check your return bus before you leave the subway.
