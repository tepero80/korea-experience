## Read four lines on the box or seller page

Weverse Shop gives a specific example of an edition that uses NFC and lists its photo-card assortment. The label matters if you are buying a gift for someone who expects an audio CD. The retailer’s separate promotional notice is an example of a bonus with its own eligibility and availability.

| Line to read | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Version | Full version name and whether the listing is a selected copy, a random copy or multiple editions. |
| Playback | CD, NFC, code or another method supported by the recipient’s device. |
| Inclusions | Number of items supplied and the pool they may be drawn from. |
| Bonus | Seller, purchase window, eligible product and any stock limit. |

## Check a sealed or opened copy

At a store, compare the label with your saved listing. If the copy is open, have the seller list exactly which cards and inserts are included. For a gift, confirm whether the music can be accessed by the recipient and whether a single code has already been redeemed.

Pack the album where the corners will not be crushed. Keep the receipt and order details if you purchased a store benefit. See [K-pop goods purchase options](/blog/k-pop-goods-without-memberships-2026) for how to check common sale conditions.
