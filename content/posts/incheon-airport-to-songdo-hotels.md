**Take airport bus 6777 or 6777-1 when it stops close enough to your hotel and leaves well after you expect to clear arrivals.** For a late arrival, a party with large bags or a long last walk, check the official Incheon-bound taxi queue. If both train legs are still running, the all-stop Airport Railroad with a transfer at Gyeyang gives you a rail option.

This guide covers the public arrivals area at Incheon International Airport and hotels in **Songdo International City (송도국제도시)**. Use your hotel's full Korean address and lobby entrance. Airport route information checked **23 November 2026**.

## Match the bus to the hotel entrance

The [airport's Bus Search](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1504/subview.do) lists both coaches under **Incheon**. Open the route's stop sequence and map the walk from the actual stop to your hotel. Compare distance, crossings, luggage and time. A stop named for a tower, park or station covers the surrounding area.

| Where you need to be | What to check first |
| --- | --- |
| POSCO Tower, Sheraton or Oakwood | The airport lists **Posco Tower & Sheraton Incheon (Oakwood Hotel)** for both routes. Confirm your hotel's actual entrance from the stop. |
| Central Park subway station, International Business District or Moonlight Festival Park | **6777** lists those stations after Incheon Startup Park. Check the station pin against your reservation. |
| Knowledge Information Complex, Techno Park or Campus Town | **6777-1** lists those stations. Use its stop list for east-side destinations. |

**Central Park** can mean a park-side bus stop or a subway-station area. This matters for hotels on different sides of the water. The airport's 6777 listing names **Central Park Station (Overseas Koreans Agency)**. The [T1 timetable](https://www.airportbus.or.kr/bus/schedule.php?area=incheon) distinguishes a park stop from that station stop. Ask the counter which stop fits your address and follow the departure board.

## Board at the terminal where you land

| Arrival terminal | Where to go | Last listed airport departures when checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Terminal 1** | Arrivals **1F**, follow the bus signs toward stop **2**. The [T1 timetable](https://www.airportbus.or.kr/bus/schedule.php?area=incheon) specifies **2B-4** for both routes. Confirm the bay on your ticket or the display. | **6777: 22:00. 6777-1: 21:30.** |
| **Terminal 2** | Transportation Center **B1**, bus stop **17** for both routes in the airport lookup. Check your departure on the B1 board. | **6777: 21:40. 6777-1: 21:10.** |

These are the [airport timetable's](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1504/subview.do) listed departures from each terminal, checked on 23 November 2026. Check your date, direction, seat availability and any replacement bay before relying on them. The lookup listed **₩8,000 per passenger one way** for both routes. Verify the actual fare at purchase. Leave time after the flight for immigration, bags and the walk to the bay. With a large suitcase or special gear, ask the ticket counter about luggage capacity and handling for the specific departure.

The airport lists [bus ticket counters at T1](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1502/subview.do) and the [counter and kiosks at T2 B1](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1503/subview.do). Show the hotel address and ask **이 버스가 이 주소에서 가장 가까운 정류장에 서나요?** — Does this bus stop at the nearest stop to this address?

## If you miss the coach

**Taxi:** Follow the designated airport taxi signs and ask for the **Incheon-bound** queue. Show **송도, 인천광역시 연수구** with your hotel's address. Ask about the meter, any overnight surcharge or toll, and whether the vehicle fits your group and bags. The [airport's taxi guide](https://airinfo.airport.kr/ap_en/1514/subview.do) has the current ranks, vehicle types and tariff information. Let the hotel know if you may reach the lobby after its late check-in cutoff.

**Rail:** Take the **all-stop AREX (일반열차)** to **Gyeyang (계양)**. Transfer to **Incheon Subway Line 1** toward **Songdo Moonlight Festival Park (송도달빛축제공원)** and get off at the appropriate Songdo station. The [Gyeyang transfer guide](https://www.ictr.or.kr/main/railway/guidance/mobile_gy.jsp) shows the station's lift routes. Check the [subway operator's first and last trains](https://www.ictr.or.kr/main/railway/guidance/timetable1_se.jsp) and the [airport train information](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1512/subview.do) together. Check both legs, the transfer, and the final hotel walk before starting. If the connection is too close, arrange the taxi from arrivals.

**Before the flight home:** Check the airport-bound stop on the [Incheon bus information system](https://bus.incheon.go.kr/bis/main.view). A near-identical stop name can refer to the opposite direction. Ask your hotel to help verify the curb, departure and payment method. Leave room for a backup ride and recheck the departure terminal with your airline.
