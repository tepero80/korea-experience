## Identify the route on your offer

Ask the employer to name the sponsoring institution, the E-2 subtype it expects and the person who will supply the documents. For an EPIK post, the [program’s visa guidance](https://www.epik.go.kr/web/epik/contents/Eligibility) lists a contract and a Notice of Appointment and directs you to the Korean mission for any additional requirements. Ask the program coordinator when those documents will be available after placement.

For a private-academy post, ask whether your employer will obtain a visa issuance confirmation in Korea and what it needs from you to start. [Article 9 of Korea’s Immigration Act](https://elaw.klri.re.kr/eng_mobile/viewer.do?hseq=70896&key=7&type=part) allows the inviting party to apply on the applicant’s behalf for that certificate before visa issuance. If the employer gives you a confirmation number, check the instructions for using it to apply at your mission. Keep the employer’s confirmation and the visa decision as separate steps in your notes.

| Step | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Employer handoff | Who applies in Korea if that step is needed, which documents they need and when they can send the reference or appointment packet. |
| Filing jurisdiction | Which embassy or consulate serves where you can apply and what proof of residence it accepts if relevant. |
| Format and submission | Which form, originals or copies the mission requires, whether it takes mail or appointments and how to pay the fee. |
| Travel and start | When the mission expects to finish and whether the employer can move your start or orientation date if your documents are delayed. |

## Get document instructions before ordering anything

For each item the sponsor or mission requests, record the exact issuer, the accepted original or copy, any authentication or translation and the accepted issue date. If they ask for a degree certificate or background check, ask them to name the accepted issuing authority and any apostille or other authentication required for that document. Use the instructions for your own application before buying a service or mailing originals.

Check that the passport name and number match the contract and application packet. If you renew your passport or find conflicting instructions, get the visa office’s directions before submitting a second form. If the employer asks you to post an original, confirm the recipient and whether the item will be returned.

## Confirm the decision before committing to a flight

The [Korea Visa Portal status page](https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?MENU_ID=10301) offers separate lookups for confirmation of visa issuance and applications filed with a mission. Use the option that matches the stage you are checking. Follow the issuing mission’s directions for obtaining the visa decision and verify the name, passport details and dates shown before committing to a flight or confirming a start date. If there is a delay, send the employer the new timeline and request a revised start date in writing.

If you are already in Korea and need to change status or employer, use the [guide to confirming a status change in Korea](/blog/changing-visa-status-while-in-korea) before assuming the overseas filing sequence applies. After visa issuance, use the [first-week checklist](/blog/moving-to-korea-checklist-everything-you-need-2026) to coordinate housing, arrival and appointments.
