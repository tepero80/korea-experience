Plan Dadaepo as a walk around wide sand and views toward the Nakdong estuary. From central Busan, take [Metro Line 1](https://www.humetro.busan.kr/english/main.do) to **다대포해수욕장역**. The port stop **다대포항역** is separate. [Busan City](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/beaches/1326147) gives Exit 2 and roughly four minutes to the beach under ordinary walking conditions.

| Your visit | How to plan it |
| --- | --- |
| Daylight shore walk | Follow the open signed approach, then pick an out-and-back stretch. Leave time for the walk to the metro. |
| Sunset | Check sunset, wind and the return train in advance. If daylight is short, choose a turnaround near the approach. |
| Fountain or swimming | Treat these as separate plans. Check the organizer’s current fountain schedule. For swimming, confirm a staffed designated zone and follow local restrictions. |

## Make the return easy

Save **다대포해수욕장** at **부산광역시 사하구 몰운대1길 14** as your beach destination. On the way out, save **다대포해수욕장역** as the return point. One is the shore area and the other is the metro station. Check for signed closures and ask about surfaces if using a stroller or mobility aid. Protect the train return rather than relying on a particular sunset or fountain event.

If the weather is poor, compare [F1963 in Mangmi](/blog/f1963-from-mangmi-station) as a separate outing or see the [Busan destination guide](/destinations/busan) for an indoor alternative.
