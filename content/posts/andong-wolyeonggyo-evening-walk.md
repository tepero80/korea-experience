**From central Andong, take bus 112 to Wolyeonggyo Bridge or use a taxi to the bank where you want to start.** Check your return before setting out. For a short evening walk, go as far as the mid-bridge pavilion if conditions are comfortable, then retrace your steps to the same bank.

## Choose an approach

| Starting point | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **City center** | The [city tourism guide](https://new.tourandong.com/public/sub5/sub1_2.cshtml) directs visitors to bus **112** near **구 안동역**, the former downtown station. Verify the current street stop and bridge-bound direction. |
| **Rail or coach hub** | The city also lists **112** for the dam area from the present station and terminal. Confirm that the selected service lists **월영교** before boarding. |
| **After the bus** | Arrange a taxi if the return times do not fit. Save the pickup side and your lodging address, and agree on a pickup point a driver can reach. |

The [Andong city bus system](https://bus.andong.go.kr/route) shows stop sequences. The [city timetable notices](https://www.andong.go.kr/portal/bbs/list.do?mId=0606010100&ptIdx=66) are a separate check for changes. Save the return direction and the actual stop. Do not assume a late service runs because your outbound bus did.

## Make the crossing as short as you need

Save **월영교, 경상북도 안동시 상아동 569**. The [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=80957) lists the bridge as open around the clock. Check signs and any current restrictions when you arrive. Confirm lighting and any boat service separately.

If you come before dusk, notice which approach is closest to your return stop. Walk to the pavilion and back for the shortest version on the bridge, or cross the full deck and retrace the crossing before heading to your pickup point. In wet or icy conditions, or if the approach is difficult to see, stay on the more comfortable riverbank or skip the outdoor walk. The [city accessibility listing](https://www.tourandong.com/public/sub1/sub3_5.cshtml?day_idx=0&id=0&idx=4&keyword=&seq=1178) describes the bridge approach. Check the exact surfaces and route if step-free access matters.

## Keep a downtown fallback

If the weather or return connection makes the bridge impractical, choose a meal in [Andong Old Market](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=977&vcontsId=174447) and check individual shop hours. For an early train or coach, use the [station and terminal guide](/blog/andong-station-and-bus-terminal-to-downtown) so you return to the current transport hub. Save separate bridge and pickup pins on your [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary).
