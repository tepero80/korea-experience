Seoul Metropolitan Library occupies the former City Hall building beside the newer glass City Hall. Come on a Tuesday to Sunday for a quiet indoor stop, and check the room hours before choosing an evening visit.

## From City Hall Station

Take Line 1 or 2 to **City Hall (시청역)**. Exit 4 leads toward Seoul Plaza and the library at **서울특별시 중구 세종대로 110**. The old stone façade faces the square; the curved glass City Hall rises behind it. Check the station's current lift route if stairs are difficult.

## Choose a self-guided stop or a tour

| If you have… | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| A short break | Enter the library, follow its posted visitor rules and see the restored central staircase and available displays. |
| More time | Explore the Seoul and world collections, then check whether the former mayor’s office and city hall history displays are open. |
| A preference for a guide | Check the city's Tong-Tong Tour page for reservation and walk-in options, language and meeting instructions. |

The building is a working library. Keep voices low and ask staff before photographing reading areas. A visit does not imply borrowing privileges. The [city's library overview](https://english.seoul.go.kr/city-hall/overview-city-hall/3-seoul-metropolitan-library/) explains the building's transition from City Hall.

For more places to read and the distinction between reading and borrowing, [see free library options for visitors](/blog/public-library-services-for-foreigners-free-books-and-media-2026).

## Check the day

The [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=80168) lists Monday closures and different closing times by room. Its Seoul and world collections close earlier than some other rooms. Holidays and temporary notices can change access. The city's [tour information](https://english.seoul.go.kr/city-hall/overview-city-hall/4-tong-tong-tour/) also notes closures and varying language availability; confirm the current departure before relying on it. If the library is closed, check the opening of the [palace and art museum nearby](/blog/deoksugung-to-seoul-museum-of-art) or choose another [indoor stop](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets).

Before leaving, check [library information](https://lib.seoul.go.kr/) and save **서울도서관** in your map.
