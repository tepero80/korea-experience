**Start at Hall 1 for an overview of Mokpo as a port city. Choose Hall 2 first if your priority is life under colonial rule and you may only have time for one building.** The halls have separate street entrances and the short walk between them is outdoors. Add the harbor only when the weather and your next departure leave room.

| If you want… | Save this place |
| --- | --- |
| Port history | **목포근대역사관 1관**, **목포시 영산로29번길 6**. The former Japanese consulate traces the port’s opening and later changes. |
| Colonial history | **목포근대역사관 2관**, **목포시 번화로 18**. The former Oriental Development Company branch covers colonial-era Mokpo through photographs and exhibits. |
| Shelter or a rest | Pick the hall you most want to see and keep time for a sheltered break. Skip the harbor walk during heavy rain, strong wind or lightning. |

## Check the two entrances and last admission

Save the actual hall number before leaving **목포역 (Mokpo Station)**. Compare walking and a taxi to that address, especially if the pavement is wet or you have luggage. Confirm the approach and access between floors with the museum if you need a step-free route.

VisitKorea lists **17:00 last admission** and **17:30 closing** for both halls, with a usual Monday closure. The National Museum of Korean Contemporary History’s directory lists **18:00** closing. For a late arrival, aim to reach your chosen hall **before 17:00** and call ahead to confirm last entry, holiday closures and whether you need a separate ticket for each hall. The information page for the hall you picked has its contact number. [Hall 1 visitor information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=60768), [Hall 2 visitor information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=75183), [National museum directory](https://www.much.go.kr/cooperation/net/mok.do)

## Add the harbor when the weather clears

1. **Visit one hall before deciding on the second.** Read the displays on how the port changed under colonial rule. The buildings are part of that history, including how institutions extracted resources. [National Museum of Korean Contemporary History](https://www.much.go.kr/cooperation/net/mok.do)
2. **Keep the outdoor portion optional.** If visibility and footing improve, search **목포진 역사공원** for a short walk to a harbor viewpoint. Allow for the uphill approach and turn back at the time you set. If there are weather warnings, wait indoors or go straight to your next departure point. [Mokpo city’s guide to Mokpojin](https://www.mokpo.go.kr/toureng/attraction/nineplace/mokpojin)
3. **Work back from your train or ferry.** For a ferry delay, confirm the operator’s next update and your actual terminal before adding even a short visit. If a replacement sailing has an early check-in, return directly to the pier. [Find your correct Mokpo ferry terminal](/blog/mokpo-station-to-correct-ferry-terminal)

If conditions improve and you have a longer window, use the [Mokpo Marine Cable Car guide](/blog/mokpo-marine-cable-car-boarding-without-a-car) to choose a boarding station and check whether the service is running again.
