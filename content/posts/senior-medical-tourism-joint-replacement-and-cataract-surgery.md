## Prepare for the consult

Ask which clinician will assess you and whether they can review your existing results before an appointment. The [Medical Korea Information Center](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient) can help you find institutions and ask about booking. Ask the clinic which records to bring, how to send them securely, and whether you can have a support person or interpreter present.

| Visit | Questions for the clinic |
| --- | --- |
| Joint | Which previous reports or image files would help your assessment? Is there a step-free route to the exam room? If you recommend treatment, who would handle my mobility support and follow-up? |
| Eye | Which previous eye exam results would help your assessment? Can I get instructions in a format I can read? If you recommend treatment, which eye checks would I need before I leave? |

## Confirm the route door to door

Map each leg from your room to the clinic and back. Ask about the drop-off point, entrance steps, lifts, accessible toilets and the distance from the entrance to the waiting area. [Seoul’s subway facility tables](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/public-transportation/subway-accessibility-facilities/) list fixtures such as elevators and voice guidance by station. Confirm which exit you need and whether the equipment along your route is operating. The [accessible Seoul transport guide](/blog/accessible-seoul-best-routes-for-wheelchair-and-limited-mobility-2026) covers vehicle and station questions if you need a backup route.

## Get a follow-up plan and an itemized quote

Ask the treating clinician which visits need to happen in Korea, what would change the timing, and when you could take your planned route home. Confirm who will be available if you have to stay longer and who can take over your care at home. Ask about the price of assessment, interpretation, follow-up and any extra visit. Check cancellation or change terms before paying.

Ask what records the clinic can provide and when. [Seoul National University Hospital’s issuance guide](https://www.snuh.org/global/en/patients/EN02005.do), for example, explains how it handles records, medical images and itemized bills. Ask your own clinic which formats it offers, what they cost and how you can get a copy if you have already left.
