## Work backward from the one thing you cannot move

If you have a scheduled call, ask for a seat and confirm whether calls are allowed at that desk or need a separate room. [Behongli’s official page](https://www.behongli.com/) has a day-pass inquiry process. Confirm the exact address, access instructions and what the operator includes before relying on it.

| Task | Where to work |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed call | Confirm a room or a desk where calls are allowed and get the entry instructions in advance. |
| Quiet work | Ask whether the desk seat includes Wi-Fi and power and whether you can leave and return. |
| Short task | Choose a café that allows the time you need. Ask about seating and work calls if relevant. |
| Backup | Save what you can use off-line and confirm your own data or a second work spot. |

## Put the stop into your travel day

Choose a room near another part of the day, or leave a buffer for the actual transfer. Save confirmation and a way to contact the venue if the building is hard to find. If an operator has not replied, keep the meeting in a known space. Use the [first-trip plan](/blog/first-trip-to-korea-planning-guide) to consolidate reservations and avoid moving between distant neighborhoods for every task.
