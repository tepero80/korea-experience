## Confirm where the parcel can go

Ask the accommodation for the exact Korean address and a delivery name it recognizes. Find out whether staff can receive packages, where the driver should leave one, and whether the desk can hold it after checkout. Include your room or reservation details only as the property asks. For an unattended rental, check whether someone can reach an outside drop-off point.

| Check | Before paying |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Confirm the site accepts your details and chosen payment method. Stop if checkout asks for verification you cannot complete. |
| Arrival | Compare the date shown for your order with the last day you can collect it. Allow time for a late parcel. |
| Fees | Read the final delivery charges, any recurring membership terms and the return conditions for the item. |
| Backup | Save an in-person shop for a purchase you need on a fixed date. |

## If tracking and the front desk disagree

Open the order list or tracking screen, **주문목록/배송조회**, and compare the delivery address and status with what the accommodation can see. [Coupang’s delivery FAQ](https://news.coupang.com/archives/60687/) explains where to check estimates and how to report a recorded delivery you did not receive. For a return, verify your collection address before leaving it behind. If the timing or verification fails, use the [online shopping comparison](/blog/online-shopping-in-korea-coupang-vs-gmarket) to decide whether another checkout is worth trying or whether to buy in person.
