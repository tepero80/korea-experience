## Choose the kind of fragrance session

Visit Seoul lists Dillyscent as a workshop where visitors can create their own scent and points to its booking channel. Use that as a starting point. Check the workshop’s current packages, scheduling and language support for your date rather than assuming every booking includes the same experience.

| Before paying | Confirm with the venue |
| --- | --- |
| What you make | Whether you choose premixed options or blend materials yourself. |
| Price | Price per participant, bottle volume and whether packaging costs extra. |
| Timing | Exact address, arrival cutoff, end time and when the bottle can be collected. |
| Access | Instruction language, group size, access needs and whether one person can watch. |

## Bring two scent ideas

Tell the instructor one style you like and one you usually avoid. Compare a small set using separate labelled strips. Ask what your workshop will let you adjust before bottling. If fragrance exposure is a concern, ask about the room and whether a companion can wait outside.

Ask how to pack and carry the bottle before boarding. If you mainly want to compare ready-made scents, the [Korean fragrance boutiques guide](/blog/korean-fragrance-boutiques-how-to-sample-scents-and-find-official-brand-stores) is a useful next step without committing to a workshop booking.
