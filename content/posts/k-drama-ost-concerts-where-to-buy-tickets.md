## Search for the performance you want

Start with the drama’s Korean title plus OST 콘서트. If the results show a fan meeting or screening, open the program and check what will be performed. An archive on Interpark records an example of a drama OST concert. That past example cannot tell you who will appear at a different event.

| Listing detail | How to read it |
| --- | --- |
| Artist lineup | Check who is actually named and verify the final program against organizer updates. |
| Orchestra or tribute | The listing may describe an arrangement or interpretation. See which titles are confirmed in the program. |
| Fan meeting | Look for the advertised music portion. Book if the program itself interests you. |
| Ticket seller | Use the link from the actual venue or organizer and identify the correct date and performance. |

## Book around the event

Verify the hall name, the city, the performance time and any ticket pickup or identity rule before payment. Save the confirmation and check transit after the show. If the organizer does not identify performers or times, leave the evening flexible.
