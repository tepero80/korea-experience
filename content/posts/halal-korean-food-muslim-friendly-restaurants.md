## Read the category before choosing a dish

| Label | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Halal certified | Ask about the current certificate and which location and menu it covers. |
| Self-certified | Ask how the restaurant applies its own standard and decide whether it suits your needs. |
| Muslim-friendly | Ask which dishes qualify, and which ingredients or preparation are shared. |
| Pork-free | Ask about the remaining ingredients and preparations you need to check. |

Use the [KTO directory](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/br/JKToffice) for labels and the [VisitKorea visitor resources](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/MuslimFriendly) to find another branch or a prayer-room lead. A seafood or vegetable dish can still have other ingredients in its sauce or broth.

## An Itaewon lead to verify

The KTO directory lists **Eid (이드)** under its halal-certified category at **67 Usadan-ro 10-gil**. Call [070-8889-8210](tel:+827088898210) or check the branch’s current notice before planning around it.

For a certification question, show **현재 유효한 할랄 인증서를 볼 수 있을까요?** (“May I see the current halal certificate?”). Ask separately about alcohol or shared preparation if those are relevant to you. Keep a second meal option in the same neighborhood in case hours or availability change.

For a trip beyond Seoul, use a separately checked [Busan meal plan](/blog/busan-station-to-nampo-muslim-friendly-dining) or [Jeju City dining guide](/blog/jeju-muslim-friendly-dining-in-jeju-city).
