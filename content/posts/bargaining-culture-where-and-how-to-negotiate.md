At Hwanghak-dong Flea Market, [VISITKOREA’s market guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=222319) says some prices are fixed while many vendors may negotiate. This describes a flea market, not a rule for every Korean shop. Ask once and respect a no.

## Read the situation before asking

| Case | A useful next step |
| --- | --- |
| A secondhand item with no marked price | Ask the price first: 얼마예요? (eolmayeyo?, how much is it?). Examine condition before considering an offer. |
| Several items from one seller | Ask whether there is a combined price. Agree on exactly which items are included. |
| A marked price or busy food stall | Treat the displayed price as the starting expectation; accept it or move on. |

## Agree on the full purchase

If you want to ask for a discount, try 조금 깎아 주세요 (jogeum kkakka juseyo, please reduce it a little). There is no guaranteed discount. Confirm the total, payment method, condition and any packing costs before paying. Do not handle fragile goods without asking.

Hwanghak-dong’s individual store hours vary; use the tourism listing to check the address and current details. For other types of shopping, compare stops in our [Seoul districts guide](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam).
