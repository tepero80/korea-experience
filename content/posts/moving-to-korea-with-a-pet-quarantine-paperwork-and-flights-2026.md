## Check the animal documents before paying for the flight

[Incheon Airport’s arrival guidance](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1479/subview.do) asks people bringing a dog or cat for a quarantine certificate issued by the exporting country’s government agency. Check your origin, transit route and pet’s age with the veterinary export authority and the Korean quarantine office. Ask what microchip and rabies evidence must appear on or accompany the certificate.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Animal | Check that the pet’s identifying details match across the documents. |
| Route | Ask about transit, country-specific papers and which office can issue or endorse the certificate. |
| Carrier | Confirm an animal reservation, carrier rules and handover arrangements with every airline. |
| Arrival | Keep the certificate accessible and ask where to meet the animal quarantine officer before customs. |

## Plan the return route at the same time

[Incheon Airport’s Korean quarantine guide](https://www.airport.kr/ap_ko/901/subview.do) notes microchip and rabies antibody documentation to check for a return to Korea. Ask for written guidance if you will cross the border again, and keep copies of approvals and contacts. The airline booking and quarantine clearance are separate checks.

See the [Incheon Airport arrivals guide](/blog/incheon-airport-survival-guide-2026) to plan the onward ride after you and your pet have cleared the formalities.
