**Choose where you want to finish the day.** A beachfront evening and a short walk back from the old-port markets call for different bases. Busan is easier when you keep one side of the city together rather than crossing it repeatedly.

## Match the base to the trip

| Base | Choose it for… | Check before paying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nampo / Jagalchi | Old-port markets, food and browsing near each other | Exact hotel entrance, the return walk and Line 1 connection |
| Haeundae | Coastal mornings/evenings and an eastern-side focus | Distance from both beach and useful Line 2 station; a beach-area address does not guarantee a sea view |
| Seomyeon | A trip divided between old-port and eastern-coast days | The actual interchange/exit and hotel surroundings you prefer |
| Busan Station | An awkward booked rail arrival or early departure | Whether saving that train connection outweighs your repeated sightseeing journeys |

The [Busan rail operator's map](https://www2.humetro.busan.kr/homepage/cyberstation/mapeng.do) shows Busan Station, Nampo and Jagalchi on Line 1, Haeundae/Dongbaek on Line 2, and the Seomyeon interchange. It does not tell you the door-to-door time from a particular hotel.

## Picture the two sides of the trip

Around the old port, plan a market or meal and a manageable browsing block. The [Nampo underground shopping center](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=70897) lies between Jagalchi and Nampo stations. Its listed hours are 10:00–22:00, with second/fourth Tuesday closures; that does not make every surrounding route covered or every business open late.

On the eastern coast, keep Haeundae and Dongbaek together. [Dongbaekseom's tourism listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=81541) describes a free coastal walking area at 67 Dongbaek-ro. Separate facilities, exposed paths and weather need their own checks. Do not assume a 24-hour park listing includes every building.

## Test one real day before booking

1. Put the hotel entrance, first anchor and evening meal in your local map.
2. Check the useful station exit, any changes, slopes/steps and the final walk with bags.
3. Add the actual arrival/departure station and time. An early train can change the best last-night choice.
4. Read room-level occupancy, cancellation and check-in conditions; ask for unanswered details in writing.

No whole district is guaranteed cheap, quiet, flat or accessible. For a family, [room capacity and bedding](/blog/booking-family-hotel-rooms-in-korea) may matter more than a neighborhood label.

Use the [two-day Busan route](/blog/seoul-to-busan-weekend-planner-train-neighborhood-base-and-route-choices) to check the base against your sightseeing clusters. If arrival and check-in do not align, plan [bag storage and retrieval](/blog/luggage-storage-in-seoul-and-incheon-airport) before adding another distant stop.
