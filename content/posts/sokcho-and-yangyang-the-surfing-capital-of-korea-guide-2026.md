Choose **Yangyang** for the surf lesson or beach outing and **Sokcho** if you also want a market, harbor or mountain day. Plan the ride to the specific lesson meeting spot and back before paying. The exact beach matters even when two beaches are on the same stretch of coast.

| Plan | What makes it work |
| --- | --- |
| Book a first lesson | Ask what equipment is included, where to meet, how to handle changing conditions and whether you can get back to your base if the lesson ends late. |
| Compare Ingu and Jukdo | [VisitKorea’s Ingu listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=112680) places Ingu south of the Jukdo headland and Jukdo Beach north of it. Save the one your operator confirms. |
| Spend time ashore | Visit the shore and cafés only where currently open, with a ride back reserved or confirmed. Use a separate Sokcho plan if exposed beach weather spoils the outing. |

## Use the right beach name and current local advice

Save **인구해변** at **강원특별자치도 양양군 현남면 인구항길 12** for Ingu, or ask your provider for the precise meeting address and pickup instructions at **죽도해변**. The [Yangyang County visitor listing](https://tour.yangyang.go.kr/pub/yy10view.do?mode=v&seq=128) identifies Ingu and its nearby surf schools. The county’s [surfing overview](https://tour.yangyang.go.kr/pub/funsurfing.do) describes the different beaches. Neither replaces day-of instructions. Ask the instructor about current conditions and heed official closures and signs at the water.
