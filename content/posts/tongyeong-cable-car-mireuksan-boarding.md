The Tongyeong Cable Car rises from the lower station on Mireukdo toward Mireuksan. Choose a clear-weather window if views are your priority, and check the operator's latest notice before setting out: operating hours and maintenance days change, and poor weather can affect service. The ride itself is not a promise of a clear view.

## Before you go

| Decision | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| View is the priority | Check wind, cloud and the operating notice before going to Mireukdo. |
| Only time for the ride | Ask about the last return and keep the summit walk optional. |
| Service suspended | Switch to a mainland outing that suits the weather. |

The [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=77581) describes a 1,975-metre cableway and a ride of about nine minutes. The upper station is not the same thing as the mountain summit: allow extra time on foot if you plan to continue, and turn back if steps or the weather do not suit your group. Wear shoes suitable for an outdoor walk and carry an extra layer.

The [Tongyeong Tourism Development Corporation](https://litt.ly/tong0tour) publishes monthly hours and maintenance notices and cautions that weekend hours may change. Its main [cable-car site](https://cablecar.ttdc.kr) carries operating information; if it is unavailable, call **1544-3303**. Check the current fare, last ticket time, expected queue and whether service is running before leaving. For wheelchair, stroller or pet boarding, ask the operator about your specific equipment and the conditions on the upper-station route.

## Find the lower station

Search **통영케이블카**, 205 Balgae-ro, Tongyeong-si (경상남도 통영시 발개로 205). [KTO's attraction listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=77581) identifies parking at the station. Confirm a live bus or taxi route from your actual starting point; the cable car is not at the intercity bus terminal or Tongyeong passenger ferry terminal.

At the lower station, check the departure status and ticket options, then follow staff directions to board. Keep your return ticket or other boarding confirmation as instructed. The gondola is only one part of a day in Tongyeong: if it is closed, the [harbour and Dongpirang](/blog/tongyeong-dongpirang-gangguan-walk) offer a different outing, though they too are exposed to the weather.

## After the ride

Do not plan a tight ferry connection immediately after the cable car. Allow for the return gondola, any queue and a separate transfer to the ferry terminal. For an overnight stay, compare accommodation around the harbour with the Mireukdo area based on the next morning's departure point; confirm location and terms with the property before booking.
