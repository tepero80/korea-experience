Use Jungang Market to orient yourself, then choose a restaurant near Gangguan Harbor. The [tourism authority’s oyster guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221355) describes multiple oyster preparations in this area. On a short stop, a bowl of soup can be simpler to order than a large set.

## Read the preparation on the menu

| Dish | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| 굴국밥 | Oyster and rice soup. Ask how the rice is served and whether the broth is spicy. |
| 굴전 | Oyster pancake. Check portion size and batter ingredients. |
| 굴찜 | Steamed oysters. Check whether the menu price is per serving or a shared plate. |
| 굴 정식 | Oyster set. Ask which dishes are served raw and whether you can request cooked alternatives. |

The [CDC recommends avoiding raw or undercooked oysters](https://www.cdc.gov/vibrio/prevention/index.html) to reduce the risk of Vibrio infection. **생굴은 빼 주세요** asks staff to leave out raw oysters. Check that the alternatives are cooked thoroughly. If staff cannot confirm what the set includes, order one clearly identified cooked dish or choose another meal.

## Put the meal on a harbor walk

The [official Jungang Market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=77738) places it near both Gangguan Port and Dongpirang. Save **통영 중앙시장 · 경남 통영시 중앙시장1길 14-16**. Check your restaurant’s hours and last order instead of relying on the market’s hours. If the restaurant has a wait, keep a second nearby choice and leave enough time to catch your bus or ferry.
