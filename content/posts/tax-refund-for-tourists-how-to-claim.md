Before splitting a purchase or choosing a refund option, ask the participating store which scheme applies and what identification it needs. Save the documents with the goods and allow time at departure; a refund estimate is not cash already received.

## Checked thresholds, not a guaranteed refund

The official tourism guide lists a minimum qualifying purchase of **₩15,000**. For its immediate-refund arrangement, a single payment must be **below ₩1,000,000**, with qualifying trip purchases totaling **at most ₩5,000,000**. Eligibility, participating seller and scheme still matter. Goods must be new/unused and exported within the stated three-month purchase period; retain passport/refund documents and be ready for inspection before checking relevant goods. Verify your transaction against the current guide: [Duty Free and Tax Refunds — KTO](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140736).

## Check your eligibility first

The KTO guide describes qualifying short-stay nonresident visitors exporting new, unused goods within three months. Residency and time spent in Korea matter. Confirm purchase-date rules with the seller or refund operator rather than treating every shopping receipt as refundable.

## Compare before you commit

| Stage | Keep ready | Verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checkout | Passport | Participating seller and operator |
| Before bag drop | Goods and receipts | Is inspection required? |
| After security | Passport and approved paperwork | Correct refund counter |
| After departure | Reference and receipts | Operator processing period |

This comparison organizes the decision. It is not a guarantee of availability or an individual price quote.

## Identify the refund route

Immediate refund, deferred airport refund and downtown refund are different processes. Ask which operator issued the receipt and what remains to be done at departure. Do not claim twice or assume a downtown payout removes export verification.

**Check the operator:** [Duty Free and Tax Refunds — KTO](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140736) · [Incheon Airport prohibited/restricted baggage items](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1433/subview.do).

## Understand the limits and final amount

KTO lists a KRW15,000 minimum and immediate-refund limits below KRW1,000,000 per payment and up to KRW5,000,000 total trip spending. Confirm current limits at purchase. Fees and eligibility mean the refund is not automatically ten percent.

## Keep a small refund folder

Keep passport, refund receipts, operator references and payment details together. Mark which purchases were processed immediately and which still need departure action. Keep goods available for inspection rather than packing them beyond reach.

## Check before airline bag drop

Confirm whether customs needs to inspect goods in checked baggage before handing the bag over. Follow current airport and operator instructions for the correct order. Terminal counters, hours and services can change.

## Follow up through the operator

If a counter is closed or payment is delayed, use the documented submission method and keep reference numbers. Ask the operator about its processing period. Do not upload passport photos or card details to public comments.

## Before you go

- Confirm visitor eligibility
- Identify one refund route
- Record the operator
- Keep goods unused where required
- Check inspection before bag drop
- Retain reference numbers

Save the current operator notice and your own booking conditions offline. Recheck any dated information before paying or traveling.
