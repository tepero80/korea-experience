**Save the Chuncheon name before you travel.** Myeongdong Dakgalbi Street is a restaurant alley in central Chuncheon. Use the full Korean pin to distinguish it from Myeongdong in Seoul. Choose a restaurant after checking its current menu and opening hours; the alley itself does not have one shared menu or minimum order.

## Find the right Myeongdong

[Chuncheon's tourism portal](https://www.chuncheon.go.kr/tour/destination/all-tour/detail/?tourId=231) locates **춘천 명동 닭갈비골목** at **강원특별자치도 춘천시 명동길 2** and describes it as one of Chuncheon's dakgalbi streets. [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=108005) identifies the alley around Joyang-dong and notes that individual restaurant hours vary.

There are other dakgalbi areas in Chuncheon. [Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=904&vcontsId=221263) distinguishes the Myeongdong/Geumgang-ro area from restaurants around Namchuncheon Station intersection and the Soyang Dam area. A restaurant's “Chuncheon dakgalbi” sign alone does not put it in this alley.

## Plan the station-to-restaurant leg

If arriving by rail, check your actual train and station through [KORAIL's reservation page](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/ticket/reservation). Chuncheon Station (춘천역) and Namchuncheon Station (남춘천역) are different stops. Enter the station on your ticket and the restaurant's exact address into a current map for walking, bus or taxi options. Allow for the return to your booked station; do not rely on a single fixed walk time or fare for every train and restaurant.

The [city listing](https://www.chuncheon.go.kr/tour/destination/all-tour/detail/?tourId=231) describes the alley as an open area, while the tourism organization's listing says vendor hours vary. Check the particular restaurant's opening and last-order times, especially if connecting to a fixed train. Nearby [Chuncheon Myeongdong Street](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=107995) provides a separate downtown browsing option if you have time before or after the meal.

## Choose and order

Korea Tourism Organization describes both iron-pan and charcoal-grilled dakgalbi in the alley, as well as makguksu buckwheat noodles. Menus and preparation differ by shop. Look at the current menu and ask the price per serving, minimum order, spice level and any charges for extras before sitting down. Use the phrase above to ask about a single portion.

If you want to finish with fried rice, check the portion and price before adding it. Ask staff when the chicken is fully cooked and follow their instructions for the pan. If you have an allergy or dietary restriction, explain it directly and ask the restaurant about ingredients and shared equipment; chicken does not establish halal certification or a particular allergen-free preparation.

## Make a comfortable return

Keep your departure station and train confirmation handy. Give the meal and return leg the time your party needs, and shorten the optional shopping stop if service is slower than expected.
