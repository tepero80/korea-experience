## Get the specification before calling a shop

Ask your treating team to write down the garment type it recommends for your case and who should make or check the measurements. [Queen Victoria Hospital’s pressure garment service](https://www.qvh.nhs.uk/our-services/support-services/pressure-garment-service/) illustrates a setting that selects style and measures patients individually. Use the instructions from your own team when booking a fitting in Seoul.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Clinic | Ask whether the clinic supplies the item and who can check the fit. If another provider supplies it, ask how to confirm its specifications with the clinic. |
| Stock | Ask the supplier whether it can provide that exact item or arrange a fitting. Confirm whether an appointment is needed and when it can be collected. |
| Fees | Get the price and ask whether fitting, alterations or a replacement are charged separately. Ask which items may be exchanged and under what conditions. |
| Paper | Keep the written specification, supplier’s contact, item label and receipt. Ask whether another fitting will be available before you travel. |

## Ask the care team about a fit concern

If what the shop can supply differs from the written recommendation, call the prescribing team before buying. Ask for a point of contact if the fit concerns you after collection or if the supplier cannot get the required item in time.
