Save **짜장면박물관 · 인천광역시 제물포구 차이나타운로 56-14** for the museum. The [tourism listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=69981) describes the old Gonghwachun building and its recreated kitchen and exhibits about delivery. [Incheon’s account of the dish](https://eng-itour.incheon.go.kr/cmn/board/BBSMSTR_000000000080/3725bbsDetail.do) connects Korean jajangmyeon to Chinese diaspora cooking in the port city and says the person behind the dish is unknown. The museum tells that history and does not serve the meal.

## Put the museum and meal in the right order

| Your situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The museum is open | Walk from Incheon Station through the Chinatown gateway. Check the [foundation’s museum listing](https://www.jcf.or.kr/main/space/museum4.jsp) and visit before last admission, then choose a restaurant. |
| It is Monday or too late | Check the holiday exception and notices. If the museum is closed, keep the exterior and an open noodle shop as separate stops. |
| You only have time for lunch | Search for **짜장면** in Chinatown, check the restaurant’s own menu and save its exact address. |

The signs **짜장면** and **자장면** both point to the black bean noodle dish. Ask if the sauce contains pork if you need to avoid it. If you order a combination with another dish, confirm whether the posted price is a set or for each plate. Follow the [Chinatown and Open Port walk](/blog/incheon-chinatown-open-port-walking-route) for the station exit, steep sections and an optional Art Platform stop.
