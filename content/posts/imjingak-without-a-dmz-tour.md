**You can plan an independent visit to Imjingak's public park and memorial area in Paju without booking a restricted-area DMZ tour.** For a public visit, put Pyeonghwa Nuri's grounds and the memorial area on your plan. Check transport back first, and treat the gondola or a restricted-area sightseeing route as separate visits.

## Choose what you actually want to see

The [Korea Tourism Organization listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=107397) describes the public resort, park and memorials. Its [official area map](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/event/2025/PajuGondola_5/enu/leaflet_enu.pdf) distinguishes Pyeonghwa Nuri and Imjingak Pavilion from the Civilian Control Line and the gondola's other station.

| Your goal | What to plan |
| --- | --- |
| **A park and memorial visit** | Use the named public grounds. Walk the Hill of Wind and the public memorial area as time and weather allow. |
| **A specific exhibit, bridge walkway or gondola** | Check that attraction's own opening, ticket and identity requirements. Stay on the signed public approach until staff confirm access. |
| **The Third Tunnel or another controlled site** | Check the current tour and identity requirements. Park access does not include these sites. |

## Save two pins and one return option

Start at **Imjingak Resort (임진각 관광지)**, **경기도 파주시 문산읍 임진각로 177**. For the park side, search **Pyeonghwa Nuri (임진각 평화누리)**, **경기도 파주시 문산읍 임진각로 148-40**. Both are in the same visitor area. Use the [Naver Map search for the park](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%9E%84%EC%A7%84%EA%B0%81%20%ED%8F%89%ED%99%94%EB%88%84%EB%A6%AC) ([Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%9E%84%EC%A7%84%EA%B0%81%20%ED%8F%89%ED%99%94%EB%88%84%EB%A6%AC) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%9E%84%EC%A7%84%EA%B0%81%20%ED%8F%89%ED%99%94%EB%88%84%EB%A6%AC%20%EA%B2%BD%EA%B8%B0%20%ED%8C%8C%EC%A3%BC%EC%8B%9C%20%EB%AC%B8%EC%82%B0%EC%9D%8D%20%EC%9E%84%EC%A7%84%EA%B0%81%EB%A1%9C%20148-40)) and read the on-site signs if you are dropped near a different part of the complex. The [Gyeonggi Tourism Organization's park guide](https://www.ggtour.or.kr/gto/notice/news/e3d05a40-b471-4432-b3a1-a50d8a7bc493?type=NOTI_REPORT) uses the park address and identifies the pinwheel-covered **Hill of Wind (바람의 언덕)**.

For a trip from Seoul, search for the connection to **Munsan Station (문산역)** first. Paju City lists a possible local bus connection with **058A or 058B** toward Imjingak and a train connection to **Imjingang Station (임진강역)** in its [transport directions](https://tour.paju.go.kr/tour/festival/festival02/festival02_1/festival_02_1_tab1_1/festival_02_1_tab1_1_01.jsp). Use these as routes to check on your actual date. Confirm the stop beside the park and a realistic return departure before boarding. If a return train or bus is impractical, arrange a taxi back to Munsan before leaving town or pick a shorter outing.

## Time the park around your last connection

Pick one priority before you go. If you have a short window, choose the Hill of Wind or the public memorial area. In strong wind or rain, check an indoor venue's current opening before depending on it. Keep enough time to walk back to the right departure point. If an extra attraction would put your return at risk, leave it for another day.

If the Third Tunnel is the real reason you are heading north, use the [DMZ tour planning guide](/blog/dmz-tour-from-seoul-complete-guide-and-what-to-expect-2026) separately. If an easy return matters more, keep the day in the [Seoul itinerary](/blog/seoul-3-day-itinerary-perfect-first-time-visitor-guide-2026) and choose a stop near a planned metro connection.
