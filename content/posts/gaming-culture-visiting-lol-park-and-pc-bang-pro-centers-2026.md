## Choose the activity first

Visit Seoul lists LoL Park’s spectator arena and a separate PC café. An arena ticket depends on what is actually scheduled. A PC café gives you computer time and can have its own registration and game access rules. Confirm the exact place you intend to use rather than assuming both parts of the visit are included.

| If you want to | Do this first |
| --- | --- |
| See an LCK match | Find the fixture and the ticket link in the official notice for that match. Check the required ticket and ID before traveling. |
| Play a particular game | Ask the café whether that title is installed and how a visitor can log in with their own account. |
| Look around without a match | Ask which public areas are open that day and save a separate afternoon activity. |
| Go with children | Ask about the venue’s age rules, the session time and whether the group can sit together. |

## Fit the stop into a day in Seoul

For a match, save the venue, gate, seat and return transport before your evening starts. See the [live esports ticket and venue guide](/blog/korean-esports-attending-live-gaming-tournaments) before paying for a ticket.

For a flexible playing session, check the [PC bang arrival and payment guide](/blog/pc-bang-culture-korean-gaming-cafes) before buying computer time. Ask the branch about the payment, visitor ID and game login. Bring your own account details and check the login works before buying extra time. Avoid storing passwords or a card on a shared computer and sign out when you finish.
