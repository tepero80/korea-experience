Choose Itaewon’s Antiques Street when you want to browse individual dealers. Use an actual brand locator if your trip is for a particular new item or authorized seller. [VISITKOREA’s neighborhood listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=90739) identifies **이태원 앤틱가구거리** and **서울 용산구 보광로 109-1** as a starting point.

## Choose the route by the purchase

| Goal | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Browse antiques or decor | Ask which dealers are open, and inspect the item, its condition and the description on the receipt. |
| Buy everyday clothing | Save the specific shop and ask about fitting or size. Give shopping time to that stop first. |
| Buy branded goods | Find the exact seller through the maker if it publishes a local directory. Keep purchase evidence. |

## Before buying something bulky

Measure the item and ask what packing will add to its dimensions and weight. Compare the cost to carry or send it before paying. A small decorative object and a piece of furniture call for very different return plans. Ask who arranges delivery and which terms the seller will put in writing.

For a branded purchase, our [seller and authenticity checklist](/blog/korean-counterfeit-products-how-to-spot-fakes) explains what to confirm before you rely on a receipt or packaging.
