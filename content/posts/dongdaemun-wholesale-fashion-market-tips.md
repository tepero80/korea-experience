**Buying one outfit for yourself? Start at Doota Mall or Hyundai City Outlet Dongdaemun.** Visit the apM wholesale buildings when you have a specific seller or order in mind. Confirm that the seller will take your order before planning a late trip.

## Choose the right kind of shop

| Your plan | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| **One item** | Doota Mall or Hyundai City Outlet. Check the exact shop, size, fitting and return terms. |
| **An order** | Pick the apM building with your seller. Confirm minimum quantity, payment and pickup. |
| **Design visit** | Start at Dongdaemun Design Plaza. Check the program and any separate reservation. |

## Retail shops for one outfit

[Doota Mall’s operator](https://www.doota-mall.com/m/en/customer/location.do) lists **10:30 to midnight** at **275 Jangchungdan-ro**. Individual shops and food outlets can close earlier. The [official Seoul visitor listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/dongdaemunarea/Doota-Dongdaemun/ENP000416) describes local designer fashion and larger brands in the same building. Check the shop you want before arriving late.

[Hyundai City Outlet Dongdaemun](https://home.ehyundai.com/mobile/DP/en/branch.do?branchCd=B00173000&branchType=30&lanType=EN) lists regular mall hours of **10:30–21:00 Monday–Thursday** and **10:30–21:30 Friday–Sunday** at **20 Jangchungdan-ro 13-gil**. Use the operator’s current-day notice for exceptions. The outlet gives Dongdaemun Station **Exit 8** and Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station **Exit 14** as starting points for its own building.

## apM wholesale hours and addresses

The [apM operator FAQ](https://www.apmstyle.com/en/faq/) lists all three buildings as opening at **20:00 on Sunday through Thursday** and closing at **05:00 the following morning**. They **do not open Friday or Saturday night**. Thursday’s session can therefore continue into Friday morning. Check the operator’s notices for holidays and temporary closures.

- [apM Place](https://apmstyle.com/en/malls/place/), **276-1 Eulji-ro (을지로 276-1)** as listed by the operator. Its focus is wholesale women’s fashion.
- [apM](https://apmstyle.com/en/malls/apm/), **40 Majang-ro (마장로 40)**. Use the full building name when meeting a seller.
- [apM Luxe](https://apmstyle.com/en/malls/luxe/), **51 Toegye-ro 73-gil (퇴계로73길 51)**. Save the shop name and floor as well as the building address.

The [apM buying guide](https://www.apmzoom.com/buying-guide/apm-buildings) describes the three as wholesale markets. Its FAQ says each shop sets its own minimum on the buying platform. Ask your seller about the policy for an **in-person** purchase, including whether it sells a single item and which payment methods it accepts.

## Confirm the order before you go

1. **Save the right shop.** Get its Korean name, building, floor and stall number. Send a photo or item reference when asking about stock.
2. **Ask how the minimum works.** Ask whether it is per style, color or size. “Can I buy one?” is **한 장만 살 수 있나요?**. Check fitting, pickup and any exchanges before payment.
3. **Confirm how to pay and collect.** Ask which payment methods the seller accepts for your order and when it will be ready. Save the itemized receipt and pickup instructions. If the shop requests a larger order than you need, switch to retail.

## Plan your evening and the trip back

For a short visit, start at [Dongdaemun Design Plaza](https://english.visitseoul.net/dongdaemunarea/Dongdaemun-Design-Plaza-en/ENP024679) for a show or the architecture, then pick one retail mall. Check the exhibition’s schedule separately from the mall’s hours. On a Friday or Saturday night, check Doota’s own shops for a retail option. If the apM seller confirms an order on a different night, make that pickup the only fixed shopping stop.

Before staying out late, check your last subway connection to your accommodation. Seoul’s [night bus information and TOPIS link](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-expands-citywide-night-bus-service/) can help you find an alternative. Check the stop direction and live service for your date. Keep your accommodation’s Korean address on your phone if you need a taxi.
