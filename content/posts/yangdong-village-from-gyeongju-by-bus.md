Start your return plan before you board for Yangdong. Gyeongju City lists bus 203 and two similarly named stops. For the village visit, look for **양동민속마을** on your actual trip, then choose a short walk through the lower lanes or a longer outing along marked uphill paths.

## Choosing the stop and the trip back

| Decision | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Bus to the village | Look for **양동민속마을** and confirm the direction on route 203. |
| Map shows **양동마을입구** | Compare it with **양동민속마을**. The city lists them as separate stops. |
| Taxi there and back | Save **경주시 강동면 양동마을길 138-18** for the visitor listing and agree a pickup point before walking farther into the village. |
| Same-day train onward | Set a village departure time that accounts for the ride back, the transfer and your actual rail station. |

Follow [Gyeongju City’s route 203 guide](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=4751) for stop names, then look up both directions in the [city’s live transport system](https://its.gyeongju.go.kr/). Some stops with similar names are farther from the visitor area. If you are starting from the KTX station, plan the connection separately using the [Gyeongju station guide](/blog/gyeongju-ktx-to-hwangnidan). Do not budget your return around a presumed bus frequency.

## Choose a walk around the time you actually have

Start at the visitor area and check the local map. The lower public lanes give you a view of tiled roofs, thatched houses and their surrounding fields without making a longer uphill walk the goal. If your group has time, suitable footing and a confirmed return, add a marked uphill branch and return by the signed public paths. Some lane surfaces are uneven or sloped. Ask about your chosen route if stairs, a stroller or a mobility aid matter.

[UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1324/) includes Yangdong and Andong’s Hahoe Village in one World Heritage listing. Yangdong is still a residential village. Stick to signed visitor areas, give residents room, and leave closed gates and house interiors alone unless permission is clear. A photo of a historic house does not mean that house is open to visitors.

The [city visitor page](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?area_uid=106&cmd=2&code_uid=1010&mnu_uid=4720) lists standard adult admission at ₩4,000 per person. Check current prices, opening and last entry for your date. The map below is an overview of the village. Use the stop name or the visitor address for the final approach.

If the return is too tight or heavy rain makes the uphill lanes a poor fit, consider the [Gyeongju National Museum](/blog/gyeongju-national-museum-silla-and-wolji-galleries) instead. If this is part of a longer heritage trip, compare the [independent transport plan for Hahoe](/blog/andong-hahoe-or-dosan-seowon-without-a-car) before booking your next base.
