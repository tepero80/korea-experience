Oil Tank Culture Park (문화비축기지) occupies a former fuel depot near Seoul World Cup Stadium. The outdoor grounds make a good architecture stop in dry weather. If seeing the inside of a tank is your main reason to go, plan around an announced programme or tour.

## From World Cup Stadium Station

Take Line 6 to **월드컵경기장역**. The [park operator](https://parks.seoul.go.kr/content.do?key=2604300014) lists Exits 2 and 3 and an approximate 10-minute walk. Save **서울특별시 마포구 증산로 87** and follow the signs to **문화비축기지**. The map pin identifies the park, so check the station lift and park entrance separately if you need a step-free route.

| What you want to do | How to plan it |
| --- | --- |
| Look at the tanks from outside | Start at the open yard and follow the signs to any accessible exterior viewpoints. Follow posted closures. |
| Go inside a tank | Use the park's current programme and verify which tank your visit covers. Seoul says the tanks no longer open for casual visits on a regular schedule. |
| Take a shorter visit | Pick one or two exterior features near your entry point and check whether the current café opening fits your plan. |
| Visit in cold rain | Choose an [indoor alternative](/blog/seoul-rainy-day-itinerary-built-around-museums-markets-and-covered-streets) unless you have confirmed interior access and are comfortable with the walks between buildings. |

## Check a guided visit separately

Seoul's listing links from the park page to its public reservation site and identifies the information building as the audio-guide pickup point. It lists audio guides in English, Japanese and Chinese as well as Korean. Check the current loan hours, availability and guided-tour language before going. Conditions can vary by programme. The [park operator](https://parks.seoul.go.kr/content.do?key=2604300014) also publishes the accessibility facilities and contact numbers for the grounds and tanks.

If you are making several stops in western Seoul, save the park entrance and the Line 6 return leg in the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
