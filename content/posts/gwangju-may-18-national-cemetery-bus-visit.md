| Starting point | Check before boarding |
| --- | --- |
| Culture Complex or Asia Culture Center | Compare the live stop and direction with the [cemetery operator’s bus 518 route](https://518.mpva.go.kr/518/contents.do?key=444). It lists this downtown area and the cemetery. |
| Gwangju Station or the bus terminal | Both appear in the operator’s route listing. Confirm the stop in your direction. Gwangju-Songjeong is a different rail station. |
| Limited time or an unclear return | Compare a taxi to Minju-ro 200 and check how to leave while you still have time. |

## Allow time and follow the cemetery’s guidance

The [operator](https://518.mpva.go.kr/518/contents.do?key=1795) lists **09:00–18:00** and asks visitors to arrive by **17:00**. Walk quietly among the graves, observe posted rules and make space for people attending a ceremony. If continuing to an indoor venue, plan it as a separate stop from the [Gwangju destination guide](/destinations/gwangju).
