**From Busan Station, take city bus 17, 88, or 101 from the stop opposite the Kyowon Building near Metro Line 1 Exit 7.** Get off at **태종대** or **태종대온천** and follow the signs to the park entrance. [Busan City’s directions](https://www.busan.go.kr/park/greencitybusan04) allow about 40 minutes for the ride. Before leaving the station, check the city bus back and decide what you will do if the park’s internal shuttle is unavailable.

## Get from the rail hall to the right stop

Start with **부산역**, 부산광역시 동구 중앙대로 206, if you arrive by KTX. The **7** in the park’s instructions refers to **Busan Station on Metro Line 1**, near the street, so follow city metro signs from the railway terminal. At street level, match the stop opposite **교원빌딩** (Kyowon Building) and verify the bus direction on the pole or in your map app. The [city’s transit page](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/public-transportation) links to its Korean bus information service. If you need a lift route, use the [metro station’s facility information](https://www2.humetro.busan.kr/homepage/default/stationinfo/page/list01.do?menu_no=1001010201&S_LINE=1&S_STATION=113) and check the crossing separately.

| Step | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Boarding | Your bus shows **17**, **88**, or **101**, with **태종대** or **태종대온천** ahead on the stop list. |
| On Yeongdo | At the Taejongdae stop, save the route to **태종대유원지** and check the station-bound stop for your return. |
| Starting in Nampo | Compare a direct bus from across from Lotte Department Store near Nampo Station Exit 6. Busan City lists **8, 17, 30, 88, or 186** from there. |

If the stop labels are hard to match, show the driver **이 버스 태종대 가나요?** (“Does this bus go to Taejongdae?”). In your maps app, save the **park** at **부산광역시 영도구 전망로 24** as well as the bus stop. The stop’s spa name is one label used for the arrival area.

## Decide how far to go inside the park

Allow a separate block for the walk or ride inside the park. Busan City lists free park entry. Allow for the city bus fare both ways and check any paid activity you plan to add. The [Busan Tourism Association notice](https://bta.or.kr/noticeDetail?keyword=&no=1637&page=1&st=) records the Danubi road train suspension from July 3, 2026 and a temporary free circuit bus. It describes Tuesday through Sunday operation and first-come tickets at the Danubi ticket office. Call **051-405-8745** or ask staff at the entrance for the current vehicle, last boarding and access before planning a ride.

If you are on foot, choose one main viewpoint and turn back while you have daylight and a workable bus home. The [city’s visitor guide](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=258) describes stairs to the lighthouse below the observatory. If stairs are a problem, aim for a viewpoint you can reach and check the surface, gradient and return route with staff before continuing.

## Protect the ride back

Use live departures from the stop where you will actually board. Add time for the walk back from your last viewpoint, road traffic and finding your train platform. If daylight or weather makes the coast a poor choice, consider the [Busan Modern & Contemporary History Museum](/blog/busan-modern-contemporary-history-museum-visit) in the Nampo area and check its opening before leaving the station. If your plan still includes Taejongdae, save both bus stops and your train time in the [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary).
