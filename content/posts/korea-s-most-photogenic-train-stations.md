Choose the photo you want before you choose a rail ticket. Jeongdongjin works for a coastal stop, Seoul 284 for a historic station building, and Gyeonghwa for a spring rail heritage scene. They have different access rules and last-mile plans.

## Compare the kind of station scene you want

| Scene | Where to start and what to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Coast** | **Jeongdongjin** is an active railway stop. Start with the station exterior or nearby beach. Check your train and return before going. |
| **Old hall** | **Seoul 284** uses the historic Seoul Station building. Start outdoors, then check the program if you want to go inside. |
| **Spring** | **Gyeonghwa** is a heritage site in Jinhae. Plan a local bus or a walk and check blossom updates. Passenger trains do not stop here. |

## Jeongdongjin: plan a photo from a public route

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s Jeongdongjin listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1591437) describes the beach directly in front of the station. Use [Korail’s timetable and booking site](https://www.korail.com/global/eng/main) for your exact travel date. Our [station-to-beach guide](/blog/jeongdongjin-station-to-beach-year-round) separates the station exit, shoreline and nearby park.

The photograph above is from July 2017. Use it to see the seafront setting. Staff and signs determine where you can stand today. Stay on permitted public paths and ask before bringing equipment onto a platform.

## Seoul 284: verify the exhibition before making a detour

[Culture Station Seoul 284’s visitor page](https://www.seoul284.org/cms/content/view/250) provides access information for the historic building. Use its [current program](https://www.seoul284.org/main) to see whether an exhibition is open. Use the frontage as a fallback and ask staff about photography inside. If you have a passenger train to catch, allow time to reach the correct modern rail terminal.

## Gyeonghwa: go for the heritage stop, not a train

[VISITKOREA lists Gyeonghwa](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=&vcontsId=81715) as a former stop in Jinhae with blossom scenery and no passenger service. Check the current blossom notices and local transport for your date. Follow local barriers and pedestrian directions. For more context, compare the approaches in the [Jinhae blossom guide](/blog/cherry-blossom-season-in-korea-when-and-where-2026).
