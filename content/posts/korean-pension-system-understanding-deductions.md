## Find the pension line and its contribution month

Look for **국민연금** on your payslip. Record the deduction, the pay period and the employer’s name. Ask payroll which contribution month the deduction covers, especially if you just started, changed employers or received an adjustment.

The [National Pension Service contribution guide](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/cntb/getOHAI0013M0.do?menuId=MN24001366) calculates contributions from **기준소득월액**, the standard monthly income recorded for pension purposes. NPS uses a reported income figure and rounds it down to the nearest ₩1,000. Payroll can tell you the base it used for the month you are checking.

| What to compare | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 rate | You pay 4.75% of the base and your employer pays 4.75%. Together they pay 9.5%. |
| 2027 rate | You and your employer each pay 5%. Together you pay 10%. |
| Pay base | For July 2026 through June 2027, NPS lists a floor of ₩410,000 and a ceiling of ₩6,590,000. The band follows its own July-to-June schedule. |

For a **₩3,000,000 pension base**, the employee share is **₩142,500 for a 2026 contribution month**. With the same base, the 2027 rate gives **₩150,000**. Open the **취득 및 상실신고** tab of the [NPS workplace guide](https://www.nps.or.kr/pnsinfo/ntpsklg/getOHAF0016M0.do) for the annual schedule. Ask payroll about any adjustment before treating the difference as a mistake.

## Check whether you were enrolled

The [NPS coverage guide for foreign residents](https://www.nps.or.kr/eng/ntnlpnsplan/frgnrlpsmrfnd/getOHAI0015M0.do) explains how country, visa status and workplace affect enrollment. Some workers on a temporary assignment may be exempt under a social security agreement after providing a certificate of coverage. If payroll shows no deduction and you expected one, ask what status was reported to NPS and how it was determined.

1. **Ask payroll for the recorded base and dates.** Try saying 국민연금 기준소득월액과 가입 시작일을 확인해 주세요. That means “Please confirm my pension income base and enrollment start date.”
2. **Compare with the NPS enrollment history.** Use the [NPS instructions for requesting an enrollment history statement](https://m.nps.or.kr/elctcvlcpt/comm/getOHAC0000M5.do?menuId=MN24001055) or contact an NPS branch. Check which employer and months appear. Keep your payslips and employment dates for comparison.
3. **Resolve any discrepancy while you can still reach payroll.** If a month appears missing, give NPS and payroll the specific employer, month and deduction amount. Ask whether it is still being processed, whether a correction is needed and who can submit it.

If you are finishing work and planning to leave Korea, take the verified dates to NPS and [check which pension refund route could apply](/blog/korean-retirement-system-for-foreigners-national-pension-refunds-2026).
