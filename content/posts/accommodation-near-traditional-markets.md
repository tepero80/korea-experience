## Check the room and route separately

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s Namdaemun listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=264&menuSn=929&vcontsId=249970) identifies a visitor lounge at Gate 2 with luggage storage and booking assistance. Confirm the current hours, capacity and terms. Keep a separate plan if you will reach the area after it closes.

| Check | Ask or verify |
| --- | --- |
| Room | Is this room over a busy street, or on a quieter side of the building? |
| Bags | Does the hotel store bags on arrival and departure day? What are its hours? |
| Door | Which street entrance works with luggage, and is there an elevator from that level? |
| After dark | Will the route from the station or taxi drop-off still be practical after stalls close? |

## Compare one nearby alternative

Keep a second accommodation option closer to the station or on a different street. Ask both properties the same questions before comparing the rate. If the market is one meal stop instead of a repeated part of the trip, use the [Seoul neighborhood chooser](/blog/seoul-neighborhood-chooser-for-first-time-visitors-where-to-stay-by-trip-style) to pick a base around your daily route.
