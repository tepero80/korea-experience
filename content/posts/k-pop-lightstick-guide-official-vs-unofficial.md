An official light stick may be designed for a particular artist and show system. A similar-looking unofficial item is not proof of compatibility, and an official older version may not be supported for every effect.

## Decide before buying

| Check | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Seller | Follow the artist or organizer's link to an authorized shop. |
| Model | Match the exact version to the event notice. |
| Use | Check pairing instructions, batteries and permitted items. |
| Cost | Check the total, delivery timing and return terms. |

For example, [Weverse's ENHYPEN notice](https://shop.weverse.io/en/shop/USD/artists/10/notices/8463) says that after its specified 2025 Japan concerts, only Ver.2 would receive wireless stage synchronization at future concerts. This is an artist-specific example, not a guarantee for another event. Verify the current notice for your own show and model.

A [TXT showcase notice](https://shop.weverse.io/zh-tw/shop/JPY/artists/3/notices/14809) recommended an official light stick but did not make it mandatory for that event; other events may have different rules. Check whether your event permits unofficial illuminated items, whether pairing is needed, and which batteries the product requires. Do not rely on a photo or low price alone as proof of authenticity. For the ticket itself, see the [concert ticketing guide](/blog/how-to-get-k-pop-concert-tickets-ticketing-guide).
