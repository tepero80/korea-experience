## Choose one coast and a way back

| Starting area | Short ride |
| --- | --- |
| Hamdeok, Jeju | Choose a staffed hire shop near the beach and return the bike there. Turn back if the wind or a road crossing feels beyond your group. |
| Gyeongpo | In Gangneung, select an open coastal section from the official map. Walk the waterfront if the rental arrangement or road conditions are unsuitable. |

For a longer ride, the [Jeju Circular Bicycle Path](https://bike.go.kr/bbs/engList.do?bbsCtgrySn=30&key=2009110748529) is 234 km in full. The official map recommends counterclockwise travel and flags city stretches with more traffic. The [East Coast Bicycle Path, Gangwon](https://www.bike.go.kr/bbs/road.do?bbsCtgrySn=8&bbsCtgrySubSn=54&key=2006176977436) is 242 km in full. It uses local roads and shared stretches, with a marked detour between Jeongdongjin and Mukho.

## Before paying for a bike

1. Ask the shop which route it recommends for your group today. Confirm the return shop and the latest time the bike is due back.
2. Check fit, brakes, lights and a helmet before riding away. Ask what the price includes and how to contact the shop if the bike fails.
3. For a one-way ride, ask the exact transport operator what bicycle size and packing it accepts on that departure. If you cannot confirm carriage or a one-way hire, choose an out-and-back ride.
4. Save the official map for the area. Set a turn-back time and check the return leg separately. The official distances describe full routes, not a beginner’s day plan.

If Jeju’s wind or a closure makes riding unsuitable, keep the [Hamdeok bus plan](/blog/jeju-airport-to-hamdeok-beach-by-bus). In Gangneung, use the [Gyeongpo walking route](/blog/gangneung-gyeongpo-lake-and-beach-walk) and skip any segment with unsafe footing.
