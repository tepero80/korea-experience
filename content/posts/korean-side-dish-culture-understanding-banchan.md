At a market, **반찬** means side dishes you can buy on their own. Pick a stall offering prepared food, ask how a portion is sold and buy only what you plan to eat. The payment and portions here work differently from side dishes included with a restaurant meal.

## Match the market to your day

| Stop | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Mangwon Market, Seoul | Read the [neighborhood listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=229713) and save **망원시장 · 서울 마포구 포은로6길 27**. Look for a stall selling side dishes for takeout. |
| Motgol Market, Suwon | The [market listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=459&vcontsId=68834) describes side-dish shops. Search **못골종합시장 · 경기 수원시 팔달구 수원천로258번길 10-12** if you are already near the Nammun markets. |

Show **조금만 포장해 주세요** to ask for a small amount to take away. Ask whether the price is per container or by weight and whether the seller can label the container. If ingredients matter, name the specific ingredient or show it in writing. A vegetable dish can still have a seafood-based seasoning or shared preparation. Ask for storage instructions and skip any item you cannot carry as directed.

If your goal is to understand what a restaurant puts on the table, use the [restaurant banchan guide](/blog/korean-banchan-side-dishes-complete-guide-2026). In Suwon, continue with the [Nammun market lunch route](/blog/suwon-nammun-market-lunch-walk).
