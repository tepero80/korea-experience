**Choose the return journey before choosing the view.** Somuuido offers a walk reached by road and a pedestrian bridge. Deokjeokdo requires a ferry, with a separate return sailing to confirm. Pick one island for the day; do not build a plan that depends on doing both.

| Trip | Plan your return |
| --- | --- |
| Bridge walk | **Somuuido (소무의도).** Confirm your bus or driving route back and the current conditions on the walk. |
| Ferry outing | **Deokjeokdo (덕적도).** Check both sailings, your landing pier, local transport and the operator’s latest status. |

## Somuuido: choose a walk and a return bus

[Incheon's island portal](https://isum.incheon.go.kr/isleInfo.do?isle=201&key=2407020003) describes a pedestrian bridge connecting Somuuido with Muuido and an approximately 2.5 km Muui Badanuri trail. Its transport information lists a connection from Incheon Airport Terminal 1 to the Muui 1 local bus. Use that as a starting point, then check the current route, boarding stop and last suitable return for your own date. The airport connection is not itself a walk to the bridge.

Search **소무의인도교** for the pedestrian bridge and save your chosen return stop before starting. Allow for the walk back to the bus or car. The portal's trail description does not establish that every section is step-free or suitable for every mobility need; check the route and current conditions before committing to a full circuit. Wind, rain or a tight bus connection are reasons to shorten the outing.

[Incheon City's island guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/IC010601/2224073) also identifies the road bridge to Muuido and the pedestrian bridge onward to Somuuido. Bridge access means this outing does not require an island ferry; it does not guarantee a particular bus frequency or journey time.

## Deokjeokdo: build the day around the boat

For a different kind of day, start with [Deokjeokdo's official directions](https://www.foresttrip.go.kr/pot/fi/dr/selectDrctnsDtlView.do?hmpgId=ID02030127&menuId=002007): the Incheon coastal route reaches Jin-ri pier, where the page describes a village bus toward Seopori and the recreation forest. It says the bus runs in connection with ferry arrivals; verify the actual service and return locally rather than assuming a bus will match your chosen activity.

Before booking, check **both** the outward and return sailings and the time needed to get back to the pier. The [Incheon Port Authority](https://www.icpa.or.kr/icferry/article/view.do?articleKey=2557&boardKey=8&currentPageNo=1&menuKey=965) says weather, tides and vessel circumstances can change sailings. If a same-day return is essential, decide in advance what you will do if it is canceled; an outbound sailing is not a guarantee of the return. Our [ferry booking and boarding guide](/blog/ferry-services-to-korean-islands-complete-schedule) explains the ticket, identification and day-of-operation checks.

Choose one modest island activity after the sailings and local transport are confirmed. Keep the ferry terminal, your accommodation and the carrier's contact details available offline, and return to the pier with the margin required by the operator.
