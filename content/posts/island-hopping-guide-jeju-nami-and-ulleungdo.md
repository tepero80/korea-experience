Start with where you will sleep, how you will reach the island and how much flexibility your return needs. Nami works as a river-island outing from Seoul. Jeju works better with its own base. Ulleungdo is a separate sea crossing you need to schedule around.

| Where | Simple plan |
| --- | --- |
| Nami (남이섬) | Go via Gapyeong Wharf. Confirm the ferry back and leave time for your mainland train, bus or shuttle. |
| Jeju (제주도) | Pick one overnight base and group your stops. Check that an early opening has a matching bus or arrange another ride. |
| Ulleungdo (울릉도) | Choose your operator and port. Book the return before rooms or onward tickets and give yourself time for a delay. |

## Nami: make the last mainland connection

The [Nami Island visitor directions](https://namisum-en.imweb.me/164) show how to reach Gapyeong Wharf (남이섬 선착장), and the operator publishes a [separate ferry schedule](https://namisum-en.imweb.me/165). The wharf address is **1024 Bukhangangbyeon-ro, Gapyeong-eup, Gapyeong-gun** (경기도 가평군 가평읍 북한강변로 1024). Use the wharf address when arranging a car. Check the last island-to-wharf departure that still leaves time for the mainland journey. The [operator's zip-wire FAQ](https://namisum-en.imweb.me/35/?bmode=view&idx=7167730) explains the return ferry for zip-wire visitors.

## Jeju: choose a base before marking sights

[Visit Jeju's bus guidance](https://m.visitjeju.net/en/themtour/view?contentsid=CNTS_200000000012534&menuId=DOM_000001832000000000) identifies transit hubs including Jeju and Seogwipo bus terminals. You can plan a Jeju trip without renting a car by choosing a base and grouping stops on a workable bus route. Give yourself a return option before adding another stop. Use the [Jeju three-day itinerary](/blog/jeju-island-complete-travel-guide-best-time-to-visit-2026) to choose the base, and add [a ferry from Jeju to a smaller island](/blog/jeju-winter-ferry-checklist-udo-gapa-marado) only after checking its separate timings and conditions.

## Ulleungdo: verify the actual terminal and two sailings

As one example, [Ulleung Cruise's route description](https://www.ulcruise.co.kr/www/aboutus/intro_ship) connects Pohang Yeongilman with Ulleungdo's Sadong port. Use the [operator's embarkation directions](https://www.ulcruise.co.kr/www/center/come_way) and the port listed on your booking rather than navigating to a generic Pohang ferry terminal. Check both directions through the [operator's sailing notices](https://www.ulcruise.co.kr/). An operator can suspend service for vessel inspections or other conditions. Keep departure, landing port, lodging and the next mainland leg together in your trip plan.

## Pick the plan you can complete

If you are tied to one day from Seoul, start by checking the Nami return. If you can change your base, choose a Jeju itinerary around where you are staying. Save Ulleungdo for a plan that can absorb a changed sailing. For a domestic passenger ferry with an exact date and route already in mind, use the [booking and boarding checklist](/blog/ferry-services-to-korean-islands-complete-schedule).
