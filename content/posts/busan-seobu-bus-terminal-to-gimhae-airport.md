**From Busan Seobu Bus Terminal in Sasang, follow signs for the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail and board toward Gaya University (Samgye).** Airport Station is the third stop. Check your airline terminal before leaving the station and leave time for the walk, check-in and security.

## Start from the terminal on your coach ticket

Look for **부산서부시외버스터미널** or **부산서부 / 사상**, at **부산 사상구 사상로 201**. [Busan’s transport directory](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/public-transportation) places Seobu at Sasang and the central bus terminal at **Nopo (노포)**. If your ticket says Nopo or Busan Central, use that terminal as the starting point in your airport route search.

From Seobu, follow signs for **부산김해경전철** and **사상(서부터미널)**. Sasang appears on more than one rail service. At a sign showing several choices, follow the Busan–Gimhae name. [The airport rail guide](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=3&MENU_ID=110) identifies Line 2 as the connection at Sasang. If you cannot find the right platform, show staff **공항역 가는 부산김해경전철이 어디예요?** (Where is the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail to Airport Station?).

## Board toward Gaya University

Open the [light rail operator’s Sasang page](https://www.bglrt.com/00011/00149.web?scode=901) and select **가야대(삼계)행** for the departure list and first and last train tables. The station sequence on that route is **괘법르네시떼**, **서부산유통지구**, then **공항**. Verify your departure and check for service changes on the travel day. At the fare gates, use a transit card or a ticket the operator accepts.

If you depend on a lift, the operator lists facilities under **편의시설** on each station page. Check the route from the street to the platform and from the airport platform to the terminal. A listing alone cannot confirm a lift is in service.

## Walk to the correct passenger terminal

Get off at **공항역**, **부산 강서구 공항진입로 165**. The [airport’s Korean directions](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhae/index.do) place the station across the crossing from domestic Gate 1. Follow live signs to **국내선** for Domestic or **국제선** for International. These are separate passenger buildings. If walking between the terminals is impractical, check the airport’s [terminal shuttle and boarding points](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=2&MENU_ID=110) before relying on it.

| Flight buffer | Start with your airline’s check-in or bag-drop deadline. Allow for the terminal walk and airport procedures. |
| Coach | Verify the next rail departure after allowing time for the coach to arrive and unload. |
| Plan B | If the coach is late or rail timing is too tight, compare a taxi and show the driver **김해국제공항 국내선** or **김해국제공항 국제선**. |

Check which airport building your airline uses if your itinerary contains both a domestic and an international segment. Match the terminal to the flight you actually board at Gimhae. Write down the latest time you can leave Seobu based on your flight and the route options you actually have. If you will miss that time, choose the backup while you can still change plans. Before travel, save your bus arrival, the rail stations and your flight deadline in the [itinerary planner](/tools/itinerary). If you need the reverse trip later, use the [Gimhae Airport to Busan guide](/blog/gimhae-airport-to-busan) to choose a transfer for your hotel.
