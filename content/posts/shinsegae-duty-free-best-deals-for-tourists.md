Before paying for a Shinsegae Duty Free order, check where you will collect it and how that location fits your actual departure. Open the [Shinsegae Duty Free homepage](https://www.shinsegaedf.com/store/en/main) and choose Myeongdong, Incheon International Airport or Online. If your order requires airport collection, use the confirmation to locate your pickup point. The shop where you browse and the counter where you collect may be different places.

## Check collection before comparing prices

| Check | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Airport and terminal | The collection location for your order, its operating window and how to reach it after your other departure checks. |
| Your records | Make sure the receipt or confirmation matches your passport and flight. Keep it accessible with your boarding pass. |
| Time and changes | Ask how to update a flight or terminal change. Read cancellation and missed-collection terms before leaving the purchase to the last minute. |

## Match the offer to the trip

If you see a promotion, compare the final price for the same item after confirming it applies to you. Include the time for pickup in the decision. An earlier flight, a terminal change or a separate airline deadline can make a lower price less convenient.

When considering purchases to bring home, check the customs rules for the place you are entering. See the [Korea duty-free steps guide](/blog/duty-free-shopping-in-seoul-best-prices-and-process) for how to work the order and pickup into your departure.
