**The [Seoul Tourism Organization lists](https://english.visitseoul.net/eventsx/2026SeoulLanternFestival/ENPi0rhml) the 2026 Seoul Lantern Festival for December 11, 2026–January 31, 2027, daily 18:00–23:00, along Cheonggyecheon from Cheonggye Plaza to Samilgyo Bridge. Admission is free.** The official page lists Gwanghwamun Station Exit 5 as 146 m from the venue. Check the organizer's notice again for your date because this is a seasonal event, not a permanent nightly display.

## Choose your visit window

| If you have… | Plan around… |
| --- | --- |
| A short evening | Pick one end of the listed festival span and follow the event signs for the displays open that night. Avoid assuming that a single entrance lets you see every installation. |
| More time | Set a return point before you start, then use the official event map or staff directions to decide whether to continue farther along the stream. |
| A fixed dinner or show | Leave a buffer for walking and crowds, and check the venue's current notice before relying on a precise finish time. |

The official venue description gives the festival span as **Cheonggye Plaza–Samilgyo Bridge**. Follow the current display map and signs to see which installations are open and decide how far to walk.

## Before you leave

1. Recheck the date, nightly hours and event notices on the [Seoul Tourism Organization's 2026 festival page](https://english.visitseoul.net/eventsx/2026SeoulLanternFestival/ENPi0rhml).
2. Choose the subway stop and meeting point that match the end of the stream you want to visit. The official page lists Gwanghwamun Station Exit 5 near the venue; check a live route from your own starting point.
3. Keep the evening flexible. Use current transit information for the return trip, and follow on-site signs if the walking direction or access changes.
4. Dress for a long outdoor stop in winter. The display is outdoors; the event page does not promise shelter or a weather-proof route.

The [Korea Tourism Organization's festival listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=90101) also lists the event dates, hours, free admission and the Cheonggye Plaza–Samilgyo span. For the organizer's live program and notices, use the official site linked from the Seoul Tourism Organization page.
