## Ask who will explain the anesthesia plan

Confirm which appointment includes the anesthesia discussion and whether an interpreter can join. [Cambridge University Hospitals’ preparation guidance](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/anaesthetic-and-pre-operative-information/) describes discussing medicines, allergies and other health information before anesthesia. Share your own current information and keep the written instructions issued by the team that will treat you.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | Ask which approach the team proposes, who will explain the options and how it handles questions before consent. |
| List | Give the team your medicines, supplements, allergies and relevant medical history. Bring the names and current doses in a format the team can review. |
| Time | Ask for individual instructions on food, drink and medicines, plus the arrival time and department. Confirm who to call if plans change. |
| Leave | Ask about discharge arrangements, whether someone must accompany you and who will answer your questions afterwards. |

## Confirm what to do if something changes

The Cambridge preparation guide also describes contacting its pre-assessment service if you feel unwell near the appointment. If you become unwell, receive a new prescription or get inconsistent instructions, contact your own treating team before following any schedule you previously saved.

See the [medication preparation checklist](/blog/pre-surgery-checklist-medications-to-avoid) to put your medicines and prescribing contacts into a format the team can use.
