Traveling alone can still include company. Plan around places and activities you would enjoy independently, so you can leave a group or change course without losing the day. No area or event guarantees safety.

## A flexible daytime start

Start at the [Seoul Tourism Plaza information center](https://english.visitseoul.net/nature/Tourist-Information-Center/ENP037583) in Jongno for a map and current advice, then choose a nearby public walk and a meal. The center lists multilingual information and holiday closures; check its current hours first. [Solo dining ideas](/blog/best-spots-for-solo-dining-hon-bap-in-busy-districts-2026) can fill the next stop.

| Before joining a group | Practical check |
| --- | --- |
| Organizer | Check a named organization, recent event details and cancellation terms. |
| Meeting | Choose a public, staffed location in daylight; confirm the exact point. |
| Return | Keep your own fare, charged phone and route to your accommodation. |
| Comfort | Tell someone you trust your plan if useful; leave if pressured or uncomfortable. |

For travel information or interpretation, the [1330 helpline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=454&vcontsId=140632) offers phone and chat. For immediate police help call **112**; for fire or emergency response call **119**, as listed in [KTO emergency guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145). If you need help, move toward a staffed public place and share your location.
