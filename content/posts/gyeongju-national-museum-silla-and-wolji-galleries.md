**Choose the Silla History Gallery for an introduction to Silla, or the Wolji Gallery for objects recovered from the former palace pond.** The collections are in separate buildings on the Gyeongju National Museum campus. If the weather is poor or you have a short window, pick a first building before you go. Visit Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond separately if you also want to see the historic site outdoors.

## Pick the building before you cross the grounds

The [Silla History Gallery](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr/eng/html/sub02/0201.html?d_mng_no=10&mode=VD) covers the story of Silla across several rooms. Start there if you want context for Gyeongju’s tombs and the kingdom’s history. The [Wolji Gallery](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr/eng/html/sub02/0201.html?d_mng_no=13&mode=VD) focuses on furnishings, vessels and other objects excavated from the pond. It fits well after seeing the palace site or before an evening visit there.

| With this plan | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| One short indoor visit | Pick the Silla History Gallery for the broad story or Wolji for one archaeological site. |
| Mostly interested in the pond | Make Wolji your first gallery and check the separate outdoor-site visit later. |
| Rain or cold wind | Save one gallery as the main visit and allow extra time for crossing the campus. |
| A child-focused activity | Check the museum’s dedicated children’s program and reservation page for your date. |

At the information point, check which rooms are open and ask the easiest way to the chosen building. Skip the second gallery if rain, a queue or your group’s pace would make the visit rushed. Bring a layer for the walk outdoors between buildings.

## Confirm the indoor hours

The [museum’s Korean visitor page](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr/kor/html/sub01/0101.html) lists 10:00–18:00 and last admission 30 minutes before closing. Its current 2026 Saturday extension runs March through October. The regular permanent galleries are free. Follow the visitor page’s links to the museum notices for date-specific changes before heading over.

The listed indoor maintenance closures are the second Mondays of March, June, September and December, when the garden and outdoor exhibits normally remain open. January 1, Seollal day and Chuseok day are full closures. If the indoor rooms are the reason for your visit, check before taking a bus across town. Use an open central site as a separate plan if your date falls on a closure.

## Save the museum then the evening stop

Enter **국립경주박물관, 경상북도 경주시 일정로 186**. The [museum’s directions](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr/eng/html/sub01/0103.html) give that address. For a bus, use [Gyeongju’s current transport portal](https://its.gyeongju.go.kr/) or a current route app from your actual stop. Confirm the return and ask the museum at [+82 54 740 7500](tel:+82547407500) about the entrance and gallery approach if you require step-free access.

If you are traveling with another person, give them both map names before you separate. The museum is **국립경주박물관** and the outdoor site is **동궁과 월지**. Recheck which entrance you arranged to use as a meeting point. A short walk on the map still needs time for crossing and both sites’ opening checks.

For the outdoor pond after the museum, open the [Donggung and Wolji evening walk](/blog/gyeongju-donggung-wolji-woljeonggyo-evening-walk) and check its own admission cutoff. If the weather clears, the [central Gyeongju itinerary](/blog/gyeongju-the-museum-without-walls-2-day-historical-itinerary-2026) helps you decide what else fits.
