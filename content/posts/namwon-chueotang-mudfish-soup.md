## What you are ordering

**추어탕 (chueotang)** is loach soup. [VisitKorea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=178406) describes versions with boiled and deboned fish or whole loach that has been ground into the broth. Namwon is associated with the dish, but the preparation and seasoning are up to each kitchen.

<GuidePhoto id="archive-chueotang-seoul-bowl-2016" caption="Chueotang photographed in Seoul in 2016. This shows the dish, not a Namwon restaurant or its recipe." />

| Your question | Show this in Korean |
| --- | --- |
| Do you serve loach soup? | 추어탕 있어요? |
| Is the loach ground in this soup? | 미꾸라지를 갈아서 넣나요? |
| Does this dish contain fish or shellfish? | 이 음식에 생선이나 조개류가 들어가나요? |

If you need to avoid an ingredient, ask about the broth and side dishes too. Pick another place if the staff cannot confirm what you need.

## Pair lunch with the garden

Search **추어탕** around that garden pin, then check the menu and opening hours for the actual day. The [KTO restaurant directory](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/asis/upload/Muslim-friendly_restaurants.pdf) lists **3대원조할매추어탕** at **요천로 1467**. This is a locator. Call [063-632-0535](tel:+82636320535) or check a current menu before building your visit around it.

If you have a fixed train or intercity bus, check the ride back from the garden before ordering lunch.
