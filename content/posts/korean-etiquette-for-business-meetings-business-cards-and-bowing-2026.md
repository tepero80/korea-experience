## Confirm the arrival plan with the host

Ask for the Korean address, entrance, room and person to call if you are delayed. Confirm who will attend, which language the meeting will use and whether someone will interpret. Share a short agenda and any documents the host requested.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Arrive | Check which entrance accepts visitors, how to register and when the host expects you. Leave time for a reception desk. |
| Greet | Follow the host’s greeting. The [U.S. International Trade Administration’s business travel guide](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/south-korea-business-travel) suggests a slight bow and using both hands when offering a card. |
| Card | Make your name and role clear. Take a moment to read the card you receive before putting it away. |
| Meet | Ask where to sit and confirm the time available. If an item needs more discussion, note who will follow up and when. |

## Send a short written follow-up

Thank the host and summarize the next agreed action, owner and date. Confirm names, titles and spelling with the information you were given. Ask before sharing any materials outside the meeting group.
