The [Assembly’s visitor office](https://reservation.assembly.go.kr/reserve/main/contents.do?menuNo=2200022) describes a guided visit to the plenary chamber’s public gallery. Build your outing around a reservation confirmation. If you want a different Assembly museum program, check its separate schedule.

| Before you go | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Reserve | Use the [official booking page](https://reservation.assembly.go.kr/reserve/contents/booking/contentsView.do?sn=K20222349017984&menuNo=2200022). The operator lists a window from 90 days ahead through the day before and no same-day bookings. Admission to this tour is free. |
| Check your group | Every participant needs identification. Passports appear on the official list. Ask **02-6788-2865** about access to the online booking process from overseas and English availability before making fixed plans. |
| Pick a date | Only the first three Saturdays of the month operate under the published schedule. Sundays and specified holidays are closed. Check the live calendar for service changes. |

## Find the tour reception

Save **국회의사당** before leaving. Use the [operator’s access diagram](https://reservation.assembly.go.kr/reserve/main/contents.do?menuNo=2200022) to enter through campus gate 1 or 2 and find **entrance 3 at the rear of the main building**. Aim to reach reception at least 10 minutes before the tour. The office says visitors arriving more than five minutes late cannot join. If you cannot confirm a booking, keep other [Seoul plans](/destinations/seoul) for that time.
