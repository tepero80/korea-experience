Choose between a tandem flight, a river trip and technical climbing by the experience you want and the conditions your group can meet. Availability is not guaranteed by a listing: check the forecast and operator's decision before traveling.

## Compare the activities

| Activity | Ask before booking |
| --- | --- |
| Tandem flight | What are the weight and health restrictions, meeting point and weather cancellation terms? |
| Rafting | Which river and course, what safety equipment and swimming ability are required? |
| Rock climbing | Is the session indoor or outdoor, and are instruction, rope and protective equipment included? |
| A mountain walk | What trail and conditions suit the least experienced person in the group? |

[VISITKOREA lists Danyang Paravillage](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=216081), including a briefing and tandem flight with an assigned pilot. Its listing says operation varies with weather. Confirm the exact pickup or meeting point and refund or rescheduling terms directly.

[Naerincheon rafting in Inje](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=89980) is a river activity with rapids. Ask the provider how water levels affect the planned course, whether a guide is included, and what to wear or leave behind. For climbing, establish your experience honestly and confirm whether the chosen area and route are open and permitted; do not assume a hiking trail is a climbing route.

Ask about age, health and accessibility needs, insurance coverage, the final cost and language of the safety briefing before paying. Keep a flexible alternative for cancellations.
