Start at **Chuncheon Station (춘천역)** and set your destination to **Soyanggang Skywalk (소양강스카이워크)**. Walk if the map shows a route you are comfortable with, or take a city bus to **소양강처녀상**. If the glass deck is closed, the nearby Maiden Statue gives you a river stop on the same outing. Check local conditions before staying out in rain, snow or strong wind.

## Choose the route from the station

| Mode | Best next step |
| --- | --- |
| On foot | Open walking directions from **춘천역** to **소양강스카이워크**. Follow signed crossings toward the river and check the return from the actual entrance. |
| By bus | Search **소양강처녀상** as the destination stop. The [city's statue listing](https://www.chuncheon.go.kr/tour/destination/all-tour/detail/?tourId=331) lists low-floor routes 11, 18 and 100 at that stop. Use the [city traffic portal](https://www.chuncheon.go.kr/traffic/) or a live map to confirm which route serves your boarding stop and direction. |
| By taxi | Show the driver **소양강스카이워크, 춘천시 영서로 2663**. Ask for the closest legal drop-off, and look up your return before leaving the station. |

If your ticket says **남춘천역** (Namchuncheon Station), recalculate from there. See the [Seoul-to-Chuncheon train guide](/blog/seoul-to-chuncheon-train-choice) for the difference between ITX-Cheongchun and the Gyeongchun Line.

## Check the glass deck before leaving

The [Chuncheon city listing](https://www.chuncheon.go.kr/tour/destination/all-tour/detail/?tourId=216) posts a **Tuesday closure** and last admission **30 minutes before closing**. It lists earlier closing times than the [national tourism listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=68510). For an evening visit, verify with the management office at **033-240-1695** before you leave. The national tourism listing shows **₩2,000** admission. Confirm the charge at the entrance.

The city describes a 174 m walkway, with 156 m of transparent glass. Bring flat shoes and use the protective covers provided at the entrance. If stairs, surfaces or entry gates affect your visit, call ahead to check the approach and deck access.

## Keep a second stop and a return

The outdoor **Soyanggang Maiden Statue (소양강처녀상)** at **춘천시 영서로 2675** is a short walk from the skywalk. It is free and has a river view near Soyang 2 Bridge. Use it if the deck is closed and outdoor conditions are still suitable. In poor weather, consider [Chuncheon National Museum](https://chuncheon.museum.go.kr/eng/sub01_01.do) as a separate indoor trip. Check its Monday and holiday closures before switching plans.

For a different outing, the [Samaksan Lake Cable Car](/blog/chuncheon-samaksan-lake-cable-car-visit) crosses Uiamho Lake from another part of Chuncheon. Compare the onward journey before trying to combine the two. Save your river stop and train station in the [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary), then leave time to get back from your actual last stop.
