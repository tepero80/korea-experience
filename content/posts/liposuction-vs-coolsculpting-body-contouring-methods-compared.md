## Compare what would happen in each plan

A liposuction proposal is a plan to remove tissue surgically. The [U.S. FDA’s non-invasive body contouring guide](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/non-invasive-body-contouring-technologies) describes fat freezing, also known as cryolipolysis, as a non-surgical cooling method. If a quote says CoolSculpting or simply “fat freezing,” write down the exact device model. Ask the clinic to show its Korean product record and confirm the proposed use. Korea’s [medical device information guidance](https://mfds.go.kr/wpge/m_529/de050901l001.do) explains where to look for the authorization number and product details.

| Check | Put in both plans |
| --- | --- |
| Method | What will the clinician do, which area is included, and who is responsible for each part? |
| Risks | Which complications would the clinician discuss, and who would reassess you for a new concern after you travel? |
| Quote | Which visits, tests, facility charges, and care are included? What would a later visit cost, and what can be refunded? |
| Dates | Which follow-up visits need to happen in person? When will the treating clinician review whether travel fits? |

## Ask about risks that change the follow-up

The FDA lists a potential complication of fat freezing in which tissue in the treated area becomes larger. Ask the clinician what changes they want to hear about and who can assess a concern that comes up after you leave Korea. For the surgical proposal, ask who would oversee anesthesia if planned, how the clinic handles urgent concerns, and what records it can provide to your usual clinician. Ask each team to name the person you should contact during the day and after hours.

## Keep the decision separate from the appointment

Compare the two written plans for the same area you want assessed. If one is a package and one is a single appointment, ask what each price covers before comparing them. If a clinic asks to treat you at the first visit, use the [guide to separating consultation from same-day treatment](/blog/same-day-consultations-and-procedures-pros-and-cons) to confirm what you have agreed to book and what you can choose later.
