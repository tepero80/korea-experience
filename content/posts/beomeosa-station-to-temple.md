Beomeosa Station and Beomeosa Temple (범어사) are different stops on the same outing. For the mountain temple, take Busan Metro Line 1 to Beomeosa Station, then make a separate uphill connection rather than assuming the temple is beside the station.

## Station to temple: choose the connection

| Choice | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Metro and bus | Take Line 1 to Beomeosa. Follow exit 5 or 7 and look up bus 90. |
| Metro and taxi | Show 범어사 and confirm the drop-off, fare estimate and return. |
| Shorter city outing | Save the mountain temple for another day when time or weather is uncertain. |

[Visit Busan](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=402) directs visitors from Beomeosa Station Exit 5 or 7 to bus 90. The city’s Korean directions refer to the temple stop as **범어사매표소**. Check the current stop name and the short walk from it. Confirm which side of the road and current bus direction locally, then note the return stop before entering. A taxi can save the bus transfer but does not guarantee that the whole temple circuit is level or step-free.

## At the temple

[KTO](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=111223) places the temple on Geumjeongsan and gives **부산광역시 금정구 범어사로 250**. From the parking area, allow time to walk into the temple grounds and return; look at the on-site map and do not enter restricted or worship-only spaces. Steps and slopes can change the visit for a mixed-mobility group, so ask staff which areas are suitable today.

Check current entry conditions with [Beomeosa](https://www.beomeo.kr/) or **051-508-3122**. If rain or low visibility makes a mountain outing unappealing, choose a central indoor stop such as the [Busan Modern and Contemporary History Museum](/blog/busan-modern-contemporary-history-museum-visit). Put the bus return and your next connection on the [trip board](/tools/itinerary).
