**Choose a consultation you can evaluate before choosing a procedure.** Start with a provider's identity, the clinician who will assess you, written costs and a follow-up plan. A discount, star score or English booking page cannot establish clinical suitability.

## Build a shortlist you can check

Use the [official registered-provider search](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredhospitals) and match the Korean legal name and branch address to the clinic you are contacting. The [registration system](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem) concerns institutions serving foreign patients; it is a starting check, not a guarantee of your outcome. We do not publish a best-clinic ranking or safety score here.

| Before paying | Ask for                                                           | If the answer stays vague                     |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Clinician     | Name, role, qualifications and who performs each step             | Pause the booking                             |
| Assessment    | A consultation that considers your history and alternatives       | Keep the appointment consultation-only        |
| Language      | Which parts have an interpreter and any extra fee                 | Arrange understanding before consent          |
| Treatment     | Exact product/device, proposed use and written explanation        | Do not choose from a promotional bundle alone |
| Quote         | KRW total including tax, consultation, follow-up and cancellation | Request an itemized quotation                 |
| Aftercare     | A named contact, review appointment and escalation route          | Keep time and budget for another option       |

## Put the appointment into your trip

Save the branch's Korean address from its own website, check the entrance and plan a direct return to your accommodation. Ask the clinician about your planned flight, walking, sun exposure and any other treatments; this guide does not prescribe a universal recovery interval. Do not fill a treatment day with a non-refundable tour before that discussion.

If you cannot understand the consent form, request clarification before signing. Keep the quote, consent, receipt and product information somewhere you can reopen without a local SIM.

## Budget without an assumed medical refund

[Article 107-3](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1030626475), checked 31 October 2026, limits the cosmetic-medical VAT provision to eligible services supplied by 31 December 2025. Do not deduct a promised 2026 refund from your budget without a current legal basis. Retail shopping refunds follow a separate system.

## Use the right help route

[Medical Korea](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient) provides non-emergency service and complaint information. In an emergency use **119 for ambulance/fire or 112 for police**, as explained by [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145). [1339 is KDCA's infectious-disease information line](https://www.kdca.go.kr/eng/4276/subview.do); it is not a general clinic-booking number.

## Your next step

Prepare a written inquiry with the [consultation booking checklist](/blog/booking-medical-consultations-in-korea-online-vs-walk-in). Then choose a [Seoul accommodation area](/blog/seoul-neighborhood-chooser-for-first-time-visitors-where-to-stay-by-trip-style) that makes follow-up practical rather than choosing a room for a clinic discount.
