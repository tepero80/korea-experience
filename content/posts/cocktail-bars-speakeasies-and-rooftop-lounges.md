## Choose by seating and how much time you have

[Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/CharlesHseoul/ENP8f9it6) lists Charles H. on the lower level of Four Seasons Seoul at 97 Saemunan-ro. Use it as a concrete indoor example. Ask your chosen venue for the policy on the actual day and seating type you intend to book.

| Stop | Confirm first |
| --- | --- |
| Lounge | Reservation, exact entrance, permitted dress and the seating window. |
| Walk-in | When the venue will put you on a list, and a nearby backup if it is full. |
| Rooftop | Whether the terrace will be open and what happens to your table if it closes. |
| Late stop | Last admission, last order and a practical route back to your accommodation. |

## Save the address and a backup

If the venue is inside a hotel, note the hotel entrance and the operator’s directions, not just the bar name. Ask whether covers, minimum spends or deposit terms apply to your reservation. If the seating or budget is uncertain, book where those terms are clear or keep the stop optional.

For an outdoor option with a more casual meal, see the [Jongno street tent guide](/blog/pojangmacha-street-tent-bars-experience) and keep a nearby indoor fallback.
