**For:** visitors arriving by high-speed train and heading for Gyeongju’s historic center. **Information checked:** 9 November 2026. This guide uses the **current KTX Gyeongju Station**, formerly Singyeongju, not the old city-center station.

## Choose the transfer before you board the train

The current train station and the historic center are separate parts of the trip. For Hwangnidan-gil, Gyeongju City’s [official area map](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/hwangridan/images/gj_map_kor.pdf) lists buses **50, 51, 70, 710, 711, 60 and 61** from the KTX station. Its route diagrams show the express 710 and 711 via the Cheonmachong back-gate stop; the summary also names Seorabeol Intersection. Confirm the stop served by the particular bus before boarding. Verify each departure and direction on the day; route variants and schedules can change. A taxi may be more convenient with heavy bags or a tight onward connection.

| Gyeongju Station (KTX) | Choose a bus or taxi to your exact destination; it is not the former city-center station. |

| Downtown stop | Find the correct return stop and check that your service reaches KTX station. |

## Bus from Gyeongju Station (KTX)

1. Save your first destination in Korean: **황리단길** for Hwangnidan-gil or **서라벌사거리** for Seorabeol Intersection. For a hotel, save its full Korean address instead.
2. At the station, check the current bus stop and departure information. Gyeongju’s [traffic information center](https://its.gyeongju.go.kr/) provides route and bus information; station staff or the tourist information desk can help if the interface is difficult to use.
3. The city map lists the routes above as options, but ask the driver or check the stop sequence for your particular trip. The same route number can have different endpoints or variations.
4. Get off at the stop that best serves your actual destination and check the walking route. The city map estimates an eight-minute walk from Seorabeol Intersection to Hwangnidan-gil; the Cheonmachong back-gate stop is another area stop. Allow for your destination, pace, weather and bags.

Show this question if needed: **이 버스가 황리단길에 가나요?** — Does this bus go to Hwangnidan-gil?

## Plan the return to your train now

Look for a trip that explicitly reaches **경주역(KTX)** and confirm the direction and last stop before boarding. Some trips may terminate before the KTX station, so confirm the actual endpoint before boarding.

Allow time for waiting and the station approach. If the bus does not fit your train, consider a taxi and confirm the complete destination **경주역(KTX)** with the driver. Ask at your accommodation or tourist information point for current service conditions rather than relying on an old timetable.

**경주역 KTX까지 가나요?** — Does this go all the way to Gyeongju KTX Station?

## A useful alternative when choosing your arrival

Gyeongju has separate express and intercity bus terminals. If choosing between a high-speed train and an intercity bus, compare the complete journey to your first stop, including the local transfer, not just the long-distance segment. [VISITKOREA’s Gyeongju overview](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221840) describes transport to the city, while the city’s [historic-area map](https://gyeongju.go.kr/design/tour2019/file/tourMap/01/tourMap3_front.pdf) shows the downtown sights and bus terminals.

Once you have arrived, use our [two-day Gyeongju itinerary](/blog/gyeongju-the-museum-without-walls-2-day-historical-itinerary-2026) for a manageable sequence of historic sites. Check actual attraction opening and admission information before booking a return train.
