## Confirm the appointment before traveling across town

Tell the booking desk what you are seeking and ask which department handles it. [Seoul National University Hospital’s outpatient instructions](https://www.snuh.org/global/en/patients/EN02003001.do) are one example of bringing identification and relevant medical records. Its [appointment page](https://snuh.org/global/en/patients/appointmentMain.do) explains how to contact its booking desk and lists referral conditions for specific patients. Ask your own provider whether those requirements apply.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Visit | Ask if this is an initial assessment or a follow-up, who will see you, and whether the first slot can cover the purpose of your visit. |
| Proof | Check what identification to bring, whether a referral is required and the accepted format and language for prior images or reports. |
| Talk | Ask who can interpret during the visit and whether there is a separate fee. Tell them if you need a written summary to take home. |
| Bill | Ask what is included in the estimate and how to obtain an itemized receipt. Confirm any authorization or paperwork directly with your insurer. |

## Keep the next step accessible when you leave

Save the confirmed location and department, then arrive as instructed. Before leaving the visit, ask the treating clinician what follow-up they recommend and how to get the written notes. If the provider cannot see you while you are in the city, ask which alternative service to contact.
