**For a show at KSPO Dome, take Line 5 or 9 to Olympic Park Station and use Exit 3.** Save the arena name and the exact gate on your organizer’s notice. Before the show, find a train that will reach your hotel station or the final line you need to change to.

## Match the venue on your ticket

Search **KSPO DOME (올림픽체조경기장)** at **서울특별시 송파구 올림픽로 424**. A search for the road address alone can return the whole park. The [venue’s directions](https://www.ksponco.or.kr/menu.es?mid=a20301030800) name **올림픽공원역 3번 출구**. From Exit 3, use the [operator’s park map](https://www.ksponco.or.kr/olympicpark/mapEng?mid=a30601000000) and follow event signs to the dome. The map labels Olympic Hall and the former handball stadium, ticketLINK Live Arena, as separate venues. If your ticket names one of those buildings, follow that venue’s directions.

For a show that uses mobile tickets, save the arrival instructions and make sure you can open your ticket the way the organizer requires. Check the location and deadline for any in-person pickup separately. On the day, check rules for bags, water and re-entry on the event listing before you decide what to carry. Use the [concert ticket guide](/blog/how-to-get-k-pop-concert-tickets-ticketing-guide) if your group still needs to confirm pickup or access.

## Choose the trip back before doors open

| Check | What to save |
| --- | --- |
| Line 5 | Select Olympic Park and your hotel station. Open the stops for your actual train in case the service ends earlier than you expect. |
| Line 9 | Check which service stops at Olympic Park, then confirm the direction, final stop and any transfer you need. |
| A change of lines | Save the last useful connection at the transfer station as well as your departure from Olympic Park. |
| A ride pickup | Agree on an organizer-approved or legal pickup point and send the driver your hotel’s Korean address. |

Use the [park’s transport guide](https://www.ksponco.or.kr/olympicpark/menu.es?mid=a30604000000) for orientation and a local map app for the trip on your exact date. A departure listed near midnight can end short of your destination. Add time for the walk from your seat, queues at the station and the transfer passage. If your route looks tight before the encore, decide whether your group will leave early or take the backup ride.

## Save an access and meeting plan

If you need step-free access, ask the organizer which gate to use and confirm the lift route from the station separately. Some organizers publish event-specific access maps and staff instructions. The [2025 accessibility mapping example](https://www.ygfamily.com/contents/attachments/2025/08/YG%2BEntertainment_2025_Sustainability%2BReport_%28ENG%29.pdf) shows the type of information to look for in your own notice. Check current event conditions before choosing a gate.

Pick a meeting point outside the busiest flow and agree on a backup if station staff direct you toward a different entrance after the show. Save the arena, meeting point, last useful departure and hotel in the [itinerary board](/tools/itinerary). If another concert is at a different venue, compare the separate [Gocheok transport guide](/blog/gocheok-sky-dome-concert-arrival-and-return) before booking your stay.
