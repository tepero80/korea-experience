Start with the [Working Holiday Info Center country and region notice](https://whic.mofa.go.kr/board.do?action=view&boardConfigNo=35&boardNo=17013&langUrl=eng&menuNo=208&page=1). Eligibility is tied to your passport and agreement. The Korean office that can accept your file may also depend on where you live. Confirm both before preparing documents or assuming you can apply from a country you are visiting.

## Why the issuing office matters

| Example | What the office publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Australia | The [Korean Embassy in Australia](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/au-en/brd/m_3306/view.do?page=2&seq=676715) states ages 18–30 at application. Check the office responsible for your Australian address. |
| Canada | The [Korean Consulate in Vancouver](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/ca-vancouver-en/brd/m_4543/view.do?page=2&seq=750893) describes Canadian residents aged 18–35 at issuance and an allowed stay of up to two years. |
| Japan | The [Korean Embassy in Japan](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/jp-ja/brd/m_1106/view.do?page=1&seq=758522) gives 18–25 as the ordinary bracket and a conditional exception up to 30. Ask your own consular office which instructions it applies. |

These are examples from particular Korean offices. Compare the age checkpoint, allowed duration, permitted work, and any repeat-participation rule on the current instructions that apply to you. A visa's entry validity and the period you can remain after entering answer different questions.

## Make a document list before spending money

1. Confirm the office, application channel and when it accepts applications. Ask if applications start on a particular date or if a quota applies.
2. Check the evidence periods for funds, insurance, background documents and travel plans. Ask whether the office accepts an itinerary before you purchase tickets or requires other specific proof.
3. Confirm translations, document dates and accepted payment method for the application fee. Keep a copy of the submitted file and proof of submission.
4. If issued the visa, confirm its entry dates and keep the [residence registration instructions](https://whic.mofa.go.kr/contents.do?contentsNo=51&menuNo=114) offline. The Info Center says registration is due within 90 days of entry. Ask the immigration office covering your address how to reserve and what to bring.

If you plan to keep working remotely for an overseas business, compare the [F-1-D eligibility questions](/blog/digital-nomad-visa-korea-requirements-and-application) with your intended activity and confirm the route with the relevant authority.
