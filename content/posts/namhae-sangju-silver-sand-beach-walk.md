Sangju Silver Sand Beach (상주 은모래비치) is a useful south-coast stop even when you are not planning to swim. The sand curves along a bay with a wooded mountain backdrop and a grove behind the shore. Come for a flexible out-and-back walk, then return to the village side for food or onward transport. Korea Tourism Organization describes roughly 2 km of sand; you do not have to walk the whole beach to enjoy it.

| Decision | What to know |
| --- | --- |
| Walk or swim? | The operator says sand access is year-round unless bad weather restricts entry. Sea bathing is permitted only during the official beach opening period. |
| Navigation | Search **상주은모래비치**. The beach operator identifies its public parking lot as **경남 남해군 상주면 상주리 1227**. |
| Without a car | From Namhae Bus Terminal, take a local bus toward Sangju or Mijo; confirm its current stopping pattern and return time before leaving. |
| Facilities | KTO lists parking and toilets. Check seasonal services locally rather than assuming showers or beach businesses are operating. |

## Plan the walk

Start near the public parking area and walk along the sand as far as your time and the conditions allow. Turn back the same way, or take a break by the grove behind the beach. Sand can make walking harder for anyone using a wheelchair, stroller or mobility aid; check the surface on arrival and keep the walk flexible.

The beach operator says bathing is restricted outside its official opening season for safety. Dates change, so check the operator's current notices before a summer swimming trip and follow on-site instructions. The operator also says severe weather may close the sand itself. A clear day does not establish that swimming is authorized.

## Getting there and what to combine

The operator directs drivers to the public lot at **Sangju-ri 1227** and lists rural buses toward Sangju or Mijo from Namhae Terminal. Timetables can change; ask the terminal or the beach information number **055-863-3573** about the stop and return bus. A taxi or rental car gives more flexibility if connections do not suit your day.

Geumsan rises behind the beach; Boriam is a separate mountain visit, not an extension of a level beach walk. For a different Namhae landscape, plan a separate drive to [Daraengi Village’s terraces](/blog/namhae-daraengi-village-terrace-walk) or to [German Village](/blog/namhae-german-village-migration-history). Allow time for the onward road and check each place separately rather than treating them as adjacent stops.
