Choose **Daegu National Museum** if you want to spend a cold or rainy afternoon on Korean clothing and regional history. Put the costume gallery first and pick a second room after you see the floor plan. The permanent displays are free. Aim to be at the door by **17:00 or earlier**. From central Daegu, save the Suseong district address and allow for a bus transfer or take a taxi if your window is short.

## Get to the museum in Suseong

Save **국립대구박물관, 대구광역시 수성구 청호로 321**. The [museum’s directions](https://daegu.museum.go.kr/kor/sub01_05.do) suggest taking Daegu Metro Line 2 to **Manchon (만촌역)**, then transferring to a city bus from **Exit 3**. Use the current bus route in your map app and select the stop by the museum. If you are coming from Dongdaegu Station, compare the door-to-door options before you commit to the metro. For a taxi, show the full Korean address and ask for the museum entrance on Cheongho-ro.

The downtown **Daegu Modern History Museum (대구근대역사관)** is a separate venue near Jungangno. Choose [the downtown history stop](/blog/daegu-modern-history-museum-downtown-visit) if you have a short gap between activities in Jung-gu or want to learn about the modern city. The national museum in Suseong is the better choice when the costume collection is your priority.

## Know when you can get in

The [museum’s visitor page](https://daegu.museum.go.kr/kor/sub01_01.do) lists **09:00–18:00**, with the normal last admission at **17:30** and possible earlier closure on the day. The [Korea Tourism Organization listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=111467) gives a more conservative **17:00** cutoff. Arrive by 17:00 to allow for either listing and give yourself more time if you want several rooms. The museum usually closes on Mondays, January 1 and the days of Seollal and Chuseok. When Monday is an eligible public holiday, it normally closes the next weekday instead. Check the operator’s notices around holidays.

| Your priority | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Clothing and textiles | Go to **복식문화실** first. Start with materials and garment details before choosing your next room. |
| A broader visit | Add the ancient or medieval culture gallery. Check which rooms are open at the information desk. |
| A family activity | Check [children’s museum sessions](https://daegu.museum.go.kr/kor/sub02_05_01.do) and reserve a place before counting on it. |
| A guided visit | Call about an English or Japanese tour at least two weeks ahead. Availability depends on staff. |

## Make the costume gallery the main stop

The [museum’s permanent exhibition plan](https://daegu.museum.go.kr/kor/sub03_01.do) includes separate galleries for ancient culture, medieval culture and costume. The [costume gallery guide](https://daegu.museum.go.kr/kor/sub03_01_03_01.do) explains how clothing and textile objects are used to trace changes in dress. Give one room time before adding a second. Allow for exhibition changes and check any special-exhibition fee at the desk.

Go outside only if the conditions suit you. If snow, ice or cold wind makes the courtyard a poor choice, use that time for another indoor gallery. For the current drop-off, entrance and any step-free access you need, call the museum at [+82 53 768 6051](tel:+82537686051) before leaving. Save an onward route back to your hotel or use the [Dongdaegu transport guide](/blog/dongdaegu-station-to-central-daegu) if you still need to plan your main rail connection.
