For a long stay, compare the whole contract rather than selecting SK Telecom, KT or LG U+ on name alone. A plan from a budget mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) is another option, but its eligibility and support may differ.

## Compare the real plan

| Check | Ask before signing |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Which passport, residence card and bank documents does this product require for my stay status? |
| Monthly bill | What is the regular price after promotions, data allowance and speed after the allowance? |
| Identity | Can this exact line and registration support the identity verification services I need? |
| Commitment | Is there a minimum term, device installment or early cancellation charge? |
| Departure | How and when can I cancel, settle the bill and keep or transfer the number? |

[Seoul’s resident guidance](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/finf/elsi/comm/contPageDetail.do?conts_no=D6067AA192E8432180EDF96112A7C241&lang=en) lists a passport and alien registration card, and sometimes a bank account, while warning that requirements vary by carrier and stay status. It identifies postpaid, prepaid, MVNO and eSIM service types. Ask the seller about your exact status and product rather than assuming every option has the same requirements.

Check device compatibility, coverage where you will actually live and travel, and the support language. Keep the written terms and cancellation instructions. If you need service for a trip while arranging longer-term documentation, compare [long-term visitor SIM options](/blog/best-korea-sim-cards-for-long-term-travelers-beyond-airport-rentals-and-prepaid-limits).
