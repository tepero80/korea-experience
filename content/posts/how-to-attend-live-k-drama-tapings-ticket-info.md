A filming location is not an audience ticket. Scripted dramas may shoot on closed sets or working locations, and we have not verified a current public drama-taping invitation for short-term visitors. Do not buy a ticket from an unverified seller or enter a set without permission.

## Identify the opportunity

| Listing | What it actually offers |
| --- | --- |
| Drama filming location | A place used on screen, subject to normal public access. |
| Broadcast audience notice (방청 신청) | An application for the named programme, date and audience, if open. |
| Studio attraction | A visitor experience; check whether it includes any active production. |

For context, [MBC's application page for a storytelling entertainment programme](https://program.imbc.com/Info/1005125100000100000?seq=556) describes recording participation, confidentiality and contact through member information. It is an example of production-specific terms, not a drama ticket or proof of a current opening. Search the broadcaster's official page for the exact show and current notice; check passport eligibility, contact method, age, filming consent, selection and identification requirements before applying. If these cannot be confirmed for a visitor, do not rely on the application.

Plan a confirmed alternative: [choose a public K-drama location](/blog/k-drama-locations-tour-itaewon-class-to-squid-game-2026), and follow staff instructions if filming restricts access.
