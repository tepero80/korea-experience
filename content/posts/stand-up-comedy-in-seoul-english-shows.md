Use the event page for your exact date. [Stand Up Seoul](https://www.standup-seoul.com/) lists stand-up and open mics and labels some events bilingual. [Seoul City Improv’s calendar](https://www.seoulcityimprov.com/calendar-events-in-seoul) lists the troupe’s own performances and says they are in English unless the listing says otherwise.

## Choose the format before you buy

| Listing | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Stand-up showcase | Check the show language, which venue is hosting it, and whether the organizer sells advance seats. |
| Open mic | Look for the audience option. A form accepting performer names is a separate action. |
| Improv show | Verify that the date is a performance if you want to watch. Some improv listings are classes, practice sessions or jams. |

Save the full address and a screenshot of the confirmation. Compare doors with showtime and check any age restriction, drink requirement and usable payment methods. For a small venue, confirm its floor or entrance with the host and ask about stairs if needed. Avoid relying on the neighborhood label alone to locate the venue.

If there is no current performance that fits your evening, look at the [Seoul indie music guide](/blog/seouls-indie-music-hubs-2026) for another event-led option.
