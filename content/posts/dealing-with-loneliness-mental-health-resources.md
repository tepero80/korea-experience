## Pick the first contact for the support you want

If you are in Seoul and someone needs immediate medical help, [call 119 for emergency assistance](https://english.visitseoul.net/medical-emergencies) and give your current address or a nearby landmark.

| What you need | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Counseling or a referral | If you live, work or study in Seoul, call the [Seoul Foreign Resident Center](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/ainf/ctrs/frct/srch/dopr/doprListPage.do?cntr_no=04E34E2046394333BCC451C20A4850D9) at **02-2229-4900**. Ask whether it is taking requests for psychological counseling or can suggest another service. Confirm eligibility and language for any current intake. |
| Medical care | Even for a short stay, look up a hospital with international services in [Seoul’s medical services guide](https://global.seoul.go.kr/hmpg/finf/welf/emer/contPageDetail.do?conts_no=4B5A1B2AF5794457AAB3E8721C95C9C9&lang=en). Ask how to request a mental health appointment and whether the clinician or an interpreter can help in your preferred language. |

## Check what the appointment includes

A desk that answers in English can help with booking. Before traveling to an appointment, ask who will communicate with you during the consultation itself and how interpretation would be arranged. If an interpreter is needed, check whether they need to be booked in advance and whether there is an extra fee.

Try this when making the first call. “I’m in Seoul and would like a nonemergency mental health appointment. Do you have an appointment in English during my stay? What is the first available date, and what would I pay for the consultation and any interpreter?”

Write down the provider, building or branch, appointment time and any documents the desk asks you to bring. Ask your insurer about coverage if you plan to make a claim.

## If the first contact has no opening

Ask whether the service can suggest another provider that can see you within your dates. If you already receive care elsewhere, you can ask your usual provider about continuity of care while you are away. Use the [guide to finding English-speaking medical care in Seoul](/blog/finding-english-speaking-doctors-in-seoul) to check the actual consultation language and reach the right branch.
