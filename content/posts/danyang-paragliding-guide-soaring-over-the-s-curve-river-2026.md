Before paying for a Danyang tandem session, ask the operator to send the meeting point and the cancellation terms in writing. Two places can appear under the same search for Danyang paragliding, and your operator’s pickup point can differ from its launch location.

## Verify the pin on your confirmation

| Place name | What it means for planning |
| --- | --- |
| **양방산활공장** | [VISITKOREA lists Yangbangsan](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=62380) at **충북 단양군 단양읍 양방산길 350**. Use this pin only if your operator confirms it. |
| **두산활공장** | [A Danyang operator lists Doosan](https://www.dypara.com/) and describes pickup from the intercity bus terminal. Confirm the actual pickup window and whether your booking includes it. |

## Get the full terms before paying

Ask what participants must declare before booking, which weight or age restrictions apply and what language is available for the safety briefing. Confirm the total for your package, including whether video, pickup or other add-ons are included. Ask what insurance or coverage information the operator can show you.

Ask the operator when and how it will tell you the weather decision. Its [published FAQ](https://www.dypara.com/bbs/faq.php) gives one example of a refund when it cancels for weather. Ask separately what happens if you cancel, arrive late or are offered a different time. Terms depend on the booking.

## Keep a day plan you can still use

Book enough time before a train or intercity bus that you can wait for news or change plans. If an operator postpones, compare the new slot with your existing return before accepting. Keep a ground-level alternative and an offline route back to the station. Use the [rail booking guide](/blog/ktx-vs-srt-high-speed-train-comparison-and-booking) when your return involves a train.
