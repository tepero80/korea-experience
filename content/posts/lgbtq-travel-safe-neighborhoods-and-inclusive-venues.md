Plan the parts of the evening you can verify: your hotel’s exact address, the venue’s information for that date and a route home after the event. Choose your accommodation around the trips you will actually make. Ask the property directly to confirm room type and any access needs.

## Before you book or leave

| Check | A workable next step |
| --- | --- |
| Venue or event | Ask the organizer about age or ID checks, entry, language and accessibility. Share only the details needed for the question. |
| Return time | Compare your expected finish with the final trip in your direction. Save a pickup point and your hotel’s Korean address as a taxi fallback. |
| Privacy | Ask before photographing people, and check an event’s photography guidance before posting images. |
| Help | Save the [1330 traveler help service](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632) for information or interpretation. Use the [emergency contacts guide](/blog/medical-emergencies-1330-vs-119-2026) for urgent help. |

## Itaewon transport and visitor information

[Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/tourist-Information-center/76) places the Itaewon Tourist Information Center inside **Itaewon Station, Line 6**, at **177 Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu**. Save **서울 용산구 이태원로 177 이태원역 관광안내소**. Check current desk hours with the operator. Open [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%9D%B4%ED%83%9C%EC%9B%90%EC%97%AD%20%EA%B4%80%EA%B4%91%EC%95%88%EB%82%B4%EC%86%8C%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%9A%A9%EC%82%B0%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9D%B4%ED%83%9C%EC%9B%90%EB%A1%9C%20177) or [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%9D%B4%ED%83%9C%EC%9B%90%EC%97%AD%20%EA%B4%80%EA%B4%91%EC%95%88%EB%82%B4%EC%86%8C%20%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%9A%A9%EC%82%B0%EA%B5%AC%20%EC%9D%B4%ED%83%9C%EC%9B%90%EB%A1%9C%20177) and confirm the exact entrance you need.

[Seoul’s transportation guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/public-transportation/) describes its late-night bus network. Check your route’s actual boarding stop, direction and final trip before relying on it. If the bus leaves you with a difficult walk, prepare the [taxi app and backup options](/blog/taba-taxi-and-kride-2026) before heading out.

For public programming, the [Seoul Queer Culture Festival organizer’s 2026 listing](https://www.sqcf.org/english/?bmode=view&idx=171594193&q=YToxOntzOjEyOiJrZXl3b3JkX3R5cGUiO3M6MzoiYWxsIjt9&t=board) includes the Seoul Queer Parade and Korea Queer Film Festival. That edition has ended. Use the organizer’s latest notice for the next confirmed location, date and admission conditions.
