The [official Suwon course](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/hdrdslt/hdrdsltView.do?crsSn=564819) identifies four public stops used in or around *Lovely Runner*. Its route is roughly 790 metres before arrival and return legs. Pick the public landmarks you want and use signed paths between them.

| Stop | What you can plan |
| --- | --- |
| Haengnidan Street (행리단길) | Begin in the café and shop area. The route includes working businesses and active residential streets. |
| Haenggung-dong Mural Village (행궁동 벽화마을) | Walk the public lanes where open. Keep doorways clear, especially when stopping for a photo. |
| Hwahongmun (화홍문) | The guide associates the confession scene with the bridge in front of the watergate. The cover photo shows the arches and pavilion of the gate. |
| Banghwasuryujeong (방화수류정) | The official course identifies the pavilion area with a cycling scene. Follow the public approach and stop if weather or closures affect the path. |

## Save the watergate separately
