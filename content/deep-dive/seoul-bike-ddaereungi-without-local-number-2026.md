**Try the visitor checkout before walking to a dock.** In the [city’s official walkthrough](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-bike-instructions-and-chatbot-service-for-international-tourists/), the app’s **Foreigner** option opens the pass selection, then asks for email and card payment. Check that your card and pass appear in the current app. Keep the confirmation email so you can recover an existing pass if needed.

| Non-member option | City’s listed price | Plan your ride around |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60-minute rental limit | ₩1,000 | A short route with time to find the return station. |
| 120-minute rental limit | ₩2,000 | A longer stretch with time left to lock and confirm the return. |

These are the [city’s listed non-member prices](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/seoul-public-bike/2-about-the-service/), per pass. Check the actual price and options shown in the tourist checkout. A day pass describes how long the pass is available after first use. The shorter rental limit still applies when a bike is out, and exceeding it can incur an extra charge.

## Rent and return the bike

1. Follow Seoul City’s [official app links and rental instructions](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/seoul-public-bike/3-rent-return-bike/). Open the tourist checkout, choose a pass and check that you have confirmation before you head out.
2. Choose a borrowing station and a return station in the app. At the dock, use the app to scan the bike’s QR code or enter its number. Check the bike before leaving the station.
3. Turn back early enough to handle a full or unavailable dock. At the return station, follow the lock instructions and wait for confirmation on your phone.

If purchase fails, check your email and card activity before trying to pay again. Use the [city’s English help flow](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-bike-instructions-and-chatbot-service-for-international-tourists/) for rental failures. If the app still shows a live rental after locking, stay with the bike and contact the operation center listed on the [city’s fare page](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/seoul-public-bike/2-about-the-service/). Keep the bike and station numbers handy.

## Start with an easy riverside option

For a Yeouido outing, use [the city’s access listing](https://english.seoul.go.kr/2025-hangang-park-sledding-hill-opening-notice/) to find Yeouinaru Station on Line 5 and check Exits 2 or 3. The map links below lead to the park. Use the Seoul Bike app to choose the actual docks near your approach and confirm availability. Keep a walk in the park as your fallback if the checkout, weather or docks do not work for you.
