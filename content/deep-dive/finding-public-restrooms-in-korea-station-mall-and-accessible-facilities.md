Look for **화장실** (restroom) on a sign or map, and ask station or venue staff if you cannot find the entrance. In Seoul, the [Smart Seoul Map](https://map.seoul.go.kr/nsmgis/) can be searched for 화장실. A map result is a lead, not a guarantee that a facility is open, free or accessible when you arrive.

<span id="subway-stations-and-public-transport-hubs"></span>

## At a subway station

Check the station map or ask staff where the restroom is before passing through a ticket gate. A facility may be inside or outside the paid area. Do not assume the gate will be opened for free or that an emergency button provides restroom access.

Seoul published a [15-minute turnstile re-entry notice](https://english.seoul.go.kr/15-min-turnstile-re-entry-introduced-by-daeun/) with a list of covered sections based on October 2023. The notice says re-entry is counted as a transfer and is not unlimited. Rules, participating sections and fare products may change; check the current sign or ask station staff about your particular station and card before exiting.

<span id="malls-department-stores-and-commercial-spaces"></span>

## At malls, parks and other venues

Look for a venue directory or ask at the information desk. Opening hours, access rules and the route from a particular entrance depend on the building. Cafés and restaurants may reserve facilities for customers or require a key or code; ask staff. Convenience-store access varies by branch, so have another option if it matters.

## Search Seoul’s official resources

The [Smart Seoul Map](https://map.seoul.go.kr/nsmgis/) provides a search field; enter **화장실**. The [Seoul public-restroom dataset](https://data.seoul.go.kr/dataList/OA-22586/S/1/datasetView.do) describes records containing addresses, opening hours, facility status and accessible-restroom fields. The dataset is updated irregularly as information changes. A record does not prove the facility is currently open, that every feature is usable, or that the route meets your needs. Confirm with the venue or operator when those details matter.

Outside Seoul, use a local government or venue directory, ask staff, and check the specific location. Do not assume a Seoul map covers every part of Korea.

<span id="accessible-facilities-and-family-infrastructure"></span>

## Accessibility and family facilities

For a wheelchair user or someone who needs step-free access, check the entire route from the street or platform as well as the restroom itself. A listing alone does not establish elevator availability, door width, turning space, grab bars or a current closure. Contact the station operator or venue in advance when those details affect your visit. Ask about baby-changing facilities for the specific location rather than assuming every station or mall has one.

## Useful Korean

- **화장실 어디예요?** (*hwajangsil eodi-yeyo?*) means “Where is the restroom?”
- Show **화장실** on your phone if pronunciation is difficult.
- Show the venue name or map listing to the station information desk or staff.
