Choose the garment before choosing a detour. [RECTO’s flagship page](https://recto.co/about/f-store) lists its Hannam store at **8 Hannam-daero 40-gil, Yongsan-gu**. Confirm hours and stock with the brand before going.

## Make a useful brand stop

| Goal | Before leaving |
| --- | --- |
| Try RECTO | Save the Hannam flagship address and item name. Ask whether your intended size is in stock. |
| Find a branch | Start at [ADERERROR’s official site](https://adererror.com/en), use Find Store and verify the specific shop and hours. Its listed office is not evidence of a retail store. |
| Compare both | Allow travel and fitting time between independently confirmed shops; online availability does not establish in-store stock. |

## At the fitting room

Bring the item number, preferred color and a backup size. Compare actual garment measurements and try the intended fit where possible. Ask about exchange conditions and keep the receipt, especially if your departure is soon. A brand’s authentication feature or tag is useful to investigate with the brand, but cannot alone settle every authenticity question.

For help with measurements before trying a garment, see the [Korean clothing size guide](/blog/korean-clothing-sizes-conversion-chart).
