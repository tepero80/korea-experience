[VISITKOREA’s retail refund guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=140736) gives a minimum purchase amount of **₩15,000** for eligible tourists and merchandise. Check that the shop participates and that the eligible purchase being processed meets the published minimum. The guide also requires goods to be unused and unopened and departure within three months of purchase.

## Check one purchase at a time

| At the till | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Below ₩15,000 | Does this sale have enough eligible merchandise to meet the minimum? |
| ₩15,000 or more | Which items qualify, and will the shop apply a deduction or issue a voucher? |
| Several slips | Which transaction is being processed? Ask before counting separate receipts together. |
| Split payment | Which amount appears on the official refund record? |

An eligible ₩15,000 purchase can meet the minimum if the other conditions also apply. The immediate method has separate ceilings under [article 6 of the relevant regulation](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031600355), including a tax-inclusive single transaction below ₩1 million and a cumulative ₩5 million purchase ceiling for transactions receiving immediate refunds. These limits refer to purchases.

## Verify the shop’s record before leaving

Show the passport details required by the seller and check the eligible amount, refund provider and procedure. If the store deducts the tax now, check the amount paid and its record. If the store issues a voucher, keep it with the sales receipt and check where you can claim. If the paperwork is incomplete, ask the same store while you are still nearby. Review the [checkout and airport options](/blog/tax-refund-instant-vs-airport-2026) before deciding what to have accessible on departure.
