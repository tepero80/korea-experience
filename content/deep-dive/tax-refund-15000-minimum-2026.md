**For eligible retail shopping, the published minimum is KRW 15,000, not KRW 5,000.** Shop at a participating tax-refund store, ask for the proper receipt and keep the goods available for any departure inspection. A store purchase does not automatically qualify every item or traveller.

## Choose the right refund route

| Route              | What happens                                                             | What to confirm                                                      |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Immediate refund   | An eligible participating shop handles the refund at purchase            | Passport/eligibility, transaction limit and your remaining allowance |
| Regular refund     | You receive the required paperwork and complete the refund process       | Provider, departure steps, goods inspection and refund method        |
| Duty-free shopping | Qualifying goods are sold through a different tax-free sales arrangement | Collection and destination-country import rules                      |

[VISITKOREA's official guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140736) lists the KRW 15,000 purchase minimum and an immediate-refund transaction amount **under KRW 1 million**. It also states eligibility conditions including unused/unopened goods and departure within three months of purchase. Check the current guide and the store's process before buying; fees and eligible tax mean you should not assume a full 10% cash saving.

## At the shop

1. Look for the participating tax-refund/tax-free indication and ask before paying.
2. Confirm that your traveller status, goods and purchase qualify.
3. Ask whether the store can apply an immediate refund or will issue paperwork for a regular refund.
4. Check the receipt details and keep the original documents together.
5. Keep eligible goods unused and accessible until the departure requirements are complete.

Useful question: **즉시 환급이 가능한가요?** — Is an immediate tax refund possible? If not, ask which refund operator and departure process apply to the receipt.

## Before checking in baggage

Review the airport and refund operator's instructions for your terminal. If goods may need inspection, resolve that before putting them into checked baggage. Keep your passport, receipts and purchases together and allow time for the actual process. This guide does not promise that every claim can be completed at an unattended kiosk.

Use the [departure packing guide](/blog/packing-korean-shopping-liquids-and-souvenirs-for-your-flight) to put purchases and documents into your return-day plan.

## Medical procedures are a separate question

[Article 107-3 of the current law](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1030626475) limits its cosmetic-medical VAT provision to eligible services supplied through 31 December 2025. Do not apply the retail shopping threshold or an old medical-tourism advertisement to a 2026 procedure quotation.
