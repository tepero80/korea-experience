**If the ticket machine declines your card, check the screen and your bank app before repeating the purchase.** Keep any receipt, and ask station staff to look up the ticket if a charge is pending. If the purchase failed, switch to the operator’s counter or a payment method the machine offers.

## Find the right backup for your ride

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s transport payment guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/contents/travel/KoreaTransportationGuide_enu.pdf) lists foreign-issued credit card options. Match the product and operator to your trip.

| What you are buying | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| KTX intercity train | Look for a newer Korail ticket machine or ask at a staffed Korail counter. Check the exact departure and whether there is a seat left. |
| Airport Railroad | The KTO lists credit-card support at AREX standard ticket machines. Choose the right service and ask at the AREX counter if payment fails. |
| Express or intercity bus | Use the ticket machine or staffed counter for that terminal. Confirm your destination terminal and departure time before paying again. |
| Seoul subway single journey or short-term Climate Card | Try an eligible newer machine on Lines 1–8. [Seoul City](https://english.seoul.go.kr/climate-cards-and-single-journey-transit-tickets-now-accepting-international-credit-cards-no-cash-needed/) lists Visa and Mastercard support and an average international-card service fee of about 3.7% for the covered transactions. Read the amount shown before you pay. |
| Ordinary T-money card or recharge | Plan for Korean won. The KTO lists cash payment for foreign visitors buying or topping up at convenience stores and stations. |

## Ask for a named service, then confirm the ticket

Show the operator, date, destination, passenger count and time on your phone. You can show **매표소가 어디예요?** for “Where is the ticket counter?” or **이 카드로 결제할 수 있나요?** for “Can I pay with this card?” Check the train or bus number on the issued ticket before you pass the gate.

If you are catching a flight, first confirm which airport rail service you need in the [Incheon transfer guide](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train). If the counter is closed, check the operator’s official online channel or the next departure. An issuer such as [Visa](https://usa.visa.com/support.html) directs cardholders to their issuing bank for the specific decline reason.

For restaurant and retail orders, use the [general kiosk and checkout guide](/blog/foreign-friendly-kiosk-survival-guide-what-to-do-when-your-overseas-credit-card-is-rejected).
