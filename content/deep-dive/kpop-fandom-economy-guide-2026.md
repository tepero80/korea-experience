## Find the collection policy for your paid order

The [April 2026 BTS Goyang notice](https://weverse.io/bts/notice/34526) described reserving a pickup window and keeping a QR code private because identity was not checked. Its missed-window rule canceled and refunded uncollected purchases. The [June 2026 XIA Seoul notice](https://weverse.io/kimjunsu/notice/36466) required purchase confirmation and valid photo ID and did not allow refunds for uncollected orders. Check the notice for your event before relying on either outcome.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Seller | Start on the artist or organizer page and follow its current seller link. Confirm the delivery mode is venue pickup. |
| Time | Save the reservation window, local cutoff and booth location. Allow travel and queue time before the last pickup slot. |
| Proof | Check whose name the order must match, which originals are accepted and where to open your pickup code. Keep the code out of screenshots you share. |
| Terms | Read cancellation, missed collection and defect procedures. Ask the official seller which contact handles a missed pickup. |

## Keep a short checklist for show day

Save the booth name, last collection time and order number in a private note. Use the seller’s current instructions for opening the QR or displaying your confirmation. Check the item and receipt before leaving the pickup area if the organizer handles exchanges there.

See the [K-pop shopping guide](/blog/k-fandom-economy-where-to-find-limited-edition-goods-without-a-fan-club-membership) if you are still looking for a store, pop-up or other place to buy the item.
