You do not need to be Buddhist to join a temple stay. The [official FAQ](https://eng.templestay.com/fe/MI000000000000000063/board/BO00000049/list.do) welcomes participants regardless of religion; the [English guide](https://eng.templestay.com/en/MI000000000000000020/html/view.do) describes day visits and overnight programmes.

## Choose your experience

| Goal | Ask the host |
| --- | --- |
| Short introduction | Is a one-day programme offered on your date? |
| Overnight experience | What ceremonies, meditation and meals are planned? |
| More privacy | Are private rooms available, or are rooms shared? |
| Mobility or diet needs | Can this particular programme accommodate them? |

Ask what chanting or other activities involve before deciding. The official FAQ says guests who are unwell or tired should tell staff if they do not want to participate in an activity; confirm your host's expectations. Check language, food, transport, room arrangements and cancellation terms before paying. A shared room may be part of the experience and a private room is not guaranteed.

If confirmation does not arrive, contact the temple or Templestay support. For help choosing a setting, compare [mountain temples and meditation retreats](/blog/mountain-temples-meditation-retreats-for-foreigners). A temple visit and a booked overnight programme are separate choices.

Ask what to bring and whether bedding or towels are supplied.
