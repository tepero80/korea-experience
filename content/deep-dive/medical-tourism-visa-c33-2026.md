## Identify the route for your local application

[Medical Korea’s visa overview](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/medicalvisa) describes direct consular applications and confirmation-of-visa-issuance applications through eligible institutions or facilitators. Ask your consular office whether it wants you to apply directly or submit a confirmation number. Take its written instructions to the hospital before requesting documents.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Office | Confirm which office takes the application based on where you apply and what residence proof it requires. |
| Case | [The Korean Embassy in Singapore’s C-3-3 checklist](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/sg-en/brd/m_2444/view.do?seq=761435) lists a medical statement, confirmed Korean hospital appointment and registration documents for its direct route. It gives separate instructions for confirmation-number holders. |
| With you | If someone will accompany you, ask about that person’s category, evidence of relationship and document dates. |
| Arrival | Check the [official arrival declaration applicability guide](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr/portal/guide/eacTargetGuide.do) separately. If you need the declaration and choose online filing, [the electronic arrival-card guide](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr/portal/guide/eacGuide.do?locale=E) says filing opens three days before arrival in Korean time. |

## Save the confirmed instructions before paying

Ask when the office will accept papers and how it treats a changed appointment. Keep the case reference and the consulate’s current list of documents. A clinic appointment and entry authorization involve separate decisions.

See the [general medical visit visa guide](/blog/korea-medical-tourism-visa-how-to-apply) if you are still checking which category applies to your passport, purpose and length of stay.
