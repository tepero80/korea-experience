**Choose the address before you leave.** Tamburins lists two separate Seongsu locations. Use the 3F Haus Nowhere Seoul listing if the complex is your destination. Use the standalone Seongsu listing if you are already on Yeonmujang 5-gil. Dosan is a separate Gangnam stop.

| Place | When it fits |
| --- | --- |
| Haus Seoul | Choose the Seoul complex and look for the Tamburins listing on level 3. |
| Seongsu shop | Choose this address for a standalone store on a Seongsu neighborhood walk. |
| Haus Dosan | Use the separate Dosan address when you already have a Gangnam plan. |

The [exhibition listings](https://www.tamburins.com/en/store/exhibition/) give dates for specific installations. An old photo or video may show a display that has ended.

## Sample first and ask before you buy

Start with fragrance test strips and leave yourself time to return to a scent after it settles. Ask the selected branch about the exact size, price and stock of an item. If a purchase affects your travel budget, [check how Korean tax refunds work](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) and ask the checkout what applies to that transaction.

If you want to stay in one neighborhood, use [the Seongsu half-day route](/blog/seongsu-dong-dior-luxury-vs-market-eats-in-24-hours) to fit sampling around a meal or short walk.
