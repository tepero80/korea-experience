**Do not travel to a pharmacy on the assumption that an old article proves it is open 24 hours or has English service.** Check the current date, location and service directly. For a life-threatening emergency in Korea, call **119** and request interpretation; do not delay emergency help to find a shop.

## Find an open pharmacy for the actual time

Use the Korean Pharmaceutical Association's [Pharm114 directory](https://www.pharm114.or.kr/), then call the selected pharmacy to confirm today's opening, service and language support. A map label or a public-night-pharmacy designation does not establish round-the-clock opening or stock.

[KTO's pharmacy communication guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=107&menuSn=923&vcontsId=217224) recommends bringing medicine names/active ingredients, packaging or photographs and relevant documents. Let the pharmacist or clinician assess the appropriate next step; this guide does not recommend a medicine, dosage or diagnosis.

## If you need language help

Use the [1330 tourist-help guide](/blog/1330-travel-helpline-8-languages-2026) for routine interpretation options, with its current channel/language conditions. Tourist information is separate from emergency dispatch or a medical consultation. For emergencies, the [official visitor-safety guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145) confirms 119 and requests for interpretation.

## Before leaving

1. Confirm the actual pharmacy and useful entrance in your map.
2. Confirm today's closing time and whether it can provide the assistance you need.
3. Bring your medicine/ingredient information and a way to communicate privately.
4. Follow professional instructions; do not rely on an old quote or a product's familiar appearance.
