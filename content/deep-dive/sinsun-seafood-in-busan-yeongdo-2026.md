**Choose Jungni for lunch. Choose Huinnyeoul if you mostly want to see the coast.** Both are on Yeongdo, but they are separate stops. If you're short on time, go directly to the stop you care about and return by bus or taxi.

## Choose your stop

| What you want | Where to go and what to check |
| --- | --- |
| Learn about the women divers | **Yeongdo Haenyeo Cultural Exhibition Hall.** Visit Busan lists 09:00–18:00 Tuesday–Sunday and Monday closure. Check holiday arrangements. |
| Sit down for seafood | **Jungni Haenyeo Village dining space.** The tourism page lists 09:00–18:00 daily. Confirm the hours and what is being served on your date. |
| Look out over the water | **Huinnyeoul Culture Village.** The upper village lane and shore path have different access. Turn back from the upper lane to avoid the stairs to the water. |

Those are the [published listings from Visit Busan](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=ko&menuCd=DOM_000000201001001000&uc_seq=1882), checked 24 November 2026. Ask separately about the dining room, last orders and seating. The exhibition description does not establish that visitors will see a dive, buy a particular shellfish, or be served outdoors.

From Nampo, the tourism office describes taking a bus from the Yeongdodaegyo/Nampo stop to **Namgogyo** (남고교), then walking to the hall. Search the named stops before leaving and check the return. Ask a taxi driver for the full Korean address if you want the dining entrance.

## Order by dish and portion

Use a posted board or point to the dish before ordering. Ask **이거 얼마예요?** (“How much is this?”), then **이게 한 접시 가격이에요?** (“Is that the price for one plate?”). For seafood you prefer cooked, ask **익혀서 나와요?** (“Is it served cooked?”) and wait for confirmation. The words **신선** (“fresh”) can appear on menus; use **중리해녀촌** for the destination instead of searching for “Sinsun seafood.”

## Shorten the coast walk when needed

The city's [Galmaetgil route description](https://www.busan.go.kr/galmaetgil_eng/trip0206) gives the connection via the Jeoryeong shore. Check the conditions on the day, and use the upper road or a bus if the steps, rain or waves make the shore a poor choice. For a sheltered afternoon near Nampo, see the [Busan history museum guide](/blog/busan-modern-contemporary-history-museum-visit).
