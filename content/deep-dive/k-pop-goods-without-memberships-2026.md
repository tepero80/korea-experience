Start with the kind of purchase. A regular album on a shop shelf, an online preorder and a concert pickup can all relate to the same artist and have different requirements.

If you mainly want a physical album on a Seoul outing, [compare the in-store and online purchase routes](/blog/k-pop-merchandise-shopping-myeongdong-vs-online) before choosing a stop.

## Choose the purchase route first

| Route | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Retail | Use the operator’s branch listing and check the item and edition with the branch. |
| Release | Open the official notice for the exact item and phase. Check account and paid artist membership requirements, as well as the sale window. |
| Pickup | Confirm whether the offer needs location verification, a concert ticket, a time slot, order QR or acceptable ID. |

[Ktown4u’s Insa-dong store listing](https://www.ktown4u.com/stores) describes regular K-pop albums and stationery. The shop is on the third floor of Annyeong Insadong at 49 Insadong-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul. From Anguk Station, use Exit 6 and follow the operator’s directions. Check the current branch hours and ask about the exact edition or benefit before going.

Address for your map search: **서울 종로구 인사동길 49, 안녕인사동 3층**.

If the edition matters more than the retailer, [read the album inclusions and format checklist](/blog/k-pop-album-unboxing-culture-photocards-and-inclusions) before buying.

## Read the rule for your sale phase

In the [September 2026 PLAVE Incheon notice](https://shop.weverse.io/en/shop/USD/artists/167/notices/14260), advance purchase required the specified paid artist membership. The later same-day sale for pickup accepted Weverse accounts and required proximity to the venue. Pickup also had a reserved time window. This event has ended. Use those details as a checklist for your own notice.

If an exclusive requires credentials you do not have, choose a confirmed open-sale period or shop for a regular edition. Before checkout, make sure the option shown is shipping or pickup as intended and that your visit fits the listed time. If you are browsing around the store, continue with the [Insadong shopping walk](/blog/insadong-traditional-crafts-and-souvenirs).
