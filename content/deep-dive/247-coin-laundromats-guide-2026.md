## Choose where to wash or dry

| Need | Where to check |
| --- | --- |
| Late wash | Look up the exact branch in the [Cleantopia store finder](https://www.cleantopia.com/kr/store/storeList.do) or your local map app. The [operator FAQ](https://m.cleantopia.com/kr/customer/faqList.do?searchCondition=8) separates its staffed counter from coin laundry and notes that branch hours can vary. |
| Dry only | Ask whether you can start a dryer on its own and whether there is time to finish before closing. The operator FAQ lists dryer-only use at its multi-shop locations. |
| Hotel | Ask what your stay includes. [L7 Myeongdong’s guest laundry](https://app.lottehotel.com/myeongdong-l7/en/facilities/laundromat) lists self-service washing and drying 24 hours on the second floor for guests. |

## Before you walk there

Search **코인빨래방** (coin laundry) or **셀프빨래방** (self-service laundry). Search your own area on [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%BD%94%EC%9D%B8%EB%B9%A8%EB%9E%98%EB%B0%A9) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EC%BD%94%EC%9D%B8%EB%B9%A8%EB%9E%98%EB%B0%A9) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EC%BD%94%EC%9D%B8%EB%B9%A8%EB%9E%98%EB%B0%A9). Check the branch name, current hours and whether an unattended door needs a code or other access method.

**지금 셀프 세탁기와 건조기를 사용할 수 있나요?** (Can I use the self-service washer and dryer now?)

Before you start a cycle, check the [Korean washer and dryer label guide](/blog/how-to-use-a-korean-laundromat-machine-labels-payment-and-common-settings) for detergent, payment and useful phrases.

## At the machine

1. Check labels on your clothes and the machine. Look for **세탁** (wash) and **건조** (dry). Check whether detergent is dispensed before adding any.
2. Read the displayed price and allowed payments on the machine. Budget for washing and drying separately and leave time for an occupied dryer or another cycle.
3. If this branch is closed or its reader rejects your payment, ask your accommodation about guest laundry or confirm another branch before leaving. Keep the receipt or payment reference if you were charged without a cycle starting.
