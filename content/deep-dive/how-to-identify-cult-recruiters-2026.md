An unexpected conversation does not tell you who a person represents. Focus on the behavior and on whether you want to continue. If a stranger asks you to go somewhere else, share your details or pay for something you did not plan, you can decline and keep walking.

| Situation | An action you can take |
| --- | --- |
| An invitation you do not want | Say “No, thank you” and leave. **괜찮아요** (gwaenchanayo, “I’m fine”) is a short way to decline. |
| The person persists or follows | Move toward staff at a shop, station or hotel. Ask for help and share your location with someone you trust. |
| You are threatened or prevented from leaving | Get to safety if you can and call **112** for police. Identify a nearby station, business or street if you need to describe your location. |

## Use the right support line

[Seoul’s official emergency information](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/living/disaster-evacuation-tips-citizens/emergency-ready-app/) lists **112** for police assistance and **119** for emergencies such as fire or a need for an ambulance. For non-urgent travel information or interpretation, use [VisitKorea’s 1330 Travel Helpline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=454&vcontsId=140632). The historical sign in the photograph is an example of tourism help signage. Save the currently published numbers before you go.
