For a special launch, follow the brand’s own announcement. For regular browsing, fit the store into [a Seongsu half-day](/blog/seongsu-dong-dior-luxury-vs-market-eats-in-24-hours). [Visit Seoul’s Yeonmujang-gil guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/other/Yeonmujang-gil/ENP7u7jrk) describes a nearby shopping street worth walking if the store is busy. Check photo rules with staff, and ask the checkout about pricing, exchange terms and any refund eligibility before paying.
