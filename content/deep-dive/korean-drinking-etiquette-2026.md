## Check what the invitation covers

If a colleague invites you to **회식** (*hoesik*), they’re inviting you to eat with a group. The [National Institute of Korean Language’s entry for 회식](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=88144&blockCount=10&currentPage=1&exaType=&mainSearchWord=%EB%81%9D&nation=eng&nationCode=6&proverbType=&searchType=E&sort=W&viewType=A&wordMatchFlag=N) gives a team meal as an example. Ask the organizer where the meal is and whether they expect to go somewhere afterward. If you’re joining for dinner only, telling them before the meal gives them one less thing to work out when the group is leaving.

When someone takes the drink order, ask for your own water or soft drink. If you need to avoid alcohol completely and the only alternative is a mixed drink you don’t recognize, ask what’s in it or choose water. You don’t have to explain what led to that choice.

## Make your choice clear at the table

Say what you would like while the bottle is still on the table. If a drink has already been poured, leave it and ask for another glass. If you had a drink earlier and want to stop, say what you’ll have next.

| Situation | What you can say |
| --- | --- |
| Skip alcohol tonight | **오늘은 술 안 마셔요.** I’m not drinking alcohol today. |
| Ask for water | **물 주세요.** Water, please. |
| Ask for a soft drink | **음료수 있어요?** Do you have any soft drinks? |
| Switch to water | **이제 물로 할게요.** I’ll have water from now on. |

The [dictionary entry for 못](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/kor/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=17254) includes a person who has cola at a group dinner because they can’t drink alcohol. **술을 못 마셔요** says you can’t drink. If you simply want to describe your choice for tonight, use **오늘은 술 안 마셔요** instead.

## Decide when you’ll leave

If people are moving to a second venue and you’re ready to go, tell the organizer or the people next to you while you’re still at the table. **저는 먼저 갈게요** means “I’ll head out first.” You can follow it with **초대해 주셔서 감사합니다**, “Thank you for inviting me.” The [dictionary models that thank-you after a meal](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=76439&nation=eng&nationCode=6).

If the group raises glasses while you’re still there, use the [guide to joining a Korean toast](/blog/korean-drinking-etiquette-pouring-and-toasting) to know what to listen for and when to raise yours.
