**Choose a meal that fits the rest of the day.** Open [Catchtable Global](https://www.catchtable.net/), confirm the branch and compare the proposed booking time with your travel plan. Allow time for the walk from the station and the entrance. Read the terms before authorizing payment, then check that the platform or restaurant actually issued a confirmation.

| Before confirming | Look for |
| --- | --- |
| Party | Number of guests, seating or course chosen, dietary needs and any age rule. |
| Cost | Deposit per booking or per person, currency and how it is handled on the day. |
| Changes | Cancellation deadline in Korean local time, refund amount and cost of reducing the party. |
| Arrival | Exact branch, address, check-in time and any grace period. |

## Read the particular deposit and late-arrival rule

Catchtable’s [L’Amant Secret example](https://www.catchtable.net/discovery/michelin-starred-restaurants-you-must-try-in-seoul.html) describes a deposit with different cancellation amounts depending on how close you are to the meal. It also describes a possible no-show decision after a late arrival of 15 minutes or more. Its [PERIGEE example](https://www.catchtable.net/discovery/where-to-eat-michelin-selected-in-seoul.html) instead illustrates a deposit and a same-day reduction charge stated per person. Use the terms attached to your booking for the price and deadlines. Save a screenshot or email and contact the restaurant promptly if your party or arrival time changes.

## What to do if the listing shows no seats

1. Try another meal time or a different date that still works with your travel. Avoid making several reservations for the same meal.
2. Check the restaurant’s direct channel. For example, the [MICHELIN Guide’s La Yeon page](https://guide.michelin.com/en/seoul-capital-area/kr-seoul/restaurant/la-yeon) lists a public phone number. Ask whether your date and party size are available, and ask for a confirmation and the cancellation terms if they can take a booking.
3. Keep a separate dining option that works without crossing the city at the last minute. Choose a place that takes walk-ins and check its current opening hours and wait. The [Gwangjang Market guide](/blog/gwangjang-market-complete-food-guide) is one flexible central-Seoul option. Check individual vendors if you need a particular dish or dietary accommodation.
