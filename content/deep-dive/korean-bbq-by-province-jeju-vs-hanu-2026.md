A barbecue stop can be part of a trip already taking you to Jeju or Gangwon. Compare dishes and serving formats before comparing prices. **흑돼지** is black pork and **한우** is Korean native cattle beef. Read the menu and origin notice for the exact item you are being sold.

## Pick a stop that fits your route

| Choice | How to order |
| --- | --- |
| Jeju grill | Look for 흑돼지구이. Ask which cuts the first order includes and how many portions are required. |
| Jeju market | Check the prepared dishes or takeaway. A dumpling or grilled snack suits a shorter stop. |
| Hoengseong hanu | Ask whether you order from a menu or select the meat at a counter and pay a separate table charge. |

The [tourism guide to Jeju black pork](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221319) describes black-pork dishes at **제주동문시장** and **서귀포매일올레시장**. Save whichever market fits the part of Jeju you are already visiting. For a sit-down grill, confirm the branch and format before booking a taxi solely for dinner.

The [Hoengseong hanu guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221268) points to restaurants near **횡성시외버스터미널** and describes **정육식당**, where shoppers may select cuts separately from their table service. Ask **상차림 비용이 따로 있어요?** to find out whether the table charge is separate. Compare weight and total for your whole party. Use the [barbecue portions and grill guide](/blog/korean-barbecue-ordering-guide-cuts-portions-grills-and-shared-side-dishes) if the menu gives a per-100-gram price and a larger first order.
