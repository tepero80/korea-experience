Bring the medication’s active ingredient, strength and packaging rather than only an overseas brand name. A pharmacist or clinician must decide what is appropriate. Confirm opening, language support and payment before making a nonurgent journey.

## Use emergency services when necessary

For a life-threatening medical problem, call 119 and provide your location; ask for English or interpretation. Do not delay urgent help for cost comparisons, insurance approval or community responses.

## Compare before you commit

| Need | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Serious medical emergency | 119 and current location |
| Routine pharmacy | Verify an open 약국 |
| Prescription need | Ask a professional or clinic |
| Insurance | Contact the official insurer |
| Police emergency | 112 |

This comparison organizes the decision. It is not a guarantee of availability or an individual price quote.

## Carry a medication record

Keep medicine names, generic ingredients, prescribed instructions, allergies and relevant records. Bring appropriate documentation and check official entry restrictions before travel. The guide does not recommend medicine or dosage.

**Check the operator:** [KTO pharmacy guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=107&menuSn=923&vcontsId=217224) · [KTO Disaster Safety Services for International Visitors](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145).

## Find a pharmacy that is actually open

Look for 약국 and use the holiday or late-night directories recommended by KTO. Call to verify hours and the service you need. A map label does not prove24-hour or English-speaking service.

## Explain your needs to a professional

Show the pharmacist your medication record rather than relying on a familiar brand name. Ask whether a clinic prescription is needed and follow professional advice. Do not substitute products based on a website comparison.

## Use clinics and insurance practically

For routine care, confirm appointment, language support, payment and documents with the provider and insurer. Retain receipts securely. Do not assume every hospital offers direct billing to an overseas insurer.

## Keep service roles clear

119 is for ambulance and fire emergencies;112 is police;1339 concerns infectious-disease information. Travel assistance is not emergency dispatch. Save the Korean accommodation address and ask staff to help explain location if needed.

## Useful phrases to show

| Korean | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 영어로 도움을 받을 수 있나요? | Can I get help in English? |
| 복용 중인 약 목록이에요. | This is my current medication list. |
| 구급차를 불러 주세요. | Please call an ambulance. |

Show one precise question at a time. A translated phrase does not replace the operator’s instructions or professional advice.

## Before you go

- Carry medication records
- Save insurer contacts
- Save the Korean address
- Verify opening hours
- Keep receipts
- Use 119 for serious medical emergencies

Save the current operator notice and your own booking conditions offline. Recheck any dated information before paying or traveling.
