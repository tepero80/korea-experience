**Use the site to reach professional help, not to guess a treatment.** For a life-threatening emergency, call [119](tel:119) and request interpretation. Provide the actual location; do not delay urgent assistance while searching shops, waiting for community replies or seeking insurance approval.

## Choose the appropriate next step

| Situation | Practical action |
| --- | --- |
| Life-threatening emergency | Call 119 for ambulance/fire assistance and request interpretation |
| Non-emergency medicine question | Contact an open pharmacy and explain your situation to the pharmacist |
| Need a clinician or uncertain next service | Seek professional medical assistance; use insurer/provider support where useful without delaying urgent care |
| Need travel-information/language help | Use the tourism assistance route; it is separate from emergency dispatch |

The [KTO international-visitor safety guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145) confirms 119 and interpretation requests. This table is navigation, not a diagnosis or symptom-triage system.

## Find the pharmacy, then confirm it

KTO links the Korean Pharmaceutical Association's [Pharm114 directory](https://www.pharm114.or.kr/) for duty-pharmacy searches. Check the correct date and area, then call to confirm actual opening, service and language support before traveling. A map label does not establish current 24-hour operation or stock.

If you cannot make that routine inquiry in Korean, use [KTO's 1330 call/chat options](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632) for travel-language assistance, subject to the service's current availability. You can show: **지금 문을 열었나요? 약사님과 상담할 수 있나요?** — “Are you open now? Can I speak with the pharmacist?” This locates professional help; an interpreter does not select treatment or replace 119 in an emergency.

## Prepare the conversation

| Bring or write down | Why it helps the professional |
| --- | --- |
| Medicine name and active ingredient, packaging/photo | Brand names can differ; do not substitute by packaging appearance |
| Your question, timing and relevant documents | Gives clearer context than an approximate product request |
| Allergies and current medicines as appropriate | Lets the professional assess the information they need |
| Reliable translation/help | Supports communication; it does not replace professional judgment |

[KTO's pharmacy guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=107&menuSn=923&vcontsId=217224) supports bringing medicine information, translation and relevant documents. Its promotional product anecdotes are not used here as evidence for medicine, supplements, hangover remedies or treatment claims.

Ask the pharmacist or clinician to explain instructions you do not understand. We do not guarantee a particular medicine, English service, price or suitability.

For other practical problems, keep the [travel help guide](/blog/korea-travel-problem-solving-guide) and your own contacts available privately. If you share a useful local update, omit medical records, passport/card details and other personal information; community discussion cannot replace urgent care.
