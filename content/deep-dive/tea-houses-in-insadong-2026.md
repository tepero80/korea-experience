## Choose the stop that fits your walk

| Choose | Good fit |
| --- | --- |
| Jeontong Dawon | Choose this for a sit-down pause with the option of seeing the [Kyung-In Museum of Fine Art grounds](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=75508). Ask if garden seats are open and take the indoor option in cold or wet weather. |
| Beautiful Tea Museum | Choose this for more tea styles and a stop in the southern part of Insadong. [VisitKorea lists teas and snacks](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=100289); check what the shop has that day. |

Save the exact alley pin before entering Insadong-gil. For Jeontong Dawon, look for the museum complex on Insadong 10-gil. For the Beautiful Tea Museum, follow its own address off the main road. If stairs or floor seating would be difficult, ask about the entrance and chair seating before you commit to a stop.

## Order by taste

- **대추차 (daechucha)** for jujube flavor, or **생강차 (saenggangcha)** for a sharper ginger drink. Both are listed by [VisitKorea for Jeontong Dawon](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=75508).
- **오미자차 (omija cha)** for a tart berry flavor. Ask whether it is offered hot or cold.
- **수정과 (sujeonggwa)** is a cinnamon drink you can ask about if it appears on the menu. The archived bowl pictured here contains pine nuts. Ask about toppings and shared utensils if that matters to you.
