Ask your host or front desk where to put waste before your first takeaway meal. Korea does not have one universal collection point or bag rule for every building. Keep packaging and leftovers separate until you know the local system.

## Sort by the actual building instructions

| What you have | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Food scraps | Ask where food waste goes and whether the building uses a designated bag or container. |
| Containers and bottles | Empty and sort as the building directs; ask which parts must be separated. |
| Bones, shells or non-food items | Ask where they belong instead of placing everything from the meal in food waste. |

[Seoul's food-waste guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/seoul-distributes-outline-of-food-waste-disposal-standard-in-ten-languages-for-foreign-residents/) gives examples of items that should not be mixed with food waste, including eggshells, plastic, foil and wooden toothpicks. It also advises removing excess moisture before food-waste disposal. This is a Seoul reference: your accommodation and district can specify the actual collection method and timing.

## A simple takeaway routine

1. Keep a container for scraps and another for packaging while you eat.
2. Check the building's labels or ask the host about bags, bins and collection times.
3. Empty and separate the items as instructed. If a material or food item is unclear, keep it aside and ask rather than guessing.
4. Before checkout, confirm where the bags should be left; do not leave them in a hallway or public bin without permission.
