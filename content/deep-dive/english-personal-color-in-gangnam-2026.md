Before booking a personal color session in Gangnam, confirm the **program, language and total price together**. An English reservation page does not by itself establish who will interpret during the appointment or whether interpretation costs extra. Treat the session as a style consultation and choose whether its outputs are useful to you.

## Compare the actual appointment

| Question | Confirm with the studio |
| --- | --- |
| Language | Is the consultation itself in English, or is there an interpreter? Is that person available at your chosen time? |
| Price | What is the total for your group, including any language fee, options and deposit? |
| Content | Is the booking for color analysis alone or a package? What, if anything, can you take away? |
| Logistics | What is the branch address, duration, cancellation deadline and late-arrival policy? |

The Korea Tourism Organization's [Cocory Gangnam listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=1592543) describes advance reservations and language choices. [Cocory's own Gangnam program page](https://cocorycolor.com/en/product/d-gn/) lists different prices and durations by group size. Prices and availability can change; ask for a written total for your group and requested language.

The [Korea Fashion Psychology Institute’s English site](https://kfp.re.kr/en) provides a separate Seoul example. Ask the studio to confirm the current entrance, floor, any translation charge and the cancellation terms before taking a time slot.

## Send a specific request

“Hello, I would like a personal color session for [number] people on [date]. Is an English consultation or interpreter available? Please confirm the program, total price including language fees, duration, address, deposit and cancellation terms. What results are included?”

Wait for the studio's confirmation before treating a time as booked. Save the reply and the applicable conditions. Ask before adding makeup, shopping or another service; a listing alone may not say what is included in your selected package.
