It describes individual ceramic workshops and says closures vary by store. Contact the specific studio before a dedicated trip; a village being accessible does not mean every workshop is open.

## Choose how it gets home

| Route | Ask the seller |
| --- | --- |
| Carry | Can it be wrapped individually in a rigid box, and what are its packed dimensions and weight? |
| Ship | Will the studio ship to your country? Request the total, tracking, estimated dispatch and breakage procedure in writing. |
| Make | If joining a class, when will firing finish, and can the completed piece be delivered to your address? |
| Use | For tableware, ask about food safety and dishwasher, microwave and heat restrictions for this particular glaze. |

Inspect the piece with permission and ask about the maker and material if provenance matters. Keep the receipt and photographs; check the airline’s baggage rules and your destination’s import charges before choosing a route. Never assume a shipping quote includes insurance or customs charges.
