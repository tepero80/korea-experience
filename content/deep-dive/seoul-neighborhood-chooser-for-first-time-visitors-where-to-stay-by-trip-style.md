Mark your three must-do places on a map. If two are around Jongno and one is across the river, test a central base before choosing a distant hotel for one fashionable neighborhood. Compare full travel chains, not station names alone.

## Choose the daily route before the room

Mark the three places you care about, the arrival airport and any intercity departure station.

Compare complete days from hotel door to first attraction and back; a station with many lines can still have a long exit walk.

For a short trip, a convenient one-base stay often beats changing hotels to sample districts.

## Compare before you commit

| Base type | Priorities it can suit | Check before booking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jongno / Anguk | Palaces and historic-center walks | Residential rules, entrance and bedding |
| Myeongdong / central Jung | Central shopping and mixed sightseeing | Slope, noise and station exit |
| Hongdae / Mapo | West-side cafés, evening area and rail access | Exact airport service and room noise |
| Gangnam / south-east base | A south/east-centered itinerary | Actual cross-city journey on sightseeing days |

This comparison organizes the decision. It is not a guarantee of availability or an individual price quote.

## Compare four useful base types

Jongno/Anguk is an editorial fit for a historic-center-heavy itinerary; check exact property access and residential setting.

Myeongdong/Jung suits central shopping and a central-city base; hotel-to-station slope and entrance still matter.

Hongdae/Mapo suits an itinerary focused on the west-side youth/café area and an airport-rail connection where applicable.

Gangnam is an option when the actual schedule is south/east-oriented; don't portray it as equally close to every palace.

**Check the operator:** [Seoul Tourism Organization: About Seoul districts](https://english.visitseoul.net/AboutSeoul) · [Seoul Tourism Organization: Accommodation](https://english.visitseoul.net/Accommodation).

## Inspect the last 500 meters

Map the actual entrance, not only the postal address; ask about stairs, lifts, slopes and construction.

Check late-night arrival, luggage and mobility requirements with the property rather than infer them from an area label.

A main-road room and a side-street room can have different noise even in the same district.

## Compare the room you will book

Confirm occupancy, bed arrangement, private bathroom, total charge and cancellation deadline.

For winter, check room-specific heating instructions; for family travelers, check age/crib/extra-bed rules.

Treat map stars, hotel name and neighborhood popularity as incomplete evidence of suitability.

## Use a compact decision worksheet

Write down your three priorities: for example, a simple airport connection, heritage walks, distance from nightlife or lift access. Compare each shortlisted property against those needs.

Check a local map for your actual travel hour. A district label alone cannot tell you the room noise, lighting at the entrance or final walking conditions.

If your itinerary is still uncertain, shortlist central options with flexible cancellation terms and check the room conditions before committing.

## Connect the base to the first full day

For a Jongno base, begin with a palace and Insadong, or make the Craft Museum the main stop in poor weather. For a Hongdae base, check your actual airport connection and leave time for the hotel’s final walk.

After selecting the base, confirm your airport route and place the first full day’s main attraction nearby.

Choose the base for this trip’s activities and transport, rather than advice intended for a long-term housing search.
