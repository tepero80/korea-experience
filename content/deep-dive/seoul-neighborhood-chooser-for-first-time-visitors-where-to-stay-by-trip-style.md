**Choose the district that fits most of your days, then inspect the actual property.** These are editorial matches, not safety, price or hotel-quality rankings.

## Pick a base from your top three priorities

| Base                        | Consider it when…                                            | Check before booking                                           |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Jongno–Anguk                | Palaces, Insadong and traditional streets lead your list     | Exact station, stairs, bedding and residential surroundings    |
| Myeongdong–Euljiro          | You want a central base for a varied short trip and shopping | Myeongdong versus Euljiro station, slope and final hotel walk  |
| Hongdae / Hongik University | Cafés, music and west-Seoul evenings matter                  | Nighttime room noise and AREX versus Line 2 exit               |
| Gangnam                     | Most commitments are south of the river in the southeast     | Gangnam Station versus Samseong/COEX; they are different bases |

The [official Seoul district overview](https://english.visitseoul.net/AboutSeoul) helps with orientation. Compare routes from each exact hotel to your three priorities at the times you expect to travel. A single “minutes from central Seoul” claim does not describe every journey.

## Jongno–Anguk: start close to historic Seoul

Choose this area when the plan repeatedly returns to palaces, Insadong and traditional culture. A modern hotel and a small hanok can involve very different room layouts, luggage access and late check-in. Use the [hanok booking checklist](/blog/hanok-stay-booking-checklist) if the stay itself is part of the experience.

<GuidePhoto id="culture-insadong-street" caption="Insadong's street-level mix of shops and traditional details gives a sense of the area. This historical view is not a hotel recommendation or a promise about current tenants." />

Respect residential lanes. Visitor-hour restrictions apply to specified Bukchon areas, rather than all of Jongno; check [Seoul's visitor guidance](https://english.visitseoul.net/hallyu/B-Hunters1e/ENNjzai4z) before a walk. A hotel booking does not grant unrestricted sightseeing access to nearby homes.

## Myeongdong–Euljiro: check which station the address really uses

The [official Myeongdong area guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/Myeong-dong/ENP000067) locates the shopping district. Pin each property separately. A listing marketed as “Myeongdong” may have a more practical route from another station or involve a hill and a longer walk with bags.

For a varied central-Seoul itinerary, test the journey to your palace visit, evening activity and airport connection before choosing between otherwise similar rooms.

## Hongdae: an enjoyable evening and a quiet bedroom are separate decisions

Hongdae's cafés and evening activity can make it an appealing base; [Seoul's nighttime guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/MapoArea/Nighttime-Adventures-in-Seoul/ENP000032) describes the atmosphere. Ask which direction the room faces, whether windows open onto a busy lane and how late check-in works.

Hongik University has different rail facilities and exits. The AREX all-stop train serves the airport journey; Express does not stop here. Check [the complete airport comparison](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train), including the final walk and last usable connection.

## Gangnam: name the destination, not just the district

Gangnam Station and [COEX near Samseong](https://english.visitseoul.net/gangnamarea/Coex-convention-center-en/ENP024663) are different hotel locations. For a conference, appointment or repeated southeast-Seoul plans, compare a base near the actual venue. If most sightseeing is around palaces and the northwest, inspect those cross-city journeys first.

## Send these questions to the property

| Question                                                                        | Why it matters                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which entrance and station exit should we use with bags? Is there a lift route? | The nearest exit may involve stairs                       |
| Can we check in at our realistic arrival time?                                  | Landing is earlier than arrival at the hotel              |
| What beds, occupancy and child rules apply to this exact room?                  | A room name does not establish space for the whole family |
| Is the room street-facing, and what is the cancellation deadline?               | Noise preferences and flexibility vary                    |
| Can you confirm the Korean address and reception instructions?                  | These help a driver and an offline arrival                |

The map uses **station reference points, not hotel entrances or verified step-free exits**. Save the actual accommodation in your [itinerary](/tools/itinerary). Keep booking codes in private notes; use the [family-room guide](/blog/booking-family-hotel-rooms-in-korea) for occupancy questions.
