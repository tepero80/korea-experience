Heating panels differ, so photograph yours and ask the host or building manager for the model-specific instructions before changing an unfamiliar setting. Ask how to adjust room heat and hot water, and what to leave set when you go out.

## Recognize common labels

| Korean | General meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 난방 | Heating |
| 온수 | Hot water |
| 외출 | Away |
| 예약 | Timer or scheduled operation |
| 전원 | Power |

These translations help you ask a question; they do not establish what every button does. For example, [Rinnai’s manual](https://old25.rinnai.co.kr/soundmanual/rinnai_sound_manual_Boiler.html) shows separate heating and hot-water controls and an away mode on one controller. Its settings and timing should not be assumed for another model.

Ask the host which display is the current reading and which is the target, how long the floor takes to warm, whether hot water is separate, and what setting to use during your absence. For a routine display error, send the host the model and the code rather than attempting to repair the equipment.
