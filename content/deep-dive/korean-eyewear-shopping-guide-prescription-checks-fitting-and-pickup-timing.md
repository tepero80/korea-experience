Buying prescription eyewear in South Korea is famously efficient, often allowing visitors to walk into a shop and walk out with custom glasses in under an hour. Because state-licensed opticians perform eye tests directly on-site and major shops house automated lens-cutting equipment, the process differs significantly from Western retail models. 

---

## Prescription Checks and Regulatory Context

In South Korea, state-licensed opticians (*안경사*) are legally authorized to perform refraction tests and issue lens prescriptions directly inside optical shops. 

* **No Appointment Needed:** You do not need a prior medical appointment or a separate prescription slip from an external ophthalmologist. Walk-ins are universally accepted at optical shops, ranging from major chains like Davichi Optical to independent local storefronts.
* **Cost:** Eye examinations at optical shops are typically provided free of charge when you purchase eyewear.
* **Exceptions:** A separate clinical visit to an ophthalmologist is necessary only if there is an underlying eye disease, if the customer is a child under the age of six, or if specialized medical diagnostics are required. 
* **Bringing Past Records:** While not mandatory, bringing an existing prescription or your old pair of glasses is recommended to help opticians evaluate recent vision shifts.

*Human Review Required:* Medical and legal regulations governing the exact scope of eye testing by opticians vary and should always be checked against official local sources if you have complex medical conditions.

---

## Frame-Lens Selection and Fitting Process

Eyewear pricing in physical South Korean shops separates the cost of frames and lenses. 

* **Frame Pricing:** Frames range widely based on material and branding. Budget options typically start around ₩20,000 to ₩30,000 KRW, while popular branded or designer frames (such as Gentle Monster or Blue Elephant) sit at higher price points.
* **On-Site Requirement:** Online sales of prescription lenses and prescription eyeglasses are strictly prohibited by South Korean law. Buyers must visit a physical brick-and-mortar storefront to finalize or pick up prescription eyewear.
* **Tax Refunds:** If you plan to claim an immediate tax refund at checkout, remember to bring your physical passport to the store.

*Human Review Required:* Retail restrictions on prescription medical devices prevent remote online ordering of corrective lenses, making in-person visits mandatory.

---

## Pickup Timing and Turnaround Realities

Turnaround times depend heavily on your prescription requirements and lens type. Major optical shopping districts are heavily concentrated in areas like Myeongdong, Hongdae, Gangnam, and Namdaemun Market.

| Requirement Type | Estimated Turnaround Time | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Single-Vision** | 15 to 60 minutes | Major shops maintain extensive stock blanks and operate automated lens-edging machines in-house for rapid same-day completion. |
| **Complex Prescriptions** | 1 to 3 days (or longer) | Progressive multi-focal lenses, severe astigmatism corrections, extreme myopia indices, or specialty coatings often require off-shelf custom ordering. |
