Choose the frame only after confirming that the shop can make your lenses and have them ready while you are still in Korea. Bring your existing prescription or glasses if useful, and ask the optical shop what information it needs and whether it can use them. Do not assume a fashion showroom offers a prescription service.

## Agree on the order

| Step | Confirm with the shop |
| --- | --- |
| Lens check | Can staff check or use your existing prescription? What additional assessment is needed, and can you understand the result? |
| Frame and lenses | Is the frame suitable for your prescription and chosen lens type? Ask for a written total with coatings and extras. |
| Timing | What date and time is pickup expected, and what happens if lenses need to be remade? Leave time for adjustment before departure. |
| Collection | Try the glasses, ask for a comfortable fit and check the order and receipt. Ask whom to contact if a problem appears later. |

The [official DAVICH Sinsa Station listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=147109) describes consultation and same-day options. Ask the branch whether your prescription and frame qualify for the time you need. A second fitting or an order with special lenses may require a different pickup date.
