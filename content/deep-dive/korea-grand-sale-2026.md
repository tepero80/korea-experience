**The [official 2027 Korea Grand Sale site](https://en.koreagrandsale.co.kr/kgs) lists December 23, 2026–February 13, 2027 as the campaign period for foreign visitors.** It groups participating offers across airlines and transport, shopping, accommodation, food and drink, beauty and health, experiences, and convenience services. The event is a directory of participating-company offers, not one discount that applies everywhere: check the terms on the individual offer before you book or buy.

## Check an offer in four steps

1. **Open the 2027 event page.** Confirm that your visit dates fall inside the campaign period. The site keeps earlier editions in an archive, so do not rely on an old screenshot or a past offer.
2. **Choose a category that matches a purchase you already plan to make.** Browse transport, shopping, accommodation, food and drink, beauty and health, experiences, or convenience services. A listing does not mean every shop or provider in that category participates.
3. **Read the participating company's own conditions.** Check the valid dates, eligible item or service, booking or purchase channel, redemption steps, exclusions, and any limit shown for that specific offer. If one of these is unclear, ask the provider before paying.
4. **Compare the final cost and keep the offer page.** Check the merchant's final price, payment and cancellation terms, and whether the campaign benefit can be used for your planned transaction. Do not assume that campaign discounts combine with tax refunds or other promotions.

| Your planned purchase | Verify on the individual offer |
| --- | --- |
| Flight or local transport | Route/date eligibility, booking channel, and whether the benefit is a fare reduction or another service |
| Hotel or experience | Participating property or operator, stay/use dates, booking method, and cancellation conditions |
| Shop, meal or beauty service | Participating branch, minimum purchase or exclusions if listed, redemption method, and valid dates |

The official Korea Grand Sale homepage identifies the 2027 campaign dates, eligible audience, and participating offer categories. Its current campaign information directs visitors to check benefit details with each participating company. The [Korea Tourism Organization's event listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=40029) provides a second official reference for the campaign dates and nationwide scope. Offer inventory and terms may change; this guide does not promise a particular discount.

If you are shopping in person, use the [Seoul shopping-district guide](/blog/seoul-shopping-districts-complete-guide-myeongdong-to-gangnam) to choose an area. A campaign offer is separate from a tax refund; review the [tourist tax-refund guide](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) and verify current eligibility and procedures independently.
