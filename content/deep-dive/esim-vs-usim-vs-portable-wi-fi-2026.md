A data-only eSIM can support maps but may not receive the phone verification needed by another service. Before purchase, check the exact model, carrier unlock, installation method, activation timing and whether a local number or SMS is actually included.

## Choose for your device and group

An eSIM needs a compatible unlocked device, a physical SIM needs a suitable slot, and pocket Wi-Fi depends on battery and staying near the router. Check the specific product's device list rather than relying only on an eSIM menu.

## Compare before you commit

| Option | Useful when | Verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eSIM | Compatible unlocked phone | Device, activation and reinstall rules |
| Physical SIM | Compatible slot or preferred swap | Size, lock and number |
| Pocket Wi-Fi | Group stays together | Battery, return and separation |
| Data-only | Maps and internet messaging | Do not assume voice or SMS |

This comparison organizes the decision. It is not a guarantee of availability or an individual price quote.

## Separate data, phone number and verification

A data-only product may not provide calls or SMS required by local services. An assigned number does not guarantee every identity or OTP flow will accept it. Read the carrier's actual conditions before buying for bookings.

**Check the operator:** [KT official tourist eSIM product information](https://m.roaming.kt.com/eng/esim) · [KTO 2026 international transportation/payment chart](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/contents/travel/KoreaTransportationGuide_enu.pdf).

## Prepare before departure

Check carrier lock, compatibility, installation instructions, activation trigger, expiry, extension and refund terms. Match passport data exactly where required. KT's voice services can require an entry check; other providers have their own rules.

## Activate without losing your backup

Follow the provider's instructions over a trusted connection and select the intended line for data. Review home-carrier roaming charges. Do not delete an eSIM as the first troubleshooting step unless support instructs you.

## If data does not work

Check activation date, line selection, provider-specified APN or roaming settings and the plan's coverage. Save support and airport-counter information beforehand. Use the APN settings supplied for your own plan.

## Compare total use rather than headline price

Check duration, fair-use limits, throttling, hotspot, calls, installation limits and cancellation. Compare the provider’s current checkout price for your dates, rather than a past discount or an unconfirmed bundle.

## Before you go

- Check an unlocked compatible phone
- Match passport details
- Check activation timing
- Identify voice and SMS needs
- Save support instructions
- Review roaming costs

Save the current operator notice and your own booking conditions offline. Recheck any dated information before paying or traveling.
