**List the tasks before choosing the product.** Maps and messaging need data; a local call, incoming text or identity check may require something different. A local-looking number is not a universal login solution.

## Compare the practical constraints

| Option | Check first | Everyday tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eSIM | Exact handset/variant, unlocked status and product compatibility | Installation and activation rules matter; do not assume it can be reinstalled |
| Physical SIM | Unlocked compatible phone and collection/activation requirements | Keep your original SIM safe and understand how you will receive home-number messages |
| Pocket Wi-Fi | Battery, connected-device limits, collection/return and coverage | The group may need to stay near the device; plan charging and return |

These are editorial tradeoffs, not a guarantee for every device, country variant or provider.

## Read the exact product's calls and texts

[KT's tourist eSIM page](https://m.roaming.kt.com/eng/esim) distinguishes data from voice/outgoing-SMS products and conditions some services on entry verification and matching passport information. Do not generalize “data-only means no incoming SMS” across all products.

KT also explicitly says its tourist eSIM **does not support Korean personal authentication**. Receiving a text or having an 010 number does not establish acceptance by a bank, ticketing service or app that requires local identity verification.

## Resolve installation uncertainty before paying

The checked KT page contains a 90-day heading and a conflicting 30-day installation caution. We do not present either as a verified universal deadline. Ask for the exact current terms of the product you intend to buy, including installation, activation, expiry and support.

Follow the selected provider's instructions. KT's one-install/no-delete warning is product-specific; do not delete an installed eSIM as a guessed troubleshooting step.

## Keep arrival usable if activation fails

Save the [hotel address and map routine](/blog/how-to-use-naver-maps-like-a-local-tips-for-english-speakers-2026), arrival instructions and support channel before flying. Have a way to contact the provider and an independent payment method; do not assume the same phone connection will solve every failure.

For a group, decide who needs an independent connection if you split up. Check the [airport-to-hotel route](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) before arrival so the first transport decision does not depend on a hurried product purchase.
