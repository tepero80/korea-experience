## Choose the place you can visit

| Place | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Blue House | [Official tourism notices](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=230192) list the public tours as suspended from August 1, 2025. Check for a public reopening notice before relying on an old reservation guide. |
| Visitor hall | [Sarangchae](https://cwdsarangchae.kr/ko/staticpage/guide/visitor/page.kto) is a separate venue. The operator lists free general admission without a reservation. Check any special program separately. |

## Visit Sarangchae near Gyeongbokgung

The operator publishes regular hours of **09:00–18:00**, with **last admission at 17:30**. Sarangchae closes on **Tuesdays** and January 1. When Tuesday is a public holiday, the operator lists a closure on the next weekday after the holiday. Use the visitor page for the current notice.

Search for **청와대 사랑채 서울특별시 종로구 효자로13길 45**. [Official directions](https://cwdsarangchae.kr/ko/staticpage/introduction/directions/page.kto) list approximately 800 metres from **Gyeongbokgung Station, Line 3, Exit 4**. Check the current exhibition list if you have time for only one room. For photography, follow the posted instructions. Professional shoots and larger equipment can require advance permission.

## Continue through Jongno

If Sarangchae is closed or you want a longer outing, use a [palace visit](/blog/korean-palaces-guide-gyeongbokgung-to-changdeokgung-2026) with confirmed entry hours, or save [a Gyeongbokgung and Tongin Market route](/blog/gyeongbokgung-to-tongin-market-lunch) for a meal nearby.
