## The service date is what matters

[Article 107-3 of Korea’s Special Tax Treatment Control Act](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?ancYnChk=&chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1032235343) limited the former foreign-tourist VAT refund to qualifying cosmetic medical services supplied through December 31, 2025. A qualifying traveler had three months from the service date to submit the service confirmation. Use the date you received treatment when reading a quote or an old receipt. A deposit paid in December does not make a service supplied in January eligible under this specific cutoff.

| Item | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Clinic bill | Ask for the total in won, including any applicable tax and separate provider fees. |
| Deposit | Confirm how it is credited to the final bill and what happens if the visit changes. |
| Private offer | Check current dates, eligibility, whether it pays cash or points, and any expiry or other conditions. |
| Store purchase | Use the store’s retail paperwork and verify the relevant departure procedure for the goods. |

## Ask for the amount you would actually pay

Ask the clinic or its official booking contact **Could you send me the itemized amount in Korean won, including any tax, fees and deposit credit?** Ask which provider issues the receipt. If an agency quotes the visit, ask what it charges directly and what you would pay separately at the clinic. Save the reply with your booking and compare written totals.

If an older review or advertisement subtracts a government rebate, get a new quote before budgeting. A private voucher has its own terms. Check the amount available for immediate use, which party funds the offer and what happens if the booking is changed. Check what documents the offer asks you to share before sending any information.

## For shopping, use the goods refund process

The [Korea Customs Service guidance](https://www.customs.go.kr/english/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?mi=10803&cntntsId=5504) covers qualifying goods bought at participating stores and taken out of Korea. A clinic’s service invoice and a shop’s retail receipt belong to different processes. If you have a retail purchase, check the store and follow the [tourist shopping tax refund guide](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) for that purchase.
