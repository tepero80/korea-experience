Decide whether you want to compare small pours, learn how the drink is made or spend time in a museum. Ask for the name and language of the exact session. A listing that mentions tasting does not tell you which drinks are available on your date.

For a shorter evening with beer rather than a rice-wine production visit, [choose a craft beer tasting stop in Seoul](/blog/korean-craft-beer-scene-microbreweries) based on the kind of space you want.

## Match the visit to your day

| Plan | Confirm first |
| --- | --- |
| Ganghwa | Geumpung Brewery session, reservation method, language and what is served. |
| Pocheon | Sansawon museum admission, current tasting availability and last entry. |
| Dinner | Food, drink and seating terms at a regular makgeolli bar. |

The [Geumpung Brewery page](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?dataSetId=249&menuSn=923&vcontsId=199699) describes a Ganghwa tasting program and gives the location **금풍양조장 · 인천 강화군 길상면 삼랑성길 8**. Before booking, ask whether your party can follow the session and whether there is an option for anyone who wants to attend without drinking.

The [tourism guide to regional makgeolli](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=221402) describes Sansawon as a traditional liquor museum in Pocheon. Save **산사원 · 경기 포천시 화현면 화동로432번길 25**. Ask whether a current museum visit includes tasting or whether a session must be booked separately. Your reservation should state where to arrive and how much time to allow. Plan your return before the first pour.

If both day trips add too much travel, see the [guide to a makgeolli bar or Jeonju kettle meal](/blog/makgeolli-bars-traditional-rice-wine-culture).
