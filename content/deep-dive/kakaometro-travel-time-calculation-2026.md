Use the route to decide which train to board and where to change. [Kakao Mobility’s KakaoMetro listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.orizinal.subway&hl=en) describes timetable-based routing and boarding guidance near exits. Treat the arrival time as an estimate for the planned route.

## Save the details that prevent a wrong turn

| Check | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Start and finish | Match the station and line to the address. Save the Korean station names alongside the English names if search is ambiguous. |
| Transfer | Save the change station and the destination shown for the next train. Follow the line color and direction on the signs in the station. |
| Boarding position | Where the app shows a suggested carriage, use it only if the recommended stairs work for you. For luggage or limited mobility, look for the station’s elevator route. |
| Station exit | Leave time to reach the hotel, museum or next departure from the indicated exit. A station name alone does not identify the door you need. |

If your rail or airport connection is time-critical, search again shortly before traveling and check announcements. For services it covers, [Kakao Map’s separate train-location feature](https://www.kakaocorp.com/page/detail/11586) can help you see a moving train. It cannot confirm that an elevator is working or that you will still catch the last onward train. Check the last **usable connection**, including the transfer, and keep an alternative exit or ride available.

For buying a transit card and deciding whether to use the subway at all, use the [Seoul transportation guide](/blog/seoul-transportation-guide-for-tourists-2026).
