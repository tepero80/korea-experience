At a self-service laundry, start by reading the instructions on **your machine or its payment kiosk**. Check the price, payment method and detergent instructions before loading or paying. Controls and services differ by location and machine.

## Read the panel

These words may help you identify a setting; they are not a universal set of buttons. The machine may show a combined program or use different labels. Check your clothing care labels and the instructions on the equipment before choosing a cycle.

| Korean | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 세탁 | Wash |
| 건조 | Dry |
| 표준 | Standard |
| 헹굼 | Rinse |
| 탈수 | Spin |
| 동작 / 일시정지 | Start / pause |
| 세제 | Detergent |
| 섬유유연제 | Fabric softener |
| 자동 투입 | Automatic dispensing |

## Before you start

1. **Choose the right machine.** Find the washer and, if needed, the dryer. Read the capacity and any restrictions displayed. Leave time for both cycles and check your clothing labels.
2. **Check detergent.** Look for **세제 자동 투입** or an instruction on the machine. [Cleantopia's self-service page](https://www.cleantopia.com/en/coin/self.do) describes automatic detergent and softener for its coin-wash service. Other machines may require a manual product: check where and what to add with the operator. Do not add your own until you know the machine's rules.
3. **Check payment and the full displayed price.** The machine or a central kiosk may take payment. Check accepted methods and whether the washer and dryer have separate charges before committing. A coin-laundry name does not establish which coins, cards or other payments your location accepts.
4. **Load and start using the posted sequence.** Select the service that matches the care labels, follow the screen or machine instructions and confirm that the cycle has started. Keep a payment reference if one is provided.
5. **Collect and dry.** When the wash finishes, follow the location's instructions for drying. If the dryer is separate, transfer only items suitable for tumble drying, check its settings and payment, and allow enough time to collect everything.

## If the instructions are unclear

Ask the posted support contact or an attendant before paying or adding detergent:

- **세제가 자동으로 들어가나요?** — Is detergent added automatically?
- **카드 결제가 가능한가요?** — Can I pay by card?
- **건조기는 따로 결제하나요?** — Is the dryer paid for separately?

If a charge goes through but the cycle does not start, note the machine number, time and payment reference, and contact the number displayed at the location.
