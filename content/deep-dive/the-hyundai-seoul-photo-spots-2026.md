**Start on level 5 if the garden is your reason for going.** The store’s [official floor guide](https://thehyundaiseoul.ehyundai.com/store/floor-guide) lists **Sounds Forest** on 5F, and [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/The-Hyundai-Seoul/ENP037170) identifies it as the indoor garden. Pop-up exhibitions can change the layout, access and photo opportunities, so check the floor and current event before you go.

| What you want | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Indoor greenery | Look for **Sounds Forest, 5F** on the directory. Check access at the entrance if an event is using the space. |
| A specific installation | Check that event’s dates, ticket or queue steps, and photography rules. A photo from a prior season may show an installation that has ended. |
| Shopping and a break | Choose one floor or store in advance. Use [the full shopping guide](/blog/shopping-at-the-hyundai-seoul-a-guide-to-korea-coolest-mall-2026) to plan a meal or browse even if a photo spot is busy. |

## Choose your station

[Visit Seoul lists Yeouinaru Station on Line 5, Exit 1](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/The-Hyundai-Seoul/ENP037170) for the street-side approach. At **Yeouido Station, Lines 5 and 9**, [the department store’s directions](https://thehyundaiseoul.ehyundai.com/about/location) identify a moving walkway near Exit 3 to the B2 entrance and an above-ground route from Exit 4 to the main entrance. Check station signs and choose the route that works for your mobility needs.

## Check the room before you set up a shot

Browse the [store’s official information](https://thehyundaiseoul.ehyundai.com/) for current events and check where the queue begins. Ask staff before setting up equipment or using a tripod. Leave space for other visitors and follow temporary signage. If the floor is full, use the visit for a meal or some shopping and check the event calendar before you return.
