**Start by checking the documents with the rental company.** A residence card is absent from [Hallasan Rent Car’s published visitor document list](https://intl.hallasanrent.com/guide), which asks for an original passport, valid International Driving Permit (IDP) and original home driving licence. That company also lists the minimum time you must have held the licence. Treat this as an example from one operator. Ask your company to confirm its rule for your issuing country and vehicle before paying.

## Before you reserve

1. Check the [Korea Tourism Organization’s driving guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140664) and confirm your specific permit and licence class with the rental company. Keep the originals together. If the company asks for written confirmation or different documents, settle that before your trip.
2. Ask about the youngest driver’s age, driving experience and the class of car you want. Confirm the card and cardholder required at the desk, and register each person who will drive.
3. Ask for the written total, the deposit or card hold, the deductible, any loss-of-use charges, and what the coverage excludes. Record existing damage and the fuel level with staff at pickup.

## Find the shuttle for your own booking

The [airport authority’s photos](https://www.airport.co.kr/jejueng/cms/frCon/index.do?MENU_ID=100) show the crossing and covered walk to the rental-car waiting area across from arrivals Gate 5. Read your booking before you choose a queue. The main terminal pin below is useful for getting to the airport. Use the airport signage for the final approach.

## Have an arrival plan without the car

If the company cannot accept your documents or the shuttle has ended, go to your accommodation by bus or taxi and revisit the decision in daylight. The [Jeju airport transfer guide](/blog/jeju-airport-to-jeju-city-and-seogwipo) helps you pick a route for your neighborhood. The [national tourist board’s Jeju bus overview](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=219&vcontsId=220897) shows how airport routes and local coastal services fit together. Check current departures before you board.
