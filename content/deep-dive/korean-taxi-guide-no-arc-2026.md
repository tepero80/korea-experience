At a Seoul taxi rank, or at a safe curb where taxis can stop, look for an available taxi. Show the driver your destination in Korean and confirm how you will pay. This works even if you have no Korean phone number or access to a booking app.

## Find a taxi and show the exact entrance

1. **Start at a rank if you can.** At an airport, railway station or hotel, use the marked queue or ask staff where to wait. For a street hail, choose a place where the driver can pull over safely.
2. **Check the windshield sign.** [Seoul’s taxi guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/taxi/) shows a red vacant light for a taxi you can hail. A green reserved light means the car is already assigned. If you booked a car, check the vehicle and licence plate against the booking.
3. **Show the destination.** Save the Korean name, street address and pin before leaving Wi-Fi. For a station or hospital, show the specific exit or entrance. You can point to the pin and say **여기로 가 주세요** (“Please go here”).
4. **Ask about payment.** Show the card you would use and ask **이 카드로 결제할 수 있나요?** (“Can I pay with this card?”). A foreign card working in one vehicle does not establish that it will work in another. [Tmoney’s taxi payment guide](https://www.tmoney.co.kr/aeb/biz/contents/contents10.dev) lists prepaid transit cards where its taxi payment system supports them. Confirm before departure and keep another way to pay.

## Read the starting fare and check additions

The [Seoul Metropolitan Government fare table](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/taxi/) lists these base fares for **standard Seoul sedans**. The amounts cover the first 1.6 km. The meter can add time and distance charges, so the starting fare is not a quote for the whole journey.

| Ride time | Starting fare |
| --- | --- |
| **04:00 to 22:00** | ₩4,800 |
| **22:00 to 23:00** | ₩5,800 |
| **23:00 to 02:00** | ₩6,700 |
| **02:00 to 04:00** | ₩5,800 |

Large and deluxe taxis have different fares. Ask about tolls or an out-of-city charge if your route needs them. If a service quotes a fixed fare, check the exact total and what it includes before accepting.

## At Incheon Airport or if street hailing fails

Use [Incheon Airport’s official taxi-rank directory](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1515/subview.do) for your arrival terminal. Follow the marked queue and ask staff whether your car is metered or has a posted fare, especially for a larger vehicle or an international taxi. If you missed a train or airport bus, compare the [transport choices for your hotel](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) before committing.

If you prefer a booking app, use the [TABA and k.ride comparison](/blog/taba-taxi-and-kride-2026). Check account verification and the payment option for the individual ride. For a street ride, ask for the receipt (**영수증 주세요**) and keep it. The plate, company and pickup time can help you identify the taxi again if you leave something behind.
