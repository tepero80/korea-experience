## Start with the unit and the reporting year

The [Korea Health Industry Development Institute’s 2025 release](https://www.khidi.or.kr/board/view?linkId=48945678&menuId=MENU00100) reports **2,011,822** foreign patients on its real-patient basis. [Medical Korea explains the counting rule](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/global): repeat visits are excluded within an individual medical institution for that measure. Read the definition before using the figure as a count of distinct people across different facilities.

| Claim | Check the original source for |
| --- | --- |
| A national total | Reporting year, included patients and the counted unit. |
| A growth rate | Whether both years use the same definition and coverage. |
| Safety or success rate | The procedure, who was included, how the outcome was defined and how long patients were followed. |

The KHIDI headline figure counts patients. A clinical outcome would need a different set of observations. Ask the clinician what evidence applies to the procedure being proposed and what follow-up is available for your actual itinerary. The [UK guidance on treatment abroad](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/in-hospital-abroad) explains which costs and follow-up arrangements to check before travel.

## Compare the arrangements you can verify

Request the name of the treating clinician, a written plan and a contact for unexpected problems from each facility. Use the [hospital and clinic comparison](/blog/choosing-between-large-hospitals-vs-boutique-clinics) to ask the same questions of both.
