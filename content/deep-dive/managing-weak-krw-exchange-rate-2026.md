## Compare the total cost in the same currency

The local currency is the **Korean won**, written **KRW**, **₩**, or **원**. Set your daily spending target in won and check your own card terms close to the trip. The [Korea Tourism Organization currency guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?menuSn=374&vcontsId=140040) has links for exchange rates and airport exchange banks.

| How you pay | What to check before you accept |
| --- | --- |
| Card in KRW | Estimate the converted amount and check any foreign transaction fee from your card provider. |
| Terminal converts the card purchase | Compare the all-in amount and currency on the screen with your card provider’s estimate. |
| Exchange a fixed amount of cash | Ask how many won you will receive after fees. Compare the same starting amount at each counter. |
| Withdraw won at an ATM | Check the ATM's fee and conversion choice, then check your bank's withdrawal fees separately. |

## Recognize the currency choice at the checkout

Say **원화로 결제해 주세요** to ask to pay in won. Check the screen before tapping, inserting or confirming. A terminal offer to turn the purchase into your home currency is dynamic currency conversion, or DCC. [Visa's explanation](https://www.visa.com/en-gb/personal/travel/dynamic-currency-conversion) says to look for the two currency amounts, the exchange rate and the markup. If the offer is unclear, decline the conversion and keep the receipt.

For example, if a bill is **₩60,000**, use ₩60,000 as the starting amount in your card provider's calculator. Include any separately charged fee once. Compare that estimate to the other amount the terminal offers in the same home currency, and verify whether your issuer charges for the foreign transaction either way. The [Visa calculator](https://www.visa.com/en-us/personal/travel/exchange-rate-calculator) gives an estimate for Visa cards. Use your own issuer's information for the actual rate and charges. The network rate applies only when the merchant or ATM leaves the conversion to the card provider.

## Decide how much cash you need first

For your first day, list the specific expense you plan to pay in cash and a small reserve. Check the opening hours of a practical counter or the availability of another compatible ATM before leaving the airport with no backup. Revisit the amount after you see how you are paying on the ground.

If an ATM offers to do its own conversion, check the amount, the machine fee and the currency before continuing. Follow the [foreign-card ATM guide](/blog/foreign-card-cash-withdrawals-in-korea-atm-labels-fees-and-fallback-options) if you need help identifying a compatible machine or understanding a failed withdrawal.
