## Your right to request your own records

[Article 21 of Korea’s Medical Service Act](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?ancYnChk=&chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1007235937) lets a patient request inspection or a copy of all or part of their own records. It limits disclosure to other people and describes exceptions. Start by asking your home clinician or insurer for the exact records and date range.

## When someone else requests records

[Article 13-3 of the enforcement rule](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1030909323) gives different documentation for eligible relatives and designated representatives. Ask the records desk which procedure applies, which identification to show and how to get its current forms. Family relationship alone does not establish automatic access.

| Who | Ask the records desk |
| --- | --- |
| You | Which identification is accepted? Can I request copies while outside Korea? |
| Relative | Which relationship evidence and patient consent are required for this request? |
| Agent | Which signed consent and authorization forms does the facility require? |
| No consent | If the patient cannot give consent, which separate procedure and evidence apply? |

Article 21 allows some records to be issued as electronically signed documents. Ask the facility how it can send the records. Confirm separately which signatures it accepts on any authorization you submit.

## Fees and translations vary by provider

[Seoul National University Hospital’s issuance page](https://www.snuh.org/global/en/patients/EN02005.do) explains its process for records such as surgery reports and discharge summaries. It directs patients to the treating doctor for an English medical certificate. Check what your provider can issue and ask for the current cost and completion date.

## A practical request checklist

Use the [English document guide](/blog/korean-medical-records-getting-english-translations) to identify what the recipient needs. Write down the record names, dates and the office contact. Agree on a secure delivery method before sharing medical information. If someone will collect a copy after you leave, ask the office to review the paperwork before your departure.
