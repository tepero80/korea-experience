A reference image helps explain shape, texture or color. Point out the part you want, such as face-framing layers, temporary waves or one particular color. Ask if the appointment needs styling, a haircut, a perm or color. These services take different amounts of time and have different upkeep.

## Pick a contact route you can use

| Salon | What its booking page says |
| --- | --- |
| Day’er | Its [English Seongsu site](https://dayer.co.kr/en/) gives a Fresha booking route and explains that services include consultation. Check the appointment listing and any length or treatment charges. |
| Lovey | The [Cheongdam booking page](https://www.cheongdamlovey.com/how-to-book/) describes messages in English and asks for the service, timing and a reference photo. The [FAQ](https://www.cheongdamlovey.com/faq/) describes cuts, color, perms and styling. Ask about the exact branch and assigned person. |

Ask whether your requested language will be available in the chair. Check the street address, service, start time, duration, full estimate and cancellation terms in the reply. If the service includes chemical work, tell the salon which treatments your hair already has and ask what result is realistic.

If a separate color consultation is part of your plan, [compare the appointment language, total cost and cancellation policy](/blog/english-personal-color-in-gangnam-2026).

If you want to keep the style after the appointment, compare rinse-out and leave-in products in the [travel hair-care routine](/blog/the-glass-hair-routine-korean-hair-care-products-for-ultimate-shine-2026).

## Send a message that makes approval easy

“I will be in Seoul on these dates. I like the shape in this photo and want to keep my usual length. Could you tell me which service to book, whether I can consult in English, how long it takes and the estimated total? I previously had these treatments. Please tell me the branch and any deposit or cancellation terms.”

If several options fit the image, ask which result can be achieved as temporary styling and how long it is expected to last. If your schedule is tight, book around the confirmed duration instead of assuming extra services will fit.
