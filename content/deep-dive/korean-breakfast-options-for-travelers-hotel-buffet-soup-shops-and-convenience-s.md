Start with your leave-by time and the first transit leg. If breakfast service begins after that, make a backup plan the night before. A hotel that serves a buffet can still sell room rates where breakfast is extra.

For a meal delivered to your accommodation, [check the address and handoff before ordering](/blog/how-to-use-baemin-without-an-arc-in-2026-pay-at-the-door-methods-and-alternatives).

## Decide by how much time you really have

| Morning plan | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast at the hotel | Check what your particular rate includes, opening time, and whether you have enough time to eat and check out. Ask the hotel about advance takeaway if needed. |
| A quiet sit-down meal | Look for **죽** (rice porridge) at a verified branch, and check its first serving time before leaving. Ask which ingredients are in the bowl. |
| Station or airport start | Buy a labeled item you can safely store or eat on your schedule. Verify whether it needs heating and check rules at your eating location. |

If you are choosing a booked buffet meal, [compare the session and total for your group](/blog/korean-buffet-culture-all-you-can-eat-options) before paying.

## Check the actual location, not the brand alone

Ask the hotel whether breakfast is included for each guest on your booking. Verify whether your departure falls on a weekend or holiday with different service hours. Save the written inclusion and total breakfast price if you are choosing between rates.

The [Bonjuk City Air Tower listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=46593) illustrates how rice porridge varies by flavor. Chicken, seafood and other versions are different menu items. Use the exact branch hours if you are planning a detour.

Packaged gimbap, fruit or a plain snack can be a simple backup depending on ingredients and availability. [The KTO food guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=205&vcontsId=140730) lists gimbap as an option at convenience stores. Check the actual label and use the [packaged-food label guide](/blog/korean-packaged-food-allergy-labels) if you need help reading it.

If you will make an early start on multiple days, use the [Seoul neighborhood guide](/blog/seoul-neighborhood-chooser-for-first-time-visitors-where-to-stay-by-trip-style) to account for the walk from your lodging to the correct station entrance.
