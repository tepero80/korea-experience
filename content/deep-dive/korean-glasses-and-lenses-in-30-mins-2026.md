## Ask for a ready-by time

1. Bring your current prescription or glasses. Tell the shop when you leave the city. Ask whether your lens type is in stock, what fitting is included and the latest time to collect it.
2. Confirm the total for the chosen frame and lenses, any deposit and the shop’s adjustment or remake process. Keep the receipt and the contact details.
3. Leave time to try the finished pair and ask for an adjustment. If the shop cannot finish before you travel, keep using your current pair if you can or ask about later pickup before committing.
