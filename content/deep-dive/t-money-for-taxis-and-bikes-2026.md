## Use the payment that matches your ride

| Ride | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Taxi | [Tmoney’s taxi payment service](https://www.tmoney.co.kr/aeb/biz/contents/contents10.dev) lists T-money prepaid transit cards. Ask **티머니 카드 돼요?** (Can I pay with T-money?) before starting, and keep a backup. For an app booking, follow its payment choice. |
| Bike | The [Seoul Public Bike price list](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/seoul-public-bike/2-about-the-service/) shows non-member options. Pay in the Seoul Bike checkout and choose a return station before setting out. |
| Train or bus | A [short-term Climate Card](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/climate-card/) covers eligible rides. Seoul Bike and Hangang Bus are excluded. Use separately stored value for appropriate routes outside that coverage. |

## Rent and return a public bike

1. Open [Seoul Bike](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/seoul-public-bike/1-seoul-public-bike/) or its current app. Check the map for a pickup and a return station. Allow time for a different dock if your first option is unavailable.
2. Confirm the pass and payment method in checkout. Seoul lists **₩1,000 for 60 minutes**, **₩2,000 for 120 minutes** and **₩3,000 for 180 minutes** for non-members. Check the time allowance and late charges before paying.
3. Follow the [city’s rent and return steps](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/seoul-public-bike/3-rent-return-bike/) and the app for the bike in front of you. Wait for return confirmation on your phone before leaving the station.

If the payment step fails, compare a different pass option or use a bus or metro for this leg. For [app-based taxis](/blog/taba-taxi-and-kride-2026), check whether the booking is prepaid before offering a card to the driver.
