## Choose the Michelin list that fits your lunch

Start with Bib Gourmand and use the price in won from the current menu. The [2026 MICHELIN Guide Korea announcement](https://guide.michelin.com/kr/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/korea-bib-gourmand-2026) describes a complete Bib Gourmand meal as under ₩45,000 per person in South Korea. Treat that as a guide-wide benchmark. Drinks, extras and a different choice of dishes can change your total.

| Listing | When to start there |
| --- | --- |
| Bib Gourmand | You want a good-value lunch such as a bowl of soup or noodles. |
| MICHELIN Selected | You have a specific dish or neighborhood in mind and can check the menu price. |
| MICHELIN Star | You want the restaurant's particular cooking and can confirm that it serves a lunch within budget. |

The [MICHELIN Guide's explanation](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/article/features/the-michelin-guide-101) distinguishes good value from the Stars awarded for exceptional cooking. A Selected listing can be a useful find even without a Bib or a Star.

## Two lunches to check against your itinerary

- **Mapo for pork soup.** [Okdongsik](https://guide.michelin.com/kr/ko/seoul-capital-area/kr-seoul/restaurant/okdongsik) serves pork soup with rice and lists kimchi dumplings. Use **서울 마포구 양화로7길 44-10** or [look up Okdongsik on Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%98%A5%EB%8F%99%EC%8B%9D%20%EB%A7%88%ED%8F%AC). Michelin directs diners to contact the restaurant about reservations.
- **Bukchon for knife-cut noodles.** [Hwangsaengga Kalguksu](https://guide.michelin.com/kr/ko/seoul-capital-area/kr-seoul/restaurant/hwangsaengga-kalguksu) lists beef-bone broth noodles and dumplings. Use **서울 종로구 북촌로5길 78** or [open the Bukchon address on Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%20%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC%20%EB%B6%81%EC%B4%8C%EB%A1%9C5%EA%B8%B8%2078). Check the exact address for this location and contact it directly before planning around a reservation.

Choose the place that matches the district you are already visiting. If you have a timed museum or palace ticket, set a limit for how long you can queue and choose a second lunch option on the same route. If you avoid particular ingredients, ask what is in the broth, filling and side dishes before ordering.

## Confirm the price before you book

1. Check that lunch is served on your date, including any midday break or last-order time.
2. Add the required dishes or courses, extra orders and drinks per person. Ask whether a listed price is for one diner or for a set shared by the table.
3. For a reservation, check what a deposit covers and when it becomes nonrefundable. Use our [Catchtable Global booking guide](/blog/catchtable-global-michelin-reservation-guide-2026) when the restaurant uses that platform.

**Editorial check.** We read Michelin's current listings for the distinctions, dishes and addresses. Check the restaurant's current menu for prices and the operator for live availability.
