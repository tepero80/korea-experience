If Incheon is where you enter Korea, follow the signs through **immigration → baggage claim → customs → the public arrivals hall**. Check your arriving flight’s terminal before you arrange a pickup. If you’re connecting to another flight, ask the airline whether to stay in the transfer area or enter Korea to collect and recheck bags.

## Before you fly

- **Save your flight number and arrival terminal.** Use the airline’s itinerary and [Incheon Airport’s live arrival lookup](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1399/subview.do). Terminal 1 (**제1여객터미널**) and Terminal 2 (**제2여객터미널**) have separate arrivals halls. Check the operating flight on a codeshare and recheck the terminal if the airline changes your flight.
- **Check your entry requirements.** Review the [official e-Arrival Card eligibility guide](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr/portal/guide/eacTargetGuide.do?locale=E). The card is free if you need to submit one. Visa and K-ETA requirements are separate. Have your passport, accommodation details and any applicable entry documents handy.
- **Save your hotel name and Korean address offline.** Include the hotel branch, entrance and how to contact the front desk if you’re delayed. Share the terminal with anyone meeting you and send your exit number once you’re in the public arrivals hall.

## Follow the right signs after landing

| Case | What to do at Incheon |
| --- | --- |
| <strong style={{ overflowWrap: 'normal' }}>Entering Korea</strong> | Follow arrival signs, clear immigration, collect any checked bags, then clear customs before meeting anyone or looking for ground transport. If directed from the concourse, take the airport’s airside shuttle to the terminal. |
| <strong style={{ overflowWrap: 'normal' }}>Airside transfer</strong> | If your airline confirms you can stay airside, follow the green **Transfer** signs and the [airport’s transfer instructions](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1462/subview.do). Ask the airline how to get the next boarding pass if you don’t already have it. |
| <strong style={{ overflowWrap: 'normal' }}>Two tickets</strong> | If you have separate bookings, need your bags or are unsure, ask the operating airline whether baggage is checked through, whether you must enter Korea and when the next check-in closes. Use the [separate-ticket transfer checklist](/blog/incheon-separate-ticket-connections) before relying on the connection. |

See the [airport’s arrival sequence](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1439/subview.do). The airport’s [SmartPass guide](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1415/subview.do) describes a departure service. For entry into Korea, follow the on-site immigration signs and ask staff which lane applies.

If a dog or cat is traveling with you, use the [Incheon pet arrival checklist](/blog/moving-to-korea-with-a-pet-quarantine-paperwork-and-flights-2026) to prepare the original documents and find the animal quarantine step before customs.

## From baggage claim to the arrivals hall

1. **Check the carousel on the screens.** If your checked bag is missing or damaged, show the airline or its handling agent your baggage tag. Report it before leaving the baggage area if possible. Save the report reference and contact instructions. See the [missing checked-bag guide](/blog/incheon-missing-checked-bag).
2. **Use the appropriate customs lane.** If you have goods to declare, follow the [airport’s customs instructions](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1445/subview.do). Ask an officer if you’re unsure about an item. The arrival card used for immigration and the goods declaration are separate.
3. **Agree where to meet.** Send the person meeting you your terminal and the numbered exit you can actually see. If taking a bus or train, verify the boarding point for your terminal before walking to it.

## Choose the ride that still works when you get out

Start with the time you can reach the **public arrivals hall**, then allow time to reach the train platform or bus stop. Check schedules for your terminal and date before relying on the last service.

| --- | --- |

| <strong style={{ overflowWrap: 'normal' }}>Hotel stop</strong> | Check whether a limousine route serves a stop you can comfortably walk from, and confirm its boarding point and last departure from your terminal. |
