**Use Sinsa to compare two frames in person.** If price is your first filter, start with Blue Elephant. If a specific Gentle Monster design or its fitting and repair services are the reason for the stop, go there first. Choose your order around the shop that matters most to you.

| If you care about | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | Note both prices in won for the actual models you try. Ask whether adjustments or any lenses are included. |
| Comfort | Check the bridge, temple pressure and whether the frame moves when you look down. |
| Aftercare | Ask where an adjustment or repair would be handled and how long it would take. Keep your receipt. |

For an online price reference checked November 24, 2026, [Blue Elephant’s ROSA black](https://blueelephant.co/product/rosa-black/4914/) was listed at **₩49,900** and [Gentle Monster’s 2026 collection](https://www.gentlemonster.com/kr/ko/category/sunglasses/2026-collection) listed **Ora 01 at ₩330,000**. These are distinct designs and different price examples. Confirm the exact shop stock and price before traveling for a particular model.

## Gentle Monster Sinsa

The [retailer’s Sinsa page](https://www.gentlemonster.com/us/en/stores/detail/779149630468970541) lists **11:00–21:00** daily and information on fitting and repair. Tell staff if you leave Seoul soon and confirm the return time for any service before handing over a frame.

## Plan the two stops from Sinsa

Use both saved addresses and plan between the exact doors. The listed hours overlap from noon to 21:00. [Seoul City’s street overview](https://english.seoul.go.kr/garosu-gil-in-sinsa-dong/) describes the Garosu-gil area starting near Sinsa Station. Check current hours and allow time to try on frames. If only one shop has the design or service you need, leave your second stop flexible.
