**Confirm the serving unit and total before ordering.** Two stalls can write the same number for different amounts. Compare the same dish and quantity when deciding what fits your budget.

## Read the unit beside the price

| What you see | What you can say |
| --- | --- |
| **1개** · one piece | **한 개 얼마예요?** (“How much is one?”) |
| **1인분** · one serving | **일 인분에 몇 개 들어 있어요?** (“How many pieces come in one serving?”) |
| **세트** · set | **세트에 뭐가 들어 있어요?** (“What comes with the set?”) |
| The amount you will pay | **총 얼마예요?** (“How much is it in total?”) |

For example, if a board says **₩4,000 per serving** and you ask for **two servings**, the example total is **₩8,000**. That is arithmetic using a made-up price. Show the quantity on your phone if there is any uncertainty. For a card ask **카드 돼요?** (“Do you take cards?”). Check before ordering, especially if you have no won on hand.

## Choose the market that fits your day

| Market | When to go and how to get there |
| --- | --- |
| [Gwangjang Market](https://english.visitseoul.net/Tourist-Attractions-list1/Gwangjang-Market/ENP000286) | Compare several menus in one food area. Visit Seoul lists Jongno 5-ga Station, Exit 8. Address: 서울 종로구 창경궁로 88. |
| [Namdaemun Market](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Namdaemun-Market/ENP000085) | Add a snack to a shopping trip. Visit Seoul lists Hoehyeon Station, Exit 7. Address: 서울 중구 남대문시장4길 21. Check stall hours. |

For an unfamiliar menu, use the [Papago walkthrough](/blog/ordering-food-traditional-markets-papago-2026), then confirm the dish name, quantity and total directly. If you cannot confirm the price, thank the vendor and compare another board before deciding.
