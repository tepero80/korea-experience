Your confirmation is only the start of the day-of plan. Read the organizer's latest notice for your exact date, ticket type and venue, and save the ticketing provider's contact details.

Looking for television soundtrack music? [Verify the OST concert and lineup](/blog/k-drama-ost-concerts-where-to-buy-tickets) before using this venue-entry checklist.

## Before leaving

| Check | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Name and ID | Match the attendee to the booking and bring the ID accepted by your event. |
| Collection | Find the booth, opening time and required proof. |
| Entry | Check gate, prohibited items and any app or network requirement. |
| Return | Check the last useful connection and a backup to your accommodation. |

For example, the [2026 JX Incheon notice](https://weverse.io/kimjaejoong/notice/38661) required a matching attendee and booking, ticket details and accepted ID at collection; presale collection also required membership proof. Those are that event's terms, not a rule for every show. Check whether your own notice accepts a physical passport, another ID or a particular ticket format, and resolve a name mismatch with the authorized seller before travel.

A separate [BTS Busan sound-check notice](https://shop.weverse.io/en/shop/CNY/artists/2/notices/13339) required its app QR rather than a capture and said sound-check attendees would exit and re-enter for the main performance. It also advised public transport. Check your event's schedule and the actual service timetable rather than assuming a late train will run. For booking preparation, return to the [concert ticketing guide](/blog/how-to-get-k-pop-concert-tickets-ticketing-guide).
