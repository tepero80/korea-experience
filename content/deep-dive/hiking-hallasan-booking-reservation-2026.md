Use the [park’s reservation site](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/main/main.do?language=en_US) to choose the route and date. The park’s [reservation notice](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/board/boardView.do?bbsId=notice_cn&seq=1286) identifies the upper paths beyond the Azalea Field and Samgakbong checkpoints as the reserved sections. A booking is an access step. Weather and trail controls can still change the hike.

## Decide how far to go

| Route | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| 성판악 | **Seongpanak.** Reserve from Azalea Field to Baengnokdam. The [park guide](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/contents/contents.do?id=61&language=en_US) lists 9.6 km one way to the summit. Check the trailhead and Azalea Field cutoffs. |
| 관음사 | **Gwaneumsa.** Reserve from Samgakbong to Baengnokdam. The [park guide](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/contents/contents.do?id=62&language=en_US) lists 8.7 km one way from the campground trailhead. Check the steep route and return ride. |
| Eorimok or Yeongsil | These non-summit routes are listed in the [park’s reservation notice](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/board/boardView.do?bbsId=notice_cn&seq=1286). Check their own cutoffs and transport. |

## Book and check again

1. Open the portal and select the trail, date and party size. The [release-day notice](https://visithalla.jeju.go.kr/board/boardView.do?bbsId=notice&seq=1297) explains when the next month opens and that the site can queue visitors. A first day that falls on a weekend or public holiday can shift release later.
2. Follow the portal’s own verification and reservation steps. Save your confirmation and check the identification requirement in the instructions. If no place is available, look at a different date or choose an open non-summit route.
3. Before setting off, compare the latest time and access notice from the [park](https://www.jeju.go.kr/hallasan/index.htm) with your route. The trailhead, uphill checkpoint and summit departure each have their own time. Follow the instructions at the gate.

Set the return ride before starting, especially if you plan to finish at a different entrance. If the route or weather is unsuitable, switch to a [lower-elevation Jeju plan](/blog/jeju-island-complete-travel-guide-best-time-to-visit-2026). The Samgakbong photograph shows one winter visit. Check the current trail rather than judging the conditions from a photo.
