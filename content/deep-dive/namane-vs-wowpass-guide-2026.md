**Start with the problem the card should solve.** For shopping, check whether your card can be topped up conveniently and what happens to the remaining money. For buses and subways, make sure you have money in the separate transport balance before tapping. If your only need is local transit, compare the [ordinary Seoul transit choices](/blog/seoul-transit-climate-card-vs-tmoney-2026) before buying a prepaid shopping card.

| What to check | What the providers document |
| --- | --- |
| NAMANE shopping | The [current NAMANE terms](https://namanecard.com/en/legal/terms) describe card purchase and top-up through supported channels. The terms identify limits by verification status and exclude some payment types such as post-billing transactions. |
| NAMANE transport | Its [visitor instructions](https://blog.namanecard.com/en/travel-card-namane-en/) describe loading the app payment balance and using the NFC transfer flow to move money into the transport balance. Check that it landed there before boarding. |
| WOWPASS shopping | The [official WOWPASS guide](https://www.wowpass.io/en?lang=en) explains loading and withdrawal at supported machines. It lists up to ₩100,000 per eligible withdrawal and a ₩1,000 fee. Confirm service at the machine you plan to use. |
| WOWPASS transport | Check the separate T-money balance and follow the official app or an eligible reload point’s instructions. A purchase balance alone will not fund the ride. |

## Confirm refunds and support before loading a large amount

1. Find a machine or app flow that supports the actions you need. Check the exact fee, ID requirement, funding method and which balance will receive the money.
2. Check how that same balance can be recovered. NAMANE’s current terms describe conditional reimbursement, including examples based on when the balance was loaded and how much was used. Check the in-app route and current conditions for your payment method. Use the refund or withdrawal instructions for the correct balance.
3. If verification, top-up or payment fails, check the receipt and your bank app before trying again. Use the provider’s official support for that transaction and keep the receipt. Keep a separate card or modest cash backup while the issue is resolved.
