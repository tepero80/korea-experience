## Confirm the event before you go

| Step | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Find the active listing | Save the organizer’s announcement with the event name, address, dates and last entry or check-in time. |
| Match the entry rule | Check whether your date uses timed tickets, advance booking, an on-site waitlist or walk-in access. |
| Check your booking | Verify that you can complete any phone or account requirement. Ask the organizer whether it offers another booking route. |
| Decide when to move on | If the queue or time slot does not fit your day, use an alternate stop you have already checked. |

Be careful with old posts and map pins. A listing can still have the previous tenant’s name. Keep the organizer's actual booking confirmation, address and Korean venue name together.

## Pick the right starting point in Seongsu

Look up **성수 연무장길 서울 성동구** for the shopping street and check the event’s exact address separately. - **Seongsu Station Exit 3** is useful for the official [MUSINSA at Daelim Changgo listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/other/2024-musinsa-daelim/ENPnh3bjr) at 74 Seongsui-ro. Check which exhibition is on before you go.
- **Seongsu Station Exit 4** serves a different venue, [MUSINSA Terrace Seongsu](https://english.visitseoul.net/other/MusinsaTerraceSeongsu/ENP58m9lc) on the third floor at 104 Achasan-ro. Its event hours can vary.

The two venues host their own programs. A listing for one building does not give you access to the other. If you have a single timed event, start the itinerary there.
