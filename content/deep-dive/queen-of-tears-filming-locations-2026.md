## Choose the real place for the scene you remember

| Scene or setting | Current visitor plan |
| --- | --- |
| The rooftop garden | [VisitKorea identifies Suwon’s Irwol Arboretum](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/HallyuNew/contentsView.do?dataSetId=76&vcontsId=228261). Visit its public grounds and exhibition greenhouse, subject to that day’s operating notice. |
| The Yongdu-ri supermarket | [Mungyeong’s tourism operator](https://www.mgtpcr.or.kr/web/board.do?bbsIdx=2208850&menuIdx=412) lists **용두리슈퍼** as an Eco World photo zone. Treat the venue as the destination and use its visitor opening information. |

**일월수목원 · 경기도 수원시 장안구 일월로 61**

Open the greenhouse information before leaving if the scene is the main reason for your visit. Use the public paths and allow time for the transfer back before adding the fortress district.

### Yongdu-ri photo zone in Mungyeong

Search **문경에코월드** for the venue.

[Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%AC%B8%EA%B2%BD%EC%97%90%EC%BD%94%EC%9B%94%EB%93%9C%20%EB%AC%B8%EA%B2%BD%EC%8B%9C) · [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/link/search/%EB%AC%B8%EA%B2%BD%EC%97%90%EC%BD%94%EC%9B%94%EB%93%9C%20%EB%AC%B8%EA%B2%BD%EC%8B%9C) · [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%EB%AC%B8%EA%B2%BD%EC%97%90%EC%BD%94%EC%9B%94%EB%93%9C%20%EB%AC%B8%EA%B2%BD%EC%8B%9C)

Check current admission, operating notices and the return before planning a separate Mungyeong outing. Ask at the visitor entrance whether the photo zone is accessible that day. The operator’s listing identifies a visitor photo zone and does not establish which elements of the older on-location set remain.

## A simple Suwon half-day

Visit the arboretum while the greenhouse is open. Afterward, choose lunch near your next transfer or use the [Suwon guide](/destinations/suwon) to decide whether you have enough time for a second district. Set the return first if you are heading back to Seoul for a fixed evening booking.
