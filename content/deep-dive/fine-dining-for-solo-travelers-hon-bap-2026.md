A solo tasting menu is possible in Seoul, but a counter seat does not automatically mean that a particular course or time can be booked for one. Start with the restaurant's party-size rules, then check the actual reservation screen for your date before you pay.

## One documented counter option

[KUT SEOUL's own solo-dining guide](https://kutseoul.com/en/blog/solo-dining-hanwoo-omakase-seoul-kut-seoul-counter/) says one guest may reserve its chef's counter in Jongno. It describes a weekday lunch counter course and another counter course for dinner and weekend or holiday lunch. The restaurant says its private rooms require at least two guests and some courses are room-only. That does not guarantee a seat on your chosen date.

KUT describes CatchTable and Naver Booking, advance payment of the course amount and cancellation deductions. The operator also advertises a December price change and a holiday menu. Check the course, total and cancellation terms offered for your date before paying. We did not complete a booking or test a card.

The restaurant lists its address as 3F, Hanol Tower, 96 Jongno, Jongno-gu, Seoul (서울 종로구 종로 96 한올타워 3층). Check the [restaurant's own location information](https://kutseoul.com/lp/honeymoon-dinner-seoul/) and current opening times before leaving. If you have allergies or access needs, ask the restaurant before paying; a request is not confirmation that a course can be adapted.

## Before you confirm

| Review | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Party | Choose one guest and the actual date. Ask directly if the screen offers no seat for one. |
| Cost | Compare the course, drinks, taxes and extras. Check how the deposit is applied. |
| Changes | Read the cancellation deadline in Korean time and the late-arrival or no-show rule. |
| Diet | Send dietary or allergy requirements in advance and wait for a clear answer. |

For platform setup, see [the Catchtable Global guide](/blog/catchtable-global-michelin-reservation-guide-2026); the restaurant's own terms and checkout govern your booking.
