**Decide where you will wear the outfit.** A modern hanbok skirt with a plain shirt can work for a neighborhood walk. For regular admission under the palace’s hanbok policy, choose a complete set and check the [English visitor rules](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E701000000.do) alongside the [Korean photo examples](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R705000000.do).

| Your plan | An outfit to try | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Café and neighborhood walk | One jacket or skirt with clothes you already own | Walk and sit in it before buying. Check fabric care. |
| Gyeongbokgung regular visit | A jeogori jacket with a hanbok skirt or trousers | Match the overlapping collar and the jacket and bottom combination to the official examples. |
| Evening program or separately ticketed visit | Dress for the weather and the activity | Check the specific event or reserved area's admission and entry instructions. |

## Bring the right pieces to the palace

The [palace authority](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ROYAL/contents/R705000000.do) includes traditional and qualifying modern hanbok. It looks at the shape of the jacket as well as the combination of garments. Its English guidance says to wear the jacket and a leg-covering hanbok skirt or trousers. Jeans with only a jeogori, or a T-shirt with a hanbok skirt, do not qualify. Show the examples to the shop if you are considering a waist skirt or an unfamiliar modern cut.

- Ask a rental shop to show you the pieces as a set. Try walking and climbing a step in the bottoms, and make sure you can keep the jacket on comfortably.
- In cold weather, test thin layers underneath while trying on the whole outfit. If you need to add outerwear, ask staff how to present the outfit at the gate.
- If buying, note garment measurements as well as the size label. Ask about laundering, alterations and returns before paying. Take a photo of how the ties or fasteners sit, with the shop’s permission.

If you want a piece to keep, use the [modern hanbok buying checklist](/blog/hanbok-fusion-fashion-where-to-buy-modern-hanbok-for-daily-wear-2026).

## Set the destination and check your visit type

Use the [official palace visitor page](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E702000000.do) for the ordinary opening and admission information. Check the program listing separately for a night event or reserved visit. Leave time to change and return a rental even if you need to buy a regular ticket or shorten the visit.
