At a busy market stall, settle the order in a few clear steps. Point to the item, ask how the price is measured and show the number you want. Ask before taking photos of a person or picking up something that is not displayed for handling. If you are still deciding, step aside so another customer can order.

## Use these checks at the counter

| Step | What to ask or show |
| --- | --- |
| Amount | One piece, one serving or the size of the pack you want. **한 개 주세요** means “One, please.” |
| Price | **총 얼마예요?** asks the total. Confirm whether any displayed price is per item or per portion. |
| Pay | **카드 돼요?** asks if card payment is possible. Confirm the payment method before packing. |
| Bag | Ask if it can be packed to go and whether it needs refrigeration. For goods, ask for a receipt and any exchange terms. |

At [Gwangjang Market](https://english.visitseoul.net/dongdaemunarea/Gwangjang-Market/ENP000286), decide whether you want the food area or the textile and clothing sections. At [Tongin Market](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Tongin-Market/ENP000281), the lunchbox cafe’s tokens apply to participating stores. Check the program and participating counter before exchanging money. Other purchases have their own terms.

## Fix a mix-up while you are still nearby

Check the quantity and receipt before leaving. If something looks different from what you agreed, ask the same vendor to confirm the item and total. Keep the receipt and the stall name when you seek help. [VISITKOREA’s 1330 service](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632) offers travel information and interpretation assistance.
