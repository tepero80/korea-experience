## Identify the company, facility and clinician

Ask the coordinator for the company’s legal name, registration number and contact details. Look it up on the [Medical Korea facilitator directory](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredfacilitators). Use the [hospital and clinic directory](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredhospitals) separately to check the exact name and branch of the facility where you would be treated. Call or email the facility through its own published contact to confirm it works with that company and has the proposed appointment on record.

| Ask whom | Ask for |
| --- | --- |
| Agency | The written list of services, line-item prices, what is paid to the company and what is paid to the facility. |
| Facility | The exact branch, appointment date, clinician and who will be available to interpret. |
| Treating team | Who will explain consent and who will answer medical questions after you leave. |

## Check names on the payment instructions

Compare the billing name with the written quote. Ask who would return a deposit if you cancel, and whether interpreting or transport charges are separate. Ask the facility how to share records securely. If the business name, venue or payment instructions differ from the documents, pause and clarify the discrepancy directly with the facility.
