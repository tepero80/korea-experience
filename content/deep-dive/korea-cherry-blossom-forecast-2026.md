## What the 2026 dates show

In February 2026, [VisitKorea published a first-bloom forecast](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=1570512). The [KMA March climate report](https://www.kma.go.kr/kma/flexer/html/press/2026/04/02/ATC202604021618462_541a4117-3530-4c59-adaf-e57521d60f7e.hwp.files/Sections1.html) subsequently recorded first blossoms at its reference trees.

| City | Historical 2026 dates |
| --- | --- |
| Seoul | Forecast April 3. Observed March 29. |
| Busan | Forecast March 25. Observed March 26. |

These are first blossoms rather than dates for full displays. The [Seoul observation notice](https://www.kma.go.kr/kma/news/press.jsp?bid=press&from=2026-01-17&mode=view&num=1194625&page=1&to=2026-04-17) identifies a KMA reference tree and a separate monitored stand at Yunjung-ro in Yeouido. The photograph above shows Yeouido Park, which is a different location.

## Check again close to the trip

1. Look at the year and location on any forecast before saving it.
2. Use the [KMA seasonal observation page](https://www.weather.go.kr/w/forecast/life/seasonal-observation/spring-flower.do?treeType=1) when the spring service is active. Pick the observation location nearest your planned stop and check dated local updates.
3. Check the event organizer separately for programme dates and any access changes. Give yourself a flexible day in case the weather or flowers change.
