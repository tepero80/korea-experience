**Plan around a place with public access.** KWANGYA@SEOUL is a fan-facing shop. HiKR Ground is a public venue with its own schedule. A company office address gives you a reference point for a neighborhood walk.

| Stop | What you can plan |
| --- | --- |
| HYBE | [Visit Seoul’s walking tour](https://english.visitseoul.net/walking-tour/K-POP-HANGANG/ENN5h154u) lists this as an exterior stop and says the interior is restricted to employees. |
| JYP | [JYP’s corporate directions](https://www.jype.com/ko/JYP/Contact) give an address at 205 Gangdong-daero. Treat it as a working office and use only public areas. |
| KWANGYA | [Visit Seoul’s shop listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/other/KwangyaSeoul/ENPx2foc9) places it on B1 near Seoul Forest Station Exit 5. Check the current store hours and event rules. |
| HiKR Ground | [KTO’s venue page](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/sp/hikr) lists public experiences, floor-specific hours and a regular Monday closure for most of the venue. Check any program separately. |

## Build a public Yongsan walk

Use the [official self-guided route](https://english.visitseoul.net/walking-tour/K-POP-Self-Guidedtour/ENNsnflcq) for a walk from the National Museum area, and follow public sidewalks near **하이브 서울 용산구 한강대로 42**. If you are arriving by Line 4 from elsewhere, check your exit and onward path against today’s map. Spread the visits across your existing city plans. Pair KWANGYA with [a short Seoul Forest outing](/blog/seoul-forest-station-short-park-walk), or give your indoor stop its own time. Keep entrances clear and respect signs and staff instructions around working buildings.
