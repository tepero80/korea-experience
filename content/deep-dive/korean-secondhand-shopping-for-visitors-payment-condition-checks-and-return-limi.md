For secondhand goods, the useful question is whether this specific item and transaction work for you. Leave time to inspect it and ask the seller questions before paying.

## Before the sale

| Item | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Clothing | Seams, stains, odor, zips, measurements and whether trying on is allowed. |
| Electronics | Power on with permission; check ports, accessories, voltage and any lock or account. |
| Collectibles | Condition, missing pieces and provenance; a label alone does not establish authenticity. |
| Payment | Ask whether your card is accepted and the total price before agreeing; carry a backup. |
| Returns | Ask what happens for a defect or change of mind, the deadline and evidence required. |

Photograph the item and any agreed terms with permission, and keep the receipt, seller name and contact. A seller’s stated policy does not settle every legal right: the applicable rules can depend on whether the seller is a business or private individual and how the purchase was made. Do not assume every used purchase is refundable, or that a no-refunds sign necessarily ends a dispute.
