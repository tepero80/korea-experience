Start with the item name. Then read the options for pieces, sauce and sides before assuming you have ordered one full bird or food for a particular number of people. Menu photographs can help you identify the dish. Use the description to check the amount.

If the portion is already clear, [compare the fried, powdered and sauced flavor choices](/blog/the-evolution-of-korean-fried-chicken-new-flavors-and-brands-2026) before you put the order in.

## Spot these words before you pay

| Menu word | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| 순살 | Boneless. Check which meat or piece is sold and what size the order is. |
| 뼈 | Bone-in. Ask whether it is mixed pieces or a specific cut. |
| 후라이드 | Fried without the seasoned coating named 양념. Check any seasoning or sauce in the actual listing. |
| 양념 | Seasoned or sauced. Ask about flavor and heat. |
| 반반 | Two halves or two flavors. Confirm both flavors and whether you chose the boneless or bone-in version. |

[BBQ Chicken’s boneless half-and-half listing](https://mt.bbq.co.kr/menu/menuView.asp?midx=975) illustrates a combination of boneless crispy chicken and seasoned chicken. This is one menu item. Check whether pickled radish **치킨무**, dips or drinks are included in your order, and read the branch’s ingredient or allergen information if needed.

## Confirm the handoff

Use **포장** for takeaway pickup and **배달** for delivery. Verify the branch or address, total including any delivery fee, and the available payment option on the checkout screen. For hotel delivery, ask where the property allows couriers to meet guests. See the [hotel food delivery guide](/blog/how-to-use-baemin-without-an-arc-in-2026-pay-at-the-door-methods-and-alternatives) if you need to switch from app delivery to a pickup order.
