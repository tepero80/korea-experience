## Find the Insadong shop

[Saegimsori’s directions](https://saegimsori.com/mainbanner/saegimsorimap.html) point to **새김소리**, first floor, **서울특별시 종로구 인사동길 33**, with routes from Anguk Exit 6 and Jonggak Exit 3. The operator lists shop hours of **10:00–20:30** and closures on the main days of Seollal and Chuseok. Use the shop hours rather than the separate customer-service hours and call [02-2278-7809](tel:+82222787809) before a time-sensitive visit.

## Approve the order before you pay

| Step | Confirm with the shop |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Show a screenshot of the exact characters. Ask the staff to check spacing and whether your requested script and size fit. |
| Design | Choose the material and any extras. The [operator’s example](https://saegimsori.com/product/detail.html?cate_no=1&display_group=2&product_no=2536) shows options such as a side inscription, case or ink. Check whether your choice is sold in-store. |
| Pickup | Get the total quote and the latest pickup time and place. Keep the receipt and leave enough time to come back. |

**이 이름으로 도장을 주문할 수 있나요?** (Can I order a stamp with this name?)

**매장에서 언제 받을 수 있나요?** (When can I pick it up at the shop?)

At pickup, compare the imprint and receipt with the characters you approved. If the shop cannot finish before your departure, use the [Insadong crafts guide](/blog/insadong-traditional-crafts-and-souvenirs) to find an in-stock gift.
