**Use the physical card's transit balance, and check the amount before confirming.** A card purchase and a top-up are separate transactions. Start with enough Korean won for your immediate trips, then add more when needed; an exact amount depends on your route.

| Place | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Participating convenience store | Ask whether that branch can reload your particular card. |
| Subway station machine | Select a language and check that the screen offers a recharge for your card. |
| Combined payment/transit card | Identify which balance you are loading; the two can be separate. |

[Korea Tourism Organization](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=140663) describes Tmoney as a rechargeable card sold at participating convenience stores and Seoul subway information centers, with station vending-machine reloading. [Seoul's transport guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/public-transportation/) says station recharging devices provide language options and accept different payment methods on selected devices. Carry cash as a practical fallback and check the machine's current screen; do not assume a foreign card works at every machine.

## At the counter

Show the card and say **“티머니 충전해 주세요”** (“Please top up my T-money”). State the amount in won and ask whether the store can process it. Check the displayed amount or receipt before leaving. If the branch cannot recharge that card, ask staff for a station machine or another participating shop; do not assume that every shop has the same equipment.

## At the machine

Choose the transit-card recharge option, place the card where the machine indicates, select an amount and follow the payment instructions. Keep the card in place until the transaction finishes and check the displayed balance. If it fails, ask station staff before repeating a payment. A single-journey subway ticket is a different product: Seoul says it cannot be used for buses.

For a combined WOWPASS product, [KTO's product explanation](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=1593470) describes separate payment and T-money transit balances. Loading one does not necessarily load the other. Confirm your own card's instructions rather than treating every product as identical.

Before departure, ask about the current refund process and fees for your actual card and balance. Do not treat an old store-specific limit as a promise. For cash if your card is declined, see [foreign-card ATM checks](/blog/foreign-card-cash-withdrawals-in-korea-atm-labels-fees-and-fallback-options).
