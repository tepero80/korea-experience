**Pick Dongmyo for street stalls and Gwangjang for dedicated vintage clothing shops upstairs.** If you have one hour, choose one. Buying a particular brand, size or style depends on what is in stock that day.

## Pick a market by what you want to do

| If you want to… | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Browse stalls for clothes, records, books or small objects | **Dongmyo Flea Market.** Start near Exit 3 on Lines 1 and 6. Check conditions on the day if it is raining or hot. |
| Browse dedicated clothing shops and then get lunch | **Gwangjang Market.** Ask for the second-floor vintage shops. Start at Jongno 5-ga Station Exit 8. |
| Visit a specific shop | Check its own hours and entrance before leaving. The market’s general hours do not establish that a particular shop is open. |

The exits above come from the [Dongmyo route from Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/editorspicks/2026-Dongmyotour/ENNn7nirg) and the city's [Gwangjang Market listing](https://english.visitseoul.net/Tourist-Attractions-list1/Gwangjang-Market/ENP000286). The Gwangjang listing describes vintage and hanbok shops upstairs. Ask for **구제 의류 매장** (secondhand clothing shops) and check whether the available stairs or entrance suit your group.

If you want to visit both, Dongmyo and Jongno 5-ga are separate stops on Line 1. Save your second market in your map first. Decide whether to ride the subway or walk based on the weather, your bags and the current route. If you need step-free access, check the exit and shop separately.

## Check a secondhand item before paying

1. **Measure.** Bring measurements from something that fits. If there is no fitting room, hold the garment against a similar piece or ask whether you may measure it.
2. **Inspect.** Check closures, hems, lining and stains in good light. Magnified photos and labels help with the conversation, but a logo does not establish authenticity.
3. **Confirm the terms.** Ask **이거 얼마예요?** (“How much is this?”), **카드 돼요?** (“Do you take cards?”) and **교환 돼요?** (“Can I exchange it?”). Ask before paying and keep a receipt if one is available.

If you want to take a break at Gwangjang, use the [market food guide](/blog/gwangjang-market-complete-food-guide) and confirm the written portion and price at the stall.
