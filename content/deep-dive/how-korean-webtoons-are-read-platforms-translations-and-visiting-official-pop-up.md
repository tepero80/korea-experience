Webtoons are digital comics commonly read by scrolling through an episode on a phone. Before a trip, search a series by its Korean and translated titles on its official platform. The [English WEBTOON catalogue](https://www.webtoons.com/en/) gives you a place to search a translated title or browse by genre. Check the language and latest episode of the actual series before assuming it is available where you live. Check access, payment and account requirements on the specific service.

If you found a series through its television adaptation, [compare the official webtoon and K-drama versions](/blog/korean-webtoon-to-drama-adaptations-timeline) before choosing where to start.

## Choose the next step

| Goal | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| Read | Search the series and language on the authorised platform. |
| Visit | Find the publisher or organiser's dated announcement for the exact event. |
| Explore | Check Korea Manhwa Museum's current exhibitions and opening conditions. |

For a pop-up, confirm the organiser, dates, address, booking and queue policy before travelling. An old post or a character photo alone does not establish that an event is official or still open; merchandise is not guaranteed. For a broader comics stop, see the [museum listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=215510) for **한국만화박물관 · 경기 부천시 길주로 1**. Check its own current visitor information before going. For a practical event-day checklist, see the [Seongsu pop-up guide](/blog/seongsu-popup-guide-2026).
