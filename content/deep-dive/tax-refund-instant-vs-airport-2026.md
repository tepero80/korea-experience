**Ask before the shop charges your card.** Show your passport and ask, “Can you deduct the tourist tax refund when I pay?” Check the amount actually charged and retain the paperwork. A tax refund sign tells you to ask which service this branch offers.

| Shop option | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Instant refund** | Check the amount charged and keep the sales receipt. A separate airport payout is normally unnecessary for that purchase. |
| **Voucher** | You pay the tax-inclusive price. Keep both the sales receipt and the refund voucher. Check the operator and departure steps. |
| **No refund** | Confirm before paying. An ordinary receipt alone is insufficient for the airport claim. |

## Compare the amounts before you choose

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s visitor limits](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=140736) put an immediate refund in the **₩15,000 to below ₩1,000,000** range for one purchase. The [Korean tax-refund regulation, Article 6](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031600355), caps your combined immediate-refund purchases at **₩5,000,000 since entry**. Use tax-inclusive purchase prices for the comparison. A qualifying purchase priced at **₩999,000** can fall within the immediate range. At **₩1,000,000**, ask whether the branch can issue paperwork for the general route.

The [KTO walkthrough](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=489&vcontsId=248765) explains the separate export and claim steps. Keep qualifying goods unused and available for inspection, and take them out of Korea within three months. Check the stay and citizenship conditions that apply to you. Incheon Customs describes the foreign visitor condition as a stay of less than six months.

## Keep the voucher and goods accessible on departure day

If a refund needs to be processed later, keep the passport, sales receipt and tax refund voucher together. At Incheon, tell the airline before it takes a checked bag containing the goods. [Incheon Airport Customs](https://www.customs.go.kr/incheon/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=6688&mi=12546) describes how to scan the refund record and what to do when an inspection is requested. Use the [Incheon terminal walkthrough](/blog/tax-refund-for-tourists-how-to-claim) to plan the sequence and find the payout area. If you fly or sail from another terminal, check that departure point’s instructions.

A downtown booth can handle records from its matching operator. Check the voucher before going there and retain the instructions for confirming export when you leave.
