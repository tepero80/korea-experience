At a hypothetical average fare of ₩1,550, ten eligible charged journeys cost ₩15,500. A three-day pass at ₩10,000 plus a new ₩3,000 card costs ₩13,000. That saves ₩2,500 only if the trips fit its service days and coverage; add airport and intercity travel separately.

## Separate a stored-value card from a time pass

A stored-value card pays a fare as you make each eligible journey. A time pass buys access to a defined network for a specified period. Compare the routes and days before comparing the products.

A payment card balance, a transit balance and a transport pass are not interchangeable product benefits.

A Seoul pass is not a national rail or Busan pass; add uncovered journeys separately.

## Compare before you commit

| Decision input | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible station pairs and bus operators | Coverage precedes price |
| Service days remaining | Recharge starts immediately |
| Expected eligible journeys | Walking days may not justify a pass |
| New physical card needed | Add KRW 3,000 card cost |
| Uncovered airport/intercity journeys | Keep them outside the savings total |

This comparison organizes the decision. It is not a guarantee of availability or an individual price quote.

## Correct the late-2026 product situation

The old 30-day prepaid Climate Card product ended on August 31, 2026. Tourist short-term products remain for 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 days; resident Climate Pass eligibility is a separate question.

Do not count resident benefits in a short-visit budget. Seoul residence and the required foreign registration are different from staying temporarily in a hotel.

For short-term products, the checked pass prices are ₩5,000 / ₩8,000 / ₩10,000 / ₩15,000 / ₩20,000 for 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 7 days. A new physical card costs ₩3,000 separately. Recheck the official Seoul page before purchase.

**Check the operator:** [Seoul Metropolitan Government: Climate Card](https://world.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/climate-card/).

## Check the route before doing arithmetic

Ask where you will board/alight and which bus operator runs the service.

Airport inbound, Sinbundang, GTX and intercity journeys cannot be smuggled into eligible savings.

A route crossing the city boundary needs the current station exception list rather than a simple color-coded Seoul map.

## Calculate the break-even transparently

Compare sum of otherwise-payable eligible fares with pass cost plus a new card when needed.

Enter the fare for your expected journey and count charged journeys after transfers. One trip with a qualifying free transfer is not two full-fare journeys.

Try a low-use version of your plan as well as the optimistic version. Walking more or skipping an excursion can remove the savings from a pass that only just breaks even.

## Explain timing and practical use

Short-term recharge starts the period that day; it is not an advance purchase or a rolling hourly timer.

Keep purchase, recharge and mobile-payment compatibility separate, particularly for foreign phones/cards.

Follow the published tap-in and tap-out instructions and read the current refund conditions before charging the pass. Do not assume every unused balance or unused day will be refunded in full.

## Make a traveler-specific recommendation

Compare the covered total, new-card cost and uncovered journeys separately. A trip with mostly walking and only a few charged rides may favor pay as you go even when a pass is convenient.

If savings are small, weigh the pass’s convenience against its coverage limits and your chance of changing the route.

Check airport and out-of-city journeys independently. A pass that fits your central Seoul days may not cover the journey from Incheon Airport or a day trip to Suwon.
