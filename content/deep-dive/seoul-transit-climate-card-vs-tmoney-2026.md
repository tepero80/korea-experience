**Choose coverage before calculating a saving.** A Seoul-focused sightseeing trip and a trip that repeatedly starts outside the covered network are different comparisons. The [Seoul government's Climate Card page](https://world.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/climate-card/) is the controlling source for routes and current products.

## Use the visitor products that still apply

The checked official page says prepaid **30-day Climate Card recharging ended 31 August 2026**. Tourist short-term products remain. The separate resident Climate Pass has eligibility conditions; do not treat it as a general short-visit recommendation.

| Short-term service days | Listed pass price per traveler |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | ₩5,000 |
| 2 | ₩8,000 |
| 3 | ₩10,000 |
| 5 | ₩15,000 |
| 7 | ₩20,000 |

Add **₩3,000 for a new physical Climate Card** if needed. Check purchase/recharge conditions. Short-term use starts on the recharge day; do not assume you can recharge several days early and choose a later activation.

## Mark excluded journeys before adding the fare total

Check each origin, destination and route against the official coverage. Sinbundang, GTX and airport/express buses should not be assumed covered. The Incheon Airport T1/T2 exception permits qualifying **alighting, not boarding**. An inbound airport train is not automatically included in a city pass.


## Work through a family example correctly

For **three travelers, each making eight eligible charged journeys**, using an **editable ₩1,550 fare assumption**:

| Option | Per traveler | Three travelers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3-day pass + new physical card | ₩10,000 + ₩3,000 | ₩39,000 |
| Pay-as-you-go fares | 8 × ₩1,550 = ₩12,400 | ₩37,200 |

Pay as you go is **₩1,800 less for the party in this example**, before any pay-as-you-go card purchase. If each needs a new card for that option, add its actual cost too. Do not enter the family's combined 24 rides as one person's usage.

## Use the comparison for your own trip

Open the [pass calculator](/tools/transit-pass) and enter journeys **per traveler**, the fare assumption, new-card costs and other transport. Count charged journeys after transfer rules, not every vehicle boarding. Run a separate scenario for children, different routes or an existing card.

Then check the [airport journey](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train) and any [intercity train](/blog/ktx-vs-srt-high-speed-train-comparison-and-booking) separately. A calculator result is a cost comparison under your inputs, not confirmation that a route or card is eligible.
