## Use the event page to choose your evening

| Venue | What to look up |
| --- | --- |
| Rolling Hall | Pick a performance from the [official concert list](https://www.rollinghall.co.kr/default/mp3/mp3_sub2.php?sub=02). The listing may distinguish standing or seated entry, and advance sale or any door allocation. If stairs or seating matter, contact the venue. |
| Jebi Dabang | Check the current program from [Jebi Dabang](https://www.ctrplus.com/jebi) before going to Sangsu. The operator describes contributions for some performances. Confirm the date’s arrangements and any seating terms. |

## Check the return as well as the ticket

Before booking, check the date, doors time, age rules, ticket collection and last useful connection back. If you have no confirmed entry, have a nearby dinner or walking plan and avoid assuming a cellar venue has step-free access.

For a quieter evening, choose a public neighborhood walk and use the [Seoul base guide](/blog/seoul-neighborhood-chooser-for-first-time-visitors-where-to-stay-by-trip-style) to plan a simple trip back. Check the actual cross-town journey before adding a second late event.
