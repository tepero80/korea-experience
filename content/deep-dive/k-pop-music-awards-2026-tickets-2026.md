## What to confirm for the next edition

1. Open the organizer’s current edition page and record the **city, venue, local date and ticket seller**. Check the buyer eligibility, account requirements and sale window on that page.
2. Before paying, confirm how you will receive the ticket and what identification or verification the event requires. Use the organizer’s venue entry instructions. Check refund, transfer and resale terms with the seller.
3. After the ticket is confirmed, plan both the venue arrival and the last journey back. If a confirmed ticket names Gocheok, use the [Gocheok concert route](/blog/gocheok-sky-dome-concert-arrival-and-return). If it is abroad, plan separately from your Korea itinerary.
