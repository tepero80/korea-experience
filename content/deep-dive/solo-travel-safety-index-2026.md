A route is complete when you can get inside your accommodation. Save the Korean road address, entrance instructions, contact number and booking reference offline. Confirm whether someone can receive you at the time you expect to arrive. If access depends on a door code, check how you can retrieve it without a data connection.

## Decide while you are still inside the terminal

| If | Next move |
| --- | --- |
| Bus or rail | Verify the destination, final stop, operating time and the route from the stop to the door. |
| Taxi | Use the marked taxi queue or confirm the plate and pickup point for a booking you made. |
| Missed ride | Ask at a staffed counter where to wait and how to reach the authorized pickup point. |
| No access | Call the property from a staffed or busy area and confirm another way in. |

At Incheon, consult the [airport taxi information](https://www.airport.kr/ap_ko/986/subview.do) for the terminal and type of vehicle. For a night bus to Seoul, compare the [Terminal 1 schedule](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1507/subview.do) or [Terminal 2 schedule](https://airport.kr/ap_en/1510/subview.do) before going to the stop. Then use the [late arrival guide](/blog/incheon-airport-late-night-arrival-guide) for the airport-specific decision. At a rail or bus station, ask staff which exit leads to the marked taxi pickup or your onward connection. Go outside when you know where you are going.

## Have a way to ask for help

Share your expected arrival time and actual change of plan with someone you trust when practical. If your battery or data connection fails, show the saved address to station staff or the authorized ride attendant. Before getting into a booked car, check that it matches your own booking details.

If you need police call **112**. For a fire or ambulance call **119**, according to the [Korea Tourism Organization’s emergency guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145). Give the station, exit number or a recognizable nearby sign. [1330](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140632) provides travel information and interpretation assistance. For broader preparation, save the [traveler help guide](/blog/korea-travel-problem-solving-guide).
