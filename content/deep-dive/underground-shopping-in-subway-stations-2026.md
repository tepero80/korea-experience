## Choose the station you want

| Stop | How it fits a trip |
| --- | --- |
| Jamsil | Use the [Jamsil Station arcade listing](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/undershop/guide/gangnam/jamsil.jsp) for a stop around Jamsil. It lists **10:00–22:00** shop hours and closures on the **first and third Monday**. Confirm holiday changes and the individual shop. |
| West Seoul | The [Yeongdeungpo Station arcade](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/undershop/guide/yeongdeungpo/yeongdeungpo_station.jsp) directly serves its rail and subway station. Use the station complex if you are already in the area. Check which part of the station you will board from. |

An open pedestrian passage does not confirm open shops. Check [the operator’s holiday guidance](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/undershop/bbs/bbsMsgDetail.do?bcd=faq&cate1=undershop&msg_seq=146) and allow time to return to the correct platform or exit. Keep any planned purchases flexible. For the two stops in south Seoul that are easy to confuse, check the [Gangnam Station and Goto Mall guide](/blog/gangnam-underground-shopping-complex).
