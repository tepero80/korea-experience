## Check the night before

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting point | For the city-run tour, check your [Paju booking](https://dmz.paju.go.kr/) for the Imjingak ticket desk and boarding instructions. For a commercial pickup, pin the exact meeting point in the operator’s message. |
| ID | Bring your **original passport**. [Paju City](https://tour.paju.go.kr/user/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?areaSe=1001&cntntsSn=374&q_gubunCode=1008) lists acceptable originals and says that photos and copies do not qualify. Have everyone in your party check their own document. |
| Access | Recheck the departure before paying for a long transfer. Paju says weather or security restrictions can cancel a reserved visit. |
| Return | Confirm where and when your operator expects to drop you off. Leave room if you have a fixed rail ticket or evening booking. |

For the city-run tour, Paju says parties can collect tickets when everyone has arrived. Use your confirmation to find the correct desk. The map identifies the public Imjingak complex. It is not the meeting point for a commercial Seoul pickup.

<GuidePhoto id="imjingak-memorial-sign" caption="An Imjingak sign and memorial ribbons in the public area, photographed in 2018. Use the operator’s instructions to find the current departure desk." />

[Paju City’s current notice](https://tour.paju.go.kr/user/tour/place/BD_tourPlaceInfoView.do?areaSe=1001&cntntsSn=374&q_gubunCode=1008) says photography and video are prohibited on the controlled tour course. Follow the operator’s instructions at each stop. If you are unsure about the steep tunnel approach, call the city desk at [031-954-0303](tel:+82319540303) and ask which parts of your booked visit are suitable. Do not assume that a short wait or skipping a stop is available.

## If the tour is canceled

Confirm refund or rebooking arrangements with the company that sold your ticket. If you are already in Paju, [VisitKorea’s Imjingak listing](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=107397) identifies Pyeonghwa Nuri Park and the public memorial area. Check what is open and confirm your return before committing to a second activity. The [independent Imjingak guide](/blog/imjingak-without-a-dmz-tour) lays out that visit separately.
