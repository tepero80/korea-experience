## Look at the doorway before moving through it

On the subway platform, follow the markings for your door and leave an open path for people getting off. [Seoul's subway safety guidance](https://news.seoul.go.kr/traffic/archives/10362) asks riders to board after others exit and check the gap between the train and platform. If you miss the moment to enter safely, take the next train.

| Where you are | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A narrow shop entrance | Take a half step to the side so someone leaving can pass. Hold a manual door briefly if the person behind is close and you have room. |
| A crowded station elevator | Wait beside the opening with your luggage close. Let passengers exit first and leave space for wheelchairs, strollers or other mobility aids. |
| A subway platform | Use the marked queue where there is one. Give people a clear exit and watch the gap when stepping aboard. |
| A Seoul city bus | Follow the marked doors and have your transit card ready. The city's visitor guide shows boarding at the front and leaving by the rear on city buses. |

## Use a pause to solve an uncertain situation

If a door has a push button or is moving on its own, use its posted instructions and wait for it to open. Leave controlled and train doors to their normal operation. If you need to pass in a crowd, a short **실례합니다** (sillyehamnida, “excuse me”) can get someone's attention. Give them time and space to move.

Before your stop, move toward an exit when space allows. Keep a backpack or rolling case from occupying the path. If you need room to turn, step out of the way and try again once the exit clears.

## Plan for the part of the journey above the platform

Check which station entrance has a usable elevator before you travel with luggage. Leave time to wait for another lift or train. The [Seoul route guide](/blog/seoul-transportation-guide-for-tourists-2026) helps you compare the walk, transfer and last connection. For the bus boarding and tapping sequence, use [Seoul's public transportation guide](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/movement/public-transportation/).

**Editorial check.** The subway and city-bus instructions come from Seoul's published guidance. The shop and elevator suggestions help a visitor handle the space in front of them.
