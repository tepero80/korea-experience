**Set up two maps before you leave.** Google Maps is useful for collecting places and can show transit for some routes. Use [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/) or [KakaoMap](https://map.kakao.com/) when you need walking, driving or a clearer last stretch to the entrance. The [Korea Tourism Organization’s transport guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/contents/travel/KoreaTransportationGuide_enu.pdf) covers local apps for visitors.

| What you need | Start here | Check before you leave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Save a hotel, café or museum | Google Maps or a local map | Match the branch and the Korean street address. |
| Find a subway or bus route | Compare Google’s available transit result with a local map | Check the departure time, station exit and walk from the stop. |
| Walk from a station or drive to a venue | Naver Map or KakaoMap | Choose the pedestrian entrance or drop-off you actually need. |
| Find a step-free approach | Venue or operator accessibility information | Identify working elevators and the entrance that serves your route. |

## A route to try in Seoul

On November 24, 2026, we checked [Seoul Station’s KTX building to Gyeongbokgung in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8%EC%97%AD+KTX+%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8+%EC%9A%A9%EC%82%B0%EA%B5%AC+%ED%95%9C%EA%B0%95%EB%8C%80%EB%A1%9C+405&destination=%EA%B2%BD%EB%B3%B5%EA%B6%81+%EC%84%9C%EC%9A%B8+%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C%EA%B5%AC+%EC%82%AC%EC%A7%81%EB%A1%9C+161&travelmode=walking) in a desktop browser. The result offered public transit. Walking, driving and cycling were unavailable for that search. Open the exact route on your own device before depending on it. A search for *Seoul Station* can also choose a different entrance within the station complex.

## Make a Korean address your backup

1. Copy the Korean name and road address from the operator, ticket or accommodation. Save the full branch name, which side of the station you need and any confirmed exit number.
2. Paste the Korean name into the local map. Compare its pin with the address, then plan the route for the time you intend to travel.
3. Save the address and exit in a note or screenshot. If you lose data, show the address to station staff or your driver and confirm the street entrance when you arrive.

## What changed in 2026

The [Korean government’s November 13 update](https://www.motir.go.kr/attach/down/095a2dda9c864e1d90d751f7668a1117/ae9b860939a149f0a75b89741e3398d8/9a9db098b587ee18b321c826f3707a49) says conditional permission to export 1:5,000 map data was granted on February 27 and reports checks on the attached conditions. [Google’s explanation](https://blog.google/intl/ko-kr/company-news/inside-google/google-maps-data-export-qa-kr/) distinguishes the geographic data used to display a map from what is needed for fuller navigation. A government decision gives travelers context. The route available in the app is the one to check before departure.
