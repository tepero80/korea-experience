**The translation helps you choose what to point at. Confirm the actual order with the vendor.** Keep the Korean name beside any translation, especially if nearby menu items look alike.

## Translate one part of the board at a time

1. In the Papago app choose **Korean** and the language you read. Open the image translation tool. The [Naver instructions](https://help.naver.com/service/16448/contents/5973?lang=ko&osType=MOBILE) show how to take a photo or select an existing one.
2. Frame the dish name, price and unit together. Take a tighter picture if the prices are on another line, or select just one section of a crowded menu. Ask before photographing where appropriate.
3. Point to the original Korean line and show the quantity you want. Ask the vendor to indicate the total on a calculator or your phone.

| Korean to show | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **이 메뉴 주세요.** | This item, please. |
| **일 인분 가격이에요?** | Is this the price for one serving? |
| **총 얼마예요?** | How much is it in total? |

## Have an offline fallback on your phone

Keep a screenshot of the phrases above and the relevant Korean dish names. Naver’s [offline help](https://help.naver.com/service/16448/contents/25501?lang=en) covers installed language packs for supported languages and modes. Plan to use image translation while connected, and test your downloaded pack before leaving Wi-Fi. For more ways to order when translation fails, use the [restaurant phrases guide](/blog/how-to-order-food-in-korean-restaurants-phrases-guide-2026).

If an ingredient or cross-contact matters, ask in writing and choose somewhere else if you cannot get a clear answer. See the [ingredient and cross-contact guide](/blog/navigating-korean-restaurant-menus-with-allergies-translation-and-cross-contact) for ways to communicate the constraint.

## Get help around Namdaemun

Visit Seoul lists the [Namdaemun Market Tourist Information Center](https://english.visitseoul.net/area/Namdaemun-Market-Tourist-Information-Center/ENP027219) near **Hoehyeon Station Exit 5**. Check the listing for current hours and languages. The tourist information desk can help with market directions. Check decisions about a dish with the person preparing the food.
