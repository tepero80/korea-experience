Look at the signs and the counter when you enter: a bakery may have self-service displays, a staff-served case, a kiosk, or a combination. Seating and packaging rules also vary by shop. If the route is unclear, ask staff before picking up an item or claiming a table.

## Choose the ordering path

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Open bread display with trays and tongs | Use the supplied tray and tongs if the shop directs customers to serve themselves; take the items to the counter and pay before eating. |
| Closed case or items behind a counter | Point to the item and ask staff for the quantity; don't open the case yourself. |
| Kiosk | Check whether your item is listed and whether there is a language option. If you cannot complete the order or payment, ask at the counter. |
| Packaged product | Check the label on the actual package; ask staff if you need help reading it. |

At [Ijin Sanghoe in Icheon](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=187326), Korea Tourism Organization describes a bakery café where visitors choose bread and use ceramic plates at their tables. This is one example, not a rule for all bakeries. Follow that day's instructions and check availability before making a special trip.

## Sitting in or taking away

Before paying, say **매장에서 먹을게요** (I will eat here) or **포장해 주세요** (please pack it to go). If there is a kiosk, look for **매장** (in-store) or **포장** (takeaway), but ask if you cannot find the choice. A shop may have limited seats, a queue, or no seating. For example, the [Korea Tourism Organization listing for Meal° Seongsu](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=177426) describes it as takeaway-only; confirm current arrangements before visiting.

If you want a table, ask **먼저 자리를 잡아도 되나요?** (May I choose a seat first?). Check posted minimum-order or time-limit notices instead of assuming that one drink per person is always required. Keep valuables with you. When leaving, look for a return station or ask staff where plates and cups go.

## Check the exact item's ingredients

Check the exact item or package. For example, the [Tous Les Jours page for its 32-layer bread](https://m.tlj.co.kr/product/detail.asp?prod_num=5096) lists egg, milk, soy, wheat and sulfites for that product. Other pastries can have different ingredients.

For an allergy, show staff the precise ingredient you must avoid and ask about shared trays, tongs and preparation surfaces. Try **이 제품에 *ingredient name*이 들어 있나요?** (Does this product contain [ingredient]?) and **같은 집게를 사용하나요?** (Are the same tongs used?). Staff may not be able to confirm every ingredient or cross-contact risk; if the answer is uncertain, choose a verified alternative.
