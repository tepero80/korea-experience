## The Background Story

Let's rewind a bit. Back in 2015, the Korean plastic surgery market was essentially the Wild West. Prices were secret, "brokers" (middlemen) were charging illegal 30-50% commissions to bring foreigners to clinics, and patients had no way of knowing if a doctor was good or just good at marketing.

Enter Dr. Hong Seung-il. He was a medical doctor who realized the system was broken. He co-founded Healing Paper and launched Gangnam Unni to bring transparency to the market. It wasn't smooth sailing—he actually got into serious legal trouble. In Korea, "referring" patients for profit is illegal. Dr. Hong was sentenced (suspended) because the app sold coupons, which the court ruled was a form of patient brokering.

But here is the twist: instead of shutting down, the company pivoted. They cleaned up their act, switched to a legal advertising model, and became the standard for safety transparency. They introduced CCTV filters to stop "ghost surgeries" (where a different doctor operates on you) and expanded to Japan. By 2023, they realized the rest of the world wanted in, so they launched the global "UNNI" app. Today, it’s not just an app; it’s a massive ecosystem that forces clinics to compete on price and quality.

## The Stuff Nobody Tells You

Here is the insider info that usually takes years to learn.

### The "Factory" Clinic Trap
You will see some clinics with thousands of reviews and insanely low prices. These are often "Factory" clinics. They are efficient, but you might only see the doctor for 3 minutes before you pass out. If you want personalized design—especially for complex procedures like breast augmentation—you might want to pay a bit more for a boutique clinic. For a comparison of premium implant brands and costs, see our guide on [Motiva vs Sebbin Korea 2026](/blog/breast-augmentation-motiva-vs-sebbin-2026).

### The 2026 VAT Refund Mess
This is critical. For years, tourists got a 10% tax refund on cosmetic surgery. The government planned to end this in 2025. It was extended to 2026, but it's on shaky ground. **Do not assume you will get this money back.** Ask explicitly: "Is the Tax Refund slip provided?" If they say no, factor that 10% loss into your budget.

### The "Deposit" Warning
Clinics will pressure you to put down a deposit (usually 10%) to "lock in" the event price. **Be careful.** These are almost never refundable if you change your mind, even if they say they are. Only pay the deposit if you are 100% sure.
