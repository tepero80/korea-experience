## Compare the setup before choosing a ride

| App | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| TABA | Try SMS verification on your international number. [TABA’s FAQ](https://www.tabataxi.app/) says phone verification is required and the current app does not support advance bookings. |
| k.ride | Open the app and try adding your own card. [Official k.ride](https://utility.kride.kakaomobility.com/) lists card and mobile payment options, and [KTO’s taxi guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?vcontsId=140661) identifies overseas cards and Google or Apple sign-in. |

## Get the pickup right

1. Save the hotel or venue name, Korean street address and entrance in a note you can open offline. Confirm the pin matches the entrance where a car can stop, then compare the estimated totals for vehicles that fit your party and bags.
2. If SMS does not arrive, check whether your original number can receive messages. A data-only travel eSIM does not by itself receive a code for that number. Try your already configured app or go to a marked taxi rank.
3. Match the car and plate shown in the app before getting in. Keep the receipt or trip details. If a payment looks duplicated, compare the final posted payment and any pending hold before reporting it.

## If you land at Incheon and the app cannot find a ride

Follow the [airport authority’s taxi and call-van guide](https://www.airport.kr/ap_ko/986/subview.do) for the taxi rank at the terminal where you actually arrive. Show the Korean address and ask at the official desk about a larger vehicle if you have bulky luggage. For a time-critical pickup, check an operator that explicitly confirms a reservation and terminal meeting point. If you are still choosing between a taxi, bus and train, check the [Incheon airport transfer comparison](/blog/airport-limousine-bus-vs-arex-express-train).
