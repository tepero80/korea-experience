**Do not book an exosome-labelled service from the marketing name alone.** Ask for the exact product, manufacturer, proposed route and evidence of authorization for that use. This guide has not verified a particular Korean product or procedure and does not recommend an exosome treatment.

## Separate three questions

| Question                       | Evidence to request                                              |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What is the product?           | Exact label, manufacturer and product identity                   |
| How is it proposed to be used? | Route, intended purpose and the clinician's written explanation  |
| What authorizes that use here? | Current regulator record and scope for the exact product and use |

“Stem-cell,” “regenerative,” “cosmetic,” “clinic use,” and a research citation are not interchangeable approvals. Do not treat microneedling or another route as a way to bypass regulation. If the provider cannot give a checkable answer, postpone the decision.

The [US FDA consumer alert](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/consumers-biologics/consumer-alert-regenerative-medicine-products-including-stem-cells-and-exosomes) warns about unapproved exosome products and misleading claims. It concerns US regulation; it does **not** establish Korean authorization or the status of a particular product in Seoul. Ask the provider for the relevant Korean regulatory record rather than using a foreign claim as a shortcut.

## Take these questions to the consultation

- What is being offered for my concern, and what are the alternatives, including no treatment?
- What evidence applies to this exact product and proposed use, rather than to a different formulation or laboratory study?
- Who assesses me and who performs each step?
- What risks, uncertainties, costs and follow-up apply to my circumstances?
- How can I verify the record independently, and may I take time to review it before paying?

We do not publish a universal benefit percentage, a fixed recovery schedule or a price ranking. A before/after image or a sales package cannot answer those questions for an individual traveller.

## Keep the trip decision reversible

Check the provider using [official registration information](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/registeredsystem), recognizing that registration does not approve a particular product. Keep consultation and treatment decisions separate, confirm deposit terms and avoid building non-refundable travel around a promised result.

For non-emergency service questions use [Medical Korea](https://www.medicalkorea.or.kr/en/convenient). For emergency assistance, [119 is ambulance/fire and 112 is police](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=248145).
