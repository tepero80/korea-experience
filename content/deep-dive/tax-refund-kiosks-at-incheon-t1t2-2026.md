Find your terminal on your airline booking. The departures kiosk handles the **export check**. A kiosk or counter after security handles any **payout still due**. The two serve different purposes, even when both are marked “Tax Refund.”

## Find the kiosk for the step you need

The [airport facility directory](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1546/subview.do) lists public kiosks by check-in area. The [airport’s tax refund page](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1425/subview.do) lists the dedicated payout points below. Recheck the directory and signs on the day, particularly where the airport lists more than one operator.

| Step | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| **T1 before security** | Public departures on Level 3 near check-in B, D, J or L. |
| **T1 after security** | Near Gate 28. In the separate Concourse, look beside the central pharmacy on Level 3. |
| **T2 before security** | Public departures on Level 3 near check-in F or G. |
| **T2 after security** | Near Gate 253, across from Gate 250. The facility directory also lists points near Gates 249 and 270. |

Follow **Tax Refund / 세금 환급** signs. At the time of review the airport listed automated payout kiosks at its dedicated T1 and T2 points as open 24 hours. The staffed desks had shorter hours. Check the current terminal listing if you are relying on staff for an early or late flight.

## Before you drop the bag

1. **Separate the receipts.** Keep your passport, the refund voucher or purchase confirmation, and the ordinary receipt. If you are missing the refund paperwork, ask the store or its refund operator. A regular receipt alone does not replace the voucher.
2. **Tell the airline at check-in if the goods are in a checked bag.** Under the airport’s procedure, staff tag the bag and return it for the refund steps. Keep carry-on goods and documents with you.
3. **Scan the passport and refund record at the public departures kiosk.** If selected for inspection, show Customs your passport, voucher and unopened, unused goods. Follow staff instructions for dropping the tagged bag at the large-baggage conveyor beside Customs once cleared.

The [Incheon Customs instructions](https://www.customs.go.kr/incheon/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=6688&mi=12546) explain which documents and goods you may need to present. Plan for this stop before security if your check-in or terminal-transfer window is tight.

## After security: collect or troubleshoot

- **Your export record is approved and a payout is due.** Follow the instructions for the refund operator on your voucher. Use the amount and payment options shown on the approved record. The airport lists card credit as an option for some payouts.
- **The machine cannot find the record.** Ask the staffed desk or your refund operator for help. Keep your voucher and kiosk confirmation. If you missed a selected inspection, ask airport staff immediately.
- **You took a city-counter refund.** The [Korea Tourism Organization’s guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=489&vcontsId=140736) calls for export confirmation at departure. Check your operator’s directions before handing over the goods.
- **The store already deducted the refund at checkout.** Keep the receipt and follow the store’s export instructions. The same purchase does not qualify for a second payout.
