# Home and guide discovery

The owner confirmed this scope after the release of 60 new guides and 15 destination hubs.

- **Outcome** — Give international travelers two ways to get started, whether they reach the homepage directly or land on a guide from search.
- **Home** — Present first-trip planning and destination exploration with equal weight on the first mobile screen. Follow with verified place photographs and a direct path to the full destination directory. Keep guide search available.
- **Guide** — Keep the opening answer and article readable. Offer the relevant local destination first, followed by the first-trip guide and destination directory. Nationwide or archived guides without a verified local match offer general planning paths. The first-trip guide leads with the destination directory and planning library, then links back to the Seoul collection.
- **Constraint** — Use the existing guide and hub records, photo rights, routes, share metadata and consent controls. Keep the existing ad below reading and the new links. The shared navigation and home layout are the scope. Article rewrites and a sitewide redesign are separate work.
- **Acceptance** — Test both paths, keyboard navigation, content and link order, photo loading and page fit at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px. Confirm the published HTML and deployed commit. The existing private report should count consented clicks by entry and source route, with missing provider observations kept distinct from zero. Follow real clicks during the weekly review.
