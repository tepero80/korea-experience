## Traveler task assessment

These are task-based persona simulations, informed by earlier independent source critiques and the real browser evidence below. They are not interviews or invented satisfaction scores.

| Need | Evidence and assessment |
| --- | --- |
| First visit and a limited budget | The Seoul-base and airport guides lead to an actual neighborhood/service choice, with rail segment/group units, hotel walk and a fallback. Search, onward links and map/address actions have working HTML controls. |
| Late arrival with luggage | The helper exercises evening versus overnight ready times and clears stale results. Terminal-specific official links and the DMC-terminating last service prevent a misleading central-Seoul promise. Hotel reception remains a separate check. |
| Family or group | Tests count a pass/card for each same-fare traveler. The actual two-room budget changes accommodation without multiplying meal costs; its downloaded card retains assumptions. Mixed child/adult fares need separate inputs, and room occupancy is checked beyond bed count. |
| Rain or limited mobility | One-area alternatives, official museum access/contact/reservation guidance and corrected route markers support a bounded outing. Overview markers and historical photos do not promise a step-free entrance or current availability. |
| Dietary restrictions and limited Korean | Ordering, barbecue and allergy guides supply practical questions/communication aids and fallback choices. They do not label an unverified dish safe or invent a firsthand visit. |
| Save, share and ask | The production account saved/reopened an edited itinerary, reviewed public fields, kept subsequent edits private and revoked the old public URL. Real Google/email sign-in returned to the original guide with its local question draft; a QA comment was received pending review and never published. |

Taken together, these tasks support the site's purpose: find an answer, make a decision, act, retain/share a useful plan and ask a specific question. Actual willingness to return or recommend must be measured after release.
